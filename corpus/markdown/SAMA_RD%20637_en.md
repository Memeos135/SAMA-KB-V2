Council of Ministers
Having perused the Transaction received from the Royal Court No. 55806 dated 10/10/1441 
AH, regarding the proposal to amend Law on Combating the Financing of Terrorism, issued by 
Royal Decree No. (M/211) dated 12/02/1439 AH.
Having perused Law on Combating the Financing of Terrorism issued by Royal Decree No. 
(M/21) dated 12/02/1439 AH.
Having perused the Implementing Regulations of Law on Combating the Financing of 
Terrorism, issued by Council of Ministers Resolution No. (228) dated 02/05/1440 AH.
Having perused the Minute No. (1053) dated 08/08/1441 AH, prepared by the Bureau of 
Experts at the Council of Ministers.
Having perused the Telegram of the Secretariat of the Council of Political and Security Affairs 
No. 9574, dated 26/09/1441 AH.
Having perused the Shura Council Resolution No. (220/41) dated 09/10/1441 AH.
Having perused the recommendation of the General Committee of the Council of Ministers 
No. (7092) dated 15/10/1441 AH
Resolved the following:
First: Amending Article No. (12) of the Law on Combating the Financing of Terrorism, issued 
by the Royal Decree No. (M/21) dated 12/02/1439 AH, to be as follows:
“The accused of crimes under this law may not be provisionally released except temporary by 
order of the Public Prosecution when there is no harm to the interest of the investigations, 
feared to scape or disappear”.
A draft Royal Decree has been prepared to that effect, and its wording is annexed herewith-
Second: Approving repealing Article No. (9) of the Implementing Regulation, Law on 
Combating the Financing of Terrorism, issued by Council of Ministers Resolution No. (228) 
dated 02/05/1440 AH. It stipulates the following:
“Security concerns referred to in Article No. (12) of the law:
1. Fear of escape or disappearance.
2. Harming the investigations interest.

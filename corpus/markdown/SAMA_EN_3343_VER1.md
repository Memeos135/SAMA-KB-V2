Loans to Deposits Ratio Guideline
1. Introduction
In line with AMA 's continuous efforts to maintain the quality and soundness
of banks' regulatory ratios and to support banks in managing their liquidity,
AMA has reviewed the existing Loans to Deposits Ratio (LOR) guidelines
to comprehensively capture banks' funding base.
These guidelines are issued by 
AMA in exercise of the authority vested in
SAMA under the Central Bank Law issued via Royal Decree 
o. M/36 dated
11 /04/ 1 442H, and the Banking Control Law issued O 1/0 l/l 386H.
These guidelines supersede the Loans to Deposits Ratio Guidelines circular
No B.C.S 392 dated O 1 /07/1427H and subsequent guidelines for calculating
Loans to Deposits Ratio (LOR) issued via SAMA circular No 39 l 00007 2844
dated 25/06/l 439H
2. Implementation Timeline
These guidelines will be effective as of J S' of June 2023.
3. Reporting Requirements
Banks are required to report the Loan to Deposit ratio (LOR) to SAMA on
consolidated basis using the updated LOR returns on monthly basis. The ratio
should include local and foreign currency transactions of resident and non­
resident entities of the bank.

numerator not exceeding unweighted denominator.

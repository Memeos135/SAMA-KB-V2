# Debt Collection Licensing

## Why this community

Licensing and permissibility of debt collection as a finance support activity, and the conditions under which finance companies may outsource collection to third parties. Covers the shift from a standalone licensing requirement to reliance on the finance support activity regime.

## How members connect

- Hierarchy: the Finance Companies Control Law's Implementing Regulation is the parent instrument from which the Licensing Rules for Finance Support Activities and the collection circulars derive authority.
- The Licensing Rules and their amendment share the same defined scope of 'finance support activities', with the amendment altering entry conditions; the cancelling circular removes a licensing prerequisite, so effective dates determine which text governs a given collection arrangement.
- Outsourcing circular chains obligation back to the licensor: a finance company remains accountable for the conduct of the collection entity it engages, linking licensing status of 'Finance Debt Collection Entities' to the outsourcing permission.
- Consumer-protection and conduct consequences flow through: who may contact a debtor depends on whether the collector is licensed, contracted, or acting for the finance company.

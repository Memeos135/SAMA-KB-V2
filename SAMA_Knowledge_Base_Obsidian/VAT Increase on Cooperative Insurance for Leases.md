# VAT Increase on Cooperative Insurance for Leases

A SAMA circular clarifying the treatment of the VAT increase from 5% to 15% (effective 1 July 2020) for cooperative insurance contracts linked to finance lease contracts. It confirms the lessor must bear the increase and may not pass it on to the lessee, relying on the Finance Lease Law provision placing cooperative insurance of the leased asset on the lessor. It binds finance companies acting as lessors.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, consumer protection

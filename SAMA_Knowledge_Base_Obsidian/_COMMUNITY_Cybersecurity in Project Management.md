# Cybersecurity in Project Management

## Why this community

Embedding cyber security requirements into the project lifecycle — the expectation that supervised entities address security risk at initiation, design and change stages of projects rather than after deployment.

## How members connect

- Single-member cluster within the governance and operational/cyber risk regime.
- Reads as a control-domain requirement that presupposes a broader cyber security framework and board/senior-management accountability for risk ownership.
- Conceptually adjacent to the cyber security awareness material; both are components of the same overarching cyber risk regime even though no edge is recorded.

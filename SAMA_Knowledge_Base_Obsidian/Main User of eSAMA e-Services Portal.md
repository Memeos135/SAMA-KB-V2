# Main User of eSAMA e-Services Portal

A SAMA circular introducing the eSAMA e-services portal (individuals, business and government tracks) for digital submission of requests such as no-objection for senior appointments and for product/service launches, with phased replacement of paper submissions and authentication via the National Single Sign-On (Nafath). It designates the financial institution's most senior executive (CEO or equivalent) as the mandatory main portal user, populated automatically via SAMA's link with the Ministry of Commerce, with authority to delegate submission rights. Responsibility for compliance with the circular and for requests filed through the portal rests with the board (or equivalent), while the CEO owns governance of user entitlements, including removal of departing users; it binds financial institutions supervised by SAMA.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, other

# Cure Rate

Defines cure rate and re-default rate as the key measures of whether a forborne credit facility has genuinely returned to performing status, assessed by performance analysis after a designated cure period. Sets an expectation that the minimum cure period used for cure-rate measurement is 12 months, and points to vintage analysis of restructured exposures. Relevant to banks classifying and reporting restructured/forborne loans.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

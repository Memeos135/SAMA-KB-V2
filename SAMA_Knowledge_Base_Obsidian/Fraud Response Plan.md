# Fraud Response Plan

Concept node for the Fraud Response Plan, the core requirement of the Respond domain of SAMA's Counter-Fraud Framework. It requires Member Organisations to define, approve, implement and maintain a plan — aligned where appropriate with enterprise incident management — covering alert and reporting channels for customers, employees and third parties, roles and responsibilities, escalation and decision-making authority, and prompt assessment, investigation and resolution of suspected or identified fraud, with compliance monitored and effectiveness periodically evaluated. It binds Member Organisations and is assessed against the Counter-Fraud Maturity Model.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, consumer protection

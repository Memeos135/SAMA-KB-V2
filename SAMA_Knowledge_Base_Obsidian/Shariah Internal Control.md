# Shariah Internal Control

Chapter Seven (Article 16) establishes the three internal control pillars for Shariah governance: a Shariah compliance function performing regular assessment of Islamic activities and corrective actions; Shariah non-compliance risk management embedded in the bank's integrated risk framework and staffed by suitably qualified risk officers; and an independent internal Shariah audit providing objective assurance. It binds local banks conducting Shariah-compliant banking and links to the product development chapter.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential

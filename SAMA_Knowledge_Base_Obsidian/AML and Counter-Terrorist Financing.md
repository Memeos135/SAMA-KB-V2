# AML and Counter-Terrorist Financing

This node is the AML/CTF compliance article of SAMA's Money Changing Business rules, requiring Money Changers to observe the AML Law and its Implementing Regulations, the Law of Terrorism Crimes and Financing and its Implementing Regulations, related SAMA circulars and instructions, and the guideline implementing UN Security Council resolutions on proliferation financing of weapons of mass destruction. It sits alongside the enforcement provisions setting out SAMA's supervisory actions and penalties (warnings, corrective action, branch closure, service suspension, licence suspension or revocation, fines under SAMA's Charter, publication of decisions). It binds licensed Money Changers.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, sanctions/TFS, governance/risk

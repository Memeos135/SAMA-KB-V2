# Rules for Engaging in Debt-Based Crowdfunding

SAMA's Rules for Engaging in Debt-Based Crowdfunding sit under the Finance Companies Control Law and govern the licensing and conduct of debt-based crowdfunding activity — raising funds from Participants via a platform to lend to an Institutional Beneficiary under a loan contract. They define key terms (Participant, Eligible Participant thresholds, Institutional Beneficiary, Platform) and impose obligations on client-money segregation, electronic-only repayment, automatic participation consent, and platform disclosure and risk acknowledgment. They bind licensed Debt-Based Crowdfunding Companies operating in the Kingdom.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, consumer protection, governance/risk

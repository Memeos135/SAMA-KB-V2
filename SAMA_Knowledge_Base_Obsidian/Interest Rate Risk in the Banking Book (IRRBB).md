# Interest Rate Risk in the Banking Book (IRRBB)

A SAMA circular issuing the revised Basel framework for Interest Rate Risk in the Banking Book, updating the 2004 principles on identification, measurement, monitoring, control and supervision of IRRBB. Key changes include guidance on interest rate shock scenarios and behavioural/modelling assumptions, enhanced quantitative disclosure requirements, an updated standardised framework, and a tighter outlier threshold of 15% of Tier 1 capital (previously 20% of total capital); it is accompanied by regulatory returns and FAQs with national discretions. It applies to domestic banks only and not to foreign bank branches.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

# Trade Governance and Valuation

## Why this community

Governance and control expectations attaching to derivative trade confirmation and valuation — who owns the process, how trades are confirmed within required timeframes, and on what basis positions are valued.

## How members connect

- The governance member sets the accountability layer (board/senior management ownership, policies, internal control) that the confirmation and valuation requirements operate under.
- Direct cross-reference: governance obligations are discharged by evidencing compliance with the confirmation/valuation duties, making the two a single obligation chain rather than parallel duties.
- Shared scope: both apply to the same population of counterparties and covered derivative transactions, so a scope carve-out for one affects the other.

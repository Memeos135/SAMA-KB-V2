# Resecuritization Exposure

Concept node for resecuritisation exposure: a securitisation exposure where an underlying tranched pool includes at least one securitisation exposure, including exposures to other resecuritisation exposures, with a carve-out for retranching where the bank can demonstrate replication of cash flows by an exposure to a pool containing no securitisations. Carries the punitive SEC-SA adjustments (underlying capital requirement computed under the securitisation framework, delinquencies set to zero for securitisation tranches, supervisory parameter p of 1.5). Binds banks holding or originating such positions for capital calculation and related Pillar 3 disclosure.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

# Transition Phase-in for Equity Exposures

A transitional provision within SAMA's Basel III-aligned capital and Pillar 3 disclosure framework addressing equity exposures. It records that the prohibition on using the IRB approach for equity exposures is phased in linearly over five years from 1 January 2022, with the applicable risk weight during that period being the higher of the IRB-calculated weight and the standardised phase-in weight, and permits SAMA to require immediate application of the fully phased-in standardised treatment; it also directs how phase-in equity exposures are reported (250% speculative unlisted equities, 400% other equities) in credit risk disclosure templates. Binds banks subject to SAMA's capital adequacy and Pillar 3 disclosure requirements.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

# Banking Control & Branch Network

## Why this community

Core prudential and institutional supervision of licensed banks — the enabling statute plus derived requirements on branch presence, financial inclusion, capital adequacy (including Shari'ah-compliant products), senior appointments and currency handling.

## How members connect

- The Banking Control Law is the parent statute; the branch network regulation, circulars on leadership appointments and damaged currency, and the Shari'ah capital adequacy requirements all cite it as their legal basis (law → regulation → circular).
- Branch network obligations are linked to financial inclusion policy objectives, making geographic coverage a supervisory outcome rather than a purely commercial choice.
- Capital adequacy add-ons for Shari'ah-compliant banking sit as an overlay on the SAMA Basel Framework, so the two must be read together when computing requirements.
- Fit-and-proper style appointment controls tie governance obligations back to the licensing power under the statute.

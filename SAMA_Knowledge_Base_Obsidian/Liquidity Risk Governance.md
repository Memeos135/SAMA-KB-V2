# Liquidity Risk Governance

The governance section of SAMA's liquidity risk management rules for finance companies, allocating ultimate responsibility for liquidity risk to the Board and setting out its duties: setting liquidity risk tolerance, approving strategy, policies, the contingency funding plan and stress testing framework at least annually, and monitoring the liquidity profile. It also defines senior management responsibilities and recommends an ALCO, plus reporting of cash flow mismatches, funding concentration and early warning signals to management, the Board and SAMA. It binds finance companies and their boards and senior management.

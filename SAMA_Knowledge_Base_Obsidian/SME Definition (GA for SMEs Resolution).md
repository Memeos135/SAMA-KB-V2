# SME Definition (GA for SMEs Resolution)

SAMA circular adopting the SME definition approved by the board of the General Authority for Small and Medium Enterprises (Monsha'at), classifying enterprises as micro, small, medium or large by annual revenue bands with full-time employee headcount used only where revenue data is unavailable (e.g. a newly established entity). It is a definitional/classification instrument rather than a conduct rule, but drives SME-related reporting, lending classification and related supervisory treatment. Addressed to banks, finance companies operating in the Kingdom, and the credit information companies (SIMAH and Bayan).

**Regimes:** banking prudential, BNPL/finance, data/credit info

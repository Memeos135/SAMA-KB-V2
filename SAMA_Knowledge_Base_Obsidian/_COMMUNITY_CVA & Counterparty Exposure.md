# CVA & Counterparty Exposure

## Why this community

Counterparty credit risk exposure measurement under internal models and the resulting CVA capital charge, including the choice between basic and standardised CVA approaches and eligibility of hedges.

## How members connect

- Exposure chain: IMM produces Effective EPE, scaled by the alpha factor, subject to stress testing, wrong-way risk and cross-product netting conditions before feeding capital calculations.
- CVA framework branches into BA-CVA (reduced and full versions, with stand-alone CVA capital as the building block) and SA-CVA (delta/vega sensitivities, vega risk buckets, eligible hedges); use of one approach is conditioned on model approval and hedging.
- Market risk RWA is the parent aggregation node that pulls in the CVA framework and its approaches.
- Wrong-way risk (general and specific) operates as a qualifying condition on IMM exposure estimates rather than a standalone charge.

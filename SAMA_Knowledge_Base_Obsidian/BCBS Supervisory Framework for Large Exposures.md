# BCBS Supervisory Framework for Large Exposures

The core body of SAMA's Large Exposure (LEX) Rules for Banks, localising the BCBS supervisory framework for measuring and controlling large exposures. It sets maximum exposure limits (including 15% of eligible capital to a single non-bank counterparty and 25% generally), exemptions and carve-outs for intra-day interbank and consolidated intra-group exposures, board-level governance, policy, stress-testing and monitoring duties, and quarterly regulatory reporting before and after credit risk mitigation. It binds all banks licensed by SAMA and their boards.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

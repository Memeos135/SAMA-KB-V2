# Minimum Capital Requirements for CCR and CVA

This node covers the minimum capital requirements for counterparty credit risk (CCR) and credit valuation adjustment (CVA) risk, including the definition of CVA risk, the scope of covered transactions (derivatives and fair-valued SFTs), the CVA portfolio concept, and the choice between the basic (BA-CVA) and standardised (SA-CVA) approaches, with SA-CVA requiring SAMA approval. It also addresses eligible and ineligible internal and external CVA hedges and their interaction with market risk capital. It binds banks calculating regulatory capital under SAMA's Basel-based framework.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

# AML/CTF Compliance Function

This node sets governance requirements for the AML/CTF compliance function: an independent, specialised AML/CTF unit with adequate human and technological resources, staffed by Saudi nationals, headed by a senior-management-level officer who reports administratively and technically to the board and has timely access to customer and transaction data. It lists the officer's mandated duties, including designing the AML/CTF programme, reviewing policies, monitoring transactions, handling internal suspicion reports and reporting to the SAFIU, and dealing with SAMA submissions. It binds SAMA-supervised financial institutions, their boards and senior management.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, governance/risk

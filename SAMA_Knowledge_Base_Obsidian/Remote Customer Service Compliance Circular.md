# Remote Customer Service Compliance Circular

This is a SAMA circular reaffirming compliance with the requirements for remote working in customer service roles, supplementing an earlier SAMA circular that required remote customer-service staff to be registered in the financial institution's official records and with GOSI, and their contracts documented on the portal designated by the competent authority. It transmits and requires adherence to the Ministry of Human Resources and Social Development decision restricting remote customer-service work to Saudi nationals. It binds all financial institutions supervised by SAMA and applies from its date of issue.

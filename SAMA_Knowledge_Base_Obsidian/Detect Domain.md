# Detect Domain

The Detect domain of SAMA's Counter-Fraud Framework, addressing risk-based identification of actual or attempted fraud where preventive controls fail, using indicators in customer behaviour and transactional and non-transactional data. It requires defined, approved, implemented and maintained fraud detection standards covering internal and external fraud, periodically reviewed and effectiveness-tested, with prescribed minimum content on data sources, detection controls (including Wholesale Payment Endpoint Security monitoring), systems, roles and responsibilities and rationale, with enhanced measures where inherent fraud risk is higher. It binds Member Organisations.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, payments

# Loans to Deposits Ratio Guidelines

SAMA's Loans to Deposits Ratio Guidelines (version 2.0, March 2023), issued under the Central Bank Law and Banking Control Law and effective 1 June 2023, superseding the 1427H circular and the 1439H LDR calculation guidance. They define the ratio (net loans over weighted deposits), the numerator deductions, the deposit and long-term debt components of the denominator, exclusion of interbank and SAMA transactions, the maturity-based weighting table, and treatment of callable and perpetual sukuk/bonds. They bind banks, which must report LDR to SAMA monthly on a consolidated basis covering local and foreign currency, resident and non-resident transactions.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

# Reverse Stress Testing

Provision within SAMA's Rules on Stress Testing for banks that defines reverse stress testing as starting from a business-failure outcome and working backwards to identify the scenarios that would produce it. It makes reverse stress testing of the business plan to the point of non-viability a requirement for banks, including assessing the likelihood of such adverse circumstances and taking remedial measures where the resulting failure risk is inconsistent with risk appetite or tolerance. Related material covers SAMA's supervisory review of stress testing programs, including via ICAAP discussions and on-site examinations.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential

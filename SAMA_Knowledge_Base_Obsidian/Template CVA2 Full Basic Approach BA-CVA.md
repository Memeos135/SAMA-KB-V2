# Template CVA2: Full Basic Approach BA-CVA

Template CVA2 is the Pillar 3 template for reporting CVA risk RWA computed under the full basic approach (BA-CVA), i.e. where eligible CVA hedges are recognised. It forms part of the CVA disclosure set that applies once the counterparty credit risk/CVA materiality threshold is satisfied, with BA-CVA being the default method absent SAMA approval to use SA-CVA. It binds SAMA-supervised banks using the full BA-CVA for part or all of their CVA risk.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

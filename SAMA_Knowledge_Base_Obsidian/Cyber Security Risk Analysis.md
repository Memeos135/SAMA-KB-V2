# Cyber Security Risk Analysis

A component of the SAMA Cyber Security Framework's risk management cycle, covering how a Member Organization determines the likelihood and impact of identified cyber security risks so they can be prioritised against risk appetite. It sits between risk identification and risk response, and its output feeds control selection and maturity assessment against the Framework's six-level maturity model. It binds SAMA-supervised Member Organizations (banks, insurers, financing and other regulated entities) subject to the Framework.

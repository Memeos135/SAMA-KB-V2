# Template CC1 - Composition of Regulatory Capital

Template CC1 is the fixed-format Pillar 3 disclosure of the composition of regulatory capital, itemising CET1, Additional Tier 1 and Tier 2 components and each regulatory adjustment and deduction, with row-level cross-references to the SAMA capital standard (SACAP), including treatment of investments in the capital instruments and other TLAC liabilities of financial entities and the 5%/10% thresholds. The surrounding material also covers capital distribution constraint disclosure (buffers, Pillar 2, leverage). It binds banks subject to SAMA's regulatory capital and disclosure framework, with certain rows applicable only to G-SIBs.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

### [[Template PV1 - Prudent Valuation Adjustments]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** Treat the prudent valuation adjustments disclosed in PV1 as an input to the regulatory capital composition shown in CC1, not as a standalone valuation note. Prudent valuation under the Basel prudent valuation guidance (as applied through the relevant SAMA circular) feeds the adjustments and deductions that determine CET1, which CC1 discloses line by line. The consequence is that any change in PVA methodology or amount has a direct capital-ratio effect, and the two disclosures must be internally consistent when assessing CET1 adequacy and buffer headroom.

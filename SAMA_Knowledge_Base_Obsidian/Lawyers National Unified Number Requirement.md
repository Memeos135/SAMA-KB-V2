# Lawyers National Unified Number Requirement

A SAMA urgent circular requiring supervised financial institutions to condition dealings with lawyers on the lawyer holding the national unified entity number beginning with (7) issued through the legal entity register, following a request from the Ministry of Justice / Saudi Bar Association and Council of Ministers decision. It builds on SAMA's earlier instructions replacing commercial registration and licence numbers with the unified national number for non-government entities. It binds all SAMA-supervised financial institutions and credit information companies.

**Regimes:** data/credit info, AML/CTF, governance/risk

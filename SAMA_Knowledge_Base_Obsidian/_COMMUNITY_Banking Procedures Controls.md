# Banking Procedures Controls

## Why this community

Operational controls that supervised institutions must apply when executing and processing banking-procedure transactions, addressing authorisation, verification and internal handling discipline.

## How members connect

- Standalone circular imposing process-level controls; sits below the governing banking regulation as an implementing/supervisory instruction.
- Interacts with governance and internal-control expectations, and indirectly with AML/CTF transaction scrutiny where handling controls support detection of irregular activity.
- Should be read alongside customer documentation and authentication requirements, since the controls determine who may act and on what evidence.

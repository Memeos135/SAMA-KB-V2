# Internal Audit Policy Principle

Sub-node covering Principle 5 of SAMA's Internal Audit Principles: the requirement for a documented internal audit policy (audit charter) for the unit, sitting alongside principles on the unit's key attributes, organisation, working procedures and scope of work. It defines what must be set out regarding the unit's mandate, authority, independence and operating framework within the bank's governance structure. Applies to locally licensed banks and their boards/audit committees.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential

# SA-CCR Netting Sets

## Why this community

Scope and aggregation rules under SA-CCR: how exposures are grouped into netting sets and hedging sets and how replacement cost is determined under margin agreements.

## How members connect

- SA-CCR is the governing methodology; netting set is its defining scope unit and determines the perimeter for offsetting.
- Hedging sets sit inside netting sets as a finer risk-category grouping for add-on aggregation.
- Margin agreement replacement cost examples are illustrative guidance applying the netting set definition to margined versus unmargined trades.

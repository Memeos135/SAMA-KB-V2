# SAMA Circular No. 341000015689 (Basel III Implementation)

This node identifies SAMA's issuance of the revised Basel III credit risk framework (Minimum Capital Requirements for Credit Risk), grounded in SAMA's Charter and the Banking Control Law, and its supersession of earlier circulars on ECAI rating mapping, IRB approaches, mortgage RWAs and MSME RWAs. It also anchors cross-references to the earlier Basel III guidance circular (No. 341000015689) for capital deduction and 250% risk-weight treatment. It binds SAMA-licensed banks calculating credit risk RWAs.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

# General Market Risk Qualitative

## Why this community

Qualitative Pillar 3 disclosure of a bank's overall market-risk management: strategy, governance, structure of the trading function, and the scope and nature of measurement and reporting systems.

## How members connect

- Single-member cluster acting as the narrative anchor for the market-risk disclosure family; the quantitative market-risk templates are read against it.
- Relies on the trading book / banking book boundary definitions and the approach-selection rules in the capital adequacy framework.
- Links upward to general governance and risk-management disclosure obligations, and sideways to the model-specific qualitative table.

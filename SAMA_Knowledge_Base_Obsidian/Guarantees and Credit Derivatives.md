# Guarantees and Credit Derivatives

Concept node on guarantees and credit derivatives as credit risk mitigants and as exposures in their own right. It covers how exposures secured by financial guarantees versus credit derivatives are allocated and disclosed (Template CR3 illustrations), and the treatment of written credit derivatives where the bank sells credit protection, including when purchased protection may offset sold protection (identical reference names, equally or more conservative terms, strike price condition). Binds SAMA-supervised banks in exposure measurement and disclosure.

**Regimes:** banking prudential

- **What this link tells you:** When a bank recognises a guarantee or credit derivative as credit risk mitigation, the decision cannot stop at eligibility — it feeds directly into how the IRB risk components are set, since the protection is reflected through the PD of the protection provider or an adjusted LGD rather than as a free-standing deduction. Both provisions sit in the same SAMA capital framework and share its defined terms, so the mitigation rules and the risk-component rules must be applied as one obligation chain. Practically, a firm approving recognition of protection must document which component it adjusts and on what basis.

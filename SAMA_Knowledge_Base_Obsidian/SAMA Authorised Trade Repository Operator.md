# SAMA Authorised Trade Repository Operator

Covers the manner of reporting to the SAMA authorised Trade Repository Operator: reporting banks must report directly, may not use agents or outsource to third-party service providers, and must sign a reporting service agreement containing counterparty consent for trade data reporting. It also requires electronic submission via system linkages and completed user testing, use of the TR's prescribed templates and fields, and reporting on execution plus subsequent life-cycle events (new trade, modification, novation/termination action types). Binds all banks in KSA entering reportable OTC derivative transactions.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential, other

# Finance & Refinance Rules

## Why this community

Licensing and conduct of finance companies, real estate finance and refinance entities, including BNPL, digital brokerage, outsourcing and advertising. Covers who may carry on financing activity, on what prudential and consumer terms, and how retail credit must be granted responsibly.

## How members connect

- Two primary laws (Finance Companies Control Law M/51 and Real Estate Finance Law M/50) sit at the top of the hierarchy; the rules, instructions and controls below are subordinate instruments issued under them.
- Responsible Retail Financing Principles act as a cross-cutting conduct layer that the BNPL Rules expressly pull in, creating a chained obligation for affordability and disclosure on top of licensing requirements.
- Outsourcing Rules for finance companies are referenced by both the BNPL Rules and the Real Estate Refinancing Rules, so delegated functions stay under the licensee's residual accountability.
- Note the defined-term interaction: BNPL uses 'Consumer' while AML/CTF obligations attach to 'Customer' — scope and CDD triggers must be reconciled rather than assumed identical.

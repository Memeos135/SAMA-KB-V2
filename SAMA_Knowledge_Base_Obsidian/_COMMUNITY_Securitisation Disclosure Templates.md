# Securitisation Disclosure Templates

## Why this community

Pillar 3 disclosure of banking-book securitisation exposures, combining a narrative/qualitative account of securitisation activity with the quantitative exposure template.

## How members connect

- Explicit cross-reference: the quantitative banking-book template is read against the qualitative table, which supplies the policy, role (originator/sponsor/investor) and risk-transfer narrative that makes the numbers interpretable.
- Shared defined terms — securitisation, resecuritisation, originator, sponsor, traditional vs synthetic — are set in the qualitative table and carried into the template's line items.
- Both sit at the disclosure layer beneath the securitisation framework rules; scope (banking book only) is the key limiting condition, with trading-book exposures disclosed elsewhere.

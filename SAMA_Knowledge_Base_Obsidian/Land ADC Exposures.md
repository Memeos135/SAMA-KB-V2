# Land ADC Exposures

Identifies "land acquisition, development and construction" (ADC) exposures as a distinct third category within the real estate asset class, separate from regulatory real estate and other real estate exposures, and carved out of those definitions. The full definition and applicable risk weights sit in the real estate section of the standardised approach; relevant to banks financing land purchase, development or construction.

**Regimes:** banking prudential

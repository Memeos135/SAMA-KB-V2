# IT Governance and Leadership Domain

The first domain of the SAMA IT Governance Framework, covering board- and senior-management-level accountability for IT: governance structures, IT strategy, enterprise architecture, IT policies and procedures, and defined roles and responsibilities. It binds Member Organizations and typically routes oversight through an IT Steering Committee (ITSC), with performance and compliance trends reported to senior management. Useful when the question is who owns IT decisions and how IT direction must be approved and monitored.

**Regimes:** governance/risk

# Compliance Unit Independence

Node on the independence of the compliance function and compliance unit under SAMA's Compliance Principles. The compliance function is defined as an independent function at the first management level below senior management that identifies, assesses, advises on, monitors and reports non-compliance risk; the compliance unit is a dedicated business unit (group, sector or department in local banks; department, division or section reporting to the most senior officer in foreign bank branches) whose head and staff perform compliance duties only. Relevant to conflict-of-interest, dual-hatting and outsourcing questions, including the document's principle on outsourcing compliance unit tasks.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential, AML/CTF

# Risk Management

This is the risk management chapter of SAMA's Rules Governing Real Estate Refinance Companies, read alongside the adjacent internal-organization and outsourcing provisions. It requires a board-approved and annually updated business strategy and risk management policy covering all risk types (including outsourced processes), plus a comprehensive framework to identify, assess, manage, monitor and report risks with early identification, assessment of risk correlations and escalation to the board, audit committee, risk and credit committee and senior management. Related provisions require separation of executive and supervisory functions, adequate and tested IT systems safeguarding data availability, integrity and confidentiality, business continuity planning, and residual company liability for outsourcing providers' non-compliance. It binds licensed real estate refinance companies.

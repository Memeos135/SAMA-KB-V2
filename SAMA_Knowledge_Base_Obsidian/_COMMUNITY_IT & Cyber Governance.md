# IT & Cyber Governance

## Why this community

Governance and control expectations for technology, cyber security and operational resilience in supervised entities — board/committee accountability, maturity-based assessment, IT and cyber risk management, third-party risk and business continuity.

## How members connect

- Two parallel SAMA frameworks (IT Governance and Cyber Security) share the same architecture: domains plus a maturity model against which entities are assessed and report to SAMA.
- Accountability chain is defined by role and forum — Board/IT Steering Committee, CIO, Cyber Security Committee, CISO — with internal IT audit as independent assurance.
- Both frameworks cross-refer to the Business Continuity Management Framework and its minimum requirements, linking control failure to resilience obligations.
- External standards (NIST, PCI-DSS, SWIFT CSCF) are cited as baselines incorporated into SAMA's control expectations, and supervision is channelled through the General Department of Cyber Risk Control.

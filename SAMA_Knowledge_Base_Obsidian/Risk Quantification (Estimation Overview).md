# Risk Quantification (Estimation Overview)

Overview node for the risk quantification section of the IRB framework: the general standards for banks' own estimates of PD, LGD and EAD, including the requirement to use all relevant material data (internal, external or pooled), to ground estimates in historical experience and empirical evidence rather than judgement alone, and to demonstrate representativeness of long-run experience. Binds SAMA-licensed banks using IRB approaches. The context also carries market risk definitional material (risk factor, risk class, sensitivity, delta risk), indicating mixed extraction.

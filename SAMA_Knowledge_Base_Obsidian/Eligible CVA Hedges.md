# Eligible CVA Hedges

Defines which CVA hedging instruments qualify as eligible hedges for CVA capital purposes and how eligible versus ineligible, and external versus internal, hedges are allocated between the CVA portfolio and the trading book. Eligible external hedges are removed from market risk capital and recognised in the CVA calculation; ineligible hedges are capitalised as trading book instruments, and internal hedges are recognised only where the trading desk offsets its position externally in specified cases. Applies to banks calculating CVA capital under BA-CVA or SA-CVA; hedge recognition is unavailable to banks using the below-threshold alternative treatment.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

# Capital Adequacy Stress Tests

## Why this community

Supervisory expectation that institutions run stress testing to assess capital adequacy under adverse conditions, as a condition of using advanced/internal capital approaches.

## How members connect

- Standalone obligation node: the stress-testing duty is expressed as a self-contained supervisory requirement.
- Legally downstream of capital adequacy rules — stress test outcomes feed the assessment of whether held capital remains sufficient.
- Consequential link to governance: results are ordinarily an input to board and senior management oversight and to supervisory dialogue.

# Real Estate Finance Law

The Real Estate Finance Law is the primary statute governing residential real estate finance in the Kingdom: it defines core terms (Real Estate Finance Contract, Borrower, Consumer, Subsidy-Eligible Individual, primary and secondary markets) and assigns SAMA the mandate to regulate and license the sector, including banks conducting real estate finance and real estate finance companies. It also provides for licensing a real estate refinance joint stock company and cooperative insurers covering real estate finance risk, requires Sharia-compliant conduct via the Sharia committees referred to in the Finance Companies Control Law, and requires SAMA to set finance standards, review contract templates and set finance-cost disclosure principles. It binds real estate finance entities (banks and licensed finance companies) and allocates publication duties to the Ministry of Housing, Ministry of Commerce and Industry and Ministry of Justice.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, consumer protection, banking prudential

# Shariah Governance Framework for Local Banks

SAMA's Shariah Governance Framework setting minimum governance requirements for local banks conducting Shariah-compliant banking. It allocates roles across the board, executive management, the Shariah committee, compliance, risk management and internal audit, and covers committee formation, internal control, and Shariah-compliant product development. It complements rather than overrides other SAMA regulations and binds licensed local banks offering Islamic banking.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential

- **What this link tells you:** Treat Shariah governance obligations for local banks as supervisory requirements enforceable through SAMA's statutory powers, not internal policy choices. The Framework is issued under the Banking Control Law, which gives SAMA authority over banks' governance, internal controls and prudential conduct; the Framework specifies board-level Shariah committee, audit and compliance structures within that mandate. This means non-compliance is a supervisory matter attracting the Law's enforcement and sanction provisions, and Shariah governance must be integrated with the bank's general governance and control framework.

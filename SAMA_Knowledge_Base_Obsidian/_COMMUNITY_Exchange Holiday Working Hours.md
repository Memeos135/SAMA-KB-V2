# Exchange Holiday Working Hours

## Why this community

Seasonal operational-readiness and business-hours expectations imposed by SAMA on currency exchange centres (and comparable outlets) around Ramadan, Eid and the Hajj season, where service continuity and cash availability for pilgrims and the public are supervisory concerns.

## How members connect

- Both are SAMA supervisory communications addressed to the same licensee population (exchange/money-changing sector) and operate as periodic instructions rather than standing regulation.
- Shared obligation logic: adjust and publish working hours, and maintain adequate staffing/liquidity so branches remain serviceable during peak seasonal demand.
- The Ramadan/Eid hours instruction dates the readiness expectation to a specific Hijri year, so the general readiness circular supplies the standing rationale and the annual notice supplies the applicable timing.

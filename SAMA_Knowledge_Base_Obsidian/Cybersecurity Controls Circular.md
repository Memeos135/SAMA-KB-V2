# Cybersecurity Controls Circular

A short SAMA circular, issued through the Payment Systems and Companies Control Department, reaffirming mandatory compliance with SAMA's cybersecurity policies, frameworks and controls as well as national cybersecurity authority requirements amid rising cyber threats to the financial sector. It directs supervised entities to implement the Essential Cybersecurity Controls and the Critical Systems Cybersecurity Controls and to prioritise cybersecurity in their approved budgets. Primarily addressed to payment systems operators and payment service providers under SAMA supervision.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, payments

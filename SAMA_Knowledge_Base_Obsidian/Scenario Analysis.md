# Scenario Analysis

Scenario Analysis measures portfolio value change from simultaneous moves in multiple risk factors, including changes in correlations and volatilities, using either historical (backward-looking) or hypothetical (forward-looking) scenarios, each with acknowledged limitations. Related scenario-selection requirements oblige banks to reflect their own business environment and risk profile, cover all material risk factors and both on- and off-balance sheet exposures, and include at least one viability-threatening (reverse) scenario. Risk coverage must span credit, market, operational, liquidity, reputational and off-balance-sheet vehicle risks.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential

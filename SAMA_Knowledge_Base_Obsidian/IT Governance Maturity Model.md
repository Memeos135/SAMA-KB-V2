# IT Governance Maturity Model

This node is the Information Technology Governance Maturity Model used to measure how well Member Organizations implement SAMA's IT Governance Framework, distinguishing six levels (0 non-existent through 5), where levels 3 to 5 require meeting all criteria of the preceding levels. It is applied through periodic self-assessment questionnaires completed by Member Organizations and reviewed and audited by SAMA to determine compliance and maturity. It supports the framework's objectives of a common approach to IT risk and an appropriate maturity level of IT controls across SAMA-regulated entities.

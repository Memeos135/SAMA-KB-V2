# Operational Requirements for Recognition of Risk Transference

This node covers the operational conditions under which a bank may recognise credit risk transference and credit risk mitigants for purchased receivables and securitised pools under SAMA's minimum capital requirements for credit risk. It sets out how guarantees and collateral covering default risk, dilution risk or both are reflected (substitution of the guarantor's risk weight, tranched or proportional cover, first-loss treatment), and how master netting agreements and VaR-based approaches affect counterparty credit risk for SFTs. It binds SAMA-licensed banks calculating RWAs under the IRB and securitisation approaches.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

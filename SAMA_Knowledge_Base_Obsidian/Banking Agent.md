# Banking Agent

A concept node defining the banking Agent under the Agent Banking Regulation: an entity contracted by a bank to provide permissible banking services on its behalf, either as an Exclusive Agent (one bank) or Multiple Agent (non-exclusive or multiple banks). It sets agent-side conditions — a commercial register permitting agent banking, prior approval from its own supervisory authority, proper IT infrastructure, and satisfying the bank's fit and proper assessment before SAMA non-objection is sought. The bank selects the agent, contracts with it directly, and bears responsibility for all its actions and omissions, including CDD/KYC and AML/CFT compliance.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, AML/CTF, governance/risk, consumer protection

### [[Know Your Customer (KYC)]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** For a bank using Banking Agents, the decision point is that the KYC definition places the identification and verification of Customers and beneficiaries on the Agent, while the agency framework keeps the bank accountable for what its Agent does in its name. Because agent contracts (Exclusive or Multiple) must be renewed on the basis of a comprehensive assessment, KYC performance is a proper subject of that assessment and of the contractual terms themselves. Consequently, renewal or expansion of agent arrangements should not proceed where the Agent's KYC execution is deficient, and reliance on an Agent does not displace the bank's own AML/CTF obligations.

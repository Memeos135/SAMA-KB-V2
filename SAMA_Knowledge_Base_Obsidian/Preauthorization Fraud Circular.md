# Preauthorization Fraud Circular

SAMA circular addressing fraudulent use of the POS preauthorization transaction and the subsequent capture/purchase advice on cards issued under international payment schemes. It requires acquirers hosting POS payments to apply the relevant international scheme rules and conditions, to enable preauthorization only for eligible merchant sectors, to monitor and investigate suspicious and high-risk transactions relative to customer segment and amount, and to align fraud monitoring and detection arrangements with those standards, alongside full adherence to SAMA's counter-fraud manual. It binds banks and payment/fintech companies providing acquiring services, effective from its date.

# Template CCyB1 - Geographical Distribution of Credit Exposures

Pillar 3 template disclosing the geographical distribution of private sector credit exposures used to compute the bank-specific countercyclical capital buffer rate, including the geographical allocation methodology and any departures from an ultimate-risk basis. Disclosed semiannually in flexible format. Mandatory only for banks with exposures to jurisdictions where the countercyclical buffer rate is above zero.

**Regimes:** banking prudential

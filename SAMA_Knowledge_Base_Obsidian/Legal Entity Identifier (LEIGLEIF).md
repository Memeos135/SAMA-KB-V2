# Legal Entity Identifier (LEI/GLEIF)

Part of SAMA's finance/refinance licensing procedures describing the steps after application completion: written notice of completeness, initial approval or reasoned rejection within 60 business days (initial approval is not a licence), referral to the Ministry of Commerce for incorporation, and post-incorporation evidence of commercial registration, full capital payment and operational readiness. It specifically requires the company to prove it has obtained a Legal Entity Identifier (LEI) from the Saudi Operating Unit accredited by GLEIF, followed by an on-site SAMA visit and issuance of the licence upon meeting requirements and fees.

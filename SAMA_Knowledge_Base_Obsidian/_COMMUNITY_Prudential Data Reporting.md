# Prudential Data Reporting

## Why this community

Prudential and financial reporting obligations: what supervised institutions must submit to SAMA, in what form and on what cycle.

## How members connect

- Single-member cluster: a circular fixing periodic financial statement and prudential data submission duties.
- Functions as the reporting limb of the wider governance and risk regime; accuracy and timeliness of submissions are the enforceable elements.

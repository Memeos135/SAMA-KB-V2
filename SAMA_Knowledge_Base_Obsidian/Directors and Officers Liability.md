# Directors and Officers Liability

Concept node explaining Directors and Officers (D&O) liability as a category of professional liability distinct from the bank's own liability, covering personal exposure of executive and non-executive directors and senior officers for alleged negligent or imprudent acts or omissions. It identifies the main sources of exposure (shareholder actions, client service relationships, employment practices, environmental claims) and explains that the bank purchases the policy to indemnify its directors and officers, including where the bank cannot legally or financially indemnify them. Relevant to boards and senior management of banks in the Kingdom considering risk transfer for governance-level liability.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential

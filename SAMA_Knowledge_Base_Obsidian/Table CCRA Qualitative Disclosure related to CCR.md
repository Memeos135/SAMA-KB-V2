# Table CCRA: Qualitative Disclosure related to CCR

Table CCRA is the qualitative disclosure requirement opening the counterparty credit risk (CCR) section of SAMA's Pillar 3 framework, which covers all banking book and trading book exposures attracting a CCR charge, including exposures to central counterparties. It frames the accompanying quantitative templates CCR1 and CCR3–CCR8. It applies to SAMA-supervised banks making Pillar 3 disclosures.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

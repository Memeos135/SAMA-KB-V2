# Guide to Calculating Early Repayment Amount

A SAMA explanatory guide (with a disclaimer that it is non-binding guidance) explaining the early repayment amount and the unified method finance providers must use to calculate it for instalment-based finance such as personal finance, vehicle lease finance and real estate finance. It restates the beneficiary's right to early settlement, the permitted compensation to the financier (reinvestment cost capped at the term cost of the following three months on a diminishing-balance basis, plus non-recoverable third-party payments), the diminishing-balance allocation and amortization table requirements, and gives worked examples. It reflects Articles 82 and 84 of the Implementing Regulation of the Finance Companies Control Law, Articles 9 and 11 of the Finance Lease Law Implementing Regulation, and Article 11 of the Consumer Finance Regulations update, and is directed at banks and finance companies dealing with finance beneficiaries.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, BNPL/finance

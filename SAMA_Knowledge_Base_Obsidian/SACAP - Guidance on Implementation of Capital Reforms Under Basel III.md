# SACAP - Guidance on Implementation of Capital Reforms Under Basel III

SACAP is SAMA's guidance document implementing Basel III capital reforms in Saudi Arabia, and it is the source referenced for the composition of regulatory capital — CET1, Additional Tier 1 and Tier 2 — including eligibility criteria, regulatory adjustments and deductions. It underpins the line-by-line definitions used in Pillar 3 capital composition templates (goodwill and intangibles, deferred tax assets, cash flow hedge reserve, provision shortfall, own-share and reciprocal holdings, threshold deductions for investments in financial entities, and Tier 2/TLAC holdings). It binds banks licensed and supervised by SAMA when calculating and reporting regulatory capital.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

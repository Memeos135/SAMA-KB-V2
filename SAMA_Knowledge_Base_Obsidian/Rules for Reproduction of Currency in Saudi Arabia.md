# Rules for Reproduction of Currency in Saudi Arabia

A SAMA circular transmitting updated controls on the reproduction, photographing, printing or design of Saudi and foreign currency circulating in the Kingdom, issued under the penal law on counterfeiting and imitation of currency. It sets a general prohibition on unlicensed reproduction, a licensing route through SAMA, and specific conditions for print and electronic/digital reproductions (size limits relative to the original note, no distortion of the King's image, no risk of the public mistaking the image for genuine currency, 'specimen' marking, and destruction of plates, files and storage media after use). It binds any natural or legal person seeking to use currency images for commercial, media or other purposes, and supersedes earlier circulars on the topic.

**Regimes:** other

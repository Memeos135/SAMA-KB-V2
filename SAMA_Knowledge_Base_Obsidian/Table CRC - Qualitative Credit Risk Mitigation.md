# Table CRC - Qualitative Credit Risk Mitigation

Table CRC is the qualitative disclosure on credit risk mitigation techniques in section 19.2.2 of SAMA's Pillar 3 framework, paired with the quantitative Template CR3. It supports understanding of how banks recognise CRM (including the simple versus comprehensive approach with supervisory haircuts under the standardised approach) when computing credit risk RWA, excluding securitisation and counterparty credit risk positions covered elsewhere.

**Regimes:** banking prudential

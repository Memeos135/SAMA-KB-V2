# BNPL & Microfinance Licensing

## Why this community

Authorisation pathway for non-bank consumer credit providers — BNPL companies and consumer microfinance — covering application content, corporate and shareholder suitability, and the substantive rulebook the applicant commits to.

## How members connect

- Both licence routes share the fit-and-proper assessment instrument, applied to founders, controlling shareholders, board and senior management.
- Hierarchy: the BNPL rulebook sets the substantive licensing conditions and ongoing obligations; the application guidelines and application pack are the procedural layer implementing them.
- The microfinance and BNPL guidance are parallel finance-company licence tracks under the same finance-companies framework, so scope of activity (deferred payment vs. microfinance lending) determines which applies.
- Downstream consequence: a licence obtained here brings the entity within consumer protection, AML/CTF and outsourcing obligations applicable to finance companies.

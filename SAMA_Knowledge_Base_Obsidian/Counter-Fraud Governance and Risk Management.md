# Counter-Fraud Governance and Risk Management

Section 3.1 of the Counter-Fraud Fundamental Requirements, setting the governance and risk-management controls: a risk-based Counter-Fraud Programme monitored via KPIs, ultimate Board responsibility for the governance structure and tone from the top, a Counter-Fraud Governance Committee meeting at least quarterly, an approved Counter-Fraud Policy, KRIs against fraud risk appetite, intelligence monitoring, and an enterprise-wide fraud risk assessment refreshed on change. It also requires counter-fraud input before launching new products or services, vetting and monitoring of third parties performing counter-fraud functions, and periodic independent audits, and encourages operational collaboration with cyber security and financial crime teams. Binds Member Organizations subject to the framework.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, AML/CTF, payments, BNPL/finance

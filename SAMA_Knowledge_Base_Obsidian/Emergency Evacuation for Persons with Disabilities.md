# Emergency Evacuation for Persons with Disabilities

A SAMA circular to all SAMA-supervised financial institutions transmitting the national guide on emergency evacuation of persons with disabilities, issued as a follow-on to SAMA's earlier instructions on services for persons with disabilities in financial institutions and to a letter from the Authority for the Care of Persons with Disabilities. The attached guide sets out definitions (emergency, evacuation routes, refuge/safe areas, emergency exits, first aider), disability types, building hazards, evacuation planning steps and self-preparedness, and safety/communication systems such as public address, assistive listening, visual alarms, tactile warning surfaces and access-card/security systems. It binds supervised financial institutions to strengthen premises security and safety arrangements so persons with disabilities can be evacuated safely.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, governance/risk

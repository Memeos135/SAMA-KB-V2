# Aggregate Capital Requirement for Modellable Risk Factors (IMCC)

A concept node relating to the aggregate internally modelled capital charge for modellable risk factors (IMCC) and the surrounding conditions for internal model use, including the risk factor eligibility test, the qualitative modellability principles, and SAMA's power to deem data unsuitable so that a factor is capitalised as a non-modellable risk factor. Context also covers the general standardised approach provisions, under which all banks — D-SIBs and non-D-SIBs — must calculate market risk capital, and internal CVA risk transfer recognition. It binds banks calculating market risk regulatory capital.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

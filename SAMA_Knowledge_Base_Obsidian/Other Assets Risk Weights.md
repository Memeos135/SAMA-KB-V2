# Other Assets Risk Weights

Relates to residual risk weight treatments in SAMA's credit risk capital rules, including "other real estate" exposures (those outside the regulatory real estate and land ADC definitions) and commercial real estate materially dependent on property cash flows, plus recognition of credit risk mitigants and guarantees covering default and dilution risk on purchased receivables. It binds SAMA-supervised banks assigning risk weights to assets not captured by the main exposure classes.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

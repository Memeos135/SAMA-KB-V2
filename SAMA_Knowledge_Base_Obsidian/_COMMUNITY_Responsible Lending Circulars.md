# Responsible Lending Circulars

## Why this community

Affordability and creditworthiness limits on individual lending, including caps on financing amounts and restrictions on security instruments taken from consumers.

## How members connect

- The Responsible Lending Principles for Individuals is the anchor standard; both circulars operate as amendments or clarifications layered onto it.
- Shared defined terms — 'individual', financing cap, debt burden ratio — mean a change in one circular re-scopes the base principles rather than creating a separate regime.
- The BNPL cap circular creates a cross-regime touchpoint: BNPL 'Consumer' treatment must be reconciled with the individual-borrower affordability tests in the base principles.
- The promissory note circular constrains a creditor's remedy, so it interacts with collection and enforcement practice downstream.

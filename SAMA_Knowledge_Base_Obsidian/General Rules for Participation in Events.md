# General Rules for Participation in Events

Second edition of SAMA's General Rules on participation in events (conferences, forums, seminars and similar), updating the earlier rules on holding and sponsoring events and extending scope to presence via "temporary booths" for institutional promotion, which are exempted from the prior non-objection requirement. The rules set minimum requirements on board-approved internal policy, documentation and annual board reporting, selection and professional conduct of representatives (confidentiality, no politically sensitive or reputationally damaging statements, no unauthorised commitments), and event-related risk assessment. They bind all financial institutions licensed and supervised by SAMA.

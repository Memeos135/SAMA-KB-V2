# IT Steering Committee (ITSC)

This node covers the IT Steering Committee under SAMA's IT Governance Framework: the board is ultimately responsible for IT governance and may delegate to senior management or the ITSC, which defines IT governance and sets IT strategy. It specifies mandating by the board, chairmanship by a senior manager, required representation (senior managers from relevant departments, the CIO, internal audit as observer), and a board-approved charter covering objectives, roles, quorum, at least quarterly meetings and minute retention. It binds Member Organizations and allocates accountability between board and ITSC for the IT strategy, IT policy and governance practices.

**Regimes:** governance/risk

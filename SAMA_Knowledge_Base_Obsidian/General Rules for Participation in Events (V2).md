# General Rules for Participation in Events (V2)

Second issue (Safar 1448 / July 2026) of SAMA's General Rules for Participation in Events, updating and replacing the first issue circulated in 1447. It broadens scope to any form of participation in any event for any purpose — hosting in whole or part, sponsoring, or presence through a temporary pavilion, in or outside the Kingdom, directly or via a third party — and retains requirements for a board-approved policy, documentation and annual board reporting, conduct obligations for representatives, and risk assessment/due diligence on content, participants, sponsors and speakers. It binds all financial institutions licensed and supervised by SAMA.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, other

# Key Governance Principles for Financial Institutions

This node covers SAMA's directive extending the Key Governance Principles for Financial Institutions Supervised by SAMA to all finance companies and payment service providers. Firms must perform a gap assessment of current governance arrangements, adopt a board/managers-approved roadmap, send the assessment and plan to SAMA's stated compliance address within the set period, and achieve full compliance within one year of the circular or by the end of the sitting board's term, whichever is earlier, with SAMA able to extend on justified grounds.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, payments, BNPL/finance

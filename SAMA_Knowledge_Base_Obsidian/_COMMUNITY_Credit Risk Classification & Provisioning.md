# Credit Risk Classification & Provisioning

## Why this community

Classification, provisioning and workout of credit exposures - how exposures are staged and defaulted, how expected credit losses are measured, and the mandatory treatment of problem assets, forbearance and restructuring.

## How members connect

- Two anchor instruments dominate: the credit risk classification and provisioning rules (staging, default definition, ECL) and the problem-loan management rules (early warning, watch list, workout, restructuring); each cites the other.
- IFRS 9 is the shared accounting reference that gives content to staging, ECL and NPV testing, while SAMA rules impose supervisory floors and definitions on top of it.
- The default and forbearance definitions are the pivot between regimes - they determine stage migration, NPL designation, cure and re-default outcomes.
- BCBS 403 supplies the international prudential taxonomy for non-performing and forborne exposures that the SAMA problem-loan rules localise.
- Workout mechanics (viability assessment, term sheet, restructuring agreement, KPIs, cure rate, collateral and LTV) form an obligation chain running from early warning detection to monitoring of the executed plan.

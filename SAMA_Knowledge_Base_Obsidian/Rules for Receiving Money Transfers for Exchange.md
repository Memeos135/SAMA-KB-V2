# Rules for Receiving Money Transfers for Exchange

Rules setting the minimum requirements exchange centres and bank-owned remittance centres must follow when receiving customer funds through electronic payment means for remittance or currency exchange purposes; they replace the earlier circular permitting such receipts. Requirements include a dedicated separate bank account, limiting inbound transfers to members with a unified reference number linked to the customer's ID and denominated in SAR, a prohibition on receiving customers' transfers from banks or exchange houses outside the Kingdom for currency exchange, verification and record-keeping on the transferring customer, restrictions on transfers back to customer accounts except for cancellations (refunded to the same payment instrument within three days), prior agreement and documentation of fees and exchange rates, customer awareness measures, and application of CDD under the AML and terrorism financing laws and their implementing regulations. It binds exchange centres and banks' remittance centres supervised by SAMA.

**Regimes:** payments, AML/CTF, consumer protection

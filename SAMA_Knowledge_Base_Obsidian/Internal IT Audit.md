# Internal IT Audit

This node addresses the internal audit function's IT responsibilities under SAMA's IT Governance Framework, together with adjacent roles and the regulatory compliance and service level management requirements it interacts with. It requires internal audit to identify the auditable IT universe, perform IT risk assessment during audit planning and carry out IT audits, while the organisation maintains a process for compliance with IT-related regulatory requirements (including data privacy) and formal internal and third-party SLAs. It binds Member Organizations and, within them, internal audit, IT management and all staff.

# Airport Exchange Travelers ID Exception

SAMA circular granting a narrow identification exception for customers of exchange center branches located in airports who are travelling outside the Kingdom: the national ID need not be presented, and a passport plus boarding pass matched against the customer's name suffices to execute transactions. It supplements the customer acceptance and identity verification policies in SAMA's manual on organising the work procedures of money exchange shops (February 2010) and rests on an approval conveyed by the Minister of Interior. It binds exchange centers of categories (A) and (B) and is effective from its date.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, other

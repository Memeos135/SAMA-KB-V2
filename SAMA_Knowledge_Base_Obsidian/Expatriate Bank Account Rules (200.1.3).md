# Expatriate Bank Account Rules (200.1.3)

Rule 200.1.3 governs bank accounts for expatriates residing in Saudi Arabia, including accounts for holders of temporary 90-day work visas opened on an employer's official request, employer notification duties on final exit, mandatory freezing and closure steps, and limits on delegation and joint accounts (permitted only for specified spouse/first-degree relative combinations with valid Iqama). It also caps standing orders for expatriates at one-year renewable, monthly domestic or cross-border transfers with verified customer data. Certain expatriate account types are expressly classified as high risk and subject to ongoing monitoring; the rules bind banks operating in the Kingdom.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, banking prudential, governance/risk

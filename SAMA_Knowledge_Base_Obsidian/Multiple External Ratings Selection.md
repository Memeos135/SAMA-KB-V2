# Multiple External Ratings Selection

This node addresses the use of multiple external ratings in determining risk weights, including the rules for selecting between conflicting ECAI ratings, and the external-ratings-based approach for securitisation exposures (SEC-ERBA). It sets out the SEC-ERBA risk-weight tables for short-term and long-term ratings, the drivers of long-term risk weights (rating grade or inferred rating, seniority, tranche maturity and, for non-senior tranches, thickness) and the operational requirements for using external assessments and inferred ratings. It binds banks holding rated securitisation exposures and those relying on external ratings for standardised credit risk weighting.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

# Controls for Dealing with Old or Damaged Devices in Local Banks

SAMA circular on controls for handling old or damaged bank equipment and assets being disposed of, such as ATMs, POS terminals, computers, imaging devices and associated documents, tapes and discs. It requires banks to adopt policies and procedures for disposal that preserve the confidentiality of information held on such items and prohibits ordinary discarding that could allow misuse. Addressed to local banks and licensed exchange houses, which retain regulatory responsibility for the information contained in the equipment.

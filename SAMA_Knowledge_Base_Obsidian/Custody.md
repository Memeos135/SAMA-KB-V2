# Custody

Governs custodial arrangements in repo transactions. Every repo market participant must fully understand the custody agreement's terms and its own rights and obligations before entering into a repo with a custodial arrangement, and a licensed bank holding eligible securities in custody for repo participants must maintain arrangements and processes to monitor and segregate those securities so they cannot be used more than once. Read with the definitions of Custodian and Held-in-Custody and the requirement that ownership fully transfers to the buyer notwithstanding custody.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential

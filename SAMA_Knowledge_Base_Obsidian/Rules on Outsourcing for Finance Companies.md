# Rules on Outsourcing for Finance Companies

SAMA's Rules on Outsourcing for Finance Companies, setting regulatory requirements for companies licensed under the Finance Companies Control Law that have entered or intend to enter outsourcing contracts. They require a board-approved and annually updated outsourcing policy, risk review at inception and renewal, due diligence and licence verification on the External Service Provider, SAMA and auditor access rights, record retention, business continuity contingency planning, and transitional steps (policy filing within 180 days, review of legacy contracts within 365 days, notification of legal or regulatory breaches). Non-compliance is treated as a violation of the Finance Companies Control Law and its Implementing Regulations; responsibility for the provider's non-compliance remains with the finance company.

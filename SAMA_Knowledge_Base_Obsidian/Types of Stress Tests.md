# Types of Stress Tests

Defines the categories of stress tests under the Rules — Regular (bottom-up, bank-conducted), Ad-hoc (irregular, bank- or SAMA-initiated), Reverse (viability-threatening scenarios, elaborated in Section 5.4), and Macro (top-down, conducted by SAMA on the Saudi banking system) — and establishes stress testing as a mandatory requirement. The mandate applies to all locally incorporated banks and to branches of foreign banks whose total assets exceed 0.5% of total Saudi banking system assets, each of which must build a program proportionate to its nature, size, complexity and risk profile.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential

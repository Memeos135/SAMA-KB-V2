# Green Team

Defined role within the F.E.E.R. Framework: the Green Team is provided by SAMA's IT Risk of Financial Sector Supervision department and acts as regulator-side overseer of each red teaming exercise. It appoints the Test Manager (and backup), approves the member organization's selection of the Red Teaming Provider, supplies sector-specific threat intelligence where applicable, and participates in scoping, feedback and lessons-learned sessions. It guides and supports the White Team but is not accountable for the member organization's testing decisions.

**Regimes:** governance/risk

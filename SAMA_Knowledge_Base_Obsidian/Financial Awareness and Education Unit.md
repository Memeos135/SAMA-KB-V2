# Financial Awareness and Education Unit

This node sits within SAMA's customer care instructions for banks and concerns the bank-side financial awareness and education function. The instrument's introduction grounds SAMA's authority under the SAMA Law and the Banking Control Law to regulate customer rights and care, and adopts internationally recognised financial consumer protection principles on complaint handling, financial literacy and clear disclosure of customer rights, responsibilities and product risks. It sets a minimum standard binding on all banks licensed and operating in the Kingdom.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, governance/risk

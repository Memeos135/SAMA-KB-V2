# Banking Book

A defined-term node for the banking book as used in SAMA's market risk capital framework, mainly through the internal risk transfer provisions. It sets when a banking book credit, equity or interest rate exposure is treated as hedged for banking book capital purposes, and requires banking book short credit or short equity positions created by internal risk transfers and not capitalised under banking book rules to be capitalised under the market risk rules. It binds SAMA-licensed banks allocating exposures across the banking/trading book boundary.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

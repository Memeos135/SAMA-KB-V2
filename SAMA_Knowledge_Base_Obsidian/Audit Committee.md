# Audit Committee

Concept node for the board-level Audit Committee as required under SAMA governance instruments, principally the Key Principles of Governance for Financial Institutions subject to SAMA supervision (third edition) and related sector governance rules. It covers the committee's composition, independence conditions for members, and its mandate over internal and external audit, financial reporting integrity and internal control oversight. It binds boards of SAMA-supervised financial institutions (banks, finance and real estate refinance companies, exchange businesses, payment and fintech companies, credit information companies), with insurance/reinsurance excluded from the governance principles' definition of financial institution.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential

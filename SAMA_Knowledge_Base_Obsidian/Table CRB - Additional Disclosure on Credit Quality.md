# Table CRB - Additional Disclosure on Credit Quality

Table CRB is the additional qualitative and quantitative disclosure on the credit quality of assets under section 19 of SAMA's Pillar 3 framework, supplementing the quantitative CR1 and CR2 templates. It sits alongside Table CRB-A (prudential treatment of problem assets) in the general credit risk information block and is addressed to banks subject to the credit risk RWA disclosure regime.

**Regimes:** banking prudential

# Quality and Performance Analysis Unit

The Quality and Performance Analysis Unit sits within the bank's Customer Care Department under SAMA's 2023 controls and supports the department's mandate to analyse complaint patterns and root causes, reduce recurrence and measure the effectiveness of remedial policies. The surrounding provisions require board-approved policies on root cause analysis, on protecting customer rights across the full customer journey (marketing and sales, after-sales quality, complaint handling, customer communication, credit advisor services) and on financial awareness, plus periodic capacity analysis and staff qualification requirements (retail banking and credit advisor professional certificates, annual training). Binds banks licensed by SAMA in the Kingdom.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, governance/risk

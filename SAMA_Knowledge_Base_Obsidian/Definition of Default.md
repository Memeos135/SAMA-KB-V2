# Definition of Default

Concept node covering how "default" is identified and reported for regulatory capital purposes, spanning the Pillar 3 disclosure templates on defaulted exposures (CR2 flows in/out of default, write-offs, returns to non-defaulted status) and the IRB backtesting of PD against realised default rates (CR9). It fixes the reference default definition used for both standardised (past due more than 90 days) and IRB exposures, including reporting net of write-offs and gross of provisions. It binds all SAMA-licensed banks for the mandatory templates, with the IRB backtesting elements binding only banks approved for F-IRB/A-IRB.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

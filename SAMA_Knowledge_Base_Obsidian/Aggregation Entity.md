# Aggregation Entity

Defines the "Entity" under SAMA's Instructions for Practicing Aggregation Activity — a firm licensed by SAMA (under the Rules of Licensing Finance Support Activities) that operates an electronic platform aggregating finance offers from Financiers for Consumers. It carries the operative duty set: platform cybersecurity and immediate breach notification to SAMA, restriction of the entity–financier electronic link to aggregation only, consumer complaint and disclosure channels, identity verification of the consumer against reliable and independent sources before passing data to the financier, electronic consumer records, and prohibitions on handling funds for financiers or joining financier marketing campaigns absent SAMA approval. Binds only SAMA-licensed aggregation entities; note the term is "Consumer", not the AML "Customer", so identity verification here is a conduct/accuracy duty distinct from full CDD.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, consumer protection, governance/risk, data/credit info

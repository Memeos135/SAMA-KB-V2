# Minimum Capital Requirements for Credit Risk

This node concerns minimum Pillar 1 capital requirements for credit risk under SAMA's Basel framework (SCRE), together with the related Pillar 3 disclosure templates for RWA, capital requirements and capital distribution constraints. It delineates credit risk from counterparty credit risk, CVA, equity positions, settlement risk, securitisation exposures and threshold deductions, each reported and capitalised separately. It binds banks subject to SAMA's capital adequacy and disclosure rules.

**Regimes:** banking prudential

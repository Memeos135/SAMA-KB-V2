# Advanced IRB Treatment

Concept node for the advanced IRB (A-IRB) treatment, where the bank uses its own estimates of PD, LGD and EAD subject to minimum requirements and SAMA approval. It is the only approach available for retail exposures and eligible retail receivables, and is restricted for corporate purchased receivables to obligors eligible for A-IRB; movement from foundation to advanced forms part of the SAMA-agreed rollout plan. Binds IRB-approved banks and banking groups.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

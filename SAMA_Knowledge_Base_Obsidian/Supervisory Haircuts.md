# Supervisory Haircuts

Concept node on supervisory haircuts used under the comprehensive approach: banks must volatility-adjust both exposure and collateral values to reflect possible future price movements before recognising collateral. Haircut size depends on the prescribed minimum holding period, instrument type, transaction type, residual maturity and the frequency of mark-to-market and remargining, with scaling rules for other holding periods. Applies to SAMA-supervised banks computing credit and counterparty credit risk exposure amounts; related supervisory parameters (supervisory factor, delta, maturity factor) appear in the SA-CCR context.

**Regimes:** banking prudential

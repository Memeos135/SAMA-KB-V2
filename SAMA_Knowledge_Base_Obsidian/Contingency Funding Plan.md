# Contingency Funding Plan

Sets the requirement for finance companies to maintain a Contingency Funding Plan addressing liquidity crisis strategy and cash flow shortfalls under stress, including definition of a liquidity crisis event, early warning indicators, activation authority, roles and escalation, and periodic testing and updating. Adjacent text covers funding diversification, funding concentration limits and the cushion of unencumbered high quality liquid assets. It binds finance companies subject to SAMA's liquidity risk management rules.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, BNPL/finance, banking prudential

# Financial Inclusion

Financial inclusion appears here as a stated regulatory objective of SAMA's Regulation on Branch Network and as a mandatory content element of each bank's Branch Network Policy. Banks must set out their approach to promoting inclusion and extending outreach of financial services, including plans for priority areas as part of CSR and plans to promote digital delivery modes. It is an expectation on all in-scope banks rather than a standalone rulebook.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, consumer protection, governance/risk

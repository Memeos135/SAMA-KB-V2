# Internal Audit Function (Credit)

Governance provisions within SAMA's credit risk classification and provisioning rules for finance companies, covering board and senior management responsibility for approving and annually reviewing ECL methodologies, quarterly reporting on exposure and ECL adequacy, model validation and documentation, disclosure, and ECL-related KPIs. It requires the internal audit function, as third line of defence, to independently evaluate the credit risk assessment, measurement and rating systems annually, with an independent third party permitted where internal audit cannot perform the review. It also mandates a comprehensive policy for managing defaulted assets (recovery strategies, collateral revaluation, write-offs, board reporting) and states the rules take effect 1 July 2021, replacing the earlier Provisioning Guidelines.

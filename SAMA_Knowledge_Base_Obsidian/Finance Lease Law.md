# Finance Lease Law

This node covers the Finance Lease Law and its Implementing Regulation, defining Lessor (a licensed joint stock company, including commercial banks), Lessee, Leased Asset, the Contracts Register and the Contracts Registration Company, and setting when finance leasing counts as a professional (licensable) activity. It imposes obligations on lessors regarding contract terms, ownership-right payment schedules, disclosure to SAMA of risks including those arising from securitisation, insurance indemnity and compliance with supervisory requirements. It binds licensed finance lessors and banks conducting finance lease activity in the Kingdom.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, consumer protection, governance/risk

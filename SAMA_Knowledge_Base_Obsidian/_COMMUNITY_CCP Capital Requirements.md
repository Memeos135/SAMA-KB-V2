# CCP Capital Requirements

## Why this community

Capital treatment of bank exposures to central counterparties (CCPs): calculating a CCP's hypothetical capital requirement and, from it, each clearing member's capital charge for its default fund contributions.

## How members connect

- Direct calculation chain: the clearing member's default fund capital requirement is derived from, and cross-references, the CCP's hypothetical capital requirement.
- Shared defined terms and inputs (qualifying CCP status, default fund contributions, trade exposures) mean both must be applied as a single sequential methodology.
- Both sit within the counterparty credit risk / capital adequacy regime, so misapplication flows straight into reported capital ratios.

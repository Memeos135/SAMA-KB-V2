# SOPE - Minimum Capital Requirements for Operational Risk

Reference node for SOPE, the Basel-derived operational risk capital standard as adopted by SAMA, providing the basis for operational risk RWA and Pillar 1 capital requirements reported in the disclosure templates. It is referenced within the same capital adequacy and Pillar 3 disclosure framework that governs capital composition, deductions and capital distribution constraints. It binds banks subject to SAMA's capital adequacy regime.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

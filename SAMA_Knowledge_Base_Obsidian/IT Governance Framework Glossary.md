# IT Governance Framework Glossary

Structural and interpretive material for SAMA's IT Governance Framework: how the Framework is organised into domains and subdomains, each with a Principle and mandated Control Requirements, the control numbering convention, and the compliance mechanics. It covers the periodic self-assessment questionnaire reviewed and audited by SAMA, the six-level IT governance maturity model (levels 0-5, with lower levels to be met before achieving 3-5), and the option of compensating controls plus a formal SAMA waiver. It applies to Member Organizations supervised by SAMA.

# Restructuring and Forbearance

A section of SAMA's credit risk classification and provisioning rules for finance companies dealing with restructuring of exposures, distinguishing forbearance (concession granted because of the borrower's financial difficulty) from renegotiation, refinancing and rescheduling. It sets identification criteria for forbearance and financial difficulty, mandates stage allocation of forborne exposures (Stage 2B, 3A or 3B), and prescribes cure/probation periods and repayment conditions for re-staging out of Stage 3, with shorter cure periods for retail customers. It binds finance companies licensed under the Finance Companies Control Law.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, governance/risk, banking prudential

# Financial Certification Statement Requirement

A SAMA circular (Arabic, poorly digitised) on the submission of periodic financial statements to SAMA, requiring supervised entities to file annual and interim/quarterly financial data within stated deadlines and in the prescribed spreadsheet format. It mandates that submissions be accompanied by a signed Certification Statement attesting to the accuracy of the reported financial statements, executed by the Chief Accountant, Chief Financial Officer and the Chief Executive Officer or Managing Director.

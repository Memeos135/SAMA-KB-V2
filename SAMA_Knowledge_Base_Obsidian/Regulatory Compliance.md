# Regulatory Compliance

Requires a Member Organization to establish a process to identify, communicate and comply with the cyber security implications of applicable regulations, run periodically and whenever new regulatory requirements take effect. The process must involve representatives from key business areas and result in updates to cyber security policy, standards and procedures. It is the Framework's hook for translating external regulatory change into internal cyber security documentation.

**Regimes:** governance/risk

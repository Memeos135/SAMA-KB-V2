# Circular on Saudization of Money Exchange Jobs

A SAMA circular (06/04/1440H) issued under Article 15(c) of the Rules Governing Money Exchange Business (Ministerial Decision 1350) requiring that all jobs in money exchange businesses be restricted to Saudi nationals, with appropriate training. Exchange houses and exchange companies had to submit a Saudization plan within two weeks and complete full Saudization within one year, with enforcement action against non-compliant firms. It binds money exchange institutions and companies operating in the Kingdom (category A/B).

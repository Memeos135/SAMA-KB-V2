# Complaint Management System Phase One Circular

SAMA circular (Arabic) launching phase one of the supervisory Complaint Management System and mandating that supervised financial institutions handle complaints end-to-end through that system. It fixes handling deadlines (five business days at level one, three business days for customer-escalated level two, two business days to respond to SAMA queries on accepted escalations) and quarterly performance indicators on complaint handling quality, escalation rates, timeliness, response quality, repeat complainants and missing-document requests; it also requires resourcing of customer-care units and updating of internal policies, SLAs and escalation mechanisms. Binds all financial institutions supervised by SAMA, with enforcement action for non-compliance from Q4 of the stated year.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, governance/risk

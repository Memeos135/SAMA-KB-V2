# Counter-Fraud Solutions and Controls for Elevated Risk

Covers enhanced counter-fraud solutions and controls applied where inherent or residual fraud risk is assessed as elevated — for example higher-risk products, channels, customers or newly identified threats. Control intensity is driven by the enterprise-wide Fraud Risk Assessment, the agreed Fraud Risk Appetite and monitored KRIs. Applies to Member Organizations supervised by SAMA.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, payments, AML/CTF

# Minimum Capital Requirements for Operational Risk

Node covering SAMA's Basel-aligned minimum regulatory capital requirement for operational risk, sitting within the wider Pillar 1 capital framework (alongside credit, market and counterparty risk RWA) and the related Pillar 3 disclosure templates, including capital distribution constraint disclosures. It sets out how operational risk RWA and capital charges are derived and reported. It binds banks licensed and supervised by SAMA on a consolidated and, where applicable, solo basis.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

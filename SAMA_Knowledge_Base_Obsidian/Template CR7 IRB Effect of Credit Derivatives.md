# Template CR7: IRB Effect of Credit Derivatives

Node covering Template CR7, the Pillar 3 disclosure showing the effect of credit derivatives used as a credit risk mitigation technique on IRB risk-weighted assets, comparing hypothetical pre-credit-derivative RWA with actual RWA by IRB exposure class. Disclosure is semiannual, fixed format, and must be accompanied by narrative explaining the RWA impact. It is mandatory only for banks that SAMA has permitted to use the foundation or advanced IRB approaches for some or all exposures.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

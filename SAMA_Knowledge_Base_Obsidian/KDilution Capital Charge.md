# KDilution Capital Charge

Concept node for KDilution, the capital requirement component attributable to dilution risk on a receivables pool, used alongside the default-risk capital charge in IRB purchased receivables and as an input where such pools sit within the securitisation framework (e.g. capital caps and KIRB-based calculations). It matters for determining aggregate capital requirements and maximum capital caps on securitisation exposures. Relevant to banks using IRB and SEC-IRBA.

**Regimes:** banking prudential

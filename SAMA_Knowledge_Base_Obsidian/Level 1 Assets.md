# Level 1 Assets

Covers the Level 1 tier of the HQLA stock — assets includable without limit and generally without haircut, held as of the first day of the stress period regardless of residual maturity. It sits alongside the 40% cap on Level 2 and the 15% sub-cap on Level 2B, and connects to the alternative liquidity approach options available where a jurisdiction has insufficient Level 1 assets in domestic currency. Applies to Saudi banks computing the LCR numerator.

**Regimes:** banking prudential

# Supervisory Slotting Criteria Approach

Describes the supervisory slotting criteria approach for specialised lending, under which banks assign exposures to internal grades using their own criteria and then map those grades to the five supervisory rating categories set out in the slotting tables. It requires demonstrable, consistent mapping and controls over overrides. Applies to banks that cannot meet the IRB requirements for PD estimation on specialised lending exposures.

**Regimes:** banking prudential

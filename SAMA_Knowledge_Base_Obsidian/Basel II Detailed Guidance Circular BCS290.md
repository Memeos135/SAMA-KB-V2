# Basel II Detailed Guidance Circular BCS290

This node concerns SAMA's Guidance Note on the scope of application of the Basel Framework, tracing the position back to the Basel II Detailed Guidance Document Circular BCS290 (June 2006). It confirms that local banks must apply SAMA's Basel requirements on both a standalone and consolidated basis, including at holding-company level and every tier of a banking group, and that prudential return requirements follow the same scope. It binds local banks and their parent/holding entities within a banking group.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

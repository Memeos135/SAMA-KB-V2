# Standardized Credit Risk Assessment Approach (SCRA)

Node in SAMA's Minimum Capital Requirements for Credit Risk setting out the Standardized Credit Risk Assessment Approach applied to unrated bank exposures, requiring classification into Grades A, B or C with base risk weights of 40%, 75% and 150% (20%/50%/150% for short-term exposures), and a 30% option where the counterparty bank meets CET1 and Tier 1 leverage thresholds and Grade A criteria. Related material addresses due diligence over external ratings and the allocation of provisions between standardized and IRB portfolios. Binds SAMA-licensed banks computing credit risk RWAs.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

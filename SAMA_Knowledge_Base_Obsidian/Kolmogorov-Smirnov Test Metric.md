# Kolmogorov-Smirnov Test Metric

A concept node covering the Kolmogorov-Smirnov distributional test metric — the largest absolute gap between the empirical cumulative distributions of risk-theoretical and hypothetical P&L — and its use, with the Spearman metric, to place a trading desk in the PLA green, amber or red zone. It carries the consequence that a red-zone desk loses internal models eligibility and must use the standardised approach until it returns to green and clears backtesting exception requirements. It binds banks with IMA-approved trading desks.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

# Treatment for Shari'ah Compliant Banks

Explains the Basel discretion allowing national supervisors to recognise Shari'ah-compliant instruments (e.g. Sukuk) as alternative HQLA for Shari'ah-compliant banks, subject to conditions or haircuts, while preserving an equivalent minimum LCR standard. SAMA states it has not exercised any alternative treatment option, so no separate Shari'ah HQLA carve-out applies in KSA.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

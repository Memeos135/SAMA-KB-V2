# Banks Remuneration Rules January 2023

The Banks Remuneration Rules (January 2023) set SAMA's minimum requirements and supervisory expectations for bank remuneration policies, governance, risk alignment, deferral, adjustment and reporting, superseding the 2010 Rules on Compensation Practices. They target risks arising from reward practices — particularly misconduct risk — rather than setting absolute pay levels, though regulatory caps still apply. They bind all locally incorporated banks licensed and operating in Saudi Arabia, extending on a group basis to majority-owned financial-sector subsidiaries inside and (subject to host-country law) outside the Kingdom.

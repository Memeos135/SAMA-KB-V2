# Code of Conduct and Work Ethics

SAMA circular (Deputy Governor for Supervision, 1440H/August 2019) issuing the Principles of Conduct and Work Ethics for Financial Institutions, with a three-month implementation window. It sets a minimum baseline of behavioural standards — professional conduct, dealings with stakeholders, gifts and hospitality, conflicts of interest, confidentiality, whistleblowing, remuneration and consequences of non-compliance — which the board must translate into an institution-specific code. It binds all 'financial institution personnel' as defined (board and committee members, executives, permanent and contract staff, consultants and third-party-supplied staff) across banks and foreign bank branches, finance and finance-lease registration companies, insurers/reinsurers and free insurance-profession firms, and credit bureaus supervised by SAMA.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, AML/CTF, consumer protection

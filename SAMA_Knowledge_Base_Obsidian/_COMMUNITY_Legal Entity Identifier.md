# Legal Entity Identifier

## Why this community

Mandatory adoption of the Legal Entity Identifier for entity identification in the Saudi financial sector, and the contractual route by which LEIs are issued, renewed and updated.

## How members connect

- The LEI circular is the obligating instrument; the SIMAH issuance agreements are the delivery mechanism giving effect to it.
- SIMAH acts as local operating unit under GLEIF's global framework, so global LEI standards feed into the local issuance terms.
- Issuance via a financial institution engages the Credit Information Law, raising consent, data-use and disclosure limits for entity data.
- Cross-regime relevance: LEI supports customer/beneficiary identification used in AML/CTF KYB and payments-message obligations.

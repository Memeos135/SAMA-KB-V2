# Dilution Risk Recognition

A concept node covering how dilution risk on purchased receivables is recognised in SAMA's Minimum Capital Requirements for Credit Risk. It addresses collateral or partial guarantees acting as first-loss protection (with a link to the securitization treatment), the exposure-weighted LGD calculation under SEC-IRBA where one mitigant covers both default and dilution risk, and the substitution/partial-coverage rules where a seller or third-party guarantee covers default risk, dilution risk or both. It binds banks applying the IRB and securitization frameworks to receivables pools.

**Regimes:** banking prudential

# Standing Payment Order for Financier Controls

Second edition (Nov 2021) of SAMA's Standing Payment Order in Favour of the Financing Entity rules, replacing the earlier real-estate-only version; it now covers all financing products and extends the beneficiary scope to government financiers including National Development Fund banks and funds. It defines the service, the Customer (a natural person holding a financing product) and reliable communication channels, and sets minimum obligations on banks (verifying stable monthly income, customer acknowledgement, notification of order terms, execution and non-execution notices, no-objection/clearance before amendment or cancellation, and respect for protected/non-deductible amounts and seizure instructions) and on the beneficiary financing entity (consent to amend on rescheduling, and issuing clearance letters within set working-day deadlines). It binds banks and finance companies operating in the Kingdom, without prejudice to responsible lending principles and collection rules.

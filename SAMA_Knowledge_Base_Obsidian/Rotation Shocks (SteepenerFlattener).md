# Rotation Shocks (Steepener/Flattener)

Defines the two rotational IRRBB scenarios — steepener (short rates down, long rates up) and flattener (short rates up, long rates down) — as weighted combinations of the absolute short and long rate shocks at each tenor midpoint, with worked examples at a 3.5-year midpoint. Post-shock rates are floored at zero. Binds banks applying SAMA's standardised IRRBB shock scenarios per material currency.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

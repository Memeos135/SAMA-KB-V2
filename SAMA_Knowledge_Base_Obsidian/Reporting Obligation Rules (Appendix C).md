# Reporting Obligation Rules (Appendix C)

Sets out the core reporting duty under SAMA's OTC derivatives trade repository framework: reporting banks must report directly to the SAMA-authorised TR Operator (no agents or outsourcing), enter into a reporting service agreement containing a counterparty-consent clause, complete systems linkage and user testing, and populate all template fields. Reporting is compulsory on first execution of a reportable transaction and on subsequent business events, subject to the T+1 deadline and a limited exemption where a trade is cancelled or fully terminated within T+1 (not applicable to cancellations for central clearing). Reporting banks must also retain records evidencing compliance.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential, other

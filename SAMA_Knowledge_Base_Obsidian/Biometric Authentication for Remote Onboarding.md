# Biometric Authentication for Remote Onboarding

A SAMA circular mandating biometric authentication when a customer relationship is established remotely, supplementing existing requirements to identify and verify an individual customer's identity using documents or data from a reliable and independent source. Institutions must adopt the biometric verification feature of the Nafath application at a high assurance level or above and complete integration with the approved service provider by the stated deadline, notifying SAMA of challenges beforehand. It binds banks (local, foreign branches, digital banks), finance companies and payment companies operating in the Kingdom.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, payments, BNPL/finance, governance/risk

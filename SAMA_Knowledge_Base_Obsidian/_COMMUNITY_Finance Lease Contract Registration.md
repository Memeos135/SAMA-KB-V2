# Finance Lease Contract Registration

## Why this community

Registration formalities for finance lease contracts, which condition enforceability and the lessor's protected position under the finance lease regime.

## How members connect

- Standalone circular imposing a procedural registration obligation on finance lessors.
- Operates as an implementing instruction beneath the Finance Lease Law and its implementing regulation (law → regulation → circular chain).
- Non-registration is a compliance and enforceability risk rather than merely an administrative lapse.

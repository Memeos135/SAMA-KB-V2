# Responsible Lending Principles for Individuals

This node sits under a SAMA circular prohibiting banks and finance companies from taking promissory notes or any commercial paper from individual (retail) customers when providing credit card finance products, issued without prejudice to existing obligations including the Responsible Lending Principles for Individuals. Financiers must update internal policies, procedures and controls, comply from 1 February 2026, submit a remediation plan to SAMA's Consumer Protection Supervision Department within the stated period, and return or cancel previously obtained non-compliant notes within the execution deadline. It binds all banks and finance companies operating in the Kingdom.

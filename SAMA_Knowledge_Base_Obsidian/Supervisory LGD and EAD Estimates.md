# Supervisory LGD and EAD Estimates

This node covers the IRB risk-quantification standards for loss given default (LGD) and exposure at default (EAD), including when banks must fall back on supervisory (foundation) parameter estimates instead of own estimates. It sets out that EAD must be no less than the current drawn amount for on-balance sheet items, must reflect additional drawings up to and after default for off-balance sheet items, and must be a long-run default-weighted average with a margin of conservatism. It binds SAMA-supervised banks using foundation or advanced IRB approaches for credit risk capital.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

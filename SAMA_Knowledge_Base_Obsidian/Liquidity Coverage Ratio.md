# Liquidity Coverage Ratio

The Liquidity Coverage Ratio as adopted by SAMA: the requirement that banks hold a stock of unencumbered high quality liquid assets sufficient to cover net cash outflows over a 30-calendar-day stress scenario, with a phase-in from 60% in 2015 to 100% in 2019. The node covers eligibility conditions for qualifying securities (ratings thresholds, issuer restrictions), caps and haircuts, and jurisdictional alternative liquidity approaches. It applies to Saudi banks subject to SAMA prudential supervision.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

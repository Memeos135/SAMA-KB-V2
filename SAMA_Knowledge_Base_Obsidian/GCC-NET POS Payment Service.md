# GCC-NET POS Payment Service

A SAMA circular activating the GCC-NET point-of-sale payment service with the Sultanate of Oman as of the stated go-live date, continuing earlier circulars on GCC-NET POS service and its tariff. It requires banks to make the technical and operational arrangements to accept and process cross-border POS transactions, apply approved pricing with GCC-NET cardholders and merchants, notify and educate cardholders that Omani purchases route through the GCC network, and accept GCC-NET branded cards on their POS terminals. It binds banks operating in the Kingdom.

**Regimes:** payments

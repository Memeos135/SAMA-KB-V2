# Foundation IRB Treatment

Concept node for the foundation IRB (F-IRB) treatment, under which a bank supplies its own PD estimates while supervisory values are used for other risk components such as LGD and EAD. Relevant to the choice of approach by asset class, to purchased corporate receivables where obligors are not A-IRB eligible, and to the phased rollout from foundation to advanced approaches agreed with SAMA. Binds banks seeking or holding IRB approval.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

# Circular Cancelling Debt Collection Licensing Requirement

A SAMA circular announcing the decision to abolish the licensing requirement for carrying out the activity of collecting debts owed to finance entities, following approved amendments to the Implementing Regulation of the Finance Companies Control Law and to the Licensing Rules for Finance Support Activities. Entities that previously obtained a SAMA licence or initial approval for this activity must comply with the relevant legal requirements to update the activity recorded in their commercial registration. It is directed to banks, finance companies and SAMA-licensed debt collection entities.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, governance/risk

# Template CR3 - CRM Techniques Overview

Template CR3 is the quantitative overview of credit risk mitigation techniques in SAMA's Pillar 3 framework, showing exposures secured by collateral, financial guarantees and credit derivatives against unsecured exposures, and complementing the qualitative Table CRC. It forms part of the section 19 credit risk disclosure set, which reports carrying values on the regulatory scope of consolidation and excludes securitisation and counterparty credit risk positions disclosed in other sections. Binding on banks subject to the Pillar 3 disclosure framework.

**Regimes:** banking prudential

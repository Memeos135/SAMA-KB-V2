# Secured Funding Run-off

Part of SAMA's LCR guidance covering wholesale funding outflow assumptions, defining unsecured wholesale funding (obligations to non-natural persons not collateralised by designated assets, excluding derivatives) and the funding callable or maturing within the 30-day horizon. It sets the run-off factors by counterparty type — 20%/40% for non-financial corporates, sovereigns, central banks, MDBs and PSEs, and 100% for other legal entities including banks, securities firms, insurers and fiduciaries — plus SAMA's discretion to impose above-0% run-off on qualifying retail term deposits. Applies to SAMA-licensed banks calculating and reporting the LCR.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

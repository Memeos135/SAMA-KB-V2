# Record Keeping

Record-keeping node drawing on Chapter Seven of the Targeted Financial Sanctions Rules together with a second SAMA instrument, covering the obligation to retain records evidencing screening, alerts, freezing actions and related TFS decisions. Useful for retention-period and evidential-availability questions, and for comparing TFS record retention with AML/CTF record-keeping duties. Binding on SAMA-supervised financial institutions.

**Regimes:** sanctions/TFS, AML/CTF, governance/risk

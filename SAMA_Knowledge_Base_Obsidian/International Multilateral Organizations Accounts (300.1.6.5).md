# International Multilateral Organizations Accounts (300.1.6.5)

Rule 300.1.6.5 sits within the diplomatic and international-body part of Section 300 and sets the conditions for opening and operating accounts for multilateral international organizations resident in Saudi Arabia, alongside neighbouring rules on embassies, resident and visiting diplomats, foreign airlines, relief committees and campaigns, friendship committees and economic/technical liaison offices. It addresses required host-authority documentation, recognition of the organization's status and authorised signatory arrangements. It binds banks onboarding international organizations and diplomatic-sector customers.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, banking prudential, governance/risk

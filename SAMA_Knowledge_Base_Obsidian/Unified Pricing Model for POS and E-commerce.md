# Unified Pricing Model for POS and E-commerce

A SAMA follow-up circular on the unified pricing model for POS and e-commerce services for mada card transactions carried out at money exchange and remittance centres, and for transfers made through e-wallets. It requires banks and payment service providers to report the extent of their compliance with and application of that pricing model to SAMA by 15 June 2022. It binds banks and licensed payment service providers acting as acquirers or wallet operators.

**Regimes:** payments, consumer protection

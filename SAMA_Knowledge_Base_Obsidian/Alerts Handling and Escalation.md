# Alerts Handling and Escalation

Chapter Five of the Targeted Financial Sanctions Rules, addressing how screening alerts must be handled, reviewed and escalated within the financial institution, including reporting matches to SAMA. It links the screening function to the freezing obligation and to SAMA's designated notification channel for immediate reporting of matches. Binding on all SAMA-supervised financial institutions.

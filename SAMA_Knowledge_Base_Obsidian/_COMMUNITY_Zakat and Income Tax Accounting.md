# Zakat and Income Tax Accounting

## Why this community

Prudential-accounting treatment of Zakat and income tax in supervised entities' financial statements — where these charges are recognised and how that recognition interacts with reported earnings and capital.

## How members connect

- A single SAMA circular carrying the operative instruction; it functions as a directive amending or clarifying accounting presentation for supervised institutions.
- Cross-regime relevance: recognition of Zakat/tax feeds retained earnings and therefore regulatory capital and provisioning disclosures, linking it to prudential reporting obligations elsewhere in the corpus.
- No internal cross-references in the graph — treat as a stand-alone instrument and read against the applicable accounting standards framework SAMA has adopted.

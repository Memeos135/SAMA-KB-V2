# Information Confidentiality and Disclosure

The confidentiality and disclosure strand of SAMA's Principles of Conduct and Work Ethics, covering safeguarding of information, disclosure mechanisms, misuse or leakage of internal (inside) information for market manipulation, and reporting of actual or potential violations. It frames staff-level secrecy and information-handling duties that overlay statutory banking secrecy and customer data obligations. Applies to all personnel of SAMA-supervised financial institutions, including contractors and third-party staff.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, data/credit info, consumer protection

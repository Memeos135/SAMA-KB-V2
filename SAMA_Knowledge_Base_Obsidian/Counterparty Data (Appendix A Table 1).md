# Counterparty Data (Appendix A Table 1)

Deals with counterparty identification and the allocation of the trade repository reporting obligation under SAMA's OTC derivatives reporting framework (Appendix A Table 1 counterparty data, read with the Appendix C allocation rules). It sets who reports in each pairing — financial versus non-financial counterparty, local versus international or qualified counterparty, transactions novated to a CCP, and two local financial counterparties each reporting with buyer/seller identification — and covers life-cycle reporting scenarios such as missing unique trade IDs, modifications and novations. It binds locally established reporting counterparties, principally SAMA-supervised banks.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential, other

# Template CCR7 - RWA Flow Statements under IMM

Covers Pillar 3 template CCR7, the RWA flow statement for counterparty credit risk exposures whose exposure value is determined under the internal models method, with rows isolating IMM model updates and methodology/policy changes. The accompanying interpretive Q&A clarifies that CCR7 splits by EAD methodology rather than risk-weighting methodology, so IRB model changes affecting IMM positions should be captured through additional inserted rows. Applies to banks with SAMA approval to use IMM; the row breakdown is flexible to show all significant RWA drivers.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

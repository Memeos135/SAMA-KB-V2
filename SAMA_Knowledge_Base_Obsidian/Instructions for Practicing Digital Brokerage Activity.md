# Instructions for Practicing Digital Brokerage Activity

Instructions issued under the Finance Companies Control Law approving and governing the practice of digital brokerage activity — intermediation between customers and financiers via an electronic platform — by entities licensed by SAMA under the rules for licensing finance support activities. They set minimum technical and organisational requirements, customer identification and verification of submitted information and documents from reliable independent sources, record-keeping, conflict-of-interest management, disclosure obligations, and mandatory contract terms between the licensed entity and both the customer and the financier. They bind SAMA-licensed finance support entities, and are relevant to banks and finance companies acting as financiers.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, consumer protection, governance/risk, data/credit info

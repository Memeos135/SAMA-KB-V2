# Threat Intelligence

Covers the scenario phase of the SAMA red teaming framework, where the Green Team (sector-wide) and White Team (internal) independently supply threat intelligence, which the Red Teaming Provider combines with its own external and engagement-derived intelligence to build attack scenarios and TTPs. High-level scenarios, the test strategy and the detailed scenarios each require agreement with the Green Team before execution, and the phase is capped at about five weeks. It binds Member Organizations, their appointed teams and the Red Teaming Provider.

**Regimes:** governance/risk

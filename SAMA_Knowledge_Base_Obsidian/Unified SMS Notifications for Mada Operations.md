# Unified SMS Notifications for Mada Operations

A SAMA circular supplementing an earlier instruction on standardising the content elements of SMS notifications sent to customers of financial institutions, extending the unified elements to newly introduced mada transaction types. Financial institutions must send notifications for those operations in line with the attached standard elements, with full application before the end of Q1 2022 and an implementation plan submitted to SAMA by 4 November 2021. It binds banks and payment institutions servicing accounts, memberships and e-wallets.

**Regimes:** payments, consumer protection

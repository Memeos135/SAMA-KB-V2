# Banking Disputes Committee

## Why this community

Handling of requests and information demands issued by the Committee for the Settlement of Banking Disputes, and the supervised entity's duty to respond to that adjudicatory body.

## How members connect

- Single-instrument community: a standalone circular addressed directly to banks on Committee-originated requests.
- Interfaces with confidentiality/banking-secrecy and customer-data rules, since disclosure to the Committee is a lawful-authority exception.
- Read alongside complaint-handling and consumer-protection materials, which the Committee route sits downstream of.

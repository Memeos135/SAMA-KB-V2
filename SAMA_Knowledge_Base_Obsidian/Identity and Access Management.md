# Identity and Access Management

Covers the SAMA Cyber Security Framework's identity and access management requirement: access to information assets must be restricted on need-to-have and need-to-know grounds, with an approved IAM policy whose compliance is monitored and whose control effectiveness is periodically evaluated. It addresses the joiner/mover/leaver lifecycle for internal staff, external staff and third parties, and formal approval of access requests. It binds Member Organizations and links into the Framework's wider cyber security risk management and compliance cycle.

**Regimes:** governance/risk

# Guidelines on Repurchase Agreements

SAMA Guidelines on Repurchase Agreements setting policy requirements for the Saudi repo market, issued under SAMA's Charter and the Banking Control Law and expressly binding on repo market participants. They apply to eligible counterparties (Section 9) and to SAR-denominated repo/reverse repo in eligible securities, without prohibiting existing foreign-currency repos governed by the GMRA, and must be read with other SAMA rules, the Capital Market Law and CMA rules, and the Bankruptcy Law. Includes defined terms (custodian, eligible security, held-in-custody, repo buyer/seller, Saudi Repo Market, SARIE, Tadawul).

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

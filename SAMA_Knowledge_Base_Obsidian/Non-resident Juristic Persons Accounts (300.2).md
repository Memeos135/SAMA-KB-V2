# Non-resident Juristic Persons Accounts (300.2)

Section 300.2 of SAMA's bank account rules, covering account opening for non-resident juristic persons, with sub-categories for GCC commercial non-banking companies, non-GCC companies with or without contracts/projects in Saudi Arabia, non-resident banks, international investment companies and funds, insurers, money changers and non-resident payment card companies. It sets the permitted account types, required documents and verification conditions for each category. It binds banks operating in Saudi Arabia when onboarding foreign legal entities.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, banking prudential, payments

# Principle 3: Board Responsibilities

Principle 3 of SAMA's Key Principles of Governance, setting out the responsibilities of the board of directors of a SAMA-supervised financial institution — the body ultimately accountable for strategy, oversight of executive management, risk and control arrangements. It should be read with the framework's definitions and with Principles 2 (composition and appointment), 4 (executive management) and 5 (board committees). It binds the board collectively and its individual members in institutions within scope (all SAMA-supervised entities except insurance and reinsurance).

**Regimes:** governance/risk

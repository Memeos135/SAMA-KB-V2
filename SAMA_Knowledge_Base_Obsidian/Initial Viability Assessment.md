# Initial Viability Assessment

Concept node describing the stage-two initial viability assessment applied to non-performing loans not yet in legal proceedings, screening borrowers via financial ratios (or cash-flow ratios for MSMEs) and the loan-to-value ratio to classify them as viable, marginally viable or non-viable. It lists indicative, non-prescriptive benchmarks including Debt/EBITDA leverage, loan service coverage against sector averages, EBITDA trend comparison and an LLCR of 1.4 for project and multi-year finance, with thresholds to be set from general market indicators and adjusted for industry. Guidance for banks' Workout Units selecting resolution strategies.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential

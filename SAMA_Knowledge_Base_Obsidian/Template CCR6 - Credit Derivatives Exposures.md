# Template CCR6 - Credit Derivatives Exposures

Node for Pillar 3 template CCR6 on credit derivatives exposures within SAMA's Pillar 3 framework; the surrounding context supplied here covers related credit risk templates (CR1 credit quality of assets and CR3 credit risk mitigation illustrations) showing how exposures secured by collateral, financial guarantees and credit derivatives are allocated. Together these are mandatory disclosure templates for banks, defining gross carrying values, defaulted exposures, ECL provisions and the treatment of multi-secured exposures. Binds all banks subject to the Pillar 3 disclosure regime.

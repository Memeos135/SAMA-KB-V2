# Circular on Promissory Notes for Credit Card Financing

SAMA circular prohibiting finance entities from requesting promissory notes or any commercial paper from individual customers when offering credit card financing products, referencing the existing instructions on finance entities' dealings with promissory notes and the responsible retail finance principles. Requires internal policies, procedures and controls to be updated, effective 1 February 2026, and a remediation plan submitted to SAMA's Consumer Protection Supervision Department covering return or cancellation of previously obtained instruments, with execution capped at a set period. Binds banks and finance companies operating in the Kingdom.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, BNPL/finance

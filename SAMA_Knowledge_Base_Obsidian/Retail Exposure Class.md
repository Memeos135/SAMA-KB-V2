# Retail Exposure Class

Defines the standardised-approach retail exposure class (excluding real estate exposures), distinguishing "regulatory retail" (product, low-value SAR 4.46 million counterparty cap, and 0.2% granularity criteria), "transactors", and "other retail", and sets the corresponding risk weights of 75%, 45% and 100%. Covers exposures to individuals and to MSMEs that meet the regulatory retail criteria; non-qualifying MSMEs fall to the corporate treatment. Binds banks computing credit risk capital.

**Regimes:** banking prudential

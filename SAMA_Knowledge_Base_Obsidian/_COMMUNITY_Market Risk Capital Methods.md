# Market Risk Capital Methods

## Why this community

The standardised (and simplified standardised) measurement of minimum capital for market risk, spanning the sensitivities-based method, default risk capital and the residual risk add-on, plus the legacy interest rate, equity, FX, commodities and options methods.

## How members connect

- The minimum capital requirements for market risk sit at the top; the standardised approach is decomposed by mandatory sub-components — sensitivities-based method, default risk capital and residual risk add-on — which must be summed.
- The sensitivities-based method fans out into defined risk classes (GIRR, CSR, equity, commodity, FX) and defined sensitivity measures (delta via PV01/CS01, vega, curvature), each with prescribed buckets and correlations.
- Default risk capital chains through gross and net jump-to-default positions and the hedge benefit ratio, and cross-cites the credit risk capital rules for risk weights and the securitisation and correlation-trading definitions for scope.
- The simplified standardised approach and the older maturity ladder, delta-plus and scenario methods are alternative or transitional regimes; eligibility to use them is a scope condition, not a free choice.
- Boundary members (leverage ratio, credit conversion factors, CCR/CVA minimums) are cross-regime references pulling in adjacent capital frameworks.

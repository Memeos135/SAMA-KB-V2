# Operational Risk Capital (ORC)

Concept node for Operational Risk Capital under SAMA's adoption of the Basel standardised approach for operational risk, covering how the Business Indicator Component and Internal Loss Multiplier feed the capital charge and how the resulting RWA and minimum capital requirement are disclosed in Pillar 3 templates (including overview of RWA and capital distribution constraint disclosures). It binds banks licensed by SAMA that must compute and publicly disclose Pillar 1 operational risk capital.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

# SAMA Circular 5863 - Economic Cities Authority Ownership Document

A SAMA circular directing all real estate finance companies to accept the ownership document issued by the Economic Cities Authority for property or land inside economic cities as an official, certified title document equivalent to a title deed used outside economic cities. It relies on the Finance Companies Control Law and the Implementing Regulation of the Finance Lease Law, read with the Economic Cities Authority statute provisions on issuing and registering ownership documents. Binds real estate finance companies operating in the Kingdom.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, other

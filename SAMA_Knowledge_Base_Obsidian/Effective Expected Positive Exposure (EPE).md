# Effective Expected Positive Exposure (EPE)

This node is the definitional glossary for counterparty credit risk exposure measures, including current exposure/replacement cost, peak exposure, expected exposure, effective expected exposure, expected positive exposure (EPE) and effective EPE, plus credit valuation adjustment and one-sided CVA, and risk-neutral versus actual distributions. It also notes the CVA materiality threshold expressed by aggregate notional of non-centrally cleared derivatives. These defined terms govern how banks measure and calibrate CCR exposures under SAMA's capital framework.

**Regimes:** banking prudential

# ABCP Programme

Covers asset-backed commercial paper (ABCP) programmes and conduits: the roles of sponsor, seller and transaction, the credit and investment guidelines, seller credit analysis, minimum asset eligibility criteria and collection/servicing controls a programme must have, and how STC criteria are assessed at conduit versus transaction level. Relevant to banks sponsoring, administering or holding exposures to ABCP conduits, including liquidity facilities and credit enhancements, and to use of the internal assessment approach.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

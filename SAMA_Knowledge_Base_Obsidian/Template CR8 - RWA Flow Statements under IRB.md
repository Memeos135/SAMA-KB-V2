# Template CR8 - RWA Flow Statements under IRB

A Pillar 3 credit-risk disclosure template (CR8) within SAMA's Pillar 3 Disclosure Requirements Framework, covering movements in credit risk risk-weighted assets for exposures treated under the internal ratings-based approach. Surrounding material sets out how standardised versus modelled (F-IRB, A-IRB, supervisory slotting) RWA are delineated, including exclusions for counterparty credit risk, CVA and banking-book securitisation, and the phase-in for equity exposures. It binds banks subject to SAMA's Pillar 3 regime that use IRB approaches; open it to confirm row/column definitions and reporting frequency.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

# Multiple External Ratings

Covers the securitisation external-ratings-based approach (SEC-ERBA), under which RWAs for externally rated or inferred-rated securitisation exposures are derived by applying prescribed risk weights to exposure amounts. Risk weights depend on short- or long-term rating grade, tranche seniority, tranche maturity and, for non-senior tranches, tranche thickness, subject to operational criteria for use of external assessments and inferred ratings. Binds banks holding securitisation exposures.

**Regimes:** banking prudential

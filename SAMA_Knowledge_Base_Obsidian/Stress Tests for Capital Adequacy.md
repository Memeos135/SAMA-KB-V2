# Stress Tests for Capital Adequacy

Relates to the capital adequacy disclosure and buffer machinery in SAMA's capital framework, notably the capital distribution constraints template — the CET1 ratio levels at which distributions become restricted once minimum ratios, the capital conservation buffer, G-SIB surcharge, countercyclical buffer, Pillar 2 and leverage buffer requirements are stacked. It also touches Tier 2 composition and the regulatory deductions for own instruments, reciprocal cross-holdings and holdings in financial sector entities/TLAC liabilities. Binds banks in respect of annual public disclosure and capital calculation.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

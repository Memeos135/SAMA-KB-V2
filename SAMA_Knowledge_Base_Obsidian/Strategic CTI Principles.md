# Strategic CTI Principles

The Strategic CTI layer of SAMA's Cyber Threat Intelligence Principles, addressing the specialised practice of identifying the objectives, motivations and intent of threat actors and mapping the financial sector's cyber threat landscape. It expects Member Organizations to identify relevant threat actors, trends and vulnerable assets, and to route this intelligence to executive management (e.g. the CISO) for strategic and resilience decisions. Applies to all SAMA Member Organizations, including where the CTI function is outsourced.

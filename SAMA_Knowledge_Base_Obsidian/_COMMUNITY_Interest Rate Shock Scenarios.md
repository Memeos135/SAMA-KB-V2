# Interest Rate Shock Scenarios

## Why this community

Prescribed interest rate risk in the banking book (IRRBB) stress scenarios that supervised banks must apply when measuring and reporting rate sensitivity.

## How members connect

- The standardised shock set is the parent requirement; parallel, short-rate, long-rate and rotation shocks are its mandated sub-scenarios, so applying the standard means applying all of them.
- Rotation (steepener/flattener) shocks are constructed from the short- and long-rate shocks, creating a definitional dependency.
- The scenario calibration is imported from Basel Committee IRRBB standards, so SAMA expectations should be read against that international baseline while SAMA's text controls.

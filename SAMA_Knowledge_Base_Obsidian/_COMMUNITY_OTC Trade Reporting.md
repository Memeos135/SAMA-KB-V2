# OTC Trade Reporting

## Why this community

Post-trade transparency and risk mitigation for OTC derivatives: who must report which transactions to an authorised trade repository, with what identifiers, and what bilateral risk-mitigation duties apply to non-cleared trades.

## How members connect

- The current requirements supersede the earlier OTC derivatives circular; the duplicate entry is the same instrument under a different identifier.
- The instrument splits into two obligation streams — Section A (reporting) and Section B (risk mitigation) — each with its own scope and counterparty duties.
- Reporting depends on mandated identifier standards (LEI, UTI, UPI) as definitional prerequisites, and on submission to a SAMA-authorised trade repository operator.

# PER and IRR Reserves

Covers the Profit Equalization Reserve (appropriated from mudarabah profits to smooth returns) and the Investment Risk Reserve (appropriated from IAH profit after the mudarib share to cushion future investment losses), both maintained at the bank's discretion. The Board must oversee whether reserve levels are appropriate and fair between existing and new IAHs, and senior management must set guidelines on PER/IRR management and on who the reserves revert to on write-off or recovery. Prudentially, PSIA funds and PER/IRR are excluded from regulatory capital, must be reflected in LCR/NSFR and Large Exposure treatment, and outsourced IAH fund management must meet SAMA outsourcing rules; SAMA may direct whether an arrangement is treated as a PSIA.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

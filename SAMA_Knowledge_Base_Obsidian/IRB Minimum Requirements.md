# IRB Minimum Requirements

Node covering the minimum requirements banks must meet to use the internal ratings-based approaches, together with the associated Pillar 3 templates (e.g. CR7 on the RWA effect of credit derivatives, CCR4 on IRB counterparty credit risk exposures by PD scale). It governs eligibility for F-IRB/A-IRB use, portfolio breakdowns and model-scope disclosure. It binds SAMA-supervised banks permitted to use IRB approaches for credit and counterparty credit risk.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

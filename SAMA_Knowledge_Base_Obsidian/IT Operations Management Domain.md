# IT Operations Management Domain

Domain of the Framework addressing day-to-day running of IT at Member Organizations, including managing assets and interdependencies, service level agreements, availability and capacity, data centre management, and system configuration and monitoring. It sets principles and control requirements for operational continuity and performance of information assets. Open this node for questions about IT service delivery, SLA governance with providers, and operational monitoring expectations.

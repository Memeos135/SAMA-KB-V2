# Financial Statements and Prudential Data Reporting Circular

A SAMA circular (Arabic, dated 1446H) directing digital brokerage companies licensed by SAMA to submit quarterly and annual audited financial statements with the auditor's report, plus quarterly and annual prudential data on SAMA's prescribed templates, each within fixed business-day deadlines after the relevant quarter-end or year-end. Templates must be filed electronically in the required format together with a signed Data Certification form executed by the Chief Accountant, CFO and CEO or Managing Director, and the annual prudential templates must be reviewed and certified by the company's external auditor. It binds only SAMA-licensed digital brokerage companies and applies from the stated commencement quarter.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

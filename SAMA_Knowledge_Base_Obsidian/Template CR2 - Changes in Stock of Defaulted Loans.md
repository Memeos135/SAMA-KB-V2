# Template CR2 - Changes in Stock of Defaulted Loans

Template CR2 is the mandatory semiannual Pillar 3 flow statement of the stock of defaulted loans and debt securities, reconciling opening to closing balances through new defaults, returns to non-defaulted status, write-offs and other changes. Defaulted exposures are reported net of write-offs and gross of allowances; for standardised approach banks, default aligns with exposures past due more than 90 days. Banks must explain significant movements and state whether off-balance sheet items are included.

**Regimes:** banking prudential

# Credit Card Issuance and Operation Rules

The Credit Card Issuance and Operation Rules, notified by SAMA circular (1446H), governing how credit cards may be issued, re-issued and operated and how fees, costs and term cost may be charged. As reflected in the accompanying FAQ, they require a documented customer request before issuance, notification of acceptance or rejection with reasons and an objection route, conditions for re-issued and supplementary cards, a minimum age condition, activation before annual fees accrue, a minimum grace period, and prohibitions on increasing agreed term cost due to late payment or default. They bind banks and finance companies operating in the Kingdom as card issuers; this node's context is drawn from the FAQ, so the operative article text must be read in the Rules themselves.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, payments, BNPL/finance

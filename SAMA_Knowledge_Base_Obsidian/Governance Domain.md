# Governance Domain

The Governance domain of SAMA's Counter-Fraud Framework, placing ultimate responsibility for creating the Counter-Fraud Programme, providing direction and setting counter-fraud culture on the Board and Executive Leadership. It requires a Counter-Fraud Strategy, a Counter-Fraud Policy setting responsibilities and mandatory requirements, and a Governance Structure with internal and external reporting proportionate to the organisation's size and complexity, including a dedicated Counter-Fraud Governance Committee chaired by an Executive Committee member and with prescribed minimum membership (e.g. Head of Counter-Fraud, CRO, COO, Head of Digital). It binds Member Organisations' boards and senior management.

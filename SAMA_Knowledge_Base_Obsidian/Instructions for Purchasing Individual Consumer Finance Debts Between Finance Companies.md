# Instructions for Purchasing Individual Consumer Finance Debts Between Finance Companies

SAMA instructions (Nov 2021) regulating the purchase/transfer of individual consumer finance debts between finance companies, issued under the Finance Companies Control Law. They bind finance companies licensed for consumer finance: no separate SAMA approval is needed per transaction, but the buying company must disclose new financing terms and costs and obtain documented customer consent, use a dedicated buyout request form, settle the outstanding debt through the SARIE system with prescribed payment details, and may not charge transfer costs other than early repayment or administrative fees. The selling company must issue liability statements and clearance letters, facilitate the transfer, and notify the customer of settlement, generally within one business day.

# Template TLAC1 - TLAC Composition for G-SIBs

A Pillar 3 disclosure template setting out the composition of total loss-absorbing capacity at resolution group level, including regulatory capital and other TLAC-eligible instruments, deductions, adjusted RWA and leverage exposure measure, resulting TLAC ratios and buffer requirements. Instructions distinguish single point of entry from multiple point of entry structures and require separate reporting per resolution group. It is mandatory only for G-SIBs and their resolution groups, so it has no application to non-G-SIB Saudi banks.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

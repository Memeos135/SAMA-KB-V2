# Remote Work Initiative Circular

SAMA circular implementing the Ministry of Human Resources and Social Development's remote work initiative in the financial sector. It requires financial institutions to record remote customer-service staff in official registers and either enrol them in social insurance or document their remote-work contracts on the ministry's designated portal, including where the worker is engaged directly or supplied through an intermediary, contractor or outsourced provider. Binds all SAMA-supervised financial institutions from the circular's date.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, other

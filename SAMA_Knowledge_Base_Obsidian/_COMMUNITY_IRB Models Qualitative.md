# IRB Models Qualitative

## Why this community

Qualitative Pillar 3 disclosure for banks permitted to use internal ratings-based models for credit risk: model scope, structure, governance, validation and supervisory approval status.

## How members connect

- Single-member cluster providing the narrative context required to interpret the quantitative IRB templates (exposure-by-PD and PD backtesting).
- Relies on defined terms from the IRB regime (PD, LGD, EAD, rating system, model validation) and on the condition that SAMA has granted IRB permission.
- Framework-annex level disclosure implementing the model-governance expectations in the capital adequacy rules.

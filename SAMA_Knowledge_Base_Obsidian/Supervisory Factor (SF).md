# Supervisory Factor (SF)

Defined-term node for the supervisory factor, the regulator-prescribed volatility parameter applied per trade and hedging set in the standardised approach to counterparty credit risk add-on calculations (e.g. 4% for FX). It sits alongside adjusted notional, maturity factor and supervisory delta as the fixed inputs banks may not calibrate themselves. Applies to banks computing CCR exposure at default under SAMA's capital rules.

**Regimes:** banking prudential

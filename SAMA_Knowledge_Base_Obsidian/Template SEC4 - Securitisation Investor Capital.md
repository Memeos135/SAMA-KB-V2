# Template SEC4 - Securitisation Investor Capital

Pillar 3 template disclosing banking book securitisation exposures where the bank acts as investor, with the corresponding risk-weighted assets and capital requirements under the securitisation framework. It mirrors SEC3 on the investor side and is confined to exposures treated under the securitisation framework. Applies to banks subject to SAMA's Pillar 3 disclosure requirements.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

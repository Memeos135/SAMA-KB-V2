# Early Repayment Provisions

The early repayment provisions (Article 10) of SAMA's mandatory standard consumer finance contract for individuals, cross-referenced in the contract summary sheet that must be signed by the beneficiary. As part of the standard form, these terms cannot be varied by the financier and set the contractual basis on which a beneficiary may settle a consumer finance facility ahead of schedule. Binds banks and finance companies offering consumer finance to individuals in the Kingdom.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, BNPL/finance

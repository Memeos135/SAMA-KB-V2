# Economic Cities Property Ownership

## Why this community

SAMA guidance on the documentary basis for property ownership within Economic Cities, affecting how supervised lenders treat such property as collateral or subject matter of financing.

## How members connect

- Isolated circular: it addresses recognition of a specific ownership document rather than cross-referencing a broader regulation.
- Practical effect is on collateral eligibility, title verification and real estate finance underwriting decisions.
- Read together with real estate finance and mortgage registration requirements outside this cluster.

# STC Short-term (ABCP) Criteria

Node covering the short-term STC criteria applicable to ABCP conduits and transactions financed by them, together with related treatment of short-term external ratings in the credit risk framework. It determines when short-term securitization exposures qualify for the alternative (preferential) capital treatment and how issue-specific short-term ratings may and may not be generalised. Binds banks calculating credit risk-weighted assets, including sponsors and investors in ABCP programmes.

**Regimes:** banking prudential

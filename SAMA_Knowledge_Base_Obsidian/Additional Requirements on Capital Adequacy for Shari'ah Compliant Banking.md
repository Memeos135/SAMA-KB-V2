# Additional Requirements on Capital Adequacy for Shari'ah Compliant Banking

SAMA prudential instructions adding Shari'ah-specific capital adequacy requirements for credit and market risk, issued under the SAMA Charter and the Banking Control Law and effective 1 January 2024. It maps Islamic contract structures (Murabahah with binding/non-binding purchase order, Ijarah Muntahia Bi Al Tamlik, Qard Hassan, and equity-type Musharakah/Mudarabah/Wakalah) to risk-weight treatment at each stage of the contract. It binds all SAMA-licensed domestic banks conducting Shari'ah compliant banking, including their majority-owned foreign subsidiaries and foreign branches, and is read alongside the SAMA Basel framework.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

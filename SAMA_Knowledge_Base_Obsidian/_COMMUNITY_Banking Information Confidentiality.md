# Banking Information Confidentiality

## Why this community

Bank secrecy and the duty to protect customer banking information, including limits on disclosure and internal handling of client data by supervised institutions.

## How members connect

- Single-instrument cluster: a standalone SAMA circular stating the confidentiality obligation.
- Interacts with other regimes rather than internal members — credit information sharing, AML information requests, and consumer protection disclosure rules — so read it as a constraint layered over those permissions.

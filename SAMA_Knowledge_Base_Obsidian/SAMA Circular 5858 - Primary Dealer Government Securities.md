# SAMA Circular 5858 - Primary Dealer Government Securities

SAMA operational guidelines (March 2019) for banks acting as Primary Dealers (Bank-PDs) in the Saudi government securities market. They set eligibility conditions (capital adequacy under ICAAP, treasury/trading capability, risk management and hedging capacity, infrastructure, and SAMA no-objection), obligations in primary auctions, underwriting, market-making and secondary trading, the requirement to form a Primary Dealers' Committee with a code of conduct, board-approved investment policy content, application of held-for-trading classification and valuation rules, liquidity-ratio treatment, and separate books, audit trails and reporting including notification of breaches of the Debt Management Office undertaking. Binds banks authorised to undertake PD business.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

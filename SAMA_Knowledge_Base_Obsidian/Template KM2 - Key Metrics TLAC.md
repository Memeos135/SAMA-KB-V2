# Template KM2 - Key Metrics TLAC

Template KM2 is the fixed-format Pillar 3 disclosure of key TLAC metrics at resolution group level, covering available TLAC, RWA and leverage exposure at that level, TLAC ratios, fully loaded ECL variants and the subordination exemption indicator, with quarterly reporting and four prior quarter-ends plus narrative commentary. It is mandatory only for resolution groups of G-SIBs and must be reproduced per resolution group under the MPE approach.

**Regimes:** banking prudential

# IRRBB Risk Management

## Why this community

Qualitative disclosure of how a bank governs and manages interest rate risk in the banking book, including risk appetite, measurement assumptions and hedging strategy.

## How members connect

- Stands as the narrative counterpart to the IRRBB quantitative template: the modelling assumptions and governance disclosed here are what make the reported IRRBB figures interpretable.
- Relies on the IRRBB defined terms (banking book scope, economic value of equity, net interest income) used consistently across the disclosure framework.
- Sits within the governance/risk-management regime, so the disclosure obligation overlaps with internal board and ALCO oversight requirements.

# Mortgage and Valuation Fees

## Why this community

Cost and product-term controls in real estate finance: amendments to mortgage product instructions and the fees chargeable for real estate valuation.

## How members connect

- Both address the pricing and disclosure side of mortgage origination for banks and real estate finance companies.
- Valuation fee limits operate as a component of the total cost the borrower bears under the mortgage product terms, so the two instruments must be read together.
- Consumer protection obligations (transparency of charges, no undisclosed cost pass-through) run through both.
- Instrument hierarchy: circular-level amendments sitting under the real estate finance law and its implementing regulations.

# Freezing and Updating of Accounts

Chapter 11 of the Deposit Taking Finance Companies Regulations sets when a DTFC must freeze a General Account, principally on expiry of the customer's or juristic entity's identification/authorisation documents (e.g. 90 days after expiry of a licence or commercial registration) or where opening documents carry no validity date, and requires monitoring of directors' and authorised signatories' IDs with suspension of their operating powers. It also requires the freezing right and customer notification to be documented in account opening forms and contracts, and interacts with SAMA-directed disclosure and blocking of balances. It binds deposit-taking finance companies in respect of all customer accounts.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, BNPL/finance, governance/risk, consumer protection

# Credit Risk Mitigation Template

## Why this community

Pillar 3 credit risk disclosure showing the extent to which exposures are covered by eligible credit risk mitigation (collateral, guarantees, credit derivatives).

## How members connect

- Standalone template whose content depends on the CRM eligibility and recognition rules in the capital adequacy framework; the template imposes the disclosure obligation, not the eligibility test.
- Cross-reads to the wider credit risk exposure and asset quality templates, since mitigated amounts must reconcile to disclosed gross exposures.

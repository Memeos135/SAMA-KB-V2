# Finance Companies Regulation (10698)

Duplicate/parallel version of the amended Implementing Regulation of the Finance Companies Control Law with the same covering circular repealing the microfinance and micro consumer finance rules. Contains the definitions chapter and general provisions establishing SAMA's regulation and supervision of the finance sector, and the licence-specific activity definitions (micro consumer finance, deferred payment/BNPL, debt-based crowdfunding, microfinance). Binds licensed finance companies and related SAMA-licensed entities in the Kingdom.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, governance/risk, consumer protection

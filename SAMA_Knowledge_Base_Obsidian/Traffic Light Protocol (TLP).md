# Traffic Light Protocol (TLP)

This node addresses the Traffic Light Protocol classification scheme as applied in SAMA's Cyber Threat Intelligence Principles: all intelligence produced by Member Organizations must be classified under TLP in line with the SAMA Cybersecurity Communication Protocols, governing how threat information may be handled and distributed. It sits alongside the Core CTI expectations on collecting data from multiple intelligence sources, defining standard operating procedures, and securely processing and storing intelligence. Applies to all SAMA-supervised Member Organizations producing or sharing threat intelligence.

# SAMA Minimum Capital Requirements for Operational Risk

SAMA's framework setting minimum regulatory capital requirements for operational risk, issued under the Central Bank Law and Banking Control Law and superseding conflicting prior Basel II guidance. It fixes the calculation methodology (Business Indicator Component multiplied by the Internal Loss Multiplier), loss data collection standards, quarterly Q17 reporting and Pillar 3-plus disclosure. It applies to domestic banks on a consolidated and standalone basis; foreign bank branches in the Kingdom follow their home regulator's capital rules.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

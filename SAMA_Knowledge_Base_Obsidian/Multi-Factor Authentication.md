# Multi-Factor Authentication

Node covering the multi-factor authentication and remote-onboarding controls in SAMA's counter-fraud requirements for elevated-risk products and services. It prohibits reliance on SMS OTPs alone for identification and transaction verification, prescribes additional authentication factors, sets content and language expectations for SMS OTP notifications, and requires impersonation and mule-account risk controls where the customer relationship is initiated remotely (including one phone number/national ID per application, with extra due diligence for exceptions). It binds Member Organizations, mandatorily for EMIs, AISPs and providers of digital payment, credit card, microfinance, BNPL or safeguarding services, and on a risk-based basis for others.

**Regimes:** payments, governance/risk, consumer protection, AML/CTF, BNPL/finance

# Inactive and Dormant Accounts

Chapter 12 of the Deposit Taking Finance Companies Regulations defines when a General Account becomes Inactive (one year without customer movement) and Dormant (a further year), imposes dual control and higher-authority approval for reactivation, restricts withdrawals from dormant accounts to duly authorised persons, and requires board-approved policies for customer contact, file isolation, supervision and annual internal audit. It clarifies that a General Account linked to a live Term Deposit Account does not accrue inactivity, and that the rules apply to all customers including those with other active accounts. It binds deposit-taking finance companies.

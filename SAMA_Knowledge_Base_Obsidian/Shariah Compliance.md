# Shariah Compliance

This node covers the Shariah compliance dimension of SAMA's Shariah Governance Instructions for Finance Companies (issued under the Finance Companies Control Law and its Implementing Regulation, effective 2023). It anchors the statutory requirement that licensed finance companies conduct financing business in a manner not conflicting with Shariah rulings as determined by their own Shariah committee, and sets out preliminary provisions, definitions (Committee, Committee members, independent member, Shariah supervision), and the roles of the board, senior management and the Shariah committee. It binds finance companies licensed for real estate finance, productive-assets finance, SME finance, finance lease, credit card finance and consumer finance; for other licensed financing activities it is guidance only, unless other rules make a provision mandatory or SAMA imposes it.

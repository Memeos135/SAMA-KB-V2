# Outsourcing Rules

## Why this community

Outsourcing and third-party risk governance for SAMA-supervised entities: when a delegated activity counts as material outsourcing, what board-approved policy and continuity controls attach, and how sector-specific rulebooks (finance companies, debt crowdfunding, foreign bank branches) inherit that regime.

## How members connect

- Shared defined terms drive scope: 'Material Functions' / 'Material Outsourcing' and 'Third-Party Service Provider' determine whether the heavier notification, due-diligence and contractual obligations are triggered.
- Obligation chain: an approved Outsourcing Policy plus Business Continuity Management arrangements are conditions attached to permitted outsourcing, with responsibility for the outsourced function remaining with the licensee.
- Parallel instruments for different populations — general Rules on Outsourcing, the finance-company version, and the foreign bank branch treatment — overlap in substance; identify which applies before citing an obligation.
- Debt-based crowdfunding entities are linked in because their rulebook cross-refers to the finance-company outsourcing rules while carrying its own defined terms (Platform, Participant, Institutional Beneficiary) and prudential limits (Risk Management Framework, Credit Limits).

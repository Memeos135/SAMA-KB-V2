# Commodity Risk Buckets

## Why this community

Standardised approach calibration for commodity market risk: the bucket structure for commodity risk factors and the prescribed correlation parameters used in aggregating sensitivities.

## How members connect

- Single-node cluster: a calibration table applied within the sensitivities-based method rather than a standalone obligation.
- Legally dependent on the trading book scope and risk-class definitions elsewhere in the market risk framework; the parameters are mandatory inputs, not firm discretion.

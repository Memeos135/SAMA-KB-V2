# Group of Connected Counterparties

Defines when two or more natural or legal persons must be aggregated as a single risk for large exposure purposes, based on a control relationship, economic interdependence, or other connections identified in the bank's own assessment. It includes indicative interdependence criteria (contagion of financial difficulty, correlated default, common dominant funding source), a 5% of eligible capital investigation trigger, and a route for banks to rebut connectedness to SAMA's satisfaction. It also distinguishes Entities Connected with the Saudi Government from commercial undertakings 50%-or-more government owned, which remain subject to the limits.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

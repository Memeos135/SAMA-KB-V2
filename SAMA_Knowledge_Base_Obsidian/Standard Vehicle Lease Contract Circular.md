# Standard Vehicle Lease Contract Circular

A SAMA circular (Finance Companies Supervision) requiring lessors to use the standard/model form contract for vehicle finance lease agreements with individuals, prohibiting contracts that depart from or amend that form. It confirms the earlier compliance deadline was postponed during COVID-19 and then reinstated, with a revised final compliance date in July 2020. It binds finance companies (lessors) licensed and operating in the Kingdom.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, consumer protection

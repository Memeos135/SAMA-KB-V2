# Template TLAC3 - Resolution Entity Creditor Ranking

Pillar 3 disclosure template requiring a G-SIB resolution entity to set out, on a legal entity basis, the ranking of creditors within its liabilities structure, splitting total capital and liabilities into excluded liabilities, TLAC-eligible amounts and residual maturity bands. It mirrors Template TLAC2 but omits the resolution-entity-as-creditor dimension, so only one column per creditor hierarchy layer is used. Binds banks designated as G-SIBs under SAMA's Pillar 3 disclosure framework, with semiannual nominal-value reporting and jurisdiction-specific narrative on credit hierarchies.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

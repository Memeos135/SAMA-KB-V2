# Rules on Purchase of Consumer Finance Debts Between Finance Companies

SAMA instructions (Arabic original) regulating the purchase/transfer of individual consumer finance debts between finance companies, issued under the Finance Companies Control Law. The buying company must hold a consumer finance licence but needs no separate SAMA approval per transaction; it must disclose the new financing terms and costs, obtain documented customer consent, use a dedicated purchase request form with prescribed data, settle the existing debt via the SARIE system with specified payment details, give the customer the payment reference, charge no transfer fees beyond early settlement or administrative fees, and withhold release of surplus funds until a clearance letter is received. The selling company must issue outstanding-obligation statements or debt-confirmation letters and effect the transfer and settlement notification within one business day, refusing only with written justification. It binds finance companies operating in the Kingdom, with consumer protection obligations toward the individual customer.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, consumer protection, payments

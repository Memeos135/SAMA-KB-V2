# Hypothetical Capital Requirement of CCP (KCCP)

Concept node for KCCP, the hypothetical capital requirement of a central counterparty computed from the CCP's exposures to its clearing members, which is the input to allocating default fund capital charges among members. It is calculated at least quarterly (more often on material change, e.g. new cleared products) by the CCP, bank or supervisor, with results shared so members and SAMA can verify. Relevant to banks that are clearing members and to their reliance on CCP-supplied data.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

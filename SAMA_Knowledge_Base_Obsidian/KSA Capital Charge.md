# KSA Capital Charge

Node for KSA, the capital charge on the underlying pool used as an input to the standardized securitization approach (SEC-SA); the attached material also touches capital-composition and capital distribution constraint disclosure templates. Relevant to determining risk weights for securitization exposures where the standardized approach applies. Binds banks computing regulatory capital and the associated Pillar 3 disclosures.

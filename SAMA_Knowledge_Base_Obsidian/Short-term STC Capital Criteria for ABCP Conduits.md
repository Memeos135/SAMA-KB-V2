# Short-term STC Capital Criteria for ABCP Conduits

This node sets out the short-term STC capital criteria applicable to asset-backed commercial paper (ABCP) conduits and the transactions they finance, distinguishing criteria that must be met at conduit level from those tested at transaction level. Conduit-level exposures qualify only if criteria are satisfied at both levels, while transaction-level exposures qualify on transaction-level compliance alone. It binds sponsors and investing/supporting banks seeking alternative capital treatment for ABCP exposures.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

# Template LI2

Pillar 3 template disclosing the main sources of difference between financial statement carrying values (under the regulatory scope of consolidation) and the exposure amounts used for regulatory capital purposes, covering off-balance sheet amounts, valuation differences, netting differences, provisions and prudential filters. Annual, flexible format with rows adapted to the bank's most meaningful drivers, supported by narrative Table LIA. Interpretive Q&A distinguishes balance sheet netting from incremental Basel netting. Binds banks subject to SAMA's Pillar 3 disclosure requirements.

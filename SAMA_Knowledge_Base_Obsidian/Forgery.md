# Forgery

A sub-element of criminal risk in SAMA's Operational Risk Management Guide covering forgery and cheque fraud, historically the second largest operational loss category for Saudi banks, typically involving failure of staff to verify authenticity of cheques or negotiable instruments such as letters of credit before negotiation. It anticipates fewer but more technically sophisticated and higher-value forgeries as document reproduction technology advances, and expects banks' risk policies to address forgery committed by electronic means, including in signature verification and payment card/payment systems contexts.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential, payments

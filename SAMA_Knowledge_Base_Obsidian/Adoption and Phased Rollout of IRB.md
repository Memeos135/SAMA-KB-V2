# Adoption and Phased Rollout of IRB

Governs adoption of the IRB approach across asset classes, permitting a phased rollout across business units, banking group entities and from foundation to advanced approaches, while requiring full application within any asset class in a unit once adopted. Banks must submit a realistic implementation plan agreed with SAMA, and no capital relief is allowed for intra-group transactions designed to reduce group capital during rollout. Binds banks seeking or holding IRB permission.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

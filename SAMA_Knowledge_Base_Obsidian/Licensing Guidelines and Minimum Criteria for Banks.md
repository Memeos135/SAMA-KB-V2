# Licensing Guidelines and Minimum Criteria for Banks

SAMA guidance document setting out the licensing process and minimum criteria for carrying on banking business in Saudi Arabia under the Banking Control Law, issued under SAMA's Charter and the BCL. It covers general licensing guidelines, restricted use of the word "bank", minimum criteria (capital, ownership, corporate governance, risk management, compliance/AML-CTF, IT and outsourcing, audit, home-supervisor oversight) and the application procedure. Binds all applicants for a banking licence, including foreign banks seeking to establish a branch, with the parent treated as the applicant.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk, AML/CTF

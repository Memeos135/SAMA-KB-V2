# Limit Setting

Section of the counterparty credit risk sound practices addressing credit limit setting for HLI and other counterparties, requiring overall credit limits at individual counterparty level that aggregate different exposure types on a comparable basis. Limits should be anchored on meaningful PFE measures covering on- and off-balance-sheet activity, with mark-to-market exposures monitored against initial PFE limits and clear procedures to reduce exposure when limits are approached. Limits are expected to be binding on banks and not driven by customer demand or competitive pressure.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential

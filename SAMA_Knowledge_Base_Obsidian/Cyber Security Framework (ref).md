# Cyber Security Framework (ref)

A cross-reference node: the Aggregation Activity Instructions are expressly without prejudice to other SAMA and statutory instruments, including SAMA's cybersecurity/technology requirements, the Finance Companies Control Law and its Implementing Regulations, the Rules of Licensing Finance Support Activities, the BCM Framework and the Financial Consumer Protection Principles and Rules. Practically, it signals that aggregation entities must layer the Instructions' platform-security and breach-notification duties on top of the applicable SAMA framework obligations. Relevant to licensed aggregation entities and, more broadly, to member organisations subject to the referenced frameworks.

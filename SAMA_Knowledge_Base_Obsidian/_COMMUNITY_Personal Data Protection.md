# Personal Data Protection

## Why this community

Protection of personal data held by financial institutions, and the interaction between KSA data protection duties and sector obligations such as CDD record-keeping, credit information sharing and outsourcing.

## How members connect

- Isolated node whose legal force derives from the national personal data protection regime as applied to SAMA-supervised entities.
- Cross-regime tension point: AML/CTF retention and reporting duties, and credit information disclosure, operate as lawful bases or carve-outs against data subject rights.
- Relevant wherever customer data is processed by third parties (reliance on third-party CDD, outsourcing, payment service providers).

# Template CCR5: Composition of Collateral for CCR

Template CCR5 requires a breakdown by collateral type of collateral posted and received to support or mitigate counterparty credit risk on derivatives and securities financing transactions, including CCP-cleared trades, distinguishing segregated from unsegregated collateral. Both legs of a transaction are reported at fair value. Mandatory for all SAMA-supervised banks subject to Pillar 3, semiannually.

**Regimes:** banking prudential

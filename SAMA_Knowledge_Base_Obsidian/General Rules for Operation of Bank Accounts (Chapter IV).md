# General Rules for Operation of Bank Accounts (Chapter IV)

Chapter IV of the SAMA bank account rules, setting the ongoing operating regime for accounts after opening: deposit rules, account closure, and the dormancy lifecycle (dormant after 24 months without a customer-initiated debit or documented contact, then unclaimed and abandoned status). It imposes dual-control reactivation, restrictions on withdrawals/transfers from dormant accounts, customer notification duties, internal audit of unclaimed/abandoned accounts at least biennially with reports to the Audit Committee, and an annual statistical report to SAMA each March. It binds all banks operating in Saudi Arabia across all customer types, including embassies and government entities.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, consumer protection, governance/risk

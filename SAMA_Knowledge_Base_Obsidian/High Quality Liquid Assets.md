# High Quality Liquid Assets

Defines High Quality Liquid Assets for LCR purposes through fundamental characteristics (low risk, ease of valuation, low correlation with risky assets, listing on a developed exchange) and market-related characteristics (active and sizeable market, low volatility, flight to quality). It sets the qualitative test that an asset's liquidity-generating capacity by sale or repo must survive severe idiosyncratic and market stress, and records SAMA's reading that qualifying instruments should at minimum be repo-able with central banks. Relevant to Saudi banks classifying assets in the LCR numerator.

**Regimes:** banking prudential

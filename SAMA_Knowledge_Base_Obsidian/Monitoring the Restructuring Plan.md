# Monitoring the Restructuring Plan

Part of SAMA's NPL/workout guidance covering how banks should design, negotiate and follow through on a restructuring or rehabilitation plan, including SWOT-based strategy formulation, borrower education where the borrower cannot fully grasp the proposal, and the choice among workout options. It states the two pre-conditions for approving a restructuring (sufficient projected cash flows to repay past-due obligations within a reasonable timeframe, and cooperative borrower behaviour) and encourages out-of-court settlement where economic loss is lower than foreclosure. Binds banks' workout units; also touches syndicated/multi-bank cases where dissenting lenders may fall back on the Bankruptcy Law.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential

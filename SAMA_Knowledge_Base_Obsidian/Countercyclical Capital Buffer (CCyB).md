# Countercyclical Capital Buffer (CCyB)

SAMA instruction on the Countercyclical Capital Buffer (CCyB), issued under the Central Bank Law and the Banking Control Law, setting the CCyB rate applicable to banks' domestic private-sector credit exposures within the Basel range of 0% to 2.5% of risk-weighted assets. It is a macroprudential capital requirement binding banks licensed and supervised by SAMA.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

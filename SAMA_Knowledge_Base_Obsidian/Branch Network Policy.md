# Branch Network Policy

The Branch Network Policy is the mandatory bank-level document, required by SAMA's Regulation on Branch Network, governing criteria for opening, closing and relocating branches, service centers and remittance centers. It must address customer-service strategy (including elderly and disabled customers), target market, delivery channels, digital adoption, agent banking/bancassurance plans, and priority-area/CSR outreach; ATMs are excluded. It must be approved by the Board (or delegated committee), or by the Chief Executive/authorised senior executive for foreign bank branches, and submitted to SAMA within four months of the Regulation's issuance with specified historical data.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential, consumer protection

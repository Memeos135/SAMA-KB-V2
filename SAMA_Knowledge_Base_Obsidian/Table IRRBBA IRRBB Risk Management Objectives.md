# Table IRRBBA: IRRBB Risk Management Objectives

This node covers the qualitative Pillar 3 disclosure of risk management objectives and governance, including interest rate risk in the banking book (IRRBB) objectives and the general Table OVA description of the bank's risk strategy, governance structure, risk culture channels, measurement systems and risk reporting to the board and senior management. The surrounding context also addresses independence of the risk control unit, backtesting/PLA and independent model validation for internal models. Mandatory for all banks, annually, in flexible format.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential

# Payment Systems Oversight

## Why this community

Authorisation, oversight and supervision of payment systems, system operators and payment service providers in KSA, benchmarked against international financial market infrastructure standards.

## How members connect

- Statutory base: the Law of Payments and Payment Services (M/21) confers SAMA's designation, licensing and oversight powers; the Oversight and Supervisory Frameworks are the implementing instruments.
- Both frameworks incorporate the CPMI-IOSCO PFMIs and the CPSS-IOSCO Disclosure Framework as the substantive benchmark, so PFMI principles become effective expectations for designated systems.
- Oversight (systemic, system-level) and supervision (entity-level conduct and prudential) are distinct but overlapping regimes — the same operator may sit under both.
- The PSP licensing guidelines translate the Law's authorisation requirement into application criteria, determining when an entity is a licensed PSP versus an operator of a designated system.

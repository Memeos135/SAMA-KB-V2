# Comprehensive Approach for Collateral

A concept node covering the comprehensive approach to collateral within credit risk mitigation, as it surfaces in SAMA's Pillar 3 disclosure framework (including the CCR5 template on composition of collateral posted/received for derivatives and SFTs, cleared or bilateral). It explains that where banks use the standardised approach for credit risk, mitigation must be measured under either the simple approach or the comprehensive approach with standard supervisory haircuts, and how collateral is then reported. It binds SAMA-supervised banks for RWA calculation and semiannual disclosure purposes.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

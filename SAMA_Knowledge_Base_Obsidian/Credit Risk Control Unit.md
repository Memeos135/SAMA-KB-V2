# Credit Risk Control Unit

Sets the organisational and control requirements for banks using internal models — an independent risk control unit separate from trading and model development, reporting to senior management, performing initial and ongoing validation (at least annually for IMA models), backtesting and P&L attribution at trading desk level. It requires active board and senior management involvement in risk control, use-test integration of model output into day-to-day risk management and trading/exposure limits, and documented policies and compliance routines. Binds banks approved to use IMM for counterparty credit risk and IMA for market risk.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

# Key Principles of Governance in Financial Institutions

SAMA's Key Principles of Governance in Financial Institutions (third edition), issued under the Saudi Central Bank Law and replacing the earlier governance principles for banks. It sets definitions (financial institution, board, executive management, executive/non-executive/independent member, relatives, major shareholders, related parties) and seven principles covering board qualifications, composition and appointment, board and executive management responsibilities, board committees, shareholder rights, and disclosure and transparency, with an annex on money exchange sector governance. It binds all SAMA-supervised financial institutions other than insurance and reinsurance companies — including banks, finance and real estate refinance companies, exchange houses, payment and fintech companies, and credit bureaus — as mandatory from the stated effective date.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential, payments, BNPL/finance, data/credit info

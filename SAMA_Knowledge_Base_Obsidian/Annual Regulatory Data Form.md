# Annual Regulatory Data Form

An annual regulatory data return used by SAMA's Exchange Centres Supervision Division to collect licensing and corporate information from licensed exchange centres: licence issue/renewal/expiry dates, branch list and addresses, ownership and partner shareholdings, executive/non-executive designations, key control-function holders (CEO/manager, internal audit) and appointment dates, cash insurance policy expiry, and contracted external third parties. It supports fit-and-proper, licensing and outsourcing oversight rather than financial reporting.

**Regimes:** governance/risk

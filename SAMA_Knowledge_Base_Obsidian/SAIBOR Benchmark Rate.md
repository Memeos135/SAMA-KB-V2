# SAIBOR Benchmark Rate

A benchmark-rate element of SAMA's Interest Rates on Assets and Liabilities reporting guidelines: banks must include benchmark rates in the rate calculation based on booking value, illustrated by a SAIBOR rate at booking date plus a fixed spread. It clarifies how floating-rate exposures are reflected in the reported annual contractual weighted-average rates, and binds all banks submitting the quarterly return.

**Regimes:** banking prudential

# Clearing Member Capital Requirement (KCMi)

Concept node for KCMi, the capital requirement attributed to an individual clearing member bank for its default fund contribution, derived from KCCP scaled by the member's share of prefunded contributions and subject to a floor. It determines the risk-weighted asset amount a member bank must carry for CCP default fund exposure and feeds the related Pillar 3 disclosure. Applies to SAMA-supervised banks acting as clearing members.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

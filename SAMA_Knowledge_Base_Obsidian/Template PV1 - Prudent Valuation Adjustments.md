# Template PV1 - Prudent Valuation Adjustments

Pillar 3 template breaking down a bank's prudent valuation adjustments for fair-valued assets by risk category (equity, interest rates, FX, credit, commodities) and by trading/banking book, following the Basel prudent valuation guidance and SAMA's circular on assessing banks' financial instrument fair value practices. Fixed format with mandatory narrative on significant changes, "other adjustments" and instruments generating the largest PVAs. Mandatory annually for all banks that record PVAs.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

The consequence is that any change in PVA methodology or amount has a direct capital-ratio effect, and the two disclosures must be internally consistent when assessing CET1 adequacy and buffer headroom.

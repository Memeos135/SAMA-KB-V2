# IRB Specialised Lending Slotting

## Why this community

Pillar 3 disclosure for specialised lending (and equity) exposures where a bank cannot estimate own PD and must instead use the supervisory slotting criteria, mapping exposures to regulatory categories and their prescribed risk weights.

## How members connect

- Single-member cluster: a disclosure template that operationalises the supervisory slotting fallback within the IRB regime.
- Depends on the defined exposure sub-classes (project finance, object finance, commodities finance, income-producing real estate) and on supervisory risk-weight tables set in the underlying capital rules.
- Sits in the same hierarchy as the other IRB templates: Basel text adopted by SAMA, then template as mandated form.

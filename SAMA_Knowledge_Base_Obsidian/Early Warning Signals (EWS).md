# Early Warning Signals (EWS)

The early warning signals component of the Rules on Management of Problem Loans, requiring banks to build documented policies, tools and governance for EWS fully integrated into their risk management framework and calibrated to each lending portfolio. It sets out the categories of indicators banks must consider — economic environment, financial (including periodic interim financials or cash-flow/turnover data for MSMEs), behavioural, third-party and operational — so deterioration is detected and acted on before default. Applies to all banks licensed under the Banking Control Law.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

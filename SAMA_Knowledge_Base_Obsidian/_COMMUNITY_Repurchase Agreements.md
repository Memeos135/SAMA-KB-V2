# Repurchase Agreements

## Why this community

The SAMA repo framework: who may transact, in what collateral, under which master agreement, and how trades are confirmed, settled, held in custody and unwound on default.

## How members connect

- The Guidelines act as the parent instrument, defining eligible counterparties, eligible securities, risk management, custody and reporting/settlement duties.
- Master agreement layer (conventional MRA and the Shariah-compliant master sale/purchase structure) carries the contractual obligation chain into events of default and early termination/settlement.
- Annex confirmation and exercise-notice forms are the documentary evidence steps that operationalise the master agreements.
- Settlement and reporting obligations tie into external infrastructure (SARIE, Saudi Exchange), fixing where legal transfer and finality occur.

# Outsourcing

Control 3.4.2 requiring cyber security requirements to be defined, implemented and monitored within the Member Organization's outsourcing policy and process, before, during and on exit from outsourcing arrangements. It expressly requires prior SAMA approval for material outsourcing, involvement of the cyber security function, and compliance with SAMA's outsourcing circular (Rules on Outsourcing) and related business continuity requirements. Binds SAMA-supervised Member Organizations; note the adjacent cloud computing control applies to hybrid and public cloud but not private/internal cloud.

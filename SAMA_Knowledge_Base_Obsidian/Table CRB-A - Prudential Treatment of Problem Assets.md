# Table CRB-A - Prudential Treatment of Problem Assets

Table CRB-A requires banks to disclose additional qualitative and quantitative information on non-performing and forborne exposures, including the bank's own definitions of non-performing and forborne exposure, whether it applies SAMA's Rules on Management of Problem Loans (No. 41033343, January 2020), materiality thresholds for past due, exit criteria and probation periods, and differences in treatment between corporate and retail lending. Values are accounting carrying amounts stated on the regulatory scope of consolidation. Mandatory for banks, annual, flexible format.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

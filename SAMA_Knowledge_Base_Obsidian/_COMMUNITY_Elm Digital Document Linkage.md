# Elm Digital Document Linkage

## Why this community

Use of a mandated electronic link to the national digital document service (Elm) for verifying customer identity and official documents.

## How members connect

- Single-node cluster; the requirement operates as an implementation directive attached to CDD/identification obligations rather than a standalone regime.
- Cross-regime relevance: identity verification for AML/CTF customer due diligence, and reliability/accuracy of customer data under consumer protection and data-quality expectations.
- Decision impact: reliance on the authoritative government source narrows scope for accepting alternative or self-declared documentary evidence.

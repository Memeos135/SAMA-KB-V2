# Cybersecurity Training

## Why this community

Workforce cyber security awareness and training as a standing control obligation — ensuring staff (and, where relevant, third parties) are competent to discharge cyber security duties.

## How members connect

- Single-node cluster: the training requirement stands as a discrete control obligation rather than part of an internal chain.
- Legally it operates as a supporting duty to the broader cyber security governance and risk framework — training is the human-control layer that makes technical controls enforceable.
- Typically cascades from framework-level obligations down to periodic delivery, coverage and record-keeping expectations for the regulated entity.

# ICAAP Risk Reporting Requirements

SAMA banking supervision communication specifying additional risk information banks must include in their ICAAP submissions, covering connected-party lending concentration, liquidity metrics (LCR, NSFR, top-ten depositor and wholesale funding concentration), off-balance-sheet derivatives activity by currency, and capital leverage ratios including the statutory ratio under the Banking Control Law. It sets reporting content and multi-year historical coverage rather than capital thresholds. Binds all banks preparing ICAAP documents for SAMA.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

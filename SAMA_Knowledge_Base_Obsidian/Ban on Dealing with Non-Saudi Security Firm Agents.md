# Ban on Dealing with Non-Saudi Security Firm Agents

A SAMA supervisory circular addressed to all banks operating in the Kingdom prohibiting them from dealing or negotiating with non-Saudi representatives of private civil security guarding and cash-transport (cash-in-transit) companies when procuring guarding or cash transport services. Banks must report the employing company to the competent regional police authority for legal action, and must fully comply with the Private Civil Security Guarding Law and the Law on Transport of Cash, Precious Metals and Valuable Documents and their implementing regulations. It binds licensed banks as a procurement/counterparty conduct restriction rather than a prudential rule.

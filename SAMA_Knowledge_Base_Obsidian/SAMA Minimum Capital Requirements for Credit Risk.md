# SAMA Minimum Capital Requirements for Credit Risk

Node marking the points where the market risk framework interfaces with credit risk capital treatment: specific risk capital for first-to-default and nth-to-default credit derivatives, internal CVA risk transfers between the CVA portfolio and the trading book, and the boundary between banking-book and trading-book credit exposures. It also records that all banks (D-SIBs and non-D-SIBs) must compute market risk capital under the standardised approach, with RWA derived by a 12.5 multiplier. Binds banks in KSA calculating credit-related market risk charges.

**Regimes:** banking prudential

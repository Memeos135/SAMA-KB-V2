# Currency Banding Card Templates Instructions

A SAMA circular transmitting the fourth edition of the instructions on the design of banding/strapping cards used for bundling sixth-issue banknote denominations, adding a card template for the new SAR 200 note and superseding the third edition. It prescribes mandatory card content (bank name and logo, two officers' names and signatures, note count and denomination, issue series) and exact colour specifications per denomination. It binds banks operating in the Kingdom and cash centres, with compliance required within three months.

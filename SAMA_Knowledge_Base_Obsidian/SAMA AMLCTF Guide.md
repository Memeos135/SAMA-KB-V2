# SAMA AML/CTF Guide

SAMA's AML/CTF Guide setting out expectations for financial institutions under SAMA supervision, including board and senior management responsibilities, appointment of an independent senior-level AML/CTF compliance officer reporting to the board, establishment of a dedicated AML/CTF unit staffed by Saudi nationals with adequate human and technological resources, and the officer's timely access to customer due diligence data and transaction records. It also covers the AML/CTF program, risk identification for new products, services, channels and customers, at least annual policy review, training, and follow-up on audit and supervisory recommendations. It binds SAMA-supervised financial institutions.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, governance/risk, sanctions/TFS

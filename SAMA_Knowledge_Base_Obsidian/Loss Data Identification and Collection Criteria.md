# Loss Data Identification and Collection Criteria

Concept node on the minimum standards banks must meet to use internal operational loss data as a direct input to the operational risk capital calculation, including the 10-year (exceptionally five-year) observation period, documented identification and collection procedures, data quality and integrity expectations, and loss event thresholds. Non-compliance forces capital of at least 100% of the BIC, possible SAMA-imposed ILM above 1, and mandatory Pillar 3 disclosure of the exclusion. Binds banks, in practice those with a Business Indicator above SAR 4.46 billion.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

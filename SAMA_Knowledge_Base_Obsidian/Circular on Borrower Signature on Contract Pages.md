# Circular on Borrower Signature on Contract Pages

A SAMA circular to finance companies, issued under the Finance Companies Control Law and the finance customer protection principles, requiring that natural-person customers sign every page of the contracts and agreements to which they are a party. Applying the same requirement to legal persons is optional, but finance companies bear the risk arising from not obtaining page-by-page signatures from corporate customers. Effective from its date and binding on all finance companies operating in the Kingdom.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, BNPL/finance

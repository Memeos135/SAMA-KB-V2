# IRRBB Quantitative Information

## Why this community

Quantitative Pillar 3 disclosure of interest rate risk in the banking book, reporting the impact of prescribed rate shock scenarios on economic value of equity and net interest income.

## How members connect

- Operates as the numerical output of the IRRBB regime whose assumptions and governance are described in the accompanying qualitative table.
- Uses the supervisory shock scenarios and measurement definitions fixed by the IRRBB standard, limiting bank discretion in presentation.
- Disclosure content is bounded by the banking book scope definition, which separates it from market risk (trading book) disclosures.

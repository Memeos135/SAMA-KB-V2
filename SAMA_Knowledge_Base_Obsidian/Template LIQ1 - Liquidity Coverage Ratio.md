# Template LIQ1 - Liquidity Coverage Ratio

A Pillar 3 disclosure template for the Liquidity Coverage Ratio, presenting unweighted and weighted values of high-quality liquid assets and net cash outflows and the resulting LCR. It complements the key metrics template, which also carries headline liquidity standard figures. Mandatory for banks within SAMA's Pillar 3 disclosure scope, using the prescribed fixed format and reporting frequency.

**Regimes:** banking prudential

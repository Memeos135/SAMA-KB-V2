# Table CRD - Use of External Credit Ratings

Table CRD is a Pillar 3 qualitative disclosure item within SAMA's Pillar 3 Disclosure Requirements Framework, requiring banks to explain how they use external credit ratings when risk-weighting exposures under the standardised approach for credit risk. Disclosure covers the ECAIs used, the asset classes each is applied to, the issuer-to-issue rating transfer process, and the mapping of each agency's rating scale to SAMA risk buckets. It binds all SAMA-supervised banks that use the standardised (or simplified standardised) approach and rely on external ratings; annual frequency, flexible format, with a reasoned explanation permitted where amounts are negligible.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

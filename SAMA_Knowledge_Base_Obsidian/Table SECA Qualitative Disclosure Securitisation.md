# Table SECA: Qualitative Disclosure Securitisation

Qualitative Pillar 3 disclosure table requiring banks to describe their securitisation strategy, risk management objectives, roles (originator, sponsor, investor), risk transfer and retained risks, and related special purpose and affiliated entities, distinguishing banking book from trading book positions. Its scope covers all securitisation exposures, including those failing risk transfer recognition, with a flexible annual format. Mandatory for all banks with securitisation exposures under SAMA's disclosure framework.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

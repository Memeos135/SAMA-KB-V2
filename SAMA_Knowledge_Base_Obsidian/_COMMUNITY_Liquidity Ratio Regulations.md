# Liquidity Ratio Regulations

## Why this community

Prudential liquidity regime: the Liquidity Coverage Ratio standard and the associated supervisory reporting of LCR and NSFR positions.

## How members connect

- Hierarchy: the amended LCR regulations set the substantive ratio requirement; the circular operationalises it through amended prudential returns and extends reporting to NSFR.
- Shared defined terms (high-quality liquid assets, net cash outflows, stable funding horizons) mean the circular cannot be read independently of the underlying regulation.
- Scope is limited to banks subject to SAMA prudential supervision; effective dates in the circular govern which return format applies.

# Expected Shortfall (ES) Model

Concept node for the Expected Shortfall model used under the internal models approach (IMA) in SAMA's Minimum Capital Requirements for Market Risk. It covers which risk factors may be calibrated within the ES model (those passing the risk factor eligibility test and satisfying the modellability principles), SAMA's power to deem data unsuitable and force exclusion, and the backtesting/exception framework used to judge model accuracy. It binds banks in Saudi Arabia that use internal models for market risk capital, subject to SAMA supervisory approval.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

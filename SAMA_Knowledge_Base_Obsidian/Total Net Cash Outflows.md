# Total Net Cash Outflows

Defines the LCR denominator: total expected cash outflows less total expected cash inflows over the 30-calendar-day stress horizon (including expected interest), together with the no-double-counting rule and related outflow treatments such as secured funding roll-off by collateral quality and 100% treatment of net derivative cash outflows. Binds SAMA-supervised banks computing the LCR.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

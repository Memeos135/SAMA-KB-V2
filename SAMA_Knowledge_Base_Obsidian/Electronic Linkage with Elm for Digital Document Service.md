# Electronic Linkage with Elm for Digital Document Service

SAMA circular directing all SAMA-supervised financial institutions to establish technical linkage with Elm in order to use the Ministry of Interior's "Digital Document" service, which allows an electronic copy of the digital ID to be taken and the identity, its validity date and an enquiry reference number to be verified (via QR reader or entry of the national ID/iqama number). It follows an earlier circular on accepting digital identity for existing customers and supplies the mechanism for transactions that require retaining an ID copy. Institutions must coordinate directly with Elm, notify SAMA of implementation difficulties, comply with applicable laws, and preserve the customer's freedom to choose between the physical or digital ID.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, consumer protection, governance/risk, data/credit info

# Preventive Measures (FIs, DNFBPs, NPOs)

Chapter Six of the CTF Law imposes the core preventive obligations on financial institutions, DNFBPs and non-profit organisations: documented TF risk assessment (including customers, geographies, products, channels and new products/technologies before use) available to supervisors, risk-based customer due diligence with enhanced measures where TF risk is high, and record keeping of transactions and documents for at least ten years (extendable by the Public Prosecution) in a form allowing reconstruction of transactions. Related chapters cover reporting to the Financial Intelligence Directorate, information sharing with domestic and foreign counterparts, and supervisory authority powers including inspection, sectoral risk assessment, guidance-issuing and cooperation.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, sanctions/TFS, governance/risk

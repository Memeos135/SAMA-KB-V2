# F-IRB Collateral Recognition and LGD

Sets out how loss given default is determined under the foundation IRB approach for corporate, sovereign and bank exposures, including the supervisory LGD values for unsecured senior and subordinated claims and the categories of collateral that may be recognised (eligible financial collateral plus eligible IRB collateral such as receivables, commercial/residential real estate and other physical collateral). It also prescribes the exposure-weighted LGD* calculation splitting an exposure into secured and unsecured parts, and bars the simple collateral approach for IRB banks. Binds SAMA-supervised banks using the IRB approach for credit risk capital.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

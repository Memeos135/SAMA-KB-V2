# Total Loss Provisions

Node for the total-loss element of the standard-form vehicle finance lease (Ijarah) contract for individuals annexed to the SAMA circular, which the contract addresses together with partial loss of the leased asset. It governs how lessor and lessee handle destruction or write-off of the vehicle and the resulting effect on the lease obligations, with the contract summary required to point the consumer to those procedures. It binds banks and finance companies acting as lessors, who must use the standard form without amendment.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, consumer protection

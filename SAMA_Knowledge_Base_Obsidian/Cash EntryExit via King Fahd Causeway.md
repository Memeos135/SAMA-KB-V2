# Cash Entry/Exit via King Fahd Causeway

A SAMA Currency Department circular setting the agreed operating mechanism with Customs for the entry and exit of Saudi and foreign currency through the King Fahd Causeway customs point. It covers automated financial disclosure declaration forms, sealing and container/seal-number reconciliation, deposit and counting at SAMA's Dammam branch, escalation of discrepancies (declared versus delivered amounts, currency integrity, delays) and handling of counterfeit notes, plus armouring and armed-escort conditions for GCC cash-in-transit vehicles. It binds all banks and money exchange companies operating in the Kingdom, with SAMA's Dammam branch and supervisory functions as distribution recipients.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, other, governance/risk

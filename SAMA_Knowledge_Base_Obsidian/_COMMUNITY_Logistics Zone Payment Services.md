# Logistics Zone Payment Services

## Why this community

Scope and treatment of payment services provided to entities established in the Integrated Logistics Zone, a special-status jurisdictional carve-out within KSA.

## How members connect

- Single-member cluster defined by a scope limitation: which SAMA payment and licensing requirements apply to zone entities and their service providers.
- Raises defined-term questions on residency/establishment status of the customer, affecting onboarding and CDD risk classification.
- Potential interaction with cross-border payment and FX treatment given the zone's distinct regulatory perimeter.

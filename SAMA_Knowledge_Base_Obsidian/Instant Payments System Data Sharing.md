# Instant Payments System Data Sharing

The substantive amendment carried by the same circular: to enable activation of and subscription to additional services under the instant payments system, banks may share specified account information — customer name, account number, mobile number, ID/CR/unified number, and email — with Saudi Payments as national system operator, on a need basis and in strict confidence. The clause is inserted into the standard current account agreement so that customer consent/disclosure is contractually grounded. It binds banks operating in the Kingdom.

**Regimes:** payments, consumer protection, data/credit info

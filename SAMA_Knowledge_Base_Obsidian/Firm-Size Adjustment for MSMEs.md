# Firm-Size Adjustment for MSMEs

Provides the IRB corporate firm-size adjustment for MSME borrowers, defined by consolidated group revenues below SAR 223 million, with a revenue floor and a prescribed adjustment to the corporate risk-weight formula. Permits substitution of total assets for revenues, subject to SAMA allowance, where revenue is not a meaningful size indicator. Binds banks using the IRB approach for corporate exposures.

**Regimes:** banking prudential

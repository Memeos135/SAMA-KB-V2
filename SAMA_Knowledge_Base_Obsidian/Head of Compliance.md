# Head of Compliance

Defined-term node identifying the Head of Compliance under SAMA's Compliance Principles: the chief executive of the compliance unit in local banks, and, in foreign bank branches, the first-management-level officer reporting directly to the branch head. The role covers coordinating identification of non-compliance risk, advising senior management on managing it, and supervising compliance officers and compliance staff (the latter two are separately defined, with "compliance officer" meaning a delegate embedded in another business unit). Relevant to appointment, reporting-line and accountability questions for banks in KSA.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, AML/CTF, banking prudential

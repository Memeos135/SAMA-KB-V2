# IRB Credit Risk Disclosures

## Why this community

IRB credit risk parameter estimation, credit risk mitigation via guarantees and credit derivatives, and the corresponding Pillar 3 disclosure templates. It covers both how a bank computes IRB inputs and how it must publicly evidence them.

## How members connect

- Parameter definitions (PD, LGD, effective maturity) are the shared vocabulary used by the expected-loss/provisioning rules, the guarantee and collateral provisions and the disclosure templates.
- Mitigation chain: recognition of guarantees, sovereign counter-guarantees and credit derivatives under IRB is expressly tied back to the standardised approach CRM eligibility and operational requirements.
- F-IRB collateral rules operate as supervisory overrides of own-estimated LGD, linking the parameter layer to the mitigation layer.
- Disclosure templates (CR6, CR9, CCR4) are downstream reporting obligations whose line items are defined by the upstream IRB parameter rules; CR9 backtesting evidences the PD estimates.

# Trading Book Exposure Value

## Why this community

Measurement of exposure value for trading book positions under the capital framework, including the look-through treatment of positions held through funds and structured vehicles.

## How members connect

- Direct cross-reference: the trading book exposure-value rules point to the CIU/structures provisions for how to value indirect exposures.
- Shared calculation chain — exposure value feeds capital requirement, so the fund treatment is a carve-out/specification within the general method.
- Both are technical definitional provisions in the prudential (capital/large exposures) regime rather than conduct rules.

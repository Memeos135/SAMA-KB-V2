# Foreign Exchange Risk Buckets and Correlations

A market risk node within SAMA's minimum capital requirements for market risk, covering how foreign exchange risk positions are grouped into buckets and correlated for capital purposes, alongside related material on internal risk transfers between the banking book and trading desks and on the simplified standardised approach (where FX risk is one of four recalibrated risk classes summed arithmetically). It binds banks calculating regulatory capital for market risk, including FX risk arising in the banking book. Relevant when determining FX capital charges, recognition of internal hedges, or use of the simplified standardised approach.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

# Cyber Security Risk Response

Sets out how a Member Organization must treat assessed cyber security risks: documenting treatment options (accept, avoid, transfer/share, mitigate through controls) and the approvals required for each. Risk acceptance requires business-owner sign-off within predefined risk-appetite limits, reporting to the cyber security committee, and must not conflict with SAMA regulations; mitigation requires evaluation, costing and selection of controls with documented residual-risk sign-off. Binds SAMA-supervised Member Organizations under the Cyber Security Framework.

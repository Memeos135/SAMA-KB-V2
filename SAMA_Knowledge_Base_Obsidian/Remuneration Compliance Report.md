# Remuneration Compliance Report

Concept node on the reporting and supervisory review obligations under the Banks Remuneration Rules: banks must file a semiannual Compliance Report to SAMA assessing alignment with the rules, disclosing gaps and action plans, with prescribed annexes on total fixed/variable remuneration, adjustments and the top 12 highest-paid employees, plus the Nomination and Remuneration Committee's terms of reference. Deadlines are before 31 March (second half-year) and 31 August (first half-year), with annual independent review results due before 31 March. Non-compliance may trigger SAMA-directed rectification, increased capital or liquidity requirements, penalties or limits on total remuneration; remuneration risk also feeds ICAAP and ILAAP.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential

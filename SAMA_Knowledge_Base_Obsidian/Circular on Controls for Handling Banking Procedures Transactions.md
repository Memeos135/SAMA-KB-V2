# Circular on Controls for Handling Banking Procedures Transactions

SAMA circular (Arabic) setting binding controls for how banks and money exchange companies handle "banking procedures transactions" received from SAMA on behalf of legally authorised government bodies — e.g. account attachment/seizure, lifting of attachment, disclosure of account information and documents under the Enforcement Law, AML Law, criminal and litigation procedure laws, and state revenue/VAT/zakat rules. It fixes maximum completion periods by category (judicial, civil/general, security), requires an immediate reply where no relationship exists, and requires dedicated, empowered and independent units with sufficient staff, technology, business-continuity plans and strict confidentiality, with a narrow exception for the customer or his authorised agent enquiring about execution affecting his own balance. Binds banks and exchange companies operating in the Kingdom, which bear responsibility toward the authorised bodies, SAMA and the customer, with enforcement action for non-compliance.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, AML/CTF, data/credit info, other

# Bankers Blanket Bond / Financial Institution Bond

Refers to the Bankers Blanket Bond / Financial Institution Bond, the principal crime and fidelity insurance cover identified in SAMA's operational risk guidance as a risk-transfer tool against employee dishonesty, loss of property on premises and in transit, forgery and related exposures. It is presented as one of a small set of coverages of primary concern to banks, used in combination with internal controls, fraud programmes and internal audit. Guidance-level material for bank directors and senior management, not a standalone binding rule.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential

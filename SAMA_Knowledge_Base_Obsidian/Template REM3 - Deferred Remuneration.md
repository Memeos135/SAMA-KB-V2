# Template REM3 - Deferred Remuneration

Template REM3 is the Pillar 3 quantitative disclosure of a bank's total outstanding deferred and retained remuneration, completing the remuneration disclosure set with Table REMA and Templates REM1–REM2. Disclosure is annual, aligned with (or promptly after) the annual report. Applies to banks subject to SAMA's Pillar 3 Disclosure Requirements Framework.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

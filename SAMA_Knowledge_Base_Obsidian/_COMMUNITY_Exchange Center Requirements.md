# Exchange Center Requirements

## Why this community

Licensing and operating conditions for money exchange businesses — physical premises standards, Saudization of staffing, and customer identification requirements at branches, including a narrow exception for airport locations serving travellers.

## How members connect

- All three attach to the same licensed activity (exchange business) and to the same defined entity, the exchange centre/branch.
- Premises requirements are the baseline licensing condition; Saudization and ID rules are operating conditions layered on the licensed premises.
- The airport traveller exception is a scope carve-out from the general customer identification duty — read it against the AML/CTF CDD baseline, since an exemption from routine ID at the branch does not displace AML obligations, thresholds or STR duties.
- Hierarchy: exchange business rules issued under the Banking Control Law / SAMA supervisory authority, refined by circulars and inquiry responses.

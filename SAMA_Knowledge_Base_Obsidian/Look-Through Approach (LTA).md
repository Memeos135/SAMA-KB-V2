# Look-Through Approach (LTA)

The look-through approach is the primary method for capitalising equity investments in funds, requiring the bank to treat the fund's underlying exposures as if held directly (adjusted for its equity share and fund leverage), and it also appears in the market risk sensitivities framework for indices and multi-underlying instruments. The source sets out eligibility conditions, consistency-of-application expectations and the interaction with the mandate-based and fall-back approaches, including for IRB banks and funds investing in other funds. Applies to SAMA-supervised banks calculating credit and market risk RWA and reporting them in Pillar 3 templates.

**Regimes:** banking prudential

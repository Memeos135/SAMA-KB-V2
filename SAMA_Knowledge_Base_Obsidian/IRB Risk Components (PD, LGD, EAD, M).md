# IRB Risk Components (PD, LGD, EAD, M)

Node for the IRB risk components (PD, LGD, EAD and effective maturity) as the bank-supplied or supervisory inputs to capital calculation; the surrounding context also carries definitional material on market risk concepts (risk factor, risk position, risk class, sensitivities) and internal risk transfers between banking and trading books. Useful as an entry point to definitions and to the boundary rules on regulatory capital recognition of internal hedges. Binds banks calculating credit and market risk capital under SAMA's framework.

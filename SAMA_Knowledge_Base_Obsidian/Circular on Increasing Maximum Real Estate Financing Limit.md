# Circular on Increasing Maximum Real Estate Financing Limit

SAMA circular exercising the discretion in Article 12 of the Implementing Regulation of the Real Estate Finance Law to reset the maximum loan-to-value ratio, raising the cap for Saudi citizens acquiring a first home to 90% while retaining 70% for banks and 85% for real estate finance companies on second and subsequent homes for all beneficiaries. It also reiterates expectations on risk management policy, credit concentration monitoring and affordability/repayment-capacity verification before granting any financing. Binds SAMA-licensed banks and real estate finance companies, effective from its date.

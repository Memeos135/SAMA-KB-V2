# Circular on Exchange Centers Readiness Ramadan/Hajj

SAMA circular, issued under the supervisory-instruction power in the Rules Governing Money Exchange Business, directing exchange centres located in Makkah, Madinah, King Abdulaziz International Airport and the Hajj City in Jeddah to raise their operational readiness ahead of the Ramadan and Hajj seasons. An annex sets minimum service standards, including adequate Saudi riyal cash and denominations, extended days and hours over the seasons and Eids, queue and crowd management, staff identification and shift schedules, visible exchange-rate boards linked to live rates, customer-rights signage, availability of card/electronic payment acceptance, functioning CCTV and storage, sufficient security personnel, modern counting/counterfeit-detection machines with prescribed handling of counterfeits, bilingual mapping of the branch location, and notification back to SAMA. It binds only the licensed exchange centres in those pilgrimage-related locations.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, governance/risk, other

# Circular on External Auditor Accounting Standards for Exchange Companies

Short SAMA circular to all money exchange businesses operating in the Kingdom, grounded in Article 18 of the Rules Governing Money Exchange Business, which requires each exchanger to appoint a SAMA-accepted licensed external auditor for annual audit. It directs the addressees to obtain and report to SAMA the accounting standards their external auditor applies when auditing the financial statements. Binds licensed exchange companies and establishments.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, other

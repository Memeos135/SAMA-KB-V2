# CVA General Qualitative Disclosure

## Why this community

Pillar 3 narrative disclosure of a bank's credit valuation adjustment (CVA) risk framework — the general qualitative explanation of how CVA risk arises from derivatives and securities financing exposures and how it is governed and managed.

## How members connect

- Single-member cluster: the entry-level qualitative CVA disclosure table that all other CVA disclosures (standardised and basic approach) presuppose.
- Sits in the disclosure layer of the capital hierarchy: minimum capital rules on CVA risk feed the disclosure obligation, not vice versa.
- Uses CVA-specific defined terms (covered transactions, CVA risk capital requirement) shared with the counterparty credit risk regime.

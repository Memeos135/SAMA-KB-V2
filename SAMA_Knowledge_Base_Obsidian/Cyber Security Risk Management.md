# Cyber Security Risk Management

Section 3.2 of SAMA's Cyber Security Framework on cyber security risk management and compliance, requiring a defined, approved and implemented risk management process covering identification, analysis, response, and monitoring/review of cyber risks. The process must protect confidentiality, integrity and availability of information assets and be aligned with the organization's enterprise risk management process, with periodic review and audit of control compliance. Binds SAMA-supervised Member Organizations.

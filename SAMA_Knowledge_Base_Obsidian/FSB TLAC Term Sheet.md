# FSB TLAC Term Sheet

This node concerns the FSB Total Loss-Absorbing Capacity (TLAC) Term Sheet as referenced in SAMA's Pillar 3 framework, governing which regulatory capital and non-capital instruments qualify as external TLAC at resolution group level, subordination requirements and exemptions, caps on non-subordinated instruments, funding-vehicle and ex ante commitment eligibility, and deductions of holdings between resolution groups. It also frames the RWA and leverage exposure bases and buffer interaction for TLAC ratios. It is relevant to G-SIBs and their resolution groups (SPE and MPE approaches) rather than to domestic-only banks.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

The disclosure obligation is therefore derivative: the table sets what must be published, the term sheet sets which instruments qualify to be listed as TLAC-eligible. In practice, an instrument's inclusion or exclusion from the disclosure must be justified against the term sheet criteria, and this only bites for banks within the TLAC scope.

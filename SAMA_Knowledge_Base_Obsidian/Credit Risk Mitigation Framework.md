# Credit Risk Mitigation Framework

Addresses recognition of credit risk mitigants — collateral, guarantees and credit derivatives — including how mitigants on purchased receivables covering default and/or dilution risk are treated, substitution of the guarantor's risk weight, and proportional/tranched coverage of uncovered portions. It also touches credit conversion factors for commitments and capital treatment of nth-to-default credit derivatives. Binds banks applying IRB and standardised credit risk treatments, including where mitigants interact with the securitisation framework.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

# Circular on Awareness of Risks of Carrying Large Cash Amounts

A SAMA circular (dated 30/04/1440H) reminding banks operating in the Kingdom to reactivate earlier circulars on cash-handling awareness and to educate customers about the security and crime risks of carrying large cash amounts. It directs banks to promote electronic channels as an alternative and to point customers to licensed cash-and-valuables transport companies as a safe option. It binds banks operating in Saudi Arabia; it is an awareness/customer-communication obligation rather than a prudential or AML reporting rule, though it sits alongside cash-crime concerns.

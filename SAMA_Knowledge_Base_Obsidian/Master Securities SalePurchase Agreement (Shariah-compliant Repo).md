# Master Securities Sale/Purchase Agreement (Shariah-compliant Repo)

This is the SAMA Guidelines on Repurchase Agreements (2021) together with the standard Master Agreement for the Sale and Purchase of Securities (2020 version) at Appendix A, the Shariah-compliant repo master documentation. It defines the core repo terms (repo/reverse repo, Repo Buyer, Repo Seller, repo market participants, Saudi Repo Market, Tadawul, SARIE, SOCPA) and sets policy requirements on legal documentation, reporting, eligible counterparties and settlement, plus close-out definitions such as Net Value, Deliverable/Receivable Securities and Transaction Costs. It binds all repo market participants transacting in Saudi Arabia, who must use the SAMA-approved MRA with any variations recorded in the Annexes and confirmations.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk, other

# Loan-to-Value (LTV) Ratio

Concept node on the loan-to-value ratio as used in SAMA's problem-loan guidelines: a readily available quantitative indicator of collateral coverage against the outstanding loan, applied alongside financial/cash-flow ratios in the stage-two screening of NPLs not in legal proceedings. Early LTV-based grouping informs the choice of workout strategy (hold/restructure, dispose, enforce) and related measures such as loan splitting or loan-to-asset swaps. Relevant to banks' Workout Units and credit risk functions.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential

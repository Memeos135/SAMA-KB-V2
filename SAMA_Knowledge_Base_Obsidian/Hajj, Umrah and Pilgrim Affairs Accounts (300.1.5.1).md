# Hajj, Umrah and Pilgrim Affairs Accounts (300.1.5.1)

Rule 300.1.5.1 governs bank accounts for Hajj, Umrah and Prophet's Mosque visit activity, including pilgrim affairs offices and tourism companies, agencies and associations organising pilgrim arrivals from abroad. It requires Ministry of Hajj and Umrah approval letters specifying the entity, its ministry-issued computer number and named authorised signatories, Saudi riyal denomination, attested lists of contracted counterparties, and a formal reactivation process for each Hajj season. It binds banks servicing pilgrimage-sector customers.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, banking prudential, governance/risk

# KSrelief as Sole Authorized Entity for Humanitarian Donations

A SAMA circular to all financial institutions under its supervision reaffirming, pursuant to a Royal Order and an earlier SAMA circular, that the King Salman Humanitarian Aid and Relief Centre (KSrelief) is the sole entity authorised to receive relief, humanitarian and charitable donations — whether from government or private sources — for delivery to beneficiaries abroad. Institutions must verify that such donations and cross-border transfers are channelled through KSrelief, review donation transactions made on foreign platforms using bank and credit cards, and continue awareness and monitoring measures when providing services.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, sanctions/TFS, payments

# Premium Residency Acceptance

## Why this community

Treatment of Premium Residency holders as an accepted customer identification/eligibility status for financial services in KSA.

## How members connect

- Single-instrument cluster: a SAMA circular directing supervised entities to accept Premium Residency as a valid status.
- Interacts with CDD/identification requirements under AML/CTF and with account-opening eligibility rules, even though those instruments sit in other clusters.

# Accessibility in Financial Institutions

## Why this community

Consumer protection obligations to make financial products, branches and channels accessible to persons with disabilities.

## How members connect

- Single-member cluster; a standalone conduct requirement directed at supervised financial institutions.
- Sits within the consumer protection regime and overlays other channel and disclosure obligations rather than replacing them.

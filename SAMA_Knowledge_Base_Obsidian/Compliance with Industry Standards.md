# Compliance with Industry Standards

Obliges Member Organizations to comply with mandatory (inter)national industry standards for cyber security, specifically naming PCI-DSS, the EMV technical standard and the SWIFT Customer Security Controls Framework (March 2017 version). It is the point where card-payment and interbank-messaging security standards become supervisory expectations under the SAMA Framework, relevant to payment-processing entities in particular.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, payments

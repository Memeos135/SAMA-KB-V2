# Eligible Counterparties

Sets out who may transact repo in Saudi Arabia, subject to the overriding condition that at least one principal must be a SAMA-licensed bank. Eligible categories include SAMA-licensed financial institutions (banks, insurers, finance companies), CMA-approved capital market institutions, Saudi-incorporated corporates (subject to sophistication assessment), and non-domiciled corporates with maturity/tenor restrictions on the repo and its collateral. Also covers adjacent legal-risk duties: full transfer of ownership of eligible securities, adequate documentation for the repo type used, confidentiality of counterparty identity except for SAMA regulatory reporting, and general compliance with applicable law.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential, other

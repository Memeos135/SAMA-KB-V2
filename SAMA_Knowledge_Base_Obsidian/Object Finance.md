# Object Finance

Defines object finance as specialized lending to fund acquisition of physical equipment (ships, aircraft, satellites, railcars, fleets) where repayment depends on cash flows from the financed and pledged asset. Where no issue-specific external rating is available, object finance exposures are risk-weighted at 100% under the standardized approach. Binds SAMA-supervised banks classifying and risk-weighting asset-based lending.

**Regimes:** banking prudential

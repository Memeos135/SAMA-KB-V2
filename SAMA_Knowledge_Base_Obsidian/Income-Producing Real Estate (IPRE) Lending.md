# Income-Producing Real Estate (IPRE) Lending

Defines Income-Producing Real Estate (IPRE) lending as a specialized lending sub-class of corporate exposures where repayment and recovery depend primarily on cash flows generated by the property (lease/rental income or sale), distinguished from other real estate-collateralized corporate exposures by the strong correlation between repayment and recovery prospects. Related standardized-approach text defines the real estate exposure class, including exposures materially dependent on cash flows generated by the property and ADC exposures. Binds SAMA-supervised banks classifying and risk-weighting real estate lending.

**Regimes:** banking prudential

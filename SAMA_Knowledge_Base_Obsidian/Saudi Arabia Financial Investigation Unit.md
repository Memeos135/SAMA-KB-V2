# Saudi Arabia Financial Investigation Unit

Defined term in SAMA's Guide to Rules Governing Banks' Remittance Centers identifying the Saudi Arabia Financial Investigation Unit as the national body receiving and analysing suspicious transaction reports relating to money laundering and terrorist financing from financial and non-financial institutions. Within this Guide it is the designated recipient of STRs from banks' remittance centres and licensed remittance companies, including where wire transfers lack complete originator information and the correspondent institution fails to respond. Binds remittance centres and their parent banks operating in the Kingdom.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, payments

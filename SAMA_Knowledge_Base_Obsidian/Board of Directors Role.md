# Board of Directors Role

Section of SAMA's stress testing rules setting out the board of directors' duties: overall responsibility and oversight of the stress testing framework, approval of the stress testing policy and its revisions, ensuring adequate resources and infrastructure, reviewing major stress test findings and remedial actions, and requiring periodic management reporting on framework effectiveness. It binds boards of banks subject to the Rules and permits the board to require independent evaluation by internal audit or an external consultant.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential

# ECAI Rating Mapping

Governs how banks apply recognised ECAI ratings to exposures: consistent use of chosen ECAIs across all exposure types (no cherry-picking), use of global rather than national rating scales unless mapped, and the hierarchy rules where one, two, or three or more ratings exist. It also introduces the issue-specific versus issuer rating principles for determining whether an exposure counts as rated. Binds banks calculating credit risk RWAs under the standardised approach.

**Regimes:** banking prudential

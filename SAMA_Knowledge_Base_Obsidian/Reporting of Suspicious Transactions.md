# Reporting of Suspicious Transactions

This node covers the suspicious transaction reporting regime in SAMA's AML/CTF guidance: financial institutions must adopt board-approved STR procedures, resource internal investigation, and report immediately and directly to the SAFIU whenever there is suspicion or reasonable grounds to suspect ML/TF, including attempted transactions regardless of amount. It also requires confidentiality and no tipping-off, response to SAFIU information requests, and separate notification to SAMA of accounts or transactions linked to UN Security Council (1267/1989/2253 and 1988) and national 1373 designated names. Binds SAMA-supervised financial institutions and their designated reporting officer.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, sanctions/TFS, governance/risk

# Operational Risk Manager

Defines the Operational Risk Manager as the senior bank officer responsible for developing the Operational Risk Management Plan and for implementing and running the Operational Risk Management Programme, reporting directly to the Managing Director/General Manager. The role interacts with the Operational Risk Management Committee (which reports to it), the Board (which approves the Plan annually) and Internal Audit in reviewing risk assessment and control matrices. Applies to banks operating in the Kingdom under SAMA's operational risk guidance.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential

# Risk Committee

Concept node for the board Risk Committee under SAMA's Key Principles of Governance for Financial Institutions and related governance instructions. It concerns the committee's formation from the board, its role in advising on risk appetite and strategy and in overseeing the risk management function independently of executive management. It applies to boards of SAMA-supervised financial institutions within the scope of the governance principles (banks, finance companies, exchange, payments/fintech, credit information companies; insurers excluded).

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential

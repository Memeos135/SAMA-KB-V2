# Template CCR3 - Standardised CCR Exposures

Template CCR3 requires disclosure of counterparty credit risk exposures calculated under the standardised approach for credit risk, broken down by regulatory portfolio and risk weight band. It complements CCR1 (exposures by approach) and CCR4 (IRB portfolios by PD scale) within the Pillar 3 CCR section covering banking-book and trading-book CCR charges. Binding on banks reporting under SAMA's Pillar 3 Disclosure Requirements Framework that use standardised credit risk treatment for CCR exposures.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

# Outsourcing Policy

The required content and governance of the written outsourcing policy under the Rules on Outsourcing for Finance Companies: board and senior management responsibilities, which functions may be outsourced, eligibility and due diligence criteria for the External Service Provider (experience, financial and technical capability, effect on the firm's risk profile), and safeguards for confidentiality of consumer data including retrieval or destruction on contract expiry or termination. It also frames ongoing monitoring, reporting to senior management, business continuity and the deadline to file the board-approved policy with SAMA. It binds finance companies licensed under the Finance Companies Control Law.

# Account Rules & Dormancy

## Why this community

Opening, operating, restricting and closing bank accounts for customers — covering eligibility and documentation, term deposits, dormancy handling, and the circumstances in which balances may be frozen, blocked or account data disclosed.

## How members connect

- Hierarchical: the general account rules act as the parent framework, with each specialised rule set expressly referencing back to it for definitions, customer categories and documentation standards.
- Shared defined terms (account holder, customer, operating/dormant status) mean any change in the general rules propagates into the term deposit, dormancy and freezing provisions.
- Obligation chain into other regimes: freezing, blocking and data-disclosure duties are the account-level execution point for AML/CTF, sanctions/TFS and judicial or supervisory orders, while dormancy triggers customer-notification and consumer protection duties.
- Disclosure of account data interacts with banking secrecy and data/credit information constraints, so permissive language must be read as an exception, not a general licence.

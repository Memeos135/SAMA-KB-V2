# Principle 4: Executive Management Responsibilities

Principle 4 of SAMA's Key Principles of Governance, addressing the responsibilities of executive (senior) management — the persons charged with running the financial institution's day-to-day business and proposing and implementing strategic decisions. It delineates management's duties relative to the board's oversight role under Principle 3. It binds senior executives of SAMA-supervised financial institutions other than insurers and reinsurers.

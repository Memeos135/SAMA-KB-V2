# Unique Product Identifier (UPI)

The Unique Product Identifier is the ISO 4914 twelve-character alphanumeric product identification code used in the trade repository reporting fields when no ISO 6166 ISIN is available, indicated by product identifier type 'U'. It sits within the contract information fields alongside the CFI code, internal unique trade ID and Unique Transaction Identifier (UTI) conventions for identifying reported OTC derivative contracts. Relevant to banks in KSA subject to the SAMA OTC derivatives reporting requirements.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, other

# Martyrs' Cheque Exemption

## Why this community

A protective carve-out from creditor enforcement: amounts paid to martyrs' beneficiaries are shielded from seizure/attachment on bank accounts, limiting what banks may freeze or set off against debts.

## How members connect

- Single-member cluster: a targeted instruction to banks that constrains execution of seizure/garnishment orders over identified funds.
- Interacts with the consumer protection and banking-account regimes (account operation, set-off and debt-collection practice), creating a duty to distinguish protected from attachable balances.
- Practical consequence is at the level of enforcement handling rather than product design: the exemption is a defined-scope exception and does not generalise to other government payments unless separately provided.

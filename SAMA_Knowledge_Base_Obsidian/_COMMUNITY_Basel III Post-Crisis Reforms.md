# Basel III Post-Crisis Reforms

## Why this community

The finalised Basel III package as adopted in KSA: revised standardised and internal-model approaches for credit, counterparty credit/CVA and operational risk, constrained by an aggregate output floor, plus the Pillar 3 disclosure regime that makes the outcomes comparable.

## How members connect

- Hierarchy: the post-crisis reforms framework is the parent; the credit risk, CCR/CVA (SCCR) and operational risk (SOPE) minimum requirements are the calculation modules beneath it.
- The output floor is the binding cross-cutting constraint — it aggregates modelled RWA across all risk types against a standardised benchmark, so a change in any single module can alter the floor outcome.
- Credit RWA depends on conversion of off-balance-sheet items via credit conversion factors, linking commitment and facility structuring directly to capital.
- Pillar 3 sits downstream: its templates and guiding principles disclose the results of each Pillar 1 module, so disclosure scope follows the approaches actually applied.

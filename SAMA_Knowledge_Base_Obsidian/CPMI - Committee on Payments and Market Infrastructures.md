# CPMI - Committee on Payments and Market Infrastructures

Reference node for the Committee on Payments and Market Infrastructures (CPMI), the BIS standard-setting body that, with IOSCO, issued the Principles for Financial Market Infrastructures and the related disclosure framework. In the SAMA payment systems oversight framework it is the source of the standards SAMA applies when setting controls, assessing risk and requiring operator self-assessment and public disclosure. It is a definitional/standard-source node rather than a directly binding obligation.

**Regimes:** payments, governance/risk

# Leased Assets Repossession

## Why this community

Enforcement route available to lessors/finance companies when recovering leased assets, channelled through competent authorities rather than self-help repossession.

## How members connect

- Single-instrument cluster: a standalone SAMA circular with no captured cross-references in this graph.
- Legally it sits downstream of the finance lease regime and consumer-protection constraints on collection and enforcement conduct; those links are not modelled here and should be verified directly.

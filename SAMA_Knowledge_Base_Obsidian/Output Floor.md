# Output Floor

Concept node for the output floor itself: the minimum level, expressed as a percentage of standardised-approach RWAs, below which a bank's internal-model-derived RWAs and resulting Pillar 1 capital requirement cannot fall. It affects how minimum capital requirements and 'pre-floor' metrics are computed and disclosed in prudential templates. Relevant to domestic banks using internal ratings-based or other internal model approaches.

**Regimes:** banking prudential

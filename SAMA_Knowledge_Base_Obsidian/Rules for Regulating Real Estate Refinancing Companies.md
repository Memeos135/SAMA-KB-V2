# Rules for Regulating Real Estate Refinancing Companies

SAMA rules issued under the Real Estate Finance Law setting the licensing, prudential and governance framework for real estate refinancing companies (joint-stock companies licensed to refinance real estate financiers and trade rights arising from real estate finance contracts). Chapters cover capital adequacy and liquidity, governance, permitted activities, internal organisation, outsourcing, risk management, compliance, internal audit, SAMA supervision and the secondary market. Binds SAMA-licensed real estate refinancing companies, with services normally directed at real estate financiers (banks and finance companies licensed for real estate finance); dealing with non-financiers or issuing securities requires a SAMA no-objection letter.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, governance/risk, banking prudential

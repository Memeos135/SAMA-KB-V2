# Reduced BA-CVA

Describes the reduced version of the basic approach for CVA risk, in which hedges are not recognised, and the associated mandatory Pillar 3 template (CVA1) disclosing the systematic and idiosyncratic CVA components and total RWA. Binds banks whose CVA RWA is measured in whole or part under the reduced BA-CVA, including a semiannual disclosure and narrative obligation on hedge types used.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

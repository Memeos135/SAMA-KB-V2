# Electronically Notarized LLC Contracts

## Why this community

Acceptability of electronically notarized limited liability company contracts (articles/memoranda) as valid documentary evidence in onboarding and credit files.

## How members connect

- Single-instrument cluster; it settles an evidentiary question rather than creating a new customer-due-diligence standard.
- Feeds directly into KYB/legal-person CDD: it identifies an acceptable form of constitutive document for verifying legal status, authorised signatories and ownership structure.
- Does not relax verification duties; identification, beneficial-ownership and record-keeping obligations under the AML/CTF regime continue to apply to the electronically notarized document.

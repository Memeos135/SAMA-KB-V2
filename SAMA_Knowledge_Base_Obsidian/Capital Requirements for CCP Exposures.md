# Capital Requirements for CCP Exposures

This node covers the capital treatment of bank exposures arising from centrally cleared transactions — trade exposures, initial margin (segregated and non-segregated) and prefunded/unfunded default fund contributions — together with the related Pillar 3 disclosure of exposures at default and risk-weighted assets split between qualifying and non-qualifying CCPs. It sits within SAMA's counterparty credit risk and capital adequacy framework transposing Basel standards. It binds SAMA-supervised banks that clear as clearing members or as clients.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

# Other Real Estate Exposures

Defines "other real estate" exposures as real estate asset class exposures that are neither regulatory real estate nor land ADC exposures, and sets their risk weights: the counterparty risk weight where not materially dependent on property cash flows (75% individuals, 85% SMEs, otherwise the unsecured counterparty weight), and 150% where materially dependent. Binds banks applying the standardised approach to credit risk.

**Regimes:** banking prudential

# Effect of Credit Derivatives

Concept node on the treatment of credit derivatives, focused on written (sold) credit protection — the effective notional amount, reduction for negative fair value, and the conditions for offsetting against purchased protection on the same reference name. It sets the "same or more conservative material terms" test (subordination, optionality, credit events, reference obligation) and the rules for identical reference names and pool-versus-single-name offsets. Binds banks recognising credit derivatives for regulatory capital purposes.

**Regimes:** banking prudential

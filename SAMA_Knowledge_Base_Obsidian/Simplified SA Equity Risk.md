# Simplified SA: Equity Risk

A component of SAMA's simplified standardised approach for market risk capital, covering the equity risk class (capital requirement CR_EQ), including add-ons for non-delta option risks on equity instruments. Equity risk capital is summed arithmetically with interest rate, FX and commodity risk requirements, and the total is multiplied by 12.5 to derive market risk RWA. It binds banks licensed by SAMA that use the simplified standardised approach rather than the sensitivities-based standardised approach or an internal models approach.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

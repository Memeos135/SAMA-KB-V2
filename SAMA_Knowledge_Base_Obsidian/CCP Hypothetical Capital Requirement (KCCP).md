# CCP Hypothetical Capital Requirement (KCCP)

Defines the hypothetical capital requirement of a central counterparty (KCCP) — a supervisory construct computed on a consistent basis solely to determine how clearing member banks capitalize their default fund contributions to a qualifying CCP (QCCP), not the CCP's own capital requirement. It sits in the first step of the two-step risk-sensitive calculation of a clearing member bank's capital requirement (KCMi), and is accompanied by definitions of QCCP, clearing member and client, including CCP-to-CCP links. It binds locally licensed banks acting as clearing members, with SAMA retaining discretion where a CCP's jurisdiction does not apply the CPMI-IOSCO Principles and on the treatment of inter-CCP margin and default fund contributions.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

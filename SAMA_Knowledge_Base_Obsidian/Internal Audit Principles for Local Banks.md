# Internal Audit Principles for Local Banks

The first issue of SAMA's Internal Audit Principles for local banks, issued under the SAMA Law and the Banking Control Law, structured in three chapters: definitions and scope; the roles of the board, audit committee and executive management toward internal audit; and detailed requirements for the internal audit unit as third line of defence (independence, audit policy, organisation, risk-based audit plan, quality assurance, reporting, subsidiaries). Binding on locally licensed commercial banks from the stated effective date.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential

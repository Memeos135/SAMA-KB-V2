# Real Estate Finance Contract

Defined term and Chapter Two subject matter: a deferred payment contract extended to a borrower to own a dwelling. The regulation requires such contracts (written or electronic) to comply with the Law, SAMA rules and any SAMA standard contract form, requires a SAMA no-objection letter before products are offered, and prescribes minimum contract content including parties and ID details, term, total finance amount, term cost and its reference rate and change conditions, APR calculated per the Finance Companies Control Law regulation, and total amount payable. It binds real estate finance entities contracting with borrowers.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, consumer protection

# Total Loss-Absorbing Capacity (TLAC)

Node covering Total Loss-Absorbing Capacity: the composition, deductions, RWA and leverage exposure bases, ratios and buffer interaction disclosed for a resolution group under the TLAC regime. Instructions distinguish single-point-of-entry and multiple-point-of-entry G-SIB structures and explain how TLAC figures relate to, but do not necessarily aggregate with, regulatory capital reported under Template CC1. Relevant to G-SIB resolution entities and their groups within scope of SAMA's disclosure and resolution-related requirements.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

# Case Management System

Concept node for the Case Management System required under the Respond domain of SAMA's Counter-Fraud Framework. It obliges Member Organisations to implement and maintain a system acting as the fraud case database — recording and monitoring suspected fraud alerts, internal and external reports, and investigations from initial assessment through to resolution — with access restriction, case ownership, workflow configurability, categorisation for trend analysis, audit of investigative steps, and retention of case evidence. It binds Member Organisations and supports the Fraud Response Plan and fraud reporting/trend analysis obligations.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, payments

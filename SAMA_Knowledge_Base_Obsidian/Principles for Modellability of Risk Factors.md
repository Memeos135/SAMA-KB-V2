# Principles for Modellability of Risk Factors

States the principles a bank must apply, beyond the mechanical RFET count, to decide whether a risk factor that passed the RFET may actually be modelled in the expected shortfall model: data accuracy and source quality matter, and banks may not rely solely on the number of real price observations. Also records SAMA's power to deem data unsuitable and force NMRF treatment, and its narrow discretion during extraordinary systemic market stress. Binds banks using the IMA.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

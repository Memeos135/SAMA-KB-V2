# Circular on Financial Statements from Exchange Centers

SAMA circular to money exchange centers setting reporting obligations under the Rules Governing Money Exchange Business (Articles 15(c), 17 and 19): quarterly financial position within 15 days of the following quarter plus externally reviewed quarterly statements within 30 days, an audited annual balance sheet within one month and audited annual financial statements within two months of financial year end. It also caps external auditor tenure at five years, requires an annual external auditor assessment of internal control systems reported separately within two months, and specifies submission channels. Binding on exchange centers from financial year 2014, with enforcement action against non-compliant centers.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, other

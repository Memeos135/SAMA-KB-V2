# Misconduct Risk

Concept node on misconduct risk as a driver of remuneration design under the Banks Remuneration Rules: ex ante non-financial performance criteria (risk management quality, legal and regulatory compliance, fair treatment of customers) must be embedded in individual performance and pay, and ex post adjustment must weigh proximity to the misconduct, rank, motivation, negligence, prior history and supervisory failures. It also requires severity to be assessed against fines, losses, reputational and customer impact, and permits freezing awards to individuals under investigation. Binds banks, their Boards, control functions and HR.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, consumer protection, banking prudential

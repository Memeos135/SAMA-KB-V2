# Term Sheet

A component of SAMA's credit workout/loan restructuring guidance describing the role and typical content of a term sheet in negotiating a restructuring with a distressed borrower. It sets out indicative headings (parties, closing date, representations and warranties, repayment and mandatory prepayment schedules, cash sweep, pricing and default interest, revised events of default, additional security, documentation, taxes, governing law) and a sequencing checklist from draft term sheet to signed workout documents. It is guidance addressed to banks' workout units and legal functions, not a binding documentary template.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential

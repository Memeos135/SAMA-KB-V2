# SAMA Collection Controls for Individual Customers via External Service Providers

A SAMA supervisory circular reminding financiers that, when outsourcing debt collection to external service providers, the financier remains responsible for verifying that those providers comply with SAMA's Collection Controls and Procedures for Individual Customers and have procedures in place to ensure compliance. It was issued in response to observed negative collection practices and warns that SAMA will take regulatory action against violators. It binds banks and finance companies operating in the Kingdom.

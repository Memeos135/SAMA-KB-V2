# Template OR2: Business Indicator and Subcomponents

Template OR2 requires annual fixed-format disclosure of the business indicator (BI) and its subcomponents — the interest, lease and dividend component, the services component and the financial component — plus the resulting business indicator component (BIC), over three reporting years. It is mandatory for all banks, with narrative explaining material changes and, where SAMA has approved exclusion of divested activities, the gross BI and the reduction attributable to those exclusions. Relevant to how the standardised operational risk capital requirement inputs are made public.

**Regimes:** banking prudential

# Real Estate Finance Data Sharing

## Why this community

Duty of residential real estate financiers to share borrower and facility data with the licensed credit bureau (SIMAH), sitting at the intersection of real estate finance rules and the credit information regime.

## How members connect

- Single-node topic with no extracted internal links; its legal force derives from credit information law/regulation and real estate finance rules read together.
- Data sharing presupposes customer consent and accuracy/update duties under the credit information regime, so the obligation is conditional, not unrestricted.
- Cross-regime flag: consumer protection and data confidentiality limits apply to what may be disclosed and for what purpose.

# NIST Standards

A definitional/reference node within SAMA's cyber security framework document, pointing to the US NIST body of information-security standards and its glossary (NISTIR 7298r2), from which many of the framework's defined terms are drawn (e.g. malware, mobile device, multi-factor authentication, confidentiality, compensating control, cryptography). It is interpretive rather than obligation-creating: it fixes the meaning of technical terms used in requirements addressed to Member Organizations affiliated with SAMA. Open it to confirm how a cyber-security term is defined, not to find a standalone duty.

# Cyber Threat Intelligence

## Why this community

Sector-wide expectations for cyber threat intelligence: how supervised financial entities must produce, classify, share and act on strategic, operational and technical/tactical CTI, and how that intelligence feeds threat-led testing.

## How members connect

- The Financial Sector CTI Principles act as the parent instrument, with the Core, Strategic, Operational and Technical/Tactical principle sets as its subordinate layers.
- CTI obligations cross-refer to the Cyber Security Framework (baseline controls) and to the Ethical Red Teaming (FEER) Framework, where intelligence drives scenario design.
- Common technical vocabulary is imported from external standards — MITRE ATT&CK, Kill Chain, Traffic Light Protocol — which govern how findings are described and how sharing restrictions are applied.
- Physical-security specifications (CCTV) sit adjacent as a conceptually related control expectation for the same regulated population.

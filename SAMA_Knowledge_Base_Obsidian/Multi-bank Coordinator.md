# Multi-bank Coordinator

Sets out the coordinator role where a distressed borrower owes multiple banks: the bank with the largest exposure normally acts as coordinator, defining goals and deadlines, managing transparent communication and creditor meeting minutes, assessing the need for external financial or legal advisers, drafting the proposed restructuring solution, and potentially continuing as agent post-restructuring. It also covers use of advisers, guarantor involvement and re-confirmation of guarantees, and the preference for out-of-court workouts. Binds banks participating in multi-creditor NPL workouts.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential

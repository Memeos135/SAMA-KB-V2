# Circular on Compliance with Cash Transport Regulations

SAMA circular reminding all exchange companies and establishments to comply with existing instructions on the movement and transport of cash and with the security safety manual, following observed non-compliance. It warns that transporting cash outside the regulated channels, or receiving cash so transported from other exchangers, exposes the party to statutory penalties under the Banking Control Law. Binds licensed money exchange businesses in the Kingdom.

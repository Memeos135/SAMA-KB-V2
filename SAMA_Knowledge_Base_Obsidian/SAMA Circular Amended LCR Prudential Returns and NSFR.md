# SAMA Circular: Amended LCR Prudential Returns and NSFR

A 2013 SAMA circular to banks transmitting the Amended LCR guidance package (guidance documents, prudential returns, FAQs and national discretions) and setting the reporting mechanics: monthly LCR prudential returns from the June 2013 month-end within 30 days of each month-end, with an extended deadline for the first submission. It confirms that NSFR reporting continues unchanged under the earlier 2012 circular pending BCBS alignment. Binds all banks except foreign bank branches for the LCR return obligation.

**Regimes:** banking prudential

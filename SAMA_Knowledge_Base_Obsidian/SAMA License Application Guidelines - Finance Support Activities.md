# SAMA License Application Guidelines - Finance Support Activities

SAMA guidance setting out how founders apply for a licence to practise finance support activities under the Rules of Licensing Finance Support Activities, including the unmodifiable application form, submission channel, and the documents that must accompany the application (commercial register, shareholder list, organisational structure, Fit and Proper forms for founders, 5%+ owners of corporate founders and board candidates, feasibility study and three-year business plan, draft third-party/related-party agreements, and an irrevocable bank guarantee equal to 20% of minimum capital). It stresses that submitting false or incomplete material information is itself a breach of the Rules and can lead to rejection or cancellation of the application. Binds applicants/founders of prospective finance support companies, not yet licensed entities.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, governance/risk

# SAMA Business Continuity Management Framework

A concept node for the SAMA Business Continuity Management Framework as an instrument referenced from the IT Governance Framework's risk, change and configuration control requirements. It is the SAMA-issued source of business continuity and resilience obligations that complement IT risk management, backup, configuration and change controls. It binds Member Organizations supervised by SAMA; the underlying BCM requirements sit in that separate framework rather than in the IT governance text.

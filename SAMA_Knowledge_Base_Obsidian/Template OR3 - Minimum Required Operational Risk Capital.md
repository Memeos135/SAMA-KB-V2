# Template OR3 - Minimum Required Operational Risk Capital

A Pillar 3 disclosure template within SAMA's Pillar 3 Disclosure Requirements Framework covering the minimum required operational risk capital, sitting alongside related capital-adequacy templates (e.g. overview of risk-weighted assets and capital distribution constraints) that translate Pillar 1 requirements into RWA and capital-charge line items. It specifies the scope, content, frequency and format (fixed or flexible) of the public disclosure rather than the underlying capital calculation itself. It binds banks subject to SAMA's Basel-aligned Pillar 3 regime at the consolidated level.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

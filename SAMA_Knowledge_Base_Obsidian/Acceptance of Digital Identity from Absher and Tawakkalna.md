# Acceptance of Digital Identity from Absher and Tawakkalna

A SAMA circular (dated 02/02/1443H) instructing supervised financial institutions to accept the national Digital Identity issued through the Absher (Absher/Afrad) platform and the Tawakkalna application, including the QR-code-bearing digital ID, as valid proof of identity on the same footing as the physical civil-status identity document. Institutions must accept the electronic ID for existing customers in transactions that do not require retaining a copy, must continue assessing and reviewing risks arising from reliance on electronic identity, and must establish a suitable means of obtaining an electronic copy where a copy is required. It binds all financial institutions supervised by SAMA and is effective from its date.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, governance/risk, other

# Rules for Bank Accounts

SAMA's Rules for Bank Accounts, in the part governing inactive account classifications: accounts become dormant 24 calendar months after the last customer-initiated debit or last reliable documented contact, with staged treatment thereafter as unclaimed and then abandoned. It sets operational controls including dual supervision for reactivation, restrictions on withdrawals and transfers, continued acceptance of incoming deposits and transfers without changing status, customer contact obligations before the five-year point, balance-return and account-closure conditions for small balances, separate secure file-keeping, and recognition of balances as liabilities on the bank's balance sheet. Binds banks operating in the Kingdom.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, consumer protection, AML/CTF, governance/risk

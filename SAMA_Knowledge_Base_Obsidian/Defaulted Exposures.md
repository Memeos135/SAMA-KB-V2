# Defaulted Exposures

The defaulted exposures section of SAMA's Minimum Capital Requirements for Credit Risk, which sits within the standardised approach chapter on individual exposures and determines risk weights for exposures that have defaulted; surrounding context includes the retail exposure class criteria (granularity, SAR 4.46 million counterparty threshold, transactor definition) from which defaulted retail exposures are excluded. It binds banks supervised by SAMA when computing credit RWAs.

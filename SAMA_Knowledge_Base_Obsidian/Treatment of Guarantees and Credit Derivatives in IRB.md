# Treatment of Guarantees and Credit Derivatives in IRB

Covers the capital treatment of guarantees and credit derivatives as credit risk mitigation, including which credit derivatives (credit default swaps and total return swaps equivalent to guarantees) are eligible, the exclusion of first-to-default and nth-to-default derivatives from CRM recognition, and the substitution of the protection provider's risk weight for the protected portion. It also addresses capital requirements for banks selling protection through basket credit derivatives and the 1250% treatment of materiality thresholds. Applies to banks calculating credit risk RWAs under SAMA's minimum capital requirements.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

# Basel III Capital Buffers

## Why this community

Bank capital adequacy under the Basel III framework as adopted in KSA, specifically the composition and calibration of regulatory capital and the macroprudential buffer layered on top of minimum requirements.

## How members connect

- Hierarchical: the Basel III capital standards set the definitional base (capital components, risk-weighted assets, minimum ratios) and the CCyB instrument is calibrated and enforced by reference to it.
- Explicit citation link — the countercyclical buffer material draws its defined terms and calculation mechanics from the capital standards rather than restating them.
- Shared consequence chain: shortfalls against the buffer trigger constraints on distributions and require supervisory engagement, so both must be read together to determine an institution's effective requirement.
- Applies to the same scope of banks licensed under the Banking Control Law, distinguishing this regime from finance-company or PSP prudential rules.

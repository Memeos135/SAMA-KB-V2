# No Dual Jobs at Exchange Centers / Localization

A SAMA circular addressed to money exchange centers prohibiting employees from holding two jobs at the same time with more than one exchange center, and reaffirming job localization requirements. It relies on the SAMA Law and the Rules Governing Money Exchange Business, together with SAMA's requirements for appointment to leading positions and its professional conduct and work ethics principles. Exchange centers must verify and remediate dual employment within six months and notify SAMA, and must restrict all activity positions to Saudi nationals without seeking non-Saudi work permits elsewhere.

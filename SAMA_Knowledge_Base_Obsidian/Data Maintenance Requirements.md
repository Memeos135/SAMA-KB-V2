# Data Maintenance Requirements

Covers data quality and retention obligations across two capital regimes: alignment, documentation and validation of risk-theoretical vs hypothetical P&L input data for market risk internal models (including notification to SAMA of changes to alignment processes), and the internal operational loss data standards under the standardised approach, including the ten-year observation period (five years exceptionally on transition) and linkage of loss data to current business activities. It also flags the consequence of failing loss data standards — capital at 100% of the business indicator component, a possible ILM above 1, and Pillar 3 disclosure. Binds banks using IMA for market risk and banks above the specified business indicator threshold for operational risk.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

# Saudi Arabia Financial Intelligence Unit

Concept node for the Saudi Arabia Financial Intelligence Unit (SAFIU) as referenced in SAMA's AML/CTF rules — the national body to which financial institutions report suspicious transactions and activities identified through monitoring. Appears alongside the Kingdom's international AML/CTF commitments (UN conventions, FATF and MENAFATF membership) framing the domestic reporting architecture. Relevant to all SAMA-supervised financial institutions subject to STR obligations.

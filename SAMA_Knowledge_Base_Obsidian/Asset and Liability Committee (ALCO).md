# Asset and Liability Committee (ALCO)

Concept node on the asset and liability committee (ALCO) within the finance company liquidity risk framework. SAMA recommends (rather than mandates) that finance companies form an ALCO of senior management including the CEO plus risk, treasury, financial control and key business functions to oversee liquidity risk, with the Board defining its mandate; committee members must ensure the framework measures exposure and produces timely reports to senior management, the Board and SAMA. Relevant to finance companies designing liquidity governance structures.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, BNPL/finance

- **What this link tells you:** For a finance company deciding where liquidity stress test results must land, the ALCO provisions and the stress testing provisions operate together within the same liquidity risk instrument. ALCO is the senior management forum drawing in risk, treasury and financial control, while the stress testing requirements (including sensitivity alongside scenario analysis) generate the outputs that inform asset and liability decisions and contingency funding. Practically, stress and sensitivity results should be escalated into the ALCO governance loop, and a company that runs the analysis without embedding it in that decision forum has a governance gap even if the modelling itself is adequate.

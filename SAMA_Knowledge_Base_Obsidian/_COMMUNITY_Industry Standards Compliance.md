# Industry Standards Compliance

## Why this community

Adherence to recognised industry and international standards where SAMA requirements incorporate or defer to them (e.g. security, operational or technical baselines).

## How members connect

- Single-node cluster operating by incorporation-by-reference: the regulatory obligation is to meet an externally defined standard.
- Legally distinct from general regulatory compliance — the enforceable content is set outside SAMA instruments, which raises version/scope questions the entity must resolve.
- Commonly paired with cyber security and operational resilience obligations, where standards supply the control baseline.

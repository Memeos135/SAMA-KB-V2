# IMA Default Risk Capital Requirement

Node on the default risk capital (DRC) requirement calculated under the internal models approach for trading book positions, sitting within the market risk capital framework and the wider RWA/minimum capital requirement disclosure architecture (Pillar 1 capital normally RWA x 8%). It relates DRC to other RWA components such as credit risk, counterparty credit risk, CVA and securitisation reported separately. Binds banks with SAMA approval to use internal models for market risk.

**Regimes:** banking prudential

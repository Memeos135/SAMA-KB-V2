# Definition of Sovereign Exposures

Defines the sovereign asset class for IRB purposes as all counterparties treated as sovereigns under the standardized approach, including sovereigns and their central banks, PSEs identified as sovereigns, MDBs qualifying for a 0% risk weight, and certain other specified entities. Determines which exposures receive sovereign treatment rather than bank or corporate treatment in capital calculations. Binds SAMA-supervised banks classifying banking-book exposures.

**Regimes:** banking prudential

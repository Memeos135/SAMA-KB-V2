# Licensed Notary Deeds

## Why this community

Acceptability of title and property deeds issued by licensed notaries for purposes of real estate finance and security documentation. The question is evidentiary sufficiency of the deed relied on when granting or securing financing.

## How members connect

- Single-member cluster: a SAMA circular instructing supervised financiers on treatment of deeds originating from licensed notaries.
- Functionally chained to real estate finance underwriting and collateral-perfection duties — the deed is the document on which the security position rests.
- Interacts with customer/property due-diligence expectations; verification of the deed source is part of the lender's documentation obligation.

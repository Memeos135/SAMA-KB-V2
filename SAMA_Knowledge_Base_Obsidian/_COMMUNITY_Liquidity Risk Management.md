# Liquidity Risk Management

## Why this community

Governance and measurement expectations for managing liquidity risk — risk appetite, funding strategy, stress testing, contingency funding planning and board/senior management accountability.

## How members connect

- Single-member cluster stating principles-level obligations that other liquidity instruments (ratios, returns) operationalise.
- Functions as the qualitative counterpart to quantitative liquidity reporting: supervisory assessment weighs process adequacy alongside reported ratios.
- Feeds the broader governance/risk regime, including ICAAP-style internal assessment and board oversight duties.

# Standardised Approach for Counterparty Credit Risk

Identifies the Standardised Approach for Counterparty Credit Risk (SA-CCR), adopted by SAMA circulars implementing the March 2014 Basel document, as the required method for determining exposure at default for derivatives and other counterparty-credit-risk instruments in the large exposure calculation. The surrounding text also sets eligible credit risk mitigation techniques for LEX purposes, expressly excluding equities, convertible bonds and UCITS, and limits recognition of IRB-only collateral. Binds banks measuring and mitigating exposures under the LEX Rules.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

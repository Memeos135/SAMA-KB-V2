# Finance Company Licensing

## Why this community

Authorisation of finance companies: which financing activities require a licence, the entry conditions and application process, and the minimum capital thresholds that vary by activity, including the lighter regime for micro finance and micro consumer finance.

## How members connect

- Clear hierarchy: the Finance Companies Control Law's Executive Regulation is the parent instrument from which licensing conditions, capital thresholds and activity definitions derive.
- Licensing requirements and minimum paid-up capital are interdependent — capital is an eligibility condition for the licence and is calibrated to the activities applied for.
- Micro finance / micro consumer finance is a scoped-out activity class with its own capital and conduct treatment, so activity classification drives the applicable threshold.
- Downstream relevance: licensed activity scope determines which consumer protection, BNPL and AML obligations attach to the entity.

# Disabled Emergency Evacuation

## Why this community

Accessibility and safety obligations requiring supervised entities' premises and emergency procedures to accommodate persons with disabilities during evacuation.

## How members connect

- Single-instrument cluster; the duty sits within consumer protection / fair treatment of customers with disabilities rather than prudential regulation.
- Applies at branch and premises level, so the obligation runs to the licensed entity's operational and business-continuity arrangements.

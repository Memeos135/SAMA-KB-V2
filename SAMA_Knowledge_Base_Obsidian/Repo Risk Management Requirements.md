# Repo Risk Management Requirements

Imposes repo risk-management obligations on banks and all other repo market participants: documented policies and procedures covering governance, authorisation, risk management, internal controls and reporting, comprehensively addressing credit, counterparty, market, legal and operational risk. Also requires coordination across treasury, back office, risk, compliance and IT with adequate supporting infrastructure, counterparty and issuer exposure limits reviewed periodically, and compliance with SOCPA-endorsed accounting treatment for repos. Adjacent provisions cover collateral valuation, agreed price sources with a waterfall approach, haircuts/margin calls, substitution and events of default.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential

# SAMA Sound Practices for Banks Interactions with HLIs

The SAMA circular transmitting the Basel Committee paper on sound practices for banks' interactions with highly leveraged institutions, issued after the near-collapse of LTCM. It requires banks to ensure credit, risk management and related function heads are conversant with the practices described, and to manage any HLI or hedge fund exposures in line with SAMA's internal control guidelines and prudent internal credit policies. Addressed to all banks, at general manager and managing director level.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

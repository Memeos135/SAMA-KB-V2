# Penalties Publication Classification

## Why this community

SAMA's enforcement transparency regime: when and how penalties imposed on supervised banks are published, and how the underlying violations are categorised by subject matter.

## How members connect

- The publication instructions set the obligation/discretion to disclose enforcement outcomes; the classification guide supplies the taxonomy used to label each published violation.
- Direct reference linkage — classification categories are the controlled vocabulary for the publication output.
- Practical consequence: the classification determines reputational exposure and how a penalty is read across regimes (AML, consumer protection, governance).

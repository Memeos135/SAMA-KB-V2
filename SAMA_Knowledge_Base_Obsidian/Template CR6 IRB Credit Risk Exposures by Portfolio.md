# Template CR6: IRB Credit Risk Exposures by Portfolio

A Pillar 3 template requiring banks using foundation or advanced IRB approaches to disclose credit risk exposures by regulatory portfolio and PD scale, with parameters such as EAD post-CRM, average PD, LGD, maturity, RWA and expected loss. Separate template sets are required where F-IRB and A-IRB are both used, and narrative must explain model scope and coverage. Binds only banks with SAMA approval to use IRB approaches for credit risk.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

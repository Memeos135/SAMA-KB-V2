# Full Version BA-CVA (Kfull)

Covers the full version of the basic approach to CVA risk capital (Kfull), which recognizes eligible counterparty credit spread hedges and is aimed at banks that actively hedge CVA; the reduced BA-CVA result is embedded in it as a conservative limit on hedging recognition. The surrounding provisions govern treatment of eligible and ineligible external hedges, internal CVA hedges between the CVA desk and trading desks, and interaction with market risk capital and IRB maturity adjustment. It binds banks using BA-CVA for covered derivative and SFT transactions.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

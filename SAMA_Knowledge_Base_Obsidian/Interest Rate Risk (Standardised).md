# Interest Rate Risk (Standardised)

Concept node on interest rate risk within the market risk capital framework, spanning the defined-terms layer (risk factor, risk position, risk bucket, risk class including general interest rate risk, sensitivity/delta) and the standardised and simplified standardised treatments of interest rate exposures, including interest rate risk embedded in commodity forward positions and funding of positions. Binds banks calculating market risk capital requirements for debt and rate-sensitive instruments.

**Regimes:** banking prudential

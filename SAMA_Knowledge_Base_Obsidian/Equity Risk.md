# Equity Risk

A concept node for equity risk as one of the defined risk classes in SAMA's market risk capital framework, alongside general interest rate risk, credit spread risk (non-securitisation and securitisation), FX risk and commodity risk. The surrounding material covers the treatment of internal risk transfers of equity and interest rate risk from banking book to trading book, and supporting definitions such as risk factor, risk position, risk bucket, sensitivity and delta risk. It binds SAMA-licensed banks calculating market risk capital requirements.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

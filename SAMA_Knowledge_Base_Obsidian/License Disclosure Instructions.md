# License Disclosure Instructions

SAMA's License Disclosure Instructions, requiring all SAMA-supervised financial institutions to state their licensing status and SAMA supervision in correspondence, printed material, brochures, email, websites, mobile apps, directories, media advertising, press releases, direct customer contact and social media. It defines Financial Institution (banks and foreign bank branches, insurers and reinsurers, free professions companies, finance companies, credit bureaus, exchange houses and cash centres, payments and payment system companies), Advertising, Footers, Correspondence and Customers, and prescribes minimum disclosure content (legal name matching the licence, entity type, capital, CR number, address, licence number and SAMA supervision), with a reduced disclosure for advertisements and an exemption for commercial paper transactions.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, payments, data/credit info, governance/risk

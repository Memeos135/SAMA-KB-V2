# Intelligence Monitoring

Sets the principle and control requirements for a defined, approved and implemented fraud Intelligence Monitoring process drawing on internal and external sources to identify and monitor emerging fraud threats, including scanning, analysis, dissemination, action and sharing with internal and external stakeholders including SAMA. It requires alignment with SAMA's Cyber Threat Intelligence Principles, periodic effectiveness evaluation, sharing of confirmed-fraud log-in data through the Sectorial Anti-Fraud Committee and analysis of data shared by peers. Binds Member Organisations.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, AML/CTF

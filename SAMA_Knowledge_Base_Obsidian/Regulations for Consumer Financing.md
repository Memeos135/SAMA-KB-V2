# Regulations for Consumer Financing

SAMA's Regulations for Consumer Financing, setting the conduct framework for granting Consumer Financing to a Borrower (a natural person financing outside trade or profession), covering financing contracts and guarantee agreements, obligations and accountability, advertising, disclosure rules, dealing with Borrowers, and dispute resolution. It requires a Borrower request via Authenticated Communication before new financing or any limit increase (each increase needing a new Financing Contract), documentation of the financing purpose, a refinancing eligibility threshold of at least 20% repayment of the original Amount of Financing, and full disclosure on refinancing. Applies to all kinds of Consumer Financing by Creditors supervised by SAMA; lease, real estate financing and margin lending are outside scope.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, consumer protection

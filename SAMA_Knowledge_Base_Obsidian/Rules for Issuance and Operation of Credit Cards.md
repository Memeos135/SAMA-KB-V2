# Rules for Issuance and Operation of Credit Cards

SAMA's Rules for Issuance and Operation of Credit Cards, issued under the SAMA Law and circulated to banks and finance companies operating in the Kingdom, replacing the earlier Credit and Charge Card controls. They cover the full product lifecycle — card issuance, the card agreement, repayment, statements, transactions, liability for unauthorised use, and fees and costs — and set defined terms such as Issuer, Customer, Credit Limit, Amount Due, Minimum Payment, Due Date, APR, Grace Period, Term Cost and Late Payment Fee. Binding on SAMA-supervised entities licensed to issue and finance credit cards, including charge (monthly settlement) cards, with a general compliance window from publication and a longer window for two specified provisions on repayment and on fees.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, BNPL/finance, banking prudential, data/credit info, AML/CTF

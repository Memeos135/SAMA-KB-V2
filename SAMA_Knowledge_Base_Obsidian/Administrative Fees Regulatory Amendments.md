# Administrative Fees Regulatory Amendments

This node covers the amended regulatory texts on administrative fees issued with the Tariff Guide circular: Article 9 of the Consumer Finance Regulations, Rule 1 of the specific conduct rules in the Principles and Rules for Financial Institution Customer Protection, and Article 11.1 of the standard individual consumer finance contract. The amendments require financiers/banks and finance companies to follow SAMA's administrative-fee instructions, bar deduction of administrative fees before contract signing (with a real-estate valuation exception after preliminary mortgage approval), and cap total costs and administrative fees at the lower of SAR 5,000 or 1% of the finance amount, excluding taxes.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, BNPL/finance

# SAMA Rules on Credit Risk Classification and Provisioning

A cross-reference node linking SAMA's Rules on Management of Problem Loans to SAMA's Rules on Credit Risk Classification and Provisioning, which the problem-loans rules must be read alongside. The surrounding context sets eligibility conditions for credit risk mitigants — leased assets, third-party cash deposits/cash-assimilated instruments (pledge or assignment must be legally effective, unconditional, irrevocable and notified), and guarantees (direct, explicitly documented, clearly defined, no unilateral cancellation clauses). Relevant to banks determining classification, Loss Given Default inputs and provisioning treatment of secured exposures.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

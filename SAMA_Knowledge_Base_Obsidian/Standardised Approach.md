# Standardised Approach

The standardised approach provisions of SAMA's market risk capital rules, mandating that all banks calculate the market risk capital charge under the standardised approach, with any alternative method requiring SAMA approval. It sets the RWA conversion (capital requirement × 12.5), SAMA's power to demand standardised-approach figures, and the conditions and indicative criteria under which a smaller bank may use the simplified alternative (non-G-SIB/D-SIB, no IMA desks, no correlation trading positions), subject to SAMA approval and oversight.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

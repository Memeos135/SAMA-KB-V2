# Due Diligence on External Ratings

Node setting SAMA's general due diligence obligation under the credit risk framework: banks must understand counterparty risk profiles at origination and at least annually, must not rely on external ratings alone, and must have policies, systems and controls demonstrable to SAMA showing that assigned risk weights are appropriate. It also links to the securitization external-ratings-based approach, where risk weights derive from external or inferred ratings subject to seniority, tranche maturity and thickness. Binds SAMA-licensed banks.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

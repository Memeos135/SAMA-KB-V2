# Government Entity Accounts

## Why this community

Account-opening rules for government entities — the conditions under which banks may open and operate accounts for ministries, agencies and other public bodies, and the approvals that gate them.

## How members connect

- The account-opening rules are the governing instrument; the Ministry of Finance appears as the authorising/confirming party whose consent or instruction underpins a government account request.
- SAMA approval and notification operates as a procedural condition layered on top of the substantive rules — the bank's ability to act depends on obtaining or filing with SAMA, not merely on customer documentation.
- Cross-regime note: government-entity onboarding still sits within the AML/CTF customer due diligence regime (legal-person identification, authorised signatories, beneficial-ownership treatment), so these rules qualify rather than displace standard KYC obligations.

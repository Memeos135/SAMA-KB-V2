# Rules Governing Credit Risk Exposure Classification and Provisioning

SAMA's Rules Governing Credit Risk Exposure Classification and Provisioning, issued under the Finance Companies Control Law (Art. 13) and its Implementing Regulation (Art. 62), setting minimum requirements for classifying credit exposures and setting provisions on an IFRS 9 basis. It defines core terms (PD, EAD, LGD, financial asset, expected credit loss, 12-month and lifetime ECL, Stage 1/2/3, past due, problem loans) and requires an effective credit risk management framework proportionate to the company's risk profile. It binds SAMA-licensed finance companies, including their boards and regulatory reporting.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, governance/risk, banking prudential

# Available Stable Funding (ASF)

This node covers Available Stable Funding — the portion of capital and liabilities deemed reliable over the NSFR's one-year horizon — and the ASF factor schedule by liability category, from 100% for regulatory capital and long-dated liabilities, through 95%/90% for stable and less stable retail and small-business deposits, 50% for non-financial corporate, operational and certain six-to-twelve-month funding, down to 0% for residual liabilities, equity without stated maturity and net NSFR derivative liabilities. It supports NSFR computation and prudential return submission by banks.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

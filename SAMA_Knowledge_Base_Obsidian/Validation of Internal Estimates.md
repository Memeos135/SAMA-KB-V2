# Validation of Internal Estimates

Concept node on the risk quantification and validation standards for internal estimates of PD, LGD and EAD — the requirement that PD be a long-run average of one-year default rates, that estimates use all relevant material data (internal, external or pooled) and be demonstrably representative of long-run experience, grounded in historical and empirical evidence rather than judgement alone. Related material also covers internal risk transfer desks and their conditions for regulatory capital recognition. Binds banks approved to use IRB approaches and, for the internal risk transfer elements, banks with trading book activity.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

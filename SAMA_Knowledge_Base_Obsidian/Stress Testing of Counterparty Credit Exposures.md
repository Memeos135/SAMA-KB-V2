# Stress Testing of Counterparty Credit Exposures

A sound-practice section of the SAMA-circulated Basel Committee paper on counterparty credit risk with highly leveraged institutions (HLIs), requiring banks to run timely, plausible stress tests of counterparty credit exposures under volatile markets. It expects stress scenarios to capture large market moves, liquidation effects, liquidity impacts on underlying markets and positions, and impairment of pledged collateral, and warns that merely widening confidence intervals or horizons for potential future exposure (PFE) is insufficient. Binds banks with trading and derivatives exposures to HLI counterparties; results must feed into limit setting and monitoring.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential

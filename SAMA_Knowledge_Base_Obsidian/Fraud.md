# Fraud

A sub-element of criminal risk in SAMA's Operational Risk Management Guide, identifying fraud as the single largest source of operational loss in the Saudi banking system, with the majority perpetrated by employees (notably cash and travellers cheque theft from branches and ATMs). It also flags customer and third-party fraud exposures arising from high ATM cash limits and network interoperability, credit card internal fraud across application, account setup, embossing and statement processes, external card fraud, and merchant fraud at point of sale. Guidance for banks' boards and operational risk functions on where fraud loss concentrates.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential, payments

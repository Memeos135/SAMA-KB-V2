# Template TLAC3 - Creditor Ranking at Legal Entity Level

Pillar 3 disclosure template TLAC3, the resolution-entity counterpart to TLAC2, showing the creditor hierarchy of each G-SIB resolution entity at legal entity level with nominal capital and liabilities, excluded liabilities, potentially TLAC-eligible amounts and residual maturity/perpetual breakdowns. Identical in structure to TLAC2 except that no exposure-to-resolution-entity information is collected, so only one column per hierarchy layer. Mandatory for G-SIBs, semiannual, fixed format, effective from the TLAC conformance date.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

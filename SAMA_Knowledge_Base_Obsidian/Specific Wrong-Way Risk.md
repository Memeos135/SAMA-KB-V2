# Specific Wrong-Way Risk

Node isolating the concept of specific wrong-way risk — where future exposure to a particular counterparty is highly correlated with that counterparty's deterioration — as distinguished from general wrong-way risk driven by macro risk factors. It sits within the counterparty credit risk stress-testing, monitoring and senior-management reporting expectations. Binds banks calculating counterparty credit exposures under SAMA's capital framework.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

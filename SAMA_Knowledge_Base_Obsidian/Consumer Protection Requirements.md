# Consumer Protection Requirements

Node capturing the consumer-facing design considerations banks must build into their internal products and services policies and procedures. It requires target-market-appropriate design, formal customer appropriateness and fairness assessments before approval, fairness and suitability of offerings, avoidance of conflicts of interest, mis-selling and unfair terms, proportionate product-risk control across the value chain (development, governance, pricing, marketing, sale, distribution), phased commercial launch, and compliance with related SAMA instruments including the Responsible Lending Principles, Financial Consumer Protection Principles and Rules, and the advertising rules. It binds banks, with duties allocated to the Board, senior management and control functions.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, governance/risk, banking prudential

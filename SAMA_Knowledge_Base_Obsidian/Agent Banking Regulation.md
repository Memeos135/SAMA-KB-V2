# Agent Banking Regulation

SAMA's Regulation of Agent Banking in the Kingdom, setting minimum standards for banks that contract agents to deliver permissible banking products and services on their behalf, including agent eligibility, commercial register requirements, fit and proper assessment, prior SAMA non-objection, and the contract template and application package. It also requires descriptions of customer due diligence (KYC and AML/CFT compliance), information security and confidentiality, and risk management, and covers data and network security, consumer protection and conflict of interest. It binds all banks wishing to appoint agents; the board of directors is responsible for compliance and the bank remains fully liable for its agents' acts and omissions.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, AML/CTF, consumer protection, governance/risk

### [[Banking Control Law]] — `cites` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** Read the Agent Banking Regulation as subordinate rules made under the Banking Control Law and the Minister's implementing powers, not as a stand-alone permission regime. That hierarchy means agency arrangements operate only within the banking licence: the bank remains the regulated principal and retains full responsibility for the agent's conduct, risk management, controls and integration with its core systems. For a decision, an agent cannot be used to place activity outside the bank's regulatory perimeter, and any gap in agent oversight is attributable to the bank under the parent law.

### [[Law on Combating Terrorism Crimes and Financing]] — `cites` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When a bank appoints or renews an agent, the Agent Banking Regulation's contracting and periodic assessment requirements must be read against the Law on Combating Terrorism Crimes and Financing, which imposes non-delegable CTF obligations on the bank itself. The agent is an outsourced delivery channel, not a substitute regulated entity, so the bank remains liable for customer identification, monitoring and reporting on business conducted through the agent, and the required pre-renewal assessment should cover CTF and sanctions/TFS exposure.

# Use of External Ratings and ECAIs

Sets the rules for banks' use of external credit assessments under the standardised approach: SAMA recognition of eligible ECAIs, consistent and non-cherry-picked use of chosen ECAIs and the global rating scale, the multiple-ratings hierarchy (higher of two, second-lowest of three or more), issue-specific versus issuer ratings, short-term rating interactions with the interbank preferential treatment, no group-wide read-across of ratings, and a prohibition on unsolicited ratings. Binds SAMA-supervised banks determining risk weights from external ratings.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

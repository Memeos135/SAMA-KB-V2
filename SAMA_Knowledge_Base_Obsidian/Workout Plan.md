# Workout Plan

Concept node within SAMA's non-performing loan (NPL) management guidance covering the dedicated Workout Unit and the restructuring (workout) plan it produces. It sets expectations that the unit sits permanently in the organisational structure reporting to Risk Management rather than the originating business line, is staffed by qualified specialists, and tailors restructuring options to each borrower subject to two pre-conditions: sufficient projected cash flows and cooperative borrower behaviour. It also frames the preference for out-of-court settlement where documented recovery exceeds foreclosure, and points dissenting syndicate lenders to the Bankruptcy Law. Binds banks operating in KSA.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential

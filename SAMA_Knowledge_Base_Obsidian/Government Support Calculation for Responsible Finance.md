# Government Support Calculation for Responsible Finance

SAMA circular confirming how government housing support from the Ministry of Municipality, Rural Affairs and Housing or the Real Estate Development Fund is treated when assessing an individual customer's income under the Responsible Lending Principles for Individuals. It restates that government subsidies may not be counted in monthly income except housing support paid periodically (non-periodic or one-off support is excluded), and that the debt burden ratio for such beneficiaries is computed on total monthly income where support is paid monthly. It binds banks, finance companies and credit information companies operating in the Kingdom.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, BNPL/finance, banking prudential

# Watch List

A concept node within SAMA's Guidelines on Management of Problem Loans (2020) describing the watch list generated from the Early Warning Signals (EWS) process: how loans enter it, minimum data content (loan details, group/related-party status, materiality, inclusion date, review timestamps and outcomes, mitigation measures, reasons), and the governance around it. It sets expectations that risk management approves removal, that a loan may only stay listed for a bank-defined period before returning to the originating unit or moving to the Workout Unit, and that the list (or material exposures) is reported monthly to a designated management committee and, for major cases, the Board. Directed at banks' credit risk, relationship management and workout functions.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential

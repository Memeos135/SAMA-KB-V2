# Margin Period of Risk (MPOR)

Defined-term node for the margin period of risk, the assumed close-out horizon used to calculate exposure on margined netting sets, together with the supervisory floors and the exceptions that lengthen them (large netting sets, illiquid collateral or hard-to-replace OTC derivatives, and a history of prolonged margin call disputes). It sets mandatory minimum assumptions banks cannot shorten. Binds banks calculating CCR capital on collateralised derivative and SFT exposures.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

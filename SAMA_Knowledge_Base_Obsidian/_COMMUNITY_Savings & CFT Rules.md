# Savings & CFT Rules

## Why this community

Interaction between retail savings-product rules and the CTF statutory framework, notably identity-document validity as a precondition to operating accounts. Covers the legislative chain amending the terrorism-financing law.

## How members connect

- The Law on Combating the Financing of Terrorism is the apex statute; the Royal Decree and Council of Ministers Resolution are the enacting/amending instruments in the law-making hierarchy.
- Savings product rules and the ID-expiry instruction are SAMA-level requirements that draw their identification and account-restriction rationale from the CTF law (and its CDD logic).
- Shared defined-term dependency: customer identification and verification standards, applied to National ID / Iqama validity, link operational account handling to statutory CTF duties.
- Practical consequence chain: expired identity document → identification no longer verified → restriction on account activity under the savings/AML-CTF regime.

# Counter-Fraud Maturity Model

Describes the six-level Counter-Fraud Maturity Model (0 non-existent to 5 continuous improvement) used to measure a Member Organisation's counter-fraud maturity, with levels 3, 4 and 5 requiring cumulative satisfaction of all preceding criteria. Level 3 requires defined and embedded policies, standards, procedures and fraud detection capability monitored via KPIs; level 4 adds effectiveness measurement through KRIs; level 5 requires continuous improvement. Used as the assessment yardstick for SAMA supervision of Member Organisations.

# Company Establishment Contracts Approval

An informational SAMA circular conveying the Ministry of Commerce's approach to approving company incorporation contracts and their amendments, under which partners and the submitting party bear responsibility for the content in accordance with the Companies Law. SAMA stresses that this streamlined ministry process does not diminish financial institutions' own obligations under SAMA-issued regulations. Addressed to payment systems and companies supervised by SAMA; issued for information rather than as a new obligation.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, payments, other

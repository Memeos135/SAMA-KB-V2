# Restructuring Agreement

Explains the restructuring agreement as the principal document governing a workout — setting out how the borrower will restructure debt and, where relevant, operations, and how and when creditors are repaid, including borrower conduct obligations during the agreement's life. There is no prescribed format; the bank's legal department should decide early whether the workout replaces the existing loan documentation or amends it by supplemental agreement, with simplified documentation contemplated for MSMEs. Addressed to banks structuring and documenting restructurings.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential

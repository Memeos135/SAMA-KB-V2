# Frequency and Timing of Disclosures

This node sets out how often and when banks must publish Pillar 3 disclosures — quarterly, semi-annual or annual depending on the template — and requires the Pillar 3 report to be published at the same time as the corresponding financial report. It sits within the broader disclosure principles (clarity, meaningfulness, consistency, comparability), board-approved disclosure policy and assurance requirements, and the conditions for signposting information held in other documents. It binds banks subject to SAMA's Pillar 3 framework.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

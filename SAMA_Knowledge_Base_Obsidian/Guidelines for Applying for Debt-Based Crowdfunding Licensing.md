# Guidelines for Applying for Debt-Based Crowdfunding Licensing

SAMA guidance explaining how founders apply for a licence to conduct debt-based crowdfunding activity under the Finance Companies Control Law and the Rules for Engaging in Debt-Based Crowdfunding. It specifies submission channels, the unmodifiable application form, the checklist of required documents (articles of association, organisational structure, fit and proper forms for founders and board/committee candidates, feasibility study and business plan, a bank guarantee for 20% of minimum capital, third-party and related-party contracts) and the consequences of false or omitted information under Article 7 of the Law. It binds applicants (founders/shareholders) seeking a DBC licence.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, governance/risk

# Retail Deposit Run-off

Covers the LCR treatment of retail deposits — deposits placed by natural persons, split into stable and less stable buckets with minimum floor run-off rates — including foreign-currency deposit treatment and exclusion of term deposits with over 30 days' residual maturity where the depositor has no legal withdrawal right. Two KSA-specific carve-outs are recorded: Saudi Arabia has no effective deposit insurance scheme (so deposit-insurance-based run-off rates do not apply), and run-off rates apply equally across currencies given the USD/SAR peg.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, consumer protection, governance/risk

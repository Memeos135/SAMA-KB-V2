# Crowdfunding Risk Reporting

## Why this community

Periodic prudential and risk reporting by debt-based crowdfunding platforms to SAMA, covering portfolio exposure, arrears and operational risk on a quarterly cycle.

## How members connect

- Single-instrument cluster: the obligation is a standalone supervisory reporting duty imposed on licensed debt crowdfunding entities.
- It functions as the ongoing-supervision limb of the crowdfunding licensing regime — breach is a licence-condition and enforcement matter, not merely an administrative lapse.
- Reporting content typically interacts with governance/risk-management expectations, so the report is evidence of the board- and management-level controls required elsewhere.

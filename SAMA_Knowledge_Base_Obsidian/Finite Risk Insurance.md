# Finite Risk Insurance

A glossary-level concept within SAMA's operational risk management guidance for banks, describing finite risk insurance as an external risk financing option that blends conventional risk transfer with self-insurance and carries an upper limit on the insurer's exposure. It sits alongside captives, risk retention groups, rent-a-captives and risk sharing pools as alternatives to conventional insurance. Relevant to Saudi banks designing the risk financing policy element of their Operational Risk Management Plan.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential

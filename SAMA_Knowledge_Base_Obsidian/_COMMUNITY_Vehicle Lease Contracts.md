# Vehicle Lease Contracts

## Why this community

Standardised documentation and regulatory amendments for vehicle finance leasing, fixing mandatory lease contract content for finance companies dealing with lessees.

## How members connect

- Hierarchy: amendments to the Finance Lease Implementing Regulation set the rule base, and the standard vehicle lease contract circular operationalises it as mandatory contract wording.
- Shared defined terms (finance lease, lessor/lessee) mean the circular must be interpreted consistently with the amended regulation.
- Consequence: deviations from the standard contract in vehicle leasing are a compliance exposure under the finance companies regime and consumer protection principles.

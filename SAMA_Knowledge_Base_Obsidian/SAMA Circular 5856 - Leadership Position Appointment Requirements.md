# SAMA Circular 5856 - Leadership Position Appointment Requirements

A SAMA circular amending Appendix 1 of the second edition of the Requirements for Appointments to Leading Positions in SAMA-supervised financial institutions, adding a further senior role to the list of positions requiring SAMA's prior written no-objection. The appendix covers board members and committee chairs/members plus senior management roles (CEO, finance, risk, internal audit, compliance, AML/CTF, financial crime, fraud, credit, operations, legal, governance/board secretary, information/cyber security, IT, HR, retail and corporate banking, ALM/SAIBOR submitter, financial enforcement) and any executive reporting directly to the CEO. It binds banks operating in the Kingdom and applies to appointments made from the circular's date.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, consumer protection

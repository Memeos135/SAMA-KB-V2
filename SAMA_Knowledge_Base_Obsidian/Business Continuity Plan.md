# Business Continuity Plan

This node covers the Business Continuity Plan and its surrounding governance layer in SAMA's BCM Framework: BCM governance (board/delegated executive ultimate responsibility, mandated BCM committee with a charter and at least quarterly meetings, a staffed BCM function and appointed BCM manager), BCM strategy, business continuity policy, and the BIA/risk assessment feeding recovery objectives. It also sets what a BCP must contain — key resources, roles and authorities, escalation, recovery within RTO/RPO/MAO, return to business-as-usual, communications and cyber security integration — plus alternative workspace and ongoing monitoring of effectiveness. It binds Member Organizations (SAMA-affiliated entities, banks in Saudi Arabia and their subsidiaries), with the board, senior management and BCM manager/coordinators as accountable roles.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential

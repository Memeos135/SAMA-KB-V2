# Currency Law

## Why this community

The statutory foundation for the Saudi riyal - issuance, legal tender status, and offences relating to currency - forming the primary-law layer above SAMA's operational currency rules.

## How members connect

- Single primary-law node that anchors, rather than depends on, lower-tier instruments.
- Supplies defined terms (currency, legal tender, issuance authority) relied on by circulars on cash handling, damaged notes and counterfeit reporting.
- Top of a law -> regulation -> circular hierarchy where implementing detail sits outside this cluster.

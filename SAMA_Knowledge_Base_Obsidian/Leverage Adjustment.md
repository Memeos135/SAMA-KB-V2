# Leverage Adjustment

Concept node for the adjustments that reconcile published accounting total assets to the leverage ratio exposure measure, as set out in the LR1 disclosure template and its instructions. It covers adjustments for entities outside regulatory consolidation, securitised exposures meeting risk-transfer recognition, any temporary exclusion of central bank reserves enacted by SAMA, fiduciary assets, regular-way purchases and sales under trade date accounting, and eligible cash-pooling transactions, with sign conventions for increases and decreases. Mandatory for all banks reporting the leverage ratio, on a quarterly basis unless SAMA specifies or approves more frequent averaging.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

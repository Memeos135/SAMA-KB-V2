# SAMA Quarterly Risk Report Requirements for Debt Crowdfunding Entities

SAMA circular requiring licensed debt-based crowdfunding entities to prepare a quarterly risk report for discussion by the risk and credit committee (if any) and the board, covering the risks identified in the Rules for Engaging in Debt-Based Crowdfunding, mitigation measures, limit breaches, assumptions underlying risk assessment, and adequacy of disclosure standards. It sets submission deadlines to SAMA for the risk report, quarterly and audited annual financial statements with auditor and board reports, and annual/quarterly prudential returns (externally audited annually and certified by Chief Accountant, CFO and CEO/Managing Director), plus publication of specified reports and statements on the company's website.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, BNPL/finance

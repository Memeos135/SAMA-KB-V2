# SFT Exposures Treatment

Addresses securities financing transaction (repo-style) exposures within the credit risk and disclosure framework, including their recognition under credit risk mitigation techniques (table CR3, covering collateral, financial guarantees and credit derivatives) and the accompanying problem-asset disclosures in table CRB-A. Specifies carrying-value reporting, the split between loans and debt securities, and narrative explanation of material period-on-period changes. Mandatory for all SAMA-supervised banks, semiannually for CR3 and annually for CRB-A.

**Regimes:** banking prudential

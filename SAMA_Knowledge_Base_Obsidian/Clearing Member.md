# Clearing Member

Sets out the capital treatment applicable to a bank acting as a clearing member, including capitalising client exposures as bilateral trades (with a reduced margin period of risk for cleared client transactions), recognition of client collateral passed to the CCP, and the conditions under which a bank that is a client of a clearing member may use the clearing member treatment. It also underpins the default fund exposure calculations attributed to each clearing member. It binds banks that clear for clients or access CCPs indirectly.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

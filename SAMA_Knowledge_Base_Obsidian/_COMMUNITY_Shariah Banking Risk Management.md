# Shariah Banking Risk Management

## Why this community

Prudential risk governance for banks, spanning the Shari'ah-compliant banking risk and governance frameworks, the standard risk taxonomy (credit, market, liquidity, operational, equity investment, Shari'ah non-compliance), stress testing, and internal control expectations.

## How members connect

- A shared risk taxonomy links the Shari'ah RMF, the Stress Testing Rules and the Operational Risk Guide: each attaches obligations to the same named risk categories.
- Obligation chain into capital: identified risks (including Shari'ah non-compliance) must feed ICAAP and regulatory reporting, and stress testing results must inform capital and board decisions.
- Governance layer is common: board and senior management roles, internal control guidelines and Company Law duties define who owns the framework and approves it.
- Version hierarchy: the current Shari'ah RMF supersedes the earlier circular, and interacts with the Shari'ah Governance Framework and PSIA Rules for investment-account holder risk.
- External standards (BCBS sound stress testing principles) are referenced as the benchmark SAMA rules localise.

# Operational Risk Loss Templates

## Why this community

Quantitative Pillar 3 disclosure of operational risk capital under the standardised approach: historical loss experience, the business indicator and its subcomponents, and the resulting minimum capital requirement.

## How members connect

- Computational obligation chain: loss history (OR1) and the business indicator components (OR2) are both inputs that feed the capital outcome disclosed in OR3.
- Both input templates cross-reference the OR3 result, so inconsistency between them is itself a disclosure defect.
- All three rely on the same defined terms (business indicator, business indicator component, internal loss multiplier) set by the operational risk capital rules.
- Read alongside the qualitative operational risk table, which supplies the framework narrative for these figures.

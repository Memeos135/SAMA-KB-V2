# Internal Audit Principles for Finance and Real Estate Refinance Companies

SAMA's Internal Audit Principles setting out the definition, independence, mandate and organisation of the internal audit function, and the duties of the board, audit committee and executive management toward it, alongside expectations on internal audit directors and staff. Internal audit is framed as an independent assurance and objective consulting function over the adequacy and effectiveness of the internal control system, governance and risk management. The Principles bind finance companies and real estate refinance companies licensed by SAMA, and serve as guidance for finance support companies and finance lease contract registration companies, which SAMA may at any time require to apply them in whole or part.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, BNPL/finance

### [[Finance Companies Control Law]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When assessing internal audit obligations of a finance or real estate refinance company, treat the Principles as an exercise of SAMA's supervisory powers under the Finance Companies Control Law rather than as free-standing good practice. The Principles are expressly issued on the basis of that Law (and its Implementing Regulation), so they carry the Law's enforceability and apply to entities within its licensing scope. For the reader this means non-compliance with the internal audit expectations is a supervisory breach, and scope questions (which entities are covered) are resolved by the Law's definitions, not the Principles alone.

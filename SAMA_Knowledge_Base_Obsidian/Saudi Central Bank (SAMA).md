# Saudi Central Bank (SAMA)

The Saudi Central Bank (SAMA) is the independent legal person established under the Saudi Central Bank Law (Royal Decree M/36, 2020), reporting to the King, with objectives of monetary stability, financial-sector stability and confidence, and support for economic growth. The Law defines "Financial Institution" as any natural or legal person subject to SAMA's supervision, oversight and regulation, and vests SAMA with powers over currency issuance, licensing and supervision of financial institutions, monetary policy, payment/settlement/clearing systems, fintech platforms, consumer protection directives, prudential policy and crime-prevention measures. It is the constitutive authority underpinning essentially every SAMA rulebook cited by supervised entities.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential, payments, consumer protection, other

### [[Real Estate Finance Law]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** These instruments meet where a real estate financing decision has both a title/collateral dimension and a capital dimension: the Real Estate Finance Law requires the finance entity to verify the dwelling title deed and the absence of competing in rem rights, and SAMA's prudential framework classifies exposures as regulatory residential or regulatory commercial real estate. Sound legal perfection of the security under the finance law is the factual predicate for treating an exposure as regulatory real estate for capital purposes.

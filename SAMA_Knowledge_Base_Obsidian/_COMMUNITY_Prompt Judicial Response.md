# Prompt Judicial Response

## Why this community

Supervisory instruction requiring financial institutions to respond promptly to judicial and court-related requests, bridging the supervisory regime with judicial process obligations.

## How members connect

- Isolated circular-level instrument: it imposes a direct conduct obligation on supervised entities rather than amending an underlying regulation.
- Cross-regime relevance: interacts with customer confidentiality, account freezing/attachment and data-disclosure duties, so it must be reconciled with bank secrecy and data protection limits before responding.

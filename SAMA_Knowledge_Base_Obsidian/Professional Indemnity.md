# Professional Indemnity

Concept node describing Professional Indemnity (PI) cover, which indemnifies the bank itself against claims by customers and third parties alleging errors, omissions, misstatement or imprudence by its directors, officers and employees in performing services. It contrasts PI with D&O: PI is limited to specified professional services (trust, brokerage, investment advisory) but protects the institution, whereas D&O is broader in wrongful acts but narrower in insureds. Guidance for bank management structuring operational risk financing for service-related liability.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential

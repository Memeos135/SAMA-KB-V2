# Template CMS2 - Comparison RWA Credit Risk Asset Class

Template CMS2 breaks the modelled-versus-standardised RWA comparison down by credit risk asset class, disclosing actual RWA and the RWA that would result from applying the standardised approach to the same exposures. The accompanying instructions define the scope of each credit risk row, its exclusions (counterparty credit risk, equity positions, settlement risk, securitisation, below-threshold amounts) and the relevant Basel/SAMA standard references, together with the derivation of minimum capital requirements. It binds banks using IRB or supervisory slotting approaches for credit risk.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

# Monitoring of Transactions and Activities

This section sets the ongoing transaction and activity monitoring obligation under SAMA's AML/CTF risk-based approach guidance, grounded in Article 13 of the AML Law and Article 69 of the Law on Combating Terrorism Crimes and its Financing. Financial institutions must document and effectively implement risk-calibrated monitoring measures approved at senior management level, use adequate automated systems alongside manual review, test those tools at least annually, and keep customer information and risk classification current. It binds SAMA-supervised financial institutions and feeds directly into suspicious transaction reporting to the SAFIU.

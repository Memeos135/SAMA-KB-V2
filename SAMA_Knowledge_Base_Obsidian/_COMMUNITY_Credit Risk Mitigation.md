# Credit Risk Mitigation

## Why this community

Recognition of credit risk mitigation under the standardised approach: when collateral, netting, guarantees and credit derivatives may reduce a capital requirement, and by how much.

## How members connect

- A single framework node governs the eligibility conditions, then branches into the three mitigation channels - collateralised transactions, on-balance-sheet netting, and guarantees/credit derivatives.
- Collateral treatment forks into the simple and comprehensive approaches, both dependent on the shared definition of eligible financial collateral.
- The comprehensive approach imports supervisory haircuts and master netting agreement rules for SFTs, so haircut recognition is the common quantitative link.
- Cross-cutting adjustments - maturity mismatch treatment and the treatment of defaulted exposures - qualify the amount of recognisable protection.

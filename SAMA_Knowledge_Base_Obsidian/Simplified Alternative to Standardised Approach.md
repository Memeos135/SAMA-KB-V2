# Simplified Alternative to Standardised Approach

A node within SAMA's Minimum Capital Requirements for Market Risk describing the simplified alternative to the standardised approach, one of the permitted methodologies for measuring market risk capital alongside the full standardised approach and the internal models approach (IMA). It sets indicative eligibility criteria (not a G-SIB or D-SIB, no IMA use on any trading desk, no correlation trading positions), makes use of the alternative conditional on SAMA approval and ongoing oversight, and preserves SAMA's power to require the full standardised approach where risks are complex or sizeable. It also covers the simplified treatment of commodity and options positions, binding SAMA-licensed banks with smaller or simpler trading books.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

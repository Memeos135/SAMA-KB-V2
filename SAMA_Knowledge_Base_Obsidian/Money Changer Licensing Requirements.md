# Money Changer Licensing Requirements

This node covers the licensing, ongoing licence-maintenance and supervisory-reporting obligations for Money Changers under SAMA's Rules for Money Changing Business, including the grounds on which SAMA may suspend or terminate a licence (failure to meet licence or business requirements, false information, obstruction of inspection, insolvency, accumulated losses of 50% or more of capital, dissolution or death of a sole proprietor). It also carries related supervisory duties: cooperation with SAMA inspectors, appointment of a licensed external auditor for a maximum of five continuous years (with SAMA able to appoint one at the Money Changer's expense), and periodic data returns on offices, senior position holders and staff. It binds licensed Money Changers, their owners and employees.

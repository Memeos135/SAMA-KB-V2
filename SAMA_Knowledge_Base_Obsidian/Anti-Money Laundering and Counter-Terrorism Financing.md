# Anti-Money Laundering and Counter-Terrorism Financing

This node covers the anti-financial-crime strand of SAMA's Principles of Conduct and Business Ethics for financial institutions, which sits under the heading on combating financial and administrative corruption and addresses money laundering, terrorist financing, suspicious transactions, and bribery/corruption as staff conduct duties. It is an ethics/governance instrument rather than the operative AML/CTF rulebook, so it frames expected employee behaviour and board-approved conduct policy rather than CDD or reporting mechanics. It binds all personnel of SAMA-supervised financial institutions (banks and foreign bank branches, insurance/reinsurance and free-zone insurance firms, finance and finance-lease registration companies, credit bureaus), including directors, executives, contract staff, consultants and third-party workers.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, governance/risk

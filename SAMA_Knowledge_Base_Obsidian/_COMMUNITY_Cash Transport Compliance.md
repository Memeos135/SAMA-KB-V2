# Cash Transport Compliance

## Why this community

Physical cash handling and transport risk: SAMA's supervisory expectations on the hazards of moving or carrying large cash amounts and on adherence to the applicable cash transport requirements. Sits at the interface of operational/security risk and AML/CTF cash-based exposure.

## How members connect

- Both members are circular-level instructions on the same subject matter — cash in transit — and one expressly references the other, forming a single directive thread.
- The awareness circular functions as risk communication supporting the compliance circular's enforceable adherence obligation on regulated entities.
- Practical linkage to cash-intensive activity monitoring under the AML/CTF regime, though the definitional anchors sit in the AML law and rules rather than in these circulars.

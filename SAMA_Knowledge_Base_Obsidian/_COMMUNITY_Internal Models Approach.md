# Internal Models Approach

## Why this community

The internal models alternative for market risk capital: supervisory permission conditions, the expected shortfall measure, risk-factor modellability, and the ongoing backtesting and P&L attribution tests that determine continued use of models.

## How members connect

- The internal models approach is the permissioned regime; its general provisions set the SAMA approval condition and the desk-level qualification tests that must be passed on a continuing basis.
- Capital is built as an aggregation of the modellable component (IMCC, derived from stressed expected shortfall with prescribed liquidity horizons and a reduced risk-factor set) plus the stress scenario charge for non-modellable factors plus the internal-model default risk charge.
- The risk factor eligibility test and its bucketing method are the gateway determining whether a factor is modellable; failure moves the factor into the NMRF regime, so the two are linked by a direct legal consequence.
- Backtesting exceptions (via hypothetical and actual P&L, and the green/amber/red zones) drive the multiplication factor and can force a desk back to the standardised approach; the PLA test uses the Spearman and Kolmogorov-Smirnov metrics comparing risk-theoretical to hypothetical P&L for the same purpose.

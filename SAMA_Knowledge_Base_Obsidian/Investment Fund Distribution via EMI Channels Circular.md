# Investment Fund Distribution via EMI Channels Circular

SAMA circular (Arabic) permitting electronic money institutions to contract with CMA-authorised capital market institutions to distribute units of publicly offered investment funds through their e-wallet applications. It sets conditions: prior notification to SAMA with confirmation that internal policies are updated, compliance with the Law on Payments and Payment Services and its Implementing Regulation (licence conditions and limits), due diligence on the products, contractual allocation of risk and liability to the CMA-supervised institution, a role limited to distribution and document handling (no investment account opening), no sale or redemption via the wallet, subscription completed only after the capital market institution confirms suitability to the customer, and clear disclosure that funds are not payment products. Addressed to payment service providers, principally EMIs.

**Regimes:** payments, consumer protection, governance/risk, other

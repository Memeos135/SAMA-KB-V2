# Protection and Custody of Safeguarded Funds

A SAMA circular reinforcing compliance with the safeguarding-of-funds requirements in Article 93 of the Implementing Regulation of the Payments and Payment Services Law. It adds two obligations: a no-objection request for a safeguarding account must be accompanied by the policies and controls governing the account's management and operation (including named authorities, multi-level control based on risk assessment, involvement of control functions, and restriction of authority to leadership positions), and the PSP must perform and document reconciliation between total e-wallet balances or executed payment transaction values and the safeguarding account balance. It binds payment service providers operating in the Kingdom.

**Regimes:** payments, governance/risk, consumer protection

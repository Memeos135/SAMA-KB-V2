# Targeted Financial Sanctions

## Why this community

The targeted financial sanctions / TFS regime: obligations to screen against designated lists, act without delay on matches, freeze and unfreeze funds, escalate and report, and maintain records and staff competence, anchored in UN Security Council resolutions and their domestic implementation.

## How members connect

- A single core instrument (the TFS Rules) is the parent obligation source; the screening, list-management, alerts, freezing/unfreezing, record-keeping and training items are its operative components rather than independent regimes.
- Obligation chain: list ingestion and maintenance feeds screening; screening output feeds alert handling and escalation; a confirmed match triggers freezing without delay and reporting, with record-keeping evidencing each step.
- Upward citation to UN Security Council resolutions establishes the legal basis and the immediacy standard that domestic rules transpose.
- Cross-regime interaction with AML/CTF: customer identification and CDD data are the input to screening, and TFS record-keeping and training duties sit alongside, but are distinct from, AML retention and training obligations.
- A duplicate/near-identical rules node exists; treat one as authoritative and reconcile before citing.

# Electronic Payments for Charities and NPOs

A SAMA circular relaxing an earlier restriction that barred charitable associations and non-profit foundations from GPRS (mobile) POS terminals, now permitting all SAMA-authorised electronic payment solutions including GPRS POS for these entities. The permission is conditional on the association or foundation obtaining approval from the National Center for Non-Profit Sector Development. It binds banks and payment service providers operating in the Kingdom, effective from issuance, and should be read with the earlier circulars on charity POS restrictions and 4G POS upgrades.

**Regimes:** payments, AML/CTF

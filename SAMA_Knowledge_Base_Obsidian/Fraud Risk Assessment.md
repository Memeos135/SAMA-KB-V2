# Fraud Risk Assessment

Node from SAMA's counter-fraud control requirements covering counter-fraud governance and enterprise-wide fraud risk assessment. It requires Member Organizations to run a risk-based Counter-Fraud Programme with board-level accountability, a quarterly Counter-Fraud Governance Committee, KPIs/KRIs against an approved Fraud Risk Appetite, intelligence monitoring, pre-launch fraud assessment of new products/services, vetting of outsourced counter-fraud resources, and periodic independent audit. It binds all financial institutions and financial service providers regulated by SAMA (Member Organizations), with the fraud risk assessment refreshed periodically and on material internal/external changes.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, AML/CTF, payments, other

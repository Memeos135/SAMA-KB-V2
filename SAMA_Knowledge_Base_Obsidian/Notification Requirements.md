# Notification Requirements

Node covering the documentation/reporting duties and the notification pathway in SAMA's New Banking Products and Services Regulation. It requires banks meeting the stated maturity levels in SAMA's Cyber Security, Counter-Fraud, Business Continuity and IT Governance frameworks (independently validated annually) to notify SAMA of new products and services, launch within 12 months of the acknowledgment receipt, and re-notify before reintroducing anything discontinued for over 12 months; SAMA may request further information or prohibit a product on safety-and-soundness grounds. It also mandates an annual CEO-signed products and services report filed by the compliance function to the Banking Licensing Division by 1 March, plus record retention and a product inventory.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential

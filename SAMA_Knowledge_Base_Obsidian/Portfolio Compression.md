# Portfolio Compression

Covers the portfolio compression requirement for non-centrally cleared OTC derivative portfolios: banks must have policies and procedures to regularly assess and, where appropriate, engage in compression with one or more counterparties, proportionate to their exposure or activity level, considering risk exposure profile, size, volatility and number of outstanding transactions. The node also touches the collateral-related reporting fields (collateralisation status, portfolio collateral, portfolio code, initial margin posted and its currency) used in trade repository reports. It binds SAMA-supervised banks.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential, other

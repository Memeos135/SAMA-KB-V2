# Template MR3: Market Risk Simplified Standardised Approach

A fixed-format Pillar 3 disclosure template within SAMA's Pillar 3 Disclosure Requirements Framework, used to report market risk capital requirements computed under the simplified standardised approach. It sits alongside the other market risk and RWA overview templates (e.g. OV1) and ties into how standardised versus modelled RWA are presented. It binds SAMA-supervised banks subject to the Pillar 3 market risk disclosure regime.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

# Circular on Deeds from Licensed Notaries

A SAMA Banking Policies Department circular informing supervised entities that deeds and instruments issued by notaries licensed by the Ministry of Justice — within their competence (e.g. powers of attorney and their revocation, company contracts and amendments) — must be accepted and treated as enforceable instruments under the Enforcement Law. It notes that the validity of a notary's licence can be verified through the Ministry of Justice notaries portal. It binds banks, insurance and reinsurance companies, finance companies, credit information companies and money exchangers.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, BNPL/finance, data/credit info, other

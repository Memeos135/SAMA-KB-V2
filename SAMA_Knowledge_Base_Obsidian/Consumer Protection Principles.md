# Consumer Protection Principles

The principles component of SAMA's Financial Consumer Protection framework: the general standards financial institutions must observe in every consumer dealing, including equitable and fair treatment (with special attention to vulnerable groups), disclosure and transparency of product information and pricing, and consumer education and awareness. Read together with the general conduct rules that operationalise them (documents provision, no post-contract fee increases, fee schedules, application notification timelines).

**Regimes:** consumer protection, governance/risk

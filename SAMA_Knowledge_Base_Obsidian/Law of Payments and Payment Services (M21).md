# Law of Payments and Payment Services (M/21)

The Law of Payments and Payment Services (Royal Decree M/21) makes SAMA the competent authority for regulating, supervising and overseeing payment systems and their operators in the Kingdom, together with its Implementing Regulation. Oversight covers existing and planned systems, assessment against safety and efficiency objectives, and standards aligned with the CPMI-IOSCO Principles for Financial Market Infrastructures. Binds payment system operators and participants subject to SAMA oversight.

**Regimes:** payments, governance/risk

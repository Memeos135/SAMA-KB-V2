# Regulatory Consolidation Templates

## Why this community

Reconciliation between accounting (financial statement) scope of consolidation and the regulatory scope used for capital purposes, including how securitisation exposures are mapped into that regulatory perimeter.

## How members connect

- LI2 explains the sources of differences against the mapping set out in LI1, so LI1 is the anchor disclosure and LI2 the explanatory bridge.
- SEC1 references LI1 because securitisation exposures must be reported by reference to the regulatory consolidation perimeter established there.
- Shared defined terms - scope of regulatory consolidation, carrying values under accounting versus regulatory treatment - govern all three templates.
- Together they form an obligation chain: define the perimeter, explain the deltas, then disclose specific exposure classes on that basis.

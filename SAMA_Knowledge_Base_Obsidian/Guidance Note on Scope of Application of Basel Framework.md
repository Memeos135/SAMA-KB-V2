# Guidance Note on Scope of Application of Basel Framework

A SAMA guidance note clarifying the scope of application of the Basel Framework in Saudi Arabia — risk-based capital and buffers, leverage, liquidity, large exposures, Pillar 2 supervisory review and Pillar 3 disclosure. It confirms that, since Basel II (Circular BCS290, June 2006), local banks apply SAMA's Basel requirements on both a standalone and consolidated basis, including at holding-company level and every tier of the banking group, with prudential returns aligned to that scope. It binds local banks and their banking groups.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

# Table CCA - Main Features of Regulatory Capital Instruments

A Pillar 3 disclosure table requiring banks to publish the main features of each regulatory capital instrument they issue (and, where relevant, TLAC-eligible instruments), supporting market assessment of capital quality and loss absorbency. The surrounding instructions also cover related capital disclosures, including capital distribution constraint triggers and the CET1 regulatory adjustments and deductions cross-referenced to SAMA's capital adequacy rules. Binds banks subject to SAMA's Pillar 3 disclosure requirements.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

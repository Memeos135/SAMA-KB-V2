# Business Continuity Management

Addresses business continuity expectations within SAMA's outsourcing framework: banks must ensure outsourcing does not compromise continuity and must hold a separate contingency plan for each Material outsourcing covering sudden termination or provider failure. Business continuity plans must document alternative third-party service providers or the process and timeline for selecting them, and set out procedures for bringing a Material outsourced function back in-house. It binds banks subject to the Rules on Outsourcing, and links to SAMA's and auditors' unrestricted access rights and to ongoing relationship monitoring proportionate to materiality.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential

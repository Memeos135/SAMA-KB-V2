# Offering of Financial Derivatives Products

Node on the additional conditions for offering financial derivatives and structured products under the New Banking Products and Services Regulation. Banks must disclose profit/loss scenarios over the product horizon, satisfy themselves of customer awareness, prior investment experience, suitability against the customer's objectives and risk appetite, and obtain signed terms; they must not recommend a derivative unless satisfied it suits that customer. New derivative products must also be shown to have a bona fide economic purpose (not mere speculation, leverage or regulatory arbitrage) with identified intended users, and the bank must demonstrate organizational capacity to manage the resulting risks.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, consumer protection, governance/risk

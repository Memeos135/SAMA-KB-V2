# Regulatory CVA Calculations

Defines regulatory CVA and CVA risk and delimits the scope of the CVA capital charge: it excludes the bank's own default, imposes constraints on the accounting-based calculation, and covers derivatives (other than those with a qualified CCP or otherwise exempt) plus fair-valued SFTs where CVA loss exposure is material. Capital is computed for a stand-alone CVA portfolio comprising covered transactions and eligible hedges. All banks with covered transactions in either the banking or trading book are bound; BA-CVA is the default approach and SA-CVA requires prior SAMA approval, with materiality assessments for SFTs to be justified to SAMA.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

# Guidelines on the ILAAP

SAMA's updated Guidelines on the Internal Liquidity Adequacy Assessment Plan (ILAAP), superseding the earlier guidelines issued under circular no. 381000120488 (3/12/1438AH) and effective from the 2021 ILAAP submission. They define the ILAAP as the bank's processes for identifying, measuring, managing and monitoring liquidity under SAMA liquidity risk management regulations, and set expectations on governance, board approval and challenge, risk appetite alignment, and strategic and liquidity planning. They bind banks supervised by SAMA, with ultimate responsibility resting on the Board of Directors and senior management; the guidelines are a starting point for supervisory dialogue, not an exhaustive standard.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

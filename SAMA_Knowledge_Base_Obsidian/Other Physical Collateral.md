# Other Physical Collateral

This node addresses other physical collateral and the residual collateral categories as they appear in the counterparty credit risk and CRM disclosure templates (CCR5 and CR3), covering cash, sovereign and government agency debt, corporate bonds, equities and "other collateral" posted or received in derivatives and securities financing transactions. It clarifies the reporting of both legs of a transaction and the allocation of multi-secured exposures. It binds all banks subject to the Pillar 3 templates and CRM recognition rules.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

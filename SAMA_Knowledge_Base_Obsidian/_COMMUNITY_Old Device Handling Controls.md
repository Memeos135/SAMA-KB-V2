# Old Device Handling Controls

## Why this community

Operational controls for banks' handling, retirement and disposal of old or damaged equipment, addressing residual risk in decommissioned assets.

## How members connect

- Single-member cluster: a SAMA controls instrument directed at local banks, sitting below general banking supervision requirements.
- Relevant to information security, customer data confidentiality and physical asset control obligations, though no cross-references are captured here.
- Treat as a specific operational duty rather than a definitional source for other regimes.

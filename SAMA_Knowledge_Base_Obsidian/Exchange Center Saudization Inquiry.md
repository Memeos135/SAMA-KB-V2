# Exchange Center Saudization Inquiry

Short SAMA circular answering exchange centers' queries on whether the child of a Saudi mother counts toward Saudization ratios, supplementing the earlier circular restricting all jobs in the exchange center sector to Saudis and requiring training/qualification of Saudi staff within one year. It confirms that categories treated as Saudis under decisions of the Ministry of Labour and Social Development are counted in Saudization percentages, must be disclosed in data submitted to SAMA, and that exchange centers must keep verifying and complying with whatever the Ministry issues on the point. It binds exchange centers of categories (A) and (B) operating in the Kingdom.

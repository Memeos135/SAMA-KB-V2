# General Account Rules

Operational rules for the DTFC General Account and linked Term Deposit Accounts. Credits are restricted to funds from the accountholder's own bank account(s) or their Term Deposit Account, with name-matching against the remitter (funds returned if mismatched) and recording of remitter name, IBAN and value date; withdrawals must go back to a source account of the accountholder, and transfers are permitted only between General Accounts in the same name. Term Deposit Accounts have a six-month minimum tenor and prescribed deal data, including Sharia-compliant commodity details. Binds deposit-taking finance companies; relevant to source-of-funds and account-integrity controls.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, AML/CTF, governance/risk, consumer protection

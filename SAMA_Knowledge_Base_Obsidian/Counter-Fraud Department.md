# Counter-Fraud Department

Sets the requirement for Member Organisations to establish and maintain a Counter-Fraud Department owning day-to-day operation of the Counter-Fraud Programme, with a minimum mandate covering policy compliance monitoring, enterprise fraud controls, Fraud Risk Assessment, intelligence analysis and sharing with SAMA and the sector, system tuning, investigations and root-cause analysis, Fraud Risk Appetite monitoring and crisis escalation, and periodic senior management reporting. It also requires alignment with Cyber Security and Financial Crime functions, which is the main cross-regime touchpoint with AML/CTF. Binds SAMA-supervised Member Organisations.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, AML/CTF

# Investment Governance Requirements

The governance section of the Banks Investment Rules allocates responsibility for investment oversight: the Board of Directors (or, for foreign bank branches, the head/regional office authority overseeing the branch) is ultimately accountable and approves the internal Investment Policy and Strategy, supported by a management-level investment committee. It prescribes minimum content of the Investment Policy, including controls and delegation of authority, eligibility criteria, prudential exposure limits and limits on local and international investments. It binds banks supervised by SAMA.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential

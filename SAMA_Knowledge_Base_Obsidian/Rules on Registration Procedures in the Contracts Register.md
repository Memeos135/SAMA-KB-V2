# Rules on Registration Procedures in the Contracts Register

Governor-approved controls, agreed with the Minister of Justice, on registration procedures in the finance lease Contracts Register — the data required for registration and the rules and procedures allowing third parties to view the register — issued under the Finance Lease Law and its Implementing Regulation. They cover definitions (registration, registered right, registrar, head and clerk of the register, executive deed extract), obligations of the licensed registration company to maintain and safeguard register data under SAMA supervision, and the lessor's duty to register contracts and amendments within a set number of business days via the register's electronic channel. They bind banks, finance companies and licensed finance lease contract registration companies operating in the Kingdom.

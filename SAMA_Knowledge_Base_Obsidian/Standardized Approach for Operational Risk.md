# Standardized Approach for Operational Risk

The single permitted methodology under the SAMA operational risk framework: operational risk capital equals the Business Indicator Component times the Internal Loss Multiplier, with marginal coefficients applied at defined BI thresholds and internal loss data feeding the ILM. It also governs which losses enter the data set, including the SAR 44,600 collection threshold (which SAMA may raise to SAR 446,000 for banks with a BI above SAR 4.46 billion) and the treatment of credit- and market-risk-related losses. Binds domestic banks calculating minimum regulatory capital.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

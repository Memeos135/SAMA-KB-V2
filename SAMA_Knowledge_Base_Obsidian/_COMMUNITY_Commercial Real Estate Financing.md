# Commercial Real Estate Financing

## Why this community

Scope definition distinguishing commercial real estate financing from other real estate finance products. Definitional scope determines which prudential, reporting and consumer-protection rules apply to a given exposure.

## How members connect

- Single-member cluster: a definitional SAMA circular whose main legal effect is to fix the boundary of a defined term.
- Acts as a gateway instrument — classification under it drives applicability of real estate finance regulation, exposure limits and reporting lines.
- Consumer-protection cross-cut: exposures classified as commercial typically fall outside consumer-facing real estate finance protections, so misclassification carries dual-regime risk.

# Financial Lease Contract for Individuals

A SAMA circular issuing the mandatory standard-form vehicle finance lease (Ijarah) contract for individuals, adopted under the Banking Control Law, the Finance Companies Control Law and the Finance Lease Law and its Implementing Regulation (which empowers SAMA to approve model lease forms protecting the parties' rights). Lessors must use the attached model form by the stated deadline and may not conclude contracts that depart from or amend it; it binds banks and finance companies acting as lessors to individual (retail) lessees.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, consumer protection

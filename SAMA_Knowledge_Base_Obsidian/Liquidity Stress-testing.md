# Liquidity Stress-testing

An annexure-level component of SAMA's ILAAP guidelines setting out liquidity stress-testing expectations for banks, covering scenario and sensitivity testing, identification of liquidity strain sources (confidence loss, contagion, external and uncorrelated events), and institution-specific, market-wide and combined scenarios. It also frames how banks must document their liquidity-specific stress-testing framework, governance and challenge of assumptions, and feed results into risk appetite, strategic planning and the contingency funding plan. It binds banks supervised by SAMA and their governing bodies, which set liquidity risk tolerance.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential

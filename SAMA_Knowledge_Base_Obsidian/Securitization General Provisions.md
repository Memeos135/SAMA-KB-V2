# Securitization: General Provisions

General provisions of SAMA's securitisation capital framework: defines securitisation exposures (including ABS, MBS, credit enhancements, liquidity facilities, swaps, tranched cover and reserve accounts), resecuritisation, eligible underlying pools, and when a bank is treated as originator. It also covers risk-weight floors and adjustments for resecuritisation exposures under the standardised approach. Binds banks holding or originating securitisation positions for regulatory capital purposes.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

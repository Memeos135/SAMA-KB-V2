# LEI Issuance/Update/Renewal Agreement via Financial Institution

Template bilateral agreement between the Saudi Credit Bureau (SIMAH), acting as the GLEIF-accredited Local Operating Unit for Legal Entity Identifiers, and a SAMA-supervised financial institution that grants credit to legal entities, enabling the institution to arrange LEI issuance, updating and renewal for its corporate clients. It sets fees per issuance/renewal request (VAT excluded), monthly invoice settlement terms, confidentiality and data-protection duties under the Credit Information Law, its Implementing Regulations, SAMA instructions and national cyber security requirements, plus amendment, severability, notice, governing law and dispute provisions. It is entered into pursuant to a SAMA circular permitting banks to sign the agreement with SIMAH.

**Regimes:** data/credit info, governance/risk, banking prudential

# Exchange Job Localization

## Why this community

Saudization/localization and job-restriction requirements applying to money exchange centers, including the prohibition on staff holding dual roles.

## How members connect

- Single-member cluster: a standalone supervisory instruction directed at licensed exchange businesses rather than part of a definitional chain.
- Sits in the licensing/conduct-of-business regime for exchangers; consequence is staffing and role-segregation compliance, with implications for internal control and AML oversight duties.

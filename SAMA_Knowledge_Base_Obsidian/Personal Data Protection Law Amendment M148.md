# Personal Data Protection Law Amendment M/148

Royal Decree M/148 approving amendments to the Personal Data Protection Law (originally issued by Royal Decree M/19), together with the enabling Council of Ministers resolution. It revises core defined terms (destruction, disclosure, sensitive data, data subject), restates the data subject rights article, and touches the cross-border transfer adequacy assessment and the fees provision, with SDAIA as the competent authority. It binds all controllers and processors of personal data in KSA, including SAMA-supervised financial institutions, and applies from the effective date of the amendments.

**Regimes:** data/credit info

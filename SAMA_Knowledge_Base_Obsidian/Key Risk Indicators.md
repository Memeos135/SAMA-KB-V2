# Key Risk Indicators

A concept node within SAMA's Counter-Fraud Framework covering Key Risk Indicators used to measure a Member Organisation's position against its approved Fraud Risk Appetite. It requires KRIs built on a documented methodology, mapped to risks identified in the Fraud Risk Assessment, forward-looking rather than merely loss-counting, with defined thresholds showing whether results sit below, at, or above appetite, and complete, accurate, timely metrics. It binds Member Organisations subject to the Framework, with Board/Senior Management oversight of appetite breaches.

**Regimes:** governance/risk

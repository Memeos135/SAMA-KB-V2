# BCBS Principles for Sound Stress Testing Practices and Supervision

This node covers the interaction between the SAMA Rules on Stress Testing and the BCBS "Principles for Sound Stress Testing Practices and Supervision" (May 2009), circulated to banks by SAMA circular of 2 August 2009. Banks must apply the BCBS guidance in addition to the Rules when designing and implementing stress testing programs, and must approach SAMA where the two are inconsistent. It also records the effective date provisions: immediate force, full framework compliance by 30 June 2012, and reporting from the half-year ending 30 June 2012 within three months of each half-year end.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential

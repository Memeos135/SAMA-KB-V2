# Template CVA2 - Full Basic Approach for CVA

Pillar 3 template CVA2 covers disclosure of RWA under the full basic approach for CVA risk (the hedge-recognising variant of BA-CVA), sitting alongside CVA1 in the CVA section of SAMA's Pillar 3 Disclosure Requirements Framework. Like the rest of that section, it applies only where the counterparty credit risk materiality threshold is met, and it binds banks measuring CVA RWA under the full BA-CVA.

**Regimes:** banking prudential

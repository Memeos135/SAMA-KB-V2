# Capital to Risk Weighted Assets Return

Covers the capital adequacy regime for DTFCs (Chapter 4) together with the completion instructions for the Capital to Risk Weighted Assets return. It sets the 20% capital adequacy requirement and record-keeping to compute it, SAMA's power to impose higher individual minimum ratios (e.g. for capital deficiency, high risk exposure, poor asset quality, rapid growth, or holding-company contagion), definitions of core and supplementary capital (including term subordinated debt and statutory loan loss reserve capped at 1.25% of RWA, supplementary capped at core), and the risk-weighting of on- and off-balance sheet exposures. Binds every DTFC; SAMA determines compliance.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, BNPL/finance, governance/risk

# SAMA Directive to Update Credit Granting Policies of Finance Companies

A SAMA circular directing finance companies to update their credit granting policies and procedures to keep pace with rapid changes in the finance sector and the activities being financed, adopt prudential measures to limit rising non-performing loans, and ensure provisions and collateral are proportionate to the risks of the activity. Issued under the Finance Companies Control Law and its Implementing Regulation. It binds licensed finance companies and SAMA-licensed crowdfunding entities.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, governance/risk

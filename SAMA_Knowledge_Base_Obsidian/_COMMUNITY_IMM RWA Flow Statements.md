# IMM RWA Flow Statements

## Why this community

Pillar 3 disclosure of movements in counterparty credit risk RWA where the bank uses the Internal Model Method, isolating period-on-period drivers of capital consumption rather than end-state balances.

## How members connect

- Single-member cluster: the flow-statement template sits in the counterparty credit risk (CCR) family of disclosure templates and depends on prior supervisory approval to use IMM.
- Legally downstream of the capital adequacy/CCR measurement rules — the template does not create the measurement obligation, it fixes the reporting format and periodicity for banks already within IMM scope.
- Flow logic (opening balance, drivers of change, closing balance) links it analytically to other RWA flow disclosures, so consistency of the closing figure with headline RWA disclosure is the practical compliance point.

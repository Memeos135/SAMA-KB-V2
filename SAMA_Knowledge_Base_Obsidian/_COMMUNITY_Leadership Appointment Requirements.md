# Leadership Appointment Requirements

## Why this community

Updated requirements governing appointment to senior leadership positions in SAMA-supervised entities (fit-and-proper style controls and supervisory clearance).

## How members connect

- Single-node cluster within the governance/risk regime, amending previously issued appointment criteria.
- Obligation chain: entity nominates, criteria are assessed, supervisory non-objection conditions the validity of the appointment.
- Decision impact: appointments made without meeting the updated criteria or clearance are exposed to challenge and supervisory action.

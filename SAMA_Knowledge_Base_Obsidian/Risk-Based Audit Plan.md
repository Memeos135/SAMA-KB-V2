# Risk-Based Audit Plan

This node addresses the risk-based internal audit plan requirement within SAMA's Principles of Internal Audit for local banks, including the underlying risk assessment methodology, the inventory of auditable units and entities, and the audit cycle. It governs how the Internal Audit Unit prioritises and schedules coverage and how the plan is approved and reported to the Audit Committee. It binds locally licensed banks and their internal audit functions.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential

# LEI Reporting Data

## Why this community

Identification and reporting data standards for derivative trade reporting — mandatory counterparty identification using the ISO 17442 LEI and the field-level data and reporting-obligation rules that govern what must be submitted.

## How members connect

- Definitional dependency: the counterparty data table and the reporting-obligation rules both incorporate the ISO 17442 LEI as the required identifier, so LEI eligibility/renewal status conditions reportability.
- Hierarchy: the reporting obligation rules (who reports, when, on what basis) sit above the appendix data tables that specify the fields to be populated.
- Cross-regime read-across: LEI-based entity identification interacts with KYC/KYB customer identification, though the LEI requirement is a reporting standard, not a substitute for CDD.

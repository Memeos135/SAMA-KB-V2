# Conflict of Interest

This node covers the conflict-of-interest strand of SAMA's conduct and business-ethics framework for financial institutions, including related themes of dealing with stakeholders, gifts and hospitality, remuneration and incentives, and reporting actual or potential breaches. It sets minimum behavioural rules that the institution's board must implement through its own conduct policy, with consequences for non-compliance. It binds directors, board committee members, executives, employees (permanent and contract), consultants and third-party personnel of SAMA-supervised financial institutions.

**Regimes:** governance/risk

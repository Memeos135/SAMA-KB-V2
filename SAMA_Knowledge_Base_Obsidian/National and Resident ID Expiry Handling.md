# National and Resident ID Expiry Handling

SAMA circular to finance companies (transmitting earlier April 2020 email instructions) on how to treat expired National IDs and Iqamas (resident IDs) for customer due diligence purposes. It relies on the Ministry of Interior's two-month automatic extension of expired National IDs and the Passports Directorate's three-month extension of Iqamas for private-sector expatriates, and permits such IDs to be treated as valid when applying CDD requirements under the AML Law and its Implementing Regulations, Article 17 of the CTF Implementing Regulations and SAMA's AML/CTF Guide. It binds finance companies operating in the Kingdom, effective from the date of the email transmission.

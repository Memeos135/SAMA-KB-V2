# SWOT Analysis

An analytical component of the workout process: the bank assesses account strengths, weaknesses, opportunities and threats alongside financial analysis (liquidity, solvency/leverage and profitability ratios, balance sheet composition, and cash-flow-based viability assessment) to determine borrower viability and shape the restructuring plan. For MSME borrowers lacking reliable audited financials, the emphasis shifts to cash-flow analysis and reasonableness testing. Directed at bank workout units, which must be independent of the original credit approval.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential

# Written Credit Derivatives Treatment

Defines written credit derivatives broadly for leverage ratio purposes (any instrument or option under which the bank effectively provides credit protection) and sets the conditions under which purchased credit protection may offset the effective notional amount of sold protection. Conditions include identical reference names (same legal entity), same or more conservative material terms, seniority/pari passu ranking, strike price limits, and full-pool coverage where protection is bought on a pool. It binds banks calculating the leverage ratio exposure measure for sold credit protection.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

# Liquidity Coverage Ratio

## Why this community

Quantitative disclosure of the Liquidity Coverage Ratio - high-quality liquid assets against net cash outflows over a 30-day stress horizon.

## How members connect

- Single-member cluster implementing the LCR minimum standard through a prescribed disclosure format, with averaging and observation-period conventions that affect how compliance is assessed.
- Depends on the liquidity regime's defined terms (HQLA levels, run-off and inflow rates, net cash outflows) set in the underlying liquidity rules.
- Read together with the qualitative liquidity management table, which supplies the explanatory context for reported figures.

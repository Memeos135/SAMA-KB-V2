# Simplified Due Diligence Measures

This node covers simplified due diligence (SDD), permitted where ML/TF risk is assessed as low and there is no suspicion of ML/TF, based on the AML Law and CTF Law Implementing Regulations. SDD is a streamlined, proportionate application of CDD rather than an exemption: identity of the customer (or person acting on their behalf) and the beneficial owner must still be verified from reliable independent sources, and the nature and purpose of the relationship and the ownership/control structure of legal persons must still be understood. The simplified measures applied must be set out in the institution's approved AML/CTF policies and procedures.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF

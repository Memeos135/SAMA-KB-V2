# NSFR Disclosure Template (NSF)

The NSFR common disclosure template with its line-by-line completion instructions, mapping asset and liability categories to available stable funding and required stable funding factors (e.g. performing loans, residential mortgages, non-HQLA securities, commodities, initial margin, derivative assets and liabilities, off-balance sheet items). Rows are compulsory for all banks, figures are quarter-end observations, and shading conventions and subtotal relationships are prescribed. Binds banks completing NSFR disclosures under SAMA's Pillar 3 framework.

**Regimes:** banking prudential

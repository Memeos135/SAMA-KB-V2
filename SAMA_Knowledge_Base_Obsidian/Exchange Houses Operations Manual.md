# Exchange Houses Operations Manual

SAMA's Manual for Organizing the Business Procedures of Exchange Houses (First Edition, February 2015), supplementing the Rules Governing Money Changing Business and existing SAMA and AML/CTF instructions. It covers licensing categories (Category A permitted to issue/accept remittances, Category B limited to currency exchange and traveller's cheques), customer classification (membership customers vs. walk-in/transient customers), customer acceptance policies, KYC and CDD, remittance and bank cheque operations, cross-border transport of cash and negotiable instruments, correspondent banking, security systems, and staffing and training. Binds licensed exchange houses operating in the Kingdom; it also restates the prohibitions on deposit-taking and on non-banks conducting banking business.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, payments, governance/risk, banking prudential

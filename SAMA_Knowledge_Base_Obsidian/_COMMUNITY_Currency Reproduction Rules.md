# Currency Reproduction Rules

## Why this community

Controls on reproducing images of Saudi currency (banknotes and coins) for advertising, media or other non-monetary use, protecting currency integrity and reducing counterfeiting risk.

## How members connect

- Single-instrument community: the reproduction rules stand alone as SAMA's issuing-authority measure over the national currency.
- Obligation runs to any party reproducing currency imagery, with SAMA prior-approval and format conditions as the operative constraint.

# Basic Approach for CVA (BA-CVA)

Covers the basic approach to CVA capital (BA-CVA) as the default methodology banks must apply unless SAMA approves use of SA-CVA. Relevant to identifying which CVA calculation approach a bank may use, the supervisory approval gate, and the related Pillar 3 reporting of standardised versus modelled RWA components.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

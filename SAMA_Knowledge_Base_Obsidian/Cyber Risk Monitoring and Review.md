# Cyber Risk Monitoring and Review

The closing stage of the SAMA Cyber Security Framework risk management cycle: ongoing monitoring of cyber security risk treatment and review of control effectiveness, with periodic review and audit of compliance with the controls. It requires the cyber security risk management process to be defined, approved, implemented and aligned with the Member Organization's enterprise risk management process, protecting confidentiality, integrity and availability of information assets. Applies to Member Organizations supervised by SAMA.

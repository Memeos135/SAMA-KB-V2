# SAMA Internal Audit Principles (Banks)

Instrument-level node for SAMA's Internal Audit Principles for local banks operating in the Kingdom (first issue), issued under SAMA's supervisory powers. It sets governance expectations for internal audit: board, audit committee and executive management duties; independence, objectivity and professional ethics of the unit; audit policy, structure, reporting lines, SAMA non-objection to appointing or changing the head of the unit; risk assessment methodology, risk-based plan, quality assurance, periodic reporting and record keeping; and audit coverage of subsidiaries. Binds locally licensed banks.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential

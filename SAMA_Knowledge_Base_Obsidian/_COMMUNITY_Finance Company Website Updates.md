# Finance Company Website Updates

## Why this community

Obligation on finance companies to keep public-facing channels (websites and social media) current and accurate, as an extension of disclosure and transparency duties toward consumers.

## How members connect

- Standalone conduct-of-business requirement; the legal hook is accurate disclosure of products, terms and licensing status to the public.
- Interacts with consumer protection principles on misleading information and with licensing-status representation rules.

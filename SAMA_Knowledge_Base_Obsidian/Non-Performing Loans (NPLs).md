# Non-Performing Loans (NPLs)

A concept node from SAMA's problem loan / NPL guidelines addressing the identification, monitoring and resolution of non-performing loans to large corporates and MSMEs, including restructuring, liquidation, foreclosed assets, provisioning and coverage levels. It sets expectations for granular NPL KPIs, portfolio monitoring, peer benchmarking and senior management reporting, and distinguishes borrower 'ability to pay' from 'willingness to pay'. It is directed at banks operating in the Kingdom and their workout and senior management functions.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential

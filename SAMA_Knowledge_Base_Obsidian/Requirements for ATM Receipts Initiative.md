# Requirements for ATM Receipts Initiative

A SAMA/Saudi Payments requirements document for the ATM Receipts initiative, aimed at reducing cardholder reliance on printed ATM receipts while keeping paper receipts available on request. It standardises ATM home-screen workflow across on-us and off-us ATMs for the four principal transactions (cash withdrawal, balance inquiry, mini statement, cash deposit) and splits requirements into cash and non-cash transaction streams. It binds mada Members (acquirers/ATM operators); it does not cover certification procedures or terms and conditions.

**Regimes:** payments, consumer protection

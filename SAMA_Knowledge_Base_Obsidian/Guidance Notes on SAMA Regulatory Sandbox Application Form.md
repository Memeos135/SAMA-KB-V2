# Guidance Notes on SAMA Regulatory Sandbox Application Form

SAMA guidance notes accompanying the Regulatory Sandbox application form, explaining the four-stage operating model: application submission and eligibility assessment (60 business days), operational readiness against the Assessment Criteria (120 days) leading to a temporary permission (Letter of Authorisation), live testing for six to twelve months, and exit/graduation toward a full regulatory licence. It clarifies that the initial approval letter permits company formation as a FinTech but does not authorise commencing operations or onboarding customers. Addressed to innovators/FinTech applicants seeking to test in the Sandbox under SAMA supervision.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, payments, other

- **What this link tells you:** When assessing a Regulatory Sandbox application, treat AML/CTF capability as a live licensing-stage question rather than something deferred to full authorisation. The sandbox guidance directs applicants to evidence how the tested activity will be conducted consistently with the AML/CTF regime, whose obligations flow from the Anti-Money Laundering Law as parent statute and apply to the activity itself, not to the permission label attached to it. Practically, a sandbox permit does not suspend customer due diligence, reporting or record-keeping duties, so weak AML controls are a proper ground to refuse, condition or limit the test.

# Credit Valuation Adjustment (CVA) Framework

Defines the Credit Valuation Adjustment (CVA) risk framework: what regulatory CVA is (counterparty-level adjustment to default risk-free prices of derivatives and SFTs), how it differs from accounting CVA, the scope of covered transactions in both banking and trading book, and conversion of CVA capital requirements into RWA via the 12.5 multiplier. Applies to all banks with covered derivative and fair-valued SFT exposures, with SAMA determining materiality of SFT CVA exposures.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

# Development Fund Loans Consideration Circular

A SAMA circular confirming that banks and finance companies must take into account loans granted to individual customers by the development funds and development banks affiliated with the National Development Fund when assessing affordability, relying on reports from licensed credit information companies. Deductions by those development lenders must be included in the customer's monthly debt-burden ratio, applying the Responsible Lending Principles requirement to count government-provided loans within monthly obligations. It binds banks and finance companies operating in the Kingdom.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, consumer protection, data/credit info

# Previous RMF for Shari'ah Compliant Banking (circular 43038156)

The earlier Risk Management Framework for Shari'ah Compliant Banking issued by SAMA circular No. 43038156 dated 27-04-1443H (02-12-2021G). It has been updated and superseded by the current Risk Management Framework for Shari'ah Compliant Banking, so it is relevant mainly for historical or transitional analysis of obligations applicable before the new framework took effect. It bound the same population of SAMA-licensed domestic banks conducting Shari'ah compliant banking activities.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential

# Vision 2030

A concept node anchoring Vision 2030 as the policy driver cited in SAMA's Regulatory Sandbox Framework, alongside the Fintech Strategy approved by Council of Ministers decision No. 574 (25 May 2022). It is context rather than an obligation: it explains why SAMA operates a live testing environment for innovative financial products/services with real consumers under defined periods and controls, and frames the four sandbox exit outcomes (new framework, amended framework, amended rules/instructions, or confirmation that no SAMA approval is needed). It binds no one directly, but shapes how Innovators and licensed entities plan licensing pathways after testing.

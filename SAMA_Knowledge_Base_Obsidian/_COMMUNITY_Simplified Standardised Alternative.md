# Simplified Standardised Alternative

## Why this community

Capital adequacy measurement for market risk: the reduced-complexity option that certain banks may apply instead of the full standardised approach.

## How members connect

- Single defined term operating as a scope/eligibility carve-out within the market risk capital regime.
- Its meaning depends on cross-reference to the standardised approach it substitutes, so it cannot be read as a free-standing rule.
- Use of the alternative is typically conditional on supervisory eligibility criteria and SAMA acceptance rather than firm election alone.

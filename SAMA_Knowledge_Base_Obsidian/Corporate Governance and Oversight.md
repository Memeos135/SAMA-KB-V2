# Corporate Governance and Oversight

Concerns the due diligence duty attaching to reliance on external ratings for risk weighting: banks must independently satisfy themselves that an external rating conservatively reflects the creditworthiness of the counterparty or covered bond, must notch up one bucket where their analysis shows higher risk, and may never notch down. It also sets the scope of the corporate exposure class (including securities firms and other financial institutions treated as corporates, exclusion of individuals, specialised lending) and the associated base risk weight table. Binds banks applying the standardised approach to credit risk.

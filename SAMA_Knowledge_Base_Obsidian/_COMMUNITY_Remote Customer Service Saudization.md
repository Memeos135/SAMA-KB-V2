# Remote Customer Service Saudization

## Why this community

Labour-market localisation requirement applied to remote customer service functions, relevant where financial institutions outsource or offshore customer-facing contact activity.

## How members connect

- Standalone ministerial decision originating outside SAMA but binding on supervised entities as employers.
- Cross-regime relevance: interacts with outsourcing governance and consumer protection expectations on service quality and customer contact channels.
- Compliance consequence sits at workforce composition level, distinct from SAMA's own licensing or conduct conditions.

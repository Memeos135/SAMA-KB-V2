# Updating Finance Company Websites and Social Media

A SAMA circular directing finance companies to keep their websites continuously updated, to verify (or otherwise delete) their social media accounts, and to publish and maintain accurate branch locations and business hours on digital mapping platforms. It sets a one-month compliance window and requires reporting of steps taken to SAMA's liaison officer within two weeks. Issued under SAMA's supervisory mandate and framed as a customer-protection measure binding on finance companies.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, BNPL/finance

# Template CR3 (Credit Risk Mitigation)

A Pillar 3 template giving an overview of credit risk mitigation techniques, showing exposures unsecured versus secured by collateral, financial guarantees and credit derivatives. It sits in the credit risk disclosure block (with Table CRC on qualitative CRM disclosure) covering items subject to credit risk RWA, excluding securitisation and counterparty credit risk positions which are disclosed elsewhere. Mandatory for banks under SAMA's Pillar 3 disclosure requirements.

**Regimes:** banking prudential

# Residual Risk Add-On (RRAO)

Concept node for the Residual Risk Add-On, the third component of SAMA's standardised approach to market risk, introduced because not all market risks can be captured by the sensitivities-based method and DRC without undue complexity. It applies a simple add-on to specified instruments carrying exotic or other residual risks (including certain instruments with embedded prepayment features and securitisation tranches), and interacts with the recognition rules for internal CVA risk transfers, which require an exactly matching external hedge with an eligible third-party protection provider. Binds all banks calculating market risk capital under the standardised approach.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

# Implementing Regulation to the AML Law

The Implementing Regulation to the Anti-Money Laundering Law (2017) elaborates the AML Law's definitions (Person, Transaction, Economic Resources, Correspondent Relationship) and the operative CDD regime. Key provisions address failure to complete due diligence (no account opening, termination of existing relationships, and consideration of an STR), the tipping-off exception permitting omission of CDD with a reasoned STR instead, and the conditions for relying on a third-party financial institution or DNFBP for customer/beneficial owner identification and verification. It binds financial institutions and designated non-financial businesses and professions, with ultimate CDD responsibility remaining with the relying entity.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF

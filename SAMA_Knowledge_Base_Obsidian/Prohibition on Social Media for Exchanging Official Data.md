# Prohibition on Social Media for Exchanging Official Data

Short SAMA circular (issued by the Payment Systems and Companies Control Department) prohibiting all financial institutions from using social media or instant-messaging applications, directly or indirectly, to exchange any official data or documents. Such exchanges must instead take place through official, secure and approved channels, on grounds of data protection, customer confidence and operational risk. Effective from its date of issue and binding on all financial institutions.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, data/credit info, payments

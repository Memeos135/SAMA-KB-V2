# Stress Testing Program

Node setting the requirement for a comprehensive stress testing programme, both for counterparty credit risk (complete trade capture, at least monthly single-factor exposure stress tests, at least quarterly multifactor and joint exposure/creditworthiness stress tests) and for banks using the internal models approach for market risk (desk-level and bank-wide scenarios). It also requires routine reporting of results to senior management and periodic reporting to the board. Binds banks using these approaches under SAMA capital rules.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

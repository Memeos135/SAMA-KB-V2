# Template IRRBB1 - Quantitative Information on IRRBB

Quantitative Pillar 3 template requiring banks to disclose measured changes in economic value of equity (∆EVE) and net interest income (∆NII) under the prescribed supervisory interest rate shock scenarios, using the bank's own internal measurement system. It is paired with the qualitative Table IRRBBA and is disclosed annually. It applies to banks falling within the IRRBB scope of application under SAMA's Pillar 3 disclosure framework.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

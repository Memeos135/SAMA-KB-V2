# Maximum Exposure Limits

This node sets the binding concentration caps under the LEX Rules: exposures to a single non-bank counterparty (excluding individuals, sole proprietorships and commercial undertakings majority owned by the Saudi Government) are capped at 15% of eligible capital, with a 25% limit applied to non-banking financial-sector subsidiaries, and it lists the exemptions. Exempted items include direct exposures to the Saudi Government, SAMA, Entities Connected with the Saudi Government, GCC central governments and central banks, government-guaranteed portions, intra-day interbank exposures, consolidated intra-group entities and central counterparties. All non-exempt exposures are fully subject to the limits; banks must still control and monitor exempted exposures.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

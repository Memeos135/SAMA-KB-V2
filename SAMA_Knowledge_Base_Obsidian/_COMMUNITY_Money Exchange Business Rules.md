# Money Exchange Business Rules

## Why this community

Licensing, conduct and prudential-supervisory framework for money exchange centers, plus SAMA's ongoing supervisory reporting and control-testing expectations for that sector.

## How members connect

- Clear hierarchy: the Rules Regulating Money Exchange Business is the base instrument, with each circular amending, clarifying or adding obligations under it.
- Reporting chain — supervisory data forms are the obligation, the RMS portal is the prescribed submission channel, creating a filing duty with defined periodicity.
- Independent periodic testing links the sector to AML/CTF control-assurance expectations (independent audit of the AML/CFT programme).
- Permitting POS services at exchange centers expands permitted activity scope and therefore extends payments-regime obligations onto exchange licensees.

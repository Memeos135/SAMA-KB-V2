# SMS Notification Governance

## Why this community

Governance of customer-facing SMS notifications by banks and payment participants, covering sender identity integrity and mandated notification content for card/mada transactions.

## How members connect

- Both impose conduct obligations on the same regulated population (banks/participants in the national payment scheme) regarding outbound customer messaging.
- Sender tag governance protects the authenticity of the channel that the unified mada notification requirement mandates be used.
- Consumer protection linkage: notification duties support customer transparency, disputes and fraud-detection rights.
- Operationally sequential obligation chain: register/control sender identity, then send the standardised notification content.

# Quarterly Currency and Customer Data Form

A SAMA reporting template (Version 2.0) issued by the Exchange Centres Supervision Division requiring quarterly submission of foreign-currency sales and purchases (by counterparty type: local banks, other exchange houses, individuals, and cross-border), inward/outward remittance volumes, largest-customer data, counterfeit currency detected, and staff data. It is a supervisory data-collection instrument rather than a rule-making text, but the underlying filing duty is enforceable against licensed exchange centres. Relevant where customer-concentration and counterfeit-note reporting intersect with AML/CTF supervision of money changers.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, payments, governance/risk

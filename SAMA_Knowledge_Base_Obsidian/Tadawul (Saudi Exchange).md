# Tadawul (Saudi Exchange)

Tadawul (defined as the Saudi Exchange) is the mandated delivery venue for non-SAMA-issued eligible securities in repo transactions where both counterparties are licensed agents and the currency is SAR, with the cash leg moving through SARIE. SAMA-issued securities instead exchange through SAMA; where a counterparty is a non-licensed agent or the currency is not SAR, counterparties may use any mutually agreed system. Relevant to repo market participants determining permitted settlement infrastructure.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, payments, other

# Risk Factor Modellability

## Why this community

Criteria determining whether a risk factor qualifies as modellable under the internal models approach, and therefore whether it is capitalised through the expected shortfall model or via the stressed capital add-on for non-modellable risk factors.

## How members connect

- Single-node cluster acting as a gatekeeping test that conditions eligibility for internal model treatment.
- Operationally tied to trading desk designation and model approval provisions elsewhere in the market risk framework; failure of the test has a direct capital consequence rather than a mere reporting one.

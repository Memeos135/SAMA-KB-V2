# Effective EPE Model

Node on the Effective Expected Positive Exposure model used under the internal models method to measure counterparty credit exposure, and the validation, backtesting and performance-assessment duties attaching to it. Covers SAMA's ability to require correction of model or data deficiencies, to require additional capital while issues persist, and to approve more conservative alternatives to alpha times Effective EPE. Binds banks holding or applying for IMM approval.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

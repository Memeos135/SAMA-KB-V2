# Circular on Timing of Adopting International Accounting Standards

SAMA circular setting the start dates for finance companies to apply International Accounting Standards in preparing their accounts and financial statements under Article 71 of the Implementing Regulation of the Finance Companies Control Law. Applicants licensed as joint stock companies begin from the start of 2015 with quarterly IAS-based statements from Q1 2015; applicants licensed as limited liability companies begin from the date of the ministerial decision converting them into a joint stock company, filing quarterly statements from the first quarter of their first financial year as a JSC or Q1 2015, whichever is later, in line with Article 72. It binds finance companies operating in the Kingdom.

# KIRB Capital Charge

Node for KIRB, the IRB capital requirement on the underlying securitized pool that serves as the key input to the internal ratings-based securitization approach (SEC-IRBA); linked material also covers capital composition and capital distribution constraint disclosures. It determines when and how a bank may use SEC-IRBA rather than fallback approaches. Binds IRB-approved banks holding securitization exposures and their Pillar 3 reporting.

**Regimes:** banking prudential

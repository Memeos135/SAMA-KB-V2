# Trade Confirmation and Valuation Requirements

Part of SAMA's trade repository reporting and risk mitigation framework for OTC derivative contracts, covering timely trade confirmation and the valuation of non-centrally cleared OTC derivatives. It requires banks to base valuations on economically similar transactions or other objective criteria, to be able to compute and corroborate valuations internally, to use mainstream (or justified novel) valuation methodologies, to review the agreed valuation process periodically, and to document fallback valuation approaches, change procedures and valuation dispute resolution with counterparties. It binds SAMA-supervised banks in their dealings with derivative counterparties, and interacts with the reporting fields and life-cycle event rules (new trade, modification, novation) in the appendices.

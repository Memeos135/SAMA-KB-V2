# Operational Risk Capital

## Why this community

Operational risk regulatory capital under SAMA's adoption of the Basel III post-crisis standardised approach, covering how the capital charge is built from business volume indicators and internal loss experience.

## How members connect

- Single calculation chain: Business Indicator feeds the Business Indicator Component, which is multiplied by the Internal Loss Multiplier (driven by the Loss Component) to give Operational Risk Capital.
- Loss-data rules act as the evidential precondition for the ILM: identification/collection criteria and the loss event type taxonomy define which losses may enter the Loss Component.
- Hierarchy runs from the Basel III post-crisis reforms as source standard, through SAMA's minimum capital requirements framework, down to the definitional and classification annexes.
- Duplicate-titled members reflect the same obligation expressed in the framework document and its detailed methodology sections.

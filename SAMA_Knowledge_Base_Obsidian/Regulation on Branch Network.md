# Regulation on Branch Network

SAMA's Regulation on Branch Network sets the licensing and supervisory framework for opening, closing and relocating bank branches, service centers and remittance centers, including the use of inactive licenses, proposal content for new licenses, and prior-approval carve-outs by zone. It replaces the Annual Branch Expansion Plan requirement once a bank's Branch Network Policy is approved, and pursues financial inclusion, geographic diversification and consumer protection objectives. It binds all locally incorporated banks and licensed foreign bank branches (the latter only where expanding beyond three branches).

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk, consumer protection

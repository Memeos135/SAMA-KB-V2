# Table LIA - Explanations of Accounting/Regulatory Differences

Table LIA is the mandatory narrative accompanying Templates LI1 and LI2, explaining the main drivers of differences between financial statement carrying values and regulatory exposure amounts, including valuation differences, netting rules, provisions and prudential filters. Accompanying Q&A clarifies treatment of 1,250% risk-weighted exposures, which liabilities go in the unallocated column of LI1, and the distinction between balance-sheet netting and incremental Basel netting. Binds all banks within the Pillar 3 scope.

**Regimes:** banking prudential

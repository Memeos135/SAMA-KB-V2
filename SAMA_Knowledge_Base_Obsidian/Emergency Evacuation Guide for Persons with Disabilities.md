# Emergency Evacuation Guide for Persons with Disabilities

A guidance publication of the Authority of Persons with Disabilities (APD) on emergency evacuation planning for persons with disabilities, setting out definitions (evacuation, emergency, first responder, safe assembly areas, escape routes), disability categories, building hazards, self-preparedness, evacuation plan steps, and accessibility features such as public address and assistive listening systems, visual alarms, refuge areas and tactile warning surfaces. It is advisory in nature and directed at facility operators and entities serving the public rather than a SAMA prudential or conduct rule, but is relevant to branch and premises accessibility obligations.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, other

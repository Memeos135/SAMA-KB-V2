# Implementing Regulations of the Law of Combating Terrorist Crimes and its Financing

These Implementing Regulations give operative detail to the Law of Combating Terrorist Crimes and its Financing, notably by enumerating the financial activities and the commercial/professional (DNFBP) activities that bring a person within the Law's scope. They also set the mandatory content of AML/CTF policies, procedures and controls — risk management before customer verification, STR reporting procedures, a senior-level compliance officer, staff screening and training, independent audit — and extend group-wide compliance to foreign branches and majority-owned subsidiaries where local CTF requirements are weaker. They bind financial institutions, designated non-financial businesses and professions, and non-profit organizations.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, sanctions/TFS, governance/risk

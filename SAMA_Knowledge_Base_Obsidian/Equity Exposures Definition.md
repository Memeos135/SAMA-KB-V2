# Equity Exposures Definition

Defines equity exposures for standardised credit-risk purposes on the basis of economic substance rather than legal form, covering direct and indirect, voting and non-voting ownership interests, instruments settleable in shares (subject to SAMA's satisfaction on debt-like treatment), and equity-linked liabilities, while excluding equity-structured debt or securitisation exposures. Sets the baseline 250% risk weight for equity holdings. Applies to banks subject to SAMA's minimum capital requirements for credit risk.

**Regimes:** banking prudential

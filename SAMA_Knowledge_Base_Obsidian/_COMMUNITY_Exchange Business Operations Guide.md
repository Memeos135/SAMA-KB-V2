# Exchange Business Operations Guide

## Why this community

Licensing and conduct framework for money exchange (currency exchange and remittance) businesses in the Kingdom, covering permitted activities, operational controls and supervisory expectations for exchange houses.

## How members connect

- Single standalone instrument in this cluster: the guide operates as SAMA's consolidated rulebook for the exchange sector rather than as a node in a citation chain.
- Sits downstream of the primary banking/exchange licensing statute and interacts with AML/CTF and remittance obligations applicable to money service providers.
- Relevance is scope-driven: it determines when an entity is treated as an exchange business rather than a bank or PSP, and which regime's customer due diligence duties apply.

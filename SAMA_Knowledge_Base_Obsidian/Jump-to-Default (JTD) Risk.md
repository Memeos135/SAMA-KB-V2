# Jump-to-Default (JTD) Risk

Jump-to-default risk is the exposure the default risk capital (DRC) requirement is designed to capture, i.e. sudden default losses not reflected in credit spread shocks under the sensitivities-based method. The node covers gross and net JTD computation, permitted offsetting against the same obligor, allocation to buckets, and partial hedge benefit across distinct obligors, for non-securitisation, securitisation non-CTP and CTP portfolios. Binds banks calculating standardised market risk capital.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

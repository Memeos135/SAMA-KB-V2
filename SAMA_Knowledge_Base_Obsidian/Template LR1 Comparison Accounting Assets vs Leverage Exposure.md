# Template LR1: Comparison Accounting Assets vs Leverage Exposure

Node for Pillar 3 Template LR1, the fixed-format quarterly reconciliation between total assets in published financial statements and the leverage ratio exposure measure. It specifies the adjustment rows (regulatory vs accounting consolidation, securitisation risk transference, temporary central bank reserve exemption, fiduciary assets, regular-way purchases and sales, cash pooling) and sign conventions, and requires banks to state the calculation basis (quarter-end, daily or monthly averaging). Mandatory for all banks, which must also explain material differences.

**Regimes:** banking prudential

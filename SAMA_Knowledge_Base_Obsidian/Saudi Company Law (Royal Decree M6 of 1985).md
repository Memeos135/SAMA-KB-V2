# Saudi Company Law (Royal Decree M/6 of 1985)

A cross-reference to the Saudi Company Law issued by Royal Decree M/6 of 1985, specifically Articles 66–82, cited in SAMA's operational risk guidance as the domestic source of joint liability of board members towards the company, shareholders and third parties for damages. It is used to frame directors' and officers' liability exposure inside the Kingdom, alongside lender liability exposures arising from lending and workout conduct.

# Basel Risk-Based Capital Framework

This node anchors the LEX Rules' key definitions to the Basel risk-based capital framework: exposure covers on- and off-balance sheet items in the banking and trading books plus counterparty credit risk instruments; a large exposure is 10% or more of eligible capital to one counterparty or connected group; and eligible capital base is effective Tier 1 capital under Basel III. It also defines SAMA, subsidiary (>50% shareholding) and control relationship, including voting agreements and influence over appointment or dismissal of governing bodies. These definitions bind banks when scoping, measuring and reporting exposures.

**Regimes:** banking prudential

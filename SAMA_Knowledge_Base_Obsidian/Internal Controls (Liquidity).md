# Internal Controls (Liquidity)

Concept node on internal controls and policy content for liquidity risk in finance companies. Senior management must implement adequate internal controls and audit mechanisms to safeguard the integrity of the liquidity risk management process, and liquidity risk policies must document sources of risk, appetite and tolerance, strategy, balance-sheet and off-balance-sheet composition, funding diversification, lines of authority and reporting, limits and ratios with exception escalation, cash-flow projections, stress testing parameters, liquid asset buffers, contingency funding and collateral management.

# Enhanced Due Diligence Measures

This node is the enhanced due diligence (EDD) section: where a customer or business relationship is classified as high risk under the institution's risk-based approach, additional information gathering, closer transaction monitoring and intensified controls are required. Financial institutions must define the risk factors used to place customers in the high-risk category, identify high-risk customers individually or by category, and apply EDD including to relationships onboarded through third-party reliance. It binds SAMA-supervised financial institutions and interacts with senior management approval requirements for high-risk relationships.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF

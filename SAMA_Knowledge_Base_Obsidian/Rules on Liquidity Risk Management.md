# Rules on Liquidity Risk Management

This is SAMA's Rules on Liquidity Risk Management for finance companies, setting out the governance, strategy, measurement, stress testing and contingency funding requirements for managing liquidity risk. It allocates ultimate responsibility to the Board (risk tolerance, organisational structure, annual approval of strategy, policies, CFP and stress testing framework) and operational responsibility to senior management, with recommended ALCO oversight and periodic reporting to the Board and SAMA. It binds licensed finance companies supervised by SAMA, not banks.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, BNPL/finance

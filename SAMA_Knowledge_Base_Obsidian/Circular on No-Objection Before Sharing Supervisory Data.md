# Circular on No-Objection Before Sharing Supervisory Data

A SAMA circular to finance companies requiring them to obtain SAMA's no-objection before providing governmental or non-governmental entities with the periodic supervisory data submitted to SAMA or with unpublished statistical data. It is grounded in SAMA's supervisory role over the finance sector and its interest in data accuracy and controlled disclosure. It binds finance companies operating in the Kingdom.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, data/credit info, governance/risk

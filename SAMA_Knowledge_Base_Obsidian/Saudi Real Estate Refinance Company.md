# Saudi Real Estate Refinance Company

Covers Chapter Three of the Implementing Regulation: SAMA licenses real estate refinance companies restricted to refinance activity with minimum capital of SAR 5 billion, and PIF (or a wholly owned entity) incorporates the Saudi Real Estate Refinance Company with SAMA's initial approval. It governs share offerings (SAMA no-objection required), the conditions and 30% cap on aggregate shareholding by real estate finance entities after market stability and a five-year period, and the requirement that PIF or the founding entity retain at least 51%. It binds the refinance company, PIF as founder, and real estate finance entities seeking to invest.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, governance/risk, banking prudential

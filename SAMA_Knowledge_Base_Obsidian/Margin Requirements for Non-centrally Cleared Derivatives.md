# Margin Requirements for Non-centrally Cleared Derivatives

SAMA's margin framework for non-centrally cleared derivatives, setting out instrument scope (all such derivatives except physically settled FX forwards and swaps, with a specific carve-out for the principal exchange leg of cross-currency swaps from initial margin) and entity scope. Covered entities are financial firms and systemically important non-financial entities — those whose group-wide month-end average notional of non-centrally cleared derivatives for March–May exceeds SAR 30 billion, excluding inter-affiliate trades — and they must bilaterally calculate and exchange initial and variation margin; central banks, sovereigns, MDBs, the BIS and non-systemic non-financial firms are outside scope. Only trades between two covered entities are captured.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

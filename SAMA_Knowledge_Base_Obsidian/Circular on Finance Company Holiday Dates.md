# Circular on Finance Company Holiday Dates

SAMA circular fixing official holiday dates for finance companies under Article 21 of the Finance Companies Control Law and Article 2 of its Implementing Regulation: four days for Eid al-Fitr starting after 29 Ramadan (Umm al-Qura calendar), four days for Eid al-Adha starting on the Day of Arafah, and National Day (23 September), with substitution rules when it falls on a Saturday or Friday. SAMA will circulate any changes to Eid dates. Binds all finance companies operating in the Kingdom and their branches.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, governance/risk

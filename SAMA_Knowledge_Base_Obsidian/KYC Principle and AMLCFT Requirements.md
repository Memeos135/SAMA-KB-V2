# KYC Principle and AML/CFT Requirements

The supervisory rules and controls in the SAMA bank account framework that anchor the Know Your Customer principle and require the Rules to be read together with the Anti-Money Laundering Law and its Implementing Regulations and the Law on Terrorism Crimes and Financing and related guidelines. It covers Compliance Department access rights to CDD and transaction data, verification of curators, legal agents, custodians and authorized persons, and ongoing risk-based monitoring of customers' transactions using automated systems integrated with core banking. It binds banks operating in Saudi Arabia at both onboarding and throughout the relationship.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, governance/risk, sanctions/TFS

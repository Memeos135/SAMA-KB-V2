# IFRS Adoption Timing

## Why this community

Timing and sequencing of the move to international accounting standards by SAMA-supervised entities, including the transition to the IFRS 9 expected-credit-loss framework. The regulatory problem is when reporting obligations change and what preparatory steps supervised firms must complete before the effective date.

## How members connect

- Both are SAMA circulars addressed to supervised institutions setting the effective/transition dates for adopting international accounting standards, so one sets the general timetable and the other the instrument-specific implementation path.
- Shared obligation chain: identify the adoption date, prepare an implementation plan, and report readiness to SAMA — a supervisory reporting duty rather than a substantive prudential rule.
- Cross-regime relevance: accounting classification and impairment feed into prudential capital and provisioning outcomes, so the timing decision has knock-on effects for regulatory reporting.
- Hierarchy note: these are circulars operating beneath the applicable banking/finance-company supervisory framework; they supplement rather than replace the underlying standards.

# Table LIA - Explanations of Differences Accounting vs Regulatory

Narrative Pillar 3 table accompanying Templates LI1 and LI2, in which banks explain the drivers of differences between accounting carrying values and regulatory exposure amounts, including scope-of-consolidation effects, valuation differences, netting rules, provisions and prudential filters. It provides the qualitative context that makes the quantitative LI templates interpretable. Applies to banks subject to SAMA's Pillar 3 disclosure framework.

**Regimes:** banking prudential

# Regulatory Real Estate Exposures

Defines the "regulatory real estate" sub-class and its four buckets: residential and commercial exposures, each split by whether they are materially dependent on cash flows generated by the property. It also sets out the material-dependence test (loan servicing and recovery depending on rental, lease or sale proceeds rather than the borrower's other income), which typically but not exclusively applies to corporates, MSMEs and SPVs. Binds banks determining risk weights for property-secured exposures under the standardised approach.

**Regimes:** banking prudential

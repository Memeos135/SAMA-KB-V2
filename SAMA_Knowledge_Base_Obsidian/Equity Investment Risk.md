# Equity Investment Risk

Section of the Risk Management Framework for Shari'ah Compliant Banking setting Principle 7.0 on equity investment risk, covering profit sharing instruments such as Mudarabah and Musharakah. It requires banks to define investment objectives, risk tolerance, expected returns and holding periods; maintain policies, procedures and management structures for acquiring, holding and exiting profit sharing investments; monitor investee performance as partners; and track how risks transform across investment and contract stages. This node also carries the framework's definitions section (Bank, Full Fledged Islamic Bank, Islamic Window, credit/market/operational/SNCR/fiduciary/liquidity risk) and the 03 March 2024 effective date.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential

# Compliance with Fire Safety Officer Duties Regulation

SAMA circular reaffirming that all supervised financial institutions must comply with the Ministry of Interior / Civil Defense regulation on the duties of the fire safety, prevention and protection officer. It highlights appointing a qualified safety officer, maintaining and periodically inspecting fire safety systems, preparing and testing evacuation and emergency plans, and documenting and retaining related records. It binds all financial institutions under SAMA supervision.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, other

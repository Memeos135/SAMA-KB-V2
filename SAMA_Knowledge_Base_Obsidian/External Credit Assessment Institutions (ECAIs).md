# External Credit Assessment Institutions (ECAIs)

This node deals with eligibility conditions for External Credit Assessment Institutions whose ratings banks may use to determine risk-weighted exposures, with stricter conditions in the securitisation context: ratings and underlying methodologies must be publicly available on a non-selective, free basis, and the ECAI must show demonstrated expertise in securitisations. It also addresses multiple-ECAI conflicts, treatment of credit risk mitigation reflected in a rating, and when exposures must be treated as unrated. It binds banks relying on external ratings for capital purposes.

**Regimes:** banking prudential

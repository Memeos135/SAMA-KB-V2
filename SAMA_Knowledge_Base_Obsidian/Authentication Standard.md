# Authentication Standard

Concept node for the authentication standard required under the Counter-Fraud Framework's prevention controls. Member Organisations must define, approve, implement and maintain a risk-based authentication standard — developed jointly by the Counter-Fraud Department and Cyber Security Team, informed by the Fraud Risk Assessment and Cyber Security Risk Assessment — covering customer, employee and third party credentials and instructions across digital and non-digital channels, with multi-factor authentication for higher-risk activity. It cross-refers to identity and access management and electronic banking provisions of SAMA's Cyber Security Framework.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, payments, consumer protection

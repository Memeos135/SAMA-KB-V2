# Circular Clarifying Article 83 on Fees and Charges

SAMA circular interpreting the cap on fees, commissions and administrative service costs in Article 83 of the Implementing Regulation of the Finance Companies Control Law and Article 9 of the Consumer Finance Regulations (the lower of 1% of the finance amount or SAR 5,000). It clarifies that the cap covers every amount the borrower must pay other than the finance amount and the term cost, including unavoidable charges such as application study, real-estate valuation, property inspection and registration fees; the financier alone must deal with and pay third parties (valuers, registration bodies) and may not delegate that payment to the borrower, and all such charges must be reflected in the APR calculation. It binds banks and finance companies operating in the Kingdom, effective from the circular date.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, BNPL/finance

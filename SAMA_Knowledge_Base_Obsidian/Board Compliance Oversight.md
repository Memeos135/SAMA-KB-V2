# Board Compliance Oversight

Node covering the board of directors' responsibilities toward compliance under SAMA's Compliance Principles, one of the document's headline sections alongside senior management duties. The Board (for a foreign bank branch, the branch's most senior officer assumes board-level duties) approves the compliance policy and oversees management of non-compliance risk. Binds boards of local banks and the equivalent officer in foreign bank branches licensed under the Banking Control Law.

# Rules on Management of Problem Loans

The framework and definitional part of SAMA's Rules on Management of Problem Loans, issued under the SAMA Charter and Banking Control Law and applicable to all licensed banks. It sets the objectives (rehabilitating viable borrowers, fair customer treatment especially for MSMEs, and adequate controls over NPL and restructuring processes) and defines key terms such as problem loans, non-performing loans, watch-list, early warning signals, cooperating and viable borrower, viability assessment, covenant and failed restructuring. It must be read together with SAMA's Rules on Credit Risk Classification and Provisioning, with a separate SAMA guidance document offering good practice.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk, consumer protection

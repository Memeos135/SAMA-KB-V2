# Systemic Payment Systems

## Why this community

Oversight of payment systems designated as systemically important, together with operational and counterparty-risk expectations for participants — including SARIE operating hours and dealings with e-commerce payment technical support providers.

## How members connect

- The systemically important payment systems standards act as the parent framework; the SARIE operating-hours notice is an operational instruction issued under that oversight regime.
- Guidance on e-commerce payment technical support companies links to the same framework through participant risk management and outsourcing/third-party dependency concerns in the payments chain.
- Cross-regime interaction: entities in scope may also be licensed PSPs, so payment-services licensing and AML/CDD duties apply in parallel to these oversight standards.
- The Ramadan/Eid hours notice is time-bound and does not alter the substantive standards.

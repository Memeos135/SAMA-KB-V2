# Non-profit, Associations and Foundations Accounts (300.1.5)

Rule 300.1.5 sets account-opening conditions for the non-profit and public sectors, covering private associations and foundations, public welfare committees, charity change/halala donation collection accounts, cooperative associations and funds, homeowners' associations, endowments and bequests, clubs, public corporations, chambers of commerce, mosque construction accounts, national societies and committees, court-related and personal debt settlement accounts. It specifies supervising-authority approvals, authorised signatories and purpose limits for each category. It binds banks onboarding NPOs and public-sector bodies, a segment carrying elevated terrorist-financing sensitivity.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, banking prudential, sanctions/TFS, governance/risk

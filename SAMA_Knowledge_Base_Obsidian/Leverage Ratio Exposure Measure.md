# Leverage Ratio Exposure Measure

Defines the denominator of the leverage ratio — the total exposure measure — and how it is built up from on-balance sheet assets, derivative replacement cost and potential future exposure add-ons, securities financing transactions and off-balance sheet items, with regulatory deductions reported as negative amounts. Includes the LR1 reconciliation template linking published financial statement assets to the leverage ratio exposure measure and the treatment of client-cleared trades, fiduciary assets and any temporary central bank reserve exemption. Binds all SAMA-supervised banks; disclosure is quarterly and on the regulatory scope of consolidation.

**Regimes:** banking prudential

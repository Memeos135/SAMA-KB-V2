# RWA Disclosure Templates

## Why this community

Pillar 3 disclosure of risk-weighted assets across credit, counterparty credit, securitisation, market risk and CVA, including the mandated comparison of internally modelled outcomes against standardised approach outcomes. It governs what a bank must publish about how RWA is calculated and how it moves period to period.

## How members connect

- All templates roll up into the overview of RWA, which acts as the anchor disclosure and fixes the risk-category taxonomy the detailed templates must reconcile to.
- Credit risk templates (standardised exposure/CRM and asset-class-by-risk-weight, IRB by portfolio and PD, credit derivative effects, IRB flow statements) inherit their measurement rules and defined exposure classes from the minimum capital requirements for credit risk framework.
- The modelled-vs-standardised comparison templates create a cross-cutting obligation chain: internal-model users must restate the same exposures on the standardised basis, linking IRB, IMM, IMA and SA-CVA disclosures to a common benchmark.
- Flow-statement templates (IRB credit risk, counterparty credit risk under IMM, SA-CVA) share a common structure requiring explanation of RWA movements, so they are read together rather than in isolation.

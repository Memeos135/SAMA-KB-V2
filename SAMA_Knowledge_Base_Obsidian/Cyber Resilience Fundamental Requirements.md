# Cyber Resilience Fundamental Requirements

The Cyber Resilience Fundamental Requirements is a SAMA-issued, risk-based control set structured around Cyber Security Leadership and Governance, Cyber Security Operations and Technology, and Resilience domains, with uniquely numbered control requirements. It is aimed at newly established entities in early stages of operation, including firms testing solutions under SAMA's Regulatory Sandbox framework, rather than at mature Member Organizations subject to the full Cyber Security Framework. Useful for determining the baseline cyber and resilience expectations applicable to FinTech entrants supervised by SAMA.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, payments

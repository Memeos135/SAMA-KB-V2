# Partial Loss Provisions

Node for the partial-loss element of the standard-form vehicle finance lease (Ijarah) contract for individuals issued under the Finance Lease Law and its implementing regulation. It concerns damage short of total destruction of the leased vehicle and the treatment of the parties' rights and rental obligations in that event, disclosed to the lessee in the contract summary. It binds lessors (banks and finance companies) that must contract on the approved form.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, consumer protection

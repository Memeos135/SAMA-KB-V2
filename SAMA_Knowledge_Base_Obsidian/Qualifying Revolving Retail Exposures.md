# Qualifying Revolving Retail Exposures

Defines the qualifying revolving retail exposure (QRRE) sub-class within the IRB retail asset class, alongside residential mortgages and other retail, and sets the cumulative criteria (revolving, unsecured, uncommitted, applied at sub-portfolio level) for that treatment. It also covers when small business exposures below the SAR 4 million threshold may be pooled and managed as retail. It binds SAMA-licensed banks using internal ratings-based approaches for credit risk capital, and interacts with Pillar 3 disclosure of problem assets and forbearance.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

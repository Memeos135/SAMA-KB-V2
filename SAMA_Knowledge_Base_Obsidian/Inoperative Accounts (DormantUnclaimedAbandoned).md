# Inoperative Accounts (Dormant/Unclaimed/Abandoned)

Defines the lifecycle classification of inoperative bank accounts — dormant (24 calendar months from the last customer-initiated debit or documented contact), then unclaimed, then abandoned — and the operational controls attaching to each stage. Sets requirements for reactivation under dual control at a higher authority, permitted credits that do not restore active status, customer contact obligations, segregated and dual-controlled file custody, treatment of balances as bank liabilities, deduction of outstanding obligations before transfer to suspense, and a limited closure option for balances of SAR 1,000 or less with prior notice. Binds banks operating in Saudi Arabia and governs their handling of affected customer funds.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, consumer protection, governance/risk

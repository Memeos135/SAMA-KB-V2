# Finance Companies Control Law

The primary statute governing finance companies in the Kingdom: it defines core terms (Finance Company, Borrower, Consumer, Finance, License), restricts the law's application to companies licensed under it, and requires Sharia-compliant conduct. It sets out SAMA licensing of specified finance activities (real estate, production asset, SME, finance lease, credit card, consumer finance, microfinance and other SAMA-approved activities) and of entities engaged in activities supporting finance activity, plus prohibitions on non-finance business, deposit-taking, trading, uncollateralised finance and related-party/connected lending. It binds licensed finance companies, their boards, directors and external auditors, with detail left to the Implementing Regulations.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, governance/risk, consumer protection

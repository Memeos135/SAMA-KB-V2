# Template OV1 (Overview of RWA)

Template OV1 is the Pillar 3 overview of risk-weighted assets, mapping RWA and minimum capital requirements across credit risk, counterparty credit risk, CVA, securitisation, market risk, operational risk, equity investments in funds, settlement risk and amounts below deduction thresholds. It fixes the row-by-row definitions and cross-references to the SAMA-adopted Basel standards (SCRE, SCCR) for each approach, including which lines capture standardised versus internal-model approaches used with SAMA approval. It binds banks subject to SAMA's disclosure framework.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

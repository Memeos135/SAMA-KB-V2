# Consumer Financing Buyout

Concept node for Consumer Financing Buyout — the purchase of an individual customer's outstanding financing debt by another financier — as referenced in SAMA's instructions on time limits for issuing clearance letters and transferring accounts and debts. The instructions define financiers (banks and finance companies under SAMA supervision), the Customer as a natural person, and account transfer as moving a balance to another bank with closure of the original account, with the stated aims of customer protection, fair dealing and enabling competition. They apply cumulatively with related SAMA instruments, including the Banking and Finance Company Consumer Protection Principles, the Consumer Finance Regulations, credit and charge card rules, and the circulars on inter-bank consumer debt buyout and real estate finance debt transfer.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, consumer protection

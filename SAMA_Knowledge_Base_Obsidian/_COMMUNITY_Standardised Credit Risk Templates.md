# Standardised Credit Risk Templates

## Why this community

Quantitative Pillar 3 disclosure of standardised-approach credit risk: the effect of credit risk mitigation on exposure amounts and the resulting distribution of exposures across asset classes and risk-weight buckets.

## How members connect

- Both templates report the same underlying standardised-approach exposure population at different stages: pre/post-CRM amounts feed the risk-weight breakdown, so the figures must reconcile.
- Shared defined terms (exposure class, on/off-balance-sheet exposure, CCF, eligible collateral and guarantees) drawn from the credit risk capital rules.
- Direct cross-reference between the two templates creates a consistency obligation rather than two independent disclosures.

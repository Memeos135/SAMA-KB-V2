# Shariah-Compliant Product Development Process

Chapter Nine (Article 18) governs how banks develop Shariah-compliant products, requiring all Shariah issues in product design and process to be referred to the Shariah committee with sufficient detail, and committee approval for every Islamic banking product and any subsequent amendment, including detailed review of contracts and documentation. It distinguishes pre-product approval (structuring, Shariah research, contract review before launch) from post-product approval review after the product is offered to customers.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential, consumer protection

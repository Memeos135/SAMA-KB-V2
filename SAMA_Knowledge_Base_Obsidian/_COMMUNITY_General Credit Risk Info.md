# General Credit Risk Info

## Why this community

Baseline qualitative disclosure on credit risk: the bank's business model as it translates into credit risk profile, the components of its credit risk management framework, and the relationship between credit risk management and the accounting treatment of impairment.

## How members connect

- Stands as the narrative gateway to the credit risk disclosure suite — the quantitative credit risk and asset quality templates are read against the qualitative framework described here.
- Uses definitions (past due, impaired, restructured exposures) that govern how the quantitative credit risk tables must be populated, so inconsistency between narrative and figures is itself a disclosure defect.
- Sits at the qualitative tier of the Pillar 3 hierarchy: an obligation to explain policy and governance rather than to compute a capital number.

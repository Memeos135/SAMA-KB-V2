# Template OR1 - Historical Losses

Template OR1 requires annual Pillar 3 disclosure of aggregate operational risk losses over the past ten years (by accounting date of the loss), including losses net of recoveries, excluded losses, loss counts, and whether internal loss data feed the internal loss multiplier (ILM), using loss event thresholds of SAR 44,600 and SAR 446,000. It applies to banks in the second or third business indicator bucket and to first-bucket banks with SAMA approval to use internal loss data in their operational risk capital requirement. Banks must add narrative explaining new loss exclusions and other material information on losses and recoveries.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

# High Quality Liquid Assets (HQLA)

A concept node within SAMA's liquidity risk management guidance for finance companies, describing the requirement to hold a cushion of unencumbered high quality liquid assets — assets convertible to cash quickly at little or no loss of value even under market stress — as a readily available funding source for unexpected net cash outflows. It sits alongside expectations on funding diversification, liquidity risk tolerance and limits, cash-flow projections, stress testing and contingency funding plans. It binds SAMA-licensed finance companies and their boards/senior management, which set the liquidity risk appetite and review it at least annually.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, governance/risk, banking prudential

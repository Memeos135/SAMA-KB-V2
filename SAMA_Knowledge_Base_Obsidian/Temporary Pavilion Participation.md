# Temporary Pavilion Participation

Defined-term node from the second issue of the events rules: a "temporary pavilion" is a space occupied at an event for the purpose of introducing and marketing the financial institution. Presence through such a pavilion is expressly brought within the definition of participation in an event, but is exempted from the requirement to obtain SAMA's no-objection. The exemption applies to financial institutions licensed by SAMA, which remain subject to the policy, documentation, conduct and due diligence requirements of the rules.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, other

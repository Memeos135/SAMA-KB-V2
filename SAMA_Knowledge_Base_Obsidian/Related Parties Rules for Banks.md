# Related Parties Rules for Banks

SAMA's Related Parties Rules for Banks (2022 update), issued under the Central Bank Law and Banking Control Law, defining related parties and control relationships and setting governance, exposure limits, security, and quarterly reporting requirements for related-party exposures and transactions. It caps exposures to non-bank related parties (5% of eligible capital, 10% for listed Saudi companies, 25% for financial-sector non-bank subsidiaries, 50% cumulative), lists exemptions, and requires immediate notification of breaches with a remediation plan. It binds banks and local banks operating in KSA and supersedes the earlier related parties rules.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

- **What this link tells you:** When assessing a bank's exposures or transactions with related parties, treat the Related Parties Rules as the operative detail layer sitting under the statutory limit in the Banking Control Law rather than as a free-standing standard. The Rules expressly build on Article 9 of that Law, supplying the definition of who counts as a related party and adding requirements and guidance on top of the statutory restriction. Practically, a transaction that satisfies the Rules must still be tested against the primary Article 9 constraint, and any ambiguity in the definition of related party is resolved by reading both instruments together.

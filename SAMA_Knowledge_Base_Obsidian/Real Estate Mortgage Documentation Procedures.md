# Real Estate Mortgage Documentation Procedures

A SAMA circular issued with the Ministry of Justice to standardise procedural requirements for documenting and registering real estate mortgages before notaries public, and mandating use of the attached registration and incident-recording forms. Conditions include attendance of mortgagor and lender representatives with valid powers of attorney, a licensed bank/finance company as mortgagee holding a valid real estate finance licence, ownership and eligibility of the mortgaged property, Shariah committee product approval, and completion of transfer and mortgage in a single procedure. It binds banks and finance companies operating in the Kingdom, which must also report notary refusals to SAMA using the prescribed form.

# Credit Risk Mitigation Qualitative

## Why this community

Qualitative disclosure of credit risk mitigation: the policies and processes for collateral valuation and management, main types of collateral and guarantors, and concentrations arising within mitigation techniques.

## How members connect

- Provides the narrative basis for the recognised CRM effects reported in the quantitative credit risk exposure templates, so eligibility conditions in the capital rules constrain what may be disclosed as mitigation.
- Depends on defined terms for eligible collateral, netting and guarantees drawn from the credit risk mitigation provisions of the capital adequacy framework, not from the disclosure standard itself.
- Complements the general credit risk qualitative disclosure: exposure policy first, then the techniques used to reduce that exposure.

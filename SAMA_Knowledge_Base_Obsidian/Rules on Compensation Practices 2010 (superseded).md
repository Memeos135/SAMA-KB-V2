# Rules on Compensation Practices 2010 (superseded)

This node marks the 2010 Rules on Compensation Practices (circular 26194/BCS/12580, 3 May 2010) as expressly superseded by SAMA's updated Banks Remuneration Rules, which set minimum requirements and supervisory guidance for bank remuneration policies to address misconduct risk and support sound risk management. The successor rules bind all locally incorporated banks licensed in Saudi Arabia and extend to majority-owned financial-sector subsidiaries at home and abroad (subject to host-country law). They impose semiannual compliance reporting to SAMA (due before 31 March and 31 August), disclosure of fixed/variable remuneration and the top 12 highest-paid employees, annual remuneration review results before 31 March, and expose banks to rectification directives, added capital/liquidity requirements or penalties for material deficiencies.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential

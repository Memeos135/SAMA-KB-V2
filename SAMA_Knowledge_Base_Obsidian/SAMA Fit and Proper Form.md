# SAMA Fit and Proper Form

This node covers SAMA's Fit and Proper Form as a mandatory attachment in the licensing pack for consumer microfinance activity, alongside the licence application letter, articles of association, shareholder list, organisational structure, feasibility study/business plan and a bank guarantee equal to 20% of minimum capital. A separate form must be completed and signed by each founding member, by each natural or legal person holding 5% or more where a founder is an entity, and by each board and sub-committee candidate, then endorsed by the person authorised to submit the application. It binds founders/shareholders (the "Applicant") and proposed directors of a finance company seeking a licence under the Finance Companies Control Law and its Implementing Regulation.

# Range of Eligible Guarantors and Protection Providers

Specifies which entities may be recognized as guarantors, counter-guarantors or credit protection providers for capital relief purposes, generally requiring a lower risk weight than the counterparty. Recognized providers include sovereigns and listed international bodies, PSEs, multilateral development banks, banks, securities firms and other prudentially regulated financial institutions, plus other externally rated entities, with a stricter rating condition where protection is given to a securitization exposure. It binds SAMA-supervised banks claiming credit risk mitigation from guarantees or credit derivatives.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

# Customer Identity Verification via Trusted Source

SAMA circular strengthening KYC and identity-proofing by requiring financial institutions to confirm that the mobile number registered on a customer's account or membership belongs to the same person, by matching the account holder's ID with the ID of the mobile subscriber through the national trusted verification service. Institutions must assess and quantify existing non-matching accounts, report results and a remediation plan to SAMA, adopt the verified mobile number in new account/membership opening (in-person and remote), and comply within 45 days, with enforcement action for non-compliance. Applies to banks, finance companies, payment service providers and category (A) exchange centres.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, governance/risk, payments

# CCR Exposure Analysis Template

## Why this community

Quantitative Pillar 3 disclosure of counterparty credit risk exposures broken down by the measurement approach used, showing replacement cost, potential future exposure, EAD post-CRM and resulting RWA.

## How members connect

- Single-member cluster: this template is the primary quantitative counterpart to the CCR qualitative table, which must explain the approaches reported here.
- Depends on approach definitions and EAD/CRM calculation rules in the capital adequacy framework; the template prescribes presentation, not methodology.
- Feeds and must reconcile with the bank's overall RWA and capital adequacy disclosures.

# Template CCR3: Standardised Approach CCR Exposures

Template CCR3 requires disclosure of counterparty credit risk exposures treated under the standardised approach for credit risk, broken down by regulatory portfolio and risk weight band. It forms part of the CCR disclosure section of SAMA's Pillar 3 framework and applies to SAMA-supervised banks using the standardised approach for the relevant exposures.

**Regimes:** banking prudential

# Table SECA - Qualitative Securitisation Disclosure

Section 21 of the Pillar 3 framework scopes securitisation disclosures and introduces Table SECA, the annual qualitative disclosure of a bank's securitisation strategy, objectives, risk management policies and activity features, distinguishing banking book from trading book positions and requiring lists of sponsored SPEs and affiliated entities involved in the bank's securitisations. Table SECA is mandatory for all banks holding securitisation exposures; templates SEC1/SEC2 capture all securitisation exposures while SEC3/SEC4 are limited to exposures treated under the securitisation framework, including originator risk-transfer recognition criteria.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

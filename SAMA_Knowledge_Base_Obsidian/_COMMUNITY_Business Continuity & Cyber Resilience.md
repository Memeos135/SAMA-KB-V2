# Business Continuity & Cyber Resilience

## Why this community

Operational and cyber resilience obligations for SAMA-supervised entities: maintaining continuity of critical services and withstanding, responding to and recovering from cyber and disruption events, including for newer licence categories such as aggregation entities and sandbox participants.

## How members connect

- Hierarchy: the Business Continuity Management Framework and the Cyber Security Framework are the parent instruments; activity-specific instructions (e.g. for aggregation activity) incorporate them by cross-reference, so the same resilience obligations attach to newly regulated entity types.
- Obligation chain within BCM: business impact analysis and risk assessment feed the business continuity plan and IT disaster recovery plan, which in turn must be exercised through periodic testing, all under a defined governance structure and crisis management arrangement.
- Cyber side mirrors this through domains (leadership and governance, operations and technology, resilience), with the CISO as the accountable role and the resilience domain expressly tied back to business continuity.
- External standards and glossaries (ISO 22301, NISTIR terminology) are cited as reference points for defined terms, while Vision 2030 and the sandbox materials supply the policy and scope context for applying the frameworks to innovative entrants.

# Financial Institutions Services Tariff Guide

The Financial Institutions Services Tariff Guide (First Edition, Rajab 1447H / 2025), issued by SAMA under the Central Bank Law, sets binding limits and criteria for fees charged for services and products, including a schedule of tariffs for basic services to individual customers and criteria for setting product/service fees. It defines terms such as Financial Institution, Customer, documented channels, identification certificate, debt-confirmation document, account statement and clearance letter. It binds all SAMA-supervised financial institutions and overrides any conflicting provisions, effective 90 days after publication.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, banking prudential, BNPL/finance, payments

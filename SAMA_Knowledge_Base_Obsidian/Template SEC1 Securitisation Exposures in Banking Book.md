# Template SEC1: Securitisation Exposures in Banking Book

Pillar 3 template disclosing all securitisation exposures in the banking book, broken down by the bank's role (originator, sponsor, investor) and by underlying exposure type, irrespective of whether the securitisation framework capital treatment applies. It captures a comprehensive view of securitisation activity, including positions that do not meet risk transfer recognition criteria (which are excluded from SEC3/SEC4). It binds banks subject to SAMA's Pillar 3 disclosure requirements.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

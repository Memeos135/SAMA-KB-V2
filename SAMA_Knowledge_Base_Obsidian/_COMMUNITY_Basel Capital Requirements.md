# Basel Capital Requirements

## Why this community

The umbrella capital-adequacy regime SAMA applies to banks under Basel II/III: scope of application, minimum credit risk capital, and the specialist add-ons for counterparty credit risk and CVA. It sets which entities are consolidated and which calculation approach (standardised vs IRB) a bank may use.

## How members connect

- Hierarchy: the Basel III Framework and SAMA's implementing circular (BCS290) sit above the topic-level chapters, which cite them as the source of the obligation to hold minimum capital.
- The minimum credit risk chapter acts as the hub, cross-referencing the IRB approach, credit risk mitigation and securitization as alternative or modifying treatments of the same exposure.
- CCR and CVA form an obligation chain off the credit risk base: derivative and SFT exposures are measured under CCR rules, then attract a separate CVA capital charge.
- Purchased receivables under IRB link back into the securitization framework, showing where an exposure changes regime depending on structure.

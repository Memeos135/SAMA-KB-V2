# Licensed Real Estate Broker Compliance

A SAMA circular requiring supervised financial institutions to deal and contract only with real estate brokers licensed by the Real Estate General Authority, relying on the Real Estate Brokerage Law's prohibition on unlicensed brokerage or real estate services. It directs firms to verify broker licensing status through the Authority's public register. It binds all institutions under SAMA supervision and licensing.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, other

# Additional Licensing Guidelines for Digital-Only Banks

SAMA guidance issued under its Charter and the Banking Control Law setting additional licensing conditions for Digital-only Banks — banks conducting banking business mainly through digital channels — to be met alongside the general Banking Licensing Guidelines and Minimum Criteria. It addresses local joint-stock incorporation, promoter experience and financial capacity, business plan content, ICAAP/ILAAP submissions, physical head-office presence, corporate governance parity with conventional banks, risk management, and AML/CFT compliance in a fully digital environment. Binds applicants seeking a Digital-only Bank licence in Saudi Arabia.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk, AML/CTF

# Template SEC1 - Securitisation in Banking Book

Disclosure template within SAMA's Pillar 3 Disclosure Requirements Framework covering all securitisation exposures held in the banking book, including those that fail the risk transfer recognition criteria and are therefore not treated under the securitisation capital framework. It sits alongside Table SECA and template SEC2 to give a comprehensive picture of a bank's securitisation activity by role (originator, sponsor, investor), separate from the capital-charge templates SEC3 and SEC4. Binds banks subject to SAMA's Pillar 3 regime.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

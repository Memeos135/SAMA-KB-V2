# Template SEC3 - Securitisation Originator/Sponsor Capital

Pillar 3 template disclosing banking book securitisation exposures where the bank acts as originator or sponsor, together with the associated capital requirements and risk-weighted assets under the securitisation framework. It is limited to exposures actually treated under that framework (i.e. meeting risk transfer recognition), avoiding double counting with SEC1/SEC2. Binds banks subject to SAMA's Pillar 3 regime.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

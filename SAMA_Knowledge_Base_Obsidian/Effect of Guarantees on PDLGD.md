# Effect of Guarantees on PD/LGD

Concept node on how the risk-mitigating effect of guarantees may be recognised in IRB parameters — through adjustment of either PD or LGD, with the LGD-adjustment option available only to banks approved for own LGD estimates — and the consistency, rating and documentation requirements that follow. It also covers related credit risk mitigation treatment for collateral, repo-style transactions and the foundation versus advanced recognition routes. Binds banks applying the IRB approach, including for retail pools where guarantees support individual obligations or pools.

**Regimes:** banking prudential

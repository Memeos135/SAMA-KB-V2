# Accounting of Zakat and Income Tax Circular

April 2017 SAMA circular addressed to all banks, insurance companies and finance companies clarifying the accounting treatment and financial statement presentation of Zakat and income tax. It withdraws the SAMA Accounting Standards for Commercial Banks with effect from 1 January 2017 and requires financial statements under IFRS as adopted by SAMA (IFRS except IAS 12 and IFRIC 21 insofar as they relate to Zakat), with a standalone guidance annexure applied consistently across the banking, insurance and finance sectors.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

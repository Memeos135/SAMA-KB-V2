# Basel 2004 IRR Principles

A concept node covering the Basel Committee's 2004 Principles for the management and supervision of interest rate risk, as referenced in SAMA's circular adopting the revised IRRBB standards. It frames the baseline supervisory expectations for identifying, measuring, monitoring and controlling interest rate risk in the banking book, and records the key revisions (shock-scenario and behavioural/modelling assumption guidance, expanded Pillar 3-style disclosure, an updated standardised framework, and a tighter outlier test moved from 20% of total capital to 15% of Tier 1 capital). It binds domestic banks in KSA; foreign bank branches are outside scope.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

# Electronic Record System

Requires every bank to build and maintain an electronic registration system as a unified database underpinning the opening, operation and follow-up of bank accounts, with prescribed minimum data fields differentiated by customer category (Saudi natural persons, GCC nationals, non-Saudi natural persons and others per the annexed classification), sourced from approved identity documents and reliable independent sources. The node also carries related obligations: Compliance Department access rights to identification, due diligence and transaction data; verification of curators, legal agents, custodians and authorised persons; and ongoing risk-based monitoring of customers and transactions where manual monitoring is expressly insufficient. Binds banks and interacts directly with the AML Law and its Implementing Regulation.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, banking prudential, data/credit info, governance/risk

# Global Legal Entity Identifier Foundation (GLEIF)

Concept node for the Global Legal Entity Identifier Foundation (GLEIF), the international body that accredits Local Operating Units authorised to issue Legal Entity Identifiers. SAMA requires supervised financial institutions, their subsidiaries and branches of foreign financial institutions operating in the Kingdom to obtain and keep current an LEI from a GLEIF-accredited unit, linking the requirement to risk management, financial stability, market transparency, Vision 2030 and G20 commitments. Relevant to any question about which body validates LEI issuers.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, data/credit info

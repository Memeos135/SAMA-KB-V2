# Foreign Exchange (FX) Risk

FX risk is one of the prescribed market risk classes in SAMA's market risk capital standard, capturing loss potential from exchange rate movements on positions whose currency differs from the bank's reporting currency. The node covers FX capital requirements under the simplified standardised approach and the treatment of FX positions in the banking book for internal risk transfer purposes. Applies to banks computing regulatory capital for market risk.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

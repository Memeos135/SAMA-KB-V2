# Dealing with E-Commerce Payment Technical Support Companies

A SAMA circular clarifying that technical connectivity and support services for e-commerce payment transactions are ancillary to payment services and therefore fall outside the current SAMA licensing perimeter, relying on the Implementing Regulation of the Payments and Payment Services Law provision excluding technical service providers from 'related payment services'. It fixes the boundary: such firms must obtain the necessary technical permits from Saudi Payments and must not contract with or onboard merchants, perform merchant KYC/verification, AML/CTF or anti-fraud checks, or complete settlement and deposit of funds into merchant accounts. It binds banks (which must deal only with licensed PSPs and report unlicensed activity to SAMA) and licensed PSPs (which may contract with technical support firms subject to outsourcing rules and mada operational, technical and fee requirements); it replaces an earlier circular on the same subject.

**Regimes:** payments, AML/CTF, governance/risk

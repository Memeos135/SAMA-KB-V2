# Template KM2 - TLAC Key Metrics

Template KM2 is a fixed-format Pillar 3 disclosure template setting out key metrics on total loss-absorbing capacity (TLAC) available and TLAC requirements measured at resolution group level, under both single point of entry and multiple point of entry resolution approaches. It prescribes the reported items (TLAC available, RWA, leverage exposure measure, TLAC ratios, subordination exemption indicators), the quarterly frequency, the five-period reporting series (T to T-4), and the requirement for narrative explanation of material changes. It is mandatory only for resolution groups of G-SIBs, with the template reproduced per resolution group under MPE.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

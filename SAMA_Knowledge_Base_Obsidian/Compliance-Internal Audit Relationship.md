# Compliance-Internal Audit Relationship

A node within SAMA's Compliance Principles circular for banks and commercial banks operating in the Kingdom, which replaced the earlier regulatory compliance manual and sets out board and senior management responsibilities, the definition and independence of the compliance function, compliance policy, compliance risk, and the compliance unit's staffing and outsourcing arrangements. The relevant point here is how compliance sits alongside internal audit as a separate, independent control function whose work is subject to audit review rather than performed by it. It binds locally incorporated banks and licensed branches of foreign banks (with the branch's senior officer standing in for the board), and was issued for guidance before becoming mandatory.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, AML/CTF

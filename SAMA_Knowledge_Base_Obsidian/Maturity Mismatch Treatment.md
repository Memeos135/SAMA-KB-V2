# Maturity Mismatch Treatment

Specifies when credit protection with a maturity shorter than the underlying exposure may be recognised: not at all under the simple approach for financial collateral, and otherwise only where original maturity is at least one year and residual maturity at least three months, with a prescribed pro-rata adjustment to the protection value. It requires conservative measurement of both exposure and hedge maturities (including embedded call options) and extends the treatment to securitisation and synthetic securitisation exposures. Binds banks recognising credit risk mitigation for capital purposes.

**Regimes:** banking prudential

# Non-Performing Loan Securitization

Chapter 23 of SAMA's Minimum Capital Requirements for Credit Risk defines an NPL securitization by reference to the underlying pool's W variable being at or above 90% at the origination cut-off date and at any later replenishment or restructuring date, and limits eligible underlying exposures to loans and loan-equivalent or subparticipation instruments (re-securitizations excluded). It restricts use of SEC-IRBA where the foundation approach is used for KIRB and applies a risk-weight floor to exposures under SEC-IRBA, SEC-SA and the look-through approach. SAMA retains discretion to impose a stricter definition, including a higher W threshold or criteria for non-delinquent exposures; it binds banks holding or originating such exposures.

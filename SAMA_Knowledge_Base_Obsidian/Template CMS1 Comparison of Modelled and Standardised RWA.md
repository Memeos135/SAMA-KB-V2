# Template CMS1: Comparison of Modelled and Standardised RWA

A Pillar 3 template comparing RWA produced by internally modelled approaches that SAMA has approved against RWA that would result from applying the full standardised approach, split by credit risk, counterparty credit risk, securitisation, market risk, operational risk and residual RWA. Its instructions define the standardised benchmark for each risk row and how totals are aggregated. Relevant to banks permitted to use modelled approaches, supporting comparability and the output floor.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

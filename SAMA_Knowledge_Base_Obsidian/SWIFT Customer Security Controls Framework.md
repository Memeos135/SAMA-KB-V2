# SWIFT Customer Security Controls Framework

Node covering the SWIFT Customer Security Controls Framework as one of the mandatory international industry standards a Member Organization must comply with under the SAMA Cyber Security Framework, together with PCI-DSS and EMV. It links to the framework's regulatory compliance and risk-response provisions, including the rule that accepted cyber risks must not conflict with SAMA regulations. Relevant to institutions operating SWIFT messaging and cross-border payment interfaces.

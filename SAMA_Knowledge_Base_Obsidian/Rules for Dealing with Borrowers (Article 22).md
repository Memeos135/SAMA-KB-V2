# Rules for Dealing with Borrowers (Article 22)

Article 22 (Section Six) of the Consumer Financing Regulations on ongoing conduct toward Borrowers: at least quarterly account statements (written or electronic, as pre-agreed) disclosing statement period dates, opening/closing balances, draw-downs, Term Cost charged, and fees and charges. It also requires at least 60 calendar days' advance notice via Guaranteed Communication before changing charges or payment methods, with the Borrower obliged to keep contact details current (failure releasing the Creditor from Article 22.5 liabilities). Related context includes affordability limits (33.33% of Gross Salary; 25% of pension), mandatory Licensed Credit Bureau checks with prior Borrower consent, and credit information registration and updating.

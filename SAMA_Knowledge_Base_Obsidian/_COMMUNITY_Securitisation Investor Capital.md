# Securitisation Investor Capital

## Why this community

Pillar 3 disclosure of a bank's capital treatment where it acts as investor in securitisation exposures, covering exposure amounts, risk-weight bands and resulting capital requirements. It sits within the securitisation framework rather than the ordinary credit-risk framework.

## How members connect

- Single-template cluster: the SEC4 template is the investor-side leg of the securitisation disclosure series, paired with the originator/sponsor templates and the qualitative securitisation narrative.
- Depends on securitisation defined terms (originator, sponsor, investor, resecuritisation) and on the hierarchy of securitisation risk-weight approaches set in the capital adequacy rules that the template merely reports.
- Obligation chain runs from the Basel-aligned capital framework adopted by SAMA down to the periodic disclosure requirement the template operationalises.

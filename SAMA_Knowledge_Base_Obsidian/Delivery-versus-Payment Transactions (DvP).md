# Delivery-versus-Payment Transactions (DvP)

Concept node for delivery-versus-payment transactions within the capital treatment of unsettled transactions and failed trades. DvP systems provide simultaneous exchange of securities for cash, exposing the bank to loss on the difference between the agreed settlement price and current market price; a capital requirement arises where payment has not taken place five business days after the settlement date, while non-DvP legs trigger a requirement from the end of the business day. Banks must monitor failed trades from the first day of failure; SAMA may waive requirements in a system-wide settlement or clearing failure, and a counterparty's failure to settle is not itself a default for credit risk purposes.

**Regimes:** banking prudential

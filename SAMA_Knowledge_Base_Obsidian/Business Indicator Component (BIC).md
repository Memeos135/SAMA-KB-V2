# Business Indicator Component (BIC)

Defines the Business Indicator Component (BIC), obtained by applying the prescribed marginal coefficients/buckets to the Business Indicator, and forming the base of the operational risk capital requirement before the Internal Loss Multiplier is applied. It is the benchmark against which the Loss Component is compared to set the ILM above or below one, and is the sole basis of the charge for banks lacking sufficient loss data. Applies to SAMA-licensed banks.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

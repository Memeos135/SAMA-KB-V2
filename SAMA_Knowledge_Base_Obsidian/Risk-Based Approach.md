# Risk-Based Approach

This node covers the risk-based approach (RBA) as set out in SAMA's AML/CTF guidance for financial institutions, describing the sequence of steps from identifying and assessing ML/TF risks through to implementing mitigating preventive measures and reviewing/updating the approach. It fixes an update cadence of at least once every two years or whenever risk factors change, and requires customer risk classification (e.g. high/medium/low) proportionate to the institution's size and business. It binds financial institutions supervised by SAMA, and drives the intensity of CDD, monitoring and senior management approval for high-risk customers.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, governance/risk

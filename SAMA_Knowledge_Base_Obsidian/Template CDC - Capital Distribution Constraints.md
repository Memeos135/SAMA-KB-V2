# Template CDC - Capital Distribution Constraints

Pillar 3 template requiring banks to disclose the capital ratio thresholds at which capital distribution constraints are triggered, covering CET1 needed for minimum ratios, buffer requirements (conservation, G-SIB surcharge, countercyclical), Pillar 2 add-ons, TLAC interaction and the leverage ratio buffer. Disclosure is annual and fixed-format, with narrative explanation where constraints have actually been imposed and a link to SAMA's published buffer/stacking-hierarchy rules. Mandatory for all banks subject to SAMA's Pillar 3 framework.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

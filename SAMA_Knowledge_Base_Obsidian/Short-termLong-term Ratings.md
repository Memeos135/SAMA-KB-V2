# Short-term/Long-term Ratings

This node sets out how short-term external ratings may be used for risk weighting: they are treated as issue-specific, cannot be generalised to other short-term exposures or used for unrated long-term exposures, and apply only to bank and corporate exposures under a fixed risk-weight table. It also governs the interaction with the general preferential treatment for short-term interbank exposures, the ECAI eligibility criteria, the bar on using one group entity's rating for another entity, and the prohibition on unsolicited ratings. It binds banks applying the standardised approach to credit risk.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

# Template CVA3: Standardised Approach SA-CVA

Template CVA3 is the Pillar 3 quantitative template reporting RWA and capital requirements for CVA risk calculated under the standardised approach (SA-CVA). It sits with Table CVAB and Template CVA4 (SA-CVA RWA flow statement) and feeds the CVA line of the RWA overview disclosures. It binds SAMA-supervised banks that have obtained SAMA approval to apply SA-CVA to some or all of their CVA risk exposures.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

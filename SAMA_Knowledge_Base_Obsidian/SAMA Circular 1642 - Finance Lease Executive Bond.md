# SAMA Circular 1642 - Finance Lease Executive Bond

SAMA circular reaffirming that lessors are responsible for the accuracy of the data they submit when applying to a finance lease contract registration company for issuance of executive-bond (writ of execution) extracts for a registered finance lease contract. Lessors must only apply where one of the cases set out in the existing controls and procedures for requesting and issuing such extracts is met, and bear liability for any prejudice to lessee rights arising from incorrect data; registration companies remain obliged to verify the lessor's entitlement and obtain the lessor's written undertakings before issuing extracts. Binds banks, finance companies and SAMA-licensed finance lease contract registration companies.

# IFRS 9

A concept node anchoring the IFRS 9 accounting framework as the reference standard used across SAMA's credit classification and provisioning rules for finance companies. It supplies the definitions relied on by the rules — probability of default, exposure at default, loss given default, financial asset, expected credit loss (12-month and lifetime), past due and the Stage 1/2/3 allocation, including SAMA's regulatory-only bifurcation into Stages 2A/2B and 3A/3B mapped to earlier regulatory categories. Relevant to finance companies applying the rules and to those reconciling accounting treatment with SAMA prudential reporting.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, banking prudential, governance/risk

# Guiding Principles of Pillar 3 Disclosures

Sets the scope, effective date and five guiding principles for banks' Pillar 3 disclosures — clarity, comprehensiveness, meaningfulness, consistency over time and comparability across banks — plus the requirement for a formal board-approved disclosure policy and assurance over Pillar 3 data. It applies to all domestic banks on both consolidated and standalone bases and expressly excludes foreign bank branches operating in Saudi Arabia; requirements took effect 1 January 2023 with first reporting as at 31 March 2023.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

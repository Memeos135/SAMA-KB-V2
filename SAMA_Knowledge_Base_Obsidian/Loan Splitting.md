# Loan Splitting

Defines loan splitting as a restructuring technique addressing collateral and cash-flow shortfalls: the loan is divided into a serviceable portion repaid in equal principal-and-interest installments from sustainable cash flow, and an "excess loan" portion that may be subordinated, tranched, non-interest bearing, and repaid from asset sale proceeds or a bullet payment at maturity. It sits among the long-term restructuring options available to banks and is typically applied to marginal borrowers. Guidance addressed to banks structuring workouts.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential

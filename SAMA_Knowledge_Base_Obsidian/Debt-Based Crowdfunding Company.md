# Debt-Based Crowdfunding Company

A Debt-Based Crowdfunding Company is a joint-stock company licensed by SAMA to operate a debt-based crowdfunding platform matching Participants' funds with financing for Institutional Beneficiaries. Its obligations include keeping Participants' funds in a segregated, dedicated Finance Amount Management Account used only for collecting and managing the Finance Amount, restricting repayment to electronic channels, obtaining prior consent for automatic participation with a right to cancel, and making platform disclosures including a pop-up risk acknowledgment from Participants.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, consumer protection, governance/risk

# SAMA OTC Derivatives Trade Repository Rules

This node sits within SAMA's New Banking Products and Services Regulation (effective 1 March 2024, superseding the 2017 guidelines) and covers the derivatives-specific conditions banks must satisfy before launching new products, including suitability, disclosure of profit/loss scenarios for structured products, bona fide economic purpose, internal capacity to manage the risks, and no adverse impact on SAIBOR/SAR. It cross-refers to SAMA's 2019 Rules on Trade Repository Reporting and Risk Mitigation Requirements for OTC Derivatives Contracts (as updated) and to a separate circular on SAR-versus-foreign-currency derivatives, and sets the documentation/checklist required for notification or non-objection. It binds banks licensed in KSA introducing new derivative or structured products.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential, consumer protection, other

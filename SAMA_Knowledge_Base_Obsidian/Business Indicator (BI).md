# Business Indicator (BI)

Defines the Business Indicator (BI), the financial-statement-based proxy for a bank's operational risk exposure, built from the interest, lease and dividend component, the services component and the financial component, averaged over three years. It is the input to the Business Indicator Component and must be disclosed with its subcomponents (Template OR2), including any SAMA-approved exclusion of divested activities. Binds all SAMA-supervised banks.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

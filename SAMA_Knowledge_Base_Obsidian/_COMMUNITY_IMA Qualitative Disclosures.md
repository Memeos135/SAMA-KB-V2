# IMA Qualitative Disclosures

## Why this community

Qualitative Pillar 3 disclosure specific to banks permitted to use an internal models approach for market risk, covering model scope, supervisory approval, validation, stress testing and backtesting arrangements.

## How members connect

- Single-member cluster that is conditional in scope: it binds only institutions with supervisory permission to use internal models.
- Supplements the general qualitative market-risk table, adding model-governance detail on top of the baseline narrative.
- Ties back to the approval and ongoing-use conditions in the capital adequacy framework, which determine whether the disclosure obligation is triggered at all.

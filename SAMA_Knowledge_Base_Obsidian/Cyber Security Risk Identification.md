# Cyber Security Risk Identification

Node on the risk identification and treatment stage of SAMA's Cyber Security Framework risk management cycle, including documenting treatment options (accept, avoid, transfer/share, mitigate) against the organization's risk appetite. Risk acceptance requires business-owner sign-off, reporting to the cyber security committee, and must not conflict with SAMA regulations; mitigation requires control selection with cost, feasibility and compliance assessment. Binds SAMA-supervised Member Organizations.

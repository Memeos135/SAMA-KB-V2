# Report on Total Remunerations for the Board of Directors and Committees

An annex/reporting template setting out the disclosure format for total remuneration paid to board of directors and board committee members, broken down by membership type (executive, non-executive, independent) and by component: fixed amounts, attendance allowances, in-kind benefits, profit share, granted shares, chairman/managing director/secretary remuneration, and other items, all in SAR thousands. It is a disclosure schedule supporting governance and remuneration transparency obligations for supervised entities' boards.

**Regimes:** governance/risk

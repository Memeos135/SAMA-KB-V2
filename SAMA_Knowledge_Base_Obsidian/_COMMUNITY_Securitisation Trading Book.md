# Securitisation Trading Book

## Why this community

Pillar 3 disclosure of securitisation exposures held in the trading book, the counterpart to banking-book securitisation disclosure.

## How members connect

- Single-member cluster whose scope is defined by the banking-book/trading-book boundary; correct book allocation determines which template applies and prevents omission or double-counting.
- Relies on the same securitisation defined terms and role classifications used in the qualitative securitisation disclosure, so it should be read alongside that narrative table.
- Exposure amounts disclosed here feed the capital-requirement view presented in the originator/sponsor and investor capital templates.

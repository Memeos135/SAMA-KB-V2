# Currency Banding and Inspection

## Why this community

Currency handling and cash-processing standards: how banknotes are inspected, sorted and presented (banding) by institutions dealing in SAMA-issued currency.

## How members connect

- Both are operational currency-issuance instructions addressed to cash-handling institutions, forming a single quality-control obligation set.
- Sequencing: inspection/fitness sorting determines outcome, banding card templates prescribe the mandated labelling of the processed bundles.
- Templates are the prescribed form supporting the substantive inspection duty — non-use of the template is itself a compliance gap.

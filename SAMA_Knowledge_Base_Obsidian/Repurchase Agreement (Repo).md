# Repurchase Agreement (Repo)

Definitional node from SAMA's Guidelines on Repurchase Agreement (Repo) setting out the core terms of the Saudi repo regime: repo and reverse repo, repo buyer (cash provider) and repo seller (securities provider), custodian, held-in-custody, eligible security, and market infrastructure references (SARIE, Tadawul, SOCPA, ICMA/GMRA). It also frames scope: the guidelines cover SAR-denominated outright sale/purchase of eligible securities with a repurchase or resale commitment, and bind all repo market participants (the eligible counterparties in Section 9), with the standard SAMA-approved Master Repurchase Agreement (2020) as the mandatory governing document under Saudi law.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential, other

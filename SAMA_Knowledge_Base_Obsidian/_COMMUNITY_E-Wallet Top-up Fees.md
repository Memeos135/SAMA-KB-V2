# E-Wallet Top-up Fees

## Why this community

Pricing and fee treatment for e-wallet funding transactions within the national payment scheme, specifically amendments to top-up charges applied through mada.

## How members connect

- Single-instrument cluster: a scheme-level fee amendment binding on issuers, PSPs and e-money/wallet providers participating in mada.
- Interacts with the consumer protection regime on fee transparency and disclosure of charges to wallet holders.
- Sits under the payments/PSP regime, where SAMA sets or approves interchange and service pricing for scheme participants.

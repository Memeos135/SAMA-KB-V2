# Responsible Retail Financing Principles

SAMA's Responsible Retail Financing Principles (issued 1439H/2018), setting affordability, disclosure and conduct expectations for financing granted to natural-person customers. They define financier, customer, finance amount, term cost (fixed and variable), total amount payable, monthly credit obligations, gross salary and gross monthly income, and are read alongside the Banking Control Law, Finance Companies Control Law, Real Estate Finance and Finance Lease Laws, consumer finance controls, collection controls and the customer protection principles. They bind banks and finance companies licensed to carry out financing activities.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, BNPL/finance, governance/risk

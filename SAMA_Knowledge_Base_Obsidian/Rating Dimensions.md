# Rating Dimensions

Node within the IRB minimum requirements on rating system design, concerning the dimensions a bank's internal rating system must capture (borrower risk versus facility/transaction characteristics, and the separate treatment required for retail-style pools). Related surrounding material governs use of external ratings, including consistent ECAI use, multiple ratings, issuer versus issue-specific ratings and short-term ratings. Applies to banks seeking or maintaining IRB approval from SAMA.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

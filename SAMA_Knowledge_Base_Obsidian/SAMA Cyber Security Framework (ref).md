# SAMA Cyber Security Framework (ref)

A cross-reference node pointing to the SAMA Cyber Security Framework as invoked within SAMA's Business Continuity Management (BCM) Framework, which sets resilience, recovery and continuity obligations for Member Organizations supervised by SAMA. The BCM context defines BCM, IT Disaster Recovery, Maximum Acceptable Outage and related recovery objectives, and requires an approved, maintained and tested Business Continuity Plan, incident response and escalation procedures, alternative workspace, and inclusion of cyber security requirements in continuity planning. It binds all SAMA Member Organizations, which must comply and embed the requirements in their BCM programme.

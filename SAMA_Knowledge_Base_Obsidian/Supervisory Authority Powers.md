# Supervisory Authority Powers

Extract from the counter-terrorism financing law covering Chapter 9 (Supervision) and related information-exchange provisions, setting out the mandate of supervisory authorities over financial institutions, DNFBPs and non-profit organisations. It grants inspection, information-demand, sectoral risk-assessment, rule-making and international cooperation powers, and lists the graduated enforcement measures available for breaches — from written warnings and compliance orders through fines up to SAR 5 million per violation, employment bans, restriction or removal of directors and senior management, suspension of activities, and licence withdrawal. It binds supervised entities and their directors, board and executive/supervisory management personally, and also frames the Financial Intelligence Directorate's information sharing and the Permanent Committee's national CTF policy role.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, sanctions/TFS, governance/risk

# IRB Approach: Overview and Asset Class Definitions

Overview provisions of the internal ratings-based approach to credit risk, covering how banks categorise banking-book exposures into asset classes and the conditions for adopting IRB. Notably it permits a phased rollout across asset classes, business units and from foundation to advanced approaches, but requires full application within an asset class in a given business unit, an implementation plan agreed with SAMA, and no capital relief for intra-group transactions designed to reduce group capital during rollout. Binds banks seeking or using SAMA approval for F-IRB, A-IRB or supervisory slotting.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

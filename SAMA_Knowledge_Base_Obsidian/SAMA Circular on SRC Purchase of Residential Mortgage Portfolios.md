# SAMA Circular on SRC Purchase of Residential Mortgage Portfolios

SAMA circular (Arabic) permitting the Saudi Real Estate Refinance Company (SRC) to purchase residential real estate finance portfolios from real estate financiers once at least three months have elapsed from grant of the underlying finance (or from receipt of the first instalment from the beneficiary, whichever applies). It is issued as an exercise of SAMA's power to exempt certain disposals of finance assets from the general Rules on Disposal of Finance Assets or Rights Arising Therefrom, and is addressed to banks and finance companies operating in the Kingdom.

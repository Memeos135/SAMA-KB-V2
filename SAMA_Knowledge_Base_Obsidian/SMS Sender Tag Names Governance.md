# SMS Sender Tag Names Governance

A SAMA circular from the Payment Systems and Companies Control Department relaying a request from the communications and IT regulator to establish governance over the registration of bulk SMS sender tag names through a unified portal for SMS service providers, aimed at preventing misuse of text messaging. It directs supervised entities to cooperate promptly with their contracted SMS providers and complete registration within the transitional period set by the regulator. It binds payment institutions and other SAMA-supervised entities that send bulk SMS to customers.

**Regimes:** payments, consumer protection, governance/risk

# Risk Bucket

Concept node for risk buckets under the standardised approach sensitivities-based method: the prescribed groupings of risk factors by risk class to which risk weights and correlation parameters are assigned for delta, vega and curvature risk. It covers sector versus index bucket mapping for multi-underlying and index options (including the 75%-constituent test) and the liquidity-horizon calibration of weights and correlations. Applies to all banks computing market risk capital under the standardised approach.

**Regimes:** banking prudential

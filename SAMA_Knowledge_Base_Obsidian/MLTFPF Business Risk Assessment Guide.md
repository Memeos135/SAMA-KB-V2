# ML/TF/PF Business Risk Assessment Guide

SAMA's Guide on assessing business risks related to money laundering, terrorist financing and proliferation financing, issued under the SAMA Law, the AML Law and the Law on Combating Terrorism Crimes and its Financing. It sets the minimum factors a financial institution must consider when conducting its enterprise-wide business risk assessment, with chapters on definitions, governance and institutional responsibility, the stages of the assessment, and final provisions, and defines terms such as business risk, risk-based approach, inherent risk, mitigating measures, residual risk, risk appetite, customer/geographic/delivery-channel and emerging risks. It applies to all financial institutions supervised by SAMA and is expressly non-exhaustive and not a single mandated methodology, without limiting other AML/CTF/CPF obligations.

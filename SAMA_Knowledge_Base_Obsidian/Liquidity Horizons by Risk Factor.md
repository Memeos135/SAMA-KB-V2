# Liquidity Horizons by Risk Factor

Node setting out liquidity horizons assigned to risk factors in the expected shortfall calculation under the internal models approach, including how to derive the horizon for multi-sector credit and equity indices by weighted average of underlying instruments mapped to the prescribed 10/20/40/60/120-day buckets, and how instrument maturity can cap the applicable horizon. It also frames the bank-wide ES calculation for modellable risk factors without supervisory constraints on cross-risk-class correlations. Binds banks with IMA-approved trading desks.

**Regimes:** banking prudential

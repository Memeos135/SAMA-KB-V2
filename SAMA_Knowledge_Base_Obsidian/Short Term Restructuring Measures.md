# Short Term Restructuring Measures

Explains short-term (temporary) restructuring measures — such as interest-only periods, moratoria, arrears/interest capitalisation and reduced payments — which do not themselves repay the obligation but give temporary relief for an identified short-term cash-flow disruption or time for the creditor to assess the case. Their use requires documented, non-speculative evidence that income will recover or that a long-term solution is not yet possible, and the guidance sets tenor caps (not exceeding 24 months, with a shorter limit indicated for certain cases). Binds banks choosing between short-term and long-term restructuring options.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential

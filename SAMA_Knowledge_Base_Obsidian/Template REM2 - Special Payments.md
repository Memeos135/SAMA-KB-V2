# Template REM2 - Special Payments

Template REM2 is the mandatory annual Pillar 3 quantitative disclosure of special payments — guaranteed bonuses, sign-on awards and severance payments — showing number of employees and total amounts for senior management and other material risk-takers, using the employee categories defined in Table REMA. Format is flexible and banks may add narrative on significant movements. Binds all banks under SAMA's Pillar 3 Disclosure Requirements Framework.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

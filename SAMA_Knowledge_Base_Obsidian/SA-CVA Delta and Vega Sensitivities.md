# SA-CVA Delta and Vega Sensitivities

Sets out how delta and vega sensitivities of aggregate CVA and of eligible CVA hedging instruments to each risk factor are computed as inputs to the standardised approach CVA capital requirement. It requires vega sensitivities to be calculated in all cases (including where the portfolio holds no options), specifies volatility shifts for both path-generation and option pricing, permits smaller shifts and computational techniques consistent with internal risk management, and addresses index hedges. Binds banks that have obtained SAMA approval to use the SA-CVA.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

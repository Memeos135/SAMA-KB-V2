# Securitization Internal Assessment Approach (SEC-IAA)

Node for the securitisation internal assessment approach (SEC-IAA), one of the approaches in SAMA's securitisation capital hierarchy, typically used for unrated exposures to ABCP conduits where the bank maps internal assessments to external rating equivalents. It sits alongside SEC-ERBA, SEC-IRBA and SEC-SA and feeds the risk-weighted asset figures reported in the Pillar 3 credit and securitisation templates. Applies to SAMA-supervised banks seeking to use internal assessments for securitisation capital purposes, subject to supervisory approval conditions.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

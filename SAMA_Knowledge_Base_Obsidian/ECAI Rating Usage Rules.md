# ECAI Rating Usage Rules

This node governs how banks may use external credit assessment institutions (ECAIs) recognised by SAMA when risk-weighting exposures under the standardised approach. It requires consistent use of chosen ECAIs across exposure types for both risk-weighting and risk management, prohibits cherry-picking between agencies, requires use of the global rating scale (national scales must be mapped), and sets the hierarchy for issue-specific versus issuer ratings; it also records the eligibility criteria expected of ECAIs (independence, transparency, disclosure). It binds banks applying the standardised approach for credit risk.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

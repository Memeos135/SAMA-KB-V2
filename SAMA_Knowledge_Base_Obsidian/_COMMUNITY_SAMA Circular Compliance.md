# SAMA Circular Compliance

## Why this community

Baseline duty of supervised entities to observe SAMA circulars as binding supervisory instruments alongside laws, rules and regulations.

## How members connect

- Single-node cluster: the obligation is generic rather than regime-specific, so it attaches to whichever rulebook the circular amends or interprets.
- Sits at the bottom of the instrument hierarchy (law -> implementing regulation -> rules -> circular), meaning circular content can tighten or supersede earlier guidance without a rule amendment.
- Practical consequence: compliance mapping must track circular issuance dates and supersession, not only the consolidated rulebook.

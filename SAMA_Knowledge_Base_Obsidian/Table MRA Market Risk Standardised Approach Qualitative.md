# Table MRA: Market Risk Standardised Approach Qualitative

Table MRA sets the qualitative Pillar 3 disclosure requirements for market risk, requiring banks to explain their market risk strategy, governance, measurement and reporting arrangements as context for the quantitative market risk templates. It orients readers to how market risk positions are identified and managed rather than prescribing capital calculations. Binding on banks reporting under SAMA's Pillar 3 framework.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

# Ministerial Decision 112203 - Remote Customer Service Saudization

A Ministry of Human Resources and Social Development ministerial decision (No. 112203, dated 18/06/1442H) issued under the Labour Law that restricts remote customer-service roles to Saudi nationals. It covers all occupations and jobs in establishments that serve customers remotely — by telephone, email, chat/electronic channels, social media or direct interaction — and applies whether staff are engaged through direct employment or indirectly via outsourcing contracts covering work forming the entity's main activity. It binds establishments operating in KSA (including financial institutions and their service providers) and exposes non-compliant establishments to the penalties in the violations and penalties schedule.

**Regimes:** other, governance/risk

# CCP Exposure Capital

## Why this community

Capital treatment of bank exposures arising from central clearing: trade exposures and default fund contributions to central counterparties, with preferential treatment conditional on the CCP meeting qualifying (QCCP) status.

## How members connect

- Definitional hierarchy: CCP is the base defined term, and QCCP is the qualifying sub-set whose status determines which capital treatment a clearing member may apply.
- Obligation chain runs from the CCP exposure capital rules to the roles they bind (clearing member, and clients accessing clearing through it) and to the exposure types captured (trade exposures, default fund contributions).
- Calculation cross-references: default fund capital depends on the CCP's hypothetical capital requirement (KCCP), which in turn feeds the per-clearing-member charge (KCMi) — a change in QCCP status cascades through all three.
- Consequence for a decision: loss or absence of QCCP status re-characterises the same exposure into a materially harsher capital outcome, so counterparty status verification is the gating question.

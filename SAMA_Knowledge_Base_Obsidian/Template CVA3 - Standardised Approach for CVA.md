# Template CVA3 - Standardised Approach for CVA

Template CVA3 is the Pillar 3 quantitative disclosure template for CVA risk risk-weighted assets calculated under the standardised approach (SA-CVA), listed in section 23 of SAMA's Pillar 3 Disclosure Requirements Framework alongside Table CVAB and Template CVA4. It applies to banks meeting the CVA materiality threshold in SAMA's revised counterparty credit risk framework and using SA-CVA for part or all of their CVA exposures. Note that the retrieved context for this node contains generic RWA/approach definitions from other templates; open the source for the actual CVA3 rows and instructions.

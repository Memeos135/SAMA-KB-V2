# ABCP Conduit / Programme

Defines the terminology used for asset-backed commercial paper structures within SAMA's credit risk capital rules — conduit (the special purpose vehicle issuing commercial paper), programme, asset pool, investor, obligor, seller, sponsor and transaction — and sets out how the simple, transparent and comparable (STC) criteria for short-term securitizations are applied. It distinguishes conduit-level exposures, where STC compliance must be met at both conduit and transaction level, from transaction-level exposures, where only the supported transactions' criteria matter, and imposes representation and warranty duties on the sponsor regarding asset nature and homogeneity. It binds SAMA-supervised banks holding or sponsoring exposures to ABCP conduits for regulatory capital purposes.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

# Independent Audit Function

This section requires financial institutions to subject their AML/CTF controls, policies and procedures to independent testing of adequacy and effectiveness, referencing Article 14/1 of the AML Law Implementing Regulations and Article 18 of the Implementing Regulations of the Law on Combating Terrorism Crimes and its Financing. The auditor must be independent of the AML/CTF function and uninvolved in the areas audited, must apply a risk-based scope focused on higher-risk areas, and senior management must remediate identified weaknesses; extension of scope to the external auditor is to be considered where necessary. It binds SAMA-supervised financial institutions and their internal/external audit functions.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, governance/risk

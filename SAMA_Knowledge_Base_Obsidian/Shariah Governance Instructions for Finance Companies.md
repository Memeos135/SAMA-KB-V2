# Shariah Governance Instructions for Finance Companies

SAMA's Shariah Governance Instructions for Finance Companies (issued under the Finance Companies Control Law and its Implementing Regulation, effective 1 May 2023), setting a minimum standard for Shariah governance and compliance. It allocates roles to the Board, senior management and the Shariah Committee, and covers Shariah supervision, committee operating controls, member independence criteria and confidentiality. Mandatory for finance companies licensed for real estate finance, productive assets finance, SME finance, finance lease, credit card finance and consumer finance; indicative for other licensed finance activities unless SAMA requires otherwise.

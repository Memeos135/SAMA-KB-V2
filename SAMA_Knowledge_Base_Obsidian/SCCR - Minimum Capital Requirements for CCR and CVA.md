# SCCR - Minimum Capital Requirements for CCR and CVA

Reference node for SCCR, the Basel-derived counterparty credit risk and credit valuation adjustment standard as adopted by SAMA, cited in the disclosure instructions as the basis for the CCR and CVA rows of the RWA overview. It separates counterparty credit risk capital charges from general credit risk and identifies the CVA capital requirement as a distinct component. It binds banks computing and disclosing capital for derivative, securities financing and other counterparty exposures.

**Regimes:** banking prudential

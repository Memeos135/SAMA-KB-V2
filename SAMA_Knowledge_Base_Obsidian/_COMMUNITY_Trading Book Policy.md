# Trading Book Policy

## Why this community

Trading book boundary and governance: how a bank documents which positions and organisational units are treated as trading activity for prudential and market risk capital purposes.

## How members connect

- The policy statement is the governing document that defines and evidences trading book classification; the trading desk is the organisational unit whose activity that policy scopes.
- Obligation chain runs from board/senior management approval of the policy to desk-level mandates, limits and reporting.
- Both terms feed the market risk capital regime, so classification decisions have direct capital-treatment consequences.

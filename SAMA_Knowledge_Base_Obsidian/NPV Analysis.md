# NPV Analysis

Concept node explaining net present value analysis as the comparative tool for selecting the workout option that maximises recovery to the bank. It sets out discounting of net cash flows (principal, profit/interest and fees less out-of-pocket costs) at the bank's standard risk-adjusted discount rate or, alternatively, the original effective interest rate used for IFRS 9 provisioning, and lists the parameters to compare across restructuring, enforcement/legal action and insolvency scenarios.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential

# Rules Governing Calculation of Annual Percentage Rate (APR)

SAMA Rules standardising how the Annual Percentage Rate is calculated and disclosed for retail lending, including definitions (APR, amount of finance, total cost of finance, total amount payable by the borrower), a mandatory net-present-value calculation formula, and required use of SAMA's Excel-based APR Calculator. They bind finance providers — banks and finance companies licensed for retail lending — across advertising, offer stage, contracts, periodic statements and any other APR disclosure, including calculators published on their websites. They are read alongside the Finance Companies Control Law and its Implementing Regulation, consumer finance regulations and related disclosure rules.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, BNPL/finance, banking prudential

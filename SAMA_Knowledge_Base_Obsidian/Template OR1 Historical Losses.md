# Template OR1: Historical Losses

Template OR1 is the Pillar 3 disclosure of aggregate operational loss experience over the past ten years, net of recoveries and of excluded losses, at loss event thresholds of SAR 44,600 and SAR 446,000, together with whether losses feed the internal loss multiplier (ILM). It is mandatory for banks in the second or third business indicator bucket and for first-bucket banks with SAMA approval to use internal loss data, disclosed annually in fixed format with narrative on new loss exclusions. Retrospective-disclosure relief does not apply, so all prior periods must be shown unless SAMA has permitted fewer years transitionally.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

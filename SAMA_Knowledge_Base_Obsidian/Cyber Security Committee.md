# Cyber Security Committee

The governance body within a Member Organization to which cyber security matters — including accepted risks within risk appetite — are reported and escalated, operating under the Board's ultimate responsibility for cyber security. It supports oversight of the cyber security policy, roles and responsibilities, budget allocation and approval of key framework documents. Relevant to Member Organizations assessing governance-layer maturity under the SAMA Cyber Security Framework.

**Regimes:** governance/risk

# Compliance Policy

Defined-term node within SAMA's Compliance Principles for banks and commercial banks operating in the Kingdom (issued under the SAMA Charter and the Banking Control Law, replacing the earlier compliance manual). It designates the Compliance Policy as the document approved by the local bank's board — or by the branch manager for foreign bank branches — setting out overall compliance responsibilities, the compliance unit's powers, and the bank's methodology for managing non-compliance risk, with mandatory content elements cross-referenced to Principle 1. It binds all licensed local banks and foreign bank branches in KSA.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, AML/CTF, banking prudential

# Electronic Payment for Personal Services

SAMA circular extending the electronic payment acceptance mandate to personal-services activities, requiring banks and payment service providers to be ready to receive merchant account and e-wallet opening requests and requests for SAMA-authorised payment means (POS devices or QR codes) through branches, website and unified call centre. It also requires use of the Merchant Category Codes designated for this sector and internal briefing of staff. It binds banks, PSPs operating in the Kingdom and Saudi Payments.

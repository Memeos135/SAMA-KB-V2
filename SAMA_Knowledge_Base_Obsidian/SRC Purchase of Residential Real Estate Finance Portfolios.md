# SRC Purchase of Residential Real Estate Finance Portfolios

SAMA circular permitting the Saudi Real Estate Refinance Company (SRC) to purchase residential real estate finance portfolios from real estate financiers after a shortened seasoning period of at least one month from the date the related financing was granted, or one month from receipt of the first instalment from the beneficiary, whichever is later, with recourse to the originator for six months from the financing date. It replaces the previous three-month seasoning requirement set in the earlier circular. Addressed to banks, finance companies operating in KSA and SRC.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, banking prudential

# Real Estate Registration Circular

## Why this community

Circular-level instruction concerning real estate registration and its treatment by supervised financial institutions, relevant to collateral, title verification and real estate finance documentation.

## How members connect

- Standalone circular: binding supervisory instruction issued under SAMA's general authority over supervised entities.
- Cross-regime relevance to real estate finance and mortgage collateral requirements, and to customer/property documentation held for onboarding and enforcement.

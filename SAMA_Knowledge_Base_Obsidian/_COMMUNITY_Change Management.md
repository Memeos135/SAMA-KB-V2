# Change Management

## Why this community

Controlled change to information assets and IT environments as a supervised cyber security control domain under the SAMA Cyber Security Framework, where uncontrolled change is treated as a source of operational and cyber risk.

## How members connect

- Single-node cluster: the change management control stands alone in the graph but sits within the Framework's control-domain hierarchy (Framework -> domain -> subdomain control requirements).
- Legally it is an obligation on the regulated member organisation's board/senior management to define, approve and periodically review a change process, not merely a technical practice.
- Interacts with incident, vulnerability and infrastructure security domains, since remediation and patching are executed through the approved change path.

# Market Risk Standardised Qualitative

## Why this community

Pillar 3 qualitative disclosure for banks using the standardised approach to market risk — describing the risk strategy, structures and processes underlying standardised market risk capital charges.

## How members connect

- Single-member cluster: this table is the entry point of the standardised market risk disclosure chain and precedes the related quantitative market risk templates.
- Presupposes the trading-book scope and the standardised approach eligibility rules in the market risk capital framework.
- Parallel obligation to the internal models qualitative table: which one applies depends on the approach the bank is approved to use.

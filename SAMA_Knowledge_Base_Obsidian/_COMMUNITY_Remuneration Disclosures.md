# Remuneration Disclosures

## Why this community

Pillar 3 remuneration transparency: qualitative governance of the remuneration policy plus quantitative disclosure of amounts awarded, special payments and deferred/retained pay for material risk takers.

## How members connect

- The qualitative table sets the policy, governance bodies and scope of covered staff; the three quantitative templates report figures against that same defined population.
- All three templates cross-reference the policy table, so definitions of 'senior management' and 'material risk takers' flow one-way from the qualitative table into the numeric disclosures.
- Together they operationalise the risk-alignment obligations of the remuneration/governance regime — deferral, malus and clawback become observable through the deferred remuneration template.

# Corporate Governance and Risk Management

Chapter 3 of the DTFC Regulations setting SAMA's internal governance and risk management requirements for deposit taking finance companies, proportionate to the nature, scale and complexity of their business. It requires a clear organisational structure, job descriptions for each senior manager tied to SAMA fit and proper pre-approval, a Management Responsibilities Description Document, and segregation of functions. Associated material addresses the compliance officer's role, KYC account acceptance standards, ongoing monitoring of accounts and transactions, suspicious transaction reporting to the FIU with notification to SAMA, and risk-based classification of accounts.

# Finance Contract Summary Form

A SAMA circular issuing the standard "Finance Contract Summary" form, grounded in Article 79 of the Implementing Regulation of the Finance Companies Control Law and Article 5(1) of the updated Consumer Finance Regulations. The form must appear at the front of every finance contract subject to those instruments and discloses beneficiary details, total cost of finance, term cost, administrative and real-estate valuation fees, instalment count and amounts, first/last due dates, total amount payable, and cross-references to key contractual clauses (including early repayment, collateral shortfall handling and termination), with a notice that the summary does not replace reading the contract. It binds banks and finance companies operating in the Kingdom.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, consumer protection

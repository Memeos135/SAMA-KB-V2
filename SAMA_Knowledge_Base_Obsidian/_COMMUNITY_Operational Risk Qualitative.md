# Operational Risk Qualitative

## Why this community

Pillar 3 narrative disclosure of the bank's operational risk management framework — policies, governance, and the business lines and event types to which the framework applies.

## How members connect

- Single-member qualitative table that provides the interpretive context for the quantitative operational risk templates in the adjacent cluster.
- Depends on operational risk defined terms (business indicator, loss event) established in the operational risk capital rules.
- Disclosure-layer obligation: it reports on compliance with the substantive operational risk governance requirements rather than creating them.

# Template CVA1: Reduced Basic Approach BA-CVA

Template CVA1 is a mandatory, fixed-format semiannual Pillar 3 template reporting the components of RWA under the reduced basic approach (BA-CVA) for CVA risk, split between systematic and idiosyncratic aggregations. Its total links to the CVA line in the RWA overview template (OV1) where the bank uses only the reduced BA-CVA, and banks must add narrative on hedges even where those hedges are not recognised. It binds SAMA-supervised banks measuring any part of CVA RWA under the reduced BA-CVA.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

# Primary Dealer Government Securities

## Why this community

Participation arrangements for government debt issuance — the primary dealer role for Saudi government securities and the supervisory expectations SAMA places on institutions acting in that capacity.

## How members connect

- Single-member cluster: a standalone SAMA circular addressed to institutions engaged in the government securities market.
- Sits in the markets/capital-raising regime rather than AML or consumer protection; linkage to other clusters would run through licensing and governance obligations of the participating institution.
- No internal cross-references captured in the graph, so obligations must be read on the face of the circular itself.

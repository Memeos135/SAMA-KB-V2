# Basel III LCR Guidelines Jan 2013

The framing and structure of SAMA's circular adopting the BCBS January 2013 Basel III LCR standard, including general and specific guidance notes, the prudential return, FAQs and national discretions, with paragraph numbering mapped to the BCBS text. It covers the objective of the LCR, the phase-in of the minimum ratio from 60% (2015) to 100% (2019), permitted use of the HQLA buffer under stress subject to SAMA assessment, and the Level 1 / Level 2 / Level 2B asset composition and caps (40% and 15%). Binds Saudi banks for LCR calculation and reporting; NSFR remains under the earlier 2012 circular.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

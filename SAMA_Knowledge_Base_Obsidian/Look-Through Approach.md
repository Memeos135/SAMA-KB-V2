# Look-Through Approach

The look-through approach requires a bank to treat the underlying constituents of index instruments, multi-underlying options and equity investments in funds as if held directly when calculating delta and curvature risk. It sets the narrow conditions for opting out (widely recognised listed indices meeting constituent-count, concentration and market-capitalisation tests), excludes index CTP instruments from decomposition, and demands consistent application over time and across identical instruments. Binds banks using the standardised approach for market risk.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

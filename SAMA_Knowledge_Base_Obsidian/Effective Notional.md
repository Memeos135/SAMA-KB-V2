# Effective Notional

Concept node for the effective notional of a derivative trade, computed from the adjusted notional multiplied by the maturity factor and the supervisory delta, then aggregated at entity, maturity-bucket, hedging-set and asset-class level to derive add-ons. The source sets it out through worked examples for credit, interest rate and margined netting sets. Relevant to banks computing counterparty credit risk exposure amounts under SAMA's minimum capital requirements.

**Regimes:** banking prudential

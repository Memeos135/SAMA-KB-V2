# Credit Risk Stage Classification

A concept node covering how finance companies must allocate credit exposures across Stages 1, 2 and 3 and the conditions for moving between them, under SAMA's classification and provisioning rules issued pursuant to the Finance Companies Control Law. It sets documented re-staging criteria from Stage 2 to Stage 1 (no exposure over 30 days past due, a 90-day cure period for non-retail, improvement in the borrower's position and removal of the SICR indicators), with a shorter 60-day cure period for retail customers, and establishes a Stage 2A/2B bifurcation for quarterly prudential reporting only, not accounting. Binds SAMA-licensed finance companies and their regulatory reporting.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, banking prudential, governance/risk

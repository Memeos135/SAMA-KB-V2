# Template CR10: IRB Specialised Lending Slotting

A Pillar 3 disclosure template within SAMA's Pillar 3 Disclosure Requirements Framework (Dec 2022, v2.1) covering IRB specialised lending exposures risk-weighted under the supervisory slotting approach. It sits in the credit risk section alongside other IRB templates (e.g. CR7 on the RWA effect of credit derivatives as CRM, CR9 on PD backtesting) and is relevant only where SAMA has approved the bank's use of IRB/slotting. It binds SAMA-supervised banks subject to Pillar 3 disclosure at the regulatory consolidation level.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

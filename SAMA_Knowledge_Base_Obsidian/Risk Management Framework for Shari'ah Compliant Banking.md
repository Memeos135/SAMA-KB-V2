# Risk Management Framework for Shari'ah Compliant Banking

SAMA's Risk Management Framework for Shari'ah Compliant Banking, issued under the SAMA Law and the Banking Control Law, setting supervisory expectations for identifying, measuring, mitigating, monitoring and reporting risks specific to Shari'ah compliant activities (credit, market, operational, liquidity, equity investment, and Shari'ah non-compliance risk). It allocates board/delegated committee and senior management responsibilities, requires an independent risk management function and CRO access to the Risk Management Committee, and addresses product structuring, contract documentation, MIS and profit sharing investment accounts. It applies to all domestic banks licensed by SAMA that conduct Shari'ah compliant banking activities, extending to majority-owned banking subsidiaries and branches, and supplements SAMA's general risk management requirements.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential

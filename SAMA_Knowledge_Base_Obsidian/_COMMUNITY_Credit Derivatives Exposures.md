# Credit Derivatives Exposures

## Why this community

Pillar 3 disclosure of credit derivative positions - protection bought and sold - as a distinct component of counterparty credit risk exposure.

## How members connect

- Single-member cluster covering a product-specific disclosure that feeds the broader CCR exposure and credit risk mitigation picture.
- Relies on notional and protection-bought/sold distinctions defined in the capital adequacy framework; the template does not create independent definitions.
- Cross-regime relevance: positions disclosed here may simultaneously be treated as credit risk mitigation for banking book exposures, so scope boundaries matter for consistency.

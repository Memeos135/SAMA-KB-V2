# Required Stable Funding (RSF)

Defines the Required Stable Funding (RSF) leg of the Net Stable Funding Ratio: the denominator measuring how much stable funding a bank's assets and off-balance-sheet exposures are presumed to need over a one-year horizon. RSF is computed by mapping carrying values of assets to prescribed categories, applying RSF factors calibrated to residual maturity, liquidity value, roll-over behaviour and credit-creation continuity, with LCR definitions applying unless stated otherwise. It binds banks subject to SAMA's NSFR scope, and SAMA may impose more stringent standards on an individual bank based on its funding risk profile.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

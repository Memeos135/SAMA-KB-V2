# Market Risk Simplified Approach

## Why this community

Quantitative Pillar 3 disclosure of market-risk capital requirements computed under the simplified standardised approach, broken down by risk class (interest rate, equity, foreign exchange, commodity, options).

## How members connect

- Single-member cluster representing the disclosure output of one permitted calculation method; its use is conditional on eligibility for the simplified approach.
- Mutually exclusive in practice with internal-models and full standardised-approach reporting for the same exposures, so the approach-eligibility rules govern which template applies.
- Read together with the general qualitative market-risk narrative, which explains the approach choice the template reflects.

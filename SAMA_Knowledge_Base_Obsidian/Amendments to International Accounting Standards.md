# Amendments to International Accounting Standards

A SAMA circular notifying supervised entities of periodic improvements and amendments to IFRS and IAS endorsed by SOCPA, listing affected standards including IFRS 1, IFRS 9 (fees in derecognition/modification of debt), IFRS 3, IAS 41, IAS 37 (cost of fulfilling a contract in onerous contracts) and IAS 16 (proceeds before intended use). It directs readers to SOCPA's international standards page for the texts and requires compliance from the date of email transmission. Addressed to finance companies operating in the Kingdom.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, BNPL/finance

# MITRE ATT&CK Framework

Concept node for the MITRE ATT&CK/kill-chain style frameworks referenced in the Operational CTI domain of SAMA's Cyber Threat Intelligence Principles as an example of an industry-standard taxonomy for attack phases and TTPs. It is cited illustratively rather than mandated by name, supporting the requirement that Member Organizations use a structured approach to analysing threat actor behaviour.

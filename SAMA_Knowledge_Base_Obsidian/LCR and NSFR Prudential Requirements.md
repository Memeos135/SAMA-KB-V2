# LCR and NSFR Prudential Requirements

A prudential-requirements section of SAMA's Profit Sharing Investment Accounts (PSIA) Rules, addressing how banks must treat PSIA funds for regulatory capital risk-weighting and in liquidity ratio calculations (LCR and NSFR). It also bars PSIA funds and the associated Profit Equalisation Reserve and Investment Risk Reserve from counting toward regulatory capital, requires concentration risk from PSIAs to be managed under the Large Exposure Rules, and subjects outsourced management of investment account holder funds to SAMA outsourcing rules; SAMA retains discretion to classify an arrangement as a PSIA. It binds banks offering profit-sharing investment accounts.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

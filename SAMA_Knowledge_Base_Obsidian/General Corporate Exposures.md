# General Corporate Exposures

A sub-node of SAMA's Minimum Capital Requirements for Credit Risk framework defining the corporate exposure class under the standardized approach, including its residual scope (incorporated entities, partnerships, trusts, funds, insurers and other financial corporates not captured as banks) and its exclusion of individuals. It splits corporates into general corporate exposures and specialized lending, and confirms that securities firms and other financial institutions are risk-weighted as corporates. It binds banks licensed and supervised by SAMA when calculating credit risk RWAs, and also carries the due diligence overlay requiring ratings-based risk weights to be adjusted upward, never downward.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

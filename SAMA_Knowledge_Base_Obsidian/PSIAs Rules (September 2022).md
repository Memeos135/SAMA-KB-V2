# PSIAs Rules (September 2022)

Cross-reference node to SAMA's PSIAs Rules (September 2022) as invoked within the Risk Management Framework for Shari'ah Compliant Banking. It captures the obligations of banks offering profit sharing investment accounts to monitor that account holders' funds are used in line with agreed terms, to comply with the risk management requirements of the PSIAs Rules and later updates, and to adopt a formal policy managing the bank's potentially conflicting roles and fiduciary/agency duties toward current account holders and PSIA holders (including asset and profit allocation across investment periods). It binds domestic banks licensed under the Banking Control Law that conduct Shari'ah compliant banking, whether full-fledged Islamic banks or Islamic Windows.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential

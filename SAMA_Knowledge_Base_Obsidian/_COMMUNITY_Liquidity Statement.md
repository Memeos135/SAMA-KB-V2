# Liquidity Statement

## Why this community

Periodic liquidity reporting to SAMA — the prudential return through which a bank evidences its holding of liquid assets against deposit and other liabilities.

## How members connect

- Single-member cluster: the return is the reporting instrument, not a standalone regime, so its obligations derive from the banking control liquidity requirements it services.
- Sits downstream of liquidity risk management expectations — the statement is the supervisory evidence of compliance with ratio and buffer obligations.
- Cross-reads with the liquidity risk management principles cluster, which supplies the governance and measurement framework behind the reported figures.

# Credit Information Law

## Why this community

SAMA's circular-level instrument communicating obligations arising under the Credit Information Law, covering the collection, sharing and use of consumer and commercial credit information.

## How members connect

- Single-node cluster sitting at the circular tier of the hierarchy (law → implementing regulation → circular), so its content is derivative of and must be read with the Credit Information Law.
- Cross-regime relevance: credit information duties intersect with consumer protection (consent, disclosure, dispute rights) and with finance/BNPL underwriting obligations that rely on credit bureau data.
- Scope caution: circular obligations bind the addressed entity classes only; do not extend them to persons outside the law's definition of credit information providers or users.

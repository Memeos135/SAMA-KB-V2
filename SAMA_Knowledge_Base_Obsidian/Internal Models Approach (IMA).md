# Internal Models Approach (IMA)

Node covering the Internal Models Approach for market risk capital and, by contrast, the modelled versus standardised split used in Pillar 3 RWA reporting. It sets when a bank may compute market risk capital using SAMA-approved internal models (expected shortfall, default risk capital, stressed ES for non-modellable risk factors) at the trading desk level, and the qualitative disclosures (Table MRB) required of IMA users on model scope, trading desk structure and key modelling choices. Binds SAMA-licensed banks that have obtained supervisory approval to use internal models, at group-wide regulatory consolidation level.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

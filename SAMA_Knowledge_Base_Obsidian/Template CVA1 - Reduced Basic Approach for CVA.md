# Template CVA1 - Reduced Basic Approach for CVA

Pillar 3 template CVA1 requires disclosure of the RWA components computed under the reduced basic approach for CVA risk — the aggregated systematic and idiosyncratic components and the total (K-reduced scaled by 12.5) — with fixed format and semiannual frequency, plus a narrative on hedge types used even where not recognised. Mandatory for banks with part or all of their CVA RWA under the reduced BA-CVA, covering only the relevant netting sets, and it links to the overall RWA template OV1.

**Regimes:** banking prudential

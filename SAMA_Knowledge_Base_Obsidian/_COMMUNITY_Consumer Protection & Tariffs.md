# Consumer Protection & Tariffs

## Why this community

Consumer protection in pricing and charging: what fees, commissions and administrative charges financial institutions may impose on consumers, how they must be disclosed, and the fair-treatment principles that constrain consumer finance terms.

## How members connect

- The tariff guide is the operative pricing schedule; the administrative fees amendments modify caps and permitted charge categories, so the guide must be read as amended.
- Consumer finance controls supply the substantive limits (e.g. charge and cost treatment in finance contracts) that the tariff guide prices, creating a rules-to-schedule relationship.
- The customer protection principles are the umbrella standards (transparency, fairness, disclosure) against which any specific tariff item is tested.
- Shared defined terms — consumer, service fee, administrative fee — determine whether a charge is permitted, capped, or prohibited.
- A duplicate tariff guide node requires version control: identify the current effective edition before advising.

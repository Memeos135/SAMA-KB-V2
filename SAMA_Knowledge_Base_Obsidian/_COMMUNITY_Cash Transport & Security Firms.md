# Cash Transport & Security Firms

## Why this community

Physical cash-in-transit and security services risk: who may be engaged to move and hold cash, and the controls applicable to secure-bag/cash transport arrangements between banks and licensed security companies.

## How members connect

- Both instruments are SAMA circulars constraining the same operational function — outsourcing of cash handling and transport to third-party security firms.
- One sets an eligibility/counterparty restriction (prohibition on dealing with agents of non-Saudi security firms), the other sets operating conditions for cash transport and secure bags; together they form a permission-then-conduct chain.
- Shared cross-regime relevance: third-party/outsourcing governance and operational risk, with implicit AML/cash-handling control consequences.

# BCBS Basel III NSFR Document Oct 2014

Node covering SAMA's adoption of the BCBS Basel III NSFR standard (October 2014): the definition of the ratio (available stable funding over required stable funding, minimum 100% on an ongoing basis), reporting frequency (at least quarterly), scope of application (consolidated basis for internationally active banks, extendable by SAMA), and specific treatment items such as derivative asset calculation, netting and margin. It also requires banks to monitor liquidity and funding risk at legal-entity, branch, subsidiary and group level. Binds banks subject to SAMA prudential supervision.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

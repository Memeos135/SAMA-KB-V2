# Table CVAB - Qualitative Disclosures for SA-CVA

Table CVAB is a qualitative Pillar 3 disclosure item within the credit valuation adjustment (CVA) risk section of SAMA's Pillar 3 Disclosure Requirements Framework, applying where the CVA materiality threshold in SAMA's revised counterparty credit risk capital rules is met. It requires banks to describe their CVA risk management framework, senior management involvement, and governance arrangements (documentation, independent control and review, independence of data sourcing from business lines). It binds all SAMA-supervised banks that use the standardised approach (SA-CVA) to compute CVA risk RWA, on an annual, flexible-format basis.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

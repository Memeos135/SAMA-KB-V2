# Banking Control Law

The Banking Control Law (Royal Decree M/5, 1386H) and its Implementation Rules (Ministerial Resolution 3/2149) form the core prudential and supervisory framework for banks operating in Saudi Arabia. Key provisions addressed include Article 12 (fit-and-proper approval for board members and senior managers, prior SAMA written approval, no dual bank directorships), Article 16 (conduct of banking business consistent with monetary and credit policy), Article 17 (periodic returns to SAMA), Article 18 (inspection, staff conduct, compliance with SAMA findings) and Article 22/25 (violations, procedures and penalties). It binds licensed banks, their boards and employees, including prohibitions on facilitating unlicensed banking or commercial activity.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

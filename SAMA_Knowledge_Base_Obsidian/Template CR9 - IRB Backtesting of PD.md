# Template CR9 - IRB Backtesting of PD

Pillar 3 template CR9 requires banks to disclose backtesting of probability of default per portfolio, comparing PD used in IRB capital calculations against observed obligor default rates over at least a five-year average annual default rate. It is mandatory for banks using A-IRB and/or F-IRB, with separate portfolio breakdowns where both approaches are used, and requires narrative on model scope and the share of RWA covered. Disclosure is annual and in flexible format.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

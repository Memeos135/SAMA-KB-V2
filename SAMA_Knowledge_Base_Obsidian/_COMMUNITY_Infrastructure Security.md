# Infrastructure Security

## Why this community

Security of network, systems and hosting infrastructure as a defined control domain, addressing hardening, segregation and protection of the environments that support regulated financial services.

## How members connect

- Isolated node in the graph; its legal force derives from the parent cyber security framework's compliance and self-assessment obligations rather than from peer cross-references.
- Supplies the technical control baseline that event/incident detection and vulnerability remediation domains presuppose.
- Relevant to outsourcing and third-party arrangements where infrastructure is hosted or operated externally but accountability remains with the member organisation.

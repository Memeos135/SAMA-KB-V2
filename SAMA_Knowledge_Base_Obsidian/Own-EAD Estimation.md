# Own-EAD Estimation

Sets out the requirements for banks' own estimates of exposure at default under the advanced IRB approach: EAD is the expected gross exposure at obligor default, floored at the current drawn amount for on-balance sheet items (subject to recognised netting), with the additional requirements focused on off-balance sheet facilities and conversion factors. Estimates must be long-run default-weighted averages with a margin of conservatism, must address instability in the undrawn limit factor approach, and reference data must not be capped to limits or outstanding principal. Binds SAMA-licensed banks approved for A-IRB.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

# CCR Qualitative Disclosure

## Why this community

Qualitative Pillar 3 disclosure on counterparty credit risk: the bank's objectives, methodologies and governance for managing CCR, including collateral and credit reserve policy, wrong-way risk, and the impact of a credit-rating downgrade on collateral it would have to post.

## How members connect

- Single-member cluster: the narrative table that frames and contextualises the quantitative CCR templates in the same disclosure suite.
- Ties the capital/disclosure regime to internal governance and risk-management obligations, since the narrative must describe actual policies rather than generic statements.
- Relies on CCR definitions and exposure measurement methods set in the underlying capital adequacy rules adopted by SAMA.

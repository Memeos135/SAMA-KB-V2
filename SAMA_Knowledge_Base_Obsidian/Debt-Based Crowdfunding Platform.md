# Debt-Based Crowdfunding Platform

Defines the Debt-Based Crowdfunding Platform as the web-based or other digital channel (websites, mobile apps) operated by a licensed Debt-Based Crowdfunding Company to conduct debt-based crowdfunding, sitting within the definitional chapter of SAMA's Rules for Engaging in Debt-Based Crowdfunding and linking to related terms (Debt-Based Crowdfunding Company, Institutional Beneficiary, Participant, Eligible Participant). The node also carries platform-level operating duties: segregation of Participant funds in a named Finance Amount Management Account, electronic-only repayment, opt-in and cancellable auto-participation, and disclosure of the business model, risks and party relationships including a pop-up risk acknowledgment. It binds joint-stock companies licensed by SAMA for debt-based crowdfunding.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, consumer protection, governance/risk

# Template CCR4: IRB CCR Exposures by Portfolio and PD Scale

Template CCR4 requires disclosure of the parameters (PD scale, EAD post-CRM, average PD, obligor numbers, average maturity, RWA) used to calculate counterparty credit risk capital requirements where an IRB approach is applied, excluding CVA charges and CCP-cleared exposures. Separate templates are required for F-IRB and A-IRB portfolios, with narrative on model scope and RWA coverage. Mandatory semiannually for SAMA-approved IRB banks.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

# Electronic Bank Document Verification

## Why this community

Authenticity and verification of bank-issued documents (e.g. certificates, statements, letters) when produced and validated electronically rather than on paper.

## How members connect

- Single-member cluster: the instruction stands alone as the operative rule on how banks issue and subsequently verify their own documents electronically.
- Legally it sits downstream of general banking supervision powers and interacts with e-transactions validity and consumer-facing service delivery obligations.
- Relevant to reliance questions: a document's evidentiary weight for third parties (including in CDD/KYC file build-up) depends on the issuing bank's verification mechanism.

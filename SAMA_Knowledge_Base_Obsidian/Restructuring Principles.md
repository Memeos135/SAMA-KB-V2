# Restructuring Principles

Appendix-level set of generic principles banks are encouraged to embed in their restructuring culture: treating restructuring as value-preserving rather than a cost centre, early identification of warning signs, good faith and seriousness by both sides, transparency and full disclosure between borrower and bank, prompt response to borrower proposals, adequate time for the borrower, sustainable rather than repeated short-term fixes, and confidentiality. It also explains restructuring agreement drafting choices (new agreement replacing prior documentation versus amendment of the original loan agreement), including simplified documentation for MSMEs. Expressed as encouraged good practice for banks dealing with borrowers.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential, consumer protection

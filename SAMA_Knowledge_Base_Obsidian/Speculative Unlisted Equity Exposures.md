# Speculative Unlisted Equity Exposures

Identifies "speculative unlisted equity exposures" — investments in unlisted companies held for short-term resale, venture capital or similar price-volatile positions acquired for capital gain — and assigns them a 400% risk weight, against 250% for all other equity holdings. Relevant to banks classifying private-equity, VC and pre-IPO holdings in the banking book under the standardised approach.

**Regimes:** banking prudential

# Operational Deposits

A segment of SAMA's amended Liquidity Coverage Ratio guidance dealing with the classification of retail deposits as "stable" versus "less stable" and the resulting run-off assumptions for the 30-day LCR outflow calculation. It records Saudi-specific national discretion: KSA has no deposit insurance scheme (so the reduced stable-deposit treatment is unavailable), run-off rates follow BCBS for all currencies, and foreign-currency (mainly USD, pegged) deposits are not treated as inherently less stable. It binds banks licensed and supervised by SAMA when completing the LCR prudential return.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

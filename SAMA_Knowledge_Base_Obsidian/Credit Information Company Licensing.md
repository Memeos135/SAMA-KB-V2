# Credit Information Company Licensing

Licensing chapter of the Implementing Regulation of the Credit Information Law (Royal Decree M/37), setting the prohibition on conducting credit information activity without a SAMA licence and the conditions for obtaining one. It binds founders and licensed credit information companies (bureaus): joint-stock form with head office in KSA, minimum paid-up capital of SAR 50 million, SAMA approval of constitutional documents and the credit information system, application contents and fees, five-year licence term with renewal fees and timing, grounds for licence expiry or cancellation, and prior written SAMA approval for branches, subsidiaries, mergers/acquisitions and amendments to constitutional documents. Relevant when assessing market entry, corporate change approvals, or the status of an entity handling consumer credit records.

**Regimes:** data/credit info, governance/risk

# Beneficial Owner

This node defines and operationalises the beneficial owner concept: the natural person who ultimately owns or controls the customer directly or indirectly, never a legal person. It sets the 25% ownership/control reference point for legal persons, with cascading fallback measures where no such person exists or where the controlling shareholder is suspected not to be the true beneficial owner (senior managing officials, sub-25% controllers, high-risk treatment and record-keeping of reasons). Financial institutions must verify beneficial ownership from authenticated independent sources and may not rely primarily on the customer's written statement.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF

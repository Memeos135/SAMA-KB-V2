# Consumer Finance Controls

This node addresses the Consumer Finance Regulations (Rules) as amended by the Tariff Guide circular, specifically the article obliging finance providers to comply with SAMA's instructions on administrative fees when granting finance. It is the anchor for how fee caps and fee-timing restrictions interact with consumer finance contracts and customer protection conduct rules. It binds banks and finance companies supervised by SAMA that grant consumer finance to individuals.

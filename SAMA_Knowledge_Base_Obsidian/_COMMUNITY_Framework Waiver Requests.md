# Framework Waiver Requests

## Why this community

The mechanism for an entity to request relief from a specific framework requirement, and the conditions under which SAMA-granted relief is valid and time-bound.

## How members connect

- Single-node cluster covering an exemption/derogation pathway rather than a primary duty.
- Directly complements the update process: both are framework-governance procedures, distinguished by general versus entity-specific effect.
- Decision consequence: a granted waiver narrows scope of an otherwise enforceable obligation only to its stated terms and period; it does not displace parallel obligations under other regimes.

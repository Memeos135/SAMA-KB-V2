# Basel Committee on Banking Supervision

This node captures the Basel Committee-derived standardised interest rate shock scenarios that SAMA applies to interest rate risk in the banking book (IRRBB), including the recalibrated currency-specific shock sizes (parallel, short and long) set out in the shock table. It requires banks to run six prescribed scenarios for economic value of equity and two for net interest income, per currency in which the bank holds material positions. It binds banks supervised by SAMA for IRRBB measurement and reporting purposes.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

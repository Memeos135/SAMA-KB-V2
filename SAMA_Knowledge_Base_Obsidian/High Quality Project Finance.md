# High Quality Project Finance

Part of SAMA's Minimum Capital Requirements for Credit Risk, this node sets the standardised-approach risk weights for specialised lending where no issue-specific external rating is used, and defines when a project finance exposure qualifies as "high quality" (80% instead of 100% operational-phase / 130% pre-operational). It also carries the IRB asset-class definitions of specialised lending and its sub-classes (project, object, commodities finance, IPRE, HVCRE). It binds SAMA-licensed banks calculating regulatory capital for credit risk.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

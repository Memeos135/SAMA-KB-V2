# Specific Operational Requirements for Credit Derivatives

Sets the operational conditions for recognizing credit derivatives as credit risk mitigation and the capital treatment where a bank instead sells protection. Only credit default swaps and total return swaps giving guarantee-equivalent protection are recognized (with a carve-out where total return swap income is booked without recognizing offsetting asset deterioration), while first-to-default and other nth-to-default derivatives give no capital relief and, when protection is sold, attract aggregated basket risk weights capped at 1250%. It binds SAMA-supervised banks using or writing credit protection.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

# Financial Viability Analysis

Concept node describing the quantitative assessment a bank's Workout Unit performs to determine whether a defaulted borrower is viable, feeding the choice between restructuring, debt swaps or legal proceedings. It covers indicative liquidity, solvency/leverage and profitability ratios, balance sheet review of total debt burden and priority claims (wages, taxes), and cash flow as the ultimate determinant of repayment capacity; for MSMEs with weak audited data, cash-flow based analysis is expected. Independence is required: the analysis must be done by workout staff not involved in the original loan approval.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential

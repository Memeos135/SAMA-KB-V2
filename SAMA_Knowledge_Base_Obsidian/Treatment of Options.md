# Treatment of Options

This node is the umbrella treatment of options in the market risk framework: the simplified carve-out treatment (with a capital requirement table for long cash/long put, long call and similar positions), the delta-plus method, and the scenario approach, plus the identification of option-like instruments bearing residual risks (gap, correlation and behavioural risk) for the residual risk add-on. It sets out which method a bank may use depending on whether it buys or writes options and its level of trading activity. It applies to SAMA-licensed banks holding option positions in the trading book.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

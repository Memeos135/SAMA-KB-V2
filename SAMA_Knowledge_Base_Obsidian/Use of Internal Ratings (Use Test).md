# Use of Internal Ratings (Use Test)

Node addressing the "use test" — the requirement that internal ratings and default/loss estimates actually drive credit approval, risk management, internal capital allocation and governance, not merely regulatory capital calculation. It binds banks with SAMA approval to apply IRB (F-IRB/A-IRB) approaches. The extracted context here is drawn from Pillar 3 disclosure templates (qualitative disclosures for the internal models approach to market risk and RWA definitions by approach), so confirm the paragraph range before citing.

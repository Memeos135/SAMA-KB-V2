# Rules for Requesting and Issuing Executive Bond Extracts for Registered Finance Lease Contracts

SAMA rules and procedures (Jan 2021) governing how lessors request, and licensed finance lease contract registration companies issue, executive bond extracts for registered finance lease contracts, issued under the Finance Lease Law and its Implementing Regulation, under which such an extract constitutes an executive deed for purposes of the Enforcement Law. They cover extracts for repossession of movable assets, for delivery of immovable leased assets, and for financial enforcement certificates, define the parties involved, and require all requests and issuance to be conducted electronically. Lessors are prohibited from repossessing movable assets except through companies specialised in repossession licensed by the Ministry of Justice; the rules supplement SAMA's earlier repossession controls and bind banks, finance companies and registration companies.

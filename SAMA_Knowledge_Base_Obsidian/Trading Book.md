# Trading Book

A defined-term node for the trading book under SAMA's market risk capital rules, drawn from the provisions on boundary treatment and internal risk transfers between the trading and banking books. It governs when a trading book leg of an internal risk transfer receives regulatory capital recognition, confirms that transfers from trading book to banking book get no capital recognition, and requires trading book legs to meet the same requirements as externally transacted trading book instruments. It binds SAMA-licensed banks determining market risk capital requirements.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

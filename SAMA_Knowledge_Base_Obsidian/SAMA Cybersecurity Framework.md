# SAMA Cybersecurity Framework

Concept node for the SAMA Cybersecurity Framework as invoked in the digital-only bank licensing context, alongside the SAMA BCM Framework and National Cybersecurity Authority requirements. It covers information system security, resilience and availability expectations proportionate to the proposed products and services, and the possibility that SAMA requires an applicant-funded independent third-party assessor to review technology/cybersecurity and AML/CFT arrangements at both design and implementation phases before operations commence. It binds licence applicants and licensed digital-only banks.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential, AML/CTF

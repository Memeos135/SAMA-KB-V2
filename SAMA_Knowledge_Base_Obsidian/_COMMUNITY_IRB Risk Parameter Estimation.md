# IRB Risk Parameter Estimation

## Why this community

Minimum qualifying requirements and estimation mechanics for banks using the Internal Ratings-Based approach to credit risk capital: how rating systems must be built, how PD/LGD/EAD are estimated and validated, and how mitigants, collateral and purchased receivables feed the risk-weighted asset calculation.

## How members connect

- Anchored by the IRB minimum requirements node, which acts as the parent obligation set and cross-refers to rating system design, risk quantification, validation, disclosure and leasing recognition.
- Shared defined terms drive the chain: the definitions of default and of loss condition PD, LGD and EAD estimation, which in turn feed the foundation vs advanced IRB treatments and the RWA computations for default and dilution risk.
- Credit risk mitigation is a second obligation strand: guarantees, credit derivatives and eligible collateral (real estate, financial receivables, other physical, leasing) adjust either PD or LGD rather than the exposure class.
- Supervisory fallbacks (supervisory LGD/EAD estimates, slotting for object finance) and transition arrangements sit as alternatives where a bank cannot meet own-estimate requirements — relevant to scope and approval decisions.

# Money Transfer Reception Rules

## Why this community

Conduct rules governing how exchange (money transfer) businesses receive and process inbound transfer instructions, including customer identification and record-keeping expectations at the receipt stage.

## How members connect

- Single-instrument cluster: the rules stand alone as the operative conduct standard for the receiving leg of a transfer.
- Sits at the intersection of the money exchange/transfer regime and AML/CTF wire-transfer obligations (originator/beneficiary information and CDD on the receiving side).
- Obligations attach to the licensed exchange entity, not the sending institution, so reliance on a correspondent's CDD must be assessed separately.

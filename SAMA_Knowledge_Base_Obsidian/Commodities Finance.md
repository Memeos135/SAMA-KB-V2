# Commodities Finance

Defines commodities finance as short-term specialized lending against reserves, inventories or receivables of exchange-traded commodities, repaid from sale proceeds where the borrower has no independent repayment capacity. Unrated commodities finance exposures attract a 100% risk weight under the standardized approach; issue-specific external ratings, not issuer ratings, govern where available. Applies to SAMA-supervised banks.

**Regimes:** banking prudential

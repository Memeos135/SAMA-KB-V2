# Asset Encumbrance

## Why this community

Pillar 3 disclosure of asset encumbrance — the extent to which a bank's on-balance sheet assets are pledged or otherwise unavailable to unsecured creditors.

## How members connect

- Single standalone disclosure template within the SAMA Pillar 3 framework; its obligation is to present encumbered versus unencumbered assets consistently with the accounting balance sheet.
- Conceptually feeds liquidity and resolution/creditor-hierarchy analysis, so it should be read alongside liquidity and balance-sheet reconciliation disclosures rather than in isolation.

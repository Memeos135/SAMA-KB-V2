# Template SEC3: Securitisation Exposures Originator/Sponsor

Pillar 3 disclosure template SEC3 within SAMA's public disclosure requirements framework, covering banking book securitisation exposures where the bank acts as originator or sponsor and the exposures are subject to capital charges under the securitisation framework. It sits alongside Table SECA and templates SEC1/SEC2 (which capture all securitisation activity, including positions failing risk transfer recognition) and excludes trading book securitisation positions. It binds banks reporting under SAMA's Pillar 3 regime.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

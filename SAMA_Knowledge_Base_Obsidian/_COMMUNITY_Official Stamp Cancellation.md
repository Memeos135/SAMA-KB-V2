# Official Stamp Cancellation

## Why this community

Removal of the formal requirement to affix an official stamp on documents/instruments handled by supervised entities, i.e. a formality-reduction measure affecting how validity and authentication of banking documentation is evidenced.

## How members connect

- Single-instrument cluster: a SAMA circular acting as an amending instruction that overrides an earlier procedural formality rather than creating a new obligation regime.
- Its legal effect is subtractive — it removes a documentary condition, so it must be read against the underlying rules/forms that previously assumed a stamped document.
- Relevant to authentication and evidentiary practice in customer documentation and contract execution; no substantive change to identification or record-keeping duties.

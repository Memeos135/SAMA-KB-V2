# NPL Operational Plan

Concept node on the operational plan component of a bank's NPL strategy under SAMA's NPL guidance. It addresses how the approved strategy is designed, communicated, embedded across strategic, tactical and operational levels, monitored against targets with corrective action, and reviewed at least annually, including documented roles, reporting lines, staff incentives, and alignment with the business plan, budget, risk appetite framework and ICAAP. It also flags external drivers such as NPL investor demand, servicing capacity and capital implications. Binds banks.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential

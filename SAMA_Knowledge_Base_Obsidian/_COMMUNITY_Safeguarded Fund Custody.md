# Safeguarded Fund Custody

## Why this community

Safeguarding of customer funds — the requirement that money held on behalf of clients be protected and held in custody separately from the firm's own assets, so it is insulated from the firm's insolvency and misuse.

## How members connect

- Standalone instrument with no internal cross-references captured; its meaning depends on which regime's defined 'funds' and 'customer/client' terms apply to the firm relying on it.
- Practically it interacts with payments/PSP and finance-company regimes (segregation, permitted holding arrangements, reconciliation duties) and with AML/CTF where safeguarding accounts hold pooled customer money.
- Scope should be confirmed before application: safeguarding obligations differ by licence type and are not a general deposit-taking permission.

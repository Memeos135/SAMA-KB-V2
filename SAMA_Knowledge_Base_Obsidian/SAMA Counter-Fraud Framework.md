# SAMA Counter-Fraud Framework

Node covering product and service risk assessment duties, which connect the regulation to SAMA's Counter-Fraud Framework and companion frameworks used as the maturity gate for the notification route. Banks must assign responsibility for new-product risk, conduct full life-cycle risk assessments as the basis for launch decisions, apply risk management standards and a risk classification (e.g. high/medium/low), and manage third-party risk where products are offered with fintechs, agents or similar partners; the risk management function must document, review and approve each product's risk profile and have the capacity to monitor risks to the bank, customers and market stability.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential, consumer protection

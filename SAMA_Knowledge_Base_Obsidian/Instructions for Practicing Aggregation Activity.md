# Instructions for Practicing Aggregation Activity

SAMA Instructions (May 2023) issued under the Finance Companies Control Law setting minimum standards and procedures for entities licensed to practise aggregation activity — platforms that present finance offers from financiers to consumers. They define key terms (SAMA, Activity, Financier, Consumer, Entity, Platform, Documented Channels), require a licence before carrying on the activity, and impose obligations on technological capability, standard interfaces with financiers, verification of consumer identity and of information and documents from reliable and independent sources, accuracy of finance offers, and creation of an electronic record per consumer with phone-number authentication and up-to-date data such as the national address. They bind licensed aggregators and operate without prejudice to the Finance Companies Control Law and its Implementing Regulations, the Rules of Licensing Finance Support Activities, the Business Continuity Management Framework, the Financial Consumer Protection Principles and Rules, and the Electronic Transactions Law.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, consumer protection, governance/risk, data/credit info

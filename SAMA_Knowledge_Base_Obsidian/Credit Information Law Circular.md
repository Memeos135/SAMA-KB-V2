# Credit Information Law Circular

SAMA circular (Arabic) issued to credit information companies under the Credit Information Law and its Implementing Regulations, addressing the sensitivity of data held by those companies. It requires credit information companies to obtain SAMA's no-objection before providing any data or statistics to governmental or non-governmental entities, except as covered by member agreements concluded under the Law and Regulations.

**Regimes:** data/credit info

# Cybersecurity Controls

## Why this community

Cybersecurity and data-protection controls that SAMA-supervised entities must implement as part of operational risk and governance obligations, with data-specific controls layered on top of the general cybersecurity framework.

## How members connect

- Hierarchy: a SAMA circular issues/enforces the controls framework, while the dated data cybersecurity controls document sets the substantive control requirements.
- Shared scope population — supervised financial institutions — and shared compliance/self-assessment and reporting duties toward SAMA.
- Data-specific controls act as a specialisation of the broader cybersecurity control domains, so the two must be read cumulatively rather than as alternatives.
- Cross-regime relevance: control obligations support confidentiality duties under consumer protection and credit/data information regimes.

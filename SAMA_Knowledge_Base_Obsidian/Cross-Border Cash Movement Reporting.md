# Cross-Border Cash Movement Reporting

A SAMA Banking Supervision circular relying on the Rules Governing Money Changing Business (Minister of Finance decision) — the duty to supply SAMA with any requested data, SAMA's power to issue supervisory instructions, and the record-keeping/financial-reporting duty — to impose a monthly return on cross-border cash movements into and out of the Kingdom through land, air and sea border crossings, using the attached form. It binds licensed money changers of Category (A) and Category (B).

# SFT Exposures (Leverage Ratio)

Covers the inclusion of securities financing transactions — repos, reverse repos, securities lending/borrowing and margin lending — in the leverage ratio exposure measure, together with the general rules on on-balance sheet exposures, fiduciary asset exclusions, securitisation exposures and central bank (SAMA) reserves. It flags structures SAMA regards as leverage-ratio-minimising, such as agency-model transacting and collateral swaps. It binds banks computing the SAMA leverage ratio.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

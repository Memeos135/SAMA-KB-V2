# Rules for Disclosure of Financing and Savings Product Prices

Updated SAMA rules requiring disclosure of prices of financing and savings products, replacing the earlier circulars on the same subject. They define and standardise APR, AER, credit card purchase rate, finance amount, maturity, monthly instalment, minimum payment, property market value, LTV, early repayment charges and deposit amount, and prescribe disclosure table formats for individuals and for micro and small enterprises. They bind all SAMA-licensed banks and finance companies, with a short implementation deadline and a confirmation report to SAMA.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, BNPL/finance, banking prudential

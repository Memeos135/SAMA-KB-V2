# Workout Unit

Sets out the organisational and conduct requirements for problem loan workout: banks must establish a dedicated Workout Unit independent of business/loan-originating units, properly staffed, with performance appraisal and compensation matrices that are not based solely on NPL volume reduction. It also covers borrower-facing elements — standardised appeal forms with defined information and timelines, a documented workout fee policy assessed against borrower cash-flow impact and communicated transparently, and a code of conduct — plus workout plan requirements including Management Committee approval and time-bound criteria for mandatory transfer of loans to the Workout Unit.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk, consumer protection

# Regulatory Residential Real Estate

Defines a "regulatory residential real estate" exposure as a regulatory real estate exposure secured by property having the nature of a dwelling and lawfully occupiable for housing purposes. The definition, read with the material-dependence test, determines which risk weight table applies to residential mortgage lending. Binds banks classifying property-secured credit exposures for capital purposes.

**Regimes:** banking prudential

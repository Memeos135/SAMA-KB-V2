# Standard Margin Agreements RC Examples

Prudential guidance on how margined netting sets are treated when a bank uses an internal model (IMM) to estimate expected exposure and EAD for counterparty credit risk. It sets out what the model must capture — unilateral vs bilateral margin agreements, margin call frequency, thresholds, minimum transfer amounts and collateral valuation — and imposes supervisory floors on the margin period of risk (five/ten business days, rising to twenty for large netting sets and for illiquid collateral or disputed trades). Binds SAMA-supervised banks that hold model approval; it sits alongside the standardised credit risk approach and RWA disclosure definitions.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

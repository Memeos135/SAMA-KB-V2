# Cyber Kill Chain Methodology

A conceptual model within SAMA's Financial Sector red teaming framework describing an advanced cyber-attack as seven end-to-end stages (reconnaissance through actions on objective), used to structure attack scenarios and to position preventive, detective, responsive and corrective controls. It also frames the distinction between conventional penetration testing and red teaming (realistic access paths, TTPs, focus on detection and response). It binds Member Organizations and their Red Teaming Providers, who must explain the kill chains executed and depict them as attack vector diagrams in the evaluation report.

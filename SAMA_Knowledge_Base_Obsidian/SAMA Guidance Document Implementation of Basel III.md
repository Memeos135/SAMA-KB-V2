# SAMA Guidance Document Implementation of Basel III

Node covering SAMA's guidance on implementing the Basel III framework in Saudi Arabia, including the glossary of SAMA-issued components (capital reforms, credit risk, CCR/CVA, market risk, operational risk, leverage, LCR, NSFR) and the guidance note on scope of application. It confirms that the framework applies on a standalone and consolidated basis, including at holding company level and every tier of a banking group. It binds all local banks supervised by SAMA.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

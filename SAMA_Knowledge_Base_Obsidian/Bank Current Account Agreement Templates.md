# Bank Current Account Agreement Templates

A SAMA circular from the Deputy Governor for Supervision updating the standard-form current account opening agreement for individuals and legal persons, previously issued under an earlier circular, and attaching the revised template (parties, customer identification and profile data, and general terms and conditions). It adds a new clause under the rights and obligations section and reminds banks that only the versions published on SAMA's website are authoritative, since they are updated immediately. It binds banks operating in the Kingdom, which must implement the change within the timeframe set in the referenced circular.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, payments, AML/CTF, banking prudential

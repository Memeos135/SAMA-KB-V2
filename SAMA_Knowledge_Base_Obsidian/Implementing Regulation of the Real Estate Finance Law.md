# Implementing Regulation of the Real Estate Finance Law

The Implementing Regulation of the Real Estate Finance Law, issued under SAMA's mandate to regulate the real estate finance sector. It sets definitions (Real Estate Finance, Borrower, Consumer, Originator, Secondary Market), licensing and activity limits for real estate finance entities, contract content requirements, insurance and disclosure duties, and the regime for real estate refinance companies including the Saudi Real Estate Refinance Company. It binds commercial banks and real estate finance companies licensed for real estate finance, plus licensed refinance companies.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, consumer protection, governance/risk

# Unsecured Wholesale Funding Run-off

Defines unsecured wholesale funding for LCR purposes as uncollateralised liabilities raised from non-natural persons (including sole proprietorships and partnerships) callable or maturing within 30 days, excluding derivative obligations, and sets the counterparty-based run-off factors (small business, operational deposits, non-financial corporates/sovereigns/central banks/MDBs/PSEs at 20% or 40%, other legal entities at 100%). It also records SAMA's discretion to impose a higher-than-0% run-off on qualifying retail term deposits where withdrawal or reputational-repayment concerns arise.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

# Portfolio Segmentation

Concept node from SAMA's Guidelines on Management of Problem Loans covering segmentation of the non-performing loan portfolio into homogeneous groups as the first step of NPL resolution. It sets out the timing (immediately on transfer to the Workout Unit, before assignment to a workout officer), the principles of proportionality and materiality in resource allocation, and stage-one criteria such as nature of business (MSME vs corporate), legal status, arrears/days-past-due buckets and loan balance thresholds. Applies to banks managing NPL stock and designing workout strategies.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential

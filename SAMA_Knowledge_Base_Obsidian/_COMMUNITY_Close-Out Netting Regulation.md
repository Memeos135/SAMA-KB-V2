# Close-Out Netting Regulation

## Why this community

Enforceability of close-out netting and collateral (financial security) arrangements in KSA, including their treatment on counterparty default or insolvency and the resulting effect on exposure measurement.

## How members connect

- Standalone regulation node; legally it interacts with prudential capital/exposure rules that permit netted exposure recognition only where netting is legally enforceable.
- Cross-references insolvency and contractual enforceability concepts, so it should be read alongside risk governance and credit exposure regimes rather than in isolation.

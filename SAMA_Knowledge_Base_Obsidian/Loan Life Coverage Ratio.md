# Loan Life Coverage Ratio

Concept node covering the Loan Life Coverage Ratio as defined in Appendix 2 of SAMA's Guidelines on Management of Problem Loans: the present value of cash flows available for debt service over the loan life plus debt reserves, divided by the outstanding loan amount, discounted at the weighted average cost of capital. It is intended for project finance and other multi-year exposures where long-term viability must be assessed, contrasted with the point-in-time DSCR, with an indicative benchmark of 1.4. Used by banks' Workout teams in viability and risk-profile assessment.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential

# Companies Law

Marks the Companies Law as the reference framework for opening and operating accounts of Saudi juristic persons under the rules — joint-stock companies (including companies under formation and formation-expense accounts requiring Ministry of Commerce approval), limited liability companies, general and limited partnerships, and GCC companies holding a Saudi commercial register. It also underpins liquidation of a company under the Companies Law as a freezing/account-closure trigger, and drives the constitutive documents banks must collect. Binds banks opening corporate accounts.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, AML/CTF, governance/risk

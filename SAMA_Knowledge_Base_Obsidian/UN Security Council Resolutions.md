# UN Security Council Resolutions

This node covers the general/closing provisions of SAMA's Targeted Financial Sanctions Rules requiring financial institutions to monitor the official Saudi channels for UNSC-derived sanctions lists and implementation mechanisms — the Permanent Committee for Combating Terrorism and its Financing (State Security) and the MOFA Permanent Committee on Chapter VII UNSC resolutions. It confirms the Rules are a minimum standard that does not displace other SAMA instructions or applicable laws, and that they take effect upon publication on SAMA's website. Binds all SAMA-supervised financial institutions.

**Regimes:** sanctions/TFS, AML/CTF, governance/risk

- **What this link tells you:** For any freezing or listing decision, the operative source is the UN Security Council designation regime as implemented domestically, not the institution's own list maintenance. The TFS rules direct the financial institution to follow the Permanent Committee (Ministry of Foreign Affairs) channel for the resolutions issued under Chapter VII, including updates on the implementation mechanism. The consequence is that currency of designations must be tracked from that official channel without delay, and reliance solely on commercial list vendors will not discharge the obligation.

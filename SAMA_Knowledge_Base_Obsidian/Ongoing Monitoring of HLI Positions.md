# Ongoing Monitoring of HLI Positions

Section requiring banks to monitor HLI creditworthiness and exposure development on an ongoing basis, with counterparty credit quality reviews of material HLI exposures at least quarterly and additional reviews triggered by exposure increases or market volatility. Monitoring must go beyond net asset value and balance-sheet measures, using quantitative risk data such as VaR supplemented by stress testing, reviews of the counterparty's risk management capability, and understanding of risk concentrations. Also requires collateral management practices capturing all positions with at least daily marking to market and timely collateral transfers.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential

# ECAI Eligibility Criteria

Sets out which external credit assessment institutions SAMA recognises for regulatory capital purposes (S&P, Moody's and Fitch) and the eight eligibility criteria an ECAI must satisfy on an ongoing basis, including objectivity, independence, international access/transparency, disclosure, resources, credibility and cooperation with SAMA. It also specifies the conflict-of-interest situations that must be disclosed, drawing on the IOSCO Code of Conduct Fundamentals for Credit Rating Agencies. It binds banks using the standardised approach to credit risk (which may only rely on recognised ECAI ratings) and conditions SAMA's own recognition decisions.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

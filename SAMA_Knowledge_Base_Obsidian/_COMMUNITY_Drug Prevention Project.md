# Drug Prevention Project

## Why this community

Sector participation in the national anti-narcotics awareness programme (Nebras) — a social/awareness obligation directed at financial institutions rather than a prudential or conduct rule.

## How members connect

- Single-member cluster with no internal legal cross-references.
- Best read as an administrative/awareness directive; any enforceable consequence lies in the expectation of institutional participation, not in supervisory ratios or customer-facing duties.

# Credit Derivative Offsetting Rules

A concept node in SAMA's market risk capital rules dealing with when specific risk capital requirements on credit derivative positions may be offset against hedged cash or derivative positions. It sets full, partial or no allowance depending on matching of reference obligation, maturity, currency and contract features, and prescribes the treatment of first-to-default and nth-to-default basket credit derivatives for protection sellers. It applies to banks computing specific risk capital for trading book credit positions.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

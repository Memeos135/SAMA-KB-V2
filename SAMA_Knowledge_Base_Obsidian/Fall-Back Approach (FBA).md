# Fall-Back Approach (FBA)

The fall-back approach is the most conservative tier for equity investments in funds, applied where neither the look-through nor the mandate-based approach can be used, and it drives the capital outcome for opaque fund holdings. The source places it within the approach hierarchy for funds and links to how the resulting RWA and minimum capital requirements are presented in Pillar 3 overview disclosures. Applies to SAMA-supervised banks with fund equity exposures.

**Regimes:** banking prudential

# Ministry of Interior Ten-Digit Computer Number (Appendix C)

Appendix C of SAMA's bank account opening rules explains the structure of the Ministry of Interior ten-digit computer number: the first digit denotes identity type (Saudi national, resident, entities such as government bodies, companies, charities, clubs and diplomatic missions), digits two to nine are serial, and the last digit is a check digit issued via the National Information Center. It supports the electronic record and identification requirements banks must maintain for natural and juristic persons, alongside Appendices A and B listing government entities and public-sector legal persons.

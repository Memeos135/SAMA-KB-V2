# Fraud Prevention

The Fraud Prevention part of the Counter-Fraud Fundamental Requirements, sitting after the governance controls and read against the framework's glossary (Fraud, Fraud case, External Fraud, Fraud Landscape, Fraud Response Plan, Fraud Risk Appetite, Fraud Risk Assessment, Electronic Money, EMI, Employee). It addresses preventive controls expected of Member Organizations to stop fraud before it occurs, complementing detection and response requirements elsewhere in the document. Applies to SAMA-supervised Member Organizations in scope of the framework.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, payments, BNPL/finance

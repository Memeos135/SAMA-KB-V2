# Internal Audit Principles

## Why this community

Governance expectations for the internal audit function in locally incorporated banks: independence, mandate, board/audit-committee reporting lines and assurance coverage.

## How members connect

- Single-member cluster forming part of the governance/risk pillar; it presupposes the board and audit-committee structures set by banking governance requirements.
- Creates an obligation chain: internal audit provides third-line assurance over compliance functions in other regimes (AML/CTF, consumer protection, operational risk), so findings feed supervisory reporting.
- Read alongside external audit and compliance-function requirements to allocate responsibility correctly when a control failure is identified.

# Internal Assessment Approach (SEC-IAA)

Governs the Internal Assessment Approach for unrated securitisation exposures to ABCP programmes (typically liquidity facilities and credit enhancements), requiring SAMA notification and approval, an existing approved IRB model, externally rated ABCP, and internal assessments mapped to ECAI criteria at investment-grade equivalent. Internal ratings must be embedded in the bank's risk management. Binds banks seeking to use internal assessments instead of external ratings for such exposures.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

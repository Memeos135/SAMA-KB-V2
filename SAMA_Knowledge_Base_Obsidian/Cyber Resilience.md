# Cyber Resilience

This node draws together the cyber resilience dimension of the SAMA BCM Framework: BCM is framed as a holistic process for building organisational resilience, with IT Disaster Recovery defined as the part of BCM covering resilience, recovery and continuation of technology infrastructure supporting critical business processes. Practical cyber implications include applying the same logical, physical, environmental and cyber security controls at the alternative data centre as at the primary site, embedding cyber security requirements in continuity planning, and including cyber scenarios in testing. The framework cross-refers to the SAMA Cyber Security Framework for threat and vulnerability management controls; it binds Member Organizations.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential

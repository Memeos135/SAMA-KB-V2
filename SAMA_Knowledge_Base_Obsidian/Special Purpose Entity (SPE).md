# Special Purpose Entity (SPE)

Concept node for the special purpose entity used in securitisation structures — the vehicle (corporation, trust or other entity) organised for a specific purpose, whose activities are limited to those needed to effect the securitisation and which is structured to isolate the SPE from the credit risk of the originator. Relevant to originator status, consolidation scope and to capital-instrument disclosure where the issuer is an SPE rather than the bank itself. Binds banks applying the securitisation framework and completing regulatory capital instrument disclosures.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

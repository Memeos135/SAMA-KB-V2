# Internal Audit Unit

This node covers the Internal Audit Unit as defined and regulated under SAMA's first-issue Principles of Internal Audit for local banks operating in the Kingdom, issued under the Saudi Central Bank Law and the Banking Control Law. It sets out the Unit's definition as an independent, objective assurance and consulting function on the adequacy and effectiveness of internal control, together with its independence and objectivity, professional competence and due care, ethics, internal audit policy, organisational structure and reporting lines, and head-of-unit requirements including SAMA no-objection for appointment and replacement. It binds locally licensed banks, their Boards, Audit Committees and senior management, with the Principles effective from the date stated in the covering circular.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential

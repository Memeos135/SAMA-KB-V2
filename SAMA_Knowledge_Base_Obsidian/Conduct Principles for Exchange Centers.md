# Conduct Principles for Exchange Centers

Short SAMA circular (1441) supplementing the earlier Principles of Conduct and Work Ethics in Financial Institutions circular. It confirms that money exchange companies and establishments operating in the Kingdom fall within the definition of "financial institutions" for purposes of those principles, and must therefore apply them to staff conduct, integrity, transparency and objectivity. It binds licensed exchange companies and establishments.

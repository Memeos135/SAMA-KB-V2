# Currency Mismatch Risk Weight Multiplier

A component of SAMA's Minimum Capital Requirements for Credit Risk (standardised approach) dealing with the risk weight multiplier applied to unhedged retail and residential real estate exposures where the borrower's income currency differs from the currency of the loan, alongside the related rule that domestic-currency ratings may only be used for domestic-currency exposures. It also touches on limited mitigation where a qualifying multilateral development bank guarantees convertibility and transfer risk. It binds SAMA-supervised banks calculating credit risk-weighted assets.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

# Collateralized Transactions

This node addresses capital treatment of collateralised and repo-style transactions, including exemptions from the one-year effective maturity floor for fully or nearly fully collateralised capital-market-driven and repo-style transactions with daily remargining, short-term self-liquidating trade finance, and other SAMA-defined short-term exposures. It also covers eligible collateral and haircuts for trading book repo-style transactions, counterparty credit risk on collateralised OTC derivatives, and minimum haircut floors for non-centrally cleared securities financing transactions. It binds banks calculating credit and counterparty credit risk capital.

**Regimes:** banking prudential

### [[Simple Approach for Collateral]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When a transaction is collateralised, the bank must make an explicit methodological election: the simple approach, which substitutes the collateral's risk weight for the counterparty's, or the comprehensive approach with haircuts. The collateralized transactions rules point to the simple approach as one of the recognised treatments, with its own eligibility, coverage and floor conditions. The consequence for a decision is that eligibility and capital outcome differ materially between the two routes, and the elected approach must be applied consistently rather than chosen transaction by transaction to optimise capital.

# Qualitative Principles of Responsible Lending

Chapter III of the Responsible Lending Principles, setting the conduct and process duties on creditors: a documented, board-approved and annually reviewed creditworthiness methodology; examination of the consumer's credit record with consent; written disclosure by the consumer of other credit obligations; registration and ongoing updating of credit information with licensed credit bureaus; and mandatory rejection of the application where consent is withheld. It binds banks and finance companies granting finance to individuals, and interacts with the credit information regime.

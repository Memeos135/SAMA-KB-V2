# Chief Information Security Officer (CISO) Role

Concept node from SAMA's cyber security framework defining the information security function and the Chief Information Security Officer role: a function independent of IT, headed by the CISO, reporting directly to the CEO/managing director (or general manager of a control function), responsible for security policies, roles and goals, risk management frameworks, identifying emerging issues and shifts in risk appetite, and supporting management in designing controls. It sits alongside the required content of the cyber security policy and the allocation of cyber security roles and responsibilities, with ultimate accountability resting with the Board of Directors (including budget and approvals). Binds Member Organisations subject to the framework.

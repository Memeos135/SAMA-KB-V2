# Template OV1: Overview of RWA

The Pillar 3 overview template presenting total risk-weighted assets and minimum capital requirements broken down by risk type (credit, counterparty credit, CVA, securitisation, market, operational, equity investments in funds, settlement risk and threshold deductions). Its instructions define which approaches (standardised, F-IRB, A-IRB, supervisory slotting, internal models approved by SAMA) map to each row. Mandatory for banks; OV1 is the denominator reference for the risk-based capital ratios disclosed elsewhere.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

# SAMA Circular on Economic Cities Property Ownership Document

A SAMA circular (Arabic) confirming earlier instructions that the ownership document issued by the Economic Cities Authority must be accepted as an official title document for any property or land owner inside the economic cities, standing in place of the title deed used outside those cities. It is a compliance/collateral-documentation instruction rather than a prudential rule. It binds banks operating in the Kingdom and real estate finance companies.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, other

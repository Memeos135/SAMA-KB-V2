# Circular on Repossession of Leased Assets via Competent Authorities

SAMA circular confirming that, under Article 25(2) of the Finance Lease Law, repossession of movable leased assets from the lessee—where the contract grants the lessor that right—may only be carried out through the competent authorities, pending Ministry of Justice licensing of specialised repossession companies. It aligns with Ministry of Interior instructions barring self-help vehicle repossession and warns that finance company staff who repossess without recourse to the competent authorities face legal action. Binds banks and finance companies licensed to conduct finance leasing.

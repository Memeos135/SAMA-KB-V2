# Penetration Testing

A concept node within SAMA's Financial Entities Ethical Red Teaming (F.E.E.R.) Framework covering penetration testing as a component of adversarial security testing, and the team structure (Green Team from SAMA's IT Risk supervision function, the Member Organization's White Team and Blue Team) that governs controlled execution of red teaming exercises. Penetration testing is defined as evaluators mimicking real-world attacks to circumvent security features of applications, systems or networks, distinct from the broader threat-intelligence-led red teaming exercise. It binds Member Organizations affiliated with SAMA and the red teaming providers they engage.

**Regimes:** governance/risk

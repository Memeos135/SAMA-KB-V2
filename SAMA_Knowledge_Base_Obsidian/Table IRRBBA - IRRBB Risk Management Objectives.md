# Table IRRBBA - IRRBB Risk Management Objectives

Qualitative Pillar 3 disclosure table requiring banks to describe their objectives, policies, governance and measurement approach for interest rate risk in the banking book (IRRBB), including definition of IRRBB, mitigation and hedging strategies, calculation periodicity, shock and stress scenarios, and any divergence between internal measurement system assumptions and those prescribed for the quantitative template. Disclosure is annual and in flexible format. Mandatory for all banks within the IRRBB scope of application under SAMA's Pillar 3 framework, read with SAMA's IRRBB circular.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

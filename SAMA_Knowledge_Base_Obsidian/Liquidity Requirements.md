# Liquidity Requirements

Chapter 5 of the DTFC Regulations, requiring every DTFC to have a Board-approved liquidity risk management plan covering management structures, net funding requirements, contingency funding planning and internal controls. It imposes hard statutory limits: a minimum 20% liquid asset holding against deposit and short-term liabilities, statutory deposits with SAMA of at least 4% of deposit liabilities, and a cap on deposit liabilities of 15 times total capital, with a one-month remediation route. It also sets monthly liquidity reporting to SAMA by the 15th business day and completion instructions for the liquidity return.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, BNPL/finance, governance/risk

# Standardised Interest Rate Shock Scenarios

SAMA's recalibrated standardised interest rate shock scenarios for interest rate risk in the banking book (IRRBB): six prescribed shocks (parallel up/down, steepener, flattener, short rates up/down) for EVE and two for NII, applied per currency where the bank has material positions. It contains the currency-specific absolute shock sizes in basis points (parallel, short, long), the parameterisation formulas, and a zero floor on post-shock rates that SAMA may revise. It binds banks measuring and reporting IRRBB.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

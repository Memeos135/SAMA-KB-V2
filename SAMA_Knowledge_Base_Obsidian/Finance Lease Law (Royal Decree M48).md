# Finance Lease Law (Royal Decree M/48)

Concept node for the Finance Lease Law (Royal Decree M/48, 13/08/1433H) as the statutory basis for SAMA's Controls and Procedures for Requesting and Issuing Extracts of the Executive Deed for a Registered Finance Lease Contract (Jumada II 1442H / January 2021). Under Article 25(2) of the Law's Implementing Regulation, the extract of the executive deed for a registered lease constitutes an executive instrument under the Enforcement Law, and the controls define the extract types (recovery of movable assets, delivery of fixed assets, financial enforcement certificate), require exclusive use of Ministry of Justice-licensed specialised recovery companies, and mandate electronic processing by all parties. It binds lessors — banks and finance companies — and SAMA-licensed finance lease contract registration companies, alongside the separate SAMA controls on lessor recovery of movable assets.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, consumer protection, other

# Circular Defining Commercial Real Estate Financing

A SAMA circular issuing a unified definition of "commercial real estate financing": credit extended to a natural or legal person to purchase, develop or build real estate, qualifying as commercial where any one of four criteria is met (corporate beneficiary; five or more residential units; predominantly non-residential use; or income-producing property). Addressees must align policies, procedures and regulatory reporting with the definition from Q3 2017. It binds banks and finance companies operating in the Kingdom.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, banking prudential, governance/risk

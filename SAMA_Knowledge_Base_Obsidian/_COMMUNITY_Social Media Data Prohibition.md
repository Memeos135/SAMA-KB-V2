# Social Media Data Prohibition

## Why this community

Restriction on the channels used to exchange official and customer-related data, specifically prohibiting social media platforms.

## How members connect

- Single-node cluster: a confidentiality and information-handling prohibition directed at supervised entities and their staff.
- Links conceptually to banking secrecy, customer data protection and operational/conduct risk governance obligations.
- Decision impact: any exchange of official or customer data over social channels is a compliance breach regardless of consent or convenience.

# Commodities Finance Slotting

## Why this community

Prudential credit risk classification for specialised lending exposures, specifically the supervisory slotting approach applied to commodities finance under the capital adequacy framework.

## How members connect

- Single-member cluster: the slotting criteria operate as a standalone supervisory grid mapping commodities finance exposures to prescribed risk categories and associated risk weights.
- Sits downstream of the capital adequacy/credit risk regime; it is an application rule rather than a source of independent conduct obligations.
- Relevant to finance companies and banks that cannot rely on internal rating estimates for specialised lending and must default to supervisory categories.

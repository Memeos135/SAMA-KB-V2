# Political Risk

Concept node treating political and regulatory instability as a source of operational risk for banks with cross-border operations or significant involvement in international trade. It expects Saudi banks operating outside the Kingdom to maintain management systems and procedures for monitoring the political and regulatory environments, including red-flag/warning indicators and alternative action plans to prevent or mitigate losses. Sits within SAMA's broader operational risk guidance directed at directors and senior management.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential

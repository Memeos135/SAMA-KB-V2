# Banknote Inspection Circular

A SAMA circular reinforcing prior security instructions by directing banks and money exchange centres to strengthen banknote examination procedures, exercise utmost care in verifying the authenticity of circulating currency, immediately report any suspected banknote to the security authorities and notify SAMA. It binds banks and exchange (money changer) centres operating in the Kingdom.

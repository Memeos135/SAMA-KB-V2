# SA-CCR Add-On Parameters

## Why this community

The parameter set used to compute the potential future exposure add-on under SA-CCR for derivative exposures.

## How members connect

- The PFE add-on is the parent obligation; effective notional, supervisory delta, maturity factor and supervisory factor are its mandated inputs.
- Effective notional is itself derived from supervisory delta and the maturity factor, creating a nested calculation dependency.
- The margin period of risk is the conditioning variable for the maturity factor, linking margining arrangements to the capital outcome.

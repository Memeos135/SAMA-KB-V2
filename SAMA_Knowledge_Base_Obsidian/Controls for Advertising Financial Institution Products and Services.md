# Controls for Advertising Financial Institution Products and Services

SAMA controls (first issue, 2023) setting the minimum standards and procedures financial institutions must follow when advertising any product or service, issued under the Saudi Central Bank Law's consumer-protection and rule-making powers. Defines Advertisement, Medium, Customer, Consumer and Products and Services, and requires due care over advertising content alongside sector laws and other content-regulation authorities. Applies to all financial institutions supervised by SAMA whenever they advertise.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, governance/risk

- **What this link tells you:** When assessing whether an advertising control is binding on a supervised institution, treat the advertising rules as an exercise of SAMA's statutory supervisory powers rather than guidance. The advertising controls reference the Saudi Central Bank Law (M/36), which is the parent enabling statute conferring SAMA's authority to regulate and supervise financial institutions and to issue and enforce such instruments. Practically, non-compliance with the advertising controls engages SAMA's supervisory and enforcement powers under the Law, so the reader should not treat marketing rules as a soft conduct expectation.

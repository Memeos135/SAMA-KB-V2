# Trading Desk Definition

Node defining the regulatory trading desk — the unit of observation for internal model approval, backtesting, PLA testing and market risk capital. It sets the mandatory attributes: an unambiguously defined group of traders or trading accounts, one (exceptionally two) head trader, one desk per trading account, a documented business strategy with annual budget and MI reporting, defined risk scope and permitted risk factors, and a clear risk management structure with reviewed trading limits. Binds banks proposing a desk structure for SAMA approval; internal operational sub-desks fall outside the approved structure and outside the capital framework.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

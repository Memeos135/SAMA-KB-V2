# Leverage Ratio Exposures

## Why this community

The non-risk-based leverage ratio: how the denominator (exposure measure) is built up across on-balance-sheet items, derivatives, securities financing transactions and off-balance-sheet commitments, deliberately limiting netting and collateral recognition.

## How members connect

- Hierarchy: the leverage ratio framework sets the ratio; the exposure measure is the operative denominator; the derivative, SFT and written credit derivative components are its defined sub-measures.
- Derivative exposure builds on replacement cost plus an aggregate add-on, with cash variation margin recognition and written credit derivative treatment as the two main adjusting rules.
- SFT treatment appears twice by design — once as the general exposure rule and once as the leverage-specific measure — and interacts with the minimum haircut floors regime, so the same transaction can be assessed under both lenses.
- Practical consequence: techniques that reduce risk-weighted assets (collateral, netting) do not necessarily reduce the leverage exposure measure, so the binding constraint must be tested separately.

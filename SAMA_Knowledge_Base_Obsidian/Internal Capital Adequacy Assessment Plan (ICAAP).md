# Internal Capital Adequacy Assessment Plan (ICAAP)

Refers to the Internal Capital Adequacy Assessment Plan as the vehicle through which banks conducting Shari'ah compliant banking must perform an annual assessment of Shari'ah Non-Compliance Risk, with the risk management function conducting the assessment, the Shari'ah compliance function reviewing compliance with laws and internal policies, and the Shari'ah audit function providing independent review. It sits alongside requirements on fiduciary duties toward current account and PSIA holders and liquidity risk policy. Binding on SAMA-licensed banks carrying out Shari'ah compliant banking activities.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

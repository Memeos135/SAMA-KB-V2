# ML/TF/PF Business Risk Assessment Guide (duplicate)

A duplicate publication of SAMA's Guide on assessing business risks related to money laundering, terrorist financing and proliferation financing, with the same covering circular and content. It sets the minimum factors and controls for a financial institution's business-wide risk assessment, defines the core risk terminology (business risk, risk-based approach, inherent and residual risk, risk appetite, customer, geographic, delivery-channel and emerging risks), and covers governance responsibility and the assessment stages. It binds all financial institutions under SAMA's supervision from the date of publication on SAMA's website and does not displace other AML/CTF/CPF obligations.

# Law of Payments and Payment Services

The Law of Payments and Payment Services — the primary statute defining payment systems, payment services, payment orders, Members and Clients, and stating the Law's aims (integrity and efficiency of payment infrastructure, user protection, innovation and competition). It reserves to SAMA the powers to license, renew and revoke licences, set legal form, capital and solvency requirements, approve products and services, set outsourcing rules, and classify systems (including designation as a Systemically Important Payment System). It binds payment system operators and payment service providers, and prohibits operating a payment system or providing payment services in the Kingdom without a SAMA licence.

# Counterparty Credit Risk Components

## Why this community

Capital-adequacy rules for measuring counterparty credit risk exposure and the recognition of collateral and maturity effects in that measurement. It sits at the junction of the CCR framework, the IRB risk parameters and the market risk framework.

## How members connect

- Shared quantitative building block: exposure at default is the common measure the CCR framework, IRB parameters and add-on rules all feed into or draw from.
- Credit risk mitigation chain: the simple approach for collateral and the maturity-mismatch treatment condition how much protection may be recognised against a CCR exposure.
- Cross-framework boundary: IRB risk components and the market risk framework are cross-referenced to allocate trading-book versus banking-book treatment of the same counterparty positions.
- Hierarchy: the CCR framework is the governing rule set; add-on and collateral provisions are subordinate computational specifications.

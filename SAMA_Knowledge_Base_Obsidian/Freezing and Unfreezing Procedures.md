# Freezing and Unfreezing Procedures

Chapter Six of the Targeted Financial Sanctions Rules, setting out freezing and unfreezing procedures for funds and other assets of designated persons and entities, including the prohibition on making funds or assets available directly or indirectly. It is the core enforceable action step of the TFS regime and defines the scope of "funds/assets" broadly (including virtual assets and any derived income). Binding on all SAMA-supervised financial institutions.

**Regimes:** sanctions/TFS, AML/CTF

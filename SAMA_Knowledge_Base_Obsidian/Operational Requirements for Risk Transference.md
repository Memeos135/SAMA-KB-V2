# Operational Requirements for Risk Transference

Covers the conditions under which internal risk transfers between the banking book and trading book, and between trading desks, receive regulatory capital recognition, including the requirement for a SAMA-approved dedicated internal risk transfer desk and exact matching of internal general interest rate risk hedges with external market hedges. It also links to the reporting of risk-weighted assets and Pillar 1 minimum capital requirements across credit, counterparty credit, equity, settlement and securitization exposures. It binds banks subject to SAMA's market risk and credit risk capital frameworks.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

# Table LIQA: Liquidity Risk Management

Node for Table LIQA, the qualitative Pillar 3 disclosure of a bank's liquidity risk management framework, governance and funding strategy. It is one of the flexible-format narrative disclosures in SAMA's Pillar 3 framework, parallel in structure to Table OVA (overall risk management approach), which requires description of risk governance, risk culture, measurement systems and reporting to the board and senior management. Binds banks subject to the Pillar 3 disclosure requirements; note the retrieved context also carries adjacent material on internal model validation and risk control units.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

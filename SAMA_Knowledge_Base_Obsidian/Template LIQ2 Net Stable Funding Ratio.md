# Template LIQ2: Net Stable Funding Ratio

A fixed Pillar 3 disclosure template covering the Net Stable Funding Ratio, requiring banks to publish available and required stable funding components by residual maturity and the resulting NSFR. It specifies the reporting content, frequency and fixed format, with accompanying narrative on material changes. It applies to banks within SAMA's Pillar 3 disclosure scope and links to the NSFR line in Template KM1.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

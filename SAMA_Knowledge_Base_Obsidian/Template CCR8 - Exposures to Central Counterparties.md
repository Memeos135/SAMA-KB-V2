# Template CCR8 - Exposures to Central Counterparties

Pillar 3 template CCR8 requires a comprehensive, fixed-format semiannual disclosure of a bank's exposures to central counterparties, split between qualifying and non-qualifying CCPs and by trade type (OTC derivatives, exchange-traded derivatives, SFTs, approved cross-product netting sets), plus segregated and non-segregated initial margin and pre-funded/unfunded default fund contributions, with EAD post-CRM and RWA. Mandatory for all banks, supported by a narrative on material changes. Adjacent context also references Table CRB-A on prudential treatment of problem assets and SAMA's Rules on Management of Problem Loans.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

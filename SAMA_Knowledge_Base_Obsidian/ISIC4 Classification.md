# ISIC4 Classification

A defined classification basis used within SAMA's Interest Rates on Assets and Liabilities reporting guidelines: Template (2) requires banks to categorise reported assets and liabilities by economic sector using the International Standard Industrial Classification, revision 4 (ISIC4). It is a reporting taxonomy requirement, applying to all banks (including foreign bank branches) submitting the quarterly return, and works alongside the product-based and Sharia/conventional templates, with classifications required to be mutually exclusive.

**Regimes:** banking prudential

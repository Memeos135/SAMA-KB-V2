# Financial Receivables Collateral

This node concerns receivables and financial collateral as credit risk mitigation, framed through the Pillar 3 disclosure templates (CR3 on CRM techniques and CCR5 on collateral composition for counterparty credit risk). It explains how secured, unsecured, over- and under-collateralised exposures are allocated across collateral, financial guarantees and credit derivatives, and how collateral posted/received in derivatives and SFTs is reported. It binds banks disclosing credit risk mitigation and counterparty credit risk exposures.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

# eSAMA Portal Main User

## Why this community

Access and accountability for the designated 'Main User' role on SAMA's eSAMA e-Services Portal, i.e. who within a supervised entity is authorised to transact with SAMA electronically and administer sub-users.

## How members connect

- Single-node cluster: the concept is a defined administrative role rather than a rule family, so its legal weight comes from cross-references in supervisory circulars requiring nomination of an authorised contact.
- Links outward to entity governance (senior management sign-off) and to reporting/notification duties channelled through the portal.

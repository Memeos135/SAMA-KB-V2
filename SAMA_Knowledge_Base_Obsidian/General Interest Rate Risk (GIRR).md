# General Interest Rate Risk (GIRR)

Concept node for general (generalised) interest rate risk, a defined risk class in the market risk capital framework alongside credit spread, FX, equity and commodity risk. It underpins delta, vega and curvature calculations (including parallel-shift curvature shocks on risk-free yield curves) and governs the dedicated internal risk transfer desk regime for hedging banking book interest rate risk into the trading book. It binds banks calculating market risk regulatory capital and managing banking book interest rate risk exposures.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

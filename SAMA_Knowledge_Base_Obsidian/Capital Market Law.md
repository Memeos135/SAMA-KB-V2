# Capital Market Law

This node is the Capital Market Law as cross-referenced within SAMA's finance regime, chiefly the Real Estate Finance Law, where it governs public offering of shares in real estate refinance companies and refinancing through the issuance of securities. It marks the boundary between SAMA-supervised finance activity and CMA-regulated securities activity. Useful when a question involves securitisation, secondary-market refinancing or offerings by SAMA-licensed entities.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, other

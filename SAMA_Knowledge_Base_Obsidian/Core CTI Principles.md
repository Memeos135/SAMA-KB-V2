# Core CTI Principles

This node covers the Core Cyber Threat Intelligence principles within SAMA's Financial Sector Cyber Threat Intelligence Principles, which set the baseline planning, production, processing and dissemination activities that underpin the strategic, operational and technical/tactical CTI domains. It includes expectations on classifying threat information under the Traffic Light Protocol, delivering actionable intelligence with defined actions, owners and response timeframes, sharing intelligence internally (e.g. with the SOC and IT), and continuously improving intelligence methods. It applies to all Member Organizations supervised by SAMA, including those that outsource their CTI capability, with phased implementation left to the entity's discretion.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, other

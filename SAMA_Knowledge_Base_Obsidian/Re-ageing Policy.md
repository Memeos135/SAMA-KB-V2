# Re-ageing Policy

Concept node on the required re-ageing policy governing the counting of days past due and the granting of extensions, deferrals, renewals and rewrites on existing facilities, including minimum policy content (approval authorities, minimum facility age, eligible delinquency levels, cap on re-ageings, reassessment of repayment capacity). It links re-ageing practice to the default trigger and the IRB 'use test', and sits alongside the treatment of authorised and unauthorised overdrafts. Binds banks using the IRB approach for credit risk.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

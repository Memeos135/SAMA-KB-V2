# Cyber Security Review

Requires the cyber security status of a Member Organization's information assets to be subject to periodic cyber security review, with particular attention to critical information assets and customer/internet-facing services. The objective is to confirm controls are securely designed and implemented and that their effectiveness is monitored; results inform maturity-level assessment under the Framework.

**Regimes:** governance/risk

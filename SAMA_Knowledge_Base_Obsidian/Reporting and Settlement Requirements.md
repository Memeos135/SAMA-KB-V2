# Reporting and Settlement Requirements

The reporting and settlement section requires bank repo participants to report repo transactions to SAMA and addresses settlement of the repo legs. The extracted context largely draws on the MRA's supporting terms — party representations and warranties (authority, acting as principal, required authorisations), margin securities exchange and settlement-system payment mechanics, and Act of Insolvency triggers including filings under the Saudi Bankruptcy Law — which shape settlement and close-out consequences for counterparties.

# IT Risk Management

This node covers the IT Risk Management domain of SAMA's IT Governance Framework, setting out principles and control requirements for identifying, analysing, treating, reporting and monitoring IT risk across processes, technology and people. It requires a defined, approved and communicated IT risk management process aligned to enterprise risk management, a centralised risk register, treatment within board/senior-management-set risk appetite, KRIs, and reporting to senior management and the IT Steering Committee. It binds Member Organizations supervised by SAMA and allocates duties to risk owners, control owners, business owners and the risk committee.

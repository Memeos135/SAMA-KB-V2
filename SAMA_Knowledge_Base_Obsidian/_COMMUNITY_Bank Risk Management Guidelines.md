# Bank Risk Management Guidelines

## Why this community

Supervisory expectations for bank internal control and risk management in higher-risk exposures — dealings with highly leveraged institutions and derivatives activity. The problem-space is prudential risk governance rather than conduct or AML.

## How members connect

- All three are SAMA guidance-level instruments (guidelines/sound practices) that sit below the Banking Control Law rather than creating standalone licensing obligations.
- The HLI sound practices document cross-refers to the derivatives risk management guidelines for counterparty exposure, collateral and valuation discipline.
- Internal control guidelines supply the control-environment baseline (board oversight, segregation, independent risk and audit) that the two activity-specific documents presuppose.
- Shared obligation chain: board/senior management accountability → risk limits and measurement → independent control and reporting.

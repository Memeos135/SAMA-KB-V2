# Multiplication Factor mc

A concept node relating to the internal models approach under SAMA's market risk framework, where a multiplication factor is applied to modelled risk measures, alongside the rules on risk factor modellability, the risk factor eligibility test (RFET) and treatment of non-modellable risk factors (NMRF). It addresses data quality expectations, SAMA discretion to deem a risk factor non-modellable, and the evidence banks must produce on modellability. It binds banks seeking or maintaining model-based market risk capital treatment.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

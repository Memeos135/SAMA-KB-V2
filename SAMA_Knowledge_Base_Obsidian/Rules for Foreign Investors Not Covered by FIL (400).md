# Rules for Foreign Investors Not Covered by FIL (400)

Chapter/section 400 of the SAMA bank account rules, addressing account opening for resident and non-resident foreign investors that fall outside the Foreign Investment Law, including investment-deposit linkage accounts and accounts for foreign financial institutions qualified by the CMA to invest in listed securities. It prescribes licensing/CR evidence, articles of association, identification and verification of beneficial owners holding ultimate control (25% threshold benchmark) and mapping of ownership and control structure. It binds banks onboarding such investors and interacts with CMA's QFI rules and FATF-based country risk screening.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, banking prudential, data/credit info

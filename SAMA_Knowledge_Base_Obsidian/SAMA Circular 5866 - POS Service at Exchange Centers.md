# SAMA Circular 5866 - POS Service at Exchange Centers

SAMA circular directing all money exchange centres in the Kingdom to make point-of-sale (POS) acceptance available and activated for customers, following SAMA's development of a unified pricing model for POS transactions carried out with mada cards at exchange centres. It relies on Article 14(b) of the Rules Regulating Money Exchange Business as the supervisory power to issue such instructions, and expressly warns that non-compliance will trigger statutory penalties. Binds licensed exchange establishments and companies operating in Saudi Arabia.

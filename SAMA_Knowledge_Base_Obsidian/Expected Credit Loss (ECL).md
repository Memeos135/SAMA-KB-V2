# Expected Credit Loss (ECL)

A concept node defining Expected Credit Loss as used in SAMA's credit classification and provisioning rules — the credit losses expected from default events, measured on a 12-month or lifetime basis per IFRS 9 — and the governance wrapped around it. The board of directors is responsible for maintaining adequate ECL provisions and overseeing credit risk practices, with documented definitions, roles, portfolio grouping, measurement methods (loss rate or PD/LGD), inputs, assumptions and qualitative adjustments, plus data integrity and model validation. Binds finance companies, their boards and those charged with governance.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, governance/risk, banking prudential

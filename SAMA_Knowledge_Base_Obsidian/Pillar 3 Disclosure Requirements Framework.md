# Pillar 3 Disclosure Requirements Framework

This is SAMA's Pillar 3 Disclosure Requirements Framework, setting the guiding principles for market disclosure (clarity, comprehensiveness, meaningfulness, consistency over time and comparability across banks) together with rules on governance and assurance of disclosed data, use of signposting to other documents, and the frequency, timing and publication of the Pillar 3 report. It requires a board-approved disclosure policy and publication concurrent with the corresponding financial report. It binds banks subject to SAMA's Basel III framework.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

# Instructions on Timeframes for Issuing Clearance Letters and Transferring Accounts and Debts

SAMA instructions fixing binding turnaround times for issuing clearance letters and for transferring customer accounts and debts, applying to banks and finance companies supervised by SAMA in respect of natural-person customers. They set one business day for a clearance letter where no outstanding obligations exist (seven business days where a credit or charge card is held), one business day for account transfer and for completing consumer finance debt transfer forms, and three/five business days for real estate finance transfer steps. They supersede the timeframes stated in several earlier SAMA circulars, do not otherwise derogate from related instructions, and exclude cases subject to judicial rulings or pending before competent authorities; compliance is required within thirty business days of issuance.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, BNPL/finance, banking prudential

# Financial Consumer Protection Principles and Rules

SAMA's Financial Consumer Protection Principles and Rules, setting the overarching principles (fair treatment, disclosure and transparency, education, conduct, data privacy, complaints handling, competition, outsourcing) and detailed general conduct rules for dealings with consumers. Rules cover contract and initial disclosure form requirements, Arabic drafting, 30-day advance notice of changes to terms, fee and commission caps and publication, application acknowledgement and decision timelines, and complaint channels. Binds financial institutions supervised by SAMA in all consumer-facing activity, including through outsourced service providers.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, governance/risk, data/credit info

# Credit Card Rules FAQ

A SAMA-issued frequently asked questions document accompanying the Credit Card Issuance and Operation Rules, circulated to banks and finance companies operating in the Kingdom for publication through customer channels. It explains, in guidance form, issuer duties on card issuance only upon a documented customer request, acceptance/rejection notification, re-issued and supplementary cards, the minimum age condition, and the treatment of annual fees, international purchase transaction fees, cash withdrawal/transfer/wallet top-up charges, grace period and late payment charges. It is expressly indicative only: it does not replace the Rules, and the Rules and other applicable laws and instructions prevail in any conflict.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, payments, BNPL/finance

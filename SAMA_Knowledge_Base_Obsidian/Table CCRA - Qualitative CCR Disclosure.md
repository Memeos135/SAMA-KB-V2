# Table CCRA - Qualitative CCR Disclosure

Table CCRA is the qualitative disclosure requirement on counterparty credit risk in SAMA's Pillar 3 framework, opening the CCR section that covers all banking-book and trading-book exposures attracting a CCR charge, including exposures to central counterparties. It sits ahead of the quantitative CCR1 to CCR8 templates and is aimed at explaining risk management objectives, collateral and wrong-way risk policies. It binds banks subject to SAMA Pillar 3 disclosure.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

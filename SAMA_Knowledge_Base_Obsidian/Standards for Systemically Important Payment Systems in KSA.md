# Standards for Systemically Important Payment Systems in KSA

A SAMA document (1436H) setting the criteria used to designate payment systems as systemically important in the Kingdom, defining such systems as those whose disruption could impair participants or cause systemic failure of the financial infrastructure. Criteria include handling interbank or customer payments that could threaten financial stability, being the principal system by daily volume and value, being used to settle for another systemically important financial infrastructure, and being a central payments infrastructure with customer confidence and no substitutes. On this basis SAMA designates SARIE (RTGS), SPAN (ATM/POS network) and SADAD (electronic bill presentment and payment) as systemically important; SAMA acts as owner, operator and sole regulator of these systems.

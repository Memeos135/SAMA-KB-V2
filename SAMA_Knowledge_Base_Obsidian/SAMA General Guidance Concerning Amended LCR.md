# SAMA General Guidance Concerning Amended LCR

SAMA's general guidance package accompanying the amended Liquidity Coverage Ratio prudential return, explaining how Saudi banks are to complete and submit it and how SAMA has aligned its paragraph numbering with the BCBS January 2013 LCR standard. It sets reporting frequency and timing (monthly returns due 30 days after month end, with transitional relief for the first submission) and confirms continued parallel monitoring of NSFR under the earlier 2012 circular. It binds Saudi banks, with foreign bank branches carved out of the monthly return obligation.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

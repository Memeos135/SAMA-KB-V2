# IOSCO

Reference node for IOSCO, the International Organization of Securities Commissions, co-issuer with CPMI of the Principles for Financial Market Infrastructures (April 2012, as amended) and the FMI Disclosure Framework. Within SAMA's payment systems oversight framework these joint standards form part of the regulatory framework operators must observe, including the disclosure framework treated as binding on payment systems and other financial market infrastructures.

**Regimes:** payments, governance/risk

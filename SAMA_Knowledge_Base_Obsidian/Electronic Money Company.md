# Electronic Money Company

Concept node for the electronic money company / e-money issuer category under the Implementing Regulation of the Payments and Payment Services Law. It relates to the licensing category authorised to issue, distribute and redeem electronic money, and to the associated obligations on issuance and redemption, use of e-money distributors, safeguarding of protected funds and customer protection. It binds entities licensed by SAMA to issue electronic money in Saudi Arabia.

**Regimes:** payments, consumer protection, governance/risk

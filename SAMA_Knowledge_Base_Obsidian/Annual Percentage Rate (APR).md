# Annual Percentage Rate (APR)

Concept node for the Annual Percentage Rate (APR) as defined and governed by SAMA's Rules Governing the Calculation of the Annual Percentage Rate. APR is the discount rate equating the present value of all borrower payments (total amount payable, comprising principal plus total cost of finance) with the present value of finance amounts made available; the Rules prescribe the net present value formula, mandate use of SAMA's Excel-based APR Calculator, and require the APR to be disclosed consistently in advertising, offers, contracts, periodic statements and any other disclosure. They bind all finance providers engaged in retail lending — banks and licensed finance companies — including duties to update policies, apply internal controls and audit over automated calculators, and align customer-facing website calculators, and are read with the Finance Companies Control Law and its Implementing Regulation, consumer microfinance rules, interest-rate disclosure rules and the Consumer Finance Regulations.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, BNPL/finance

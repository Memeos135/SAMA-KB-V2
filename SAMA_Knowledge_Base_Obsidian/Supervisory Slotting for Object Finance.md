# Supervisory Slotting for Object Finance

Part of the supervisory slotting approach for specialized lending: banks that cannot estimate PD must map internal grades of object finance exposures to five supervisory categories (strong to default) with prescribed unexpected loss risk weights, with slotting criteria set out separately. It also notes the standardized approach fallback risk weight for object finance where no issue-specific external rating exists. Binds banks holding specialized lending exposures under SAMA credit risk capital rules.

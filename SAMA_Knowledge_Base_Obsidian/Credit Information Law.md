# Credit Information Law

The Credit Information Law establishes the principles and controls for collecting, exchanging, protecting and correcting consumer credit information, and defines key terms including Credit Information, Consumer, Member, Companies, Credit Record and Public Records. It applies to licensed credit information companies, members, and government and private entities that maintain credit information, imposing licensing, accuracy, updating, confidentiality and consumer-consent duties, and listing the acts treated as violations. Note the broad definition of Consumer (natural or corporate person engaging in credit transactions), which differs from consumer definitions in other SAMA regimes.

**Regimes:** data/credit info, consumer protection

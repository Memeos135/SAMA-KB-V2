# MSME Exposures

Defines how micro, small and medium enterprise exposures are classified for credit risk RWA purposes: MSMEs meeting the "regulatory retail" criteria fall in the retail exposure class, while those failing the criteria are treated as corporate MSME exposures. It also sets out the retail class structure (regulatory retail non-transactor, regulatory retail transactor, other retail) and the product criterion, excluding real estate/mortgage, derivatives and securities. Applies to SAMA-supervised banks using the standardized approach.

**Regimes:** banking prudential

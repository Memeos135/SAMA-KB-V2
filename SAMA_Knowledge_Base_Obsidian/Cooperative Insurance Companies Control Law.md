# Cooperative Insurance Companies Control Law

A cross-reference node for the Cooperative Insurance Companies Control Law as used in SAMA's draft Bancassurance Rules: it is the licensing basis for the "Company" (an insurer authorised to conduct insurance business in KSA), alongside the Banking Control Law for the "Bank". The Rules' scope article makes that Law, its Implementing Regulation, the Banking Control Law and other SAMA and competent-authority instruments residually applicable to anything not specifically addressed in the Rules. Relevant to determining which regime governs a given bancassurance question.

**Regimes:** other, governance/risk

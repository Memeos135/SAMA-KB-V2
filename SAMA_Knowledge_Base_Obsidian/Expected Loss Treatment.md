# Expected Loss Treatment

Explains how expected loss amounts are calculated under the IRB approach (PD x LGD x EAD, with best-estimate or supervisory LGD for defaulted exposures) and how the shortfall or excess against eligible provisions is deducted from or added to regulatory capital under the Basel III definition of capital rules. Securitization exposures are excluded from the EL amount and treated separately. Applies to IRB banks supervised by SAMA.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

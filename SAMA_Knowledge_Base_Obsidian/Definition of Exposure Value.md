# Definition of Exposure Value

Sets out how exposure value is measured for large exposure purposes across instrument types: banking book on-balance sheet non-derivative assets at accounting value, OTC derivatives and other counterparty credit risk instruments via SA-CCR, securities financing transactions, trading book positions (including options and credit derivatives with sold protection), and exposures to central counterparties with a table of clearing-related exposure values (segregated initial margin at zero, non-segregated at nominal, default fund contributions, equity stakes). It notes that large exposure measurement can differ from risk-based capital measurement. Binds banks computing exposures against LEX limits.

**Regimes:** banking prudential

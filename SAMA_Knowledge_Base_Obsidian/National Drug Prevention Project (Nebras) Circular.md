# National Drug Prevention Project (Nebras) Circular

A circular from SAMA's Finance Companies Supervision Department transmitting a Ministry of Finance letter and a cable from the National Committee for Narcotics Control announcing the launch of the National Drug Prevention Project (Nebras), and asking supervised entities to coordinate with the Committee's General Secretariat and use the Nebras branding when preparing similar preventive awareness programs. It is an administrative/coordination instruction rather than a prudential or AML obligation, addressed to banks, insurance companies, finance companies and the Saudi Credit Bureau.

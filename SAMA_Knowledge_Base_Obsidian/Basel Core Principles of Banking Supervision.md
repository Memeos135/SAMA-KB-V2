# Basel Core Principles of Banking Supervision

Reference in SAMA's Banking Licensing Guidelines to the Basel Committee on Banking Supervision's Core Principles for Effective Banking Supervision, used as the benchmark for assessing whether a foreign bank branch applicant is subject to adequate prudential supervision at home. SAMA considers whether the home supervisor consolidates supervision in line with Basel principles and will co-operate with SAMA on the Saudi branch. Relevant to foreign bank branch applicants and their home regulators.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

# Non-Modellable Risk Factor (NMRF)

Concept node for non-modellable risk factors: risk factors that fail the risk factor eligibility test (RFET) or whose data SAMA deems unsuitable must be excluded from the ES model and capitalised separately as NMRFs. It sets out the RFET observation thresholds (24 or 100 real price observations, with 90-day gap and monitoring conditions) and the criteria for what counts as a real price, including third-party vendor prices and committed quotes. Applies to banks using the internal models approach for market risk.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

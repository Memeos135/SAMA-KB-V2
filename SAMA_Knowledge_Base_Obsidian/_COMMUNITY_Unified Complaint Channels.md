# Unified Complaint Channels

## Why this community

Consumer protection channel requirements for receiving and answering customer complaints — mandating an accessible unified contact line and a standardised form of response to complainants.

## How members connect

- Both sit in the consumer-protection/complaint-handling regime and bind the same supervised entities' customer-facing complaint function.
- Obligation chain: the toll-free number governs intake/accessibility; the response template governs the content and format of the outgoing reply, closing the same complaint lifecycle.
- Shared defined concepts of 'complaint' and complainant communication, so the two instruments are read together when assessing complaint-handling adequacy.

# Materiality Thresholds for Commercial Entities

Sets the two materiality thresholds for a bank's investments in significant minority- or majority-owned and controlled commercial entities — 15% of capital individually and 60% of capital in aggregate — with amounts below the thresholds risk-weighted under the normal equity/subordinated-debt rules and excess amounts at 1250%. Also carries the 150% weight for non-equity subordinated debt and capital instruments, plus related real-estate collateral definitions and operational requirements. Binds banks calculating credit RWAs.

**Regimes:** banking prudential

# Threat Management

Covers the threat intelligence and threat management process that a Member Organization must define, approve, implement and periodically evaluate, drawing on internal telemetry and external sources including SAMA, government agencies and security vendors. It requires a documented analysis methodology, capture of threat attributes such as actors, motivation and modus operandi, actionable follow-up, and sharing of relevant intelligence with SAMA and other stakeholders. Applies to all SAMA-supervised Member Organizations and links into the enterprise-aligned cyber security risk management process.

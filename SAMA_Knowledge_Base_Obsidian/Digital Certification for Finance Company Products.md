# Digital Certification for Finance Company Products

A SAMA circular permitting finance companies to offer financing products and credit cards to individuals and SMEs through electronic channels using approved digital certification (e-signature) services, subject to the Electronic Transactions Law and its implementing regulations. It imposes minimum conditions including level-3 compliance with the Cyber Security Framework verified independently, prior SAMA no-objection for e-services, use of a provider accredited by the National Center for Digital Certification, preservation of KYC/identity and capacity verification, telephone confirmation of customer consent, cooling periods before approval (24 hours for individuals, three business days for entities), risk-based financing caps and record retention. It binds licensed finance companies and excludes products under SAMA's regulatory sandbox.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, AML/CTF, consumer protection, governance/risk

# Unified POS and E-commerce Pricing

## Why this community

Standardisation of merchant pricing/interchange arrangements across point-of-sale and e-commerce acceptance, addressing fee transparency and equal treatment of acceptance channels.

## How members connect

- Single-member cluster: a unified pricing model applying to acquirers/payment service providers in the domestic scheme.
- Links to the payments/PSP regime (acquiring licence conditions) and to merchant-facing conduct duties on fee disclosure.
- Decision consequence: channel-based fee differentiation between physical and online acceptance is constrained by the unified model rather than left to commercial negotiation.

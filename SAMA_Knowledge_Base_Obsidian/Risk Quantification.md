# Risk Quantification

Section of the IRB minimum requirements governing quantification of risk components — PD, LGD, EAD and, where relevant, maturity — including estimation standards, data requirements, long-run and downturn considerations, and the treatment of defaulted exposures. It underpins the parameter inputs used in the IRB risk-weight functions and in purchased receivables and credit risk mitigation treatments. Applies to banks approved by SAMA to use foundation or advanced IRB.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

# Applying Commercial Register and Trade Names Laws

SAMA circular directing supervised financial institutions to apply the new Commercial Register Law and Trade Names Law, including regularising their own commercial registers within the five-year grace period for branch registers and urging customers to do the same. It requires institutions to verify the continued validity of customers' commercial registration and to treat a suspended register (Article 15 of the Commercial Register Law) the same as an expired one, and addresses money-exchange licensees who must incorporate a company rather than a sole proprietorship if carrying on activities beyond exchange business. Binds all financial institutions under SAMA supervision.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, governance/risk, other

# Eligible Collateral and Valuation

Annexure and related provisions of the finance-company provisioning rules identifying eligible collateral for provision calculation (cash, gold, deposits and certificates of deposit, government securities and sukuk, listed shares, investment-grade corporate bonds/sukuk, receivables, financial and Kafalah guarantees, immovable and other physical collateral, leased assets) and setting collateral valuation and haircut expectations. It obliges finance companies to document valuation frequency, apply conservative haircuts feeding into LGD, stress-test collateral portfolios, and revalue immovable collateral securing Stage 3 or defaulted exposures at least annually by licensed appraisers, with the lower of two valuations used where judgement is involved.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, governance/risk, banking prudential

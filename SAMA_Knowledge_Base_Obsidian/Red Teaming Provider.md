# Red Teaming Provider

Defines the external party engaged to design and execute red teaming tests for a Member Organization, together with the selection, approval and procurement expectations set out in the framework and its Appendix A (experience, references, staff certification, defined methodology, governance, quality assurance and risk management). Procurement follows Green Team approval and covers NDA clauses, liability for test consequences and a Letter of Authorization. It binds the Member Organization as the procuring entity and shapes contractual duties on the provider.

**Regimes:** governance/risk

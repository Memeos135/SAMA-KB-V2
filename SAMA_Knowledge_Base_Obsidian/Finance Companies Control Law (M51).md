# Finance Companies Control Law (M/51)

The Finance Companies Control Law (Royal Decree M/51) is the primary statute for licensing and supervising finance companies in Saudi Arabia and the legal basis on which SAMA issues subordinate rules such as the BNPL Rules. It confers on SAMA the power to license, regulate and supervise finance activity and to issue implementing rules and instructions. Binds all finance companies, including BNPL companies and other entities conducting finance activities in the Kingdom.

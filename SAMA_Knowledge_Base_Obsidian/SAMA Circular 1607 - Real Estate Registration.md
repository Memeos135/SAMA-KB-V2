# SAMA Circular 1607 - Real Estate Registration

SAMA Circular 1607 informs supervised finance providers of the phased rollout of real estate in-kind (title) registration under the Real Estate In-Kind Registration Law and its implementing regulation, following notification that Al Falah district in Riyadh is the first declared real estate area. It requires financing entities holding rights in a declared area to accept the ownership registration deed issued by the General Real Estate Authority as the recognised ownership document for that area, and places on them the ongoing duty to monitor subsequent GREA announcements. It is addressed to banks and finance companies operating in the Kingdom.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, banking prudential, other

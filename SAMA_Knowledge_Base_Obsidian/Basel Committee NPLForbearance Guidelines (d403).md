# Basel Committee NPL/Forbearance Guidelines (d403)

Node for the Basel Committee guidelines on the prudential treatment of problem assets (d403), covering harmonised definitions and treatment of non-performing exposures and forbearance. SAMA circulated these guidelines to banks operating in the Kingdom, stressing compliance and identifying their use in asset-quality monitoring and supervision, internal ratings-based credit risk management, Pillar 3 asset-quality disclosure, and published asset-quality indicators. It binds banks under SAMA supervision as a supervisory expectation layered on the existing accounting and regulatory framework.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

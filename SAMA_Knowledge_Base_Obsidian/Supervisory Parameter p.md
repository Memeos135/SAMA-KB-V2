# Supervisory Parameter p

Node on the supervisory parameter p used in the securitization internal ratings-based approach to set the capital surcharge above KIRB; the surrounding material also describes other supervisory-set parameters (supervisory delta, supervisory factor, maturity factor) and curvature risk weights used elsewhere in the capital framework. It fixes regulator-determined inputs that banks may not substitute with their own estimates. Binds banks calculating risk-weighted assets for securitization and counterparty/market risk exposures.

**Regimes:** banking prudential

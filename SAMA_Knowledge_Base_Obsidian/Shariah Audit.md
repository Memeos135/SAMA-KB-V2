# Shariah Audit

This node addresses the Shariah oversight/monitoring function within SAMA's Shariah Governance Instructions for Finance Companies, where "Shariah supervision" is defined as monitoring and verifying the extent to which the company's business complies with Islamic Shariah rulings and principles. It sits alongside chapters on board and senior management responsibilities, rules governing the Shariah committee's work, and independence and confidentiality of information. It applies to finance companies licensed for the listed financing activities, with guidance status for other licensed financing activities.

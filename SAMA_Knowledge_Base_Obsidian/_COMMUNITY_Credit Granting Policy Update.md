# Credit Granting Policy Update

## Why this community

Supervisory direction requiring finance companies to revise their credit granting policies, affecting affordability/creditworthiness assessment and internal credit approval standards under the finance companies regime.

## How members connect

- Single-member cluster: a SAMA directive that operates as a binding instruction layered on the Finance Companies Control Law and its implementing regulations.
- Obligation runs to licensed finance companies' boards/senior management, who must reflect the update in approved internal credit policy.
- Interacts with responsible-lending and consumer protection duties (debt burden ratio, disclosure) and with credit information use in underwriting.

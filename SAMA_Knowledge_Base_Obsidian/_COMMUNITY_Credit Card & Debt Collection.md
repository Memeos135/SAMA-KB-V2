# Credit Card & Debt Collection

## Why this community

Consumer credit lifecycle for individuals: credit card issuance and operation, cost-of-credit disclosure (APR and related consumer finance controls), and the conduct limits that apply when the resulting debt is collected.

## How members connect

- The credit card rules operate as the primary conduct regime and cross-refer to the consumer finance controls circular for pricing/APR and term-clarification obligations (Articles 10 and 11).
- Collection of card and consumer finance arrears is governed by the debt collection regulations, so the issuance rules import those constraints on contact, disclosure and treatment of individual customers.
- Shared scope anchor: the natural-person 'individual customer' / consumer, which limits these obligations relative to corporate exposures.
- Duplicate documents in the cluster are near-identical versions of the same instrument and should be reconciled by issuance date before citing.

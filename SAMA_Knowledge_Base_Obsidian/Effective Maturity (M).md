# Effective Maturity (M)

Concept node for Effective Maturity (M), the maturity input to IRB corporate, sovereign and bank risk-weight functions. It fixes M at 2.5 years under the foundation approach (0.5 years for repo-style transactions), requires measurement of M per facility under advanced approaches subject to a one-year floor and five-year cap, and specifies treatment of cash-flow schedules, netted derivatives and revolving exposures; adjacent market risk material addresses maturity weighting and the three-month floor for default risk charge positions. Binds banks using IRB approaches and, for the DRC aspects, banks under the market risk framework.

**Regimes:** banking prudential

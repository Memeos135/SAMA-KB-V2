# Mule Accounts Detection

Node covering fraud response, root cause analysis and counter-fraud technology requirements, together with the additional controls mandated for products and services of elevated risk. It requires a fraud case management repository, root cause and lessons-learnt processes, corrective and victim-remediation actions, and real-time 24/7 fraud detection able to freeze accounts, block transactions and screen high-risk accounts, IPs, devices and locations, including detection of mule accounts used to receive fraudulent funds and launder proceeds of crime. Mandatory for licensed EMIs, AISPs and Member Organizations offering digital payment, credit card, microfinance, BNPL or fund-safeguarding services; risk-based for other Member Organizations.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, payments, governance/risk, BNPL/finance

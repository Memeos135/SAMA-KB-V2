# Preventive Measures for FIs and DNFBPs

The preventive-measures provisions of the KSA Anti-Money Laundering Law binding financial institutions and DNFBPs: risk-based customer due diligence, PEP identification and additional measures, cross-border correspondent banking safeguards and the shell-bank prohibition, and originator/beneficiary information requirements for wire transfers (with a bar on executing transfers where required information cannot be obtained). The excerpt also includes the supervisory authorities' powers and duties, including information collection, on-site and offsite supervision, sectoral risk assessment, issuing binding guidance, international cooperation and fit-and-proper screening.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, banking prudential, governance/risk, payments

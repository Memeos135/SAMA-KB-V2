# Eligible Purchased Receivables

Defines eligible purchased receivables and divides them into retail and corporate receivables, setting when the top-down approach may be used instead of individual obligor default-risk assessment, and which IRB approaches (F-IRB or A-IRB) are available for each. Notes that use of the top-down treatment for on-balance sheet corporate exposures requires SAMA approval. Binds banks applying the IRB approach to purchased receivable portfolios.

**Regimes:** banking prudential

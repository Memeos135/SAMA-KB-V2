# Counterparty Credit Risk Templates

## Why this community

Pillar 3 counterparty credit risk disclosure: the core templates through which a bank presents its aggregate CCR exposure picture, the standardised measurement of that exposure, and its exposures to central counterparties.

## How members connect

- Shared regime and defined terms: all three are CCR disclosure templates sitting under the same Pillar 3 disclosure requirements, using a common exposure/EAD and risk-weighted assets vocabulary.
- Obligation chain by aggregation: the overview template summarises CCR exposures by measurement approach, with the standardised-approach and CCP templates supplying the disaggregated components that must reconcile back to it.
- Cross-references run both ways between the summary and the CCP template, so scope decisions (qualifying vs non-qualifying CCP, cleared vs bilateral) taken in one template determine what is reported in the other.
- Hierarchy: these are template-level instruments implementing higher-level capital adequacy and disclosure rules, not free-standing obligations - read them against the parent framework for scope and applicability.

# Confiscation Provisions

The confiscation and international-cooperation provisions of the KSA Anti-Money Laundering Law: on conviction for money laundering or a predicate offence the competent court must confiscate laundered funds, proceeds (including intermingled funds up to the intermingled value) and instrumentalities, subject to good-faith third-party rights. It also covers non-conviction-based confiscation (death, flight, absence), value-based confiscation of substitute assets, and mutual legal assistance, information exchange, recognition of foreign confiscation judgments and extradition. These provisions bind courts and competent authorities rather than supervised entities directly, but are relevant to asset-freezing and cooperation requests.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, sanctions/TFS, other

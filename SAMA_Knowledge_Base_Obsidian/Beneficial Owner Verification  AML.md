# Beneficial Owner Verification / AML

A SAMA circular (Arabic) instructing supervised financial institutions to use channels made available by the General Authority for Awqaf — technical integration, the data office email/data-sharing forms, the endowment deed verification service, and the public register of endowments — to verify beneficial owner data for awqaf (endowments) as part of customer due diligence. Institutions must also notify the Authority, via its designated form, of any discrepancies between CDD results and the Authority's beneficial owner data, without prejudice to STR obligations and personal data protection requirements. It binds all financial institutions under SAMA supervision from the date of publication.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, data/credit info, governance/risk

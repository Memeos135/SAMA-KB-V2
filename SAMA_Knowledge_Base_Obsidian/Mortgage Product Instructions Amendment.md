# Mortgage Product Instructions Amendment

A SAMA circular amending paragraph 3 of the mandatory instructions applicable when offering residential real estate finance products to individuals, so that the finance offer may be delivered to the customer electronically as well as on paper, and replacing the earlier instructions. It sets minimum conduct requirements: affordability assessment before any offer, documented plain-language explanation of terms and risks, a written offer valid for at least fifteen working days including the finance contract, disclosure form and (for variable-rate products) a credit risk acknowledgment, and an independent qualified credit advisor consultation documented before contracting. It binds banks and real estate finance companies supervised by SAMA.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, BNPL/finance

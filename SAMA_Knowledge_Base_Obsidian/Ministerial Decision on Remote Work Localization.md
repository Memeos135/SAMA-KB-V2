# Ministerial Decision on Remote Work Localization

This node is the Ministry of Human Resources and Social Development ministerial decision (with its procedural guide) localising remote customer-service occupations to Saudi nationals, covering both direct and indirect contracting, including outsourced service arrangements forming the entity's main activity. It applies to all occupations and roles delivering customer service remotely by phone, email, chat, social media or direct interaction, with penalties under the standing violations and penalties schedule and application irrespective of the entity's Nitaqat band; enforcement looks at actual work performed, not only job titles. As transmitted by SAMA, it binds SAMA-supervised financial institutions alongside other private-sector entities.

# Cyber Security Leadership and Governance

The Cyber Security Leadership and Governance domain of the Cyber Resilience Fundamental Requirements, setting board- and management-level expectations: a resourced cyber security governance structure, approved and communicated policies, procedures and supporting security standards, periodic review against the evolving threat landscape, embedding cyber and fraud risk considerations in the business operating model, password controls, and documented, centrally registered IT/cyber risk assessments. It also carries the defined terms for cyber security governance, incident, incident management, policy and risk. Binds entities within scope of the Fundamental Requirements, with cross-references to other SAMA frameworks.

**Regimes:** governance/risk

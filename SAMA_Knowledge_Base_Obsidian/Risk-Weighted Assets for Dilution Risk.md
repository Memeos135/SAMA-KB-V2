# Risk-Weighted Assets for Dilution Risk

Sub-section of the IRB purchased receivables chapter addressing the separate capital treatment of dilution risk (the risk that receivable amounts are reduced by credits, offsets or disputes with the obligor) and how it is combined with or separated from default risk. It explains recognition of guarantees and collateral covering dilution risk alone, default risk alone, or both, including interaction with securitisation first-loss treatment. Binds IRB-approved banks holding purchased receivable pools.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

# Basel III Framework

This node is the Basel III anchor for SAMA's prudential rulebook, mapping the local glossary abbreviations (SACAP, SCRE, SCCR, SMAR, SOPE, SLEV, SLCR, SNSF) to the SAMA circulars adopting the post-crisis final reforms on capital, credit, counterparty credit and CVA, market, operational risk, leverage and liquidity. It also frames the Pillar 3 key metrics disclosure (Template KM1), a mandatory quarterly, fixed-format overview of capital, leverage and liquidity metrics with accompanying narrative. It binds all SAMA-licensed banks subject to the Basel framework.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

### [[Pillar 3 Disclosure Requirements Framework]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** Treat the Basel III capital framework as the source of the measured amounts and the Pillar 3 framework as the binding rule on how those amounts must be publicly disclosed. The link is a hierarchy and defined-term relationship: the Pillar 3 requirements rely on the capital framework's glossary and calculation outputs, while specifying that disclosure is made at consolidated level unless SAMA directs otherwise. Consequently, a bank that is compliant on capital measurement can still breach the disclosure obligation if reporting level, scope or breakdowns diverge from the Pillar 3 requirements.

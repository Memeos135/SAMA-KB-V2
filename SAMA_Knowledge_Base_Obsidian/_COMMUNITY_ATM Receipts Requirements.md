# ATM Receipts Requirements

## Why this community

Consumer-facing disclosure standards for ATM transaction receipts — what information must be presented to the customer at the point of cash withdrawal or other ATM services.

## How members connect

- Single-instrument community: a SAMA initiative imposing minimum receipt content and availability duties on card issuers/ATM operators.
- Sits in the consumer protection and payments regime; consequence is a service-quality obligation testable against banks' ATM channel practice.

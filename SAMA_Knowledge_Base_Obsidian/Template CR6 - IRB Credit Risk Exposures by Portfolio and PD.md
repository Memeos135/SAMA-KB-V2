# Template CR6 - IRB Credit Risk Exposures by Portfolio and PD

Pillar 3 disclosure template CR6 setting out the portfolio-by-portfolio, PD-band presentation of IRB credit risk exposures, including EAD post-CRM, average PD, number of obligors, average LGD, average maturity, RWA and expected loss. Instructions specify separate template sets where a bank uses both F-IRB and A-IRB, coverage of key group-wide models, and narrative commentary on material changes. Mandatory for SAMA-licensed banks with approval to use the foundation or advanced IRB approaches.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

# Reduced Set of Risk Factors

Concept node defining the reduced set of risk factors used for the indirect calibration of the expected shortfall measure to a stress period, including the requirement for SAMA approval, compliance with modellable-risk-factor data quality standards, and the 75% explanatory-power threshold measured at group level across IMA-approved desks. It also frames identification of the stressed period by maximising the aggregate capital requirement for modellable risk factors. Binds banks using internal models for market risk capital.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

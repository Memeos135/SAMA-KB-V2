# Responsible Lending Principles for Individual Customers

SAMA's Responsible Lending Principles for Individual Customers, issued by circular in 1439H/2018 and amended shortly after, setting the framework for credit granted to individuals. It defines core terms (Creditor, Consumer, Finance Amount, Term Cost, Monthly Credit Obligations, Gross Salary) and imposes qualitative conduct duties plus quantitative deductible-ratio caps. It binds all creditors supervised and licensed by SAMA — banks and finance companies — across personal, vehicle, credit card and real estate finance directed at individual consumers, including where related work is assigned to third parties.

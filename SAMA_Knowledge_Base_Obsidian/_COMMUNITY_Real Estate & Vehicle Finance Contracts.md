# Real Estate & Vehicle Finance Contracts

## Why this community

Consumer-facing real estate and vehicle finance/lease contracting: mandatory contract content, risk disclosure, insurance and loss allocation, early repayment/early ownership, and default and termination consequences.

## How members connect

- Finance Lease Law (M/48) sits at the top of the hierarchy; the individuals' lease contract, contract-registry powers decision and early-repayment guide all cite or implement it.
- The early repayment calculation methodology is the shared computational rule linking real estate finance instructions, vehicle early-ownership provisions and BNPL treatment.
- Mandatory instructions for real estate finance to individuals attach the variable-rate credit risk acknowledgment as a required consumer disclosure step.
- Insurance, total/partial loss, default/termination and ownership transfer certificate operate as one allocation-of-risk chain within the individual lease contract.
- Cross-regime note: BNPL sits in a separate licensing regime with its own 'Consumer' concept, so read the shared early-repayment logic without assuming identical scope.

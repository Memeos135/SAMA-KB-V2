# Banks Investment Rules

The Banks Investment Rules (Version 1, April 2022) are SAMA's framework for how banks conduct and manage local and international investments across the trading and banking book. They set definitions, general prudential expectations on concentration and liquidity, documented investment rationale, governance, risk management and SAMA approval requirements. They bind banks licensed and supervised by SAMA, including foreign bank branches.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

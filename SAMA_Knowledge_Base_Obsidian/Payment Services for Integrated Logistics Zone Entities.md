# Payment Services for Integrated Logistics Zone Entities

A SAMA circular instructing payment sector companies to take account of the special nature of entities licensed under the Integrated Logistics Special Zone regime (Royal Order 17/1 dated 01/02/1440H) when providing payment services, and to update their policies and service agreements accordingly. It sets identity verification requirements for such entities: a copy of the commercial registration issued by the General Authority of Civil Aviation, the articles of association and annexes, and identification of persons authorised to open and manage accounts. Binds all licensed payment service providers dealing with ILSZ-licensed entities.

**Regimes:** payments, AML/CTF

# Simplified SA: Commodities Risk

The commodities risk class (CR_COMM) under SAMA's simplified standardised approach for market risk, capturing commodity price exposure plus non-delta option risks on commodity instruments. It is one of the four risk classes summed arithmetically before applying the 12.5 factor to obtain market risk RWA. Binds SAMA-licensed banks applying the simplified standardised approach, and is relevant to commodity positions held in the banking book that fall within the market risk capital scope.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

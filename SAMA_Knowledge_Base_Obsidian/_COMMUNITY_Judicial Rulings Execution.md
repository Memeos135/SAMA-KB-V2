# Judicial Rulings Execution

## Why this community

Supervisory instruction to financial institutions on timely implementation of court and adjudicatory decisions, including execution orders affecting customer accounts and funds.

## How members connect

- Single-member cluster: a standalone SAMA circular imposing a compliance timeliness obligation rather than a substantive licensing regime.
- Sits at the circular tier of the hierarchy, operating as an execution/enforcement overlay on customer account handling duties found in banking and consumer protection rules.
- No internal cross-references captured; linkage to other regimes must be established externally (e.g. account freezing/seizure and customer notification duties).

# Minimum Capital Requirements for Market Risk

SAMA's Minimum Capital Requirements for Market Risk framework (Basel FRTB as adopted in KSA), covering trading book/banking book boundary, internal risk transfers, the standardised approach, the internal models approach and related definitions. It obliges all banks — D-SIBs and non-D-SIBs — to compute market risk capital under the standardised approach, with RWAs derived by a 12.5 multiplier, and conditions recognition of internal risk transfers (including CVA-related transfers) on documentation and exact external hedging.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

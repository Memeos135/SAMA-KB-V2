# Guidelines on Physical Security for Saudi Banks

A cross-reference to SAMA's Guidelines on Physical Security for Saudi Banks (1995), including the Minimum Physical Security Standards issued that year, cited as a companion instrument to the operational risk guidance. It addresses the physical-security controls that mitigate robbery, burglary and related crime exposures at branches and ATMs, and is intended to be applied together with the internal control and disaster recovery guidelines by banks in the Kingdom.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential

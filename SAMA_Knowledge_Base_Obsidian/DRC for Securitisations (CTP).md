# DRC for Securitisations (CTP)

Addresses the default risk capital requirement for securitisation exposures in the correlation trading portfolio (CTP), computing gross JTD on the same basis as securitisations (non-CTP) — i.e. market value with no separate LGD ratio — with related rules on offsetting limits, tranche-based risk weights, the hedge benefit discount and simple summation across buckets. Binds banks holding CTP securitisation positions under the standardised market risk approach.

**Regimes:** banking prudential

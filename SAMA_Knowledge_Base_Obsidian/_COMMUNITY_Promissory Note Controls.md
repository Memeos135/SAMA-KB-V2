# Promissory Note Controls

## Why this community

Debt collection and repayment instruments used by financiers — the issuance and enforcement of promissory notes and the use of standing payment orders against borrower accounts.

## How members connect

- Both instruments govern how a financier secures and collects instalments, so they share the same consumer-protection concern about coercive or excessive collection mechanics.
- Linked conceptually rather than by citation: each constrains a distinct collection tool, and a financier's practice must satisfy both where the two are used together.
- Both operate downstream of finance company licensing and responsible lending obligations, and interact with consumer disclosure duties at contract formation.

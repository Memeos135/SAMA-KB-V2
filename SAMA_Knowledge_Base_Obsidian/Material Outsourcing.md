# Material Outsourcing

This node concerns the concept of Material outsourcing under SAMA's Rules on Outsourcing and the heightened obligations it triggers. Material arrangements require Board-approved policy coverage, a documented business case and risk-profile impact analysis, separate contingency planning, regular internal audit reporting to the Board or Board Audit Committee, immediate SAMA notification on termination with transition arrangements, and enhanced register entries (risk assessment date, approving body, commencement/expiry and notice periods, audit dates). SAMA may also require an external expert assessment of risk management around a Material outsourcing. It binds banks subject to the Rules.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential

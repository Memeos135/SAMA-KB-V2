# Template CCR8: Exposures to Central Counterparties

A mandatory fixed-format Pillar 3 template disclosing a bank's exposures to central counterparties, split between qualifying (QCCP) and non-qualifying CCPs, and broken down by trade exposures, segregated and non-segregated initial margin, and pre-funded and unfunded default fund contributions, reported as EAD post-CRM and RWA. Disclosure is semiannual with a narrative explaining significant period-on-period changes. It binds all banks subject to SAMA's Pillar 3 framework; the surrounding text also includes Table CRB-A on prudential treatment of problem assets (non-performing and forborne exposure definitions).

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

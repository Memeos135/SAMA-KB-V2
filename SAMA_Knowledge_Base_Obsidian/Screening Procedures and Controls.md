# Screening Procedures and Controls

Chapter Three of the Targeted Financial Sanctions Rules, covering screening procedures and controls that SAMA-supervised financial institutions must apply against sanctions lists. It is the operative source for when and against whom screening must be performed under the TFS regime (as distinct from general AML/CTF customer due diligence). Binding on all financial institutions within SAMA's regulatory and supervisory perimeter.

**Regimes:** sanctions/TFS, AML/CTF, governance/risk

# Beneficial Owner Inquiry Service for Non-Profit Sector

A SAMA circular directing supervised financial institutions to use the National Center for Non-Profit Sector Development's electronic service for inquiring about beneficial owner information of associations and non-profit foundations, as part of satisfying customer due diligence obligations. Where a beneficial owner is undisclosed by the association/foundation, or where discrepancies appear in the beneficial owner data, the institution must apply its CDD measures and consider suspicious transaction reporting, consistent with the AML Law, the CTF Law and their implementing regulations, and without breaching the Personal Data Protection Law and SAMA instructions; the Center is also to be notified.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, data/credit info

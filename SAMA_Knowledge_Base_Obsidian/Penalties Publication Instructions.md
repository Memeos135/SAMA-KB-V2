# Penalties Publication Instructions

SAMA instructions on publication of penalties, issued under the Finance Companies Control Law and its Implementing Regulation. Finance and refinance companies must disclose, in their annual board of directors' reports under a "SAMA enforcement decisions" heading, violations for the current and preceding financial year, showing the violation subject, number of enforcement decisions (monetary and non-monetary) and total fines in SAR, using categories for supervisory instructions, consumer protection and AML/CTF due diligence. SAMA will reconcile disclosures against its records, publish violations on its website, and act against non-compliance; effective from the FY2021 board report.

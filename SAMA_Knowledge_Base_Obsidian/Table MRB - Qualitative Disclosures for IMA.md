# Table MRB - Qualitative Disclosures for IMA

Pillar 3 disclosure table MRB within SAMA's Pillar 3 Disclosure Requirements Framework, setting the qualitative narrative banks must publish when they use the internal models approach (IMA) for market risk. It sits among the framework's other qualitative tables (e.g. credit-quality and problem-asset tables and the remuneration table REMA), each of which specifies purpose, scope, content, frequency and format. It binds banks in scope of the Pillar 3 regime, specifically those with SAMA approval to use internal models for market risk.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

# Leverage Ratio Framework (SLEV)

Node covering the leverage ratio as a non-risk-based capital standard within SAMA's prudential and disclosure framework, including its role in key prudential metrics reporting (KM1) and as a trigger for capital distribution constraints alongside risk-based buffers. It governs how banks measure and disclose Tier 1 capital against the leverage ratio exposure measure. Mandatory for all banks in scope of SAMA's Pillar 3 disclosure requirements.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

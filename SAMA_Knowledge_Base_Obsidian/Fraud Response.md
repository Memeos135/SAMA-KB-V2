# Fraud Response

The response pillar of SAMA's Counter-Fraud Framework, dealing with what a firm must do once fraud is suspected or confirmed — the Fraud Response Plan, reporting protocols, team responsibilities, information logging, investigation and corrective action. It interacts with cyber incident response and financial crime reporting obligations. Applies to Member Organizations under SAMA supervision, with oversight by the Counter-Fraud Governance Committee.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, AML/CTF

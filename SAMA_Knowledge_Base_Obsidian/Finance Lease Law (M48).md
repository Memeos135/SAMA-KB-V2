# Finance Lease Law (M/48)

Material issued under the Finance Lease Law (Royal Decree M/48) and its Implementing Regulation: a SAMA circular to the Saudi Finance Lease Contract Registration Company ("Sijil") transmitting the Governor's decision setting the competencies of the Head of the Contract Registry, plus the decision text itself. It governs appointment (subject to SAMA no-objection), the registration duties, liability and record-retention obligations of the registry head and registrars, complaint/grievance routes against refusal to register, and their duty to verify identity, capacity and Arabic-language documentation; it binds the registration company and its registry officials, with knock-on effects for lessors registering leases.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, governance/risk, other

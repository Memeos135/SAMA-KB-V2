# IT Change Management

## Why this community

Controls over changes to IT estate — change and release management, patching and pre-deployment testing — as a component of the IT governance expectations applicable to supervised entities.

## How members connect

- Patch management is treated as a species of change: it must run through the change/release control and be validated by testing before deployment.
- Testing is the common control condition linking the change and patch obligations; the governance glossary supplies the defined terms used across them.
- Hierarchy is framework-to-control: the IT governance instrument sets the umbrella requirement, the individual control statements specify what must be done.

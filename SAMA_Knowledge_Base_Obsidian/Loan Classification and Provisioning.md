# Loan Classification and Provisioning

Sets the classification and provisioning rules for DTFC loan portfolios: quarterly review and classification per Appendix C, contagion of non-performing status across all loans of a single borrower, and specific treatment of renegotiated/restructured and twice-restructured facilities (Substandard, Doubtful or Loss depending on repayment of past-due principal and profit). It also mandates profit suspension on non-performing exposures, reversal of accrued but uncollected profit, conditions for return to accrual, and minimum provisioning percentages (Normal 1%, Watch 5%, Substandard 25%, Doubtful 75%, Loss 100%), with a floor where IFRS impairment is lower. Binds every deposit-taking finance company supervised by SAMA.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, banking prudential, governance/risk

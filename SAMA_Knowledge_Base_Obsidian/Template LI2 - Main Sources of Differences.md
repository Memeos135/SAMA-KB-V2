# Template LI2 - Main Sources of Differences

Pillar 3 template reconciling financial statement carrying values (on the regulatory scope of consolidation) to the exposure amounts used for regulatory capital purposes, isolating drivers other than consolidation scope: off-balance sheet amounts, valuation differences, Basel netting rules, provisions and prudential filters. Columns split items by credit risk, counterparty credit risk, securitisation and market risk frameworks; format is flexible and rows adaptable, with Table LIA as the accompanying narrative. Mandatory for all banks under SAMA's Pillar 3 framework, reported annually.

**Regimes:** banking prudential

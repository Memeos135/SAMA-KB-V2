# GCC-NET POS Payments

## Why this community

The GCC-NET point-of-sale payment service — cross-border regional card acceptance and switching arrangements at POS involving Saudi payment infrastructure.

## How members connect

- Isolated node in the graph: no internal citation links, so its obligations must be read against the wider payments/PSP regime rather than inferred from neighbours.
- Relevant cross-regime touchpoints to check separately include payment service provider licensing and operational requirements, merchant acceptance/acquiring duties, and consumer protection on disputed or cross-border transactions.

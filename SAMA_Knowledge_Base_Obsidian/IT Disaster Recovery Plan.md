# IT Disaster Recovery Plan

This node addresses the IT Disaster Recovery Plan under the SAMA BCM Framework: a defined, approved and maintained IT DRP aligned to the BIA that restores data, systems, networks, services and applications after a disruptive incident. It requires an alternative data centre sited on a risk assessment showing it does not share the primary site's risks and subject to SAMA approval, capacity and configuration parity with the primary site, equivalent logical, physical, environmental and cyber security controls, backup/recovery processes with offsite storage, and formal third-party contracts aligned to BIA/RA outcomes. Accountability sits with the IT manager for keeping DR arrangements current, with the BCM manager accountable for integration into the BCM programme; it binds Member Organizations.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential

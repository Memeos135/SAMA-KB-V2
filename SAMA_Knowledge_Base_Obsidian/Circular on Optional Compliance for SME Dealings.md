# Circular on Optional Compliance for SME Dealings

A SAMA circular making compliance with specified articles of the Implementing Regulation of the Finance Companies Control Law optional for finance companies when dealing with small, medium and large enterprises. The definitions of SMEs and large enterprises are those in the referenced SAMA circular adopting the Small and Medium Enterprises General Authority board decision. It effectively narrows certain conduct/disclosure obligations to non-enterprise (retail) dealings.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, consumer protection

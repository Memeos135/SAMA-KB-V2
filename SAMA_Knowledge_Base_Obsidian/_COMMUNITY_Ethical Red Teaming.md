# Ethical Red Teaming

## Why this community

SAMA's cyber resilience testing regime for supervised financial entities: intelligence-led ethical red teaming, the roles of the parties involved, and the mandated reporting and remediation outputs.

## How members connect

- The F.E.E.R. framework is the parent instrument; all team roles, methodologies and deliverables are defined terms drawn from it.
- Role hierarchy: White Team commissions and controls the exercise, the Red Teaming Provider (with Threat Intelligence input) executes against the Cyber Kill Chain methodology, the Blue Team defends, and the Green Team supports.
- Obligation chain in deliverables: the Red Team Evaluation Report and Blue Team Report feed the Remediation Plan, which is the entity's corrective-action commitment to the supervisor.
- Scope boundary: red teaming is distinguished from ordinary Penetration Testing, so the two cannot be substituted for one another in demonstrating compliance.

# Standardized Approach Credit Risk Mitigation

Addresses the interface between standardized and IRB treatments for provisions and the recognition of credit risk mitigants on purchased receivables, including attribution of general provisions on a pro rata or entity basis and SAMA discretion over internal allocation methods. It also explains how guarantees covering default risk, dilution risk or both are recognised, including proportional and tranched coverage. Binds banks operating partial-use or mixed standardized/IRB credit risk frameworks.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

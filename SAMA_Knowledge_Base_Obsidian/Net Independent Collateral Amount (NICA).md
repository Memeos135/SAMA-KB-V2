# Net Independent Collateral Amount (NICA)

Concept node for the net independent collateral amount (NICA), the collateral term used in counterparty credit risk exposure measurement for derivatives to reflect independent collateral held or posted that is not variation margin. It is referenced within SAMA's minimum capital requirements and related prudential disclosure templates; the surrounding text in the sources also covers regulatory capital deductions and credit risk mitigation reporting. Binds banks calculating and disclosing counterparty credit exposures under SAMA's prudential framework.

**Regimes:** banking prudential

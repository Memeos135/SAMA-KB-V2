# Money Exchange Circulars

## Why this community

Supervisory requirements addressed to money exchange companies and exchange centres, covering workforce Saudization, external audit standards and the submission of financial statements to SAMA. A licensee-specific compliance and reporting cluster within the money-changing sector.

## How members connect

- Common addressee class — licensed exchange companies/centres — which defines the scope limit of all three circulars.
- Two of the three form a reporting/assurance chain: external auditor accounting standards govern the quality of the financial statements that must be filed with SAMA.
- The Saudization circular is conceptually adjacent rather than dependent — a separate obligation head (labour/nationalisation) imposed on the same licensees.
- All sit at circular level and operate under the money exchange licensing and supervision framework; the enabling law/rules are not represented in this cluster.

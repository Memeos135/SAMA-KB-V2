# Real Estate Finance Contracts Circular

An Arabic-language SAMA circular confirming that real estate finance contracts concluded with individual (retail) customers are subject to the finance laws, their implementing regulations and SAMA instructions, whether the real estate financier contracts in its own name, as agent, or on behalf of another party. Financiers must not enter new contracts unless this is satisfied and must correct arrangements with third parties accordingly, including cases where the credit-granting decision does not rest with the real estate financier. It binds banks and real estate finance companies operating in the Kingdom.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, consumer protection

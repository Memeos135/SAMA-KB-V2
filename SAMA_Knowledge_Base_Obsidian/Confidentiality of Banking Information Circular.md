# Confidentiality of Banking Information Circular

A Banking Supervision circular reaffirming a series of earlier SAMA circulars on disclosure of banking data: institutions must not provide any financial or banking information about their customers or their banking dealings to any party, whether individuals, entities, government bodies, or any other local or foreign party, without first referring to SAMA and obtaining its no-objection. Recipients must cascade it to all departments and branches and confirm compliance within a week. It binds banks, licensed money exchange institutions, and the Saudi Credit Bureau (SIMAH).

**Regimes:** data/credit info, consumer protection, governance/risk

# Template LIQ1: Liquidity Coverage Ratio

A fixed Pillar 3 disclosure template within SAMA's Pillar 3 Disclosure Requirements covering the Liquidity Coverage Ratio, i.e. the breakdown of high-quality liquid assets and weighted/unweighted cash inflows and outflows underlying the reported LCR. It sets what liquidity data banks must publish, at what frequency and in what fixed format, together with narrative commentary on drivers of change. It binds banks subject to SAMA's Pillar 3 regime and feeds the LCR figures also summarised in Template KM1.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

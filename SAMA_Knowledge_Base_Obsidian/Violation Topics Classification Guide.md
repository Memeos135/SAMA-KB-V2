# Violation Topics Classification Guide

The annex to SAMA's banking penalty publication circular: a mapping table translating the specific violation cited in a penal decision into one of the permitted public disclosure categories. Categories are limited to breach of SAMA supervisory instructions, consumer protection instructions, due diligence instructions, ATM/POS service-level instructions, and AML/CTF due diligence instructions; underlying decision topics range across banking procedures, account disclosure and seizure, liquidity, SAIBOR, interest rates, branch and ATM matters, outsourcing, products and services, clearing, e-service levels, internal policies, security, Saudization programmes, payment system operating rules, dispute/chargeback handling, and account opening rules.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, consumer protection, AML/CTF, payments, banking prudential

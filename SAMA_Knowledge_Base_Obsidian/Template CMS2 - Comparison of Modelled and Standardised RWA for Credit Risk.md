# Template CMS2 - Comparison of Modelled and Standardised RWA for Credit Risk

A Pillar 3 disclosure template requiring banks to compare, for credit risk specifically, RWA calculated under SAMA-approved modelled (IRB/supervisory slotting) approaches with RWA under the standardised approach, at portfolio/asset-class granularity. The instructions define the reference standardised approach (including credit risk mitigation via simple or comprehensive approach with supervisory haircuts) and the equity exposure IRB phase-in, and set out row scope exclusions for counterparty credit risk and banking-book securitisations. Applies to banks permitted by SAMA to use IRB approaches for credit risk.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

# Micro Finance and Micro Consumer Finance Activities

Node on microfinance and micro consumer finance activities under the amended Executive Regulation: the standalone Microfinance Activity Rules and the Micro Consumer Finance Companies Rules were repealed and these activities are now regulated within the Executive Regulation itself, with the applicable controls set out in its Article Nine. Definitions distinguish a microfinance company, a micro consumer finance company and a deferred payment (BNPL) company, each licensed for that single activity. Existing licensees were given a transition period to rectify their position.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, consumer protection, governance/risk

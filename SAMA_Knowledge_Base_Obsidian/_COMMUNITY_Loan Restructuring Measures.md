# Loan Restructuring Measures

## Why this community

Credit risk workout and forbearance: the recognised restructuring techniques a creditor may use with a distressed borrower, and their classification as short- or long-term measures.

## How members connect

- "Restructuring" is the umbrella defined term; each technique (forgiveness, splitting, debt-to-equity, debt-to-asset swaps) is an enumerated instance referenced from it.
- The short-term/long-term split drives differing treatment, monitoring and documentation expectations for each measure.
- Cross-regime consequence: restructuring decisions interact with provisioning/classification rules and, for retail exposures, with consumer protection and fair-treatment obligations.

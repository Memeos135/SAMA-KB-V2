# Leverage Ratio Framework

The leverage ratio component of SAMA's Basel-aligned capital framework and its associated Pillar 3 disclosure requirements, including the key metrics template (KM1) and the capital distribution constraints template (CDC). It defines the ratio as a non-risk-based backstop reported alongside risk-based capital and liquidity metrics, and identifies the leverage ratio buffer as a trigger for restrictions on capital distributions. Mandatory for all SAMA-supervised banks at consolidated group level, with quarterly (KM1) and annual (CDC) reporting frequencies.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

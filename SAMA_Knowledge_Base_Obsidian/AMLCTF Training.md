# AML/CTF Training

This node covers AML/CTF training obligations under SAMA's guidance: institutions must budget for and deliver ongoing training to senior management and staff, including annual specialised training for compliance function personnel, reflecting current ML/TF typologies and the institution's internal controls. The requirement derives from Article 14/1 of the AML Law Implementing Regulations and Article 18 of the Implementing Regulations of the Law on Combating Terrorism Crimes and its Financing, with the institution responsible for determining appropriate level and type of training. It binds SAMA-supervised financial institutions, their boards and senior management.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, governance/risk

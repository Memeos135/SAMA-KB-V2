# POS Devices Upgrade to 4G Technology

SAMA circular mandating the migration of point-of-sale terminals in the Kingdom to at least 4G connectivity, prompted by the telecoms regulator's decision to update terminal specifications and the phased shutdown of 2G and 3G networks. It requires POS suppliers to provide 4G-capable devices and obtain technical certification from Saudi Payments (mada network compatibility), makes 4G support the minimum for new and replacement installations from the stated date, and obliges banks and payment service providers hosting electronic payment services to execute a replacement plan for their existing POS fleet with minimum annual completion percentages and periodic progress reporting to SAMA and Saudi Payments. Binds banks, PSPs operating in the Kingdom, POS suppliers and Saudi Payments.

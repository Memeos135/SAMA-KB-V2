# General Directorate of Financial Intelligence

The General Directorate of Financial Intelligence (formerly the Financial Investigation Unit) is the national FIU that receives and analyses suspicious transaction reports under the AML Law and its Implementing Regulation. The cited provisions govern STR filing where CDD cannot be completed or would tip off the customer, the FIU's operational and strategic analysis functions, its confidentiality and information-security duties, and its required operational independence and autonomy. It binds financial institutions and DNFBPs as reporting entities, and frames the FIU's own institutional obligations.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, governance/risk

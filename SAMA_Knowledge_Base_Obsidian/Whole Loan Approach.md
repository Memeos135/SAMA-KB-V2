# Whole Loan Approach

Refers to the "whole loan" method of risk weighting real estate exposures, under which a single risk weight determined by the exposure's LTV band is applied to the total exposure amount rather than splitting it into secured and unsecured portions. It is the treatment used for regulatory commercial real estate exposures that are materially dependent on property cash flows (and other specified real estate categories). Applies to banks computing standardised-approach credit RWAs.

**Regimes:** banking prudential

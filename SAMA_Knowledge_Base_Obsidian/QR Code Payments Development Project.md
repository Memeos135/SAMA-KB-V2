# QR Code Payments Development Project

A SAMA circular on the QR Code payments development project run with Saudi Payments, issued under SAMA's authority over payment and settlement systems (confirmed by Council of Ministers Resolution No. 226). It directs entities that had already launched QR-based payment services to comply with the project's technical and commercial standards for the payment environment and to complete required testing and certification with Saudi Payments before end-2021. It binds banks and SAMA-licensed fintech/payment companies offering QR code payment acceptance.

**Regimes:** payments

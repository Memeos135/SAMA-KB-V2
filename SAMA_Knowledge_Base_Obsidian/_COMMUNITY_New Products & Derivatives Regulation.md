# New Products & Derivatives Regulation

## Why this community

Pre-launch regulatory clearance and governance for new banking products and services, including the specific regime for offering financial derivatives. Central question is when a product may be launched on notification versus requiring SAMA non-objection, and what internal governance must precede either.

## How members connect

- The New Banking Products and Services Regulation is the parent instrument; the governance, risk-assessment, compliance and notification/non-objection members are its constituent obligation sets.
- Two-track procedural chain: product risk classification determines whether the bank notifies SAMA or must obtain prior non-objection before offering.
- Board and senior management duties form an accountability hierarchy — approval of the product framework at board level, execution and risk assessment at management level, independent verification by compliance.
- Cross-regime hooks: consumer protection requirements and the Counter-Fraud Framework must be addressed within the product risk assessment; derivatives offerings additionally trigger OTC trade repository reporting.

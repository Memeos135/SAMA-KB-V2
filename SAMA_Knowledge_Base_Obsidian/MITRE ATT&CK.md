# MITRE ATT&CK

This node concerns the MITRE ATT&CK framework as referenced and defined in SAMA's Cyber Threat Intelligence Principles: an open-source taxonomy of tactics, techniques and procedures used by threat actors. It is cited in the Operational CTI domain, where Member Organizations are expected to structure attack-phase analysis and adopt a TTP taxonomy for threat actors, tools and malware. Relevant to entities supervised by SAMA that must evidence a structured operational CTI practice.

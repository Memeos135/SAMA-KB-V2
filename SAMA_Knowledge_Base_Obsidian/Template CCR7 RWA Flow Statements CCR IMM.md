# Template CCR7: RWA Flow Statements CCR IMM

A Pillar 3 template presenting the flow statement of risk-weighted assets for counterparty credit risk exposures calculated under the Internal Model Method (IMM), explaining movements in RWA over the reporting period by driver. The accompanying text sets out how RWA and minimum capital requirements (normally RWA × 8%) are allocated across credit risk, CCR, CVA, equity, settlement and securitisation rows, and how standardised versus IRB/slotting approaches are distinguished. It applies to banks with SAMA approval to use internal models for CCR.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

# Benefiting from GAA Services for Verifying Beneficial Owner Data

A SAMA circular (Ref. 482008.0.22, 29/01/1448H) directing supervised financial institutions to use the General Authority for Awqaf's channels — technical integration, the data office email/data-sharing forms, the endowment deed verification service, and the public register of endowments — when identifying and verifying beneficial owner data for waqf (endowment) entities. It is grounded in the Banking Control Law framework and the AML Law's Implementing Regulation CDD obligations, and requires institutions to notify the Authority, via its designated form, of any discrepancies found between Authority data and their own CDD results, without prejudice to STR reporting duties and consistent with the Personal Data Protection Law. It binds all financial institutions under SAMA supervision from the date of publication.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, data/credit info, governance/risk

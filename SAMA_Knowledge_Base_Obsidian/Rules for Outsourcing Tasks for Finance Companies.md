# Rules for Outsourcing Tasks for Finance Companies

SAMA rules (2018) governing outsourcing by finance companies, issued under the Finance Companies Control Law and its Implementing Regulation. They define outsourcing and service provider, distinguish material from non-material functions (including any arrangement involving sharing, transferring, processing or storing beneficiary data), and require a board-approved annually updated outsourcing policy, risk assessment at contracting and renewal, due diligence on the provider's licences and qualifications, data confidentiality and retrieval/destruction safeguards, record keeping, and monitoring. Responsibility remains with the finance company, and SAMA and the external auditor retain access to the provider's information and premises.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, BNPL/finance, data/credit info

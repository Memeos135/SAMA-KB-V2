# Template CMS1 - Comparison of Modelled and Standardised RWA

Template CMS1 is the Pillar 3 comparison template requiring banks that use internal models to disclose actual RWA alongside RWA as they would be if the full standardised approach were applied, broken down by risk type (credit, counterparty credit, CVA, securitisation, market, operational and residual RWA). Its instructions define, per row, what counts as the standardised approach and which exposures fall into modelled versus standardised cells. It applies to banks with SAMA approval to use modelled approaches under the disclosure framework.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

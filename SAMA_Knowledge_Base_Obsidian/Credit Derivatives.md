# Credit Derivatives

Covers the capital treatment of credit derivatives under SAMA's credit risk framework from both sides: banks selling protection through first-, second- and nth-to-default baskets must aggregate underlying risk weights (capped at 1250%) against the notional protection, while as a mitigation technique only credit default swaps and total return swaps equivalent to guarantees are eligible and nth-to-default protection bought gives no capital relief. Also sets the protected/uncovered portion split and 1250% treatment of materiality thresholds. Binds SAMA-supervised banks.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

# Reduction of Recourse on Real Estate Financiers for SRC Portfolios

A SAMA circular easing the terms on which real estate financiers may sell residential real estate finance portfolios to the Saudi Real Estate Refinance Company (SRC): sale is permitted after at least one month from the financing grant date or one month from receipt of the first instalment from the beneficiary, whichever is earlier, with a shortened recourse period on the originating financier. It also exempts portfolios sold to SRC from the prior SAMA no-objection requirement under the rules on disposal of finance assets, subject to continued compliance with statutory periods for the disposed contracts and notification to SAMA within five business days of the final sale contract. Binds banks and finance companies operating in the Kingdom and SRC.

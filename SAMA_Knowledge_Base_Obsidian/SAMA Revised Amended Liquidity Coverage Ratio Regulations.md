# SAMA Revised Amended Liquidity Coverage Ratio Regulations

A 2014 SAMA circular to all banks issuing a revised package of guidance for the Amended Liquidity Coverage Ratio, following a comprehensive gap review of SAMA's Basel III regulations against BCBS standards. The package comprises general and specific guidance, the prudential return, FAQs and national discretions, and banks are also directed to benchmark liquidity practices against BCBS sound liquidity risk management principles. It binds all banks supervised by SAMA and supersedes/updates the earlier 2013 LCR guidance.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

# Products and Services Risk Assessment

Requirements for banks to assign responsibility for and conduct full risk assessments of new products and services across their life cycle, to maintain risk management standards (due diligence, approvals, identification/measurement/monitoring/reporting, change management), and to apply a risk classification process producing an overall rating such as high/medium/low. It also requires third-party risk management where products are offered with fintechs, agents or similar partners, and gives the risk management function documentation, review and approval duties over product risk profiles, alongside internal audit, product development and ongoing monitoring obligations. Binds banks licensed under the Banking Control Law.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential, consumer protection

# QR Code Payments Project

## Why this community

A single, isolated node on the development of QR code-based payment acceptance, sitting within the payments/PSP space. It is project- or initiative-level material rather than a standing obligation set.

## How members connect

- Singleton community with no internal legal cross-references in the graph.
- Natural linkage points lie outside this cluster: payment service provider licensing, merchant acceptance and merchant-as-customer KYB duties.
- Treat as context material; any enforceable requirement must be sourced from the payments regulations it would sit under.

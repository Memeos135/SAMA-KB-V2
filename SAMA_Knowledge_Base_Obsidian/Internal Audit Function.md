# Internal Audit Function

Concept node for the Internal Audit Function as defined in SAMA's Internal Audit Principles for Finance Companies and Real Estate Refinance Companies: an independent, objective assurance and consulting activity assessing the quality, adequacy and effectiveness of the internal control system and of governance, risk management and control processes. The node covers the department's independence and objectivity, conflicts of interest, audit charter, policy, plan, reporting, external quality assessment and its relationship with first- and second-line units. It binds licensed finance companies and real estate refinance companies through their boards and audit committees.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, BNPL/finance

# Electronic Delegation for Judicial Notifications

A SAMA circular urging financial institutions to use the Ministry of Commerce "Electronic Delegation" service to appoint a representative to receive judicial notifications served against them and to monitor judicial orders via the Najiz portal. It relies on the Royal Order approving electronic means of judicial service and on the Enforcement Law and its Implementing Regulations (notification timing rules under Article 34), with the aim of allowing institutions to settle claims within the notification period and avoid coercive court measures. Addressed to SAMA-supervised financial institutions, issued through the payments supervision department.

**Regimes:** payments, governance/risk, other

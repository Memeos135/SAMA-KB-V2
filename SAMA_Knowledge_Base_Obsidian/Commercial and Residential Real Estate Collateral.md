# Commercial and Residential Real Estate Collateral

This node sets when commercial and residential real estate may be recognised as eligible collateral for corporate claims under IRB — requiring legal enforceability and a perfected lien, objective fair-market valuation, and revaluation at least annually (more often in volatile markets) — and excludes income-producing real estate falling in the specialised lending asset class. It also carries the standardised approach definition of the real estate exposure class (regulatory residential/commercial real estate, other real estate, and ADC exposures). Binds banks calculating credit risk capital on real estate-secured exposures.

**Regimes:** banking prudential

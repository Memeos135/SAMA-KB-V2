# Credit Limits

Covers the quantitative credit limits and the pre-financing credit checks in the debt-based crowdfunding rules: aggregate outstanding finance through the platform capped at 40 times the company's capital and reserves, financing restricted to Saudi-registered commercial enterprises with consumer-purpose financing prohibited, a SAR 7,500,000 cap per MSME Institutional Beneficiary (carve-outs for large enterprises and licensed real estate developers), and per-Participant concentration limits — each subject to SAMA written non-objection where relief is sought. It also ties in mandatory credit-record checking and credit bureau registration on beneficiary consent, with refusal of the finance request absent consent, plus KYC, information security and Electronic Transactions Law verification duties. Binds the licensed Debt-Based Crowdfunding Company.

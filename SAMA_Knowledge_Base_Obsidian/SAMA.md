# SAMA

Node for the Saudi Central Bank as the issuing and supervisory authority of the Minimum Capital Requirements for Market Risk (December 2022). SAMA sets scope of application, approves trading desks and internal models, imposes backtesting add-ons or multiplication factor increases, and may disallow model use or require standardised approach calculations. It is the counterparty of every supervisory obligation in the framework for banks operating in KSA.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

# Template TLAC2 - Material Subgroup Entity Creditor Ranking

A Pillar 3 disclosure template requiring, on a legal entity basis, disclosure of creditor ranking in the liabilities structure of each material subgroup entity that has issued internal TLAC to a G-SIB resolution entity, including excluded liabilities, TLAC-eligible amounts and residual maturity buckets. The accompanying material also covers the parallel template for resolution entities themselves, which omits the resolution-entity-as-creditor information. Mandatory for G-SIBs only, reported semiannually per resolution group.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

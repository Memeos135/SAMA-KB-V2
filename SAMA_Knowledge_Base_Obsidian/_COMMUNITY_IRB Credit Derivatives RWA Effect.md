# IRB Credit Derivatives RWA Effect

## Why this community

Pillar 3 credit-risk disclosure showing the effect of credit derivatives used as risk mitigants on risk-weighted assets calculated under the internal ratings-based approach. It addresses transparency over how recognised credit protection changes IRB capital outcomes.

## How members connect

- Single-member cluster: a standalone disclosure template within the SAMA-adopted Pillar 3 framework, sitting under the capital adequacy/Basel regime rather than conduct or AML rules.
- Legally dependent on the IRB permission and the credit risk mitigation eligibility rules that determine when a credit derivative may reduce RWA at all.
- Links upward through the disclosure hierarchy: Basel standard as adopted by SAMA circular, then the template as the prescribed reporting form.

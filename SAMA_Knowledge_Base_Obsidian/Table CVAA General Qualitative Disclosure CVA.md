# Table CVAA: General Qualitative Disclosure CVA

Table CVAA is the general qualitative disclosure table for credit valuation adjustment (CVA) risk under SAMA's Pillar 3 framework, covering a bank's CVA risk management, covered transactions and hedging. It is one element of the CVA disclosure set (CVAA, CVA1–CVA4, CVAB) and applies only where the CCR/CVA materiality threshold in SAMA's revised risk-based capital charge for counterparty credit risk is met. It binds SAMA-supervised banks with covered derivative and SFT exposures in banking and trading book.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

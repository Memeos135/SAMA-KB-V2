# Counterparty Credit Risk (CCR) Framework

SAMA's minimum capital framework for counterparty credit risk and credit valuation adjustment risk, setting out the definition of CCR (bilateral risk of loss before final settlement), the recognised CCR-related risks such as rollover risk and general/specific wrong-way risk, and the permitted CVA approaches (BA-CVA, SA-CVA and alternative treatment). It binds banks supervised by SAMA, effective 1 January 2023, and requires quarterly reporting of CCR and CVA risk-weighted assets and capital charges on SAMA's Q17 template within 30 days of quarter-end. Related disclosure guidance maps CCR and CVA amounts to specific rows of the Pillar 3 RWA overview template.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

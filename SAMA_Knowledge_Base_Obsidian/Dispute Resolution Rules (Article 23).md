# Dispute Resolution Rules (Article 23)

Article 23 (Section Seven) of the Consumer Financing Regulations governing account statement errors and disputes: it defines what counts as an account statement error (failure to credit payments, accounting errors, improper fees) and sets the Creditor's investigation duty within 60 calendar days and no later than 90 calendar days from receipt of the Borrower's notice. Pending satisfactory resolution, the Borrower need not pay the disputed portion (including Term Cost) and the Creditor may not collect it, nor make or threaten adverse credit reporting or delinquency reporting on that basis. Confirmed errors must be corrected and refunded with notice by Guaranteed Communication means.

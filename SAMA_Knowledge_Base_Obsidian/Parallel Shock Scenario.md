# Parallel Shock Scenario

Defines the parallel shock scenario within SAMA's standardised IRRBB shock set: a constant upward or downward shift applied across all time buckets using the currency-specific parallel shock size in Table 1 (e.g. SAR 275 bp, USD 200 bp). It is one of the six EVE scenarios and is subject to the zero floor on post-shock rates. Applies to banks measuring IRRBB per material currency.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

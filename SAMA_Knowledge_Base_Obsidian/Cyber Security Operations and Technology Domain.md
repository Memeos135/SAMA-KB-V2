# Cyber Security Operations and Technology Domain

A domain node within SAMA's Cyber Security Framework covering the operational and technological control areas that Member Organizations must define, approve, implement and monitor — including asset and identity controls, secure disposal, payment systems and electronic banking services. It sits alongside the framework's maturity model, under which controls must be formally documented, approved and demonstrably implemented to reach the target maturity level. It binds Member Organizations supervised by SAMA (banks, insurers and financing companies), with ultimate accountability resting with the Board.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, payments

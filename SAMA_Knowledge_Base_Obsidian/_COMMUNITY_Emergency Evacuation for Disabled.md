# Emergency Evacuation for Disabled

## Why this community

Accessibility and safety obligations toward persons with disabilities in financial-sector premises, specifically emergency evacuation arrangements.

## How members connect

- Standalone guidance instrument; operates as a conduct/consumer-accessibility expectation rather than a prudential rule.
- Connects conceptually to consumer protection principles on fair and equal access to financial services and premises.
- Guide-level status means it sets expected practice; supervisory scrutiny attaches to whether the entity can demonstrate equivalent protection.

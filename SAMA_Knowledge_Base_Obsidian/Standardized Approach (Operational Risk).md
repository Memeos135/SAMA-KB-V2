# Standardized Approach (Operational Risk)

Node on the standardised approach as the required methodology for calculating operational risk capital (and, by contrast, the standardised approach used for credit risk RWA reporting in the same framework). It anchors the BI/BIC/ILM calculation chain that replaces earlier and internal-model based approaches. Applies to SAMA-licensed banks for regulatory capital computation and Pillar 3 disclosure.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

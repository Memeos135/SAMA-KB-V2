# Guidelines for Applying for BNPL License

SAMA guidance setting out how founders apply for a licence to conduct Buy Now Pay Later activity under the Finance Companies Control Law and the Rules Regulating BNPL Companies. It specifies submission channel and format, the unmodifiable application form, the checklist of required documents (draft articles of association, organisational structure, fit and proper forms for founders and board/committee candidates, feasibility study and business plan, and an irrevocable bank guarantee equal to 20% of minimum capital), and the licensing procedure. Binds applicants (founders/shareholders) seeking BNPL authorisation; false or omitted material information is treated as a violation of the Finance Companies Control Law and may cause rejection or cancellation of the licence.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, governance/risk

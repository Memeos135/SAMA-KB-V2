# IRRBB Disclosure Templates

## Why this community

Pillar 3 disclosure of interest rate risk in the banking book (IRRBB), covering both the qualitative description of a bank's IRRBB governance and measurement approach and the quantitative impact of prescribed interest rate shock scenarios on economic value of equity and net interest income.

## How members connect

- Both members are components of the same IRRBB disclosure requirement: the narrative table supplies the modelling assumptions, measurement basis and risk appetite that make the quantitative template interpretable.
- The quantitative template explicitly refers back to the qualitative table, creating a paired obligation - a bank cannot satisfy one without the other.
- Shared defined terms (banking book, economic value of equity, net interest income, supervisory shock scenarios) and a common reporting frequency and scope of application under the disclosure framework.
- Sits in the governance/risk regime as a downstream implementation of the banking capital and risk management rules, not a standalone rule.

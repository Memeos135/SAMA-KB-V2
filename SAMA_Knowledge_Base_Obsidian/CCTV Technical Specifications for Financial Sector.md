# CCTV Technical Specifications for Financial Sector

SAMA's CCTV/security surveillance technical specifications (v1.0, July 2019) for financial-sector facilities, setting operational deployment requirements for CCTV rooms and cameras, access restriction and logging, daily system checks, vendor/technician attendance controls, incident reporting for overwritten or missing footage, and cooperation with law enforcement through bank procedures. It also fixes retention rules: a 90-day minimum for head office, branches, cash centres and ATMs, one year for footage relating to a customer claim or complaint, and five years where a claim or complaint is rejected, plus surveillance-objective standards (identification, recognition, detection) for camera coverage. It binds banks and financial organisations operating the covered facilities in the Kingdom.

# Assessing Effect of Credit Derivatives

This node covers regulatory capital treatment of credit derivatives: eligibility of credit default swaps and total return swaps as guarantee-equivalent protection (with an exclusion where net swap income is booked without recognising deterioration of the protected asset), ineligibility of first-to-default and other nth-to-default derivatives as credit risk mitigants, and the capital treatment for banks selling such basket protection. It also sets the general substitution-based risk weighting of protected and uncovered portions and the 1250% treatment of amounts below materiality thresholds. Binds SAMA-supervised banks buying or selling credit protection.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

# Framework Waiver Request Process

Appendices C and D of the IT Governance Framework setting out how a Member Organization requests a waiver from a specific control it cannot implement. The request must explain why the control cannot be met and describe available or suggested compensating controls, be approved by the CIO and IT steering committee, be signed by the CIO and business owner, and be issued formally by the CEO or managing director to SAMA's General Department of Cyber Risk Control. The control remains binding until the waiver is granted, and any waiver is valid for one year with renewal the Member Organization's responsibility.

# Services for Persons with Disabilities in Financial Institutions

SAMA instructions on services provided to persons with disabilities by financial institutions, aimed at financial inclusion, equal access and removal of physical and behavioural barriers so customers can manage their finances independently and privately. Requirements include establishing a dedicated administrative unit, accessible built environment across premises and branches, accessible self-service and electronic channels, fairness and transparency in dealings, and financial education for this group. They apply to all financial institutions supervised by SAMA, including banks, finance companies, insurers/reinsurers, credit information companies, exchange businesses and payment companies.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, governance/risk

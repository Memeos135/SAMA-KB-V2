# Securitisation Originator Capital

## Why this community

Pillar 3 disclosure of securitisation exposures where the bank acts as originator or sponsor, together with the associated capital requirements.

## How members connect

- Single-member cluster linked to the securitisation exposure templates: exposures reported there are mapped here into risk-weight bands and resulting capital charges.
- Turns on the originator/sponsor definitions and on whether significant risk transfer conditions are met — a classification question that drives whether exposures remain in scope of the securitisation framework.
- Sits at the bottom of the hierarchy (framework rules to disclosure template); the template prescribes presentation, not the underlying capital treatment.

# Short Rate Shock Scenario

Defines the short rate shock scenario in SAMA's standardised IRRBB framework: a shock largest at the shortest tenor midpoint that decays exponentially towards zero at the longest tenor via a shaping scalar with decay parameter x = 4 (unless SAMA determines otherwise). Includes a worked example converting a 100 bp short shock to 41.7 bp at a 3.5-year tenor midpoint. Applies to banks calculating IRRBB per material currency.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

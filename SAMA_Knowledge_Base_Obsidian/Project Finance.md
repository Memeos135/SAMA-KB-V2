# Project Finance

Defines project finance as a specialized lending sub-class where repayment and security derive primarily from a single project's revenues, typically through an SPV, and sets its capital treatment where no issue-specific external rating exists: 130% pre-operational, 100% operational, and 80% for high-quality operational exposures. It specifies the operational phase test (positive net cash flow covering remaining obligations and declining long-term debt) and the cumulative conditions for "high quality" status. Applies to SAMA-supervised banks calculating credit risk RWAs.

**Regimes:** banking prudential

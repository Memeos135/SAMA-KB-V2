# Branding and Surveillance Requirements

## Why this community

Two conduct/operational control requirements applied to supervised entities' customer-facing presence: how products may be marketed under a brand other than the licensee's own, and the physical security surveillance standards for premises.

## How members connect

- Both are supervisory conduct requirements addressed to the licensed entity at the point of customer interaction — one governing identity/disclosure of who is actually providing the product, the other governing the physical environment where that interaction occurs.
- The link is thematic rather than a citation chain: neither instrument is the legal basis for the other, so each should be read against its own scope and addressee.
- Brand-identity rules matter for consumer protection and accountability (the consumer must be able to identify the licensed provider); surveillance requirements sit in the operational risk and fraud/physical-security space.

# SAMA Confirmation of Real Estate Financier Obligations in Secondary Market

A SAMA circular confirming that a real estate financier that transfers its contractual rights in the secondary market (including through licensed real estate refinancing companies) is not released from its obligations under the Real Estate Finance Law, its Implementing Regulation, related instructions and the contracts, and must continue performing them for the contract term. It cites examples such as prompt updating of customers' credit records within the period set in the Credit Information Law Implementing Regulation, and handling early repayment and debt rescheduling. It also addresses enabling a third party assigned collection duties while the financier retains responsibility; binds banks and finance companies.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, consumer protection, data/credit info

# Guidance Document Concerning NSFR

This is SAMA's guidance document implementing the Basel III Net Stable Funding Ratio, covering scope of application, calculation and at-least-quarterly reporting frequency, minimum requirements, and detailed factor guidance for liabilities, capital and derivative positions (including replacement cost, eligible bilateral netting and cash variation margin treatment). It cross-references earlier SAMA Basel II and leverage-ratio circulars for scope and derivative measurement. It binds banks supervised by SAMA, primarily internationally active banks on a consolidated basis.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

# Instructions for Financiers Dealing with Promissory Notes

SAMA instructions setting minimum requirements for how financiers deal with promissory notes, issued under the Central Bank Law and the Finance Companies Control Law and effective from the date stated in the covering circular. They require a board-approved policy covering steps before and during court enforcement of a promissory note, documentation (financing contract, matured note, proof of default and of contact with the customer), designated responsible departments and approval authority, and compliance obligations where enforcement is outsourced to a third party. They bind banks and finance companies operating in the Kingdom and operate without prejudice to related SAMA instructions on collection, standard contracts, outsourcing and blank promissory notes.

# Circular on Land Financing Subject to Article 12

Urgent SAMA circular confirming that land financing granted to individuals falls within Article 12 of the Implementing Regulation of the Real Estate Finance Law. It responds to recurring queries on scope and applies to all real estate financiers — banks and finance companies — operating in the Kingdom.

# Credit Information Company

Concept node for credit information companies as they arise within SAMA's bank account rules; the surrounding material concerns documentation for opening accounts for legal persons (joint-stock companies including those under formation, LLCs, partnerships, GCC commercial entities) and the periodic updating of customer identification and information. It sets when identity documents must be refreshed (on expiry for documents valid under five years; every five years or on expiry for longer-dated ones; five years maximum for accounts opened under official letters or open-term licences), permits online identity updating with exclusions for incompetent persons, and lists triggers for updating customer information including behavioural change and ML/FT risk-driven due diligence. Binds banks; open it for entity KYC documentation and refresh cycles rather than for credit bureau licensing rules.

**Regimes:** data/credit info, AML/CTF, banking prudential

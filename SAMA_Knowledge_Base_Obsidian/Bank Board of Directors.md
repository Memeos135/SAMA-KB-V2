# Bank Board of Directors

This node covers the Bank board of directors' accountability under the Agent Banking Regulation (notably Articles 3 and 25): the board must ensure regulatory compliance, approve the policies and procedures governing agent selection and management, and ensure mechanisms exist for departments to implement SAMA instructions and to monitor agent activity proportionate to risk. It confirms the Bank is solely responsible for agent selection and fully liable for all acts and omissions of its Agents, including unauthorised acts connected to Agent Banking. Binds Banks contracting Agents; relevant where responsibility for outsourced customer-facing activity is contested.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential, consumer protection, AML/CTF

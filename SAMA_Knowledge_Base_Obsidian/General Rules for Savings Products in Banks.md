# General Rules for Savings Products in Banks

A SAMA rulebook setting general requirements for savings products offered by banks operating in the Kingdom, issued in both Arabic and English. It binds SAMA-licensed banks in the design, disclosure and administration of savings/deposit products offered to customers. The available extract is limited to the title/issuance page, so the substantive obligations must be read in the full document.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, banking prudential, governance/risk

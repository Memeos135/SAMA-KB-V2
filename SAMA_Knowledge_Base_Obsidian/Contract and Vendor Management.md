# Contract and Vendor Management

Control 3.4.1 requiring Member Organizations to embed defined and approved cyber security requirements into contract and vendor management processes, monitor compliance, and periodically measure control effectiveness. It addresses when the cyber security function must be involved (e.g. due diligence), baseline security requirements applicable in all cases, and rights to conduct periodic reviews across the contract life-cycle. Binds SAMA-supervised Member Organizations contracting with third parties.

**Regimes:** governance/risk

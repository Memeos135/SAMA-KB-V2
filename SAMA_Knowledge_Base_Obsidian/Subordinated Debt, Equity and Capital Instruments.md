# Subordinated Debt, Equity and Capital Instruments

This node covers the standardised-approach treatment of subordinated debt, equity and other regulatory capital instruments issued by corporates or banks, including the scope carve-outs for instruments deducted from capital, weighted at 250% under the Basel III capital framework, or at 1250%, and equity fund investments treated elsewhere. It also carries the substance-over-form test for identifying equity holdings and the remaining eligibility conditions for high-quality project finance. It binds banks computing credit RWAs.

**Regimes:** banking prudential

# Loans to Deposits Ratio (LDR)

The defined regulatory metric within SAMA's 2023 guidelines: net loans (loans and advances less loan-loss provisions, unearned commission income and commission in suspense) divided by maturity-weighted deposits (deposits and repos plus long-term debt such as sukuk/bonds, syndicated and subordinated debt), with interbank and SAMA transactions excluded unless SAMA specifies otherwise. SAMA expects banks to keep total LDR below 90%, subject to the numerator not exceeding the unweighted denominator, with weights ranging from 100% for demand/overnight funding to 190% for over-five-year and perpetual instruments.

**Regimes:** banking prudential

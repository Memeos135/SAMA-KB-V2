# Template OR3: Minimum Required Operational Risk Capital

Template OR3 covers disclosure of the minimum required operational risk capital, sitting within the Pillar 3 suite alongside the overview of RWA and minimum capital requirements (normally RWA × 8%, subject to floors or jurisdictional adjustments) and capital distribution constraint disclosures. The accompanying material explains how RWA and capital requirements are allocated across credit risk approaches, counterparty credit risk, CVA and equity positions. It binds SAMA-supervised banks subject to the Pillar 3 framework.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

# Transactors

Defines "transactors" for credit risk RWA purposes: obligors on credit card/charge card facilities whose balance was repaid in full at each scheduled repayment date over the previous 12 months, and overdraft obligors with no drawdown over the same period. The classification matters because regulatory retail exposures to transactors attract a 45% risk weight instead of 75%. Applies to banks applying the standardised approach for credit risk.

**Regimes:** banking prudential

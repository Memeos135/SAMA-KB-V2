# Securitisation Exposure

A concept node within SAMA's Minimum Capital Requirements for Market Risk covering the default risk charge (DRC) treatment of securitisation exposures outside the correlation trading portfolio. It sets how gross and net jump-to-default positions are computed for tranches — market value rather than an LGD-adjusted exposure, since LGD is embedded in securitisation default risk weights — and restricts offsetting to tranches sharing the same underlying asset pool, with limited recognition of decomposition into replicating tranches or single-name equivalents. It binds banks calculating market risk capital under the standardised approach.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

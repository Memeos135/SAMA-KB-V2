# Updated Debt Collection Regulations for Individual Customers

SAMA circular transmitting the updated Debt Collection Regulations (controls and procedures) for individual customers, issued under the Central Bank Law and superseding the earlier collection circular. It defines key terms (Customer, Guarantor, Third Party, default, involuntary/voluntary change of circumstances, total disability, documented contact, telephone contact, complaint) and sets minimum conduct rules on contacting customers and guarantors, rescheduling for defaulting customers, death/total-disability waivers, telephone contact limits, and deduction of due instalments from customer accounts including joint/solidary borrowers per agreed liability shares. It binds banks and finance companies supervised by SAMA and, expressly, third parties carrying out collection on their behalf.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, BNPL/finance, governance/risk

# Currency Exchange Centers Readiness for Ramadan and Hajj

A SAMA circular, issued under the money exchange business rules, directing currency exchange centers in Makkah, Madinah, King Abdulaziz International Airport and the Jeddah Hujjaj City to raise premises readiness and operate at full capacity for the 1447H Ramadan and Hajj seasons. An annex lists minimum operational and consumer-facing requirements (cash and denomination availability, suitable hours, crowd management, staff ID, displayed exchange-rate boards linked live to the system, receipts for all transactions, customer-rights signage, POS acceptance, CCTV, security staff, counterfeit-detection machines, mapping the location, and reporting unlicensed exchange activity to security authorities and SAMA).

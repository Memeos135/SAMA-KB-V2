# Inferred Ratings Operational Requirements

Node on the operational requirements for using external credit assessments and inferred ratings under the external-ratings-based approach to securitization exposures (SEC-ERBA), including the risk weight tables for short-term and long-term rated positions and the drivers of the weight (rating grade, seniority, tranche maturity, tranche thickness). It conditions eligibility to apply SEC-ERBA on meeting the stated operational criteria. Binds banks risk-weighting rated or inferred-rated securitization positions.

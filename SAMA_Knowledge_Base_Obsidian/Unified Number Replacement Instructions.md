# Unified Number Replacement Instructions

SAMA circular and accompanying instructions on replacing commercial registration and licence numbers with the National Information Center unified number beginning with 7 as the digital identifier for non-governmental entities. It extends the migration deadline, consolidates and supersedes all earlier replacement circulars, and sets requirements to add and link a unified-number field alongside the retained CR field in systems, to inventory affected customers, business partners and members, and to manage migration impacts on high-risk areas including credit, holds/blocking, dormancy, local and UN lists, KYC and beneficial ownership verification, with periodic reporting to SAMA. Binds all SAMA-supervised financial institutions and credit information companies.

**Regimes:** data/credit info, AML/CTF, governance/risk, sanctions/TFS

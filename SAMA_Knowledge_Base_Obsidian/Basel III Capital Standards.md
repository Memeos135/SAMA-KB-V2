# Basel III Capital Standards

SAMA circular to all banks on applying the Basel III countercyclical capital buffer (CCyB) in Saudi Arabia, explaining its macroprudential purpose and the 0%–2.5% range of risk-weighted assets. It sets out the calculation methodology — reciprocity with buffer rates published by the Basel Committee, a default maximum rate where no rate is published, inclusion of private-sector and non-bank financial exposures (excluding interbank and public sector), ultimate-risk geographic allocation, and weighted averaging across jurisdictions — together with the effective date and SAMA's pre-announcement mechanics for increases and decreases. Binds all banks operating in the Kingdom.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

# Counter-Fraud Technology

Addresses the technology component of the risk-based Counter-Fraud Programme — systems and tools used to prevent, detect and respond to fraud, supported by KPIs, Key Risk Indicators, enterprise-wide Fraud Risk Assessment and independent audit of control design. It also covers assessment of fraud vulnerabilities before launching new products, services or digital platforms, and vetting of third parties or managed services used for counter-fraud functions. Binds Member Organizations; proportionality to size and nature of business applies.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, payments

# External Credit Ratings Usage

## Why this community

Standardised-approach exposure classification and the permitted use of external credit ratings: which ECAI ratings may be relied on, and how corporate, retail, specialised lending and real estate exposures are bucketed for risk weighting.

## How members connect

- Ratings-usage rules (eligibility, mapping, multiple ratings, issue vs issuer, short vs long term) are the shared gateway conditions for every rated exposure class in the cluster.
- Independent due diligence is an express obligation that overlays external ratings - a bank may not mechanically adopt an ECAI grade.
- Exposure-class definitions nest: specialised lending subdivides into project, object, commodities, IPRE and HVCRE; real estate subdivides into regulatory residential/commercial, other and land ADC.
- Modifiers apply across classes: loan-to-value, whole-loan vs loan-splitting, material dependence on cash flows, transactor status and the currency-mismatch multiplier all change the resulting weight.
- Supervisory slotting and expected-loss treatment link this standardised classification back to IRB-style outcomes for specialised lending.

# Portfolio Reconciliation

Sets the portfolio reconciliation obligation within SAMA's risk mitigation requirements for non-centrally cleared OTC derivatives. Banks must document the reconciliation process agreed with financial counterparties and their efforts with non-financial counterparties, and must set scope and frequency by reference to the counterparty's risk exposure profile, size, volatility and number of outstanding transactions, ensuring material terms and valuations (including variation margin) are exchanged and reconciled at regular intervals. It binds SAMA-supervised banks entering into bilateral OTC derivative transactions.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential, other

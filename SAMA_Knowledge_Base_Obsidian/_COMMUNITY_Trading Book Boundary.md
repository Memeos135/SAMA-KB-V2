# Trading Book Boundary

## Why this community

Scope boundary rules that determine whether an instrument or position sits in the trading book or the banking book, and therefore which capital regime (market risk vs credit/banking book treatment) applies. Also governs how risk may be moved between the two books.

## How members connect

- Shared defined terms: 'Trading Book', 'Banking Book' and 'Trading Desk' are the definitional anchors on which the boundary rule operates; assignment must be to a designated trading desk.
- Obligation chain: designation/classification at inception, restrictions on reclassification, and supervisory notification/approval flow from the boundary provision to the two book definitions.
- Internal risk transfer is the controlled exception to the boundary — it cross-references both books and sets conditions (e.g. recognition and hedging eligibility) for transferring risk between them.
- Hierarchy: the boundary provision is the operative rule; the definitions are the interpretive layer it references.

# Exposure Measurement

The section of the HLI sound practices dealing with measuring and limiting counterparty exposure: banks taking OTC derivatives positions against HLIs should build meaningful measures of potential future exposure (PFE), apply timely and plausible stress tests reflecting market, credit and liquidity risk interaction and collateral value effects, and feed these into decision-making. It also covers limit setting, requiring aggregate counterparty-level limits across on- and off-balance-sheet exposures, monitoring of mark-to-market exposure against PFE limits, and procedures to reduce exposure as limits are reached. Limits should be binding and not driven by customer demand.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

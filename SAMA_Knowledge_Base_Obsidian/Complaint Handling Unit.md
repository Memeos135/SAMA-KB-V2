# Complaint Handling Unit

The Complaint Handling Unit is one of the three mandatory units of a bank's Customer Care Department under SAMA's 2023 controls, responsible for receiving and resolving customer complaints efficiently and within regulatory time limits. It must offer multiple accessible complaint channels — at minimum a toll-free number, website, mobile applications, email and branches — prominently published on the bank's homepage and platforms, and operate under service-level agreements and an escalation mechanism reaching the most senior executive. Binds SAMA-licensed banks; the department head (or delegate) holds decision authority for complaint settlements up to a defined riyal threshold.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, governance/risk

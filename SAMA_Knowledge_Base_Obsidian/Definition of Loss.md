# Definition of Loss

Concept node on the definition and measurement of operational risk loss: what counts as gross loss, net loss and recovery, the loss data set standards under the standardised approach, and the loss event thresholds (SAR 44,600 / SAR 446,000) used in the internal loss multiplier and related Pillar 3 disclosure rows. It specifies inclusions in gross loss (direct P&L charges, consequential external costs, provisions, pending losses) and that recoveries reduce losses only once payment is received. Binds banks calculating operational risk capital and disclosing operational loss data to SAMA.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

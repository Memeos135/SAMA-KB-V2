# Remediation Plan (RP)

The plan the White Team must draft after the exercise, built on the Red Team Evaluation Report and the Blue Team Report, identifying agreed areas of improvement, priorities and a roadmap for strengthening prevention, detection, response and recovery. Progress must be monitored and reported periodically to the Member Organization's Cyber Security Committee and to the Green Team. It binds the Member Organization as the remediating party.

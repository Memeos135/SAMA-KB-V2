# SARIE

SARIE is a defined term in the Guidelines on Repurchase Agreements, meaning the Saudi Arabian Riyal Interbank Express (or any future instant payments system), and it is the mandated cash settlement channel for repo transactions. Where both counterparties are licensed agents and the transaction is in SAR, cash legs must settle through SARIE (with securities settling via Tadawul, or via SAMA for SAMA-issued securities); otherwise any mutually agreed system may be used. The requirement binds repo market participants transacting in the Saudi repo market.

**Regimes:** payments, banking prudential, other

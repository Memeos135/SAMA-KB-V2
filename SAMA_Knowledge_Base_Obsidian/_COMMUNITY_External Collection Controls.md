# External Collection Controls

## Why this community

Consumer-protection controls on the use of external (outsourced) debt collection service providers when pursuing individual customers, including permitted contact practices and the creditor's retained responsibility.

## How members connect

- Standalone conduct instrument that constrains outsourcing: the finance provider or bank remains accountable for the third party's conduct toward the Consumer.
- Cross-regime interaction with consumer protection principles (fair treatment, privacy of debt information, complaint handling) and with outsourcing/governance expectations on third-party oversight.
- Relevant to BNPL and finance-company portfolios where the counterparty is a 'Consumer' with protective rights distinct from the AML notion of 'Customer'.

# SA-CCR for OTC Derivatives

Concept node on the standardised approach for counterparty credit risk (SA-CCR) for derivatives and its place in SAMA's Pillar 3 counterparty credit risk disclosures. It covers the scope of CCR charges across banking and trading book (including exposures to CCPs), the CCRA/CCR1–CCR8 disclosure set, and the SA-CCR components reported in CCR1 (replacement cost, potential future exposure, alpha of 1.4, EAD post-CRM, RWA). Binds all SAMA-supervised banks; disclosure is mandatory and semiannual.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

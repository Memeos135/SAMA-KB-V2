# Fraud Detection

A control domain within SAMA's Counter-Fraud Framework covering the detection pillar: identifying suspected fraud through monitoring, alerting, intelligence on emerging threats and analysis of internal and external data sources. It sits alongside prevention and response controls and relies on defined terms such as Fraud, Fraud case, External Fraud and Fraud Landscape. It binds Member Organizations supervised by SAMA, with Board and Counter-Fraud Governance Committee accountability for the wider programme.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, AML/CTF, payments

# Template IRRBB1: Quantitative Information on IRRBB

Node for the Pillar 3 quantitative disclosure template on interest rate risk in the banking book (IRRBB1), sitting alongside the qualitative Table IRRBBA within SAMA's Pillar 3 Disclosure Requirements Framework. It requires banks to disclose measured changes in economic value of equity (ΔEVE) and net interest income (ΔNII) under the prescribed Basel supervisory interest rate shock scenarios, computed using the bank's own internal measurement system (IMS) as referenced in SAMA's IRRBB circular. Binds banks within the Basel IRRBB scope of application; disclosure frequency is annual, with supporting qualitative explanation of modelling assumptions.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

# Regulatory Sandbox FAQ

A bilingual FAQ explaining the purpose, benefits and application logic of SAMA's Regulatory Sandbox, which permits testing of new business models, products and services in a live environment under pre-defined limits (client numbers, transaction volume and value). It clarifies that the Sandbox is reserved for propositions with no existing regulatory framework or licensing path, and that exemptions are assessed case-by-case under the published Regulatory Sandbox Framework. It is guidance for prospective applicants (fintechs and other innovators), not a binding rule instrument.

**Regimes:** payments, governance/risk, other

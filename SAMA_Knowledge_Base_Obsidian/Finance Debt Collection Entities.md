# Finance Debt Collection Entities

SAMA circular on the outsourcing of debt collection by banks and finance companies to finance-entity debt collection firms, issued under the SAMA Law, the Finance Companies Control Law and the Banking Control Law. It cancels the earlier circulars that had restricted such outsourcing to debt collection entities licensed by SAMA and that had set transition periods for entities holding initial approval; going forward, outsourcing may be to firms meeting the requirements set in the applicable laws, regulations and instructions, after obtaining SAMA's no-objection letter to contract with a debt collection provider, without a requirement that the provider be SAMA-licensed. Compliance with the outsourcing rules and other related instructions remains mandatory. It binds banks, finance companies and licensed finance-entity debt collection firms operating in the Kingdom.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, governance/risk, consumer protection

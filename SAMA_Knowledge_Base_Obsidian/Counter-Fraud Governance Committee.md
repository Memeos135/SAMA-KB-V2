# Counter-Fraud Governance Committee

Node covering the governance body/committee layer of SAMA's Counter-Fraud Framework, sitting above the Counter-Fraud Department and responsible for oversight, approval and escalation of the Counter-Fraud Programme, Fraud Risk Appetite and periodic reporting to senior management and the Board. It operates alongside the maturity-model expectations that policies, standards and procedures be formally approved and monitored. It binds Member Organisations supervised by SAMA and, in practice, their boards and senior management.

**Regimes:** governance/risk

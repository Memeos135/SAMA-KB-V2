# Exchange Companies Procedures Update

A SAMA circular updating operating procedures for money exchange establishments and companies, issued under the Rules Governing Money Exchange Business. It requires minimum CCTV footage retention (one year), provision of account statements to verified members on request, retention of all customer transaction records for at least ten years with retrievable electronic archiving, recording of residential or national address at relationship establishment, a prohibition on deleting any transaction or membership record, and business continuity/disaster recovery plans tested periodically. It binds all licensed exchange establishments and companies, with compliance required within four months and enforcement action for non-compliance.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, payments, governance/risk

# Forbearance

A concept node in SAMA's credit classification and provisioning rules distinguishing forbearance from renegotiation/refinancing/rescheduling. Forbearance arises where the borrower is in financial difficulty and the finance company grants a concession it would not otherwise consider; such exposures are identified at individual exposure level and may only be reported in Stage 2B, 3A or 3B, with defined situations (partial write-off, collateral repossession pending disposal, further forbearance) that do not permit re-categorisation as performing. Renegotiation applies only where there is no financial deterioration and no reduction in present value. Binds finance companies in their classification, ECL measurement and regulatory reporting.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, banking prudential, governance/risk

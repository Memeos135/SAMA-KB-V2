# Simple, Transparent and Comparable (STC) Framework

This node describes the Simple, Transparent and Comparable (STC) framework as adopted in SAMA's credit risk capital rules, which limits alternative (preferential) securitisation capital treatment to traditional securitisations, ABCP conduits and conduit-financed transactions that meet defined criteria. It allocates disclosure duties to the originator/sponsor and an independent compliance assessment duty to the investor, with criteria required to be met on an ongoing basis. It binds banks holding or originating securitisation exposures that wish to apply the alternative capital treatment.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

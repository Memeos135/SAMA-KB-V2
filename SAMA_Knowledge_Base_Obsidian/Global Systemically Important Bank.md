# Global Systemically Important Bank

Defines Globally Systemically Important Banks under the Basel/FSB methodology (cross-jurisdictional activity, interconnectedness, size, substitutability, complexity), adopted by SAMA circular, with the G-SIB list reviewed annually by the FSB. Its operative consequence in the LEX Rules is that where the lending bank and/or counterparty bank is a G-SIB (or D-SIB), the bank-to-bank exposure limit tightens from 25% to 15% of the lending bank's eligible capital base. Binds banks licensed and operating in the Kingdom when calculating interbank large exposure limits.

**Regimes:** banking prudential

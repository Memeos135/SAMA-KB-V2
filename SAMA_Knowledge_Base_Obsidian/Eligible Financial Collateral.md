# Eligible Financial Collateral

Defines which collateral qualifies for recognition in credit risk mitigation under SAMA's credit risk capital standard, distinguishing eligible financial collateral (as under the standardized approach) from additional eligible IRB collateral such as receivables, commercial and residential real estate and other physical collateral. It also sets the foundation IRB LGD floors for unsecured and subordinated claims and the legal-certainty, enforceability and documentation conditions for recognizing financial receivables. It binds SAMA-supervised banks calculating regulatory capital for credit risk.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

# Delta-Plus Method

This node explains the delta-plus method, one of the three permitted treatments of options under the simplified standardised approach for market risk: delta-weighted option positions are folded into the general market risk calculation, with separate add-ons for gamma and vega risk and specific risk determined via delta-equivalents. It sits alongside the simplified (carve-out) approach and the scenario approach and references the use of an approved exchange model or the bank's own option pricing model under SAMA oversight. It applies to SAMA-licensed banks that write options and calculate market risk capital under the simplified standardised approach.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

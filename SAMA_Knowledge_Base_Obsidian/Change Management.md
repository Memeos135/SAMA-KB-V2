# Change Management

Sets out the SAMA Cyber Security Framework's change management control: a defined, approved and implemented change process with monitored compliance and periodically evaluated control effectiveness. It requires impact assessment and classification of changes, security testing (including penetration testing and code review of internally and externally developed applications), business-owner and cyber security function approval before the Change Advisory Board, CAB approval, post-implementation review, segregation of development/test/production environments and duties, and emergency change and roll-back procedures. It binds Member Organizations and feeds the Framework's cyber risk management cycle.

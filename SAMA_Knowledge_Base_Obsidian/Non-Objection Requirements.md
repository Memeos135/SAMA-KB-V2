# Non-Objection Requirements

Node covering when a bank must obtain SAMA's prior non-objection rather than merely notify before launching a product or service. Non-objection is required for home loan products, financial lease products, financial derivatives, and anything not covered by existing SAMA rules; banks failing the required framework maturity level must seek non-objection for all products. Launch must occur within 12 months of the non-objection, and re-offering a product discontinued for more than 12 months requires a fresh non-objection.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential

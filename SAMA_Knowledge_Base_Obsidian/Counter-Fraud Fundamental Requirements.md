# Counter-Fraud Fundamental Requirements

The Counter-Fraud Fundamental Requirements framework itself, structured as numbered control requirements addressed to Member Organizations, covering counter-fraud governance and risk management, fraud prevention, and related domains, together with a defined-terms section (e.g. Fraud, External Fraud, Fraud Risk Appetite, Fraud Risk Assessment, Electronic Money, EMI, Employee). It sets expectations for a proportionate, risk-based counter-fraud programme spanning people, process and technology, with board accountability and independent assurance. Applies to SAMA-supervised Member Organizations within its stated scope, including finance companies and payment service providers.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, payments, BNPL/finance, AML/CTF

# Verification of Economic Total Loss for Leased Vehicles

SAMA circular directing finance entities carrying out vehicle finance leasing to verify economic total loss determinations for individually leased vehicles by relying on reports from valuation firms licensed by the Saudi Authority for Accredited Valuers, in applying the Finance Lease Law provision on destruction of the leased asset. It also requires the lessor, on notice of damage, to exercise due care with all parties so the lessee can continue benefiting from the vehicle before it is treated as an economic total loss. It binds banks and finance companies operating in the Kingdom.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, consumer protection

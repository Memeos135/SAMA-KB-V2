# Caps for Securitization Exposures

Node on the caps that limit maximum capital requirements for securitisation exposures, applied on top of the SEC-IRBA/SEC-ERBA/SEC-IAA/SEC-SA calculations, including caps available to originators and sponsors and the maximum risk weight for senior positions. It operates within the definitional framework for securitisation, resecuritisation, originator and sponsor set out in the securitisation chapter. Binds SAMA-supervised banks holding securitisation exposures in the banking book.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

# Annex 2 Confirmation Form

Annex II is the standard form of Confirmation under the SAMA-approved Master Agreement for the Sale and Purchase of Securities, evidencing each individual repo transaction and incorporating the unilateral undertakings of Seller and Buyer to purchase/sell the Second Purchased Securities at the Second Purchase Price upon satisfaction of the relevant Exercise Condition and delivery of an Exercise Notice. It specifies required transaction terms (Second Purchase Date, Pricing Rate, settlement accounts, additional terms), and the Confirmation together with the Agreement is prima facie evidence of agreed terms, prevailing over the Agreement in case of conflict for that transaction only. Applies to both counterparties documenting repo transactions in Saudi Arabia.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk, other

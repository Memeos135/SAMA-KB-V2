# Early Warning Signals

A concept node from SAMA's guidance on management of problem loans setting out early warning signals (EWS) as a mandatory framework element for identifying borrowers likely to face payment difficulties before default. It covers the three-stage EWS process (identification, action, monitoring), allocation of responsibility to an independent risk/middle or back office function, escalation to the Workout Unit, staff training, incentives and supporting data infrastructure. It binds SAMA-regulated banks in respect of corporate and MSME lending.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential

# Table CRA - General Qualitative Info about Credit Risk

Table CRA is the general qualitative credit risk disclosure in SAMA's Pillar 3 framework, requiring banks to describe their credit risk management objectives and policies — how the business model shapes the credit risk profile, criteria for credit risk policy and limits, the structure of the credit risk management and control function, its interaction with risk control, compliance and internal audit, and credit risk reporting to executive management and the board. It sits at the head of the section 19 credit risk disclosure suite (CR1, CR2, CRB, CRB-A, CRC, CR3, CRD, CR4, CR5). Mandatory for all banks, annual, flexible format.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

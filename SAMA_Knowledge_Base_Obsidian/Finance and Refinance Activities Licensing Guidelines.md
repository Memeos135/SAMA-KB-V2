# Finance and Refinance Activities Licensing Guidelines

SAMA guidelines explaining how to apply for a licence to practise finance or refinance activities under the Finance Companies Control Law and its Implementing Regulations. They set out application instructions (no modification of the form, submission by founders or an authorised representative, soft-copy filing with originals on request), the documents required (draft articles of association, organisational structure, fit and proper forms for founders, 5%+ owners of corporate founders and board/committee candidates, feasibility study and business plan, an irrevocable bank guarantee equal to 20% of minimum capital, and draft third-party/related-party contracts), and the consequences of false or omitted material information. They bind applicants — founders or shareholders seeking incorporation approval and a licence.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, governance/risk

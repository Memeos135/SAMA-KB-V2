# Regulatory Data Forms

## Why this community

Periodic supervisory reporting: the prescribed return forms and the manual governing how supervised entities submit regulatory data to SAMA through the Returns Management System.

## How members connect

- The RMS guidance manual is the procedural hub; each form is a defined return whose content, frequency and submission channel it governs.
- Members share a common data spine (entity identifiers, balance-sheet and customer/currency data), so definitions and reporting periods must be read consistently across forms.
- Hierarchy runs from the supervisory reporting obligation down to the manual (process) and the individual forms (content), with annual, quarterly and comparative cadences distinguishing scope.
- Accuracy and timeliness of these returns is an enforceable supervisory obligation, not a voluntary data exchange.

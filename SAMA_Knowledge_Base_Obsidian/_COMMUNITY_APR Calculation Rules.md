# APR Calculation Rules

## Why this community

Cost-of-credit transparency in consumer finance: how the Annual Percentage Rate must be computed, what fees and charges enter the calculation, and how the resulting figure is disclosed to the consumer.

## How members connect

- Hierarchy runs from the Regulations for Consumer Financing (substantive disclosure and pricing obligations) down to the dedicated APR calculation rules, with the circular acting as SAMA's interpretive gloss on the fees-and-charges article.
- Shared defined term 'APR' anchors the cluster: the annex description and the calculator operationalise the same definition the regulations impose, so a divergence in inclusion of a fee changes the disclosed rate.
- Obligation chain: identify chargeable items → include/exclude them per the fees clarification → compute per the APR rules → disclose in the contractual form referenced by the annex.
- The calculator is a supervisory reference point rather than an independent source of obligation; its outputs are meaningful only against the rules and annex it implements.

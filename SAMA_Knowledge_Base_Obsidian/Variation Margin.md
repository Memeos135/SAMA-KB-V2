# Variation Margin

Concept node for the margin calculation and model-governance side of SAMA's non-centrally cleared derivatives framework, sitting alongside the variation margin exchange obligation. The supplied material addresses calibration of initial margin models to a 99% confidence interval over a 10-day horizon using a stressed historical period not exceeding five years, the default expectation that banks use the standardised schedule, and the internal governance, validation, testing and SAMA approval required before any model may be used. It binds covered entities engaging in non-centrally cleared derivatives, and constrains their model risk governance.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

# Counter-Fraud Fundamental Requirements Guide

SAMA circular adopting the Counter-Fraud Fundamental Requirements guide, issued under the SAMA Law and its supervisory powers to raise financial-sector maturity against evolving fraud typologies. It imposes procedural obligations on addressed institutions: a gap assessment against the guide, a board-approved remediation roadmap, submission of both to SAMA by a stated deadline, quarterly compliance reporting until full compliance, and full compliance by a fixed end date. Addressed to finance companies and payment service providers.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, payments, BNPL/finance

# Anti-Money Laundering Law

Node covering the Anti-Money Laundering Law regime as applied by SAMA, together with associated SAMA instruments — notably the guide on assessing business risks related to money laundering, terrorist financing and proliferation financing. The guide sets the minimum factors, criteria and controls a financial institution must consider when performing its enterprise-wide business risk assessment (inherent risk, customer/product/delivery-channel/geographic and emerging risks, mitigating controls, residual risk and risk appetite), without imposing a single mandatory methodology and without limiting obligations under the AML Law, the Law of Combating Terrorism Crimes and its Financing, and related regulations. It binds all financial institutions licensed, regulated and supervised by SAMA.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, sanctions/TFS, governance/risk

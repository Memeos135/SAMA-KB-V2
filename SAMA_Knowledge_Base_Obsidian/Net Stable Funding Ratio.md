# Net Stable Funding Ratio

Node within SAMA's liquidity framework circular adopting the Basel III LCR text; despite the NSFR label, the captured material addresses unsecured wholesale funding run-off treatment, in particular funding from small business customers (treated like retail deposits where aggregated funding from one customer, on a consolidated/affiliated-group basis, is below the €1 million threshold) and the graduated run-off factors for non-financial corporates, sovereigns, central banks, MDBs, PSEs (20%/40%) and other legal entity customers (100%). It binds SAMA-licensed banks calculating liquidity ratios and defines counterparty categorisation for the outflow denominator.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

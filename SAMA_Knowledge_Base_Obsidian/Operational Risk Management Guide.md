# Operational Risk Management Guide

A SAMA guide for bank directors and senior management explaining the nature of operational risk (treated as pure risk: loss or no loss) and the techniques for identifying, controlling and financing it, including internal controls, retention and insurance transfer. It contains a defined-terms glossary (Operational Risk Manager, Operational Risk Management Committee and Plan, risk financing policy, retention guidelines, risk assessment and control matrices) and requires a board-approved plan reviewed at least annually, plus periodic independent review of the risk management function. Addressed to banks operating in the Kingdom and intended to be read with SAMA's internal control, disaster recovery and physical security guidelines.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential

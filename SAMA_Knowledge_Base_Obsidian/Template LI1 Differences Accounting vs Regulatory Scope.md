# Template LI1: Differences Accounting vs Regulatory Scope

Node covering Template LI1 in SAMA's Pillar 3 framework, which maps the carrying values in a bank's published financial statements to the carrying values under the regulatory scope of consolidation and allocates them across the credit risk, counterparty credit risk, securitization and market risk frameworks (or items not subject to capital requirements). It is the starting point for the linkage exercise continued in LI2 and explained qualitatively in Table LIA. It binds all banks subject to the Pillar 3 disclosure requirements.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

# Expected Credit Loss Provisioning

The expected credit loss elements of SAMA's Rules Governing Credit Risk Exposure Classification and Provisioning for finance companies: definitions aligned to IFRS 9 (probability of default, exposure at default, loss given default, financial asset, 12-month and lifetime ECL, Stage 1/Stage 2 exposures) and governance duties such as documented and board-approved ECL methodologies reviewed at least annually, quarterly board reporting on provision adequacy, annual independent internal audit review, and a default-asset management policy covering recovery strategies, collateral revaluation and timely write-offs. Effective 1 July 2021, replacing the earlier Provisioning Guidelines circular.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, governance/risk, banking prudential

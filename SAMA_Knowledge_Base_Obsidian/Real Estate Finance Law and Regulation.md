# Real Estate Finance Law and Regulation

SAMA circular under the Real Estate Finance Law and its Implementing Regulation adjusting the maximum loan-to-value cap in Article 12 of the Implementing Regulation, raising the ceiling for citizens acquiring a first home to 90%, while banks remain at 70% and real estate finance companies at 85% for a second or subsequent home for all beneficiaries. It also reiterates the requirement to review risk management policy, monitor and measure credit concentrations, and verify the beneficiary's repayment capacity — taking existing financial and credit obligations into account — before granting any financing. Binds SAMA-licensed banks and real estate finance companies, effective from its date.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, banking prudential, consumer protection

# Judicial Orders Execution

## Why this community

How payment service providers must give effect to judicial orders and enforcement decisions affecting customer funds and accounts, including freezing, seizure or transfer directions.

## How members connect

- Single-member cluster where the obligation originates outside SAMA rules (courts/enforcement authority) but is executed by SAMA-supervised entities.
- Cross-regime overlap with sanctions/TFS freezing duties and with consumer protection limits on account access and notification.
- Constrains contractual terms with customers, since compliance with a lawful order overrides ordinary service obligations.

# Rules Regulating Money Exchange Business

Concept node for the Rules Regulating Money Exchange Business (issued by Minister of Finance decision), the licensing and supervisory regime for exchange centres in the Kingdom; Article 14(b) empowers SAMA to issue any instructions needed to apply supervisory requirements to exchange business, and Article 31 obliges centres to provide SAMA with any data or reports it requests within the time it sets. The linked circular exercises those powers by updating the monthly, quarterly and annual supervisory return templates and requiring electronic submission through SAMA's Returns Management System within specified deadlines (sales/purchases, currencies, largest customers, counterfeit currency, staff data, financial-statement comparison, annual regulatory data). Binds exchange establishments and companies operating in Saudi Arabia, with statutory action for non-compliance.

**Regimes:** other, governance/risk, AML/CTF

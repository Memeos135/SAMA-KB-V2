# PD Estimation

Covers the probability of default estimation requirements under the IRB approach: PD must be estimated for each internal borrower grade (corporate, sovereign, bank) or each retail pool, and must be a long-run average of one-year default rates, subject to specific retail carve-outs. Also relevant to specialised lending and HVCRE sub-classes, where meeting PD estimation requirements determines eligibility for foundation versus advanced treatment. Binds SAMA-licensed banks applying IRB approaches for regulatory capital.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

# Table CVAB: Qualitative Disclosures SA-CVA

Table CVAB sets the qualitative disclosure requirements for banks that have received SAMA approval to use the standardised approach for CVA (SA-CVA), covering how the approach is applied within the CVA portfolio. It complements the quantitative SA-CVA templates (CVA3 and CVA4) and applies only where the CCR/CVA materiality threshold is met. It binds SAMA-supervised banks approved for SA-CVA; banks without approval must use BA-CVA.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

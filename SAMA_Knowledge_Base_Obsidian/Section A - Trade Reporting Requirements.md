# Section A - Trade Reporting Requirements

Section A sets the trade reporting regime for OTC derivative contracts: which transactions are reportable, direct reporting to the SAMA authorised Trade Repository Operator, the required reporting service agreement and consent clause, electronic submission and template field completion, and reporting triggers at first execution and on subsequent life-cycle events such as modification, novation and termination. Applies to all banks in KSA with OTC derivative transactions.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential, other

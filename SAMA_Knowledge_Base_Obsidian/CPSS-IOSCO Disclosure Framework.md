# CPSS-IOSCO Disclosure Framework

Defined-term node for the CPSS-IOSCO (CPMI-IOSCO) Disclosure Framework for Financial Market Infrastructures, the document setting standard public disclosure requirements for FMIs including payment systems. Under SAMA's oversight framework, a systemically important payment system operator must publicly disclose its PFMI self-assessment summary in line with this framework after obtaining SAMA's no-objection letter, and the framework is listed among the requirements binding payment systems and other FMIs.

**Regimes:** payments, governance/risk

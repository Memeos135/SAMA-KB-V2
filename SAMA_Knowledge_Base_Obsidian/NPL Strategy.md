# NPL Strategy

Covers the NPL strategy and EWS-monitoring requirements under SAMA's problem loan framework: banks must adopt a Board-approved NPL strategy setting quantitative short, medium and long-term targets, resource allocation and controls, and must consider the full range of resolution options (hold/restructure, portfolio sales, write-offs, collateral repossession, legal and out-of-court routes) applying proportionality and materiality. It also requires monitoring of triggered EWS with corrective action plans, and periodic review of the strategy and its operational plan within the bank's risk management framework. Binds licensed banks.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

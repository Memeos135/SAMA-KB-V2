# Bankruptcy Law

Marks where the Bankruptcy Law interacts with the account rules: opening dedicated liquidation / financial restructuring accounts (court order, appointment of the bankruptcy officeholder or committee, prescribed account naming and operation), and the mandatory freezing of accounts on death, loss of legal competence, judicial or general-assembly liquidation, or the commencement of liquidation or administrative liquidation procedures. It also touches SAMA-directed compulsory deductions from salary accounts as priority claims. Binds banks holding the relevant customer accounts.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk, other

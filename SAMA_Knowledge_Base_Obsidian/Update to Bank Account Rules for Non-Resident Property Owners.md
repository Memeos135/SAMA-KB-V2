# Update to Bank Account Rules for Non-Resident Property Owners

A SAMA circular (Ref. 482004268, 16/01/1448H) amending the Bank Account Rules to add account-opening requirements for a new customer category: non-resident non-Saudi natural persons and non-resident legal persons covered by the Non-Saudi Real Estate Ownership Law (Royal Decree M/186) and its Implementing Regulation. It sets the documentary requirements (Saudi-issued digital ID, passport, foreign bank statement, broker/developer agreement, commercial registration for legal persons) and general controls, including verification through a reliable independent Saudi source with biometric/attribute checks, a ban on joint accounts and non-resident authorised signatories, limits on remote accounts and no payment or credit card issuance, restriction of property-related transactions to approved Saudi electronic channels, and ongoing ID validity and data-update duties. It binds all banks operating in the Kingdom, effective from its date.

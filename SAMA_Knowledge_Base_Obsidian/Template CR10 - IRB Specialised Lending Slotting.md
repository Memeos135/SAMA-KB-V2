# Template CR10 - IRB Specialised Lending Slotting

Pillar 3 template CR10 covers IRB disclosures for specialised lending (and equity) exposures treated under the supervisory slotting approach, sitting alongside CR7 (effect of credit derivatives as CRM on IRB RWA) and CR9 (PD backtesting) in the same framework section. It applies to banks using IRB approaches under SAMA's Pillar 3 Disclosure Requirements Framework. Open it for the slotting category breakdown, remaining maturity bands and applicable risk weights.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

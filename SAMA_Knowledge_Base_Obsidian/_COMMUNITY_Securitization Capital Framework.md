# Securitization Capital Framework

## Why this community

Regulatory capital treatment of securitization and resecuritization exposures: when risk transfer is recognised, how exposures are ranked into calculation approaches, and the preferential STC regime. Also covers the external-rating infrastructure and conduit/SPE structures that the treatment depends on.

## How members connect

- Defined terms drive the whole cluster: traditional vs synthetic securitization, resecuritization, SPE, ABCP conduit and sponsor determine whether the securitization framework applies at all.
- Gateway obligations chain into calculation: operational requirements for risk transference and due diligence must be met before a bank may use the hierarchy of approaches (SEC-IRBA, SEC-ERBA, SEC-IAA, SEC-SA).
- Parameters are shared across approaches - KIRB, supervisory parameter p, attachment/detachment points, caps and dilution risk - so a change in one input flows through several treatments.
- Eligibility of ECAIs and inferred-rating rules condition the ratings-based approaches; STC criteria (term and short-term ABCP) act as a qualifying overlay for reduced charges.
- SAMA circulars (Basel III implementation; SRC residential mortgage portfolio purchases) localise the Basel text to KSA, including the KSA capital charge.

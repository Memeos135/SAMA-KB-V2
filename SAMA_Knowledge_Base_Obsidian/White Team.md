# White Team

Defined role within the F.E.E.R. Framework: the White Team is the small group inside the member organization (maximum five people, including a White Team Leader and SPOCs such as the CISO) that is aware of the red teaming test and controls its execution. It contracts and coordinates the Red Teaming Provider, defines scope with the Green and Red Teams, monitors the exercise, and decides whether to stop, postpone, adjust or continue when the Blue Team detects Red Team activity or where business disruption risk arises. It also organises debriefing, replay exercises and remediation follow-up.

**Regimes:** governance/risk

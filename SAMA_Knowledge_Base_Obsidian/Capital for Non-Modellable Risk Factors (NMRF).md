# Capital for Non-Modellable Risk Factors (NMRF)

Node covering the treatment of non-modellable risk factors (NMRF) under the internal models approach for market risk: where a risk factor fails the risk factor eligibility test (RFET) or the data quality/modellability principles are not met to SAMA's satisfaction, it must be excluded from the expected shortfall model and capitalised separately as an NMRF (stressed expected shortfall). It also records SAMA's narrow discretion, in extraordinary systemic stress, to continue treating widely non-modellable factors as modellable without reducing capital. Binds banks with IMA-approved trading desks, and frames SAMA's supervisory approval/rejection powers over model inputs.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

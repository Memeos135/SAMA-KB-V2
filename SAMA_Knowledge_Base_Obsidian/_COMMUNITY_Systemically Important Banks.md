# Systemically Important Banks

## Why this community

Identification of domestic systemically important banks in Saudi Arabia and the additional capital burden imposed on them, adapting the Basel Committee's systemic-importance framework to the local market.

## How members connect

- SAMA's D-SIB framework is the operative instrument; it cites the BCBS G-SIB methodology as the source standard it localises.
- The assessment methodology determines designation and bucketing, which is the legal trigger for the Higher Loss Absorbency surcharge.
- HLA sits on top of minimum capital and buffer requirements, so designation has consequential effects across the wider capital adequacy regime.
- Designation is periodic and status-based — a researcher must check both the methodology's indicators and the timing/phase-in provisions.

# Executive Management

Defined-term node for "Executive Management" (also rendered as senior management) as used across SAMA instruments for finance companies and real estate refinance companies: the persons entrusted with running the company's day-to-day business and with proposing and implementing strategic decisions. The term fixes who bears management-level accountability distinct from the Board and the Audit Committee, notably in supporting and not impeding the internal audit function. It binds SAMA-licensed entities through the obligations that attach to this layer in the underlying instruments.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, BNPL/finance

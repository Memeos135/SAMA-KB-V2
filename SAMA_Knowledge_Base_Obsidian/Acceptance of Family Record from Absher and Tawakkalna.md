# Acceptance of Family Record from Absher and Tawakkalna

A circular extending SAMA's earlier acceptance of the Absher/Tawakkalna digital identity to the electronic Family Record document for citizens issued via Absher and displayed in Tawakkalna, readable by QR code. It obliges all financial institutions to accept this electronic Family Record in processes that do not require taking a copy of the document, with the same rules applicable to printed versions. It binds all financial institutions supervised by SAMA.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, other

# Prohibited Activities (Art 20)

Article 20 of SAMA's Rules for Agent Banking, setting out activities a banking agent may not perform, forming the negative counterpart to the permissible activities article. It constrains what banks may lawfully delegate under an agent banking contract and is relevant to breach analysis, contract drafting and supervisory inspection of agents. It binds licensed banks as principals and, through contract, their appointed agents.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk, consumer protection

# Template CR3: Credit Risk Mitigation Techniques Overview

Node covering Template CR3 in SAMA's Pillar 3 framework, the overview disclosure of the extent to which credit exposures are covered by credit risk mitigation techniques (collateral, guarantees and credit derivatives). It forms part of the credit risk disclosure set, which also includes qualitative Table CRC on CRM techniques and the credit quality templates CR1/CR2, and excludes securitization and counterparty credit risk positions disclosed elsewhere. It binds banks subject to the Pillar 3 disclosure requirements.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

# Profit Sharing Investment Account (PSIA)

Defines the Profit Sharing Investment Account: an account managed by a bank on Shari'ah compliant terms under a management agreement in which the IAH and bank share profits in a specified ratio and the IAH bears losses not caused by the bank's negligence, misconduct, fraud or breach of contract. It distinguishes unrestricted PSIA (bank invests at its discretion and may commingle funds) from restricted PSIA (investment limited by IAH-specified conditions), which must be accounted for separately. The definition drives disclosure duties to IAHs and prudential treatment for banks offering these accounts.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, consumer protection, governance/risk

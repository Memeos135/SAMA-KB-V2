# Compliance Function

Node covering the compliance function's duties under SAMA's New Banking Products and Services Regulation, alongside the adjacent risk management, internal audit and product development (business unit) roles. It requires pre-launch review of every new product or service from a compliance, regulatory and financial-crime angle, ongoing conformity with SAMA and other regulators' rules, at least annual assessment of non-compliance risk, and annual reporting of that risk and its mitigation to the Board. It binds licensed banks and, internally, their control functions and product owners.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential, consumer protection, AML/CTF

# Sharing Supervisory Data Consent

## Why this community

Confidentiality and controlled disclosure of supervisory information: supervised entities must obtain SAMA's prior no-objection before sharing data, correspondence or findings originating from the supervisory relationship with any third party.

## How members connect

- Single-instrument cluster; the obligation is a standalone prior-consent gate rather than part of a chained rulebook.
- Legally it sits in the governance/supervisory-relationship regime and conditions any outward disclosure (auditors, consultants, group entities, foreign regulators) that other regimes might otherwise assume is permitted.
- Interacts with outsourcing, group reporting and data-sharing arrangements: a permission to share data under another framework does not displace the SAMA no-objection requirement.

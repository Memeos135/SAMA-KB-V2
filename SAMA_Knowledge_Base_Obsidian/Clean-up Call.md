# Clean-up Call

Sets the conditions a clean-up call must satisfy in a securitization — it must not be structured to avoid loss allocation or to provide credit enhancement, and must only be exercisable when 10% or less of the original portfolio or reference portfolio remains. Where the criteria are not met, the originating bank must treat traditional securitization exposures as if unsecuritized and may not recognise gain on sale, while protection-buying banks in synthetic deals must hold capital as if unprotected; an exercised call functioning as credit enhancement counts as implicit support and is deducted from capital. It binds originating and protection-purchasing banks under SAMA's securitization capital framework.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

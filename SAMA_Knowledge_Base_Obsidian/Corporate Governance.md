# Corporate Governance

This is the corporate governance chapter (and related board/senior-management provisions) of SAMA's Rules Governing Real Estate Refinance Companies, issued under the Real Estate Finance Law and Finance Companies Control Law. It sets fit-and-proper and eligibility criteria for board and committee members, mandates board committees (audit, risk and credit, executive, nomination and remuneration) and a senior-management assets and liabilities committee, requires board-approved refinance policies that take effect only after a SAMA no-objection letter, and imposes personal liability on directors, the CEO/general manager, managing director and senior executives for breaches. It also requires adherence to SAMA's Principles of Corporate Governance for supervised financial institutions and the adoption of internal governance regulations submitted to SAMA. It binds licensed real estate refinance companies (joint stock companies) and their boards and senior management.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, BNPL/finance

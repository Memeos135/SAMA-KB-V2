# Cash Flow Analysis

Concept node treating cash flow analysis as the core test of financial viability in an NPL workout, linking income statement and balance sheet to show forecast cash generation and repayment capacity, with weak cash generation signalling reliance on collateral, liquidation or bankruptcy. It also covers the forward-looking monthly cash budget, encouraged for all borrowers and particularly useful where micro and small enterprise financial statements are unreliable, with monthly monitoring and immediate corrective action.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential

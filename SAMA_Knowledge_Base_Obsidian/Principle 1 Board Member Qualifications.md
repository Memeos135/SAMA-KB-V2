# Principle 1: Board Member Qualifications

Principle 1 of SAMA's Key Principles of Governance, addressing the qualifications required of board members of SAMA-supervised financial institutions. It works together with the framework's definitions of executive, non-executive and independent members, including the listed independence-disqualifying factors (ownership thresholds, kinship, recent employment, remuneration and credit-exposure limits, tenure of more than nine years). It binds the financial institution and its nominating/appointing bodies when selecting and assessing board and committee members.

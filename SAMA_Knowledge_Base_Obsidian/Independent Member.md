# Independent Member

A concept node in SAMA's corporate governance principles addressing independent board membership: annual board assessment of independence, notification duties when independence may be impaired, and minimum numbers of independent members (not fewer than two or one third of the Board, whichever is greater). It also covers SAMA's prior written no-objection for board/committee nominations and leadership appointments, five-working-day notification of departures or loss of independence, multiple-directorship restrictions, tenure guidance and conflict-of-interest disclosure. It binds boards, nominees and members of SAMA-supervised financial institutions.

# Principles for Financial Market Infrastructures (PFMIs)

Node on the Principles for Financial Market Infrastructures (PFMIs) issued by CPMI-IOSCO as adopted in SAMA's oversight framework for payment systems. It explains SAMA's principles-based oversight activities — ongoing monitoring, operator self-assessment against the PFMIs, and independent supervisory assessment — and the scope covering the national payment system, systems operated in or across the borders of the Kingdom, systemically important payment systems (SIPS) and non-classified systems. Under the Implementing Regulation, SIPS operators must self-assess at least annually or on material operational change or SAMA request, and must publicly disclose the self-assessment summary in line with the CPMI-IOSCO disclosure framework only after a SAMA no-objection letter; non-classified operators self-assess on the periodicity set in their licence or by SAMA. It binds payment system operators.

**Regimes:** payments, governance/risk

# Standardized Approach for CVA (SA-CVA)

Covers the standardized approach for CVA risk capital (SA-CVA), which banks may use only with prior SAMA approval; absent approval the BA-CVA applies. Useful for questions on the approval condition, scope of covered derivatives and fair-valued SFTs, and how SA-CVA output feeds RWA and Pillar 3 disclosure rows.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

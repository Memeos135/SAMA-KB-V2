# NPL Securitization

Defines non-performing loan (NPL) securitisation for capital purposes by reference to a minimum threshold for the pool's W variable at origination and on subsequent pool changes, and limits the eligible underlying assets to loans and loan-equivalent or sub-participation instruments (no resecuritisation). It also records SAMA's discretion to apply a stricter definition, restricts use of SEC-IRBA where the foundation approach is used to compute KIRB, and applies a risk-weight floor across SEC-IRBA, SEC-SA and the look-through treatment. Binds banks holding or originating exposures to NPL securitisations.

**Regimes:** banking prudential

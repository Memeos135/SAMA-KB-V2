# Authorized Humanitarian Donations

## Why this community

Channelling of humanitarian and charitable donations through a single state-authorized entity (KSrelief), as an AML/CTF and terrorist-financing control over the non-profit sector.

## How members connect

- Single-node cluster: the obligation stands alone as a directive-level control rather than part of a rule chain.
- Legally it sits at the intersection of AML/CTF duties on financial institutions and NPO-sector abuse risk, restricting permissible counterparties/purposes for cross-border and domestic donation flows.
- Consequence for a decision: collection or transfer of humanitarian donations outside the authorized channel should be treated as a prohibited activity and a red flag for reporting, not merely a policy preference.

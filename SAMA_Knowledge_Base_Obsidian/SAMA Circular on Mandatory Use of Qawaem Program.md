# SAMA Circular on Mandatory Use of Qawaem Program

SAMA circular (Arabic) issued under the Finance Companies Control Law and its Implementing Regulation making use of the Ministry of Commerce/SOCPA "Qawaem" program mandatory as the source of financial statements for commercial entities that are required to file there, before any credit facility is extended. It binds all finance companies licensed in the Kingdom, with credit information companies also on the distribution list, effective from the date stated in the circular. Relevant to credit-underwriting evidence standards rather than prudential capital.

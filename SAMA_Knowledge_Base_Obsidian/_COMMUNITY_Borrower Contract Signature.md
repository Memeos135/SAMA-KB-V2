# Borrower Contract Signature

## Why this community

Evidential integrity of consumer finance documentation: lenders must ensure the borrower signs the contract pages, so that informed consent covers the whole agreement and not only a signature block.

## How members connect

- Single-instrument cluster; a documentation-execution requirement layered on top of finance/consumer-credit contracting rules.
- Connects legally to disclosure and pre-contractual transparency duties: page-level signature is the evidence that terms were presented to the Consumer/Borrower.
- Relevant to dispute handling and enforceability, since defective execution undermines the creditor's evidentiary position.

# Credit Risk

Section of the Risk Management Framework for Shari'ah Compliant Banking setting Principle 2.0 on credit risk: banks must have methodologies, systems and resources to identify, measure, manage and report credit exposures arising from Shari'ah compliant financing. It requires defined criteria for approving, renewing and refinancing financing consistent with the approved risk appetite and SAMA's responsible lending requirements, plus processes for monitoring counterparty repayment ability, asset performance, exposure classification, documentation completeness, collateral and risk differentiation across contract stages. Binds banks conducting Shari'ah compliant banking in KSA.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential

### [[Rules on Stress Testing for Banks]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** If credit exposures are booked through Shari'ah compliant structures, they still fall inside the stress testing perimeter. The Stress Testing Rules require a holistic approach covering all material risks the bank is or could be exposed to, and the Shari'ah framework identifies credit risk as one of the risks generated by Shari'ah compliant banking activities — so the Shari'ah-specific features of those exposures are not a carve-out but an input to scenario design and coverage. The consequence is that a stress testing programme that omits or generically aggregates Shari'ah compliant credit exposures would be incomplete against the holistic-coverage requirement.

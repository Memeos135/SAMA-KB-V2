# Legal Entity Identifier (LEI)

Identifies the ISO 17442 Legal Entity Identifier as the required 20-character code for identifying parties in OTC derivative trade reports, including counterparties, the other counterparty's broker (as defined under the Capital Market Law), and clearing members of each counterparty. The LEI also forms the middle component of the internal unique trade ID generated by the reporting counterparty. Relevant to banks completing TR reporting templates.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential, other

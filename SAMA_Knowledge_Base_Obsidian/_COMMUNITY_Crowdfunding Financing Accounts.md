# Crowdfunding Financing Accounts

## Why this community

Segregation and handling of funds in debt-based crowdfunding: use of pooled accounts to hold and manage financing value on behalf of participants and beneficiaries.

## How members connect

- The pooled-account instruction defines and operationalises the 'Financing Value Management Account' as the required vehicle for holding participant funds.
- Core legal point is client-money segregation: platform funds must be kept distinct from participant financing value, with controls on permitted movements.
- Connects outward to the debt-crowdfunding rulebook and to AML/CTF expectations, since pooled structures affect identification of the underlying beneficial holder of funds.

# Trade Repository Reporting and Risk Mitigation Requirements for OTC Derivatives

SAMA requirements (version 4.0, September 2025) in two parts: Section A sets trade reporting obligations for OTC derivative transactions to the SAMA-authorised Trade Repository Operator, and Section B imposes risk mitigation requirements on banks entering non-centrally cleared OTC derivatives regardless of notional size or hedging purpose. It defines reportable transactions (interest rate, FX, equity, credit and commodity derivatives), counterparty scope including branches, subsidiaries and SPVs, and detailed reporting fields. Binds all banks in KSA, including locally incorporated banks and SAMA-licensed foreign bank branches; effective 1 February 2026, superseding the earlier circular.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk, other

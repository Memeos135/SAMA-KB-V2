# Timeframes for Real Estate Debt Transfer Circular

A circular reaffirming, by reference to earlier SAMA instructions on time limits for issuing clearance letters and transferring accounts and indebtedness, that real estate financiers must execute customer requests to transfer murabaha-based real estate finance indebtedness within the prescribed timeframes and comply with SAMA instructions on purchase and transfer of real estate finance debt between financiers. Business days linked to mortgage release procedures are excluded from those timeframes where the delay is caused by an external party, and financiers must update internal policies accordingly. It binds banks and real estate finance companies operating in the Kingdom.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, consumer protection

# Exposure at Default (EAD)

Concept node for Exposure at Default, the exposure measure that, with PD and LGD, drives IRB risk-weighted assets and expected loss, and that underpins several Pillar 3 credit risk templates. Related context covers carrying amounts of secured and unsecured exposures under credit risk mitigation reporting and the prudential treatment of non-performing and forborne exposures. Relevant to banks calculating credit risk capital and preparing mandatory disclosures.

**Regimes:** banking prudential

# Template GSIB1: Disclosure of G-SIB Indicators

Node for Pillar 3 Template GSIB1, the annual disclosure of the indicators feeding the Basel Committee methodology for assessing global systemic importance. It covers at least the 12 assessment indicators and is mandatory only for banks classified as G-SIBs, with a leverage ratio exposure measure above EUR 200 billion, or included in the assessment sample by supervisory judgment; SAMA states it need not be completed unless SAMA identifies the bank as a G-SIB. Sits in the macroprudential supervisory measures chapter together with Template CCyB1.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

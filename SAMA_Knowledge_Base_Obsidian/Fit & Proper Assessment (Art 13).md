# Fit & Proper Assessment (Art 13)

Article 13 establishes the fit and proper assessment the Bank must perform on a prospective Agent, its proprietors and persons managing the Agent Banking business, testing competence, integrity, adequacy of management structures and funding sources. It is a gating condition referenced by the eligibility and application provisions and underpins the Bank's declaration to SAMA. Binds Banks; sits within the Agent Banking Regulation's contracting section.

# Negotiating Strategy

Guidance on how a bank should prepare and conduct negotiations with a distressed borrower over a rehabilitation/restructuring plan, including prior strategy formulation, SWOT-based assessment of the account, root-cause analysis, borrower education where the borrower lacks sophistication, and valuation issues in loan-to-equity conversions. It also situates negotiation within the wider NPL strategy implementation framework (monitoring of results, embedding roles, responsibilities and reporting lines). Applies to banks' workout units and management responsible for NPL resolution.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential

# Ownership Transfer Certificate

Annex/clause of SAMA's mandatory model vehicle finance lease (Ijarah) contract for individuals dealing with transfer of ownership of the leased asset, including the ownership transfer certificate and the related procedures (including early ownership acquisition and delays in completing transfer formalities). It sits within the standard form that lessors must apply unchanged. Binds banks and finance companies acting as lessors to individual lessees.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, consumer protection

# Table REMA - Remuneration Policy

Table REMA is the qualitative Pillar 3 disclosure table on a bank's remuneration policy and the key features of its remuneration system, covering the oversight body, external advisers, scope across business lines and foreign subsidiaries/branches, identification of senior management and material risk-takers, independence of risk and compliance staff pay, and how current and future risks are reflected in remuneration. It is mandatory for all banks, annual, with flexible format, and sits alongside quantitative templates REM1–REM3. Binding on banks subject to SAMA's Pillar 3 Disclosure Requirements Framework.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

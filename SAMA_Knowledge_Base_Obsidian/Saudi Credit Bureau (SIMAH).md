# Saudi Credit Bureau (SIMAH)

Node for the Saudi Credit Bureau (SIMAH) in its role as the SAMA-licensed credit information company acting as the accredited Local Operating Unit (LOU) for the Legal Entity Identifier in the Kingdom, and as first party to the standard agreement on issuing, updating and renewing an LEI through a financial institution. The agreement lets SAMA-supervised financial institutions register and renew LEIs on behalf of their existing or prospective corporate customers under the LEI regulatory framework (ROC charter, GLEIF master agreement) and SAMA instructions. It binds SIMAH and the signing financial institution.

**Regimes:** data/credit info, governance/risk

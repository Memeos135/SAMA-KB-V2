# Template LI2 - Differences in Regulatory Exposure Amounts

Pillar 3 disclosure template LI2 within SAMA's Pillar 3 Disclosure Requirements Framework, requiring banks to explain the main drivers of difference between accounting carrying values (under the regulatory scope of consolidation) and the exposure amounts used for regulatory capital purposes, broken down by credit risk, counterparty credit risk, securitisation and market risk frameworks. Differences arising purely from scope of consolidation belong in LI1; LI2 covers off-balance sheet amounts, valuation and netting differences, provisions and prudential filters. Mandatory for all banks, annual frequency, flexible format, with narrative support via Table LIA.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

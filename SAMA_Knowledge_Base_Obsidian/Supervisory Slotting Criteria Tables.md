# Supervisory Slotting Criteria Tables

Concept node for the supervisory slotting criteria tables that define, for each sub-class of specialised lending, the assessment factors and characteristics corresponding to each of the five supervisory categories. Banks must assign exposures to internal grades using their own rating systems and then demonstrate that the mapping to the supervisory categories is consistent with the preponderance of the stated characteristics, guarding against overrides that undermine the mapping. Binds banks applying the slotting approach and their internal rating governance.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

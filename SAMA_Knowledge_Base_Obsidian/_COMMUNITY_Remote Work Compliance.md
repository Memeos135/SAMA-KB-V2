# Remote Work Compliance

## Why this community

Regulatory treatment of remote/telework arrangements in financial institutions, covering both labour-market localisation conditions and the control and consumer-protection conditions for serving customers from outside branch premises.

## How members connect

- The two SAMA circulars expressly reference the Ministerial Decision on remote work localisation, making the labour instrument the upstream authority for Saudisation crediting of remote roles.
- The initiative circular and the remote customer service circular are conceptually paired: one enables the arrangement, the other sets conduct, confidentiality and supervision conditions when the remote role is customer-facing.
- Cross-regime interaction: remote handling of customer interactions engages banking secrecy, personal data protection and consumer protection duties, and does not relax CDD or record-keeping requirements.
- Hierarchy to observe: ministerial decision (labour law) then SAMA circulars applying it to supervised entities.

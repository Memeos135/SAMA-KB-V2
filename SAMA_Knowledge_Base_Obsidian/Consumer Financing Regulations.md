# Consumer Financing Regulations

The core scope and contracting provisions of SAMA's Consumer Financing Regulations, defining Consumer Financing (deferred debt or deferred repayment under a Financing Contract), the Amount of Financing basis (Gross Salary or monthly pension at application), and the form requirements for Financing Contracts and Guarantee Agreements. It excludes lease financing, real estate financing and margin lending. It binds Creditors dealing with Borrowers, including rules on Authenticated Communication for new financing and limit increases, documenting the purpose of financing, and the 20% repayment threshold before refinancing.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, consumer protection

# Saudi Central Bank Law

The Saudi Central Bank Law (Royal Decree M/36, 2020) constitutes SAMA as an independent legal person reporting to the King and sets its objectives (monetary stability, financial sector stability and confidence, economic growth) and its full range of duties and powers. These include currency issuance, supervision and regulation of financial institutions, issuing regulations and directives, monetary and FX policy, operating and licensing payment/settlement/clearing systems and fintech platforms, consumer protection directives, prudential policy, and crime-prevention measures relating to financial institutions. It is the enabling instrument for essentially all SAMA rulemaking and binds any natural or legal person falling within the defined term "Financial Institution".

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential, payments, consumer protection, other

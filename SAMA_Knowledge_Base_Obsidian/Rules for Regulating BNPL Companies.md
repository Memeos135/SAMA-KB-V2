# Rules for Regulating BNPL Companies

The Rules Governing BNPL Companies define BNPL activity as financing that lets a Consumer buy goods or services with no term cost borne by the Consumer, and require a SAMA licence (joint stock company form) before conducting it. The Rules cover licensing procedures and minimum operating standards, and expressly operate alongside the Finance Companies Control Law, senior appointment requirements, outsourcing rules, financial-crime instructions, external audit (non-objection for auditor appointment), complaint handling and consumer data confidentiality, and Saudization thresholds. They bind companies licensed by SAMA for BNPL activity; note the distinct defined term "Consumer" (beneficiary of the activity) versus "Customer" in AML/CTF instruments, and the role of "Stores" as sales outlets.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, consumer protection, AML/CTF, governance/risk

# Simplified Approach for Commodities

This node covers the commodity risk component of the simplified standardised approach (SSA) for market risk capital, including how commodities positions and commodity option risks feed the Pillar 3 market risk disclosure template for banks using the SSA. It sits within SAMA's Basel-aligned capital framework and identifies which capital requirement blocks (outright products versus option risks under the simplified, delta-plus and scenario methods) apply to commodities. It binds SAMA-licensed banks that determine market risk capital requirements under the simplified standardised approach rather than the sensitivities-based standardised or internal models approaches.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

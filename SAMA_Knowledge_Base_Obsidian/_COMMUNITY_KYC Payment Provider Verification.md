# KYC Payment Provider Verification

## Why this community

Customer identification and verification duties owed by payment service providers, i.e. how KYC obligations under the AML/CTF regime are applied within the licensed PSP perimeter.

## How members connect

- Single-member cluster anchored on the defined term 'Customer' and the verification steps required before or during establishment of a business relationship.
- Sits in the hierarchy below the AML/CTF Law and Implementing Regulations, as applied to PSPs by payment-sector rules and circulars.
- Interacts with merchant onboarding (KYB) and with reliance on third parties for CDD, which do not displace the PSP's own responsibility.
- Feeds downstream controls such as monitoring, record-keeping and suspicious transaction reporting.

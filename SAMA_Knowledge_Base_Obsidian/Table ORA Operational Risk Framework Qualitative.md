# Table ORA: Operational Risk Framework Qualitative

Table ORA is the mandatory qualitative Pillar 3 disclosure on a bank's operational risk management framework, requiring description of policies and guidelines, the structure of the operational risk management and control function, the measurement system feeding the operational risk capital charge, reporting to executive management and the board, and risk mitigation/transfer (including insurance and outsourcing policy). It applies to all banks, annually, in flexible format. Useful when assessing operational risk governance disclosure obligations rather than capital computation.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential

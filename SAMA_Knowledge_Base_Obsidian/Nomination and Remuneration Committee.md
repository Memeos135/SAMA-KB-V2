# Nomination and Remuneration Committee

Concept node for the Nomination and Remuneration Committee (NRC) established by the board under SAMA governance and compensation instructions. It governs board and executive nominations, assessment of member independence against the disqualifying criteria in the governance principles, succession, and the design and review of remuneration policy. It binds boards of SAMA-supervised financial institutions falling within the scope of the governance principles.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential

# Complaint Management System

## Why this community

Consumer protection: SAMA's phased mandate for supervised entities to operate a complaint management system with defined intake, handling and reporting obligations.

## How members connect

- Single-member cluster: a standalone circular setting the first-phase implementation obligation and timeline.
- Reads across to consumer protection principles and to conduct reporting duties owed to SAMA, though those instruments sit outside this cluster.

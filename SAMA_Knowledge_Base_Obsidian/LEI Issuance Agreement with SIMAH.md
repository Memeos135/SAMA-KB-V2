# LEI Issuance Agreement with SIMAH

Template agreement attached to the LEI circular between SIMAH (as GLEIF-accredited local operating unit) and a SAMA-supervised financial institution, enabling the institution to issue, update and renew Legal Entity Identifiers for its legal-entity customers. It recites the governing framework: the ROC charter, the ROC–GLEIF memorandum of understanding, the GLEIF–SIMAH master agreement, and SAMA instructions. Binds the two contracting parties and shapes how corporate customer identifiers are obtained in practice.

**Regimes:** data/credit info, governance/risk

# Template OR2 - Business Indicator and Subcomponents

Template OR2 requires annual Pillar 3 disclosure of the Business Indicator (BI) and its subcomponents — the interest, lease and dividend component, services component and financial component — plus the resulting Business Indicator Component (BIC) used in the operational risk capital calculation, over three reporting periods. It is mandatory for all SAMA-supervised banks, with narrative on significant changes and additional narrative where SAMA has approved exclusion of divested activities from the BI.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

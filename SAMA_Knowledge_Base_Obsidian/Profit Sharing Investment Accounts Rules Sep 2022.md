# Profit Sharing Investment Accounts Rules Sep 2022

SAMA's Profit Sharing Investment Accounts Rules (Sep 2022), issued under the SAMA Charter and Banking Control Law, set minimum requirements for banks offering PSIA products, covering definitions, operation, board and senior management responsibilities, prudential treatment, disclosure and periodic statements, and separation of restricted from unrestricted PSIA accounts. They aim at consumer protection, transparency, financial stability and Shari'ah compliance, and bind all local Saudi banks licensed under the Banking Control Law that conduct Shari'ah compliant banking. The Rules took effect on 13 March 2023 and are to be read with the wider SAMA regulatory framework.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk, consumer protection

# Accounting vs Regulatory Consolidation

## Why this community

Disclosure of the gap between the accounting scope of consolidation and the regulatory scope of consolidation, and between carrying values and regulatory exposure amounts. Addresses the risk that published financial statements are read as if they matched prudential figures.

## How members connect

- LI1 maps financial statement categories to regulatory risk framework categories; LI2 then reconciles those carrying values to the exposure amounts actually used for capital purposes.
- Table LIA is the qualitative counterpart: it requires narrative explanation of the differences that LI1 and LI2 present numerically, so it cross-references both.
- The cluster relies on a shared distinction between the accounting consolidation perimeter and the prudential consolidation perimeter — the defined scope determines every downstream capital and disclosure figure.

# White Land Fee Registration

## Why this community

Registration treatment of land subject to the white land fee regime, as it bears on financing and collateral taken over undeveloped land. The compliance issue is whether and how a financier may deal with, or rely on, such land where a fee liability or registration requirement attaches.

## How members connect

- Single-member cluster: a standalone SAMA circular directing supervised lenders on handling lands falling within the white land fee registration requirement.
- Links outward to real estate finance and collateral-valuation obligations rather than to a sibling instrument inside this cluster.
- Read together with title-documentation and real estate financing scope instruments before drawing conclusions about applicability.

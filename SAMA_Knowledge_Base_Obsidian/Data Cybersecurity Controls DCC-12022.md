# Data Cybersecurity Controls DCC-1:2022

A SAMA circular requiring supervised financial institutions to adopt the National Cybersecurity Authority's Data Cybersecurity Controls (DCC-1:2022), invoking the SAMA Cyber Security Framework provisions on regulatory compliance and compliance with national/international industry standards. Institutions must ensure continuous compliance, complete a self-assessment using the attached assessment tool, and achieve full implementation no later than end of September 2023, alongside urgent cyber risk assessment and management. It binds SAMA-supervised financial institutions, including payment systems and companies.

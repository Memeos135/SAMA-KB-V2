# Loss Component (LC)

Defines the Loss Component (LC) as fifteen times the average annual operational risk losses over the preceding ten years, the internal-loss input to the Internal Loss Multiplier. Related provisions set loss event thresholds (SAR 44,600 and SAR 446,000) for capturing, excluding and disclosing net losses and recoveries. Applies to SAMA-supervised banks calculating operational risk capital and making related disclosures.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

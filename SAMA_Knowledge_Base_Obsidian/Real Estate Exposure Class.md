# Real Estate Exposure Class

Sets the perimeter of the real estate exposure class under the standardised approach: immovable property (land and attached buildings), split into regulatory real estate, other real estate, and land acquisition, development and construction (ADC) exposures. It also gives the LTV-based risk weight table for regulatory commercial real estate materially dependent on property cash flows and the risk weights for "other real estate" (counterparty risk weight, or 150% where materially dependent). Binds banks classifying and risk-weighting property-secured lending.

**Regimes:** banking prudential

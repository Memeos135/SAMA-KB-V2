# Compliance Department

A concept node sitting inside SAMA's Rules for Advertising Products and Services, covering the internal compliance ownership of advertising: the requirement to adopt an advertising policy aligned with these Rules and other SAMA/competent-authority instructions, and to monitor adherence including by licensed third-party advertisers. It also frames the substantive advertising duties the function must police — fair competition and sector reputation, no use of customer personal data without written consent, no unsuitable insurance/credit ads or ads to under-18s, opt-in/opt-out consent for marketing channels, and the binding nature of advertised terms. It binds all financial institutions supervised by SAMA, which remain liable for third-party non-compliance.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, governance/risk, data/credit info

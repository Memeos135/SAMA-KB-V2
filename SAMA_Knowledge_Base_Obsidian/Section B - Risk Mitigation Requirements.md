# Section B - Risk Mitigation Requirements

Section B imposes risk mitigation requirements on banks that are contracting parties to non-centrally cleared OTC derivative transactions, applying regardless of outstanding notional amount or whether the trade is for hedging; centrally cleared trades (directly or through indirect client clearing) are outside its scope. Related reported data points include confirmation timestamps and master agreement details. Issued under SAMA's Law and the Banking Control Law, superseding the earlier circular, effective 1 February 2026.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential, other

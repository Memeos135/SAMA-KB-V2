# Insurance VAT on Leases

## Why this community

Treatment of a VAT rate increase as it flows through cooperative insurance premiums attached to lease finance products, and who bears the incremental cost.

## How members connect

- Single-member cluster: an isolated tax-pass-through instruction affecting pricing and cost allocation in lease contracts.
- Connects conceptually to consumer protection and contract-amendment principles, since repricing an existing lease-linked insurance charge touches disclosed cost terms.

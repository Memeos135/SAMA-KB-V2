# SARIE System

Reference in the Remittance Centers Guide to the Saudi Arabian Riyal Interbank Express (SARIE) system as an approved channel—alongside SWIFT—for executing outbound and inbound domestic and international remittances. It frames the expectation that remittance activity flows through recognised interbank transfer infrastructure, and sits next to requirements on contracting with and supervising money transfer service providers. Applies to bank remittance centers and licensed remittance companies.

**Regimes:** payments, AML/CTF, governance/risk

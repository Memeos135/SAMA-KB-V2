# Finance Statement Publishing

## Why this community

Disclosure obligations requiring finance companies to publish financial statements, supporting supervisory transparency and market/consumer visibility of licensee financial condition.

## How members connect

- Single-instrument cluster within the finance companies regime; the publication duty derives from the finance company licensing and supervision framework.
- Interacts with governance and accounting/audit requirements — the content published is defined by the applicable accounting standards, while the publication trigger and timing are supervisory.

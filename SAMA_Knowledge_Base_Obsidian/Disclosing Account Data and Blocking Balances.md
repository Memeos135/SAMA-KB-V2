# Disclosing Account Data and Blocking Balances

Chapter 14 of the Deposit Taking Finance Companies Regulations governs disclosure of customer account data and balances and the blocking of funds at the request of official authorities, requiring that such communications pass through SAMA only. It requires searches to cover all relationship types (active, closed, suspense, inactive, dormant General Accounts and Term Deposit Accounts) and sets the scope and duration of blocking, including the carve-out for accounts of companies in which the customer is merely a shareholder and the requirement to maintain a block until SAMA notifies otherwise. It binds deposit-taking finance companies responding to authority or SAMA requests.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, sanctions/TFS, BNPL/finance, governance/risk

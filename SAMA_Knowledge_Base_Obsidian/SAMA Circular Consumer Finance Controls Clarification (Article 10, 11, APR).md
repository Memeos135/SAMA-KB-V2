# SAMA Circular: Consumer Finance Controls Clarification (Article 10, 11, APR)

A SAMA circular to banks operating in the Kingdom clarifying three points in the updated Consumer Finance Regulations: when the borrower's right to terminate or withdraw from the finance agreement lapses (it ends within the stated day-count from contract signature once the customer has actually benefited from the financing, e.g. drawing cash, shares deposited to the customer's portfolio, or taking possession/use of the financed asset), the obligation to use SAMA's initial disclosure form for finance contracts, and the requirement to follow SAMA's published guide for calculating the annual percentage rate. It is an interpretive/clarifying instruction rather than a new rule, and binds banks as an operational compliance requirement.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, BNPL/finance

# Law on Combating the Financing of Terrorism

Node for the Law on Combating the Financing of Terrorism, issued by Royal Decree M/21 dated 12/02/1439 AH, with its Implementing Regulations issued by Council of Ministers Resolution 228 dated 02/05/1440 AH. It is the primary KSA statute criminalising terrorist financing and underpinning CTF obligations that SAMA-supervised entities apply through CDD, reporting and targeted financial sanctions requirements. In the supplied context it appears as the instrument amended by Resolution 637 and Royal Decree M/142 (Article 12; repeal of Article 9 of the Implementing Regulations).

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, sanctions/TFS

# Previous OTC Derivatives Requirements (circular 42056371)

Identifies the prior SAMA circular No. 42056371 dated 10-08-1442H on trade repository reporting and risk mitigation for OTC derivatives, which is expressly superseded by the version 4.0 requirements. Relevant mainly for determining which regime applies to a transaction before the 1 February 2026 effective date of the updated requirements. Applies to the same population of SAMA-supervised banks in KSA.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, other

# Treatment of Maturity Mismatches

This node governs recognition of credit protection where the hedge matures before the underlying exposure: maturity mismatches are not recognised under the simple approach for financial collateral, and elsewhere protection counts only if original maturity is at least one year and residual maturity at least three months, with a pro-rata adjustment formula. It also prescribes conservative measurement of both the exposure's effective maturity and the hedge's shortest possible maturity (including call options), alongside related maturity-scaling rules for default risk charge positions. It binds banks applying credit risk mitigation and market risk capital rules.

**Regimes:** banking prudential

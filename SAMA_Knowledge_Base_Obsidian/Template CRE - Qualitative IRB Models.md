# Template CRE - Qualitative IRB Models

Table CRE is a Pillar 3 qualitative disclosure on internal ratings-based (IRB) models used to compute credit RWA, covering model development, approval and change governance, independence between model development, risk management and internal audit, model reporting, the scope of SAMA's approval by entity/portfolio/exposure class, and EAD coverage split between standardised, F-IRB and A-IRB. It is mandatory for banks using F-IRB or A-IRB for any exposures; annual, flexible format.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

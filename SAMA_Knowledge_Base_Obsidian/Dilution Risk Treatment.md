# Dilution Risk Treatment

Node covering the capital treatment of dilution risk on purchased receivables under SAMA's adopted Basel III credit risk rules, including how collateral and guarantees acting as first-loss protection are recognised when they cover default losses, dilution losses or both. It sets out the substitution and proportional/tranched treatments for guarantees and the interaction with the securitisation framework and SEC-IRBA exposure-weighted LGD. It binds SAMA-supervised banks calculating credit risk capital under IRB approaches; the extract also carries unrelated market risk internal risk transfer material.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

### [[IRB RWA for Purchased Receivables]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** For purchased receivables, dilution risk is a distinct capital charge that sits alongside default risk within the same IRB RWA computation, not an alternative to it. The framework's structure for purchased receivables expressly separates risk-weighted assets for default risk from risk-weighted assets for dilution risk, with further provisions on purchase price discounts and credit risk mitigants. The consequence is that a bank cannot treat dilution as immaterial by default; it must either quantify and capitalise it under the dilution provisions or demonstrate the conditions on which the framework permits it to be disregarded.

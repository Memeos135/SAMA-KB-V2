# Net Jump-to-Default Risk Positions

Covers net jump-to-default positions: the offsetting of long and short JTD amounts to the same obligor (where permissible) to produce net long/short exposures, which are then allocated to buckets for the default risk capital requirement. Related material addresses net long and net short treatment for correlation trading portfolio positions and nth-to-default products. Binds banks applying the standardised approach to market risk default risk.

**Regimes:** banking prudential

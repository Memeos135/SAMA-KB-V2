# Cyber Security Leadership and Governance Domain

Concept node for the Cyber Security Leadership and Governance domain of the SAMA Cyber Security Framework, covering the cyber security policy's mandatory content (definition, objectives and scope, board intent statement, allocation of responsibilities, links to standards and procedures) and the definition of cyber security roles and responsibilities. It places ultimate responsibility on the Board of Directors, including budget allocation and approvals, and requires that all stakeholders understand and discharge their roles. Binds Member Organizations subject to the Framework.

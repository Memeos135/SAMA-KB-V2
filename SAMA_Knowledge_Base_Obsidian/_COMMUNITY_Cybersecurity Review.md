# Cybersecurity Review

## Why this community

Independent periodic review of the effectiveness of the cyber security function, strategy and controls, as required under SAMA's cyber security governance expectations for supervised member organisations.

## How members connect

- Single-node cluster: the review obligation stands as a discrete control domain within the cyber security governance regime rather than sharing definitions with adjacent clusters.
- Legally it sits downstream of the board/senior-management accountability chain — the review is the assurance mechanism that evidences the governance obligation has been discharged.
- Cross-regime relevance: findings feed the institution's risk governance and internal control reporting to SAMA, and overlap with operational-risk and outsourcing oversight duties.

# Electronic Judicial Notifications

## Why this community

Covers the arrangement by which judicial notifications are served electronically on financial institutions through a delegated channel, and the compliance duties that follow from receiving such notices (e.g. acting on court/enforcement instructions affecting customer accounts).

## How members connect

- Single-member cluster: the delegation instrument is the sole node, so linkage is external rather than internal.
- Legally it sits at the intersection of judicial/enforcement authority and SAMA-supervised entities' duty to execute and acknowledge notified orders.
- Interacts with account operation, freezing/attachment and customer-notification duties in the consumer protection and banking-operations regimes.

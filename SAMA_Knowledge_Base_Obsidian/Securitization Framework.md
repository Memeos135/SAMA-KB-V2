# Securitization Framework

This node sets out SAMA's securitisation framework concepts and capital treatment: what counts as a securitisation exposure (including ABS, MBS, credit enhancements, liquidity facilities, swaps, tranched cover and originator-held reserve accounts), the definition of resecuritisation, and when a bank is treated as an originator. It also covers risk-weight mechanics under the standardised securitisation approach (SEC-SA), including the 15% risk-weight floor, inference of risk weights for market risk hedges, and the adjustments applied to resecuritisation exposures (supervisory parameter p of 1.5, delinquencies set to zero). It binds banks holding or originating securitisation positions for risk-based capital and related disclosure purposes.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

# Stress Testing and Scenario Analysis

Covers the mandatory liquidity stress testing programme for finance companies, requiring multiple scenarios of varying severity and time horizons, company-specific and market-wide, run individually and in combination, plus sensitivity analysis alongside scenario analysis. Includes the defined terms for stress test, HQLA, unencumbered assets, net cash outflows and contractual maturity mismatch, and notes SAMA may impose specific liquidity limits. Useful for confirming that stress testing is a requirement, not a recommendation.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, BNPL/finance

# Eligible Purchased Receivables

## Why this community

Conditions under which purchased receivables qualify for the dedicated capital treatment, including operational criteria and the distinction between default and dilution risk.

## How members connect

- Standalone node defining a qualifying exposure class; its criteria gate entry into a specific risk-weight methodology.
- Connects conceptually to credit risk mitigation and residual-asset rules, since receivables failing the eligibility tests revert to general exposure treatment.

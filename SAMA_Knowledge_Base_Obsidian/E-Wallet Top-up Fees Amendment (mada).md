# E-Wallet Top-up Fees Amendment (mada)

A SAMA circular amending the pricing applied to e-wallet top-up transactions funded by mada cards through the e-commerce path, replacing percentage-based pricing with capped/fixed amounts for the Merchant Service Charge and the Interchange Fee. Use of these amended fees is restricted to that specific transaction type, and all fees must be disclosed in new and updated Merchant Service Agreements with documented merchant acceptance before signing. It binds banks, payment service providers and Saudi Payments from the stated effective date.

**Regimes:** payments, consumer protection

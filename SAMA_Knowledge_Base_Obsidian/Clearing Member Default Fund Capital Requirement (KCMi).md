# Clearing Member Default Fund Capital Requirement (KCMi)

This node prescribes how a clearing member bank capitalises its default fund contributions to a qualifying CCP, through the risk-sensitive capital requirement KCMi calculated in two steps: the CCP's hypothetical capital requirement (KCCP) and then the member-level allocation with a floor. It also allocates responsibility for producing and sharing the underlying data and sets a minimum quarterly calculation frequency for KCCP, subject to SAMA requiring more frequent calculation. It binds banks that are clearing members of CCPs and, in practice, the CCPs and supervisors supplying the inputs.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

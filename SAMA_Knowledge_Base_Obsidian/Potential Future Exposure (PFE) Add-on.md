# Potential Future Exposure (PFE) Add-on

Concept node for the potential future exposure (PFE) add-on component used in measuring counterparty credit exposure of derivative transactions, sitting alongside replacement cost in the exposure calculation and feeding the leverage ratio exposure measure (reported with the prescribed alpha factor) as well as capital requirements. It is defined and applied within SAMA's minimum capital requirement and leverage ratio/disclosure rules for banks. Binds banks subject to SAMA's prudential capital, leverage and Pillar 3 disclosure framework.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

# Internal Models Approach: General Provisions

Orientation node for the Internal Models Approach (IMA) to market risk capital under SAMA's Basel-aligned framework, covering the general architecture of IMA use (trading desk scope, SAMA approval, and the split between expected shortfall, default risk capital and stressed ES for non-modellable risk factors) and the related Pillar 3 qualitative disclosure expectations for IMA banks. It also situates IMA against standardised approaches in the RWA overview reporting. Binds SAMA-licensed banks that have SAMA approval to use internal models for market risk capital.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

# Deposit Taking Finance Companies Regulations 2020

SAMA's 2020 Regulations for Deposit Taking Finance Companies, applicable to all DTFCs in KSA and issued under the Finance Companies Control Law and its Implementing Regulation. It sets the conditions for exercising deposit-taking activity — mobilising savings and time deposits from non-individual customers and lending out of those deposits — together with corporate governance, prudential, liquidity and depositor record-keeping requirements (records preserved five calendar years), and applies in addition to the general finance company regime.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, banking prudential, governance/risk, AML/CTF

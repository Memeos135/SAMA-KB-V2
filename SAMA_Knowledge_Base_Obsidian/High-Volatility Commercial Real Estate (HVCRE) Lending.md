# High-Volatility Commercial Real Estate (HVCRE) Lending

Defines High-Volatility Commercial Real Estate (HVCRE) lending as financing of commercial real estate with higher loss-rate volatility than other specialized lending, covering property types SAMA categorizes as higher-volatility, loans funding their land acquisition, development and construction (ADC) phases, and other ADC loans repaid from an uncertain future sale or substantially uncertain cash flows. Sits alongside the IPRE definition and the standardized-approach real estate exposure class. Binds SAMA-supervised banks classifying real estate and ADC exposures for capital purposes.

**Regimes:** banking prudential

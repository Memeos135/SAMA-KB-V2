# Central Bank Law

Reference node for the Central Bank Law (Royal Decree M/36, 11/04/1442AH), cited here as one of the statutory bases on which SAMA issued the Related Parties Rules for Banks alongside the Banking Control Law. It is the enabling authority establishing SAMA's supervisory and rule-making powers rather than a substantive conduct rule. Relevant to anyone tracing the legal basis and enforceability of SAMA banking instruments.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential, other

# Deposit Taking Finance Companies (DTFCs) Regulations 2020

SAMA's 2020 regulatory framework for Deposit Taking Finance Companies, covering SAMA's supervisory approach, corporate governance, prudential requirements and operational rules for deposit accounts. It authorises DTFCs to mobilise savings and time deposits from non-individual customers only and to lend from those deposits, subject to liquidity and other prudential ratios, depositor record-keeping (retained five calendar years after the relevant financial year) and detailed conditions for opening and linking general and term deposit accounts. It binds all DTFCs in the Kingdom, in addition to the Finance Companies Control Law, its Implementing Regulation and general SAMA finance company rules.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, banking prudential, governance/risk

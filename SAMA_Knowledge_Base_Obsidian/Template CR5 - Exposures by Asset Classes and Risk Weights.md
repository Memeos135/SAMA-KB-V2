# Template CR5 - Exposures by Asset Classes and Risk Weights

Template CR5 is a Pillar 3 quantitative disclosure breaking down standardised-approach credit exposures post-CCF and post-CRM by regulatory asset class and applied risk weight band. It is part of the same SAMA Pillar 3 framework document that also sets out related disclosures on reconciliation of accounting and regulatory exposure amounts and asset encumbrance. It binds SAMA-supervised banks applying the standardised approach for credit risk.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

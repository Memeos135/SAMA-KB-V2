# Consumer Finance & APR Rules

## Why this community

Consumer finance conduct rules: cost-of-credit disclosure via APR, standardised contract terms, and the transfer/buyout of individual consumer debts between finance companies including clearance-letter timeframes.

## How members connect

- The debt-purchase (buyout) instructions and the clearance-letter timeframe instructions form a sequenced obligation chain - transfer of the debt triggers deadlines for issuing the clearance letter and moving the account.
- APR is the shared defined term: the calculation rules fix the method, and the standard consumer finance contract requires its disclosure, so buyout pricing comparisons rest on the same measure.
- The SAMA standard contract is the consumer-facing instrument carrying early repayment, default and late-payment notice terms, with the finance lease executive bond circular as a related enforcement mechanism.
- Credit information company reporting links the conduct regime to the credit-information regime - status changes on buyout, clearance and default must be reflected in the consumer's record.

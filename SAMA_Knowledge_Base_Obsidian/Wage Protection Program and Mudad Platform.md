# Wage Protection Program and Mudad Platform

A SAMA circular responding to the Ministry of Human Resources and Social Development's concerns about integration failures between the Mudad platform's payroll management system and partner financial institutions, which delay employers' compliance with the Wage Protection Program and workers' receipt of wages. SAMA stresses adherence to the service level agreement with Mudad and the exercise of due care in resolving technical issues. It binds financial institutions and payment companies connected to the Mudad payroll ecosystem.

**Regimes:** payments, governance/risk

# IMA Model Requirements: Risk Factors

Covers the qualitative and organisational conditions a bank must meet to use internal models for market risk: an independent risk control unit reporting to senior management, regular backtesting and P&L attribution at trading desk level, at least annual independent model validation by a unit separate from model development, and active board and senior management involvement in risk control. It also addresses alignment between regulatory capital models and day-to-day internal risk management models, and how risk factor data quality feeds model eligibility. Binds banks seeking or holding SAMA IMA approval.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

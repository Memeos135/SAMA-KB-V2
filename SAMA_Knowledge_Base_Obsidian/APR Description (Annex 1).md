# APR Description (Annex 1)

Annex 1 to the Regulations for Consumer Financing, describing the Annual Percentage Rate (APR) and its calculation convention: the discount rate equating the present value of all payments due from the Borrower (the Total Amount Payable) with the present value of the Amount of Financing made available. It sets the calculation assumptions — a 12 equal-month year, inclusion of all unavoidable costs and fees but exclusion of default/breach charges, continuation of the contract for the agreed term, and freezing of unquantifiable variable charges at initial level — and requires expression in percentage points to at least two decimal/basis points with rounding. Binds Creditors granting Consumer Financing whenever an APR must be calculated or disclosed.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, consumer protection

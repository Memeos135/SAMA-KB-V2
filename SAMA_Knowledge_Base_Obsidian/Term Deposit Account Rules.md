# Term Deposit Account Rules

Operational rules within the Deposit Taking Finance Companies Regulations governing how a DTFC opens and maintains Term Deposit Accounts and their mandatory linkage to a General Account, whose sole purpose is to service Term Deposit operations. It sets preconditions for opening (an active General Account plus a signed/accepted Term Deposit Deal), naming and one-to-one linkage rules, the prohibition on de-linking or recycling an account for another deal, and the permitted credit entries to the General Account (funds from the accountholder's own bank accounts with remitter name matching, or maturity proceeds with principal and profit credited separately, including Islamic commodity deal details). It binds licensed deposit-taking finance companies dealing with their depositors.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, governance/risk, AML/CTF

# Financial Sector Cyber Threat Intelligence Principles

SAMA's Financial Sector Cyber Threat Intelligence Principles, structured by domain: core CTI principles built on the intelligence lifecycle (roles and responsibilities, dedicated CTI team, collection, processing, analysis, dissemination and continuous improvement) and strategic CTI (identifying the organisation's cyber threat landscape, threat actors, motivations, attribution and sector trends for executive management such as the CISO). It is expressed as expectations on Member Organizations to build and operate their own CTI capability as part of sector cyber resilience in KSA. It binds SAMA-supervised Member Organizations in the financial sector.

**Regimes:** governance/risk

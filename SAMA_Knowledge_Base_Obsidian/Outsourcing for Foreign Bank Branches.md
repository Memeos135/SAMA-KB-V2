# Outsourcing for Foreign Bank Branches

Sets the outsourcing regime specific to foreign bank branches in Saudi Arabia, covering both Material and non-Material arrangements. Branches must book KSA business locally unless SAMA agrees otherwise, maintain sufficient local staffing and control, and may not outsource key management responsibilities such as business decision-making, compliance and AML/CFT; other functions (e.g. internal audit, risk management) may be outsourced proportionately. Outsourcing to head office or a group member does not reduce the branch's or its management's legal obligations in KSA, must be audited annually by group internal audit or an independent third party with findings shared with SAMA, and any other regulator's report on the controls must be submitted to SAMA immediately.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential, AML/CTF

# Implementing Regulation of Finance Companies Control Law

SAMA's Implementing Regulation of the Finance Companies Control Law (as amended by Governor's decision, Rajab 1447/December 2025), issued with a covering circular that also repeals the Microfinance Activity Rules and the Micro Consumer Finance Companies Rules and gives finance companies a rectification period. It sets the core definitions (financing activities, finance contract, instalment, term cost, total cost of financing, financing amount, APR, board, senior management, exposure, large exposure, controlling interest) and the licensing, prudential, governance and conduct framework for finance companies, including dedicated licence categories for micro consumer finance, BNPL (deferred payment), debt-based crowdfunding and microfinance companies. It binds finance companies operating in the Kingdom, SAMA-licensed digital brokerage firms and licensed debt collection entities.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, governance/risk, consumer protection

# Permissible Activities (Art 19)

Article 19 of SAMA's Rules for Agent Banking, which delimits the banking activities and services a contracted agent may carry out on behalf of the principal bank. It operates as the positive scope boundary of the agency arrangement, read together with the prohibited-activities article and the consumer protection and supervision sections of the same rules. It binds banks that outsource customer-facing activity to agents, and indirectly the agents themselves through the agent banking contract.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk, consumer protection, payments

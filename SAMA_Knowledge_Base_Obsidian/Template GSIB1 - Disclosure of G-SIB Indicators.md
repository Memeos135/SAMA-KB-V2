# Template GSIB1 - Disclosure of G-SIB Indicators

Pillar 3 template requiring disclosure of the twelve indicators feeding the Basel methodology for assessing global systemic importance, with an annual reference date, prior-exercise web link and optional narrative on estimates, structural changes and bucket allocation. Scope is limited: it applies to banks classified as G-SIBs, those exceeding the EUR 200 billion leverage exposure threshold, or those included in the assessment sample; in the SAMA context it is not required unless SAMA identifies the bank as a G-SIB.

**Regimes:** banking prudential

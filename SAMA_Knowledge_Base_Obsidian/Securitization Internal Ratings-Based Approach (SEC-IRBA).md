# Securitization Internal Ratings-Based Approach (SEC-IRBA)

Identifies the internal ratings-based approach for securitization exposures (SEC-IRBA), including the definition of KIRB, attachment and detachment points, the supervisory parameter p, risk weight calculation, and the alternative capital treatment available for term and short-term securitizations meeting the STC criteria. It sits alongside the external ratings-based (SEC-ERBA) and internal assessment (SEC-IAA) approaches and the treatment of non-performing loan securitizations, with dilution risk recognition illustrated separately. It binds banks with SAMA approval to use IRB modelled approaches for securitization positions, and feeds the RWA disclosure templates.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

# Template CVA4: RWA Flow Statements SA-CVA

A Pillar 3 disclosure template within SAMA's Pillar 3 Disclosure Requirements Framework covering credit valuation adjustment (CVA) risk RWA disclosure, including flow statements for RWA under the standardised approach to CVA (SA-CVA) and the components of the reduced basic approach (BA-CVA) with their aggregation of systematic and idiosyncratic CVA risk. It is mandatory for banks whose CVA risk RWA are measured under the relevant approach, with semiannual frequency, fixed format, and an accompanying narrative on hedging. It binds SAMA-supervised banks subject to the capital and disclosure framework and links to the OV1 overview of RWA.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

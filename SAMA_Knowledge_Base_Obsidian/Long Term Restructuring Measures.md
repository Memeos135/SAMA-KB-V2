# Long Term Restructuring Measures

A taxonomy node within SAMA's NPL/loan workout guidance distinguishing long-term (permanent) restructuring measures — such as debt-for-asset or debt-for-equity swaps, conditional debt forgiveness, profit/interest rate reduction, extension of maturity, debt consolidation, fresh money and additional security — from short-term/temporary relief measures, which are limited in tenor and used only where a documented, temporary cash-flow disruption exists. It informs how banks select and evidence a forbearance solution for distressed borrowers, including MSMEs, and how the chosen measure is then reflected in restructuring documentation. It binds banks conducting NPL workout and restructuring activity under SAMA supervision.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential

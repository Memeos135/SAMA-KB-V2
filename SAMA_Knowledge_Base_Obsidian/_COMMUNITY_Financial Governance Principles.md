# Financial Governance Principles

## Why this community

The institutional governance, control and conduct architecture SAMA imposes on banks, finance and real estate refinance companies, and exchange centres — board and committee mandates, the three lines of defence (compliance, internal audit, risk), Shariah governance, remuneration, and code-of-conduct obligations.

## How members connect

- Hierarchy: governance principles documents sit at the top, with sector-specific compliance and internal audit principles (banks vs finance/real estate refinance companies) elaborating the same core duties for different licensee populations.
- Obligation chains run board → committees (audit, risk, nomination and remuneration, executive) → executive management → control functions (compliance unit/officer, internal audit unit, Shariah committee), each with defined independence, reporting-line and escalation duties.
- Shared defined terms and the three lines of defence model tie compliance, internal audit and risk together, including the second-line/third-line relationship and quality assurance over audit work.
- Cross-regime hooks: compliance principles pull in AML/CTF obligations, information confidentiality/classification, and conduct rules; remuneration rules (2023, superseding the 2010 compensation practices) attach malus/clawback to misconduct risk and require compliance reporting.

# Compliance with SAMA Circulars

A SAMA circular (transmitted via the Finance Companies Supervision Department) reaffirming that supervised financial institutions must comply with circulars issued by SAMA. It announces the SAMA Circulars Portal as a transparency tool and expressly states that publication on the portal does not relieve any institution of its obligation to comply with instructions delivered through official written correspondence or official SAMA departmental email. Addressed to finance companies operating in the Kingdom, effective from the date of email transmission.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, BNPL/finance

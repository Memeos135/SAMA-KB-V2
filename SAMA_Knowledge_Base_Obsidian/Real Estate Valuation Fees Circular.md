# Real Estate Valuation Fees Circular

SAMA circular on real estate valuation fees in individual (retail) real estate finance contracts. It prohibits financiers from charging the valuation fee to the customer before initial credit approval is granted, and requires them, before contracting steps begin, to inform the customer and obtain a written acknowledgement that the fee is non-refundable where the financing is not completed for reasons attributable to the customer. It binds banks and real estate finance companies operating in the Kingdom.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, BNPL/finance

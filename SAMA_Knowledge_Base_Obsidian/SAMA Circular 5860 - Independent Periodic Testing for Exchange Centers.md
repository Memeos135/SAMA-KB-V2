# SAMA Circular 5860 - Independent Periodic Testing for Exchange Centers

A SAMA circular requiring exchange centres to comply with two provisions of the Rules Governing Money Exchange Business: an independent periodic test, at least annually, of the centre's operations and automated systems and its adherence to internal policies and procedures, to be completed by end of July each calendar year; and an external auditor assessment of internal control systems whose report must at minimum record any observations on the centre's compliance with the Rules. Acknowledgement of receipt is required within a week, and SAMA reserves supervisory action against non-compliant centres. Binds exchange centres operating in the Kingdom.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, AML/CTF, other

# Internal Audit Policy

Node on the Internal Audit Policy required under SAMA's Internal Audit Principles for finance companies and real estate refinance companies: a formal document prepared by the head of internal audit and approved by the Board, whose mandatory contents are set out in the seventh principle. It establishes the internal audit function's mandate, independence, objectivity and conflict-of-interest safeguards. Binds SAMA-licensed finance and real estate refinance companies and their Boards.

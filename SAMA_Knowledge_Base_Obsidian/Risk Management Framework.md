# Risk Management Framework

Sets the risk governance requirement for Debt-Based Crowdfunding Companies: a board-approved, annually updated risk management policy covering an enumerated risk taxonomy (credit, market, operational, legal, reputational, technology, fraud, information security, cyber, ML/TF, outsourcing, platform cessation and Participant-protection/default risk) supported by an identification, assessment, monitoring and escalation framework. Adjacent provisions require an external auditor with SAMA non-objection, business continuity plans, ten-year record retention, staffing adequacy, wind-down planning, and compliance with SAMA information security and AML/CTF and financial-crime requirements proportionate to activity and risk. Binds the licensed company's board and senior management.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, AML/CTF, BNPL/finance

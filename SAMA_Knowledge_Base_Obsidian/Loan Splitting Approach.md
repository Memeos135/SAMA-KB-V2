# Loan Splitting Approach

Refers to the alternative loan-splitting treatment for real estate exposures, whereby the exposure is divided into a portion covered by the property value (attracting a preferential risk weight) and a residual portion risk weighted as an exposure to the counterparty. It sits alongside the whole loan approach in the standardised approach to real estate and applies to banks calculating credit risk capital requirements.

**Regimes:** banking prudential

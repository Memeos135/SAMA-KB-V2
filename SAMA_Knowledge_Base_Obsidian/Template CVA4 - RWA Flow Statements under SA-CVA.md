# Template CVA4 - RWA Flow Statements under SA-CVA

Template CVA4 is the Pillar 3 RWA flow statement for CVA risk exposures measured under the standardised approach (SA-CVA), showing period-on-period movements in CVA RWA and their drivers. It sits in section 23 of SAMA's Pillar 3 Disclosure Requirements Framework and applies to banks that exceed the CVA materiality threshold and use SA-CVA. The retrieved context mixes in definitions from other templates (BA-CVA / general RWA overview), so the underlying document should be consulted for CVA4's own rows and frequency.

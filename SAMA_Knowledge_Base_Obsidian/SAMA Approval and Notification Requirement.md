# SAMA Approval and Notification Requirement

A cluster of provisions in SAMA's bank account rules requiring the bank's head-office compliance department to obtain SAMA's prior approval (and, for certain categories, the approval of the sector supervisor such as the Ministry of Hajj and Umrah or MHRSD) before opening or transferring specified account types. It fixes escalation and turnaround timelines (same-day referral to compliance and SAMA, maximum two working days to open), documentation of refusals at branch level, dual control over the account, and restrictions such as holding all Hajj organizer accounts at a single bank and limiting sub-account use to defined purposes. It binds licensed banks operating in Saudi Arabia and their compliance functions.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, governance/risk, banking prudential

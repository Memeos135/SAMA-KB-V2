# CS01 Sensitivity

CS01 is the credit spread delta sensitivity used for credit spread risk classes (non-securitisation, securitisation non-CTP and CTP): the change in instrument value from a one basis point move in the credit spread at a given tenor. The standard permits proxying PV01 to CS01 where counterparty-specific money market curves are unavailable. Relevant to banks computing standardised market risk capital.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

# LCR and NSFR Ratios

Part of SAMA's ILAAP guidelines addressing how banks must express and disclose liquidity risk appetite and limits by reference to regulatory metrics — LCR, NSFR, Loan-to-Deposit Ratio and the SAMA Liquidity Ratio — under both normal and stressed conditions, and distinguish these from internally set treasury limits. It also covers the three-year funding strategy requiring Board (or delegated) approval and the quantification of liquidity risk, including comparison of internal assessments with LCR/NSFR outputs. Binds SAMA-supervised banks, their boards and treasury/risk functions.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

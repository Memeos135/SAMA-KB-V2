# Exposures to Central Counterparties (QCCP)

Appendix X of SAMA's Large Exposure Rules addresses bank exposures to central counterparties, exempting clearing-related exposures to qualifying CCPs (QCCPs) from the large exposure limits while retaining regulatory reporting, and requiring non-QCCP clearing and other exposures to be aggregated and held within the 25% eligible capital base limit. It adopts the risk-based capital definition of QCCP and disapplies the connected-counterparties concept to clearing-related CCP exposures. It binds banks that clear through CCPs.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, payments

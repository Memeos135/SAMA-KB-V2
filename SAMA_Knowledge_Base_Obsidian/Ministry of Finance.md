# Ministry of Finance

Concept node identifying the Ministry of Finance's approval role in SAMA's bank account opening rules for government entities: foreign-currency government accounts, transfers of government accounts between banks, closure requests routed through SAMA, limits on credit/overdrafts to government bodies, Saudi-only signatories, and donation-receipt accounts; MoF approval is also required for sports club accounts. It binds banks opening or maintaining accounts for government entities and public-sector bodies.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, governance/risk, banking prudential

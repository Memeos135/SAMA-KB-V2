# Default and Contract Termination

Section of SAMA's mandatory model vehicle finance lease (Ijarah) contract for individuals, issued by circular to banks and finance companies under the Banking Control Law, the Finance Companies Control Law and the Finance Lease Law and its implementing regulation. This node covers the breach, default and contract-rescission provisions, alongside related consequences of late payment, lessee withdrawal, expiry without ownership transfer, and death or incapacity of the lessee. It binds lessors, who must use the approved form without amendment and may not conclude contracts conflicting with it.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, consumer protection

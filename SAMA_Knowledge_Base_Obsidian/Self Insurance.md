# Self Insurance

Concept node covering self insurance as an internal risk financing option for operational risk, including unfunded and funded retention, single parent captives and contractual transfer of risk, governed by formal loss retention guidelines set in the bank's Operational Risk Management Plan. It also explains that risk retention groups, group captives and risk sharing pools, though formed as insurance companies, are in substance self-insurance vehicles whose participants bear virtually all their own losses. Guidance for bank boards and senior management deciding how much operational risk to retain versus transfer.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential

# Secondary Market (Refinance)

This node covers the definitions chapter and the secondary market chapter of SAMA's Rules Governing Real Estate Refinance Companies, including the concluding provisions. It defines key terms (real estate refinance company, real estate financier, real estate finance contractual rights, borrower, primary vs secondary market, refinance activity) and conditions trading of real estate finance contractual rights in the secondary market on a prior SAMA no-objection letter and a submitted comprehensive plan covering sukuk structuring, collection procedures, credit enhancement and a feasibility/impact study; SAMA may restrict or prohibit such trading. It binds licensed real estate refinance companies and is read together with the Real Estate Finance Law and Finance Companies Control Law and their implementing regulations.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, governance/risk

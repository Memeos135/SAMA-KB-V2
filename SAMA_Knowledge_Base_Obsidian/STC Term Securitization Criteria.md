# STC Term Securitization Criteria

Node covering the simple, transparent and comparable (STC) criteria for traditional term (non-ABCP) securitizations under SAMA's minimum capital requirements for credit risk. It sets the scope of the STC framework, the disclosure duty on originators/sponsors, the investor's own obligation to assess STC compliance before applying alternative capital treatment, and the requirement that criteria be met on an ongoing basis. Binds locally incorporated banks acting as originator, sponsor or investor in securitization positions.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

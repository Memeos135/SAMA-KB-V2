# Master Netting Agreements for SFTs

Concept node on the recognition of master netting agreements for securities financing transactions and margin agreements, covering how netted exposure (E*) is used as EAD, treatment where one margin agreement spans several netting sets, and collateral taken outside a netting set. It also notes the VaR models alternative to standard haircuts for SFT counterparty credit risk, subject to eligibility criteria and supervisory recognition of the internal model. Binds SAMA-supervised banks; use of the VaR approach requires SAMA approval.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

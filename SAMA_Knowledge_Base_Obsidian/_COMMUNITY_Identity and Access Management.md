# Identity and Access Management

## Why this community

Identity and access management as a mandated control domain — governing user identification, authentication, authorisation, privileged access and periodic entitlement recertification.

## How members connect

- Single-node cluster covering a defined technical control domain whose obligations attach to the member organisation, not to a service provider by default.
- Interacts with customer-facing regimes: authentication expectations here sit alongside payments/PSP customer authentication and consumer-protection duties on unauthorised transactions.
- Access controls are the evidentiary basis for accountability in incident investigation and audit, tying this node back to the audit and review obligations.

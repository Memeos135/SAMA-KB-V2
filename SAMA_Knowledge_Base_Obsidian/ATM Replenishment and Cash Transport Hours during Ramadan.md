# ATM Replenishment and Cash Transport Hours during Ramadan

A SAMA circular setting the permitted hours for cash-in-transit and for ATM replenishment and maintenance during Ramadan 1447H, distinguishing 24-hour coverage in major cities, malls, airports and train stations from restricted hours elsewhere. It also reaffirms compliance with the security requirements of the Law on Transporting Money, Precious Metals and Valuable Documents, its Implementing Regulation and related SAMA circulars. It binds banks and currency exchange centers operating in the Kingdom, subject to any security-authority determinations.

**Regimes:** payments, governance/risk, other

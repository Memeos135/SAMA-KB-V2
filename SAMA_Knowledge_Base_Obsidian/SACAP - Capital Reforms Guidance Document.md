# SACAP - Capital Reforms Guidance Document

Cross-references to SAMA's SACAP capital rules used as the definitional backbone for the Pillar 3 capital composition disclosures (Template CC1 line items), covering regulatory adjustments and deductions such as goodwill and intangibles, DTAs, cash flow hedge reserve, provision shortfall, own-credit-risk gains, pension fund assets, own shares, reciprocal cross-holdings and the 10%/5% threshold deductions for investments in capital instruments and other TLAC liabilities. It binds banks in computing CET1, AT1 and Tier 2, with certain threshold conditions applying only to G-SIBs.

**Regimes:** banking prudential

# Complaints and Disputes Handling

This node covers the complaints and disputes part of the Implementing Regulation of the Payments and Payment Services Law, together with the regulation's definition of a "complaint" as an expression of dissatisfaction or objection submitted by a customer or consumer to the licensee through available channels. It sits alongside the customer protection and financial inclusion part, and governs how licensees receive, handle and escalate complaints and disputes. It binds SAMA-licensed payment service providers and payment system operators dealing with customers and consumers.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, payments

# IFSB Standards

Reference to the Islamic Financial Services Board (IFSB) standards, identified in the SAMA covering circular as the international benchmark with which the updated Related Parties Rules for Banks were aligned. It is a standard-setting reference, not directly binding of itself; its effect flows through the SAMA rules that adopt it. Useful for interpreting the treatment of Shari'ah-based and Shari'ah-compliant exposures and Shariah Committee members as related parties.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk, other

# SAMA IT Governance Framework

Governance-domain node of SAMA's Counter-Fraud Framework, covering the Counter-Fraud Governance Structure and its interaction with SAMA's wider governance instruments. It places ultimate responsibility on the Board and Executive Leadership for the Counter-Fraud Programme (Strategy, Policy, governance structure and internal/external reporting) and requires a dedicated Counter-Fraud Governance Committee headed by an Executive Committee member, with mandated representation including the Head of Counter-Fraud, CRO, COO and Head of Digital. It binds Member Organisations proportionately to their size and complexity.

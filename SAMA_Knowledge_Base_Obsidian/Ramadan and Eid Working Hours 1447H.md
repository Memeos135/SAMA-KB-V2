# Ramadan and Eid Working Hours 1447H

A SAMA circular setting Ramadan daily working hours and the Eid al-Fitr and Eid al-Adha holiday dates for 1447H for supervised financial institutions. It fixes bank public-dealing hours, flexible six-hour hours for bank-affiliated money transfer centers and payment service providers, four working-day holidays for each Eid, continuous operation of branches serving pilgrims at Hajj entry points and the Holy Sites, and an obligation to open selected branches during Eid holidays and notify SAMA and the public of their names and hours.

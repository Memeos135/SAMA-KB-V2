# ABCP Conduit

This node defines the ABCP conduit and related terminology (sponsor, transaction, beneficial interest) used in the securitisation and short-term STC provisions of SAMA's credit risk capital framework. It clarifies that a conduit may finance transactions of different asset types provided each underlying transaction is homogeneous, and that programme-wide credit enhancement does not by itself defeat STC eligibility. It is relevant to banks acting as sponsor, liquidity provider or investor in ABCP programmes.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

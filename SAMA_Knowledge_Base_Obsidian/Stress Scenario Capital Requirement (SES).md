# Stress Scenario Capital Requirement (SES)

Concept node covering the stress-scenario dimension of SAMA's market risk capital framework: the mandatory stress testing programme for banks using the internal models approach (desk-level and bank-wide), and the three prescribed correlation scenarios (medium, high, low) under which the sensitivities-based method capital requirement must be recalculated, with the highest result binding. It binds banks calculating market risk capital, with results to be escalated to senior management and periodically to the board. Relevant where a bank must combine SAMA-specified scenarios with its own bank-specific stress scenarios.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

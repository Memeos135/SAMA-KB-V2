# Assessing Effect of Guarantees

This node governs how the risk-mitigating effect of guarantees may be reflected in IRB parameters — via adjustment to PD or LGD estimates, with the LGD route available only to banks approved for own LGD estimates, and consistency required across guarantee types and over time. It also requires borrower ratings for both obligor and each recognised guarantor at outset and on an ongoing basis, retention of information on the borrower absent the guarantee, and equivalent treatment for retail pool assignment. Adjacent context sets effective maturity (M) rules. Binds banks applying IRB to corporate, sovereign and retail exposures.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

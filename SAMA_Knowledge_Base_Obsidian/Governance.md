# Governance

The governance section of SAMA's risk mitigation requirements for non-centrally cleared OTC derivative contracts, sitting alongside the reconciliation, compression and dispute provisions. It concerns board and senior management oversight of the bank's OTC derivative risk mitigation framework, including escalation of material disputes and ownership of the required policies and procedures. It binds SAMA-supervised banks.

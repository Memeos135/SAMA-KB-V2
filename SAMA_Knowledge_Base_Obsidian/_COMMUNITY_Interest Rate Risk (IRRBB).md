# Interest Rate Risk (IRRBB)

## Why this community

Identification, measurement, governance and supervisory reporting of interest rate risk arising from banking book positions, and its treatment within internal capital adequacy assessment.

## How members connect

- The IRRBB framework is the operative Saudi requirement and references the Basel principles as the international standard it implements.
- IRRBB feeds the ICAAP reporting chain: measured exposure and stress outcomes must be surfaced in internal capital assessment and risk reporting to SAMA.
- Shared technical concepts (economic value and earnings perspectives, shock scenarios, banking book scope) are defined once and relied on across all three members.
- Hierarchy runs from international principles to domestic framework to periodic reporting obligation.

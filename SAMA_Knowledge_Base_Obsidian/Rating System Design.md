# Rating System Design

Node setting out the design standards for internal rating systems under the IRB framework: the definition of a rating system, use of multiple rating methodologies, the two required rating dimensions (borrower default risk and transaction-specific factors), and documentation obligations for rating criteria, exceptions, default/loss definitions and statistical models. It prohibits cherry-picking borrowers across rating systems to lower capital. It binds banks seeking or maintaining IRB approval from SAMA.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

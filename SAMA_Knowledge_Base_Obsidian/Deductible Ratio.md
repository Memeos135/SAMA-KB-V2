# Deductible Ratio

The quantitative limb of the Responsible Lending Principles: caps on how much of a consumer's income may be absorbed by monthly credit obligations, tiered by total monthly income bands (e.g. up to SAR 15,000; above SAR 15,000 and below SAR 25,000). Separate sub-limits apply to salary-deduction-linked obligations (33.33% for employees, 25% for retirees), to obligations excluding real estate finance, and to total obligations, with a higher ceiling for consumers benefiting from Ministry of Housing or Real Estate Development Fund mortgage support. It binds creditors when assessing affordability before granting finance to individuals.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, BNPL/finance

# Framework Update Request Process

Appendices A and B of the IT Governance Framework describing the procedure and form for a Member Organization to request an amendment to the Framework. The request must be justified with pros and cons, approved by the CIO and then the IT steering committee, and submitted formally in writing by the CEO or managing director to the manager of SAMA's General Department of Cyber Risk Control, which evaluates and responds. The existing Framework continues to apply while the request is pending.

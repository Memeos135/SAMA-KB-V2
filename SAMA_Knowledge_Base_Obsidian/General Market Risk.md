# General Market Risk

A concept node within SAMA's minimum capital requirements for market risk framework, covering general market risk as one of the risk components measured under the trading book regime, including the treatment of internal risk transfers between the banking book, trading desks and the internal risk transfer desk, and the mandatory use of the standardised approach. It sets when internal risk transfer legs receive regulatory capital recognition and how market risk RWA are derived (capital requirement multiplied by 12.5). It binds all SAMA-licensed banks, both D-SIBs and non-D-SIBs.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

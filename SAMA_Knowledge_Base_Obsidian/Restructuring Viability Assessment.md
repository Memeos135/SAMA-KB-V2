# Restructuring Viability Assessment

A component of SAMA's Rules on Management of Problem Loans setting the viability conditions a bank must satisfy before granting long-term or short-term restructuring measures to a borrower in difficulty. It requires documented evidence of borrower affordability, treatment of outstanding arrears, avoidance of serial or consecutive restructurings, and escalated internal controls (including designated Senior Management Committee approval) where a loan has been restructured before. It binds banks licensed under the Banking Control Law and interacts with the NPL strategy, workout unit independence and performance management requirements in the same instrument.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential, consumer protection

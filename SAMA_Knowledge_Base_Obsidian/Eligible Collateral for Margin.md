# Eligible Collateral for Margin

Concept node on collateral eligibility, valuation and substitution for margin purposes under the SAMA non-centrally cleared derivatives margin rules, plus the treatment of provided initial margin. It covers dispute resolution over collateral value, the conditions for substituting alternative collateral (mutual agreement, continued eligibility, sufficiency after haircuts), and the requirement that initial margin be exchanged gross and held so it is immediately available on counterparty default while protecting the poster in the collecting party's insolvency. Binds covered entities exchanging margin bilaterally.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

# DRC for Non-Securitisations

Sets out the default risk capital requirement for non-securitisation exposures under the standardised approach, including that no hedging is recognised across buckets (total DRC is the simple sum of bucket-level charges) and that no diversification benefit applies between non-securitisation, securitisation (non-CTP) and CTP DRC. Also covers zero risk weighting of certain sovereign, PSE and MDB claims and treatment of equity investments in funds. Binds banks calculating market risk capital under the standardised approach.

**Regimes:** banking prudential

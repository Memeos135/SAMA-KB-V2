# Circular Raising BNPL Individual Financing Cap

A SAMA circular that raises the ceiling in paragraph (1) of Article 22 of the Rules for Regulating Buy Now Pay Later (BNPL) Companies on total outstanding financing per individual consumer, from five thousand to ten thousand Saudi riyals, exercising the Central Bank's express power in that article to vary the amount. It confirms that all other provisions and requirements — notably the debt burden ratios under the Responsible Lending Principles for Individuals — continue to apply. It is addressed to banks, finance companies and BNPL companies operating in the Kingdom.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, consumer protection

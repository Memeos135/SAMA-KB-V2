# Nth-to-Default Credit Derivatives

Sets the specific risk capital treatment for basket credit derivatives: first-to-default protection is capped at the lower of the summed specific risk charges of the basket's reference credits or the maximum credit event payment, with limited offset where the derivative hedges an underlying position; for nth-to-default with n greater than one, the (n-1) lowest charges are disregarded and no offset is permitted. Externally rated first- or nth-to-default derivatives require the protection seller to use the rating-based treatment. Applies to banks holding or writing such positions in the trading book.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

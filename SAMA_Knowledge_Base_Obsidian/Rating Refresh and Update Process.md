# Rating Refresh and Update Process

Part of SAMA's Basel-aligned capital framework dealing with the upkeep of rating systems: banks using internal ratings must document rating system design, criteria, review frequency, exception handling and the history of major changes to the risk rating process, and must periodically re-test criteria against the current portfolio and external conditions. The node also carries the eligibility expectations for external credit assessment institutions (ECAIs) regarding independence, timeliness of rating actions, transparency and disclosure of methodologies, default definitions and transition/default rates. It binds locally incorporated banks applying IRB approaches and, indirectly, conditions SAMA's recognition of ECAIs whose ratings banks may use.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

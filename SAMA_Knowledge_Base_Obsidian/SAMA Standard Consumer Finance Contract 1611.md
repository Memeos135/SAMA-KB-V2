# SAMA Standard Consumer Finance Contract 1611

SAMA circular issuing the mandatory standard-form consumer finance contract for individuals, aimed at reducing divergence between financiers' contracts and limiting disputes. The template covers scope, definitions, formation and term, financed asset, financial obligations, instalments, payment methods, late payment/default, termination, early repayment, fees and administrative charges, guarantees, undertakings of financier and beneficiary, breach, death or incapacity, documentation, amendment, notices and governing law, plus a contract summary sheet and repayment schedule annex. It binds banks operating in the Kingdom (local banks, foreign bank branches, digital banks) and finance companies, which may not conclude contracts departing from or amending the template from the stated compliance date.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, BNPL/finance, banking prudential

# Life Cycle Events (Appendix B)

Appendix B lists the reportable lifecycle events for OTC derivative transactions and the field-status conventions (mandatory, conditionally mandatory, potential, not relevant) that apply depending on the reported action type; it is also the source of guidance on reporting error amendments. It operates with the T+1 reporting timeline (before 23:59:59 of the next business day, excluding Fridays, Saturdays and KSA holidays) and the treatment of trades novated for central clearing via early termination plus a new report referencing the Linked UTI. Binds reporting banks, which must also keep records demonstrating compliance.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential, other

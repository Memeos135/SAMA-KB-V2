# Standardised Approach (Market Risk)

Concept node for the standardised approach as used in SAMA's Pillar 3 RWA disclosure instructions (notably the overview of RWA template), which define standardised-approach RWA by contrast with modelled approaches the bank has SAMA approval to use. Despite the label, the supplied material concerns the standardised approach to credit risk (including simple and comprehensive credit risk mitigation with standard supervisory haircuts, failed trades and non-DvP transactions), the F-IRB, A-IRB and supervisory slotting rows, and the phase-in of the prohibition on the IRB approach for equity exposures. Binds banks completing SAMA's capital adequacy and disclosure returns.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

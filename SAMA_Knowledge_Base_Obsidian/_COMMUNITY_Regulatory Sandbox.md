# Regulatory Sandbox

## Why this community

SAMA's Regulatory Sandbox admission regime: how a firm applies to test an innovative financial product or service under a time-limited, supervised permission, and the criteria SAMA uses to decide readiness and progression.

## How members connect

- The Guidance Notes sit above and interpret the Application Form, telling applicants what disclosure SAMA expects at each field — a guide-to-form hierarchy rather than a standalone obligation set.
- The Sandbox Stages document defines the lifecycle gates (application, evaluation, testing, exit), so obligations in the form and guidance attach to a specific stage.
- The Operational Readiness Assessment Criteria supply the substantive admission test — governance, risk and consumer-protection safeguards a candidate must evidence before live testing begins.
- Note scope limits: sandbox authorisation is a conditional, testing-only permission and does not displace licensing, AML/CTF or consumer-protection duties applicable to the eventual activity.

# Systemically Important Banks List

## Why this community

Macroprudential designation of domestic systemically important banks (D-SIBs), which determines which institutions carry heightened capital, governance, recovery-planning and supervisory intensity requirements.

## How members connect

- Single-member cluster: an annual designation list, i.e. an applicability instrument rather than a substantive obligation — it identifies who is in scope of D-SIB add-ons.
- Legal effect flows outward: the list triggers requirements set elsewhere in the prudential/governance regime (capital surcharge, recovery planning, board and risk oversight expectations).
- Designation is periodic; the 2025 list supersedes prior-year lists, so entity scope must be re-checked each cycle rather than assumed stable.

# Publishing Finance Company Financial Statements

A circular confirming that finance companies must publish on their websites the reports and financial statements referred to in Articles 72 and 73 of the Implementing Regulation of the Finance Companies Control Law — audited annual and quarterly financial statements, the auditor's report and the board of directors' report — within ten business days of the end of the prescribed submission period. It supplements the earlier circular on providing SAMA with quarterly risk reports and financial statements. It binds finance companies operating in the Kingdom and the Saudi Real Estate Refinance Company.

# Simplified Standardised Approach

Node describing the standardised (and simplified standardised) approaches used as the benchmark for comparing modelled RWA against fully standardised RWA across credit, counterparty credit, securitisation, market and operational risk, including definitions of each row/cell for the comparison disclosure. It also covers the phase-in for equity exposures out of the IRB approach and residual RWA not captured elsewhere. Binds banks, particularly those with SAMA approval to use internal models, which must also compute the standardised benchmark.

**Regimes:** banking prudential

# Executive Committee

A concept node within SAMA's corporate governance principles for financial institutions dealing with board-formed committees, their composition, meeting quorum and voting, secretaryship, member duties and reporting lines to the Board. The surrounding text sets out committee frequency (at least six meetings a year for the committee in question), the chairman's reporting duty to the Board, and the Audit Committee's composition, appointment by the Ordinary General Assembly, term limits and required qualifications. It binds boards of SAMA-supervised financial institutions; forming committees does not relieve the Board of its responsibilities.

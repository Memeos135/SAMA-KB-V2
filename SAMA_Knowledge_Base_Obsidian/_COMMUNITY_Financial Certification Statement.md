# Financial Certification Statement

## Why this community

A requirement for a financial certification/attestation statement to be provided in connection with specified financial documentation or reporting, addressing accountability for the accuracy of certified financial information.

## How members connect

- Single-instrument cluster: a discrete SAMA requirement operating as a documentary/attestation condition rather than a full regime.
- Ties into governance and accountability chains — certification places responsibility on the attesting party for the accuracy of the underlying figures.
- Read with related documentation and authentication instruments in this graph, since certification substitutes for or supplements other formalities.

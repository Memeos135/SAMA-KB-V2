# BCBS G-SIB Assessment Methodology

This node covers SAMA's August 2014 circular and enclosed framework for identifying Domestic Systemically Important Banks (D-SIBs) in Saudi Arabia, developed by reference to the BCBS G-SIB assessment methodology and the G20/FSB mandate to extend it domestically. It sets an indicator-based measurement approach using size, interconnectedness, substitutability and complexity, and imposes additional Higher Loss Absorbency (HLA) capital requirements on designated banks. It binds all banks addressed by SAMA Banking Supervision, effective 1 January 2016.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

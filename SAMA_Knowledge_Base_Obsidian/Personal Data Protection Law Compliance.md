# Personal Data Protection Law Compliance

A SAMA circular reminding supervised financial institutions of their obligations under the Personal Data Protection Law (Royal Decree M/19) and the data governance policies, controls and rules issued by SDAIA. It requires institutions to review and align internal policies and procedures with those instruments within the statutory compliance period, perform a regulatory gap analysis, and submit a remediation timeline to the board for approval, with communications routed to a designated SAMA compliance mailbox. Effective immediately for entities under SAMA supervision.

**Regimes:** data/credit info, governance/risk

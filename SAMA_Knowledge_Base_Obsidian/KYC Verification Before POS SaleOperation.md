# KYC Verification Before POS Sale/Operation

A SAMA circular to payment service providers reinforcing that KYC/customer verification procedures must be applied and tested for effectiveness before selling or activating POS terminals, including where marketing and promotion of terminals is outsourced to a party that must itself be SAMA-licensed. It also requires internal policies covering all customer segments, risk identification, monitoring and reporting, and confirmation that a POS device is linked only to the merchant's own bank account with no transfer to another account. It binds licensed payment service providers, with grounding in the Banking Control Law and the Payment Service Provider Regulations (licensing scope and fraud-detection obligations).

**Regimes:** payments, AML/CTF, governance/risk

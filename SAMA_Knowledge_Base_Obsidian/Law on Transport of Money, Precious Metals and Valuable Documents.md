# Law on Transport of Money, Precious Metals and Valuable Documents

Royal Decree-approved Law on the Practice of Transporting Money, Precious Metals and Valuable Documents, defining those terms and setting a licensing regime administered by the Ministry of Interior / Public Security, with three-year renewable licences, fees, geographic scope, mandatory insurance cover, and advance notification of high-value transports. It applies to financial institutions whose business requires such transport (banks, exchange houses, investment and insurance firms), precious metal factories, showrooms and outlets, and licensed private security companies. Relevant to SAMA-supervised entities as an external statutory obligation layered on their cash operations.

**Regimes:** other, governance/risk

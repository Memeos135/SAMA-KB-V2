# Red Team Evaluation Report (RTER)

The deliverable the Red Teaming Provider must produce at the end of the exercise, assessing the Member Organization's resilience against the executed attacks and covering scope, control framework references, execution methodology, kill chains and TTPs, timeline, tools, risk rating, recommendations and conclusions. It is issued to the White, Blue and Green Teams via secure channels under a need-to-know protocol and is classified Confidential. It feeds directly into the Blue Team Report discussion and the Remediation Plan.

**Regimes:** governance/risk

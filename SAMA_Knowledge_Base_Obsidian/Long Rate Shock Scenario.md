# Long Rate Shock Scenario

Defines the long rate shock in SAMA's standardised IRRBB framework: greatest at the longest tenor midpoint, derived as one minus the short-rate shaping scalar, and used only as an input to the rotational (steepener/flattener) scenarios rather than as a standalone prescribed scenario. Relevant to banks computing the six EVE shock scenarios per material currency.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

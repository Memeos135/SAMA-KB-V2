# Institutional Beneficiary

Covers the Institutional Beneficiary — a commercial enterprise registered in Saudi Arabia seeking or holding financing via a crowdfunding platform — and the diligence, disclosure and contractual obligations owed in respect of it. It requires documented CDD policies under the AML Law and the Law of Combating Terrorism Crimes and its Financing (legal status, identity, address, conflicts, solvency, credit history, business plan), publication of beneficiary-level project and financial information to Participants, and mandatory service-agreement terms including credit-record consent and material-change notification. Binds the licensed Debt-Based Crowdfunding Company; the Institutional Beneficiary bears the contractual undertakings.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, AML/CTF, data/credit info, consumer protection

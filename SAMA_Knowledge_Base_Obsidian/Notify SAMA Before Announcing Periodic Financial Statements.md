# Notify SAMA Before Announcing Periodic Financial Statements

SAMA circular, issued under the Finance Companies Control Law and its Implementing Regulation, requiring finance companies listed on the Saudi Exchange to notify SAMA before announcing periodic financial statements on Tadawul's website. They must provide SAMA with the draft financial-results announcement in Arabic and English at least five business days before publication, without prejudice to other applicable rules. Binds listed finance companies only.

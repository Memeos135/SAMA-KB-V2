# IRB Treatment of Expected Losses and Provisions

Chapter of the SAMA credit risk framework dealing with the IRB treatment of expected losses and eligible provisions, sitting alongside the unexpected-loss capital produced by the risk-weight functions. It covers calculation of expected loss amounts, calculation of provisions, and the comparison of EL against provisions for capital purposes. It applies to banks using the IRB approach under SAMA approval.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

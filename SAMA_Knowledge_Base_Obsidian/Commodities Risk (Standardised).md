# Commodities Risk (Standardised)

Concept node on commodities risk as a market risk class, covering the risks captured (spot directional, basis, interest rate/cost of carry, forward gap) and the measurement options under the simplified standardised approach, alongside the general structure of the standardised approach and the RWA conversion using the 12.5 multiplier. Notes that all banks, D-SIB and non-D-SIB, must calculate market risk capital under the standardised approach. Binds banks holding commodity or commodity-derivative positions.

**Regimes:** banking prudential

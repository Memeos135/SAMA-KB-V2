# FX Risk Buckets

## Why this community

Standardised approach calibration for foreign exchange risk: bucket (currency pair) definitions and correlation parameters used to aggregate FX sensitivities into a capital charge.

## How members connect

- Single-node cluster functioning as a prescribed parameter set within the sensitivities-based method.
- Interacts with the trading book boundary and with banking book FX positions that are nonetheless subject to market risk capital; parameters are fixed by the regulator and not adjustable by the firm.

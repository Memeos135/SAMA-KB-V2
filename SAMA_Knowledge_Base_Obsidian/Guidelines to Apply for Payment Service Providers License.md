# Guidelines to Apply for Payment Service Providers License

SAMA guidance setting out the procedural and documentary requirements for applying for a payment service provider licence under the Implementing Regulation of the Law of Payments and Payment Services. It explains the two-stage process (in-principle approval, then final licensing) and the four licence categories: micro and major electronic money institutions (e-wallet) and micro and major payment institutions. It binds prospective applicants seeking to provide payment services in the Kingdom and is to be read alongside the Implementing Regulation and other SAMA rules.

**Regimes:** payments, governance/risk

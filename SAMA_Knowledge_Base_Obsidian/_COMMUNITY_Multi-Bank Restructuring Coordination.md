# Multi-Bank Restructuring Coordination

## Why this community

Coordinated multi-creditor handling of a distressed borrower: how banks jointly manage a restructuring process and agree to hold enforcement in abeyance while a workout is negotiated.

## How members connect

- The coordinator role is the procedural anchor — the standstill arrangement is the instrument that role is expected to convene and administer among participating creditors.
- Linkage is operational-obligation based: appointment of a lead/coordinating bank triggers the standstill negotiation and information-sharing expectations across creditors.
- Both terms sit inside the same credit-risk / problem-loan regime and should be read as defined terms of that framework, not as general commercial concepts.

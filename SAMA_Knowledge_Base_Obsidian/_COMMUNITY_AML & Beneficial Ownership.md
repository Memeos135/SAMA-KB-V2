# AML & Beneficial Ownership

## Why this community

The AML/CTF/PF statutory core and its preventive-measure obligations for financial institutions, DNFBPs and NPOs, including beneficial ownership identification, business-level risk assessment, and the governance and supervisory architecture supporting them.

## How members connect

- Law-to-guidance chain: the AML Law and the Combating Terrorism and Financing of Terrorism Law set the offences and preventive duties; the Preventive Measures provisions specify CDD, record-keeping and reporting; the ML/TF/PF Business Risk Assessment Guide operationalises the risk-based approach.
- Institutional linkage: the Financial Intelligence unit receives STRs, Supervisory Authority Powers grant SAMA inspection and sanctioning authority, and confiscation provisions supply the asset-recovery consequence.
- Beneficial ownership is the shared defined concept binding the Wathiq verification-service circulars and the NPO inquiry service to the CDD duty in the AML Law — verification source, not substitution for the obligation.
- SAMA Law (M/36) is the enabling authority for the circulars; governance principles and the compliance circular connect AML accountability to board and senior management responsibility.
- Note scope drift: pricing-disclosure rules and pre-investment notification sit here via the SAMA Law authority link, not via AML substance.

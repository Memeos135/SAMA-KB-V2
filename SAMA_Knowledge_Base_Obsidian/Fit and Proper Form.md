# Fit and Proper Form

This node covers SAMA's Fit and Proper Form as used within the licensing pack for debt-based crowdfunding activity under the Finance Companies Control Law and the Rules for Engaging in Debt-Based Crowdfunding. A completed and signed form is required for each founding member and each candidate board and sub-committee member, endorsed by the person authorised to submit the application, alongside the licence application letter, corporate documents, organisational structure, feasibility study/business plan, a bank guarantee equal to 20% of minimum capital, and draft third-party and related-party agreements. It binds Applicants (founders/shareholders) seeking SAMA approval to incorporate and license a debt-based crowdfunding company; false or omitted material information is treated as a breach of the Finance Companies Control Law and can lead to rejection or licence cancellation.

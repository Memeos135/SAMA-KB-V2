# Customer Due Diligence Measures

## Why this community

The core AML/CTF customer due diligence regime: identifying and verifying the Customer and Beneficial Owner, and calibrating the intensity of measures (simplified, standard, enhanced) to assessed ML/TF risk, including the PEP trigger.

## How members connect

- All members hang off the same defined term chain: Due Diligence Measures presupposes identification/verification of the Customer and the Beneficial Owner, so the Beneficial Owner definition governs the scope of every variant.
- Enhanced and Simplified Due Diligence are risk-calibrated derogations from the baseline Due Diligence obligation, not free-standing regimes — they inherit its elements and vary only intensity/timing.
- PEP status is a mandatory EDD trigger, linking status-based classification to the escalated obligation set (senior management approval, source of funds/wealth, ongoing monitoring).
- Preventive Measures for FIs and DNFBPs is the umbrella obligation layer that imposes the CDD duties on the obliged-entity population, showing the law → implementing rules hierarchy.

# Cyber Security Risk Management and Compliance Domain

Concept node for the Cyber Security Risk Management and Compliance domain of the SAMA Cyber Security Framework, requiring a defined, approved and implemented cyber security risk management process covering identification, analysis, response and monitoring/review, aligned with the Member Organization's enterprise risk management process. The stated objective is protection of the confidentiality, integrity and availability of information assets, with compliance against cyber security controls subject to periodic review and audit. Applies to Member Organizations supervised by SAMA.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential

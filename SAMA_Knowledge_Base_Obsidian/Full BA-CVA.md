# Full BA-CVA

Covers the full version of the basic approach for CVA, which recognises eligible CVA hedges, together with the treatment of eligible and ineligible, external and internal CVA hedges across the CVA portfolio and trading book, and the option to cap the IRB maturity adjustment factor at 1 for netting sets contributing to CVA capital. Applies to banks electing the hedge-recognising BA-CVA rather than the reduced version.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

# PCI-DSS Standard

Node covering the framework's mandatory compliance with international industry standards, under which Member Organizations must comply with PCI-DSS alongside the EMV technical standard and the SWIFT Customer Security Controls Framework. It connects to the framework's regulatory compliance process and to the payment systems control area referencing SARIE and mada rules and standards. It binds SAMA Member Organizations handling card and payment data.

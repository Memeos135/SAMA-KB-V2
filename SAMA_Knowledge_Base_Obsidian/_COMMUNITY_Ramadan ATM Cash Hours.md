# Ramadan ATM Cash Hours

## Why this community

Continuity of cash services during Ramadan — permitted or expected hours for ATM replenishment and cash-in-transit operations, sitting at the intersection of operational resilience and physical security/safe-transport requirements.

## How members connect

- Single-member cluster: a standalone SAMA instruction to banks and cash-services providers on timing of replenishment and transport activity.
- Legally it functions as a temporary variance/clarification against normal cash-handling and security-of-transport expectations, not a change to underlying licensing obligations.
- Read alongside seasonal working-hours instructions for other licensee types, which share the same continuity-of-service objective.

# Operational Risk Disclosure Templates

## Why this community

Pillar 3 public disclosure of operational risk under the standardised approach, covering the loss data, business indicator inputs and the resulting minimum capital requirement.

## How members connect

- The three templates form a single computational chain: historical loss data (OR1) and the business indicator with its subcomponents (OR2) are the inputs that feed the minimum required operational risk capital reported in OR3.
- They share the defined terms of the standardised operational risk regime (business indicator, business indicator component, internal loss multiplier), so a bank's reporting choices in one template constrain the others.
- All sit at the same hierarchical level as disclosure templates issued under the supervisory disclosure framework, meaning a gap or inconsistency in OR1/OR2 is directly auditable against the OR3 outcome.

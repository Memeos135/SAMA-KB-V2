# Risk Management Guidelines for Derivatives

A cross-reference node to the Basel Committee's earlier risk management guidelines for derivatives (and the related 1998 CPSS/Euro-currency Standing Committee report on OTC settlement and counterparty risk), which identify types and sources of counterparty risk in OTC transactions and sound practices for each risk type. Within the HLI paper these guidelines are the antecedent material drawn on and applied to the specific risks posed by highly leveraged counterparties. Relevant to banks running OTC derivatives books.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

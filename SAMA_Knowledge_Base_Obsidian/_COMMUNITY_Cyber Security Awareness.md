# Cyber Security Awareness

## Why this community

Cyber security awareness obligations — expectations that supervised entities build and maintain staff and, where relevant, customer awareness of cyber threats as part of their cyber risk control environment.

## How members connect

- Single-member cluster in the governance/operational-risk regime with no captured internal cross-references.
- Functions as a human-factor control domain that complements technical and lifecycle controls such as project-stage security requirements.
- Interacts indirectly with consumer protection where awareness activity is customer-facing (fraud and scam prevention messaging).

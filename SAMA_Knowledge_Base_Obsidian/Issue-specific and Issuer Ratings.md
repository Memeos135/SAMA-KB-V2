# Issue-specific and Issuer Ratings

This node covers the rules on when a bank may map an external credit assessment to an exposure for standardised credit risk weighting, distinguishing issuer-level ratings (presumed to cover senior unsecured claims only) from issue-specific ratings, and how low-quality ratings contaminate unrated pari passu or subordinated claims. It also requires the relied-upon rating to reflect the full credit exposure (principal and profit/interest) and bars double-counting where credit enhancement is already embedded in an issue rating. It binds SAMA-supervised banks calculating minimum capital requirements for credit risk, with an adjacent passage on classifying instruments as equity exposures.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

# Malus and Clawback Mechanisms

Concept node covering ex post risk adjustment of variable pay: malus (reducing unvested deferred remuneration before vesting) and clawback (recovering paid or vested amounts), together with deferral thresholds — at least three years' deferral and above 60 percent deferred for the most senior and most highly paid employees. Adjustments must be documented in bank policies and applied for subsequent risk outcomes, misconduct, material error, performance downturn or risk-management failure, with justifications for reductions submitted to SAMA. Binds locally incorporated banks, their Boards and remuneration committees.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential

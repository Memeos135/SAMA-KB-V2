# Liquidity Risk Tolerance

Defined-term node for liquidity risk tolerance: the maximum level of liquidity risk a finance company is willing to accept in both normal and stressed conditions, aligned with its business strategy. The Board must establish it, and it must be reviewed at least annually and supported by quantitative measures such as liquid asset holdings, maturity mismatches, funding concentration and contingent liquidity obligations. Binds finance companies through the Board's liquidity governance duties.

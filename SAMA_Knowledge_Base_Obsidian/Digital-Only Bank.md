# Digital-Only Bank

Defines the Digital-Only Bank concept under SAMA's Additional Licensing Guidelines and Criteria — a bank conducting banking business mainly through digital channels such as web and mobile applications. It sets licensing conditions layered on top of the general Banking Licensing Guidelines and Minimum Criteria: local joint-stock incorporation, promoter experience and financial capacity, a detailed business plan, ICAAP/ILAAP submissions, a Saudi head office (branches not expected, though SAMA may exceptionally require customer service centres), and adherence to the same corporate governance, risk control and AML/CFT expectations as conventional banks in a fully digital environment. It binds applicants for a digital-only banking licence in Saudi Arabia and, once licensed, the resulting banks.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk, AML/CTF

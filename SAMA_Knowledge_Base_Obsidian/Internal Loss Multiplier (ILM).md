# Internal Loss Multiplier (ILM)

Defines the Internal Loss Multiplier (ILM), the scaling factor reflecting a bank's own operational loss experience, equal to one when the Loss Component equals the Business Indicator Component and moving above or below one as losses are high or low relative to BIC. Sets the loss-data quality conditions (ten years, with a five-year transitional minimum subject to SAMA approval) and requires banks with insufficient data to use BIC alone or seek SAMA's direction. Binds SAMA-supervised banks and drives Pillar 3 loss disclosures.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

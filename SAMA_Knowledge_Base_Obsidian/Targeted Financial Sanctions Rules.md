# Targeted Financial Sanctions Rules

This is the parent node for SAMA's Targeted Financial Sanctions Rules (First Issue, 1446H/2025), issued by the Deputy Governor for Market Conduct and circulated to all SAMA-supervised financial institutions. It sets the minimum mandatory obligations for implementing TFS arising from UN Security Council resolutions on counter-terrorism/terrorist financing and on preventing the proliferation and financing of weapons of mass destruction, and it supersedes the earlier SAMA circulars on those UNSCR implementation mechanisms. It binds financial institutions subject to SAMA regulation and supervision, requires the Rules to be presented to the board (or equivalent for non-joint-stock entities), and covers definitions, governance, screening, list management, alerts, freezing/unfreezing, record keeping, training and closing provisions.

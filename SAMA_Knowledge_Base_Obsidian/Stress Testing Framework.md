# Stress Testing Framework

Section 4 sets the minimum content of a bank's stress testing framework: a holistic approach covering all material internal and external risks, shocks of sufficient magnitude, capture of exceptional but plausible events, account of domestic, regional and global market developments, and an adequate historical time horizon. It also requires the stress testing process to be documented in policy or standard operating procedures, including allocation of responsibility (which may sit with the Chief Risk Officer). Compliance with the framework is assessed by SAMA through supervisory review and on-site examination.

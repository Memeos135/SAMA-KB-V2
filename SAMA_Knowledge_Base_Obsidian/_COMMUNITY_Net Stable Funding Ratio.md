# Net Stable Funding Ratio

## Why this community

Pillar 3 liquidity disclosure of the Net Stable Funding Ratio, i.e. the requirement to publish the stable funding position over a one-year horizon in SAMA's prescribed format.

## How members connect

- Single-member cluster: the NSFR disclosure template sits under the standardised liquidity disclosure regime, taking its defined inputs (available and required stable funding) from the underlying NSFR standard rather than defining them itself.
- Obligation chain runs from the Basel-based liquidity standard adopted by SAMA to the periodic disclosure duty operationalised through this template.

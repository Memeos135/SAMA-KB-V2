# Terms of Reference for Finance Companies Committees

A circular from SAMA's Finance Companies Supervision Department notifying the Governor's decision to form a General Committee of General Managers/CEOs of finance companies, with the attached Terms of Reference for Finance Companies Committees. Issued under the Finance Companies Control Law and Article 98 of its Implementing Regulation (which allows the Governor to form committees to propose measures for developing the finance sector), the ToR set out the committee structure (general committee, executive committee, sub-committees), formation, nomination and removal, meetings and quorum, agendas, minutes and reporting, responsibilities, and SAMA's role and that of its representatives. It binds finance companies participating in the sector-level committee framework.

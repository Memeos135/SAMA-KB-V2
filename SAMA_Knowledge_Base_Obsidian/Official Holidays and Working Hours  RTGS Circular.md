# Official Holidays and Working Hours / RTGS Circular

A SAMA circular (Arabic) approving the official holiday schedule and working hours for financial institutions and for the Saudi RTGS system for the four coming years (1447–1450H), published on SAMA's website. It directs institutions to follow the published holiday dates and working-hours instructions, to notify SAMA in advance of branches operating during official holidays, and to announce them by suitable means. Addressed to all financial institutions supervised by SAMA.

**Regimes:** payments, other

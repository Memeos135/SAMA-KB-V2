# Ministry of Hajj and Umrah

Sets the Ministry of Hajj and Umrah as the gatekeeper authority for accounts of pilgrim affairs offices and of tourism companies, agencies and associations organising pilgrim arrivals: an approval letter naming the entity, its ministry computer number and the authorised signatories is required to open, and a fresh letter plus attested contracted-party list is required to reactivate the account each Hajj season. It confines such accounts to Saudi riyal, channels deposits and withdrawals to listed contracted service providers (with cash carried through ports evidenced by Zakat/Customs documentation), restricts petty-cash payments, requires compliance department sign-off to operate the account, and bars investing balances. Binds banks holding Hajj/Umrah organiser accounts.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, banking prudential, governance/risk

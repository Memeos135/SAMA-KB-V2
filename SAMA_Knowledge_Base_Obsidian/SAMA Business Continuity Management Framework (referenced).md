# SAMA Business Continuity Management Framework (referenced)

A cross-reference within SAMA's IT Governance Framework to SAMA's separate Business Continuity Management Framework, invoked where IT risk, availability and resilience obligations of Member Organizations connect to enterprise continuity requirements. It is a pointer node rather than a standalone rule set: the substantive continuity obligations sit in the referenced BCM Framework, while the surrounding IT governance provisions require IT risk management to be defined, approved, aligned to enterprise risk management and reported to senior management and the IT Steering Committee. Binds SAMA-supervised Member Organizations.

# Digital Onboarding Security

## Why this community

Minimum control requirements for remote (non-face-to-face) customer registration, identity verification and authentication in digital channels — the operational layer where e-KYC/CDD duties, payments and lending onboarding, and fraud controls meet.

## How members connect

- The Minimum Verification Controls document acts as the parent instrument: it references the onboarding controls, MFA, biometric verification and the lending-application special controls as constituent requirements.
- Registration/onboarding controls in turn invoke OTP as an authentication factor, forming an obligation chain from customer registration to step-up authentication.
- Hierarchy and supersession matter: the earlier e-wallet security controls are referenced but superseded, so the current baseline applies across channels rather than only to wallets.
- Sector-specific overlays (lending applications) sit on top of the common baseline, so finance/BNPL providers must satisfy both the general and the special controls, alongside AML CDD identification duties.

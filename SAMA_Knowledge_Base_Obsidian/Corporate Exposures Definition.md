# Corporate Exposures Definition

The IRB definition of corporate exposures — broadly a debt obligation of a corporation, partnership or proprietorship — with an option for banks to separately identify micro, small and medium-sized entity (MSME) exposures, and five specialised lending sub-classes carved out within the corporate class. Sits within the credit risk framework's exposure categorisation rules and binds banks assigning banking-book exposures for capital purposes. Related disclosure guidance addresses how corporate versus retail loans are categorised for non-performing and forborne exposure reporting.

**Regimes:** banking prudential

# Equity Investments in Funds

Concept node for the capital treatment of banks' equity exposures and equity investments in funds under SAMA's credit risk framework, including the look-through, mandate-based and fall-back approaches, funds investing in other funds, partial use, and the leverage adjustment. It also covers what counts as an equity holding (instruments conveying the economic substance of equity ownership) and the applicable risk weights, including the higher weight for speculative unlisted equity. Binds banks calculating credit RWA, with several determinations subject to SAMA's satisfaction or approval.

**Regimes:** banking prudential

### [[Securitization External Ratings-Based Approach (SEC-ERBA)]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** Where a fund in which the bank invests holds securitisation positions, this link tells you the risk weight is not determined by the equity-funds provisions alone: under look-through, the underlying exposures are weighted as if held directly, which pulls in the securitisation hierarchy and, where the operational conditions for using external credit assessments are met, the external-ratings-based approach. The equity-funds rules therefore act as a routing provision into the securitisation framework rather than a self-contained treatment. For the decision at hand, the bank must confirm the SEC-ERBA operational requirements (including those on inferred ratings) are satisfied at the underlying-exposure level, or fall back to the next approach in the securitisation hierarchy.

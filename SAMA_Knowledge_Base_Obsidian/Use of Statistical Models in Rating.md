# Use of Statistical Models in Rating

Node within the IRB minimum requirements addressing reliance on statistical models and other mechanical methods in assigning ratings or estimating risk components, including expectations on data quality, human oversight, model validation and documentation. It sits in the rating system design part of the IRB minimum requirements, adjacent to rules on external ratings and their mapping to risk weights. It binds banks using or applying for the IRB approach.

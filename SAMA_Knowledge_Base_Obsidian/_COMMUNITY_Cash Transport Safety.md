# Cash Transport Safety

## Why this community

Physical security and operational-risk requirements for the transport of cash and valuables, including licensing/authorisation of transport activity and the safety standards supervised entities and their contractors must meet.

## How members connect

- Isolated node: obligations are self-contained rather than cross-referenced within this cluster.
- Sits in the operational-risk/outsourcing chain - the financial institution remains accountable for a third party performing cash transport.
- Bridges to currency handling and branch/ATM operations rules in adjacent regimes.

# Payment Services Regulation

## Why this community

Licensing and conduct of payment services in KSA: who may provide payment, e-money, payment initiation and account information services, how protected funds must be safeguarded, and how payment systems and PSPs must treat consumers (advertising, complaints, conflicts of interest).

## How members connect

- Hierarchy runs from statute to instrument: the Saudi Central Bank Law and the Law of Payments and Payment Services confer SAMA's authority, and the Payment Services Regulation operationalises it for licensed entities.
- Shared defined actors — Payment Service Provider, Electronic Money Company, Payment System Operator, Agent/E-Money Distributor — carry the obligations across the Regulation and the consumer-facing rules.
- Prudential/infrastructure limb: designation of Systemically Important Payment Systems is anchored to the international Principles for Financial Market Infrastructures.
- Conduct limb: Financial Consumer Protection Principles, advertising rules and complaints/disputes handling apply to PSPs alongside the Regulation, creating a payments/consumer-protection cross-regime overlay for the same customer relationship.

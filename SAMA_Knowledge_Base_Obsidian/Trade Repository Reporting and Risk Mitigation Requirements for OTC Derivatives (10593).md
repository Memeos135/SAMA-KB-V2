# Trade Repository Reporting and Risk Mitigation Requirements for OTC Derivatives (10593)

The consolidated SAMA requirements for OTC derivative contracts, issued under SAMA's Law and the Banking Control Law, split into Section A (reporting to the SAMA authorised Trade Repository Operator) and Section B (risk mitigation for non-centrally cleared trades). It defines reportable transactions by product type (rates, FX, equity, credit, commodity), by the reporting counterparty's status as a KSA-licensed bank, subsidiary, SPV or SAMA-licensed foreign bank branch, and by the other counterparty's status, with examples resolving cross-border booking. Binds all banks in KSA with OTC derivative transactions; effective 1 February 2026, superseding the earlier circular.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential, other

# Technical Linkage with Wathiq Beneficial Owner Verification Service

SAMA circular requiring supervised financial institutions to establish technical linkage with the Ministry of Commerce's Wathiq service to verify beneficial owner identity data disclosed by companies. Institutions must observe the applicable conditions, notify the Ministry via the platform where a company has not disclosed a beneficial owner or where discrepancies emerge from their due diligence, and notify SAMA of implementation difficulties. It applies to all financial institutions under SAMA supervision, without prejudice to STR obligations and personal data protection requirements.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, data/credit info, governance/risk

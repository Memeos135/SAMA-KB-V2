# Template SEC2: Securitisation Exposures in Trading Book

Pillar 3 template disclosing securitisation exposures held in the trading book, broken down by the bank's role and underlying exposure type; capital charges for trading book securitisation positions are dealt with under the market risk disclosures rather than the securitisation framework templates SEC3/SEC4. Together with SEC1 it gives a complete picture of securitisation activity without double-counting capital requirements. Applies to banks subject to SAMA's Pillar 3 disclosure framework.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

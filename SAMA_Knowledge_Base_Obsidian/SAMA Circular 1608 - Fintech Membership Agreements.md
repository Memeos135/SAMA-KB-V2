# SAMA Circular 1608 - Fintech Membership Agreements

SAMA circular permitting credit information companies to conclude membership agreements with fintech firms that hold, or have applied for, a licence/permit from SAMA or the Capital Market Authority without first obtaining SAMA's written no-objection, relaxing the earlier general no-objection requirement. It sets conditions: verification of the licence/permit application before signing, that the fintech's intended activity involves entering a credit relationship with the consumer, suspension of membership rights (including access to consumer credit records) until a copy of the licence/permit is provided, and limiting ceased or expired-licence fintechs to updating records of their existing dealings rather than enquiring on new consumers. It binds credit information companies operating in the Kingdom, which retain full responsibility for compliance with the Credit Information Law, its Implementing Regulation and related SAMA instructions.

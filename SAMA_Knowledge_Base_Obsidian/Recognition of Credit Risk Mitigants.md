# Recognition of Credit Risk Mitigants

Part of the IRB chapter on purchased receivables in SAMA's credit risk capital framework, addressing how collateral and guarantees are recognised against default risk and dilution risk. It sets out substitution of the guarantor's risk weight where a guarantee covers the pool's default and/or dilution risk, proportional or tranched treatment of partially covered exposures, and the interaction with securitisation first-loss treatment. It binds banks authorised by SAMA to use the internal ratings-based approach.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

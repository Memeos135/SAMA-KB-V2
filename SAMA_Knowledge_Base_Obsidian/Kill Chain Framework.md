# Kill Chain Framework

This node covers the kill chain concept in SAMA's Cyber Threat Intelligence Principles — defined as the Lockheed Martin-derived model taxonomizing the phases of a cyber attack from reconnaissance through actions on objectives. Under the Operational CTI domain, Member Organizations are expected to define and taxonomize attack phases using recognised industry frameworks such as the kill chain or unified kill chain. It binds SAMA-supervised Member Organizations building an operational CTI capability.

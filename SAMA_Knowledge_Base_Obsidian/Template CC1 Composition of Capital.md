# Template CC1: Composition of Capital

Node within SAMA's Pillar 3 Disclosure Requirements Framework covering Template CC1, the fixed-format disclosure setting out the composition of a bank's regulatory capital (CET1, Additional Tier 1 and Tier 2, together with the associated regulatory adjustments and deductions cross-referenced to the SAMA capital rules). It sits alongside CC2 (reconciliation to the accounting balance sheet) and the capital distribution constraints disclosure, and banks must be able to trace CC1 figures back to their published balance sheet. It binds SAMA-supervised banks subject to the Pillar 3 framework, with certain rows relevant only to G-SIBs or TLAC-related holdings.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

### [[Template TLAC1 - TLAC Composition for G-SIBs]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** For a bank in scope, the TLAC composition disclosure builds on the regulatory capital composition disclosure: the CET1, Additional Tier 1 and Tier 2 amounts determined for capital purposes are the starting point, with TLAC-eligible non-capital instruments added and buffer-related capital excluded from TLAC. The cross-reference reflects a hierarchy — capital definitions govern, TLAC reporting applies them at resolution group level. The consequence is that the two disclosures must be internally consistent; scope also differs, since TLAC1 applies only to G-SIB resolution groups while the capital composition disclosure applies more broadly.

# Qualifying Revolving Retail Exposures (QRRE)

Sets out the IRB retail asset class sub-classes (residential mortgages, qualifying revolving retail exposures, other retail) and the cumulative criteria a sub-portfolio must meet to be treated as QRRE, including that exposures be revolving, unsecured and uncommitted, applied at sub-portfolio level. Also covers when small business exposures below SAR 4 million may be pooled and managed as retail. Binds banks using the internal ratings-based approach.

**Regimes:** banking prudential

# Due Diligence Measures

This node covers customer due diligence obligations, with particular emphasis on reliance on third parties (another financial institution or a DNFBP, including group entities) to perform CDD under the AML Law Implementing Regulations. Reliance is conditional: the third party must be regulated and supervised and apply CDD and record-keeping, information must be obtainable immediately and without delay, high-risk country exposure must be considered, and the relying institution must assess and manage the associated ML/TF risk, verify the third party's capability at least annually, and monitor such relationships. Ultimate responsibility remains with the financial institution.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF

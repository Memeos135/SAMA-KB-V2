# Interest Rates on Assets and Liabilities Reporting Guidelines

SAMA guidelines (version 3, September 2020) governing the quarterly regulatory return on weighted-average interest/commission rates and balances for assets and liabilities. They set the submission channel and deadline (30 calendar days after quarter end), CFO sign-off, domestic-only scope, three reporting templates (by product, by sector using ISIC4, and Sharia-compliant vs conventional), and definitions of rates, balances and maturity buckets, plus the asset/liability reporting categories. They bind all banks operating in Saudi Arabia, including branches of foreign banks, and supersede the earlier guidance note on quarterly prudential returns for loans and deposits commissions.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

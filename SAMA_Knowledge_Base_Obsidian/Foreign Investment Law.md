# Foreign Investment Law

A scope marker: Part 400 of the account rules covers resident and non-resident foreign investors that fall outside the Foreign Investment Law regime, including foreign financial institutions qualified under CMA's rules to invest in securities listed on the Saudi Exchange. It sets the documentary conditions (home-country licence or commercial register, activity licence from the CMA/SAMA counterpart, constitutive documents), beneficial-owner identification at the 25% threshold and mapping of the ownership and control structure, plus the bar on non-Saudi owners delegating account management. Binds banks opening accounts for foreign investors.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, banking prudential, other

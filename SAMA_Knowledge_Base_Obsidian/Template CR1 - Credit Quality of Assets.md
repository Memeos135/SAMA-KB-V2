# Template CR1 - Credit Quality of Assets

Template CR1 is the mandatory semiannual Pillar 3 disclosure giving a comprehensive picture of the credit quality of a bank's on- and off-balance sheet assets: gross carrying values of defaulted and non-defaulted loans, debt securities and off-balance sheet exposures, allowances/impairments, and the split of ECL provisions between specific and general categories for SA and IRB exposures. Columns on ECL provisions apply only to banks using an ECL accounting model, and banks must state their definition of default in narrative. Excludes securitisation and counterparty credit risk positions, which are disclosed elsewhere.

**Regimes:** banking prudential

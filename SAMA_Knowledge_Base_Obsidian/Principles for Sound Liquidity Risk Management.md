# Principles for Sound Liquidity Risk Management

SAMA circular to all banks (attention CFOs and CROs) revisiting the earlier BCS circular on Principles for Sound Liquidity Risk Management and Supervision, which transposed the 2008 Basel Committee principles into banks' internal systems and processes. It requires banks to commission an internal audit assessing their implementation of those principles and to report the findings to the Director of Banking Supervision by the stated deadline. Binds all SAMA-licensed banks.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

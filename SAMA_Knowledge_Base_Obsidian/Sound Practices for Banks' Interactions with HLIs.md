# Sound Practices for Banks' Interactions with HLIs

The Basel Committee sound practices paper, circulated by SAMA, on banks' credit relationships with highly leveraged institutions (HLIs), including hedge funds. It sets expectations on board-approved credit risk strategy and risk appetite, HLI-specific policies and procedures, due diligence and financial information, exposure measurement, limit setting, collateral and covenants, and ongoing counterparty monitoring. It applies to Saudi banks that deal with counterparties meeting the HLI/hedge fund definition, alongside SAMA's internal control guidelines and each bank's own credit policies.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

# Template LR2 - Leverage Ratio Common Disclosure

A fixed-format Pillar 3 disclosure template within SAMA's disclosure requirements framework, setting out the common disclosure of a bank's leverage ratio, its components (Tier 1 capital, total exposure measure) and the resulting ratio. It sits alongside the key metrics and capital-distribution-constraint disclosures that also report leverage figures. Mandatory for banks subject to SAMA's Pillar 3 regime, on the frequency and format prescribed for the template.

**Regimes:** banking prudential

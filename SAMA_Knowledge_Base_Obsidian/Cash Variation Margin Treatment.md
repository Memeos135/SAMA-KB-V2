# Cash Variation Margin Treatment

Concept node setting the conditions under which cash variation margin exchanged on derivative transactions may be treated as a form of pre-settlement payment and used to reduce the replacement cost component of the leverage ratio exposure measure. Conditions include non-segregation and free use of cash received, at least daily mark-to-market and margin exchange, eligible currency, full mark-to-market amount subject to thresholds/minimum transfer amounts, and coverage by a single legally enforceable master netting agreement. Applies to banks calculating the leverage ratio exposure measure, including clearing members offering client clearing.

**Regimes:** banking prudential

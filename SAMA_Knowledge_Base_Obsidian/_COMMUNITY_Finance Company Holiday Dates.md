# Finance Company Holiday Dates

## Why this community

Supervisory instruction to finance companies on holiday dates, relevant to timing of obligations such as instalment due dates, notice periods and business-day computation.

## How members connect

- Single-instrument cluster with no internal edges captured.
- Its practical effect is procedural: it conditions deadline calculation under other finance-company rules rather than creating a substantive obligation of its own.

# IT Project Management

Subdomain 3.4.10 of SAMA's IT Governance Framework requiring a formally defined and approved IT project management framework, policy and procedure covering the project lifecycle from initiation to closure. It prescribes minimum project documentation (project charter, requirements and information flow analysis, feasibility/cost-benefit, detailed plan), an IT project steering committee with business and technical representation, and risk assessment, mitigation and monitoring across the lifecycle. It binds Member Organizations subject to the Framework.

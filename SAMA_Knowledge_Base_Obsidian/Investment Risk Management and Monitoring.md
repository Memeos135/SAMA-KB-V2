# Investment Risk Management and Monitoring

This part of the Banks Investment Rules covers the risk dimension of investment activity: the Investment Strategy must address credit, market (including IRRBB and FX), operational, liquidity and concentration risks, and its implementation must be overseen and supported by documented operational guidelines and monitoring mechanisms integrated into the bank's risk management framework. Banks must assess current and prospective investments, report identified risks to the responsible committee, and comply with SAMA's AML/CTF requirements before entering into any investment. It binds banks supervised by SAMA.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential, AML/CTF

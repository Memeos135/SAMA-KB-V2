# Table OVA - Bank Risk Management Approach

This node relates to the Pillar 3 risk management overview disclosure and the accompanying definitions/instructions for allocating RWA and minimum capital requirements across risk types and approaches — credit risk (standardised, F-IRB, A-IRB, supervisory slotting), counterparty credit risk, CVA, equity positions, settlement risk and securitisation — including the phase-in for equity exposures under IRB and the treatment of modelled approaches requiring SAMA approval. It binds SAMA-supervised banks preparing Pillar 3 reports and shapes how they narrate their risk management approach.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

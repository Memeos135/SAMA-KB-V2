# Regulatory Exposures Reconciliation

## Why this community

Reconciliation between accounting carrying values in published financial statements and the exposure amounts used for regulatory capital purposes, explaining the differences by risk framework.

## How members connect

- Single-member cluster: a Pillar 3 linkage template that bridges financial-statement scope of consolidation with the regulatory scope.
- Cross-references the credit, counterparty credit, securitisation and market risk frameworks, since each column maps exposures to the applicable capital treatment.
- Sits in the disclosure hierarchy below SAMA's capital adequacy rules - it evidences, rather than establishes, the exposure measurement obligations.

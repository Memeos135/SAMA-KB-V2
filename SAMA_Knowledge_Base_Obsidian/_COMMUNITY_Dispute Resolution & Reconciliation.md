# Dispute Resolution & Reconciliation

## Why this community

Post-trade risk-mitigation duties for non-centrally-cleared derivatives: periodic portfolio reconciliation between counterparties and the procedures for identifying, escalating and resolving the discrepancies that reconciliation surfaces.

## How members connect

- Sequential obligation chain: reconciliation is the detection duty; dispute resolution is the remediation duty triggered by a reconciliation mismatch (terms or valuation).
- Shared defined terms — portfolio, counterparty, valuation — mean a discrepancy identified under one member is the trigger condition of the other.
- Both typically require agreed written procedures with the counterparty in advance, and escalation/notification thresholds, so drafting is done together.

# Regulatory Sandbox Framework

This node covers SAMA's Regulatory Sandbox arrangement as referenced in, and operationalised through, the Cyber Resilience Fundamental Requirements: a controlled 'live' testing environment (open to local and international firms) for new digital financial solutions in KSA, established in November 2019. The linked Fundamental Requirements set baseline cyber security and resilience controls for newly established entities in early operational stages, and SAMA may block a sandbox graduation or licence request where an entity cannot demonstrate compliance. SAMA owns and interprets the requirements; they are addressed to senior/executive management, information asset owners and heads of cyber security in participating entities.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, payments, other

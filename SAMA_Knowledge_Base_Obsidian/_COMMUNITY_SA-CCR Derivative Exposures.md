# SA-CCR Derivative Exposures

## Why this community

The standardised approach to counterparty credit risk (SA-CCR) as applied to OTC derivative and margined exposures, including how replacement cost, collateral and margin are recognised. Practically, it determines the exposure amount a bank must hold capital against for derivatives.

## How members connect

- Single governing method: SA-CCR is the parent rule and every other member either defines an input to it or illustrates its application.
- Definitional chain: replacement cost depends on the net independent collateral amount and on the treatment of cash variation margin, so those definitions are load-bearing for the calculation.
- Worked examples (sample portfolios, standard margin agreements) are interpretive aids subordinate to the operative SA-CCR provisions, not independent obligations.
- Scope carve-outs: written credit derivatives and general derivative-exposure treatment set the perimeter of what SA-CCR captures.

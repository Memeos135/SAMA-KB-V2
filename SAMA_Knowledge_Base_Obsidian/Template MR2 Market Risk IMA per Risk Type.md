# Template MR2: Market Risk IMA per Risk Type

Template MR2 is the quantitative Pillar 3 disclosure of market risk capital requirements under the internal models approach, presented per risk type and component — including unconstrained and constrained expected shortfall, non-modellable risk factors, the default risk capital requirement, any amber-zone trading desk surcharge, and standardised capital for desks ineligible for the IMA. It applies to banks with SAMA approval to use internal models for market risk.

**Regimes:** banking prudential

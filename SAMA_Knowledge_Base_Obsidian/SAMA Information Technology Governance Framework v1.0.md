# SAMA Information Technology Governance Framework v1.0

SAMA's Information Technology Governance Framework (v1.0) sets principles and mandated control requirements for how SAMA-regulated entities (Member Organizations) govern and manage IT. It is structured into four domains (IT governance and leadership, IT risk management, IT operations management, system change management), each broken into subdomains with a stated principle plus numbered control requirements, and compliance is assessed through periodic self-assessment against a six-level maturity model, reviewed and audited by SAMA. Departures require compensating controls, internal risk acceptance and a formal waiver request to SAMA.

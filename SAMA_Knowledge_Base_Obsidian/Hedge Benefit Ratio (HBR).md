# Hedge Benefit Ratio (HBR)

Concept node for the hedge benefit ratio (HBR), the partial-hedge discount applied to net short exposures within a bucket when aggregating weighted net JTD into the default risk capital requirement, reflecting that long and short positions in distinct obligors do not fully offset. Associated source material also includes SAMA Pillar 3 disclosure templates (e.g. capital distribution constraints, remuneration policy), so the node spans both the capital calculation and disclosure frameworks for banks.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

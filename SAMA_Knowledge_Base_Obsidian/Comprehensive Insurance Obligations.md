# Comprehensive Insurance Obligations

Node for the insurance provisions of the standard-form individual vehicle finance lease (Ijarah) contract mandated by SAMA, covering cover on the leased vehicle and the disclosure of insurance in the contract's financial summary. It sits alongside the loss, maintenance and inspection clauses and determines how insurance costs and duties are allocated between lessor and lessee. It binds banks and finance companies as lessors, which may not conclude non-conforming or amended contracts.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, consumer protection

- **What this link tells you:** These provisions form a single obligation chain within the same vehicle lease instrument: the comprehensive insurance requirement determines that cover must exist and on what terms, and the total loss provisions determine how the parties' rights and the outstanding obligations are settled once that cover is triggered. A failure or gap at the insurance stage directly changes who bears the loss at the total loss stage. For a compliance decision, assess the two together rather than in isolation, since an insurance arrangement that satisfies the letter of the cover requirement may still produce a non-compliant settlement outcome.

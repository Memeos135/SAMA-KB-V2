# Sandbox Application Form

Guidance notes for completing the SAMA Regulatory Sandbox Stage 1 application form, setting out its four sections: about your business, about your innovation, minimum viable product and technological readiness, and team background. It explains the information SAMA expects (contact details, business name, country of incorporation, registered address) and confirms a 60-business-day response window, with further information requested at Stage 2. Relevant to applicants preparing a sandbox submission; it does not confer permission to operate.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, other

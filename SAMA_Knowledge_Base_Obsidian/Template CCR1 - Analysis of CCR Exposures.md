# Template CCR1 - Analysis of CCR Exposures

Template CCR1 requires banks to disclose an analysis of counterparty credit risk exposures broken down by the calculation approach used (e.g. SA-CCR, internal models method), showing exposure at default and associated RWA. It forms part of the CCR disclosure set (CCRA and CCR1 to CCR8) applying to banking-book and trading-book exposures subject to a CCR charge. Mandatory for banks under SAMA's Pillar 3 Disclosure Requirements Framework.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

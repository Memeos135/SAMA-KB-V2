# Principles for Financial Market Infrastructures

This node covers the Principles for Financial Market Infrastructures (CPMI-IOSCO, April 2012 and subsequent amendments) as incorporated by the Implementing Regulation of the Payments and Payment Services Law, including the defined term and the chapter imposing PFMI-based requirements on payment system operators. It addresses periodic self-assessment of PFMI compliance by operators of unclassified payment systems on terms agreed with SAMA under their licence, and related disclosure expectations drawn from the CPMI-IOSCO disclosure framework. It binds payment system operators (both systemically important/designated and unclassified) supervised by SAMA, and indirectly their participants through cooperation duties.

**Regimes:** payments, governance/risk

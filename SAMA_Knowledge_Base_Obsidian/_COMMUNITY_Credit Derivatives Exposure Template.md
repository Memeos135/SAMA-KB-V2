# Credit Derivatives Exposure Template

## Why this community

Pillar 3 disclosure of credit derivative positions, distinguishing protection bought from protection sold and separating notionals from fair values, covering both hedging and intermediation activity.

## How members connect

- Single-member cluster: credit derivatives operate both as CCR-generating exposures and as credit risk mitigants, linking this template to the CCR exposure and collateral templates.
- Governed by the same Pillar 3 disclosure framework setting format, frequency and scope of application.
- Uses credit derivative and protection buyer/seller concepts defined in the capital adequacy and CRM rules; wrong-way risk commentary sits in the qualitative table.

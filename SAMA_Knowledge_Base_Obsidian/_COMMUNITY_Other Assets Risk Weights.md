# Other Assets Risk Weights

## Why this community

Capital adequacy treatment of residual balance-sheet exposures that do not fall in a named asset class, i.e. the fallback risk-weight bucket in the credit risk standardised approach.

## How members connect

- Isolated node functioning as the catch-all class once all specific standardised-approach exposure categories are exhausted.
- Interacts with the credit risk mitigation and exposure-classification rules that determine when an item drops into this residual bucket.

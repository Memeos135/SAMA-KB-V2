# Corporate Governance & Audit

## Why this community

Governance, control and assurance architecture around credit risk measurement: who owns oversight, who independently controls the rating/credit risk function, and who provides independent review.

## How members connect

- The credit risk control unit provision cross-refers to the corporate governance and oversight requirements, making independence of the control function an element of board-level accountability.
- Internal and external audit provisions in turn reference the control unit, creating a three-layer assurance chain (board oversight → independent control function → independent audit review).
- Shared subject matter: all three allocate roles and responsibilities over the same credit risk rating and measurement processes, so a gap in one layer undermines reliance on the others.
- Hierarchically, these are supervisory requirements that condition permission to use internal approaches; failure is a governance breach, not merely a methodological one.

# Financial Receivables as Collateral

Part of SAMA's Minimum Capital Requirements for Credit Risk framework, setting the eligibility, legal-certainty and risk-management conditions under which financial receivables may be recognised as credit risk mitigation. It defines eligible receivables (short-dated commercial/financial claims, excluding securitisation, sub-participation and credit derivative exposures) and requires perfected first-priority security, enforceable documentation, credit-risk assessment of the receivables pool, appropriate margining and ongoing monitoring. It binds banks in Saudi Arabia calculating regulatory capital for credit risk.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

# Eligible Securities

Concerns what qualifies as repo collateral and how it is valued on default. The guidelines' eligibility list includes bonds/sukuk listed on the Saudi Exchange and anything else SAMA specifies, with legal maturity at least matching the repo term and a prohibition on securities issued or guaranteed by the repo seller; the extracted context also reaches the MRA close-out machinery defining Deliverable/Receivable Securities, Net Value, Transaction Costs and Default Market Value. Binds repo market participants and, on the MRA terms, the non-defaulting party exercising close-out valuation discretion.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential, other

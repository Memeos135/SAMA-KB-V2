# ISO 17442 Legal Entity Identifier (LEI)

Defines the identification format for parties to a reported OTC derivative contract: the ISO 17442 Legal Entity Identifier, a 20-character alphanumeric code, used for the reporting counterparty, other counterparty, broker (as defined in the Capital Market Law), and clearing members. Where the other counterparty is a natural person or a KSA corporate without an LEI, a prescribed client code (CLC) convention based on National ID, ISO 3166 country code plus national ID, or commercial registration number applies. Binds reporting banks completing Appendix A counterparty data fields.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential, data/credit info, other

# Correlation Trading Portfolio (CTP)

Concept node for the correlation trading portfolio, one of the securitisation credit spread risk classes used in the market risk capital framework. It matters for bucketing, delta and curvature aggregation, and correlation parameters, where CSR securitisation (CTP) is treated differently from non-securitisation and non-CTP securitisation exposures. It binds banks computing market risk capital under the standardised sensitivities-based method and internal models, including backtesting expectations.

**Regimes:** banking prudential

# Collective Investment Undertakings & Structures

Appendix IX of SAMA's Large Exposure Rules governs how banks assign exposures when investing through funds, securitisation vehicles and other structures with underlying assets. It sets the 0.25% of eligible capital base threshold that determines whether a bank may treat the structure itself as a distinct counterparty or must look through to underlying assets, addresses pari passu and tranched structures, and requires identification of third-party risk factors (manager, originator, liquidity provider, protection seller) that can create a group of connected counterparties. It binds banks measuring large exposures.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

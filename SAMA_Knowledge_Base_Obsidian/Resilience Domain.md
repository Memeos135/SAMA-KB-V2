# Resilience Domain

The Resilience domain of the Cyber Resilience Fundamental Requirements, addressing continuity and availability of services and data for entities in scope, on a risk-based basis. It sits alongside the Leadership and Governance and Operations and Technology domains within the same numbered control structure. Relevant when assessing resilience expectations for early-stage or Regulatory Sandbox entities, as distinct from the fuller BCM Framework applicable to Member Organizations.

**Regimes:** governance/risk

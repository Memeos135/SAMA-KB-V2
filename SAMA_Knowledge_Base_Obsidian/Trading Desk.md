# Trading Desk

Defines the trading desk as the unit of observation for the market risk capital framework: an unambiguously defined group of traders or trading accounts with a head trader (or up to two with separated or hierarchical authority), a defined risk scope and permitted risk factors, a documented business strategy with annual budget and management information reporting, and a clear risk management structure with reviewed trading limits. Desk structures require SAMA approval, though banks may create operational subdesks internally without approval (which have no capital-framework effect). It binds SAMA-licensed banks organising trading activity for market risk capital purposes.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

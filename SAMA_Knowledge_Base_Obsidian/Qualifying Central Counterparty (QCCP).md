# Qualifying Central Counterparty (QCCP)

Defines the qualifying central counterparty (QCCP) status that determines whether a bank's cleared exposures attract preferential capital treatment or the punitive non-QCCP treatment. The concept operates across the counterparty credit risk rules and the Pillar 3 disclosure templates, which require exposures to be reported separately for qualifying and non-qualifying CCPs. Relevant to all SAMA-supervised banks with cleared derivatives or securities financing transactions.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

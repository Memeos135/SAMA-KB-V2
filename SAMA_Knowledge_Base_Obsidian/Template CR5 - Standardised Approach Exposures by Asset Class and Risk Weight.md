# Template CR5 - Standardised Approach Exposures by Asset Class and Risk Weight

Pillar 3 disclosure template CR5 requiring banks to present standardised approach credit exposures (post-CCF and post-CRM) allocated across asset classes and regulatory risk weight buckets. It provides the instructions and definitions for the risk-weight grid used to show the distribution and average riskiness of standardised portfolios. Applies to SAMA-licensed banks using the standardised approach for credit risk RWA.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

# Head of Contract Registry Powers Decision

Governor's decision (transmitted by circular to the Saudi Company for Registration of Finance Lease Contracts, "Sijil") setting the powers, duties and liabilities of the Head of the Contract Registry and registry clerks under the Finance Lease Law and its Implementing Regulation. It covers SAMA no-objection for appointment and SAMA's power to remove for breach, reporting to the company's CEO, responsibility for correct registration of finance lease contracts and supporting data, ten-year retention of records, verification of contracting parties' identity and capacity, Arabic-language documents and certified translation, official seals (including approved electronic means), and grievance routes against refusal to register. Binds the registry company and its registry officers; lessors bear costs where work is performed at their premises.

# Social Responsibility Day

## Why this community

Sector-wide social responsibility expectations — a SAMA directive marking a designated social responsibility observance and the associated participation expectations for supervised entities.

## How members connect

- Single-member cluster: an isolated SAMA circular with no captured cross-references.
- Conduct/corporate-citizenship in character rather than a prudential or AML obligation; enforceability is weaker than rule-based instruments and should be characterised as a supervisory expectation unless the text states otherwise.
- Relevant to governance and reputational-conduct workstreams rather than to any licensing or customer-facing regime.

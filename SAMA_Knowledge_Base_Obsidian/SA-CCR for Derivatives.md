# SA-CCR for Derivatives

Concept node on the standardized approach for counterparty credit risk (SA-CCR) as referenced in SAMA's credit risk capital standard, covering how exposures giving rise to counterparty credit risk (OTC and exchange-traded derivatives, long settlement transactions, securities financing transactions) are measured and fed into risk-weighted assets. It describes the netting-set calculation of replacement cost plus potential future exposure scaled by an alpha factor, including conservative proxies where inputs are unknown, notably when applying the mandate-based approach to equity investments in funds. It binds SAMA-supervised banks with derivative and SFT exposures.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

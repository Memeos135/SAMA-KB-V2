# Law on Combating Terrorism Crimes and Financing

The legal-basis and definitions component of SAMA's AML/CTF Guide, identifying the Law on Combating Terrorism Crimes and Financing (Royal Decree M/21, 12/02/1439H) alongside the Anti-Money Laundering Law (Royal Decree M/20, 05/02/1439H) and their Implementing Regulations, and confirming SAMA as a designated supervisory authority empowered to issue instructions, rules and guidance to financial institutions. It also references KSRelief as the sole authority for collecting donations destined outside the Kingdom and the Council of Ministers resolution making SAMA the competent authority for payment and financial settlement systems. Relevant to institutions determining the statutory source of their AML/CTF and TF obligations.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, sanctions/TFS, payments

- **What this link tells you:** When a bank appoints or renews an agent, the Agent Banking Regulation's contracting and periodic assessment requirements must be read against the Law on Combating Terrorism Crimes and Financing, which imposes non-delegable CTF obligations on the bank itself. The agent is an outsourced delivery channel, not a substitute regulated entity, so the bank remains liable for customer identification, monitoring and reporting on business conducted through the agent, and the required pre-renewal assessment should cover CTF and sanctions/TFS exposure. Where the agent performs customer-facing onboarding, treat this as third-party involvement in CDD rather than a transfer of responsibility, and confirm the specific obligations in the AML/CTF primary texts.

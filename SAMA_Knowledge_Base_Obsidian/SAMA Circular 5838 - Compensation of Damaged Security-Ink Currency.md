# SAMA Circular 5838 - Compensation of Damaged Security-Ink Currency

A SAMA circular transmitting binding controls and procedures for compensating Saudi banknotes stained by security ink (from intelligent banknote neutralisation systems) as a result of technical or human error. It prohibits circulation of ink-stained notes, requires them to be presented to a SAMA branch with the prescribed compensation request and declaration forms, requires counting/sorting/ATM and self-service equipment to reject such notes, requires cash-staff training, bars their inclusion in bank deposits at SAMA branches (with a penalty per note under Article 23 of the Banking Control Law), and places operating costs and liability on the requesting bank, including for failures by its cash-in-transit contractor. It binds banks, exchange institutions and companies, and licensed cash-transport companies operating in the Kingdom.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk, other

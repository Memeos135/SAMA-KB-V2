# Customer Due Diligence (CDD)

The KYC/CDD component of the Exchange Houses Operations Manual: identifying and verifying customers and beneficial owners from reliable independent sources, risk-rating customer types, keeping identification data updated, monitoring changes, and making identification records available to the AML/CTF compliance officer. It sets transaction-tier requirements for currency exchange (ID copy, system registration and receipt content at defined SAR thresholds, passport/visa/entry-stamp checks for visitors, pilgrims and Umrah performers, GCC nationals treated as citizens), requires high-value individual exchange to be done only through a membership relationship consistent with the customer's income and occupation, and requires enhanced due diligence for corporate customers. Binds licensed exchange houses and their staff.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, payments

# Comprehensive Approach

A concept node within SAMA's Minimum Capital Requirements for Credit Risk framework covering the adoption and phased rollout of the internal ratings-based (IRB) approach across asset classes and business units, alongside the structural map of the standard's securitization, fund-investment and unsettled-trade chapters. It sets expectations that a bank moving to IRB must apply it to all exposures in the relevant asset class within a business unit and must agree a realistic implementation plan with SAMA, with no capital relief for intra-group transactions designed to reduce group capital charges. It binds banks licensed and supervised by SAMA that calculate credit risk-weighted assets.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

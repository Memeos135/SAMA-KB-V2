# Founding Day Holiday

## Why this community

Observance of the Founding Day public holiday (1447H) by SAMA-supervised institutions, affecting business days, settlement windows and regulatory deadline calculation.

## How members connect

- Isolated announcement-type node; its legal relevance is derivative — it modifies when duties falling on working days become due.
- Connects conceptually to payment system operating calendars and to any regulation computing periods in business days.

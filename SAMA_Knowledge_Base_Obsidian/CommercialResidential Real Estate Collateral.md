# Commercial/Residential Real Estate Collateral

This node covers the treatment of commercial and residential real estate as eligible collateral, including the regulatory definitions used for exposure classification (general vs income-producing RRE/CRE, land acquisition/development/construction) and the operational conditions for recognition under IRB. Eligibility requires legal enforceability and a perfected lien, valuation at or below current fair value, and revaluation at least annually. It binds banks calculating credit risk capital and related Pillar 3 disclosures.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

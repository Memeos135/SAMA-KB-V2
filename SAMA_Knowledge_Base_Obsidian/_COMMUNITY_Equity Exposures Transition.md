# Equity Exposures Transition

## Why this community

Transitional treatment for equity exposures — time-limited phase-in arrangements that defer or modify the full capital treatment of equity holdings.

## How members connect

- Standalone transitional provision: it operates as a carve-out qualifying the general capital treatment of equity exposures.
- Time-bound by nature — its effect depends on the transition window, so scope and expiry must be checked before relying on it.
- Cross-regime consequence: relief here affects capital adequacy outcomes and therefore feeds stress testing and supervisory capital assessment.

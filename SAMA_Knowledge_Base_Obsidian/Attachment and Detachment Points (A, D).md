# Attachment and Detachment Points (A, D)

A concept node for the tranche-level parameters (attachment point A and detachment point D) used to locate a securitisation exposure in the loss waterfall and derive its risk weight under the securitisation capital approaches, principally SEC-IRBA. The source material sets out the definitions and illustrative worked examples, including how the parameters are adjusted where default and dilution losses do not share a single common waterfall. Relevant to SAMA-supervised banks calculating credit risk RWA for held securitisation positions, with supervisory consultation expected for non-standard structures.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

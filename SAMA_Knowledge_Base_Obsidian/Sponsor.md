# Sponsor

A concept node for the role of the "sponsor" as used in SAMA's Minimum Capital Requirements for Credit Risk. It covers two distinct uses: the sponsor/developer strength factor in the supervisory slotting criteria for specialised lending (project finance and income-producing real estate), where track record, financial standing and strategic commitment drive the slotting category; and the sponsor of an ABCP conduit under the STC criteria, where the sponsor must provide full liquidity and credit protection support and a single "main sponsor" must carry the majority of that support. It binds banks in Saudi Arabia calculating credit risk RWAs, particularly under the IRB/slotting and securitization frameworks.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

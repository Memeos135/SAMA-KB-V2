# Development Fund Loans

## Why this community

Treatment of subsidised development fund lending — how financiers must account for and consider government development fund loans when assessing or extending credit.

## How members connect

- Single-instrument cluster: a SAMA circular directing supervised financiers on how development fund loans are to be considered in credit decisions.
- Sits downstream of the finance/credit regime (law → implementing regulation → circular) as a supervisory clarification rather than a standalone rule set.
- Interacts with responsible-lending and affordability obligations under consumer finance and credit information regimes.

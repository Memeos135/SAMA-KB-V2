# Rating Refresh Process

## Why this community

Governance of the frequency and triggers for refreshing obligor and facility ratings so that internal ratings remain current.

## How members connect

- Stands alone in the graph but is functionally a rating system design obligation, supporting the use test and the integrity of PD estimates.
- Timeliness of refresh is a precondition for relying on internal ratings in capital calculation and credit decisioning.

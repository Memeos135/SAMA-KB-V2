# Template LIQ2 - Net Stable Funding Ratio

A Pillar 3 disclosure template for the Net Stable Funding Ratio, reporting available stable funding and required stable funding components and the resulting NSFR. It is the disclosure counterpart to the NSFR standard and feeds the liquidity metrics shown in the key metrics template. Mandatory for banks in SAMA's Pillar 3 scope, in the prescribed fixed format and frequency.

**Regimes:** banking prudential

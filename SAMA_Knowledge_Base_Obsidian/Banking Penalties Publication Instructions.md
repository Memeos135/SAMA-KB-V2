# Banking Penalties Publication Instructions

A SAMA circular requiring banks operating in the Kingdom to publish in their annual reports the violations for which SAMA issued penal decisions, under a heading for SAMA penal decisions, covering the current and prior financial year. Disclosure must follow the prescribed table format showing the violation topic, the number of penal decisions and the total fines in Saudi riyals, using only the topic categories in the attached classification guide; SAMA will republish this information on its website.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential, consumer protection

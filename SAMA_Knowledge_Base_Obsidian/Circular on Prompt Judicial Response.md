# Circular on Prompt Judicial Response

A SAMA circular directing all supervised entities to respond promptly to judicial authorities, answer their requests within the prescribed statutory time limits, and receive and execute court judgments and decisions without delay. It follows an earlier circular on enforcing judicial rulings and warns that delay or procrastination may trigger supervisory/statutory action against both institutions and individual employees. It binds banks, finance companies, money exchangers and credit information companies operating in the Kingdom.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, other

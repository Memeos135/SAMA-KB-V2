# Circular on Accepting Electronically Notarized LLC Contracts

A SAMA circular instructing finance companies to accept electronically notarised articles of association of limited liability companies that carry only the Ministry of Commerce and Investment seal and no notary public stamp, following a Ministry of Commerce telegram permitting electronic notarisation for LLCs owned by Saudi partners (non-minors), a Saudi individual, or a registered resident. Companies must verify the incorporation contract data through the existing verification service used by banks and financial institutions or via the Ministry's 'Aamaly' page. Binding on finance companies operating in the Kingdom from its date.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, AML/CTF, governance/risk

# Training and Awareness

This node points to the training and awareness chapter (Chapter Eight) of SAMA's Targeted Financial Sanctions Rules (First Edition, 1447H/2025), which set minimum obligations for implementing TFS relating to terrorism financing and proliferation financing of weapons of mass destruction. The Rules are mandatory on all financial institutions regulated and supervised by SAMA and replace the earlier SAMA circulars on UNSC implementation mechanisms. The training/awareness component sits alongside chapters on governance, screening, list management, alert handling and escalation, freezing/unfreezing, and record keeping.

- **What this link tells you:** If the question is whether staff competence is a separate housekeeping matter, the answer is no: the TFS rules carry their own training and awareness obligation, making staff capability part of the sanctions compliance duty itself. The training requirement exists so that freezing, non-provision of funds and reporting duties under the TFS rules are actually executed by the people who encounter matches. Consequently, inadequate or undocumented sanctions training is itself a compliance exposure, and evidence of training should be retained as part of the TFS control file.

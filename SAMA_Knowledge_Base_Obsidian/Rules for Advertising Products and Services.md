# Rules for Advertising Products and Services

SAMA's Rules for Advertising Products and Services, issued under the Saudi Central Bank Law (Royal Decree M/36), governing the content, language and mandatory disclosures of any advertisement by a SAMA-supervised financial institution. Requires due diligence against misleading, tempting or exaggerated messaging and prescribes minimum advertisement content (institution identity, product name, key terms and conditions, target customer/consumer, all fees and commissions including VAT, and for finance products the APR and finance term, with fixed/variable term cost disclosure for real estate finance). Applies to all financial institutions supervised by SAMA when advertising any product or service to Customers and Consumers as defined.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, BNPL/finance, payments

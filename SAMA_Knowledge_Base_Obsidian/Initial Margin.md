# Initial Margin

Concept node covering the initial margin obligation under SAMA's margin framework for non-centrally cleared derivatives: how the required amount is computed (approved quantitative portfolio margin model versus the standardised margin schedule, including the net standardised initial margin formula using NGR), when it must be collected (at trade outset and on an ongoing basis as potential future exposure changes), and the bar on switching between model and schedule bases to cherry-pick outcomes. It also carries the restriction that collected initial margin generally may not be re-hypothecated, re-pledged or re-used except on the narrow SAMA-approved basis. It binds covered entities — banks and other financial firms and systemically important non-financial entities — transacting with another covered entity.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

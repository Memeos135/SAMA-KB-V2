# Internal Models Method (IMM)

Concept node on the Internal Models Method for estimating exposure/EAD for counterparty credit risk, which requires prior SAMA approval, is available to both IRB and standardised credit risk banks, and must be applied to all in-scope CCR exposures (or to a full category such as OTC derivatives or SFTs) with a transition plan for remaining material exposures. Related model-governance expectations (independent risk control unit, backtesting, independent validation, board and senior management involvement) apply to internal models used for capital purposes. Binds banks seeking or using model-based capital treatment.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

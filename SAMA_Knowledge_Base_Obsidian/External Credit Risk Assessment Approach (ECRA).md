# External Credit Risk Assessment Approach (ECRA)

Node within SAMA's Minimum Capital Requirements for Credit Risk covering the external ratings route for assigning risk weights — the External Credit Risk Assessment Approach used for rated bank exposures, and the parallel external-ratings-based treatment of securitization exposures (SEC-ERBA), including the 15% risk-weight floor, tranche maturity/thickness adjustments and the operational criteria for relying on external credit assessments. External assessments must come from an ECAI recognised by SAMA and must capture the full credit risk of all amounts owed. Binds SAMA-licensed banks calculating credit risk RWAs under the standardized approach.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

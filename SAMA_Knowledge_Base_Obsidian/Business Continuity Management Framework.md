# Business Continuity Management Framework

SAMA's Business Continuity Management Framework, setting principle/objective/control-consideration requirements for member organisations to build organisational resilience and ensure continuity and availability of operations and services. Covers BCM strategy, business continuity policy, BIA and risk assessment, resilient architecture and the crisis management plan (including crisis declaration criteria and command centre arrangements). Requirements are drawn from SAMA expectations and standards such as ISO 22301, and all member organisations must comply and integrate them into their BCM programme.

**Regimes:** governance/risk

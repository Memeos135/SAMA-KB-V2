# Liquidity Risk

Section of the Risk Management Framework for Shari'ah Compliant Banking setting Principle 6.0 on liquidity: banks must maintain liquidity management policies and a reporting framework addressing liquidity exposures for each category of current accounts and PSIAs, individually and in aggregate. It also requires periodic review of the liquidity risk profile and policies as market conditions change, with corrective measures. Binds banks conducting Shari'ah compliant banking in KSA.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential

- **What this link tells you:** When assessing liquidity risk in a Shari'ah compliant book, the bank cannot rely on its general liquidity risk policies alone; it must also satisfy the additional expectations in the Risk Management Framework for Shari'ah Compliant Banking, which lists liquidity risk among the risk types arising from Shari'ah compliant activities. The Framework is expressly supplementary to SAMA's existing risk management requirements, so both sets of obligations apply cumulatively rather than one displacing the other. Practically, liquidity risk identification, measurement, monitoring, reporting and control must be documented in a way that captures the features specific to Shari'ah compliant products, with board and senior management oversight evidenced.

# Implementing Regulation of the Finance Lease Law

The Implementing Regulation of the Finance Lease Law, setting definitions (Lessor as a licensed joint stock company including commercial banks, Lessee, Leased Asset, Contracts Register, Contracts Registration Company, Securitization) and the test for when finance leasing is a professional activity requiring licensing. It also covers securitization against lessor rights with continuing disclosure duties to SAMA, prior licensing before contracting, mandatory separation of lease value from ownership-right value in payment schedules with recovery on early termination, insurance indemnity, and SAMA's power to set contract standards. It binds lessors licensed to carry out finance lease and shapes lessee rights.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, consumer protection, governance/risk

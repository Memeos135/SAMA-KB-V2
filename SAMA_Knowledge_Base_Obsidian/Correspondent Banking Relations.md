# Correspondent Banking Relations

The section of the Exchange Houses Operations Manual addressing correspondent banking relationships used by exchange houses to route inbound and outbound domestic and international remittances. It sits within the manual's framework for remittance and bank cheque business and its AML/CTF requirements, and is relevant to due diligence on and oversight of counterparty institutions. Binds licensed exchange houses, in practice Category A houses permitted to issue and accept remittances.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, payments, governance/risk

# Exposure Asset Classes

## Why this community

Classification of banking book exposures into IRB asset classes — sovereign, bank, corporate, retail and equity — which determines the applicable risk weight function and capital treatment.

## How members connect

- The categorization node is the gateway obligation: every exposure must be assigned to a defined class before any risk weight function applies.
- Each class node supplies the operative definition (sovereign, bank, corporate, retail), with sub-classes such as QRRE and the MSME firm-size adjustment refining the treatment within a class.
- Equity and subordinated debt form a boundary problem: the equity definition, speculative unlisted equity and materiality thresholds for commercial holdings determine when an instrument leaves the corporate class.
- Classification outcomes feed directly into the IRB risk weight functions and, downstream, into the parameter estimation requirements of the IRB minimum-requirements cluster.

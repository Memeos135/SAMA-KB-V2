# Senior Management Role

Companion provision to the board section, allocating to senior management responsibility for designing, developing and implementing the stress testing framework, including organizational structure, qualified staff, supporting infrastructure and data processes. Senior management must oversee scenario definition and methodology, ensure results are clearly compiled and communicated to the board, committees, business areas and SAMA, prepare action plans, and periodically reassess and update the framework subject to board approval where needed.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential

# Wage Protection Program

## Why this community

Wage payment integrity for employers using the Wage Protection Program and the Mudad platform, sitting at the intersection of payroll disbursement obligations and the banks/PSPs that execute salary transfers.

## How members connect

- Single-member cluster: the obligation chain runs from labour-sector wage protection requirements to the financial institutions that must support compliant salary payment channels.
- Links outward to payment account and transfer rules, since wage disbursement is effected through regulated payment/banking services.
- Cross-regime interaction with CDD/customer identification where the employer is the institution's customer and employees are payees.

# Restructuring

Concept node covering restructuring/workout of distressed exposures under SAMA's problem-loan guidance, including the generic restructuring principles set out in the appendix (good faith, transparency, early engagement, sustainable rather than repeated short-term solutions) and the role of the restructuring agreement as the governing workout document. It addresses both the conduct expectations between bank and borrower and the documentation choice between a replacement agreement and an amendment to the original facility. Directed at banks dealing with borrowers, with simplified documentation contemplated for MSMEs; the principles are framed as encouraged practice rather than hard prohibitions, while surrounding supervisory expectations on problem-loan management remain binding.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential

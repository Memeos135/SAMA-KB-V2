# SMS Services Subscription Circular

SAMA circular on subscription to SMS services, issued following a communication from the Minister of Communications and Information Technology on regulating SMS subscriptions under class (B) licence terms. It requires all financial institutions operating in the Kingdom to contract only with SMS service providers licensed by the Communications and Information Technology Commission, with licence status verifiable via the CITC website, effective from its date. It binds banks, finance companies, insurance sector companies, payment companies and exchange centers (A and B).

**Regimes:** governance/risk, payments, other

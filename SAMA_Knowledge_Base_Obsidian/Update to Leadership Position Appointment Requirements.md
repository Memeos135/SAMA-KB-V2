# Update to Leadership Position Appointment Requirements

SAMA circular announcing an update to the Requirements for Appointment to Leading Positions in SAMA-supervised financial institutions (second edition, previously issued by circular 17/1594), extending their scope to cover "activities supporting finance activity". The updated version is published on SAMA's website and applies from the circular's date. Distributed to banks, finance and real estate finance companies, finance-support companies, credit information companies, exchange businesses, and payment companies and systems.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, BNPL/finance, payments, data/credit info

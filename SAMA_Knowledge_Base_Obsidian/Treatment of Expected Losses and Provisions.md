# Treatment of Expected Losses and Provisions

Node on the recognition of expected losses and provisions in regulatory capital, sitting alongside the Pillar 3 historical loss disclosure (Template OR1) for operational risk. The extracted content specifies the ten-year aggregate loss data, loss event thresholds (SAR 44,600 and SAR 446,000), exclusions and their role in the internal loss multiplier and operational risk capital calculation. It binds banks in the relevant business indicator buckets and those with SAMA approval to use internal loss data.

### [[SAMA Guidance Document Implementation of Basel III]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** The treatment of expected losses and provisions is not a free-standing accounting question; it is a component of the Basel-based capital framework SAMA has made binding on banks in the Kingdom on both a standalone and consolidated basis. The guidance document is the parent instrument that gives the EL calculation, the provision calculation and their comparison their enforceable force and their scope of application. So when assessing a bank's regulatory capital position, apply the EL-versus-provisions rules as SAMA-mandated capital requirements rather than as optional Basel commentary, and check the applicable version and any SAMA-specific adjustments in the guidance document.

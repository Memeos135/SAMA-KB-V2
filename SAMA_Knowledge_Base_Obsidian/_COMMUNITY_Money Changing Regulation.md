# Money Changing Regulation

## Why this community

Licensing and conduct regime for money changers in KSA, spanning market entry, AML/CTF duties and customer-facing protections for a sector treated as higher-risk for financial crime.

## How members connect

- The Rules Regulating Money Changing Business is the governing instrument; licensing, AML/CTF and customer protection are its obligation streams.
- Cross-regime interaction: AML/CTF duties here layer on the national AML Law and its Implementing Regulation, so the sector rules supplement rather than replace CDD, record-keeping and STR obligations.
- Licensing conditions are continuing conditions — AML/CTF and consumer-protection breaches feed back into licence suspension or withdrawal.
- The AML term 'Customer' and the consumer-protection addressee overlap but are not identical; walk-in exchange transactions raise occasional-customer and threshold questions.

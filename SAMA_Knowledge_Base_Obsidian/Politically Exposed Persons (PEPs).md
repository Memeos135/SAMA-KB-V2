# Politically Exposed Persons (PEPs)

This node covers the PEP element of customer due diligence within SAMA's AML/CTF risk-based approach guidance, requiring financial institutions to take reasonable measures to determine whether a customer or beneficial owner is a politically exposed person, whether foreign or domestic. Where the PEP relationship is high risk, enhanced due diligence must be applied and extended to family members and close associates. It binds financial institutions supervised by SAMA and links to Article 8 of the AML Law and its Implementing Regulations, which require internal procedures and tools for PEP identification.

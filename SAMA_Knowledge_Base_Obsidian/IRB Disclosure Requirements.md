# IRB Disclosure Requirements

This node covers Pillar 3 disclosure obligations for banks using IRB approaches, including template CR7 on the effect of credit derivatives on IRB risk-weighted assets, and the overarching disclosure principles (meaningful, consistent over time, comparable across banks, proportionate to complexity). It also requires a formal board-approved disclosure policy and assurance arrangements for Pillar 3 data. It binds SAMA-supervised banks, with CR7 mandatory for those on F-IRB or A-IRB.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

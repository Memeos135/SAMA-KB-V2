# Technical and Tactical CTI Principles

The Technical and Tactical CTI layer of SAMA's Cyber Threat Intelligence Principles, covering identification and handling of technical components and indicators of cyber attacks. It sits alongside the Core, Strategic and Operational domains within the same instrument and feeds detection and mitigation activity, with outputs classified under the Traffic Light Protocol per SAMA's Cybersecurity Communication Protocols. Applies to SAMA Member Organizations, including those with outsourced CTI capability.

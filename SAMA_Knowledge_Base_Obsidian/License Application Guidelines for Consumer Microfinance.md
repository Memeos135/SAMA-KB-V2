# License Application Guidelines for Consumer Microfinance

SAMA guidelines on how to apply for a licence to engage in consumer microfinance activity under the Finance Companies Control Law and its Implementing Regulation. They set application submission rules, the definition of Applicant (founders/shareholders), the accuracy obligation with false or omitted material information treated as a violation of Article 7 and grounds for rejection or licence cancellation, and the checklist of required documents (articles of association, shareholder list, organizational structure, feasibility study and business plan, fit and proper forms for founders, 5%+ owners and board/committee candidates, and an irrevocable bank guarantee equal to 20% of minimum capital).

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, governance/risk

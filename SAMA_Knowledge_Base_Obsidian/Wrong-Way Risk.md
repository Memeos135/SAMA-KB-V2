# Wrong-Way Risk

Node addressing general and specific wrong-way risk in counterparty credit risk management: banks must identify exposures where risk factors are positively correlated with counterparty creditworthiness, design stress tests and scenario analysis accordingly, and monitor and report wrong-way risk by product, region and industry to senior management and the board or its delegated committee. It links wrong-way risk identification to the stress-testing and risk-appetite framework. Binds SAMA-supervised banks with derivative, SFT and other counterparty exposures.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

# National Address for Postal

## Why this community

Obligation on supervised entities to capture and use the Saudi National Address for customer correspondence and postal delivery, supporting identification and reliable customer communication.

## How members connect

- Single-node topic: the requirement stands alone in the graph, with no internal cross-references extracted.
- Read it as an adjunct to customer identification/record-keeping and consumer-notification duties, since address data feeds both CDD records and service of notices.
- Scope should be checked against the specific instrument that imposes it before extending it across regimes.

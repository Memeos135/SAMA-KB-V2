# Founding Day Holiday 1447H

A SAMA circular declaring the Founding Day (22 February) official holiday for 1447H and fixing the corresponding Sunday as a non-working day for supervised financial institutions. It also requires banks, currency exchange centers and payment service providers to keep designated branches at airports and border crossings operating during the holiday, notify SAMA of those branch names and hours, and publicise them. It binds all financial institutions under SAMA supervision.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, payments, other

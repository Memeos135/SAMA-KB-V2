# Specific Risk

Concept node addressing issuer-specific market risk and the boundary/internal risk transfer rules that determine whether banking book credit, equity and interest rate exposures hedged internally receive capital recognition in the trading book. It sets conditions for exact matching with external third-party hedges, treatment of the internal risk transfer desk, and the consequence where conditions are not met (external hedge fully capitalised, trading book leg excluded). Binds banks operating trading desks and transferring risk between banking and trading books.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

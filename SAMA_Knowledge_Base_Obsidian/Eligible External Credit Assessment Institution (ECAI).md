# Eligible External Credit Assessment Institution (ECAI)

This node covers the eligibility and use of External Credit Assessment Institutions under SAMA's Minimum Capital Requirements for Credit Risk. It sets recognition criteria (including resources, credibility, disclosure of conflicts of interest and cooperation with SAMA through notification of methodology changes and data access) and the stricter conditions for using ECAI ratings on securitization exposures, notably free, non-selective public availability of ratings and methodologies, demonstrated securitization expertise, treatment of multiple differing ratings, and no double-counting where credit risk mitigation is already reflected in the rating. It binds banks relying on external ratings for risk weighting and conditions ECAI recognition by SAMA.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

# Complaints Handling

The complaints-handling and related privacy/competition/outsourcing elements of SAMA's consumer protection framework. It requires financial institutions to offer multiple accessible complaint channels (toll-free number, branches/website, apps, email), publish the complaint mechanism, issue reference numbers and handling periods by SMS, document communication channels and retain records for at least five years, and resolve complaints fairly and without delay. Principle 6 additionally ties consumer data privacy mechanisms to the Personal Data Protection Law.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, data/credit info, governance/risk

# SAMA Logo Use Prohibition

## Why this community

Protection of SAMA's identity and the avoidance of implied regulatory endorsement: supervised entities are barred from using the SAMA logo (and, by extension, any suggestion of SAMA sponsorship) in their materials.

## How members connect

- Single-instrument cluster; a prohibition addressed to all supervised entities rather than a regime-specific rule.
- Doctrinally adjacent to consumer-protection and advertising/disclosure obligations, since the harm addressed is misleading consumers about official backing.
- Licensing status may be stated as a fact where required, but that is distinct from logo use, which remains prohibited.

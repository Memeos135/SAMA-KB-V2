# Regulatory Retail Exposures

Definitional node within SAMA's standardised approach to credit risk setting out when a retail exposure qualifies as "regulatory retail" — product type, an absolute size cap per counterparty (SAR 4.46 million), and a granularity limit of 0.2% of the overall regulatory retail portfolio (excluding defaulted exposures). It also positions regulatory retail against "other retail" and drives the applicable risk weights (75% generally, 45% for transactor exposures, 100% for other retail). Binds banks calculating risk-weighted assets for credit risk under SAMA's minimum capital requirements.

**Regimes:** banking prudential

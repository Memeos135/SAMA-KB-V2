# Payment Initiation Service

This node covers payment initiation service as regulated under the Implementing Regulation of the Payments and Payment Services Law, in particular the part setting requirements for providing payment account information and payment initiation services. It is relevant to the open-banking style licensing category, contractual and information disclosure duties, and the scope-of-application provisions for licensees. It binds SAMA-licensed payment service providers offering initiation services and, indirectly, account-holding institutions.

**Regimes:** payments, consumer protection

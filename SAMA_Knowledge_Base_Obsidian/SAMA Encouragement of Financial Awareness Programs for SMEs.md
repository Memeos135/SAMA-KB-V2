# SAMA Encouragement of Financial Awareness Programs for SMEs

A SAMA circular encouraging all financiers to develop periodic financial awareness programs tailored to small and medium enterprises, aligned with the Financial Sector Development Program under Vision 2030, and to use varied awareness channels. It asks institutions to submit a quarterly report on their contribution to a designated SAMA email address. Addressed to banks and finance companies; framed as encouragement rather than a hard prohibition, but with a reporting expectation.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, BNPL/finance, other

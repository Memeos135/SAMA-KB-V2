# Rules for Opening Accounts for Juristic Persons (300)

Section 300 sets the documentary and authorisation requirements for opening accounts for juristic persons, split between resident entities (licensed businesses and shops, SPEs, foreign schools, e-commerce without premises, freelance permit holders, money changers, all company forms, foreign-investment entities, non-profit and public bodies, diplomatic missions and liquidation/restructuring accounts) and non-resident entities (GCC and non-GCC companies, banks, funds, insurers, money changers and payment card companies). It also covers specialised accounts such as real estate escrow, payment companies' client collection accounts and debt-based crowdfunding collection accounts. It binds banks onboarding corporate and institutional customers in the Kingdom.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, banking prudential, payments, governance/risk

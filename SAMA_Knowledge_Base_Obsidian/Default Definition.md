# Default Definition

Definitional node from SAMA's rules on credit risk exposure classification and provisioning for finance companies, setting out the IFRS 9-aligned vocabulary (probability of default, exposure at default, loss given default, financial asset, expected credit loss and 12-month/lifetime ECL, staging) and, separately, SAMA's regulatory definition of default. It binds licensed finance companies, which must adopt the regulatory default definition consistently for regulatory and financial reporting purposes or document justified reasons for departure. Default is triggered by either qualitative 'unlikeliness to pay' indicators (Stage 3B allocation, charge-off/specific provision, sale at material credit loss, concessionary restructuring, bankruptcy or enforcement action) or the quantitative 90-days-past-due criterion.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, governance/risk, banking prudential

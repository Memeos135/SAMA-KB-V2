# Real Estate Finance Law (M/50)

Node anchored on the Real Estate Finance Law (Royal Decree M/50) as the statutory basis SAMA invokes when issuing subordinate rules for the real estate finance sector; the attached source is SAMA's circular and Rules for Regulating Real Estate Refinance Companies, approved by Governor decision and applicable to SAMA-licensed refinance companies. It defines the core terms of the sector (real estate financier, real estate finance contract, beneficiary, primary and secondary market, refinancing activity) and covers capital adequacy and liquidity, governance, permitted activities, internal organisation, outsourcing, risk management, compliance, internal audit, SAMA supervision and secondary-market dealings. It binds licensed real estate refinance companies, and is addressed for information to banks and finance companies operating in the Kingdom.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, governance/risk, banking prudential

# Rules for Opening Accounts for Natural Persons Inside Saudi Arabia (200.1)

Section 200.1 of SAMA's Rules Governing the Opening of Bank Accounts and General Operational Guidelines sets the account-opening and identification requirements for natural persons resident in Saudi Arabia, broken down by category (Saudi nationals, GCC nationals, expatriates, tribe members, Beluchis and Turkistanians, airline/ship crew, salary-payment prepaid records, credit cards for non-resident employees, and blood-money collection accounts). It prescribes acceptable identity documents, authorised signatories/delegation limits and risk classification at onboarding. It binds banks and other SAMA-supervised entities licensed to take deposits in the Kingdom.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, banking prudential, governance/risk

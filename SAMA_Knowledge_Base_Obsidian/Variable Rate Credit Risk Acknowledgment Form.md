# Variable Rate Credit Risk Acknowledgment Form

Prescribed acknowledgment form annexed to the real estate finance circular, by which an individual customer accepts the credit risks of variable-rate (variable term cost) real estate finance; it must be included in the finance offer pack in the attached format for variable-cost products. The lender must document the customer's receipt of the form and allow the customer to take the documents away for independent advice, and cannot conclude the contract otherwise. Binds banks and real estate finance companies offering variable-rate products to individuals.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, BNPL/finance

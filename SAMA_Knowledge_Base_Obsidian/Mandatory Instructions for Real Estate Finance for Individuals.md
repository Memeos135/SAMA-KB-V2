# Mandatory Instructions for Real Estate Finance for Individuals

SAMA circular imposing mandatory pre-contract conduct requirements on banks and real estate finance companies before offering residential real estate finance to individuals: affordability/creditworthiness assessment against approved credit policy, a clear explanation of terms and risks by a competent officer, a written finance offer valid for at least fifteen business days containing printed copies of all documents in final form, an explanation by an independent qualified credit advisor (not the officer who dealt with or issued the offer), and a reflection period of at least five business days before contracting. Contracting is prohibited unless all steps are completed and documented in the finance file; full compliance was required by 12/08/2018.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, BNPL/finance

# RWA for Operational Risk

A concept node on risk-weighted assets and Pillar 1 capital requirements for operational risk as reported in SAMA's Basel-aligned RWA overview template. The surrounding instructions position operational risk as one line of the total RWA build-up alongside credit risk, counterparty credit risk, CVA, equity, settlement and securitisation exposures, with capital normally computed as 8% of RWA subject to floors or jurisdictional scaling. It binds banks subject to SAMA capital adequacy and disclosure requirements.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

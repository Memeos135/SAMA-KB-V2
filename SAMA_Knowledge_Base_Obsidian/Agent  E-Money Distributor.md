# Agent / E-Money Distributor

Concept node for agents and electronic money distributors under the Implementing Regulation of the Payments and Payment Services Law, addressed in a dedicated part of the Regulation. It concerns the conditions for a licensee to appoint and use agents/distributors, the licensee's continuing responsibility for their conduct, and related oversight expectations. It binds licensed payment service providers and e-money issuers that operate through third parties, and is relevant to AML/CTF and outsourcing analysis of agent networks.

**Regimes:** payments, AML/CTF, governance/risk, consumer protection

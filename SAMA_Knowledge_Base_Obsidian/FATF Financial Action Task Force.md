# FATF Financial Action Task Force

A concept node anchored in SAMA's guide for regulating the operations of exchange houses (money changers), where FATF is defined as the intergovernmental standard-setter for AML/CTF and proliferation-financing measures, and its public statements on high-risk/non-cooperative jurisdictions are pointed to as a reference source. The surrounding material sets out exchange-house obligations on enhanced due diligence, screening membership-relationship customer names against asset-freeze lists (including UN Security Council designations and other national/bloc lists), transaction monitoring for activity without clear economic or lawful purpose, and reporting suspicions to the FIU or police. It binds licensed Category (A) and Category (B) exchange houses; the appendix also lists reference links to the Saudi AML Law and Bylaws, FATF, Basel and UN sources.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, sanctions/TFS, governance/risk

# Shari'ah Committee

Defines the Shari'ah Committee as the body responsible for supervising compliance with Shari'ah principles and rules and their application within the bank, with 'Shari'ah Compliant' meaning compliance with that committee's decisions. Related definitions include Islamic Window (the branch or dedicated unit of a conventional bank conducting Shari'ah compliant business) and Investment Account Holders. These terms scope the PSIA Rules, which apply to local Saudi banks licensed under the Banking Control Law that conduct Shari'ah compliant banking.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential

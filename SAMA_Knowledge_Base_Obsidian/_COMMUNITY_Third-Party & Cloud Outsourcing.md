# Third-Party & Cloud Outsourcing

## Why this community

Supervisory expectations for risk that a regulated entity transfers to external parties — outsourcing arrangements, cloud hosting, vendor contracting and third-party cyber exposure — where accountability remains with the licensed institution.

## How members connect

- Common control logic: materiality assessment, SAMA notification/no-objection where required, and non-delegable accountability for outsourced functions.
- Contract and vendor management supplies the enforceable hooks (audit and access rights, data location, sub-contracting, exit) that the outsourcing and cloud requirements rely on.
- Third-party cyber security cross-references both outsourcing and cloud computing, treating the vendor lifecycle as an extension of the institution's own control environment.
- Hierarchy runs from framework-level governance requirements down to arrangement-specific rules for cloud and vendor onboarding.

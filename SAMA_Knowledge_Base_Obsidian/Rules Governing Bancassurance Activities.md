# Rules Governing Bancassurance Activities

SAMA's Rules Governing Bancassurance Activities, defining key terms (Company, Bank, Bancassurance Activities, Agreement, Authorised Employee, Client) and setting the scope, objective and core requirements for marketing and distributing insurance products through banks. The rules regulate the bank–insurer relationship, requiring the activity to be conducted directly through the bank as a distribution channel rather than as an insurance agency relationship. They bind licensed banks and licensed cooperative insurance companies, with the Cooperative Insurance Companies Control Law, the Banking Control Law and other SAMA instructions applying residually.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, governance/risk, banking prudential

### [[Banking Control Law]] — `cites` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** For a bank distributing insurance, the Banking Control Law is the source of SAMA's authority to condition and restrict that activity, and the Rules Governing Bancassurance Activities set the specific conditions for how banks may market and sell insurance products. The decision point is therefore twofold: the activity must be permissible for the bank under its banking authorisation, and the distribution conduct must meet the bancassurance conditions. This is a cross-regime interaction with the insurance supervisory framework, so breaches can be assessed under both the banking and insurance regimes.

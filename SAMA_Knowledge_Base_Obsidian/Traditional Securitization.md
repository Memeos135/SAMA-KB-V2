# Traditional Securitization

Defines traditional securitisation for capital purposes: a structure where cash flows from an underlying pool service at least two stratified tranches of differing credit risk, with investor payments dependent on pool performance rather than on the originator's obligation, and where junior tranches absorb losses without interrupting payments to senior tranches (distinguishing it from ordinary senior/subordinated debt). Sits within the scope provision requiring banks to apply the securitisation framework based on economic substance rather than legal form, with SAMA consultation encouraged in doubtful cases. Binds banks determining regulatory capital on securitisation exposures.

**Regimes:** banking prudential

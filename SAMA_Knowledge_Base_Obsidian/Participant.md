# Participant

Covers the Participant (natural or legal person funding an Institutional Beneficiary through the platform) and the protections attached to that status: a complaints-handling function with SAMA-specified response times, staff confidentiality of Participant data and transactions surviving termination of employment or licence, and exposure caps limiting a Participant to 25% of a requested Finance Amount and SAR 250,000 of aggregate outstanding financing (Eligible Participants excluded). Binds the licensed Debt-Based Crowdfunding Company and its employees.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, consumer protection, data/credit info, governance/risk

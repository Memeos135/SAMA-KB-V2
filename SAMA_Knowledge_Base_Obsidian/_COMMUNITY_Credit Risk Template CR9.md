# Credit Risk Template CR9

## Why this community

A single standardised credit-risk disclosure template within the Pillar 3 / public disclosure framework, prescribing the fixed format and data fields a bank must publish for its credit risk exposures.

## How members connect

- Isolated node: the template is a form-level instrument that operationalises the broader credit risk disclosure requirements rather than creating standalone obligations.
- Hierarchy point: template sits at the lowest tier (framework rules -> disclosure requirements -> prescribed template), so its content is only enforceable as read with the parent disclosure rules.
- Read together with the disclosure frequency/timing rules, which fix when this template must be published.

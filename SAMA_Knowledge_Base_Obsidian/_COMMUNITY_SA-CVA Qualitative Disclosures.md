# SA-CVA Qualitative Disclosures

## Why this community

Narrative disclosure specific to banks using the standardised approach for CVA risk (SA-CVA), covering governance, risk management and the conditions of use of that approach.

## How members connect

- Single-member cluster that is conditional on approach eligibility: the obligation only bites where the bank is approved for / applies SA-CVA.
- Legally downstream of the general CVA qualitative disclosure — read together as general-then-specific within the same disclosure regime.
- Shares defined terms and scope boundaries with the CVA capital requirement rules and counterparty credit risk framework.

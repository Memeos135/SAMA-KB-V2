# Advertising Policy

The conduct and consent provisions of SAMA's Rules for Advertising Products and Services (Articles 8–14), covering fair competition and sector reputation, prohibition on using customer personal data in advertising without written consent, a ban on advertising insurance or credit products to individuals under 18 Hijri years or where unsuitable, opt-in consent and easy permanent opt-out for marketing channels, the binding effect of advertised offers and specifications on the institution and their inclusion in the contract, use of official channels or licensed third parties with the institution retaining responsibility for third-party non-compliance, and the requirement to adopt an internal advertising policy aligned with SAMA rules. Binds all SAMA-supervised financial institutions.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, data/credit info, governance/risk

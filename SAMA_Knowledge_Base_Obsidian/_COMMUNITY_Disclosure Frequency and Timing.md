# Disclosure Frequency and Timing

## Why this community

Timing dimension of the public disclosure regime: how often and by when supervised banks must publish Pillar 3 information, and how disclosure cadence tracks reporting periods.

## How members connect

- Isolated node that functions as a cross-cutting timing obligation applying to the full set of disclosure templates, including credit risk templates.
- Compliance consequence: frequency/timing determines the deadline against which any individual template's publication is assessed.

# New Banking Products and Services Regulation

SAMA's New Banking Products and Services Regulation (November 2023, v2.0), replacing the 2017 guidelines, setting minimum requirements for how banks identify, assess, monitor and manage risks arising from new or materially modified products and services, and when they must notify SAMA or obtain non-objection before launch. It applies to all banks licensed under the Banking Control Law and defines "product or service", "new product or service" and "existing product or service". Consult it for pre-launch regulatory clearance, governance and post-launch monitoring/audit duties.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential, consumer protection

- **What this link tells you:** Before launching anything new, treat the New Banking Products and Services Regulation as a supervisory gate that operates on the authority SAMA derives from the Banking Control Law over banks' permitted activities. Practically, product design is not a purely internal commercial decision: the notification or no-objection process must be completed in the prescribed form before the product goes to market. - **What this link tells you:** When scoping internal audit coverage of new products, the benchmark is not only the institution's own product policies but the SAMA new-products regime itself.

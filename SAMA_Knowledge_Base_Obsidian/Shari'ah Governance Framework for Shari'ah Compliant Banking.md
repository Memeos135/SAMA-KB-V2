# Shari'ah Governance Framework for Shari'ah Compliant Banking

The Shari'ah Governance Framework for Shari'ah Compliant Banking issued by SAMA in February 2020, referenced as the companion instrument that must be read together with the Risk Management Framework for Shari'ah Compliant Banking and any subsequent updates. It is the governance-side source for the Shari'ah Committee's role in approving Shari'ah rules and principles applied to a bank's products and activities, which the risk framework then treats as a compliance and operational risk obligation. It binds SAMA-licensed banks conducting Shari'ah compliant banking activities; the full text is not contained in this node's context.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential

- **What this link tells you:** Risk management and Shari'ah governance are designed as paired obligations: the Risk Management Framework sets expectations for identifying, measuring, monitoring, reporting and controlling risks unique to Shari'ah compliant activities, while the Shari'ah Governance Framework supplies the governance structures, including board and senior management oversight, through which those controls are exercised. Neither can be assessed in isolation, since Shari'ah non-compliance risk is itself managed through the governance arrangements. For a compliance decision, confirm that risk reporting lines and Shari'ah governance bodies are mutually consistent, as a defect in one framework will usually surface as a breach under the other.

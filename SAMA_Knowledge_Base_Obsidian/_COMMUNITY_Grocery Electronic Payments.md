# Grocery Electronic Payments

## Why this community

Mandating or expanding acceptance of electronic payment at grocery and retail food outlets, addressing cash displacement in a high-volume consumer sector.

## How members connect

- Standalone directive addressed to acquirers, PSPs and, through them, merchants in the defined retail category.
- Depends on the POS device and acceptance infrastructure standards handled elsewhere in the payments cluster.
- Consumer protection angle: merchants may not refuse or surcharge electronic payment where acceptance is mandated.

# Bancassurance Agreement

The defined term "Agreement" in SAMA's draft Rules Governing Bancassurance Activities: the contract under which an insurance Company and a licensed Bank agree that the Bank will carry out Bancassurance Activities. The node also carries the surrounding definitions, scope, objective and practice requirements, including that Bancassurance Activities must be conducted directly through the Bank as one of the Company's marketing and distribution channels and that the contractual relationship must not amount to an insurance agency relationship. It binds both the insurance Company and the Bank.

**Regimes:** other, governance/risk, consumer protection

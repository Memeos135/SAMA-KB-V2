# Table ORA - General Qualitative Operational Risk

Table ORA is the general qualitative Pillar 3 disclosure on a bank's operational risk management framework under SAMA's Pillar 3 Disclosure Requirements Framework. Banks must describe their operational risk policies and guidelines, the structure of the operational risk management and control function, the measurement system used for the capital charge, reporting to executive management and the board, and mitigation/transfer arrangements including insurance and outsourcing policy. It is mandatory for all SAMA-supervised banks, annually, in flexible format.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential

# Implementation Rules for Banking Control Law

These Implementation Rules, issued by Ministerial Resolution under the Banking Control Law and circulated by SAMA, operationalise Articles 12, 16, 17, 18 and 22 of that Law. They cover fitness and prior SAMA approval for bank board members and senior managers (including multiple-board prohibition and disclosure of prior liquidation or removal), conduct of banking business consistent with monetary and credit policy, periodic supervisory and statistical reporting to SAMA, inspection and staff conduct requirements, and the procedures and penalties SAMA may apply. They bind all banks operating in Saudi Arabia and their employees.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

# Royal Decree M/142 - CFT Amendment

Royal Decree No. M/142 dated 19/10/1441 AH enacting the amendment to Article 12 of the Law on Combating the Financing of Terrorism, limiting provisional release of persons accused under that Law to temporary release ordered by the Public Prosecution subject to investigation and flight-risk conditions. It is the enacting instrument for Council of Ministers Resolution 637 and directs ministers and heads of independent entities to implement it within their competences.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, other

# Oversight Framework for Payment Systems and Operators

An extract from SAMA's oversight framework for payment systems and their operators, stating that SAMA is the competent authority for regulating, supervising and overseeing payment systems and operators in the Kingdom under the Payment Systems Law and its implementing regulation. Oversight is described as monitoring and assessing existing and planned systems against safety and efficiency objectives and requiring changes where needed, with standards set by reference to the CPMI-IOSCO Principles for Financial Market Infrastructures (PFMI). It is relevant to operators of payment systems and participants subject to SAMA oversight.

# Unified Complaint Response Template

A SAMA circular mandating use of a unified template for responding to complaints received through the SAMA Cares ("Sama Tahtam") system, issued after SAMA observed quality deficiencies in responses. It prescribes response formats by category — requesting missing documents, informing the customer of the outcome and corrective action taken with supporting documents, and responding to complaints escalated to SAMA with reference to the specific instruction, contract or terms relied on — and requires institutions to strengthen their customer care function and maintain and periodically measure a service-level agreement for timely complaint handling. It binds all financial institutions supervised by SAMA.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, governance/risk

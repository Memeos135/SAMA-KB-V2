# Materially Dependent on Cash Flows

Defines when a regulatory real estate exposure (residential or commercial) is treated as "materially dependent on cash flows generated by the property" — i.e. where loan servicing and recovery on default depend on lease, rental or sale proceeds from the secured property rather than the borrower's other repayment capacity, with an indicative income threshold and expectation that it applies mainly to corporates, MSMEs and SPVs. The classification drives which risk-weight table applies under the standardised approach for credit risk, including the higher LTV-banded weights for commercial exposures and the 150% weight for cash-flow-dependent "other real estate". It binds SAMA-licensed banks calculating minimum capital requirements for credit risk.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

# Banking Disputes Committee Requests Circular

A SAMA circular requiring banks and finance companies to respond to document and information requests from the General Secretariat of the Banking and Finance Disputes and Violations Committees for case preparation, within the deadlines set out in an annexed schedule (generally three, seven, ten or fifteen business days by document type). Where a deadline cannot be met, the institution must reply immediately explaining why and state a supply date not exceeding 60 days from the request. SAMA states it will take regulatory action for non-compliance; it binds all banks and finance companies operating in the Kingdom.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, consumer protection, BNPL/finance

# Digital Identity Acceptance

## Why this community

Recognition of government-issued digital identity and digital attestation channels (Absher, Tawakkalna) as acceptable proof of identity, family status and contract certification by supervised finance entities. Sits at the intersection of customer identification/KYC evidence rules and consumer-facing contracting formalities.

## How members connect

- All three items address what counts as an acceptable identification or certification instrument, extending the traditional national ID/paper-document baseline to state digital platforms.
- The family record and digital identity circulars are directly cross-referenced, the former building on the identity-acceptance position for a specific document type.
- Digital certification of finance company products relies on the same acceptance premise: if the digital identity is valid for verification, it can underpin electronic execution and attestation of product documentation.
- Practical consequence spans regimes: CDD/identification evidence under AML/CTF, and documentation/disclosure duties toward the Consumer in finance company dealings.

# Business Impact Analysis and Risk Assessment

The BCM Framework's requirements for Business Impact Analysis and Risk Assessment: member organisations must define and maintain a BIA/RA methodology and periodically identify, assess and treat threats (including single points of failure across people, process, technology and premises) that could disrupt critical activities. The BIA must prioritise products, services, functions and processes and determine financial, operational, customer, legal and regulatory impacts, RTOs, RPOs and MAO, interdependencies and recovery resources. Results and the prioritised list must be endorsed by the BCM committee and periodically updated.

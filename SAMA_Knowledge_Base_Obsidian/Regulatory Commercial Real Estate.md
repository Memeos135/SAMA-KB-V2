# Regulatory Commercial Real Estate

Defines "regulatory commercial real estate" residually as any regulatory real estate exposure that is not regulatory residential real estate, and locates it within the four-bucket regulatory real estate taxonomy split by material dependence on property cash flows. Determines which risk weight schedule applies to commercial property-secured lending. Binds banks applying the standardised approach for credit risk.

**Regimes:** banking prudential

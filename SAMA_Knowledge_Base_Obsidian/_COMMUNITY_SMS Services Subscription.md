# SMS Services Subscription

## Why this community

SAMA instruction on subscription to SMS notification services, i.e. the institution's duty to enrol and alert customers about account and transaction activity through text messaging.

## How members connect

- Single-member cluster: a standalone circular with no internal cross-links captured in the graph.
- Its legal weight comes from the consumer protection regime — customer notification supports transparency, unauthorised-transaction detection and dispute/complaint rights.
- Read alongside advertising/disclosure and payments rules, as notification duties interact with consent, data confidentiality and fee-disclosure obligations.

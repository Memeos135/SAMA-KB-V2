# Consumer Financing Regulations

## Why this community

The consumer finance regime governing finance providers' dealings with individual borrowers: pre-contract disclosure, cost-of-credit transparency (APR), contractual terms, borrower treatment, and complaint/dispute handling.

## How members connect

- The Consumer Financing Regulations are the parent instrument; the APR annex, the borrower-dealing article and the dispute-resolution article are internal components that give effect to its disclosure and conduct obligations.
- Shared defined terms (Consumer/Borrower, finance contract, cost of credit) bind the members: the APR methodology in the annex is the calculation basis for the disclosure duties in the main text.
- The Finance Contract Summary Form is the prescribed disclosure vehicle referencing the Regulations — a form-level obligation chain from rule to standardised customer-facing document.
- Cross-regime note: the consumer-protection concept of Borrower/Consumer is not co-extensive with the AML concept of Customer; CDD duties run in parallel and are not satisfied by consumer-disclosure compliance.

# Residential Real Estate Finance Data Sharing with SIMAH

A SAMA circular (Arabic) directing all financing entities to share individuals' residential real estate finance data with the Saudi Credit Bureau (SIMAH), in line with forthcoming SAMA guidance and templates. It is grounded in the SAMA Law, Credit Information Law, Banking Control Law and Finance Companies Control Law, and is aimed at credit-risk transparency and credit assessment; compliance with the Personal Data Protection Law and other regulators' instructions is expressly preserved. It binds banks, real estate finance companies and credit information companies, effective from its date.

**Regimes:** data/credit info, BNPL/finance, consumer protection

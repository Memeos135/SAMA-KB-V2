# D-SIB Assessment Methodology

The assessment methodology component of the D-SIB framework: an indicator-based scoring approach reflecting size, interconnectedness, substitutability and complexity, calibrated to the operational dynamics of the Saudi banking system and the externalities a bank's failure would impose domestically and cross-border. It produces the scores used for D-SIB designation and bucket allocation. Applies to banks assessed by SAMA's Banking Supervision Department.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

# Registration/Onboarding Controls

Section 3 of the Minimum Verification Controls governs registration and onboarding of users by member organizations offering e-wallet, lending, crowdfunding or other fintech services. It requires one-to-one linkage of a phone number or National ID/Iqama to a single application, a secure user validation process, strong authentication from an independent licensed trusted party (National Single Sign-On for e-wallets; an equivalent trusted-party authentication for lending platforms and analogous robustness for other models), verification that the mobile number is registered to the same user through a trusted party, OTP verification sent to that verified number, session timeout, SMS notification of registration and account status changes, single-device binding or per-login OTP with no concurrent sessions, and secure deactivation/reactivation and device re-registration processes. These are identity-verification and authentication duties that sit alongside, not in place of, AML/CTF customer identification and verification obligations.

**Regimes:** payments, BNPL/finance, governance/risk, AML/CTF

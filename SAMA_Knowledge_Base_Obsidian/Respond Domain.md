# Respond Domain

The Respond domain of SAMA's Counter-Fraud Framework, governing the reaction to actual or suspected fraud incidents to limit losses and support recovery. It requires a defined, approved, implemented and maintained Fraud Response Plan, aligned where appropriate with enterprise incident management, monitored for compliance and effectiveness, and requiring prompt and competent assessment, investigation and resolution, with minimum content on alerting and reporting channels for customers, employees and third parties, roles and responsibilities, and decision-making authority and escalation/referral procedures inside and outside the organisation. It binds Member Organisations, including their investigation and customer-resolution functions.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, consumer protection

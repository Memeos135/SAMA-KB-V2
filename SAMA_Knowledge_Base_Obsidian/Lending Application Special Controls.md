# Lending Application Special Controls

Section 5 of the Minimum Verification Controls imposes additional obligations on lending companies over and above the onboarding and general controls: assurance that the recipient IBAN belongs to the loan requester, use of a trusted and authorized digital signature provider, and secure creation, storage and management of promissory notes through a national trusted party such as Nafith. It binds SAMA-supervised member organizations providing lending products via digital applications, and interacts with finance/BNPL contracting and enforceability of security instruments.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, governance/risk, payments

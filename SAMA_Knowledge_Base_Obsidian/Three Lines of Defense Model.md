# Three Lines of Defense Model

Concept node covering the first/second/third line structure underlying SAMA's Internal Audit Principles for finance companies and real estate refinance companies: business units that own and take risk within limits, control and oversight units, and the independent internal audit department. It matters mainly for the required relationship and demarcation between internal audit and the first and second line units, so that assurance stays independent of risk-taking and control functions. Binds SAMA-licensed finance and real estate refinance companies.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, BNPL/finance

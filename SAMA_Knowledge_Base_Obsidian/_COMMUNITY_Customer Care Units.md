# Customer Care Units

## Why this community

Consumer protection governance inside financial institutions: the mandatory establishment, structure and staffing of a customer care function covering complaints, quality analysis and financial awareness.

## How members connect

- The establishment controls act as the parent obligation and cross-refer to each mandated sub-unit, creating a structural hierarchy.
- Complaint Handling, Quality and Performance Analysis, and Financial Awareness units are the prescribed components — each carries its own functional mandate under the same instrument.
- Professional certification requirements attach to staff of the complaint-handling function, linking fitness/competence duties to the consumer protection regime.
- Consequence chain: failure to constitute the units or certify staff is a supervisory breach independent of any individual complaint outcome.

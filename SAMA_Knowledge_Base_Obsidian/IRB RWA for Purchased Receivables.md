# IRB RWA for Purchased Receivables

Concept node for the IRB treatment of purchased receivables, covering risk-weighted assets for default risk and for dilution risk, treatment of purchase price discounts and recognition of credit risk mitigants, together with eligibility conditions for the asset class. Purchased receivables also appear as a separate F-IRB/A-IRB line in Pillar 3 credit risk and credit derivative CRM templates. Relevant to banks with IRB approval that acquire receivables portfolios.

**Regimes:** banking prudential

### [[Dilution Risk Treatment]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** For purchased receivables, dilution risk is a distinct capital charge that sits alongside default risk within the same IRB RWA computation, not an alternative to it. The framework's structure for purchased receivables expressly separates risk-weighted assets for default risk from risk-weighted assets for dilution risk, with further provisions on purchase price discounts and credit risk mitigants. The consequence is that a bank cannot treat dilution as immaterial by default; it must either quantify and capitalise it under the dilution provisions or demonstrate the conditions on which the framework permits it to be disregarded.

### [[Purchased Receivables Quantification]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** If you are deciding how much capital a purchased receivables portfolio attracts under the IRB approach, you cannot read the RWA calculation in isolation from the parameter-quantification rules for that same exposure class. Both sit in the same SAMA capital framework and use the same asset-class taxonomy, in which corporate purchased receivables are carved out of the general corporate class, so the quantification requirements define the PD/LGD/EAD inputs the RWA provisions then consume. Practically, an internal model that satisfies the RWA formula but not the quantification conditions for the receivables class is not compliant, and the exposure would need to fall back to the treatment SAMA prescribes.

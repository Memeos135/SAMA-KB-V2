# IMCC: Capital for Modellable Risk Factors

Node on the internally modelled capital charge for modellable risk factors (IMCC), the ES-based component of the IMA market risk capital requirement, alongside surrounding capital-adequacy reporting context such as RWA/minimum capital requirement templates and capital distribution constraint disclosures. Useful for understanding how modelled market risk capital aggregates into Pillar 1 requirements and disclosure. Binds banks with SAMA approval to use internal models.

**Regimes:** banking prudential

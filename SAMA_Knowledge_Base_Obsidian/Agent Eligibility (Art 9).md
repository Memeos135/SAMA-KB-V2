# Agent Eligibility (Art 9)

Article 9 sets the minimum eligibility conditions an entity must meet to be contracted as a banking Agent, including satisfying the Article 13 fit and proper requirements, holding a commercial register permitting Agent Banking activity, obtaining any approval from its own supervisory/regulatory authority before the Bank seeks SAMA's non-objection, and adequate IT infrastructure. The Bank must submit a CEO (or designated senior officer) declaration confirming the fit and proper assessment and that eligibility minimums are met. Binds Banks proposing Agents and, indirectly, prospective Agents.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

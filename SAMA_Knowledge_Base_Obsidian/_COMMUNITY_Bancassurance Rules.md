# Bancassurance Rules

## Why this community

The bancassurance regime: conditions under which banks may distribute insurance products of licensed insurance companies, and the allocation of duties between the two regulated parties.

## How members connect

- The Rules Governing Bancassurance Activities is the hub instrument, expressly referencing the required Bancassurance Agreement and the separate Bank Obligations and Company Obligations sets.
- The Rules cite the Cooperative Insurance Companies Control Law, anchoring the insurance-side licensing and supervisory basis (law -> rules -> contractual agreement).
- Bank Obligations and Company Obligations are the two mirrored duty sets that the Bancassurance Agreement must give contractual effect to, creating a cross-sector obligation chain over one customer relationship.
- Cross-regime flag: the bank's consumer protection and disclosure duties run alongside the insurer's product and conduct duties, so responsibility for customer-facing conduct must be read from both duty sets together.

# Fraud Risk Appetite

Requires Member Organisations to define a Fraud Risk Appetite stating tolerated fraud risk, derived from the Fraud Risk Assessment and aligned to overall risk appetite, with measures, thresholds and limits addressing impact on both the organisation and its customers. Breach of a customer-impacting limit triggers escalation to senior management and a CEO-level crisis management process with at least weekly meetings until resolution; the appetite must be reviewed annually, Board-endorsed and updated for material business model changes. Connects to the KRI requirements and the Fraud Risk Assessment methodology.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, consumer protection

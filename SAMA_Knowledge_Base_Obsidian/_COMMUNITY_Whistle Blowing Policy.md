# Whistle Blowing Policy

## Why this community

Whistleblowing and protected internal reporting within SAMA-supervised financial institutions: establishing channels for raising concerns about violations or misconduct and protecting those who report. A governance and conduct-control obligation rather than a prudential one.

## How members connect

- Standalone policy-level instrument with no internal edges; it is the parent obligation for institution-level whistleblowing arrangements rather than a member of an obligation chain in this cluster.
- Cross-regime relevance: interacts with governance/internal-control frameworks, AML/CTF suspicious-activity escalation, and consumer protection complaint handling — but those linkages are not captured here and should not be assumed as textual cross-references.
- Applies to institutions within SAMA's supervisory perimeter; the precise addressee scope and any confidentiality/non-retaliation protections must be read from the policy text itself.

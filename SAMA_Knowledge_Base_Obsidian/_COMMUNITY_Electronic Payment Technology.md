# Electronic Payment Technology

## Why this community

SAMA directives on the payment-acceptance technology layer — POS device standards and connectivity upgrades, use of locally sourced technology products, and extending electronic acceptance to charities and non-profit entities.

## How members connect

- Shared subject matter: the physical/technical POS estate that banks, payment service providers and merchant acquirers must deploy and maintain to SAMA specification.
- Obligation chain runs from acquirer/PSP to the merchant or beneficiary entity — the 4G upgrade instruction sets the device baseline that the charities/NPO acceptance directive relies on.
- The local technology products guidance sits alongside as a sourcing/procurement expectation applied to the same device and terminal population.
- Cross-regime touchpoint: NPO acceptance sits close to AML/CTF concerns over cash collection by non-profits, so acceptance mandates carry indirect financial-crime rationale.

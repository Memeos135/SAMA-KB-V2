# Investment Funds Indirect Participation

## Why this community

Treatment of investment funds that participate indirectly in financing activity - whether such participation falls inside the licensed finance activity perimeter and the conditions SAMA attaches.

## How members connect

- Isolated circular in this graph; it functions as a perimeter/scope clarification for finance companies and their funding structures.
- Decision relevance: it bears on whether an indirect funding arrangement triggers finance-company licensing and related conduct obligations, and on the boundary with capital-markets supervision of funds.

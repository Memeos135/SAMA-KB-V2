# Supervisory Slotting Approach for Specialized Lending

Concept node for the supervisory slotting approach, the fallback IRB treatment for specialised lending (project, object, commodities finance, income-producing real estate and HVCRE) where a bank cannot meet PD/LGD estimation requirements. It requires mapping of internal grades to five supervisory categories with prescribed unexpected-loss risk weights, permits SAMA-approved preferential weights for strong and good exposures with short residual maturity, and sets the expected loss calculation for slotted exposures. Applies to banks using IRB approaches for specialised lending portfolios.

**Regimes:** banking prudential

# Reduced Version BA-CVA (Kreduced)

Sets out the reduced version of the basic approach to CVA risk capital (Kreduced), which gives no recognition to counterparty credit spread hedges and is intended for banks that do not hedge CVA. Capital is the discounted aggregation across in-scope counterparties of stand-alone CVA capital, using a supervisory correlation parameter to combine systematic and idiosyncratic CVA risk components. It applies to banks calculating CVA capital under BA-CVA, and the same quantity also feeds the full BA-CVA calculation as a floor on hedging benefit.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

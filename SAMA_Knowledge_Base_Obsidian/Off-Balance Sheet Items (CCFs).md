# Off-Balance Sheet Items (CCFs)

Addresses off-balance sheet items in the leverage ratio (conversion of commitments and similar exposures via credit conversion factors), the settlement-date accounting treatment that routes exposures into the off-balance sheet rules, and the treatment of cash pooling arrangements where individual customer balances are or are not transferred daily into a single account balance. It also lists structures SAMA views as leverage-ratio-minimising. It binds banks computing the SAMA leverage ratio exposure measure.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

# Mudarabah Contract

Defines Mudarabah as a partnership contract where the capital provider (Rabb-ul-Mal / IAH) supplies capital managed by a partner (the bank as Mudarib), with profits shared per the contracted ratio and losses borne by the capital provider unless caused by misconduct, negligence or breach. It sits alongside Musharakah and Wakalah definitions and underpins how banks may raise PSIA funding and how loss-bearing and credit risk are allocated to IAHs. Relevant to banks structuring Shari'ah compliant deposit-like funding under the PSIA Rules.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

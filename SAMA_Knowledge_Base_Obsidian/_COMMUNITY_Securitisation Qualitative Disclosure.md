# Securitisation Qualitative Disclosure

## Why this community

Pillar 3 qualitative disclosure obligations for securitisation activity — what a bank must narrate about its role (originator, sponsor, investor, servicer), objectives, risk-transfer intent and accounting/regulatory treatment of securitised exposures.

## How members connect

- Single-member cluster: the qualitative securitisation table sits at the top of the securitisation disclosure chain and frames the quantitative templates that follow.
- Relies on the securitisation definitions and the significant-risk-transfer / originator-sponsor concepts used in the capital adequacy framework; disclosure scope follows the prudential consolidation scope.
- Hierarchy: Basel-aligned SAMA capital rules set the treatment; this table sets the narrative reporting duty on that treatment.

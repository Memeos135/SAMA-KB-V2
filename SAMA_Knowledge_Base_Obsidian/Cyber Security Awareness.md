# Cyber Security Awareness

Section of SAMA's Cyber Security Framework requiring a defined, approved cyber security awareness programme covering staff, third parties and customers of the Member Organization, aimed at building a risk-aware culture. It links to the cyber security policy requirement that relevant stakeholders be made aware of expected behaviour, and sits within the framework's governance domain. Applies to SAMA-supervised Member Organizations and is measured under the maturity model.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, consumer protection

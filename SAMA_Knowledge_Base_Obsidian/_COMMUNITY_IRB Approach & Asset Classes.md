# IRB Approach & Asset Classes

## Why this community

The scope and architecture of the internal ratings-based approach in KSA: which exposures fall into which asset class, which IRB variant applies, and on what timetable a bank may adopt IRB. Asset-class classification is the gateway decision that drives all downstream risk weighting.

## How members connect

- Taxonomy hierarchy: the overview provision defines asset classes, which then branch into corporate, specialised lending sub-classes, retail and qualifying revolving retail definitions.
- Approach selection: the foundation versus advanced distinction determines whether a bank supplies own estimates or uses supervisory values for a given asset class.
- Fallback route: supervisory slotting, with its criteria tables, applies where a bank cannot estimate parameters for specialised lending.
- Implementation layer: the SAMA Basel III guidance and the phased rollout provisions are the national adoption instruments conditioning use of the framework, and are cited by the substantive rules.

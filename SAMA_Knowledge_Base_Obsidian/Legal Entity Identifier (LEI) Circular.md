# Legal Entity Identifier (LEI) Circular

SAMA circular requiring financial institutions under its supervision, their subsidiaries, and branches of foreign financial institutions operating in the Kingdom to hold an active and up-to-date Legal Entity Identifier (LEI) issued through a GLEIF-accredited operating unit, building on an earlier LEI circular. It also permits those institutions to sign an agreement with SIMAH, the accredited local operating unit, to issue, update and renew LEIs on behalf of their current and prospective corporate customers. Rationale is risk management, financial stability, market transparency and international (G20/GLEIF) commitments.

# Cash Transport Safety Compliance

A SAMA (then SAMA agency) circular from the Exchange Centres Supervision Division reaffirming mandatory compliance with the rules and instructions governing the movement of cash and with the Security Safety Guide, following earlier circulars on contracting with cash transport companies, use of high-specification security bags, and organisation of cash movement for local banks and exchange houses. It highlights vehicle requirements for transporting cash and valuables above the stated threshold and refers to the Law on Trading in Money and Precious Metals. Binds all exchange centres (money changers) operating in the Kingdom.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential, other

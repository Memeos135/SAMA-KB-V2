# Saudization and Recruitment Services Contracting

A SAMA circular to banks and Class (A) money exchangers reinforcing Saudization obligations and setting requirements for contracting with recruitment/outsourcing service providers. It requires localisation across all job grades including senior and leadership roles, restricts branch, remittance-centre and cash-centre positions to Saudis (with a narrow non-Saudi customer-assistance exception carrying no systems access or account-opening authority), and confines cash handling, funds transfer, information security and AML/CTF roles to Saudi nationals. It also bans direct or indirect dealings with marketers of foreign banks' services in the Kingdom, and assigns HR to build localisation and training plans with Compliance overseeing implementation and quarterly progress reporting.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, AML/CTF, other

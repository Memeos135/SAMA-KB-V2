# Compliance Unit

Concept node for the "Compliance Unit" as a defined term: the compliance function or department within a supervised institution, which under the Compliance Principles for Finance Companies and Real Estate Refinance Companies reports directly to the Audit Committee. The node covers the unit's required key attributes (independence, resourcing, permanent standing) and its duties to identify, assess and monitor compliance risk with laws and SAMA instructions. It binds licensed finance companies and real estate refinance companies, alongside parallel compliance requirements for other SAMA-supervised institutions.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, BNPL/finance, AML/CTF

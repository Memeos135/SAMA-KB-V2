# Operational Risk Framework

## Why this community

Qualitative Pillar 3 disclosure of a bank's operational risk management framework, including governance, policies, risk appetite and the approach used to determine operational risk capital.

## How members connect

- Single-member cluster acting as the narrative counterpart to quantitative operational risk capital reporting.
- Draws its defined terms and scope from the operational risk capital regime and from broader governance and internal control requirements.
- Disclosure obligation is enforceable at the reporting-entity level and must reflect the bank's actual approved framework, not a generic description.

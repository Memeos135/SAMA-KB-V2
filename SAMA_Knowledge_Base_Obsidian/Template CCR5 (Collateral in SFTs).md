# Template CCR5 (Collateral in SFTs)

Pillar 3 disclosure template within SAMA's public disclosure framework requiring a breakdown of collateral posted and received in support of counterparty credit risk exposures from derivatives and securities financing transactions, including CCP-cleared trades. It is mandatory for all banks, reported semiannually, with a flexible row structure (columns fixed) and an accompanying narrative explaining material period-on-period changes. Binds banks subject to SAMA's Pillar 3 disclosure requirements at the level of what must be publicly disclosed, not how exposures are calculated.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

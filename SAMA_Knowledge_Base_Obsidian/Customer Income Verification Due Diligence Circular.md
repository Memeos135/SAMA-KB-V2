# Customer Income Verification Due Diligence Circular

Short SAMA circular (Arabic) reminding finance companies to exercise due diligence before granting finance, proportionate to potential risk, and to verify the authenticity of income-evidence documents submitted by the customer, strengthening related procedures. Issued under the Finance Companies Control Law and its Implementing Regulation. Addressed to all finance companies operating in the Kingdom.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, consumer protection, governance/risk

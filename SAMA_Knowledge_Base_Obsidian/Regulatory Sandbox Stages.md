# Regulatory Sandbox Stages

Describes SAMA's Regulatory Sandbox operating model as four sequential stages: application assessment (decision within 60 business days), operational readiness against the Assessment Criteria (120 days, ending in a temporary permission/Letter of Authorisation), live testing of six to twelve months, and graduation/exit. It also sets out the two possible outcomes of initial screening, including grounds for rejection where regulations already exist, the concept falls outside SAMA's regulatory perimeter, or no regulatory oversight is needed. Binds FinTech innovators seeking temporary permission to test in the KSA market; it is process guidance rather than a licensing regime.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, other

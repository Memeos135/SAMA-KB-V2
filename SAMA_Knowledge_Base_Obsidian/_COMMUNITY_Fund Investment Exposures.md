# Fund Investment Exposures

## Why this community

Capital treatment of a bank's equity exposures to investment funds, and the counterparty-credit-risk inputs those treatments depend on. It covers the tiered hierarchy of measurement approaches and the leverage adjustment applied to fund exposures.

## How members connect

- The equity-investments-in-funds rule is the parent obligation; it prescribes a descending hierarchy of the look-through, mandate-based and fall-back approaches according to the granularity of information available to the bank.
- Both the look-through and mandate-based approaches require the fund's underlying derivative and off-balance-sheet positions to be risk-weighted, which pulls in the counterparty credit risk framework, SA-CCR and the credit derivatives rules as computational cross-references.
- The leverage adjustment is a corrective step applied to the risk weight produced under the look-through and mandate-based approaches, so it is legally dependent on them rather than a stand-alone requirement.
- Scope note: these are credit-risk capital rules for banking-book fund holdings; the market-risk look-through concept is a distinct provision.

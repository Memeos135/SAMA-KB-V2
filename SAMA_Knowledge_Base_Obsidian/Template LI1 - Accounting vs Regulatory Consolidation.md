# Template LI1 - Accounting vs Regulatory Consolidation

Template LI1 reconciles the balance sheet under the accounting scope of consolidation with the regulatory scope of consolidation and maps carrying values to the credit risk, counterparty credit risk, securitisation and market risk frameworks (or to a not-subject-to-capital-requirements column). It feeds Template LI2 and the three-step link between published financial statements and Template CC1 capital composition. Mandatory for all banks in scope, annual frequency, with narrative in Table LIA.

**Regimes:** banking prudential

# Second Line of Defense Relationship

This node concerns the Internal Audit Unit's relationship with second line of defence functions (such as risk management and compliance) and with external auditors, and separately the internal audit of the bank's subsidiaries' activities, as set out in SAMA's Principles of Internal Audit for local banks. It governs coordination, non-duplication and the boundary between assurance provided by the third line and control functions in the second line, confirming internal audit supplements rather than substitutes management oversight. It binds locally licensed banks and their group entities.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential

# Basel III Post-crisis Reforms

Context node identifying the Basel Committee's December 2017 Basel III post-crisis reforms as the source of SAMA's revised operational risk regime, which adopts the revised standardized approach as the sole method and aims to reduce RWA variability. It also carries the legal basis (Central Bank Law, Banking Control Law), the supersession of prior Basel II detailed guidance, and the scope, reporting and disclosure provisions. Relevant when tracing why a national requirement departs from or mirrors the Basel text.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

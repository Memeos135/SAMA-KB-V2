# SAMA APR Calculator

SAMA Rules requiring finance providers engaged in retail lending to use SAMA's issued Excel-based APR Calculator and the prescribed net present value formula when determining APR. Finance providers must align policies and procedures, maintain internal controls and audit over the calculator, reconcile any automated tool against SAMA's Excel version, and keep customer-facing website calculators updated. The prescribed APR applies to advertising and promotional material, the offer stage, the financing contract, periodic statements and any other APR disclosure; the Rules are read with the Finance Companies Control Law and its Implementing Regulation, consumer microfinance rules, interest-rate disclosure rules and the Regulations for Consumer Financing.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, consumer protection

# Loan Classification and Provisioning

## Why this community

Credit risk recognition: how deposit-taking finance companies and other supervised lenders must classify assets by quality, review loan portfolios independently, and provision accordingly.

## How members connect

- Appendix C is the shared classification taxonomy (asset quality grades) that both the loan review and provisioning rules depend on — a common defined-terms anchor.
- Loan Classification and Provisioning sets the substantive obligation chain: classify, then provision at the level the classification dictates.
- The Loan Review Function rule imposes the governance layer — an independent internal function that tests whether classifications applied are accurate, making it a control over the provisioning obligation rather than a parallel duty.
- Scope point: the loan review requirement is framed for DTFCs; confirm applicability before extending it to other licensee categories.

# Template CR7 - IRB Effect on RWA of Credit Derivatives

Template CR7 is a Pillar 3 quantitative disclosure showing, by IRB portfolio (sovereign, banks, corporate, specialised lending, retail sub-classes, equity, purchased receivables, split F-IRB/A-IRB), hypothetical pre-credit derivatives RWA against actual RWA, to isolate the mitigating effect of credit derivatives used as a CRM technique. It is mandatory semiannually, fixed format, for SAMA-supervised banks using F-IRB and/or A-IRB, with narrative commentary explaining the RWA impact.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

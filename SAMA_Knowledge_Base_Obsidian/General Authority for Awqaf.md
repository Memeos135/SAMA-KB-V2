# General Authority for Awqaf

Concept node covering the General Authority for Awqaf's role in SAMA's account opening rules for endowment (waqf) and bequest accounts, including the endowment registration certificate, endowment deed, authorised signatories and administrator ID requirements. It sets restrictions on cross-border transfers and receipts, checkbook-only withdrawals, prohibition of ATM/credit cards, and notification of SAMA, distinguishing endowments administered by the Authority, those outside its administration, and foreign endowments. It binds banks opening such accounts.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, governance/risk

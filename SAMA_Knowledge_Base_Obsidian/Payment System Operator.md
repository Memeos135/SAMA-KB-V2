# Payment System Operator

Concept node for the Payment System Operator under the Implementing Regulation of the Payments and Payment Services Law. It relates to the parts of the Regulation on payment systems, including designation and classification of important payment systems, application of the CPMI-IOSCO Principles for Financial Market Infrastructures, system powers and rules, and settlement finality and insolvency protection. It binds operators of payment systems subject to SAMA oversight in the Kingdom.

**Regimes:** payments, governance/risk, banking prudential

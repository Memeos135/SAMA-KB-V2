# Table LIQA - Liquidity Risk Management

A qualitative/quantitative Pillar 3 table requiring banks to describe their liquidity risk management framework and liquidity position, including liquidity risk governance and risk tolerance, funding strategy and diversification, risk mitigation techniques, stress testing use and contingency funding plans, plus bank-specific metrics, concentration limits and entity/branch-level liquidity exposures. The table is mandatory for all banks, disclosed annually in a flexible format tailored to the bank's business model and liquidity risk profile.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

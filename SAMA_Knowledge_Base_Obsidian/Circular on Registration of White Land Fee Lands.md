# Circular on Registration of White Land Fee Lands

A SAMA circular from the General Directorate of Supervision of Finance Companies reminding banks and finance companies to register lands subject to the White Land Fees Law within the prescribed period so that beneficial owners do not incur financial penalties. It is issued under SAMA's charter, the Banking Control Law and the Finance Companies Control Law, and refers to the White Land Fees Law and its Implementing Regulation. It binds banks and finance companies operating in the Kingdom.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, banking prudential, other

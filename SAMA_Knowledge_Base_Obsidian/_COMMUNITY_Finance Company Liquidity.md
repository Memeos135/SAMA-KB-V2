# Finance Company Liquidity

## Why this community

Prudential liquidity and funding requirements for finance companies, especially deposit-taking finance companies, together with the governance and capital scaffolding that makes those requirements enforceable.

## How members connect

- The Deposit Taking Finance Companies Regulations sit at the top of the hierarchy and pull in capital, liquidity and governance requirements as licensing/ongoing conditions.
- The liquidity risk management rules operationalise the headline liquidity requirement through named components: governance of liquidity risk, stress testing/scenario analysis and a contingency funding plan.
- Stress testing and the contingency funding plan are legally paired - scenario outcomes are the trigger and calibration basis for the funding plan.
- Corporate governance and risk management obligations allocate board/senior management accountability for the liquidity limits and appetite, linking prudential ratios to individual responsibility.

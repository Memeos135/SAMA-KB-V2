# Master Repurchase Agreement (MRA)

Concept node for the standard Master Repurchase Agreement (MRA) for the Sale and Purchase of Securities (2020 version) approved by SAMA and appended to the repo guidelines. All repo market participants must document repo transactions in Saudi Arabia under this standard MRA, with any variations placed in Annexures, mutually agreed, and made clear in trade confirmations or equivalent agreed form; the GMRA remains referenced for certain cross-border/foreign-currency repos.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

# Business Viability Analysis

Concept node on the qualitative counterpart to financial analysis in NPL workouts: assessing the borrower's business model, medium- to long-term plan and projections, sustainable versus unsustainable debt, and alignment with existing credit terms. It also covers scenario and sensitivity testing of management assumptions, legal-documentation checks (cross-default clauses, notification obligations), collateral validity and enforceability, and the option to appoint a financial advisor for an independent business review. Binds banks resolving distressed exposures.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential

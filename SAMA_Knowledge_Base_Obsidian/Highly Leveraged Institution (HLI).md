# Highly Leveraged Institution (HLI)

Defined term underpinning the whole instrument: highly leveraged institutions are large financial institutions subject to very little or no direct regulatory oversight and very limited public disclosure, that take on significant leverage (broadly, the ratio of risk to capital). Hedge funds are the primary example, though not all hedge funds qualify and other institutions may share some HLI attributes. The definition scopes which counterparty relationships attract the paper's enhanced due diligence, exposure measurement, limit, collateral and monitoring expectations for banks.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential

# Cyber Incident Management

## Why this community

Detection, triage, escalation and supervisory notification of cyber security events and incidents affecting SAMA-regulated entities, including the monitoring capability (SOC/SIEM) and threat intelligence that feed it.

## How members connect

- Definitional chain: an event is the raw observable, an incident is the qualified subset that triggers response and regulatory reporting duties; the two nodes are explicitly related.
- Capability-to-obligation linkage: SOC and SIEM are the monitoring and correlation functions referenced by both event management and threat management requirements.
- Supervisory hook: incident management connects to SAMA IT risk supervision, i.e. the reporting/escalation channel to the regulator and the basis for supervisory review of the entity's response.
- Threat management is upstream input, informing detection use-cases and prioritisation rather than creating a separate reporting duty.

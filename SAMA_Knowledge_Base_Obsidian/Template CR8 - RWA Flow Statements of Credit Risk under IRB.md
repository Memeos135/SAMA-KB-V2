# Template CR8 - RWA Flow Statements of Credit Risk under IRB

Pillar 3 disclosure template CR8 requiring a quarterly flow statement explaining period-on-period movements in credit risk RWA computed under IRB approaches, attributing changes to drivers such as asset size, asset quality, model updates, methodology and policy, acquisitions and disposals, and foreign exchange. It is a fixed-format reconciliation supported by narrative commentary. Binds SAMA-licensed banks using IRB approaches for credit risk.

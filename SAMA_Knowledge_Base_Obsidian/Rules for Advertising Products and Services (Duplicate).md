# Rules for Advertising Products and Services (Duplicate)

A version of SAMA's Rules for Advertising Products and Services, issued under the Saudi Central Bank Law, setting the regulatory framework and minimum content standards for advertising financial products and services. It defines Financial Institutions, Customer, Consumer, Products and Services and Advertising, and imposes due diligence on advertisement content, an Arabic-language requirement, and mandatory disclosures (institution identity, product name, key terms, target customer, all fees and VAT, APR and finance term, fixed vs variable term cost for real estate finance). It binds all financial institutions supervised by SAMA when advertising any product or service. Marked as a duplicate of the same instrument recorded elsewhere in the knowledge base.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, BNPL/finance, payments

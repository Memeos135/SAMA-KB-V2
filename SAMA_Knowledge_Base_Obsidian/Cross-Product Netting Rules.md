# Cross-Product Netting Rules

Node setting out the Cross-Product Netting Rules permitting netting across SFTs, or across SFTs and OTC derivatives, for regulatory capital computation under the internal models method. It imposes legal criteria: a written bilateral cross-product netting arrangement creating a single legal obligation for a Cross-Product Net Amount on default or insolvency, supported by reasoned legal opinions on enforceability under the law of every relevant jurisdiction (counterparty's charter/branch, governing law of transactions, and law governing the netting contract), plus internal verification procedures before including a transaction in a netting set. Binds banks with IMM approval seeking netting recognition.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

# Hierarchy of Securitization Approaches

Sets out the ordered hierarchy of approaches banks must apply to calculate capital for securitization exposures, and how it interacts with credit risk mitigation — including full or pro rata credit protection, the decomposition of tranched protection into protected and unprotected sub-tranches, and the exclusion of SPEs as eligible guarantors. It also carries the core definitions of securitization exposure, resecuritization and originator status. It binds banks acting as originators, investors, protection buyers or protection providers under SAMA's credit risk capital rules.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

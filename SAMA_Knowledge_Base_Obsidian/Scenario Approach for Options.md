# Scenario Approach for Options

This node covers the scenario approach for options under the simplified standardised approach for market risk, where general market risk capital is set by the worst-loss cell of a scenario grid combining changes in the underlying and its volatility, with specific risk calculated separately from delta-equivalent positions. It is presented as an alternative to the simplified carve-out treatment and the delta-plus method. It binds SAMA-licensed banks using the simplified standardised approach for options portfolios, subject to supervisory expectations on the models used.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

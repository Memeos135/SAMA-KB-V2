# Operational Risk

Node on operational risk within SAMA's Shari'ah compliant banking risk framework, defined as losses from inadequate or failed internal processes, people and systems or from external events, and situated within the board/senior management oversight and risk management requirements of the framework. It sits alongside the requirement that the risk management function be independent of risk-taking functions and that policies establish clear roles, accountability and reporting lines for risks from Shari'ah compliant activities. Binds banks licensed under the Banking Control Law offering Shari'ah compliant products, including Islamic Windows.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential

### [[Risk Management Framework for Shari'ah Compliant Banking]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When deciding how to treat operational risk arising from Shari'ah compliant products, the bank cannot rely on the Shari'ah framework alone: that framework is expressly supplementary to SAMA's existing risk management requirements, and it names operational risk as one of the risk categories to which Shari'ah compliant activities expose the bank. The general operational risk regime therefore continues to bind in full, with the Shari'ah framework adding expectations for risks unique to those activities (including Shari'ah non-compliance manifesting as an operational risk event). Practically, the reader should apply the baseline operational risk obligations first and then layer the Shari'ah-specific expectations on top, rather than treating them as alternatives.

# Market Risk

Section of the Risk Management Framework for Shari'ah Compliant Banking setting Principle 3.0 on market risk for all assets held, including illiquid or highly volatile ones. It requires a market risk strategy and limits, an independent market risk function or middle office, pricing/valuation/income recognition policies, MIS and stress-condition reporting to senior management and board, and explicit treatment of product-specific exposures (Salam and Parallel Salam commodity price risk, Sukuk and Murabahah asset values) and FX risk. Binds banks conducting Shari'ah compliant banking in KSA.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential

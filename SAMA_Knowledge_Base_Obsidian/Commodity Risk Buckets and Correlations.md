# Commodity Risk Buckets and Correlations

Concept node on the commodity risk class in the standardised market risk framework: the bucketing of commodity risk factors into defined groups and the correlation parameters applied when aggregating delta, vega and curvature sensitivities within and across buckets. It also intersects with the treatment of commodities risk in the banking book for internal risk transfer recognition. It binds banks calculating market risk regulatory capital requirements.

**Regimes:** banking prudential

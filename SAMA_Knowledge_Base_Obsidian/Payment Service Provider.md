# Payment Service Provider

Concept node for the Payment Service Provider (licensee) under the Implementing Regulation of the Payments and Payment Services Law, which replaced SAMA's earlier Rules Regulating Payment Service Providers. It anchors the licensing requirements, scope of application, outsourcing, audit and risk management duties, customer protection and financial inclusion obligations, contract and disclosure requirements, and supervisory/enforcement powers applicable to PSPs. It binds all persons licensed by SAMA to provide payment services in the Kingdom, including banks where relevant.

**Regimes:** payments, consumer protection, governance/risk

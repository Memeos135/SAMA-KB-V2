# SAMA Whistle Blowing Policy

Concept node for whistle blowing obligations under the Counter-Fraud Framework's Detect/internal fraud controls. Member Organisations must define, approve, implement and maintain a multi-channel process allowing employees and third parties to report suspected fraud violations without fear of repercussion, must comply with SAMA's Whistle Blowing Policy for Financial Institutions, and must take no action against whistle blowers who report in good faith. It sits alongside internal-fraud monitoring controls such as employee activity logging and monitoring of anonymous reporting lines.

# Secondary Market

Defined term: trading of the rights of real estate finance entities arising from primary market contracts. It frames the Real Estate Refinance Company's objectives — stability, growth and liquidity in the secondary market, intermediation between the sector and domestic/foreign funding sources — and the enabling powers to acquire and manage finance obligations, issue securities under the Capital Market Law with SAMA no-objection, and assign real estate assets and derived rights in refinancing transactions without borrower, debtor or guarantor consent. Relevant to real estate finance entities, refinance companies and SAMA pricing oversight.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, banking prudential, other

# Template MR1 - Market Risk under Standardised Approach

Pillar 3 template disclosing market risk capital requirements calculated under the standardised approach, broken down by risk class components. It covers trading book and banking book positions subject to market risk capital requirements, excluding counterparty credit risk charges on the same exposures, which are disclosed in the counterparty credit risk section. Binds banks subject to SAMA's market risk and Pillar 3 frameworks.

**Regimes:** banking prudential

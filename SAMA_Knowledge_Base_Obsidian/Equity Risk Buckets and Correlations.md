# Equity Risk Buckets and Correlations

Concept node on the equity risk class within the standardised market risk capital framework: how equity exposures are grouped into buckets (by issuer characteristics) and the intra- and inter-bucket correlation parameters used to aggregate weighted sensitivities. It also connects to disclosure and RWA reporting lines that separate equity positions from general credit risk. It binds banks calculating Pillar 1 market risk capital and completing related regulatory reporting.

**Regimes:** banking prudential

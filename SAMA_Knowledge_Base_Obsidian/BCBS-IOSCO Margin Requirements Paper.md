# BCBS-IOSCO Margin Requirements Paper

Reference node for the BCBS-IOSCO margin requirements for non-centrally cleared derivatives, which SAMA's framework adopts and localises, including the phase-in timetable. It informs the instrument scope element: margin applies to all non-centrally cleared derivatives except physically settled FX forwards and swaps, with a carve-out for the fixed physically settled FX legs of cross-currency swap principal exchanges from initial margin (variation margin still applies to all components). Relevant to banks and other covered entities determining product scope and implementation dates.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

# ML/TF Risk Assessment

This node is the institutional ML/TF risk assessment section of SAMA's AML/CTF guide: financial institutions must assess, understand and document their money laundering and terrorist financing risks across customers and beneficial owners, products/services/transactions, geographies, and delivery channels. It requires pre-launch risk assessment of new products, services, business practices and new or developing technologies, regular updating of customer risk profiles, and a mechanism to furnish the underlying information and reports to SAMA on request. It explicitly discourages wholesale de-risking, requiring mitigation rather than exclusion of high-risk customer classes.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, governance/risk

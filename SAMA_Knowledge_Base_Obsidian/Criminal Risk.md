# Criminal Risk

A category within SAMA's Operational Risk Management Guide identifying criminal activity by employees or third parties as historically the largest source of operational loss in Saudi banks, based on insurance claims data. It groups fraud, forgery, counterfeit currency, robbery/burglary, electronic crime and retail electronic crime as distinct sub-elements to be addressed in a bank's operational risk plan and control framework. Relevant to banks' internal control, insurance and board-level risk oversight rather than to AML reporting duties as such.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential

# Circular on Prohibition of SAMA Logo Use

A SAMA circular prohibiting supervised entities from using SAMA's logo on their printed materials or publications without prior approval, to prevent reputational harm, commercial exploitation of the logo, or misleading customers. Entities must ensure their branches comply, and SAMA may take action against violators under the relevant laws. It binds banks, insurance and reinsurance companies, finance companies, credit bureaus, insurance-related professions firms, and money exchange institutions/companies.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, governance/risk

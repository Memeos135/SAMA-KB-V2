# Material Functions

The defined term and classification test under the Outsourcing Rules: a function is material where its default or disruption could affect the finance company's activities, reputation or financial position, or where the outsourcing involves sharing, transferring, processing or storing consumer data and information. Finance companies must classify each proposed outsourcing case-by-case and obtain SAMA's written non-objection at least 30 working days before commencement, renewal or material change, submitting prescribed details. Appendices give non-exhaustive examples (e.g. external and internal audit, customer care and complaint handling, IT/cloud and security operations, brokerage and agency activity, HR provision, debt collection, archiving).

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, governance/risk, data/credit info

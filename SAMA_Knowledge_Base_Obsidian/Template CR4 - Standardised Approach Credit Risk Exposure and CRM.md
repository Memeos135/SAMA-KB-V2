# Template CR4 - Standardised Approach Credit Risk Exposure and CRM

Pillar 3 disclosure template CR4 within SAMA's Basel-aligned public disclosure framework, covering standardised approach credit risk exposures before and after credit conversion factors and credit risk mitigation, broken down by asset class with associated RWA and RWA density. It sets out the fixed format, definitions and instructions banks must follow, including the treatment of on- and off-balance sheet exposures and the simple versus comprehensive CRM approaches. It binds SAMA-licensed banks that compute credit risk RWA under the standardised approach, at the regulatory scope of consolidation.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

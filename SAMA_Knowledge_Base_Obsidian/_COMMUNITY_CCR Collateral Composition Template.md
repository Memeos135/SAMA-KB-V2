# CCR Collateral Composition Template

## Why this community

Pillar 3 disclosure of the collateral used to mitigate counterparty credit risk, split between derivative transactions and securities financing transactions, and between segregated and unsegregated, received and posted.

## How members connect

- Single-member cluster: it operationalises the collateral and credit risk mitigation policies described narratively in the CCR qualitative table.
- Cross-references the CRM recognition rules in the capital adequacy framework, which determine which collateral may reduce EAD reported in the exposure template.
- Segregation categories connect to downgrade-trigger and margining disclosures required qualitatively.

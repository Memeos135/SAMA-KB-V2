# Minimum Verification Controls v1.0

SAMA's Minimum Verification Controls (v1.0) sets a baseline set of cyber and customer-verification controls covering registration/onboarding, general operating controls and additional lending-specific controls. It applies to any member organization under SAMA supervision providing e-wallet services, lending products, crowdfunding or other fintech business models, subject to applicability against the stated objectives. Key themes include identity and phone-ownership verification through a licensed trusted party, OTP and multi-factor authentication, device binding, fraud handling, SMS notification, terms and conditions/data privacy consent, and board-approved internal policies reflecting these controls.

**Regimes:** payments, BNPL/finance, governance/risk, consumer protection

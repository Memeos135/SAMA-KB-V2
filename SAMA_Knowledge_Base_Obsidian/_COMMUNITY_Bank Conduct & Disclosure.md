# Bank Conduct & Disclosure

## Why this community

Conduct, confidentiality and disclosure obligations of banks toward customers, counterparties and authorities — covering what must be disclosed (licensing status, account terms), what must be kept confidential (bank–customer secrecy), and the lawful gateways for sharing account and payment data.

## How members connect

- Shared axis of banking secrecy: the confidentiality/disclosure rules set the default duty, and the disclosure-to-authorities and instant-payments data-sharing items function as carve-outs or permitted purposes.
- Standardised current account agreement templates operationalise disclosure duties at the contract level, referencing licence disclosure and payment-system data-sharing terms as customer-facing consent/notice.
- The code of conduct sits above as the institutional governance layer and pulls in AML/CFT obligations, which themselves override secrecy for reporting and CDD purposes.
- Cross-regime tension to flag: consumer-protection/contract disclosure vs AML/CTF reporting vs data confidentiality — the same customer data is governed by three different duty sets.

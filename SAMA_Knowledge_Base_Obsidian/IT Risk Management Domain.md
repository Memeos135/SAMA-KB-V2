# IT Risk Management Domain

Domain 3.2 of the Framework requires Member Organizations to define, approve, implement and communicate an IT risk management process aligned with enterprise risk management, covering risk identification and analysis, treatment, reporting, and monitoring/profiling. It extends to information assets (business processes and data, applications, infrastructure) and to third-party relationships and people aspects, and requires a centralized risk register, defined risk appetite, IT key risk indicators, and treatment plans approved consistent with governance arrangements. Relevant to outsourcing/third-party risk questions and to how IT risk acceptance is evidenced.

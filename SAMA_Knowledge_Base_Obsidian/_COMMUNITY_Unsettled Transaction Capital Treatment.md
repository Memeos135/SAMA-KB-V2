# Unsettled Transaction Capital Treatment

## Why this community

Capital consequences of transactions that have not settled on the contractual date, including failed trades and the distinction between delivery-versus-payment and free-delivery settlement.

## How members connect

- The unsettled-transactions rule is the operative capital requirement; the DvP definition sets the scope condition that determines which capital charge schedule applies.
- Timing triggers (business days past the agreed settlement date) drive escalating capital treatment, so the two members form a single condition-to-consequence chain.
- Small cluster: it functions as an annex-level detail to the wider credit and counterparty credit risk capital framework.

# SAMA Circular 5885 - Standard Real Estate Financing Contracts

SAMA circular updating the mandatory standard-form contracts for retail real estate finance — the Murabaha real estate finance contract and the Ijara real estate finance contract for individuals — supplementing the earlier circular requiring use of those standard forms. It requires all real estate financiers to adopt the attached updated forms by the stated deadline and prohibits concluding contracts that depart from, or amending, those forms thereafter; the annexed contract covers lease periods, variable rental payment calculation, late payment, early repayment/early ownership, title transfer, default and termination, withdrawal rights, and a borrower-facing contract summary sheet. Binds banks and real estate finance companies operating in the Kingdom in their dealings with individual consumers.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, consumer protection

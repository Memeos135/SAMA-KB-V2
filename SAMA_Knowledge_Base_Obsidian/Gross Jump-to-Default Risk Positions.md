# Gross Jump-to-Default Risk Positions

Defines gross jump-to-default (JTD) risk positions as the first step in the standardised default risk capital (DRC) calculation: each exposure's JTD is computed separately before offsetting against the same obligor, allocation to buckets and application of risk weights. It applies to banks computing DRC for non-securitisation portfolios, securitisations (non-CTP) and correlation trading portfolios, and interacts with look-through for credit and equity derivatives and LGD treatment. Binding on banks using the standardised approach for market risk.

**Regimes:** banking prudential

# Annual Percentage Rate Calculation (Annex 1)

Annex 1 to the Consumer Financing Regulations setting the mandatory method for computing the Annual Percentage Rate: the discount rate equating the present value of all Borrower payments (Total Amount Payable) with the present value of financing amounts made available. It fixes calculation conventions — a 12 equal-month year, inclusion of all unavoidable costs and fees but exclusion of default/non-compliance charges, assumed contract validity and performance, fixed treatment of unquantifiable variable charges, and expression to at least two decimal/basis points. It binds Creditors whenever APR must be calculated or disclosed.

# Independent Audit Function

## Why this community

The obligation on obliged entities to maintain an independent audit function that tests the adequacy and effectiveness of the AML/CTF compliance programme.

## How members connect

- Stands alone in the graph as one of the mandated internal control pillars, but is legally dependent on the preventive-measures programme it is required to test.
- Its scope is defined by reference to the CDD, monitoring, reporting and training obligations it audits — findings feed governance and board-level accountability.

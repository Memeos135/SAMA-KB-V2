# SAMA Circular 5864 - Social Responsibility Day

SAMA circular inviting all SAMA-supervised financial institutions to support the Kingdom's national Social Responsibility Day (established by Council of Ministers decision, observed in March each year) through developmental initiatives or awareness campaigns, and to report their contribution back to SAMA. It is an exhortative/administrative communication rather than a prudential or conduct obligation, though it carries a reporting expectation and references a guidance manual for use of the campaign logo. Addressed to the full population of institutions under SAMA supervision.

**Regimes:** other, governance/risk

# SAMA Statutory Reserve for Money Exchange Centers

SAMA circular establishing a statutory reserve for money exchange centers, requiring them to appropriate a minimum share of annual net profit (after covering accumulated losses) until the reserve reaches a set proportion of paid-up capital, with appropriation suspendable at that point. Use of the statutory reserve requires SAMA's prior written no-objection, and the requirement is stated to be without prejudice to the separate cash reserve obligation under the Rules Governing Money Exchange Business. It binds money exchange centers operating in the Kingdom with immediate effect.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk, payments

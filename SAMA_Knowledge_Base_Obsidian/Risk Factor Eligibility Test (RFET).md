# Risk Factor Eligibility Test (RFET)

Sets out the Risk Factor Eligibility Test used to decide whether a market risk factor is modellable (capitalised within the expected shortfall model) or non-modellable (capitalised as an NMRF via stressed ES). Addresses real price observation counting, maturity bucket allocation for credit spread risk factors, treatment of parametric curve/surface parameters, and systematic versus idiosyncratic credit and equity risk factors. Binds banks using the IMA; SAMA retains discretion over data suitability and exceptional relief in systemic stress.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

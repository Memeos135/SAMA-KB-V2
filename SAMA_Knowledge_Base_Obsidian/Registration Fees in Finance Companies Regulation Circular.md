# Registration Fees in Finance Companies Regulation Circular

Arabic SAMA circular clarifying that fees charged by the contract registration company fall within the fees, commissions and administrative service costs subject to the ceiling in Article 83 of the Implementing Regulation of the Finance Companies Control Law (the lower of 1% of the finance amount or SAR 5,000). It confirms such registration fees may not be passed on to the customer outside that cap, consistent with the earlier circular treating all unavoidable charges beyond the finance amount and term cost as within the ceiling. Addressed to banks, finance companies and the Saudi lease contract registration company.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, consumer protection

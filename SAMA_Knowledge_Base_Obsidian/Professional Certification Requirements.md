# Professional Certification Requirements

SAMA circular (Arabic, dated 1444) reaffirming the standing obligation for staff of supervised institutions to hold prescribed professional certifications, supplementing earlier circulars on mandatory certification and on the updated target population for the credit advisor certificate. It identifies three certification tracks and the roles caught by each: retail banking fundamentals for customer-facing bank staff (customer care, call centre, branch staff and managers), the credit advisor certificate for staff directly involved in granting credit to individuals and branch managers, and the exchange/remittance certificate for front-line staff of bank-affiliated remittance centres and exchange houses; outsourced/third-party personnel are expressly included. It binds banks, their remittance centres, finance companies and exchange centres operating in the Kingdom, sets a one-year period from job commencement to obtain the certificate and a minimum compliance ratio, and carves out finance lease contract registration companies, the Saudi Real Estate Refinance Company and support-activity companies from the credit advisor requirement.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, consumer protection, BNPL/finance

# Agent Due Diligence (Art 14)

Article 14 requires Banks to run due diligence on prospective and existing Agents, covering moral/business/professional suitability, adverse credit bureau or other credible-source information, criminal records (particularly ML/TF, fraud and integrity), business experience, sources of funds, and verification of the Agent's legal status and address. It also requires defined internal roles for agent management, periodic reviews with early-warning indicators and corrective actions, and monitoring processes for AML/CFT, embezzlement and financial fraud, with requirements communicated to and monitored at Agents. Binds Banks; distinct from customer-level CDD under Article 15.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, governance/risk, data/credit info, banking prudential

# Unified Number Requirements

## Why this community

Identification of counterparties and service providers by the National Unified Number, including its use for legal representatives and the replacement of superseded identifiers.

## How members connect

- Explicit cross-reference: the lawyers requirement relies on the unified-number replacement instructions for which identifier is authoritative.
- Shared defined term (National Unified Number) that carries into identification/verification and record-keeping obligations across regimes.
- Hierarchy of specificity: general replacement instruction (all entities) narrowed by a sector-specific requirement for engaged lawyers.

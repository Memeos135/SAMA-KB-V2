# Minimum Licensing Criteria

The substantive criteria section of SAMA's Banking Licensing Guidelines, listing the minimum standards an applicant must satisfy: capital adequacy, bank ownership, corporate governance, risk management and controls, compliance and AML/CTF, IT and accounting systems and outsourcing, audit arrangements, and home-supervisor oversight for foreign branches. The criteria are expressly non-exhaustive and SAMA may impose additional prudential conditions case by case, including on newly licensed banks. Applies to all banking licence applicants, local and foreign.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk, AML/CTF

# Know Your Customer (KYC)

A KYC chapter within SAMA rules addressed to DTFCs, setting the minimum design of a Know Your Customer programme: customer acceptance policy, customer identification, ongoing monitoring of high-risk accounts and risk management. It prohibits anonymous, numbered-without-full-details, fictitious or inaccurate-name accounts, requires refusal of onboarding and reporting to SAMA's financial investigation unit where KYC doubts arise, and includes account-opening agreement acknowledgements (source of funds, real beneficiary, periodic data update at least every five years, freezing on non-update or suspected fraud) plus limits on remote opening of a General Account. It binds the licensed institutions themselves and, operationally, all their staff.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, governance/risk, consumer protection

# Collateral and Contractual Provisions

Section covering collateral, early termination and other contractual provisions in dealings with HLIs, requiring these terms to be aligned with counterparty credit quality rather than driven solely by net asset value. It addresses initial margin where financial information is insufficient, internal guidelines for minimum transfer amounts and loss thresholds, two-way margining and rehypothecation rights, and haircut policies reflecting price volatility and wrong-way risk between counterparty default and collateral impairment. Applies to banks using secured loans, repos and OTC derivatives with HLI counterparties.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential

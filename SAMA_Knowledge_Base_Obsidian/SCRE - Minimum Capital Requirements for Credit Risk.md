# SCRE - Minimum Capital Requirements for Credit Risk

Reference node for SCRE, the Basel-derived credit risk standard as adopted in SAMA's rulebook, cited throughout the disclosure instructions as the source of RWA and capital requirements for credit exposures. It is the anchor for the standardised approach provisions, the F-IRB and A-IRB provisions, the supervisory slotting approach, the equity exposure IRB phase-in and the treatment of failed trades and non-DvP transactions. It binds banks calculating and disclosing credit risk capital under SAMA's framework.

**Regimes:** banking prudential

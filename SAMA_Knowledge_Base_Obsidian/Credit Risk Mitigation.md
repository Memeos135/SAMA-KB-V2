# Credit Risk Mitigation

This node covers credit risk mitigation (CRM) as it appears in SAMA's Basel-aligned capital and Pillar 3 disclosure material: the qualitative and template disclosures on CRM techniques (Table CRC, Template CR3, and the CRM effects shown in CR4) and the rule that, under the standardised approach, mitigation is recognised via either the simple or the comprehensive approach with standard supervisory haircuts. It sits within the credit risk section that excludes securitisation exposures and counterparty credit risk, which are disclosed separately. It binds SAMA-supervised banks calculating risk-weighted assets and preparing Pillar 3 disclosures on a standalone and consolidated basis.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

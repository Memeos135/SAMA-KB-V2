# Blue Team Report (BTR)

The report prepared by the Blue Team during the lessons learned phase, setting out the exercise from the defender's perspective: alerts and events detected, actions initiated, their results, and recommendations for improvement. It is produced alongside the finalised Red Team report, supports the joint replay exercise and 360-degree feedback, and is a required input to the Remediation Plan. It binds the Member Organization's detection and response function.

**Regimes:** governance/risk

# SARIE Instant Payments

## Why this community

The instant payments infrastructure operated within the SARIE environment — rules governing participation, clearing and settlement of real-time transfers.

## How members connect

- Single-member cluster: the system operates as a defined payment infrastructure to which participation, operational and settlement obligations attach.
- Cross-regime relevance: payments/PSP licensing, consumer protection for transfer errors, and AML/CTF transaction monitoring all reference activity conducted through it.

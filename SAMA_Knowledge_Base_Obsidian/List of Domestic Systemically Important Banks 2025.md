# List of Domestic Systemically Important Banks 2025

SAMA circular publishing the annual list of Domestic Systemically Important Banks (D-SIBs), designated by reference to the earlier SAMA circular setting out the D-SIB identification methodology and associated capital requirements, based on year-end data. It names four banks (Saudi National Bank, Al Rajhi Bank, Riyad Bank and Saudi Awwal Bank) as D-SIBs for the stated year. It binds the designated banks, which become subject to the D-SIB capital surcharge and related supervisory expectations.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

# Remuneration Policy

Concept node on the mandatory written Remuneration Policy under the Banks Remuneration Rules: a policy for Senior Management approved by the General Assembly and a separate policy for all other employees approved by the Board. It prescribes minimum content, including objectives, scope covering all employee categories and outsourced third-party arrangements, pay structure and mix, perquisites, an approval authority matrix, and the roles of control functions and HR. It binds locally incorporated banks and their boards, with the Nomination and Remuneration Committee and Board holding oversight duties.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential

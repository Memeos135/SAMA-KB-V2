# Sanctions Lists Management

Chapter Four of the Targeted Financial Sanctions Rules, on management of sanctions lists — the lists issued under the UNSCR implementation mechanisms for counter-terrorism/TF and for preventing WMD proliferation and its financing. It governs how financial institutions must obtain, update and maintain those lists as the reference data for their TFS obligations. Binding on all SAMA-supervised financial institutions.

**Regimes:** sanctions/TFS, AML/CTF, governance/risk

# Quality Assurance

## Why this community

Quality assurance as a control-function obligation: the requirement that regulated entities test and validate the effectiveness of their compliance and operational controls rather than rely on design alone.

## How members connect

- Single-node cluster: QA appears as a standalone control expectation not yet linked to a parent framework in this graph.
- Legally relevant as an assurance layer over other regimes (AML/CTF programme testing, governance and risk oversight), so its obligation content is normally borrowed from those instruments.
- Practical consequence: absence of documented QA evidence is typically read as a control-effectiveness gap, not merely a documentation gap.

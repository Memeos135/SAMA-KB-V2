# The Market Risk Framework

Node covering SAMA's minimum capital requirements for market risk, focusing on internal risk transfers between the banking book and trading book, the SAMA-approved internal risk transfer desk, GIRR hedge-matching conditions, CVA risk transfers, and the general provisions and structure of the standardised approach. It requires all banks, D-SIB and non-D-SIB alike, to compute the market risk capital charge under the standardised approach, with RWA derived by a 12.5 multiplier. It binds SAMA-supervised banks with trading book activity.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

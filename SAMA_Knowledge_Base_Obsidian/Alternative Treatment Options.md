# Alternative Treatment Options

Sets out the Basel III alternative liquidity approaches (ALA) available to jurisdictions with an insufficient domestic-currency supply of HQLA, including the qualifying criteria (structural insufficiency, mitigation capacity, commitment to supervisory monitoring, disclosure, self-assessment and peer review) and the supervisory obligations attaching to their use. Critically, SAMA records that it has not adopted any alternative treatment option, so the section is contextual rather than operative for KSA banks.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

# Definition of Retail Exposures

Defines the retail exposure class under the standardised approach to credit risk, splitting it into "regulatory retail" exposures (subject to product, low-value SAR 4.46 million and 0.2% granularity criteria), exposures to "transactors", and "other retail", and excluding real estate exposures. It sets the corresponding risk weights of 75%, 45% and 100%. Binds SAMA-supervised banks calculating regulatory capital for credit risk.

**Regimes:** banking prudential

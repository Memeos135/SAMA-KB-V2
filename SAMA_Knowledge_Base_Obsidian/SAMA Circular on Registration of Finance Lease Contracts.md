# SAMA Circular on Registration of Finance Lease Contracts

A SAMA circular (Arabic) requiring licensed banks and finance companies to begin registering finance lease contracts with the Saudi Company for Registration of Finance Lease Contracts (Sijil) from a specified commencement date. It relies on the Banking Control Law, the Finance Companies Control Law and the Finance Lease Law (including the registration company mandate) together with the Implementing Regulation obligation on the lessor to register the contract and its amendments within ten business days and to record dispositions affecting lessor and lessee contractual rights. It binds banks and finance companies operating in the Kingdom acting as lessors.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance

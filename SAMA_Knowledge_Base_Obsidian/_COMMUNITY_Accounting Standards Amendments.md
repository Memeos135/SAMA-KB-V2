# Accounting Standards Amendments

## Why this community

Adoption of and amendments to international accounting standards by supervised entities, and SAMA's supervisory expectations on financial reporting treatment.

## How members connect

- Standalone node: obligations flow from SAMA's endorsement of the standards rather than from a bespoke SAMA rulebook article.
- Links outward to prudential and governance regimes through provisioning, capital and disclosure consequences.
- Changes are typically communicated by circular, so effective dates and transition provisions determine the applicable reporting period.

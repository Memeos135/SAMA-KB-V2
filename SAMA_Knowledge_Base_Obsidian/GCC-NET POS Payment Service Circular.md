# GCC-NET POS Payment Service Circular

A SAMA circular activating the GCC-NET point-of-sale payment service for acceptance of transactions in Qatar as of a specified date, supplementing earlier circulars on the GCC-NET POS service and its tariff. It requires banks to make the technical and operational arrangements to accept and process GCC-NET POS transactions on their systems and terminals, to settle commercial terms with GCC-NET cardholders and acquiring merchants in line with approved pricing, to inform and educate cardholders that purchases in Qatar are routed through the Qatari national network linked to GCC-NET, and to accept and process all GCC-NET branded cards. It binds banks operating in the Kingdom.

**Regimes:** payments

# Local Technology Products Guide Circular

Short SAMA circular (Payment Systems and Companies Control Department) forwarding a communication from the Communications, Space and Technology Commission about its published guide to locally developed technology products, presented as an indicative, non-binding list serving sectors including banking, insurance and finance. The circular is for awareness only, but reiterates that supervised entities must continue to comply with SAMA's existing instructions, particularly those governing outsourcing to third parties. Addressed to SAMA-supervised financial and payment entities.

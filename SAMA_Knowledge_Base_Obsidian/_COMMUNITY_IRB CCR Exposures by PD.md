# IRB CCR Exposures by PD

## Why this community

Pillar 3 disclosure of counterparty credit risk exposures for banks using internal ratings-based approaches, broken down by probability-of-default band.

## How members connect

- Single-member cluster: an IRB-specific CCR template whose applicability is conditional on SAMA approval to use internal ratings for the relevant portfolios.
- Legally dependent on the CCR overview disclosure - it disaggregates the IRB portion of total CCR exposure and must be consistent with it.
- Uses IRB defined terms (PD scale, EAD, average risk weight) imported from the capital adequacy framework rather than defined in the template itself.

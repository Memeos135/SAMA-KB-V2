# Regulatory Reporting

This node covers the regulatory reporting obligations under SAMA's Large Exposure (LEX) Rules for Banks (Section 7), including reporting of exposures to groups of connected counterparties and, for foreign bank branches otherwise outside the substantive limits, the requirement to report their 50 largest exposures and document large exposure/risk concentration policies. It sits alongside the scope provisions setting application to locally incorporated banks (including their foreign branches and subsidiaries) and foreign banks operating in the Kingdom, on both consolidated and standalone bases. It binds licensed banks supervised by SAMA; foreign branches under home-country consolidated supervision remain subject to the reporting element even where the limits do not apply.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

- **What this link tells you:** A bank assessing whether it is within large exposure limits must also answer a second question: what it is obliged to report and on what template and frequency. The LEX Rules define the substantive limits, the single counterparty and connected-counterparty perimeter, and the inclusions/exclusions, and then hand off to the regulatory reporting requirements for the disclosure mechanics. For a compliance decision, breach analysis and reporting obligations are distinct but chained — an exposure can be within limit yet still reportable, and reporting definitions must track the LEX perimeter definitions exactly.

# Systemically Important Payment System (SIPS)

SIPS is a classification category under the payment systems regime: SAMA may designate any payment system as a systemically important payment system and set tailored classifications and requirements for it. The surrounding provisions define the licensing, capital/solvency, product approval and outsourcing powers SAMA holds over payment system operators and payment service providers, and define core terms such as Payment Services, Payment Order, Member and Client. Relevant to operators and PSPs assessing whether heightened oversight applies to their system.

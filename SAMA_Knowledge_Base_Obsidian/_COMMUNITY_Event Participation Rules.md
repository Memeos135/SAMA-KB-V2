# Event Participation Rules

## Why this community

Administrative rules governing how supervised entities may host, sponsor or take part in events and exhibitions, including temporary pavilion presence — essentially conduct and prior-approval conditions on promotional/representational activity rather than prudential regulation.

## How members connect

- A common rule-set hierarchy: the hosting/sponsoring rules and the participation rules are companion instruments issued under the same supervisory mandate, with the later version cross-referring to the earlier.
- Participation rules extend the general framework to a specific fact pattern (temporary pavilions), so the specific rules must be read as an overlay on, not a replacement for, the general rules.
- Shared scope concepts (event, sponsorship, participation, approval/notification thresholds) determine which instrument governs a given activity.
- Versioning matters: V2 participation rules supersede prior text, so the applicable obligation depends on the date of the event.

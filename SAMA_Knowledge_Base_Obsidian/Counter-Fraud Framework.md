# Counter-Fraud Framework

SAMA's Counter-Fraud Framework, a principle-based instrument structured around four domains (Governance, Prevent, Detect, Respond) with Principles and mandated Control Requirements, plus a six-level Counter-Fraud Maturity Model (0–5) used to measure a Member Organisation's counter-fraud maturity. It obliges Member Organisations to establish a Counter-Fraud Programme and a dedicated Counter-Fraud Department responsible for day-to-day operation, including fraud risk assessment, control design, intelligence sharing with SAMA and the sector, systems tuning, investigations, root-cause analysis, monitoring of fraud risk appetite and periodic senior-management reporting. It binds SAMA-supervised Member Organisations and is the entry point for scope, applicability, responsibilities and maturity-level expectations.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, payments, AML/CTF

# Payment Fraud Circulars

## Why this community

Payment-fraud prevention and customer-facing controls in card and mada transactions: pre-authorisation abuse, identity verification against a trusted source, standardised transaction notifications, and fee treatment on claims.

## How members connect

- Shared subject population: payment service providers, card issuers and acquirers supervised under the payments/PSP regime.
- Obligation chain from identity assurance at onboarding/transaction stage, through fraud-specific controls, to customer notification and dispute/claims handling.
- Cross-regime linkage: identity verification via a trusted source touches AML/CTF customer identification standards, while notification and fee rules engage consumer protection duties.
- All are circulars — supervisory instruments elaborating existing payment regulations rather than creating a new licensing regime.

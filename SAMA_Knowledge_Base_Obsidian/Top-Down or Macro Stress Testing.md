# Top-Down or Macro Stress Testing

Part of the stress testing rules describing SAMA's supervisory top-down (macro) stress testing alongside banks' bottom-up tests, comprising use of banks' submitted bottom-up results, requiring banks to run SAMA-specified sensitivity or scenario tests and report results, and SAMA's own system-wide tests using macroeconomic and bank data. It obliges banks to submit data and results in prescribed formats and clarifies that SAMA will not formally share its own test results, and that stress testing reporting is separate from ICAAP reporting.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

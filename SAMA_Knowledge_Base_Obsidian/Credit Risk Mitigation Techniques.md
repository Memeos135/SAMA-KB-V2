# Credit Risk Mitigation Techniques

Sets which credit risk mitigation techniques may reduce exposure values under the LEX Rules: only unfunded credit protection and financial collateral eligible under the standardised approach for risk-based capital, expressly excluding equities, convertible bonds, UCITS and IRB-only collateral. Recognition is mandatory where the same technique was used for risk-based capital purposes, and it covers maturity-mismatch treatment and on-balance sheet netting of loans and deposits. Applies to banks measuring and reporting exposures net of eligible CRM, and interacts with the substitution rule that shifts exposure to the protection provider.

**Regimes:** banking prudential

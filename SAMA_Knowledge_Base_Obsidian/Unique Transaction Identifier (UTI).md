# Unique Transaction Identifier (UTI)

Defines the trade identification convention for OTC derivative reporting to the SAMA-authorised Trade Repository: until a global Unique Transaction Identifier is available, the reporting counterparty (the "generating entity") must create an internal unique trade ID, unique to each contract and never reused, including in trades with foreign counterparties. It fixes the code's composition (an 'E02' prefix, the generating entity's 20-character LEI, and an entity-generated unique code) and its format limits, and states that the internal ID cannot itself be amended by a modification report. Binds all banks in KSA with reportable OTC derivative transactions.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential, other

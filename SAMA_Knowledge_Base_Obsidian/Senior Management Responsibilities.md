# Senior Management Responsibilities

Provisions making senior management accountable for the design, implementation and compliance of new products and services with Board-approved policies, for keeping offerings within approved scope, and for periodically reviewing policy adequacy and proposing amendments to the Board. Senior management must also identify and mitigate actual or perceived reputational effects and confirm full operational readiness (processes, controls, systems) and any other authorities' approvals before launch. Binds senior management of SAMA-licensed banks.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential

# Credit Quality Disclosures

## Why this community

Pillar 3 credit risk disclosure: the quality of exposures (defaulted vs non-defaulted, provisions), movements in defaulted stock, and the extent to which credit risk mitigation reduces exposure.

## How members connect

- The credit quality of assets template is the hub: the defaulted-stock reconciliation, the CRM overview and the supplementary qualitative tables all tie back to its exposure and provision figures.
- Shared defined terms — 'default', 'past due', accounting provision categories and 'problem assets' (restructured/non-performing) — are set in the qualitative tables and applied in the templates.
- The prudential treatment of problem assets table supplies the classification and write-off policy that explains the numbers reported in the quantitative templates.
- Duplicate labelling of the CRM techniques overview indicates the same disclosure obligation surfacing in more than one place; treat as one requirement.

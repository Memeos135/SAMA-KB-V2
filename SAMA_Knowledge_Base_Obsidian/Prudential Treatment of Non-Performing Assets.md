# Prudential Treatment of Non-Performing Assets

A SAMA circular (Arabic) on the prudential treatment of non-performing assets, requiring banks to comply with the Basel Committee guidelines on problem assets — notably the harmonised definitions of non-performing exposures and forbearance. It affects asset-quality monitoring and supervisory comparability, internal ratings-based credit risk systems, Pillar 3 asset-quality disclosure, and published asset-quality indicators; addressed to all banks operating in the Kingdom.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

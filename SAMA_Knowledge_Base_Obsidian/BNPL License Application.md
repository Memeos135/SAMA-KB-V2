# BNPL License Application

SAMA guidance setting out how founders/shareholders (the "Applicant") apply for a licence to conduct Buy Now Pay Later activity, including the mandatory application form, checklist of attachments (articles of association, organisational structure, fit and proper forms for founders and board/committee candidates, feasibility study and business plan) and an irrevocable bank guarantee equal to 20% of the minimum required capital. It binds prospective BNPL companies seeking authorisation; false or omitted material information is treated as a breach of the Finance Companies Control Law and can lead to refusal or cancellation of the licence. Useful for pre-licensing readiness, not for ongoing BNPL conduct obligations.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, governance/risk

- **What this link tells you:** In deciding whether a BNPL applicant is in scope of licensing and what standards it must meet, the starting point is that BNPL licensing derives from the Finance Companies Control Law read together with the Rules Regulating BNPL Companies; the application guidelines are procedural rather than the source of the obligation. This means a BNPL provider is being licensed within the finance company regime, so finance company requirements apply unless the BNPL Rules expressly modify or exempt them. Note the cross-regime point that a BNPL "Consumer" is not the same defined term as an AML "Customer", so AML/CTF duties must be assessed separately from the licensing analysis.

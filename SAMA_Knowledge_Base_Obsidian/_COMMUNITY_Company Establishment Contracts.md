# Company Establishment Contracts

## Why this community

SAMA's prior-approval gate over establishment/incorporation documents of regulated entities, i.e. licensing and corporate-governance control at formation and at amendment of constitutive contracts.

## How members connect

- Single-member cluster: an approval requirement that conditions the legal effectiveness of corporate formation steps for supervised entities.
- Connects upward to licensing regimes (banking, finance, payment services) where establishment documents form part of the licence application file.
- Consequence for decisions: changes to the establishment contract cannot be treated as internal corporate matters — they trigger a separate supervisory consent obligation.

# Template MR3 - Market Risk under SSA

Pillar 3 template MR3 in SAMA's Pillar 3 Disclosure Requirements Framework, showing the components of the market risk capital requirement under the simplified standardised approach (SSA): interest rate, equity, commodity and FX outright products plus option risk under the simplified, delta-plus and scenario methods, and securitisation. Fixed format, semiannual, with row definitions keyed to SMAR14. Mandatory for banks that use the SSA to determine market risk capital requirements.

**Regimes:** banking prudential

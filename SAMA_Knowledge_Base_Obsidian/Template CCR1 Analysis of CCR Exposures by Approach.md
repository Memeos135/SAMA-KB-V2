# Template CCR1: Analysis of CCR Exposures by Approach

Template CCR1 requires banks to disclose counterparty credit risk exposures analysed by the approach used to determine exposure at default and RWA, distinguishing modelled approaches used with SAMA approval from standardised approaches. It is part of the CCR disclosure set (CCRA, CCR1, CCR3–CCR8) in SAMA's Pillar 3 Disclosure Requirements Framework and binds SAMA-supervised banks.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

# Table CCA - Main Features of Capital Instruments

Table CCA is the Pillar 3 disclosure of the main features of regulatory capital instruments (and, where relevant, other TLAC-eligible instruments), covering issuer, governing law, regulatory treatment, tier eligibility, amount recognised, maturity, call, coupon, conversion and write-down terms and subordination ranking. It is read together with the capital composition and regulatory adjustment rules cross-referenced to the SAMA capital standard. It binds banks issuing regulatory capital instruments under SAMA's disclosure framework.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

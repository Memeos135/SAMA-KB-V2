# Capital Composition Templates

## Why this community

Disclosure of the composition and quality of regulatory capital, buffers, leverage and the constraints that follow when buffer requirements are breached. Covers what counts as capital, on what terms, and what a bank may distribute.

## How members connect

- The Basel III capital reforms guidance is the parent instrument; the composition of capital template, the main features table for capital instruments and the distribution-constraint template all draw their eligibility criteria and deduction rules from it.
- Key metrics operates as the summary disclosure that must be consistent with the detailed capital composition, countercyclical buffer, leverage ratio and distribution-constraint templates.
- Distribution constraints link buffer shortfalls to an enforceable limit on dividends, buybacks and discretionary bonuses, and connect to creditor ranking disclosure where loss absorbency is relevant.
- Leverage ratio templates form a pair: the reconciliation from accounting assets to leverage exposure supports the common disclosure figure, and prudent valuation adjustments feed capital composition.

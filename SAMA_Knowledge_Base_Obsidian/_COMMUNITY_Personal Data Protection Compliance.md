# Personal Data Protection Compliance

## Why this community

Application of the Kingdom's Personal Data Protection Law to SAMA-supervised entities, and how data-protection duties overlay financial-sector confidentiality, credit information and customer-record obligations.

## How members connect

- Single-member cluster acting as the sector-level compliance hook for a general (non-SAMA) statute, so the linkage is law-to-supervisory-instruction rather than internal cross-reference.
- Interacts across regimes: banking secrecy, credit information sharing, AML record-keeping and consumer protection all involve processing of personal data and must be read consistently with PDPL lawful-basis and retention rules.
- Practical consequence: data-subject rights and cross-border transfer limits do not override statutory AML/CTF record retention or supervisory reporting duties, so conflicts must be resolved by identifying the governing legal obligation.

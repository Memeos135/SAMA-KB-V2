# Accounts for Liquidation and Financial Restructuring (300.1.7)

Sub-rule 300.1.7 of SAMA's Rules Governing the Opening of Bank Accounts and General Operational Guidelines, sitting within the chapter on resident juristic persons. It sets the account-opening conditions and documentation for accounts used in liquidation and financial restructuring proceedings of entities, distinguishing them from ordinary operating accounts. It binds SAMA-licensed banks onboarding liquidators/restructuring trustees and the entities under those procedures.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, banking prudential, governance/risk

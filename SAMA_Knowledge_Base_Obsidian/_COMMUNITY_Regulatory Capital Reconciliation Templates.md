# Regulatory Capital Reconciliation Templates

## Why this community

Reconciliation between published financial statements and the regulatory scope of consolidation, and from the balance sheet to regulatory capital. It addresses the problem of explaining why accounting figures differ from prudential figures.

## How members connect

- The linkage templates form an obligation chain: differences between accounting and regulatory scope, then main sources of those differences, then a narrative explanation table — each successive item explains the preceding one.
- The capital reconciliation template ties balance-sheet line items to the composition of regulatory capital, which in turn is governed by the Basel III capital reforms guidance and the main features table for capital instruments.
- Key metrics summarises outputs from capital composition, countercyclical buffer geographical distribution and leverage ratio disclosures, and must be internally consistent with them.
- Banking-book securitisation exposures are disclosed by reference to the regulatory scope of consolidation, tying that template into the accounting-versus-regulatory linkage set.

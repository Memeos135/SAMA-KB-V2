# Annex 3 Exercise Notice Form

Annex to a SAMA-issued master repurchase-style agreement setting out the exercise mechanics for the parties' undertakings (Seller/Buyer Exercise Conditions tested against Market Value or Default Market Value of the Second Purchased Securities versus the Second Purchase Price), including delivery of an Exercise Notice on an Exercise Date and deemed acceptance if the Undertaking Party does not respond within the stated window. The extract also carries the Events of Default and Early Termination Date provisions, including designation by notice and Automatic Early Termination where elected. It binds the contracting counterparties to the master agreement rather than imposing a free-standing supervisory obligation.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, other

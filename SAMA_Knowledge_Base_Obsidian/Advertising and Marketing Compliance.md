# Advertising and Marketing Compliance

A short SAMA circular reaffirming existing advertising and marketing instructions after SAMA observed non-compliant promotional material from supervised institutions. It restates that promotional advertising of products and services must be clear and must not contain wording that directly or indirectly misleads customers, requires institutions to review all existing promotional advertising for conformity, and warns that enforcement action will be taken against violators. It binds banks, finance companies and payment companies operating in the Kingdom, effective from its date.

**Regimes:** consumer protection

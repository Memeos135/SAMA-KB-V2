# Rules Governing Real Estate Refinance Companies

SAMA's Rules Governing Real Estate Refinance Companies, defining the licensed real estate refinance activity and the permitted business of a Real Estate Refinance Company (a joint stock company licensed to refinance real estate financiers and trade real estate finance contractual rights in the secondary market). Sets objectives, permitted activities (acquiring finance rights, issuing securities under the Capital Market Law, providing finance solutions, investing surplus cash) and multiple SAMA no-objection requirements. Applies to companies licensed by SAMA for real estate refinance activity, alongside the Real Estate Finance Law and the Finance Companies Control Law and their implementing regulations.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, governance/risk, banking prudential

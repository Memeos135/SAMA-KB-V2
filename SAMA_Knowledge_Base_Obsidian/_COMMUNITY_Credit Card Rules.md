# Credit Card Rules

## Why this community

Issuance and operation of credit cards by banks and credit card issuers, covering disclosure, fees and charges, billing, cardholder consent and conduct obligations toward consumers.

## How members connect

- The FAQ is an interpretive companion to the substantive Rules and derives its answers from them, so the Rules govern where the two diverge.
- Both share the same defined terms (issuer, cardholder, credit limit, statement cycle) and the same scope of licensed card issuers.
- Hierarchy: binding Rules at the top, supervisory clarification (FAQ) below, informing consumer-protection expectations.

# Highly Leveraged Institutions

## Why this community

Supervisory expectations for banks dealing with highly leveraged counterparties (hedge funds and similar), addressing counterparty credit risk that conventional current-exposure measures understate.

## How members connect

- The SAMA sound practices document is the parent instrument; exposure measurement, limit setting, collateral/contractual provisions and ongoing monitoring are its component obligation areas.
- Definitional trigger: whether a counterparty falls within the HLI definition determines whether the enhanced due diligence, collateral and monitoring expectations apply.
- Measurement chain: potential future exposure feeds exposure measurement, which in turn drives limit setting and is stress-tested; monitoring closes the loop by tracking positions against those limits.
- Collateral and contractual provisions are treated as risk mitigants that must be documented and enforceable, not as a substitute for measurement or limits.

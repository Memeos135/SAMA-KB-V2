# Retail Exposures Definition

Standardised-approach criteria for the retail exposure class, including the low-value threshold (aggregate exposure to one counterparty not exceeding SAR 4.46 million), the granularity criterion (0.2% of the overall regulatory retail portfolio, excluding defaulted retail exposures), and the definitions of transactors and other retail. It sets the applicable risk weights: 75% for regulatory retail, 45% for transactor exposures and 100% for other retail. Binds banks classifying and risk-weighting retail credit exposures.

**Regimes:** banking prudential

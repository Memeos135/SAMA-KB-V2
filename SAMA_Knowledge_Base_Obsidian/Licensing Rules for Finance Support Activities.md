# Licensing Rules for Finance Support Activities

SAMA's Licensing Rules for Finance Support Activities (amended version, Rajab 1447/December 2025), issued under the SAMA Law and the Finance Companies Control Law and its implementing regulation. They define finance support activities (activities supporting or complementary to financing, expressly including digital brokerage for finance entities but excluding debt collection for finance entities), and set licence application requirements, minimum capital (SAR 2 million for digital brokerage), fit-and-proper/management conditions, conduct of activity, and supervision and compliance provisions. They bind any person seeking or holding a SAMA licence to carry on a finance support activity, notably digital brokerage platforms.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, governance/risk

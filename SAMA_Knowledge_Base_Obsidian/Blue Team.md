# Blue Team

Defined role within the F.E.E.R. Framework: the Blue Team is the member organization's cyber-security monitoring function (e.g. SOC) that analyses alerts and events and follows agreed incident response procedures, without prior knowledge of the simulated attack. Its detection and response performance is the object being evaluated; after the test it produces a blue team report covering detected alerts, actions taken, outcomes and improvement recommendations, and participates in the joint replay exercise and lessons-learned phase.

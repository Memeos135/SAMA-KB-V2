# SARIE Working Hours during Ramadan and Eid 1447H

A SAMA circular fixing the operating hours of the Saudi Arabian Riyal Interbank Express (SARIE) system during Ramadan 1447H and the Eid al-Fitr and Eid al-Adha closure periods, noting that clearing houses will follow the system's hours and days. Issued under the SAMA Law and the Banking Control Law, it is addressed to banks operating in the Kingdom.

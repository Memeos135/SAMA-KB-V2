# KYC Verification for Payment Service Providers

A SAMA circular from the Payment Systems and Companies Control Department reminding payment service providers to verify the legal/regulatory status of the customers they serve, including checking the validity of official identification documents and reflecting this in service policies and agreements (e.g. obliging counterparties to notify changes in their legal status). It ties this to the PSP Regulations provision allowing a PSP to refuse payment orders where execution would breach its legal obligations, and transmits lists of entities with cancelled commercial registrations for re-assessment. Binds all licensed payment service providers in KSA.

**Regimes:** payments, AML/CTF, governance/risk

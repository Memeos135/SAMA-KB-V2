# Finance Company Debt Issuance via SPV

SAMA circular on the issuance of debt instruments by finance companies through special purpose entities, grounded in Article 14 of the Finance Companies Control Law (permitting issuance of securities and sukuk under the Capital Market Law) and Article 66 of its Implementing Regulation (requiring a SAMA no-objection letter before issuing securities), and reflecting coordination with the Capital Market Authority. It confirms that finance companies may apply to the CMA to establish a special purpose entity under the CMA's SPV rules, must notify SAMA of that step, and must obtain SAMA's prior no-objection before issuing any debt instrument through the SPV. It binds finance companies operating in the Kingdom.

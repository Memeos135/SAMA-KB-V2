# Maturity Ladder Approach

Concept node for maturity-based treatment in market risk capital, covering the maturity ladder logic used for slotting positions by residual maturity and the related maturity-scaling and offsetting rules (for example, that derivative contract maturity, not underlying maturity, governs offsetting, and that maturities under three months are floored at three months for default risk charge purposes). Relevant to banks calculating standardised market risk capital, including the default risk charge and commodity/interest rate maturity bands.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

# Prevent Domain

The Prevent domain of SAMA's Counter-Fraud Framework, covering proactive controls that stop fraud before harm occurs to the organisation or its customers. It requires a defined, approved and implemented Fraud Risk Management Framework aligned with enterprise risk management and addressing intelligence monitoring, fraud risk assessment, fraud risk appetite and KRIs, together with fraud prevention standards (internal and external fraud) that are periodically reviewed, monitored for compliance, measured for effectiveness, and risk-proportionate, including the approach to limits and thresholds for preventive controls. It binds Member Organisations and their business, risk and control owners.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, payments

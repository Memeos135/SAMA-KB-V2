# Information Confidentiality and Classification

This node is the confidentiality and information-disclosure strand of SAMA's Principles of Conduct and Business Ethics for financial institutions, covering the duty to preserve confidentiality, the mechanisms for disclosing information, and the related prohibition on using or leaking inside information to manipulate the market. It operates as a minimum conduct standard that each institution's board-approved ethics policy must reflect. It binds all personnel of SAMA-supervised financial institutions, including directors, executives, employees, consultants and staff engaged through third parties.

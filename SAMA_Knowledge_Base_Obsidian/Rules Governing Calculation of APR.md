# Rules Governing Calculation of APR

Rules standardising the calculation and disclosure of the Annual Percentage Rate (APR) for retail lending, mandating use of SAMA's Excel-based APR Calculator and a net present value formula. They apply to APR shown in advertising, offers, contracts, periodic statements and any other disclosure, and require providers to update policies, maintain internal controls over calculator integrity, and align customer-facing website calculators. Bind all finance providers engaged in retail lending, read together with the Finance Companies Control Law, consumer microfinance and consumer financing regulations.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, BNPL/finance

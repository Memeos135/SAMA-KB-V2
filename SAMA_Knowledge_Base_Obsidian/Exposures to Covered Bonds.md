# Exposures to Covered Bonds

Node covering the covered bond exposure class within SAMA's standardized approach to credit risk, sitting alongside the other individual exposure classes (sovereigns, PSEs, MDBs, banks, corporates, retail, real estate). It requires banks to perform due diligence so that external ratings conservatively reflect the creditworthiness of both the covered bond and the issuing bank, with an upward risk-weight adjustment where the analysis shows higher risk and no downward adjustment permitted. Binds SAMA-licensed banks.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

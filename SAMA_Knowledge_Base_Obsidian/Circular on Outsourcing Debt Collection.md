# Circular on Outsourcing Debt Collection

SAMA circular on outsourcing debt collection for banks and finance companies, which cancels the earlier circulars that had restricted such outsourcing to SAMA-licensed debt collection entities and set transitional deadlines. Going forward, collection may be outsourced to entities meeting the requirements set out in the applicable laws, regulations and instructions after obtaining SAMA's no-objection letter, without a standing requirement that the counterparty be SAMA-licensed, and without prejudice to the outsourcing rules and other relevant instructions. Binds banks and finance companies operating in the Kingdom and SAMA-licensed debt collection entities.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, governance/risk, consumer protection

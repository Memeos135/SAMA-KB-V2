# Collateral Management

The collateral section of SAMA's Rules on Management of Problem Loans, setting governance, classification and general management requirements for collateral and guarantees taken by banks, applicable across the whole credit process regardless of loan performance. It requires Board-approved (or delegated) collateral policies reviewed at least every three years, valuer governance and quality assurance, internal audit review, documented collateral types (financial collateral, leased assets, other funded credit protection, guarantees) and legal enforceability review including for foreign branches and subsidiaries. It binds banks licensed under the Banking Control Law.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential

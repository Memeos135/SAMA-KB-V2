# Council of Ministers Resolution 637 - CFT Amendment

Council of Ministers Resolution No. 637 dated 17/10/1441 AH approving an amendment to Article 12 of the Law on Combating the Financing of Terrorism (issued by Royal Decree M/21 dated 12/02/1439 AH), restricting provisional release of the accused to temporary release ordered by the Public Prosecution where investigations are not harmed and there is no flight or disappearance risk. It also approves repealing Article 9 of the CTF Implementing Regulations and annexes a draft Royal Decree giving effect to the amendment. It is a legislative instrument addressed to state authorities rather than a supervisory obligation on financial institutions.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, other

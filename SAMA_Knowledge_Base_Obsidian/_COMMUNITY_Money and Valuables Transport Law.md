# Money and Valuables Transport Law

## Why this community

Statutory regime governing the licensed transport and physical protection of cash, precious metals and valuable documents, including who may carry out such activity and under what security conditions.

## How members connect

- Single-member cluster at the law tier; it is the primary instrument for the cash-in-transit activity and the source of any implementing rules.
- Interacts with banking operational risk and physical security obligations, and indirectly with AML/CTF concerns over cash and precious metals movement.
- No internal edges recorded; downstream regulations or licensing conditions would attach beneath this law.

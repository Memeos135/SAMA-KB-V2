# Loan Review Function of DTFCs

Chapter 6 (Asset Quality) provisions of the Deposit Taking Finance Companies Regulations 2020 establishing the mandatory loan review function in every DTFC. It requires the loan portfolio and lending activity to follow a board-approved written lending policy, timely identification and internal classification of problem accounts, board/management reporting on credit risk and control effectiveness, and continuous maintenance of adequate loss provisions. It binds deposit-taking finance companies licensed by SAMA, with duties allocated to the board or its delegated authority and senior management.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, governance/risk, banking prudential

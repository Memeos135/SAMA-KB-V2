# Operational Readiness Assessment Criteria

Covers Stage 2 of the Regulatory Sandbox: after a favourable initial screening the applicant receives an initial approval letter allowing company formation/update at the Ministry of Commerce as a FinTech, but expressly not permitting operations. Compliance with the Operational Readiness Assessment Criteria (AC) — covering fit and proper approvals for management, shareholder approval, a three-year financial model, performance metrics, IT/helpdesk support and other minimum compliance items — is assessed and monitored by the sandbox Risk Management Unit, culminating in a No Objection Letter to go live at Stage 3. Binds sandbox applicants before customer onboarding.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, other

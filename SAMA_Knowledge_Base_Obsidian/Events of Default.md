# Events of Default

This node covers the Events of Default machinery in the SAMA-approved Master Agreement for the Sale and Purchase of Securities annexed to the Guidelines on Repurchase Agreements, including notice/service rules (delivery in person, courier, facsimile, registered mail, Electronic Messaging System) and when a Default Notice is deemed effective. It also sets out the close-out mechanics once a default occurs: determination of Default Market Values, Cash Margin, Equivalent Margin Securities, Second Purchase Prices and Cash Equivalent Amounts as at the Early Termination Date, and their replacement by a single Cash Settlement Amount payable by the Undertaking Party to the Exercising Party. It binds both counterparties (Buyer and Seller) to any repo transaction documented under the mandated MRA.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential, other

# Cash Transport System Recommendations

A 1437H SAMA circular transmitting the recommendations of a joint security committee (Public Security, Ministry of Finance, SAMA) on the transport of cash, precious metals and valuable documents. It requires banks to strengthen oversight of contracted cash-transport companies inside cash centres and apply penalties for breaches, separates cash-transport duties from ATM-replenishment duties, sets crew and armed-escort composition, mandates dual-controlled sealing of ATM cassettes under CCTV, encourages high-specification tracking/ink-degradation containers, allows use of SIMAH credit records in screening job applicants, and requires banks to report their monitoring procedures to SAMA. It binds banks and licensed cash-transport companies.

# Risk-Weight Treatment of Credit Protection

Sets out how risk weights are applied where eligible credit protection is recognized: the protected portion takes the protection provider's risk weight while the uncovered portion retains the counterparty's, and amounts below a materiality threshold are treated as retained first loss and risk weighted at 1250%. It also covers substitution treatment for guarantees over purchased receivables covering default and/or dilution risk, including proportional and tranched coverage. It binds SAMA-supervised banks applying credit risk mitigation under the standardized and IRB approaches.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

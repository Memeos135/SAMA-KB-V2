# SAMA Internal Control Guidelines for Commercial Banks

A cross-reference to SAMA's Internal Control Guidelines for Commercial Banks Operating in the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia (1989), identified as a companion instrument to the operational risk guidance. It underpins the internal-controls leg of operational risk mitigation, to be read together with the disaster recovery planning and physical security guidelines to form an integrated operational risk control programme for banks.

# Customer Protection Requirements

This node sets the customer-protection floor for Money Changers: displaying customer rights and exchange-rate boards at head office, branches and electronic platforms; issuing transaction receipts (paper or electronic); providing complaint channels with resolution within seven days and escalation notice to SAMA; and safeguarding customer information against disclosure to unauthorised third parties without SAMA's prior non-objection. Adjacent provisions cover head office/branch and ownership changes requiring SAMA non-objection, and IT and operational requirements. It binds licensed Money Changers dealing with Customers.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, governance/risk, data/credit info

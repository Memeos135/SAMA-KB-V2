# Trading Book / Banking Book Boundary

Node governing the boundary between the trading book and the banking book, focusing on internal risk transfers — internal written records of risk transfers within or between the books. It denies any regulatory capital recognition for transfers from the trading book to the banking book, and conditions recognition of banking-book credit, equity and general interest rate risk hedges on an exactly matching external hedge with an eligible third-party protection provider, with defined consequences when those conditions fail. Binds banks determining which positions attract market risk versus banking book capital treatment.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

# Cyber Security Risk Management

## Why this community

The end-to-end cyber security risk management lifecycle expected of supervised entities: identify, analyse, respond to, and continuously monitor cyber risk within the institution's overall risk governance.

## How members connect

- A parent obligation (risk management) explicitly references each lifecycle stage, creating a hierarchical obligation chain rather than four independent duties.
- The stages are sequentially dependent: identification scopes the assets/threats, analysis assigns severity, response selects treatment, monitoring feeds results back into identification.
- Shared defined terms (cyber risk, risk appetite, treatment/acceptance) run across all members, so a scope limit at the parent level constrains every downstream stage.
- Interacts with enterprise risk governance and board/senior management accountability regimes — cyber risk is not carved out from general risk oversight duties.

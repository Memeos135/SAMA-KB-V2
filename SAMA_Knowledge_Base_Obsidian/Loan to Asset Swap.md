# Loan to Asset Swap

Defines the loan-to-asset (debt-for-asset) swap, under which a loan or part of it is transferred into "other assets owned" and ultimate recovery depends on selling the asset; it is normally combined with conditional loan forgiveness or partial repayment plus maturity extension. Banks are expected to support such swaps with a proper business plan, monitor volumes by asset type, respect regulatory limits on holdings, and weigh recovery outcomes against foreclosure and legal-route recovery rates. Addressed to banks managing repossessed assets and problem-loan recovery.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential

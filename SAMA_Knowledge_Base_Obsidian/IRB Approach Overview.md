# IRB Approach: Overview

Node covering the internal ratings-based (IRB) approach to credit risk under SAMA's risk-based capital rules, including the conditions for adoption: SAMA approval, phased rollout across asset classes and business units, movement from foundation to advanced approaches, and the requirement for an agreed, realistic implementation plan that is not driven by capital minimisation. Once IRB is adopted for an asset class in a business unit, it must be applied to all exposures in that asset class in that unit, and no capital relief is granted for intra-group transactions engineered to reduce the group charge during rollout. It binds banks seeking or holding SAMA permission to use F-IRB or A-IRB.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

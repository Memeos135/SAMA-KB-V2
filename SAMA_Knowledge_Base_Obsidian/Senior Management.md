# Senior Management

Defined-term node for "Senior Management" as used in SAMA finance-company instruments, including the Shariah Governance Instructions where the Board and senior management must have a reasonable understanding of Shariah principles and their application in Islamic finance. The term identifies the layer accountable for implementing governance, compliance and control requirements below Board level, and overlaps with "Executive Management" in related SAMA texts. It binds SAMA-licensed finance companies.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, BNPL/finance

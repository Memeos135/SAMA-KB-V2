# Securitization Standardized Approach (SEC-SA)

Node for the securitisation standardised approach (SEC-SA), the formula-based fallback in SAMA's securitisation capital hierarchy used where SEC-IRBA and SEC-ERBA/SEC-IAA are unavailable or not permitted. It links the standardised credit risk treatment of underlying pools to securitisation risk weights and to how resulting RWA are reported in the Pillar 3 overview and securitisation templates. Binds SAMA-supervised banks calculating regulatory capital for banking book securitisation exposures.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

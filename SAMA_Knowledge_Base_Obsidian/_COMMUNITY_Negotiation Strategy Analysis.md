# Negotiation Strategy Analysis

## Why this community

Workout negotiation planning: the analytical preparation a creditor is expected to perform before agreeing terms with a distressed counterparty.

## How members connect

- The negotiating strategy is the governing step; SWOT analysis is referenced as an input method supporting it.
- Both sit upstream of formal restructuring measures, linking preparatory analysis to the eventual documented agreement.
- Practical consequence: adequacy of this preparation is evidence of prudent credit governance rather than a stand-alone obligation.

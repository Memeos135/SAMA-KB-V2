# Credit Risk Mitigation for Securitization Exposures

This node covers the general provisions of the securitisation framework, including scope, the definitions of traditional and synthetic securitisation, and the treatment of credit risk mitigation applied to securitisation exposures. It explains that capital treatment follows economic substance rather than legal form, how RWAs and exposure amounts are computed, and how provisions and purchase price discounts interact with 1250% risk-weighted positions. It binds banks with originated, retained, repurchased or invested securitisation exposures; SAMA consultation is expected where classification is uncertain.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

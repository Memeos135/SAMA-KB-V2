# Finance Company Regulations

## Why this community

Licensing, ownership and conduct of SAMA-supervised finance companies — covering entry into finance/refinance and finance-support activities, debt-based crowdfunding, real estate finance and mortgage documentation, portfolio and debt transfers, and ongoing governance, disclosure and fraud-control duties.

## How members connect

- The Finance Companies Control Law is the statutory anchor: nearly every rule, guideline, circular and form in the cluster cites or is issued under it, forming a law → implementing rules → licensing guideline → circular hierarchy.
- Licensing pathways share common gatekeeping instruments — the Fit and Proper assessment for controllers/senior officers and entity identification requirements — so the crowdfinance and finance/refinance guidelines converge on the same suitability and identification evidence.
- Asset-side instruments interlock: portfolio purchases (including residential real estate finance) and inter-company consumer debt purchases depend on the mortgage documentation and real estate valuation obligations that establish enforceable security and valuation quality.
- Ongoing supervisory obligations attach to licensed status rather than to a product line: pre-announcement notification of periodic financial statements, the mandatory compliance certificate, committee terms of reference and counter-fraud requirements apply across the licensee population.

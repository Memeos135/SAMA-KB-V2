# Template MR2 - Market Risk IMA per Risk Type

Pillar 3 template MR2 in SAMA's Pillar 3 Disclosure Requirements Framework, requiring banks using the internal models approach to disclose market risk capital components by risk type — including unconstrained and constrained expected shortfall, non-modellable risk factor charges, default risk capital, the amber-zone P&L attribution surcharge and standardised charges for IMA-ineligible desks. Row definitions cross-reference the Basel market risk standard (SMAR) and link to the overall RWA template OV1. Mandatory for banks with approved internal market risk models.

**Regimes:** banking prudential

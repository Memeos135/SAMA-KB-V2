# Quality Assurance

Subdomain 3.4.11 of SAMA's IT Governance Framework requiring a defined, approved and communicated quality assurance process that independently verifies changes and developments to information assets against business, user and regulatory requirements before release to production. Key points are an independent QA function with its own reporting line and authority for objective evaluation, mandatory pre-production QA assessment of all changes, minimum quality requirements, QA record management and reporting of results to stakeholders. It binds Member Organizations.

**Regimes:** governance/risk

# IRB Approach: RWA for Purchased Receivables

A chapter-level node of SAMA's minimum capital requirements for credit risk dealing with how internal ratings-based (IRB) banks calculate risk-weighted assets for purchased receivables, which straddle the corporate and retail asset classes. It sets when foundation versus advanced IRB may be used (A-IRB only for corporate obligors themselves eligible for A-IRB; A-IRB only for eligible retail receivables) and lists purchased receivables as separate asset classes for the rule that IRB adoption must be extended across an entire asset class. Binds SAMA-licensed banks approved to use the IRB approach for credit risk.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

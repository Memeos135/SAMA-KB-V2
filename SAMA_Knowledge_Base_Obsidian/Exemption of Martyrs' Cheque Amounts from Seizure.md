# Exemption of Martyrs' Cheque Amounts from Seizure

SAMA circular exempting from judicial seizure the cheque amounts payable to families of martyrs, following arrangements with the Ministry of Justice and building on the Enforcement Law's list of non-seizable assets and SAMA's updated exempt-amounts list. It sets the exempt proportion available to the customer and conditions release on presentation of a notary-issued declaration in the attached form. It binds banks operating in the Kingdom.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, banking prudential, other

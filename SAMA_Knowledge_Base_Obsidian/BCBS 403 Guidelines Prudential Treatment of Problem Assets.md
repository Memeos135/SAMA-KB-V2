# BCBS 403 Guidelines Prudential Treatment of Problem Assets

The definitional and scoping part of SAMA's Rules on Management of Problem Loans (issued under the SAMA charter and the Banking Control Law), which adopts the BCBS 403 definitions of non-performing exposures and forbearance as endorsed by SAMA circular no. 381000099757 dated 23/09/1438AH. It defines problem loans, non-performing loans, watch-list, early warning signals, cooperating and viable borrower, viability assessment, covenant and KPIs, and states the objectives (borrower rehabilitation, fair customer treatment especially for MSMEs, adequate controls). It applies to all banks licensed under the Banking Control Law.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk, consumer protection

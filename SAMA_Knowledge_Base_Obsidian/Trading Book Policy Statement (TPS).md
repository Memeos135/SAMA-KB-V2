# Trading Book Policy Statement (TPS)

A concept node covering the trading book policy framework in SAMA's market risk capital rules, in particular the documentation and treatment of internal risk transfers within the banking book, between banking and trading books, and between trading desks. It sets the conditions under which a banking book credit, equity or general interest rate risk exposure is deemed hedged (exact matching external hedge with an eligible third-party protection provider, documentation, dedicated internal risk transfer desk) and the capital consequences where those conditions fail. It binds SAMA-licensed banks calculating regulatory capital for market risk.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

# Internal Ratings-Based (IRB) Approach

This node covers the Internal Ratings-Based approaches to credit risk — Foundation IRB, Advanced IRB and the supervisory slotting approach — which banks may use only with SAMA approval, and their interaction with the securitisation framework (SEC-IRBA, KIRB, attachment/detachment points) and equity investments in funds. It also reflects the phase-out of IRB for equity exposures. It binds banks seeking or holding SAMA approval to use internal models for credit risk RWA.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

# Conditional Loan Forgiveness

Defines conditional loan forgiveness as a long-term restructuring option in which the bank waives part of the debt subject to conditions, typically to induce owners to inject additional funds and align their interests with the bank where the net present value of the company is below the total loan. It appears alongside other options (loan splitting, loan-to-asset swap, partial repayment, maturity extension) that banks may combine. Relevant to banks selecting and justifying restructuring measures for viable versus marginal borrowers.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential

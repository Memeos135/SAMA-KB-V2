# Template CCR6: Credit Derivatives Exposures

A Pillar 3 disclosure template within SAMA's Basel-aligned disclosure rules covering credit derivatives exposures used for counterparty credit risk reporting, capturing protection bought and sold by product type. It sits in the counterparty credit risk (CCR) template series alongside CCR1–CCR8 and specifies purpose, scope, content, frequency and format for the disclosure. It binds banks subject to SAMA's Pillar 3 disclosure framework; note the surrounding text also carries credit risk mitigation templates (CR1, CR3) with definitions of unsecured/secured carrying amounts.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

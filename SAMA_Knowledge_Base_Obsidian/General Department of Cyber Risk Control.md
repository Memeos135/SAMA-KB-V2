# General Department of Cyber Risk Control

A concept node for SAMA's General Department of Cyber Risk Control, the supervisory unit associated with issuing and overseeing compliance with SAMA's IT governance, cyber security and related technology risk requirements. It is relevant as the supervisory counterparty for IT and cyber risk reporting, assessment and remediation expectations rather than as a substantive control set. It concerns Member Organizations in their dealings with SAMA supervision.

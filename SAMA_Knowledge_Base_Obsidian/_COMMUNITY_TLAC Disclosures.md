# TLAC Disclosures

## Why this community

Total loss-absorbing capacity disclosure for globally systemically important banks and their material subgroups, including the ranking of creditors at resolution and subgroup entity level. This is a resolvability regime distinct from going-concern capital adequacy.

## How members connect

- The FSB term sheet is the source standard: the TLAC key metrics, composition and creditor-ranking templates all derive their eligibility criteria, subordination requirements and scope from it.
- The TLAC concept entry supplies the defined term that binds the composition and key-metrics templates together.
- Creditor ranking is disclosed at two levels — resolution entity and material subgroup entity — which must be read as a pair to assess where losses fall in a resolution.
- TLAC key metrics parallel the going-concern key metrics disclosure, so the two regimes must be reconciled for the same entity.

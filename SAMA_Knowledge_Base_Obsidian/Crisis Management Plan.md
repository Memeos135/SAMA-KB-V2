# Crisis Management Plan

This node sets the crisis management requirement in the SAMA BCM Framework: a defined, approved and implemented crisis management plan enabling a managed response to major incidents, including rapid communication with internal and external stakeholders. Minimum content includes criteria for declaring a crisis and establishment of a command centre for centralised management and emergency command; compliance must be monitored and effectiveness periodically measured. It links to the testing requirements, since BCP and DR test scenarios must cover activation and involvement of the crisis management team, and it binds Member Organizations.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential

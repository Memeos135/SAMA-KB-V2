# Loss Event Type Classification

Detailed rules on identifying, collecting and treating operational loss data: defining gross loss, net loss and recoveries; listing what must be included (direct P&L charges, consequential costs, provisions, material pending losses, material timing losses giving rise to legal risk) and what must be excluded (general maintenance contracts, post-loss business enhancements, insurance premiums); and fixing the date of accounting as the reference date, including for legal loss events. It also requires SAMA approval for excluding losses from the Loss Component, which should be rare and strongly justified. Binds banks building the standardized approach loss data set.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

# Internal Audit Plan

Node on the Internal Audit Plan required under SAMA's Internal Audit Principles for finance companies and real estate refinance companies (eighth principle), sitting alongside requirements on audit reporting, working policies and procedures, external quality assessment and record retention. It governs how the audit department scopes and schedules its assurance work and reports outcomes to the Board and Audit Committee. Binds SAMA-licensed finance and real estate refinance companies.

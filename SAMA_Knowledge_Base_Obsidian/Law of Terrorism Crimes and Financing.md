# Law of Terrorism Crimes and Financing

Marks the cross-reference obliging these account rules to be read together with the AML Law and its Implementing Regulations, the Law on Terrorism Crimes and Financing and the guidelines issued thereunder. It carries the associated ongoing due diligence duties: verifying the status of curators, legal agents, custodians and authorised persons; automated (not manual) transaction monitoring proportionate to the bank's risk profile and integrated with core systems; and the compliance department's right of timely access to CDD data, transaction records and identification information. Binds banks as AML/CTF-obliged financial institutions.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, sanctions/TFS, governance/risk

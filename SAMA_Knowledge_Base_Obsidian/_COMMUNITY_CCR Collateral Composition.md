# CCR Collateral Composition

## Why this community

Pillar 3 disclosure of the collateral posted and received against derivative and securities-financing transactions, evidencing credit risk mitigation in the CCR framework.

## How members connect

- Single-member cluster: a supporting template that substantiates the risk mitigation assumed in the CCR exposure calculations disclosed elsewhere.
- Depends on eligible-collateral and segregation concepts defined in the credit risk mitigation rules of the capital framework.
- Practical consequence: collateral recognised here should be traceable to the exposure reductions reflected in the CCR measurement templates.

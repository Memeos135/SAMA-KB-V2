# Simple Approach for Collateral

This node sets out the simple approach to recognising financial collateral, under which the counterparty's risk weight is substituted by the risk weight of the collateral instrument for the collateralised portion, subject to the collateral being pledged for at least the life of the exposure and revalued at least every six months. Surrounding provisions address collateral segregation by custodians, capital charges on both legs of repo and securities lending transactions, agent-arranged repo-style transactions with a performance guarantee, and reuse of collateral. It binds banks using the standardised approach for credit risk mitigation.

**Regimes:** banking prudential

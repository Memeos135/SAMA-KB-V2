# Internal Control Guidelines for Commercial Banks

A cross-reference node to SAMA's Internal Control Guidelines for Commercial Banks, invoked in the 1999 HLI circular as the existing framework under which exposures to highly leveraged institutions and hedge funds must be managed. It functions here as the domestic regulatory anchor that, together with a bank's internal credit policies and procedures, gives effect to the Basel HLI sound practices. It binds all banks addressed by the circular.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential

# Template SEC4: Securitisation Exposures Investor

Pillar 3 disclosure template SEC4 covering banking book securitisation exposures where the bank acts as investor, limited to positions treated under the securitisation framework and subject to its capital charges. It is the investor-side counterpart to SEC3 and excludes trading book securitisation positions, which are disclosed elsewhere. Applies to banks subject to SAMA's Pillar 3 disclosure requirements.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

# Board of Directors Responsibilities

Provisions placing oversight duties on a bank's Board for the offering of new products and services: ensuring senior management develops and implements internal policies and procedures, ensuring product risks are managed and consumer needs and rights addressed, and testing whether product offerings remain within the Board-approved risk appetite. Where offerings fall outside appetite, the Board must review and revise the appetite with documented justification and risk assessment and ensure policies are updated. Binds boards of banks licensed under the Banking Control Law.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential, consumer protection

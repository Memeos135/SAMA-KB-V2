# Whistle Blowing Policy for Financial Institutions

SAMA's Whistle Blowing Policy for Financial Institutions (v1.0, August 2019) sets the minimum framework each financial institution must adopt for receiving and handling violation reports, covering the reporting unit, reportable cases, whistle blower and institution obligations, protection from retaliation, confidentiality, report processing and KPIs. It requires secure reporting channels, objective and progressive handling with corrective action plans, an internal approval/oversight policy, and an automated register of report data. It binds SAMA-supervised financial institutions and shapes duties toward employees and stakeholders who report suspected violations.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, consumer protection

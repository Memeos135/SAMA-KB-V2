# Fees on Mada Claims Circular

SAMA circular (1443) imposing a refund deadline and penalty fees on mada-related customer claims. Banks must correctly refund customers for erroneous or incomplete transactions across ATM, POS and e-commerce services within two business days, and banks failing to do so incur a per-claim settlement fee under a set schedule. It binds banks operating in the Kingdom, PSPs and Saudi Payments, and is grounded in SAMA's authority over payment and settlement systems.

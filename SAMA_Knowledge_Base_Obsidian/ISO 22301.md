# ISO 22301

This node covers the introductory and scope provisions of the SAMA BCM Framework, which states that its requirements are based on SAMA requirements, industry practice and international standards including ISO 22301 and ISO 27001, plus BCI and DRII guidance. It defines scope and applicability — all SAMA-affiliated Member Organizations, all banks operating in Saudi Arabia, banking subsidiaries of Saudi banks and subsidiaries of foreign banks in the Kingdom — and allocates responsibility: SAMA owns, interprets and periodically updates the document, while Member Organizations adopt and implement it. It identifies the target audience (board, CEO, CRO, CIO/CISO, BCM managers, internal audit) and the route for a Member Organization to request amendments through SAMA.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential

# Risk Factor Modellability

This node covers risk factor modellability under the internal models approach for market risk: the risk factor eligibility test (RFET), the qualitative principles a risk factor must satisfy on data quality and accuracy, and the consequence that failing factors are excluded from the expected shortfall model and capitalised as non-modellable risk factors (NMRF). It also records SAMA's discretion over evidence banks must produce (for example regression diagnostics for multi-factor beta models and price recovery testing) and the narrow, systemic-stress circumstance in which a failed factor might still be treated as modellable without reducing capital. It binds SAMA-licensed banks seeking or holding approval to use internal models for market risk capital.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

# Head of Counter-Fraud

Node identifying the senior individual accountable for the Counter-Fraud Programme and the day-to-day running of the Counter-Fraud Department, including reporting line considerations, independence and escalation to senior management. Relevant to allocating personal accountability for fraud risk assessment, detection systems, investigations and intelligence sharing with SAMA. Applies to Member Organisations under the SAMA Counter-Fraud Framework.

**Regimes:** governance/risk

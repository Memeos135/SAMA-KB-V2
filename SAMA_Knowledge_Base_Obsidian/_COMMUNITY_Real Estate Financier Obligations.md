# Real Estate Financier Obligations

## Why this community

Clarification that a real estate financier's obligations continue in the secondary market context, i.e. after refinancing or transfer of mortgage assets.

## How members connect

- Single-member cluster: a SAMA confirmation interpreting existing real estate finance obligations rather than creating new ones.
- Obligation chain: Real Estate Finance Law and implementing regulations to the originating financier, with duties surviving securitisation/assignment of the financing portfolio.
- Cross-regime relevance to consumer protection (continuity of borrower rights and servicing standards) after asset transfer.

# SAMA Business Continuity Minimum Requirements

A node covering business continuity concepts and related minimum expectations in SAMA's cyber security framework, including the ISO 22301-based definition of business continuity and the requirement that continuity arrangements be preserved when services are outsourced to cloud providers (alongside SAMA prior approval, data location in the Kingdom, data segregation, use limitation, audit/review rights and exit provisions). Binds Member Organizations affiliated with SAMA. Useful when assessing continuity obligations in a cloud or third-party context.

# Level 2A and 2B Assets

Covers the Level 2 tier of HQLA: Level 2A assets subject to a 15% haircut and capped, together with Level 1, at 40% of the stock, and the optional Level 2B category (including qualifying common equity shares at a 50% haircut) capped at 15% and counted within the 40% limit. It notes that caps apply after haircuts and after unwinding short-term securities financing and collateral swaps maturing within 30 days, and records that SAMA does not currently permit Level 2B assets in LCR computation. Binds Saudi banks calculating HQLA.

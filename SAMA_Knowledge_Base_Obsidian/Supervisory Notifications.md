# Supervisory Notifications

A control section (3.7) of SAMA's Counter-Fraud Framework requiring Member Organisations to notify SAMA immediately of newly observed fraud typologies and significant fraud incidents, whether or not a financial loss occurred. Notifications go to SAMA's General Department of Cyber Risk Control and cover external fraud attempts, significant internal employee fraud against customers, and serious integrity/honesty misconduct touching regulatory obligations. It binds all Member Organisations within the framework's scope and sits alongside fraud MI/reporting duties to senior management.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, AML/CTF, consumer protection

# Fire Safety Compliance

## Why this community

Physical safety and premises-governance compliance for supervised entities, requiring appointment and proper functioning of a fire safety officer in line with the national fire safety regulation.

## How members connect

- Single-care cluster: a SAMA instruction directing supervised institutions to comply with an externally issued (civil defence / national) fire safety regulation.
- Legal chain runs law/regulation → SAMA circular: SAMA does not create the substantive fire safety duty but makes adherence a supervisory expectation for licensees.
- Sits within operational risk and internal-controls governance (business continuity, branch premises, staff and customer safety) rather than any conduct or AML regime.

# Circular on Investment Funds Indirect Participation in Financing

A SAMA circular, issued under the Finance Companies Control Law and coordinated with the Capital Market Authority, clarifying the activities finance companies may undertake with CMA-licensed persons to diversify liquidity sources. It permits selling finance portfolios to investment funds, entering loan participation agreements with investment funds for co-financing, and allowing funds to invest amounts with finance companies provided the credit-granting decision remains with the finance company.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, governance/risk

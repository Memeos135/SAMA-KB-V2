# Ministry of Human Resources and Social Development

Concept node within SAMA's bank account opening rules covering the Ministry of Human Resources and Social Development as the licensing authority for private associations (and comparable Da'wah offices and branches) whose bank accounts banks may open in Saudi riyal only, subject to specified documentation and operating controls. The surrounding rules also address Hajj/Umrah organisers' account segregation and account opening for minors, persons with upper-limb disabilities and legally incompetent persons through legal representatives. It binds SAMA-supervised banks as part of customer identification and account-opening due diligence.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, consumer protection, governance/risk

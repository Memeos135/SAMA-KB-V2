# Supervisory Slotting for Commodities Finance

Part of the supervisory slotting approach for specialized lending, covering commodities finance exposures where banks do not meet IRB PD estimation requirements and must slot internal grades into five supervisory categories with prescribed risk weights. It sits alongside the standardized approach fallback of a 100% risk weight for commodities finance without an issue-specific external rating. Binds SAMA-supervised banks with specialized lending portfolios.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

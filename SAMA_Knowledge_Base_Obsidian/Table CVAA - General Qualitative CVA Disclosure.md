# Table CVAA - General Qualitative CVA Disclosure

Table CVAA is the general qualitative disclosure requirement on credit valuation adjustment (CVA) risk under SAMA's Pillar 3 framework, and heads the CVA disclosure set that also includes CVA1/CVA2 (basic approach) and CVAB/CVA3/CVA4 (standardised approach). The whole CVA section applies only where the materiality threshold in SAMA's revised risk-based capital charge for counterparty credit risk is met. Binds banks subject to the Pillar 3 regime that exceed that threshold.

**Regimes:** banking prudential

# Guide to Rules Governing Banks' Remittance Centers

SAMA's Guide to Rules Governing Banks' Remittance Centers (Jumada Al-Akhir 1438H) consolidates operating, customer-acceptance, AML/CFT and service-quality rules for bank remittance centers and licensed remittance companies. It covers definitions, working hours, membership opening, KYC/CDD, domestic and international remittance execution, and contracting with money transfer service providers. It binds banks operating remittance centers and licensed remittance companies in the Kingdom.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, payments, consumer protection, governance/risk

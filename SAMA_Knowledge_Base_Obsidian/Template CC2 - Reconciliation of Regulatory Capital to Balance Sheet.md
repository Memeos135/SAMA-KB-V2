# Template CC2 - Reconciliation of Regulatory Capital to Balance Sheet

A Pillar 3 disclosure template requiring banks to reconcile the regulatory capital components disclosed under the composition-of-capital template to the balance sheet in their published financial statements, expanding balance-sheet lines that feed regulatory capital elements and deductions. It relies on the regulatory scope of consolidation and the CET1 adjustment items (goodwill, intangibles, DTAs, cash flow hedge reserve, provision shortfall, own credit risk, holdings in financial entities) cross-referenced to SAMA capital adequacy provisions. Binds banks subject to SAMA's Pillar 3 disclosure framework.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

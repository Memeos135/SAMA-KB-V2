# Finance Lease Contracts Register

Governor-issued controls (agreed with the Minister of Justice under the Finance Lease Law and Article 30 of its Implementing Regulation) covering registration procedures in the finance lease Contracts Register, the data registration requires, and the terms for third-party access to the register. Key duties include the lessor registering a contract and any amendment within ten business days via the register's electronic portal, and the licensed Contracts Registration Company maintaining, securing and — where SAMA licenses more than one company — unifying and exchanging register data under SAMA supervision. It binds banks, finance companies acting as lessors, and finance lease contract registration companies operating in the Kingdom.

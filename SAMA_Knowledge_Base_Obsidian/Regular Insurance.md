# Regular Insurance

Concept node on conventional (regular) insurance as an external risk financing option for operational risk, alongside self insurance and alternatives such as risk retention groups, agency captives, pools and finite risk insurance. It frames insurance as a means of funding loss exposure rather than controlling risk, to be combined with internal controls, segregation of duties, audit and physical security in an integrated operational risk programme. Addressed to directors and senior management of banks operating in the Kingdom.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential

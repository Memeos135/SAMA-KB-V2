# BCM Governance

The governance layer of SAMA's BCM Framework: a defined, approved and maintained business continuity governance framework and structure, monitored by senior management and communicated to employees and third parties. Allocates ultimate responsibility for the BCM programme to the board or a delegated executive, requires board-mandated BCM committee with senior-management representation and a charter (including at least quarterly meetings), adequate budget, an established BCM function, and an appointed, suitably qualified BCM manager/head. Binds member organisations' boards and senior management.

**Regimes:** governance/risk

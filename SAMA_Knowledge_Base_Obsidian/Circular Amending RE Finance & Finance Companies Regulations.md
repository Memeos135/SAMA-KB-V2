# Circular Amending RE Finance & Finance Companies Regulations

A SAMA circular (Arabic, dated 1443/08/21) notifying the market of two regulatory amendments: the Minister of Finance's decision deleting Article 4 of the Implementing Regulation of the Real Estate Finance Law, and the Governor's decision amending Article 16 of the Implementing Regulation of the Finance Companies Control Law. The amended Article 16 confirms that SAMA specifies in the licence which financing activity or activities a finance company may conduct and may attach special conditions limiting geographic area, permitted beneficiaries or other matters. Addressed to banks and finance companies operating in the Kingdom.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, governance/risk

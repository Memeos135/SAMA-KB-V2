# SA-CCR Sample Portfolio Examples

Worked sample-portfolio illustrations showing how SA-CCR inputs and outputs (and related credit risk mitigation disclosures) are calculated and presented for representative netting sets and collateral arrangements. Useful as interpretive support for applying the exposure methodology and for completing the counterparty credit risk disclosure templates rather than as a stand-alone obligation. Relevant to banks computing and disclosing counterparty credit risk exposures under SAMA's capital and Pillar 3 frameworks.

**Regimes:** banking prudential

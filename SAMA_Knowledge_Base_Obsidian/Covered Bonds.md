# Covered Bonds

Appendix VIII of SAMA's Large Exposure Rules defines covered bonds and sets the eligibility conditions for assigning a reduced exposure value (as low as 20% of nominal) rather than the default 100%, with the issuing bank treated as the counterparty. Conditions cover the composition of the cover pool, 10% over-collateralisation, LTV caps for residential and commercial real estate, and the operational requirements for eligible CRE/RRE collateral referenced to the Basel II standardised approach and SAMA's local guidance. It binds banks holding covered bonds when measuring large exposures.

**Regimes:** banking prudential

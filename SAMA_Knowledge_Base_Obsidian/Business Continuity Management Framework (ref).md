# Business Continuity Management Framework (ref)

A cross-reference to SAMA's Business Continuity Management Framework as one of the instruments preserved alongside the Aggregation Activity Instructions. It signals that an aggregation entity's technology, platform operation and interfaces with financiers must also satisfy SAMA's continuity and resilience requirements, in addition to accuracy-of-information and electronic record duties. Binds licensed aggregation entities and, in its own right, SAMA member organisations subject to the BCM Framework.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, BNPL/finance

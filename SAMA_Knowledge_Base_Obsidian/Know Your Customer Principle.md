# Know Your Customer Principle

The Know Your Customer provisions of the Remittance Centers Guide, requiring identity verification from official ID documents, prohibition of fictitious or anonymous names, assessment of source of funds and purpose of the relationship, identification of persons acting on behalf of the customer and of owners/controllers, and enhanced due diligence for high-risk customers, PEPs and high-risk jurisdictions. It also permits simplified measures for low-risk relationships but not where ML/TF is suspected, and discourages wholesale de-risking of customer categories. It binds remittance centers and licensed remittance companies alongside SAMA's AML/CFT rules and FATF standards.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, payments, governance/risk

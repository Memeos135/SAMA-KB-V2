# Electronic Payment for Retail Sector

SAMA circular (1441) directing banks and payment service providers to be ready to receive and process requests from retail-sector merchants for electronic payment acceptance, following the mandatory roll-out agreed with the Anti-Commercial Concealment Program, Ministry of Municipal and Rural Affairs and Ministry of Commerce. It requires readiness to open merchant accounts and e-wallets, to install SAMA-authorised payment means (mada POS devices, e-wallets) through all channels, to apply accurate Merchant Category Codes, and to brief branches and customer-service staff. It binds banks operating in the Kingdom, PSPs and Saudi Payments.

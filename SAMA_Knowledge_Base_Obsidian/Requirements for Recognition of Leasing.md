# Requirements for Recognition of Leasing

A section of SAMA's credit risk capital framework dealing with recognition criteria connected to leasing exposures, appearing alongside supervisory slotting criteria for income-producing real estate (pre-leasing, stabilisation and construction-phase indicators) and collateral/netting treatment for securities financing transactions. It informs how leasing and related property exposures are graded and how collateral value is recognised for capital purposes. Applies to banks calculating credit risk capital under SAMA rules.

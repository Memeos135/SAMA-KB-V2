# E-Wallet Security Controls (superseded)

A superseded SAMA control set specifying minimum cyber-security and customer-onboarding requirements for member organizations offering e-wallets, lending products, crowdfunding or other fintech business models under SAMA supervision. It covers registration/onboarding validation (one phone number or National ID/Iqama per application, strong authentication via an independent trusted party such as national single sign-on), terms and conditions and data-privacy consent, awareness programmes, inactive-account policy, contingency and back-up arrangements, and multi-factor/one-time-password requirements for wallet transfers, marketplace and bill payments, password resets, reactivations and high-risk or above-threshold transactions (thresholds circulated separately by memo). Because it is superseded, use it only for historical or transitional analysis; it binds SAMA-supervised fintech providers rather than customers.

**Regimes:** payments, governance/risk, BNPL/finance, consumer protection

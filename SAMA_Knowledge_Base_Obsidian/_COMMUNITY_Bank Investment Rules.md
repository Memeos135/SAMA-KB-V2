# Bank Investment Rules

## Why this community

Prudential conditions on banks' investment activity — what banks may invest in, the internal governance that must sit behind investment decisions, and the ongoing risk oversight and prior-approval gates SAMA imposes.

## How members connect

- The Banks Investment Rules act as the parent instrument; the governance, risk-monitoring and approval members are its component obligation sets rather than free-standing instruments.
- Obligation chain: board/committee governance duties feed the investment approval controls, which in turn trigger continuing measurement, limit-monitoring and reporting duties.
- Approval requirements operate as scope gates — certain investment categories or thresholds cannot be entered without SAMA or internal senior authorisation, so the approval node conditions the other two.
- Shared subject matter (the bank as licensed entity and its permitted investment exposures) keeps definitions consistent across all members.

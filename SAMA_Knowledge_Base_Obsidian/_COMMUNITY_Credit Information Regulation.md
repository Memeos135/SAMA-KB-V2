# Credit Information Regulation

## Why this community

The credit information regime: licensing of credit bureaus, the content and use of credit records, and the data-quality and reporting duties of member entities feeding the system.

## How members connect

- The Executive Regulation is the hinge instrument, defining the credit record and setting the licensing conditions for credit information companies — both other members are referenced from it (law → executive regulation → circular).
- The Qawaem circular is a downstream operational mandate that determines the source and standardisation of financial data entering credit records.
- Shared defined terms (credit information, credit record, member, consumer/subject) govern consent, disclosure and correction rights across all members.
- Cross-regime interaction: credit record use intersects with responsible lending/affordability duties and with data protection and consumer protection obligations.

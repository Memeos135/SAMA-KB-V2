# Non-Performing Assets Treatment

## Why this community

Prudential classification and treatment of non-performing exposures and forbearance — when an asset must be recognised as non-performing, how restructuring affects that status, and the resulting capital and provisioning consequences.

## How members connect

- Direct citation link: the SAMA prudential rules on non-performing assets draw on the Basel Committee's guidelines on prudential treatment of problem assets (definitions of non-performing exposure and forbearance).
- Hierarchy is international standard → domestic supervisory rule, so the Basel text supplies the defined terms that the SAMA instrument makes binding in KSA.
- Obligation chain runs from classification triggers to provisioning/capital treatment and supervisory reporting.

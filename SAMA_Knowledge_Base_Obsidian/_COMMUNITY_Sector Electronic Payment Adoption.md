# Sector Electronic Payment Adoption

## Why this community

Mandated adoption of electronic payment acceptance across defined merchant sectors (grocery, retail, personal services), progressively extending POS/e-payment obligations to categories of merchants.

## How members connect

- Parallel sector-by-sector circulars applying the same underlying obligation to accept electronic payments, differing only in the merchant population covered.
- Shared definitional dependence on merchant categories and on payment service providers' onboarding duties, which brings merchant-as-customer / KYB obligations into play.
- Implementation-phase instruments under the national payments framework, coordinated with acquirers who must enable the covered merchants.
- Sequential rollout gives a timing/scope reading: each circular sets the effective perimeter for its sector.

# Template REM1 - Remuneration Awarded

Template REM1 is the Pillar 3 quantitative disclosure of fixed and variable remuneration awarded during the financial year, broken down for senior management and other material risk-takers as defined in Table REMA. It is part of the remuneration disclosure set (REMA, REM1–REM3) and is published annually, as soon as possible after the annual report if not simultaneous. Applies to banks under SAMA's Pillar 3 Disclosure Requirements Framework.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

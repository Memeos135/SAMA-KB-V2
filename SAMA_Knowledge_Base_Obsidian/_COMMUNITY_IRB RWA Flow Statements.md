# IRB RWA Flow Statements

## Why this community

Pillar 3 disclosure of period-on-period movements in credit risk RWA computed under the IRB approach, explaining the drivers of change (asset size, asset quality, model updates, methodology, FX and other).

## How members connect

- Single-member cluster: a prescribed flow-statement template completing the IRB disclosure set alongside the exposure and mitigation templates.
- Obligation arises from the bank's IRB approval and the general Pillar 3 disclosure duty; content is defined by fixed movement categories rather than free narrative.
- Cross-reads with other IRB templates because closing balances must reconcile to the RWA figures disclosed elsewhere in the same set.

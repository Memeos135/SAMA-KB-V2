# Chief Information Officer (CIO)

This node concerns the Chief Information Officer role under SAMA's IT Governance Framework, requiring appointment of a full-time senior manager for the IT function at senior management level, with conditions including Saudi nationality, sufficient qualification and a written no-objection, and CIO representation on the IT Steering Committee. Related glossary definitions (e.g., Chief Operating Officer, enterprise architect, database administrator) sit in the framework's glossary appendix. It binds Member Organizations regulated by SAMA.

**Regimes:** governance/risk

# G-SIB Indicators Disclosure

## Why this community

Disclosure of the indicators used to assess systemic importance, feeding the identification and any higher loss-absorbency requirement for global systemically important banks.

## How members connect

- Single-member cluster: a standalone disclosure template whose content is driven by the systemic importance assessment methodology rather than by other disclosure tables.
- Scope limit matters - the obligation only bites for banks meeting the size or designation threshold, so applicability must be confirmed before treating it as a live requirement.
- Cross-references the capital adequacy regime, since indicator scores drive any additional capital buffer.

# SAMA Payment Services Regulation 1430

This is the Implementing Regulation of the Law of Payments and Payment Services (issued under Royal Decree M/26), which replaced SAMA's earlier Rules Regulating Payment Service Providers from its issuance date, with a corrective-action plan required within six months. It sets the framework for licensing payment service providers, agents and electronic money distributors, outsourcing/audit/risk management duties, customer protection and financial inclusion, contract and information disclosure requirements, e-money issuance and redemption, safeguarding of protected funds, account information and payment initiation services, and the designation, oversight and settlement finality regime for payment systems, plus supervision, enforcement and complaints handling. It binds licensed PSPs and payment system operators subject to the Law; note the source is largely in Arabic.

**Regimes:** payments, consumer protection, governance/risk

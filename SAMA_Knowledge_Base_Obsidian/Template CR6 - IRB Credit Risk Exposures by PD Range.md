# Template CR6 - IRB Credit Risk Exposures by PD Range

Template CR6 is a Pillar 3 quantitative disclosure presenting IRB credit risk exposures by regulatory portfolio and PD range, with associated parameters (PD, LGD, EAD, maturity), RWA, RWA density, expected loss and provisions. It supports comparison of model outputs against actual outcomes for banks with SAMA approval to use F-IRB or A-IRB approaches. It binds SAMA-supervised banks using IRB approaches for credit risk.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

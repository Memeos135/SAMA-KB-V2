# Combating Terrorism and Financing of Terrorism Law

The Law on Combating Terrorism Crimes and their Financing, setting the statutory definitions (terrorist crime, terrorism financing crime, terrorist, terrorist entity), offences, and the institutional architecture for CTF in the Kingdom. It establishes the Permanent Committee for Combating Terrorism and its Financing (implementation of UN Security Council resolutions), the Committee for Mutual Legal Assistance, and the General Directorate of Financial Intelligence as the national FIU receiving, analysing and disseminating suspicious transaction reports and empowered to obtain further information from financial institutions via the supervisory authority. It binds state authorities, financial institutions, DNFBPs and any person within its criminal scope.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, sanctions/TFS, governance/risk

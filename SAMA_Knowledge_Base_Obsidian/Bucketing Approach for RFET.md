# Bucketing Approach for RFET

Node on the bucketing method for satisfying the Risk Factor Eligibility Test, i.e. how real price observations are allocated to standardised or own-defined buckets and counted for modellability purposes, with contextual material on RWA reporting rows distinguishing modelled from standardised approaches. Relevant to banks determining which risk factors qualify for the ES model versus NMRF treatment. Binds SAMA-licensed banks applying the IMA for market risk.

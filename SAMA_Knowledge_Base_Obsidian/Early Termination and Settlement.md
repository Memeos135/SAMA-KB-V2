# Early Termination and Settlement

This node sets out the trigger events (including Act of Insolvency, failure to provide margin, untrue representations, suspension or expulsion from an exchange, and uncured breaches after 30 days' notice) and the designation of an Early Termination Date by the non-Defaulting Party on not more than 20 days' notice, or automatically where Automatic Early Termination is elected in Annex I. It links to the defined terms governing exercise of the unilateral undertakings (Exercise Date, Exercise Notice, Exercising Party, Exercised Transaction) and to equivalence of securities on corporate events. It binds Buyer and Seller under each repo transaction governed by the SAMA-approved MRA.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential, other

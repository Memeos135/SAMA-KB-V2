# Operational CTI Principles

The Operational CTI layer of SAMA's Cyber Threat Intelligence Principles, focused on understanding the nature, intent and timing of specific attacks and the behaviour of threat actors on a network. It expects Member Organizations to define and taxonomize attack phases using recognised frameworks and to identify tactics, techniques and procedures (TTPs) of relevant actors, tools and malware. Binds SAMA Member Organizations, including those outsourcing CTI.

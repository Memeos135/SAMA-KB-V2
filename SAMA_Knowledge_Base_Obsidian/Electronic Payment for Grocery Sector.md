# Electronic Payment for Grocery Sector

A SAMA circular transmitting instructions (originally issued by email in April 2020) requiring readiness to supply electronic payment acceptance to the grocery and foodstuff retail sector, in support of the national anti-commercial-concealment programme and reduced cash use. It requires readiness to open merchant bank accounts and e-wallets, to receive and process requests for SAMA-authorised acceptance means (POS terminals and QR codes) across all channels, to apply the merchant category code 5411, and to brief staff internally. It binds banks and payment service providers operating in the Kingdom.

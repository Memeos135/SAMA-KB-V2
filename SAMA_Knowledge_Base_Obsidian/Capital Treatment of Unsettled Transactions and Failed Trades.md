# Capital Treatment of Unsettled Transactions and Failed Trades

Concept node for the capital treatment of unsettled securities, commodities and foreign exchange transactions and failed trades (settlement risk) under SAMA's credit risk framework, together with how such exposures are reflected in Pillar 3 capital and RWA disclosure templates. It sets out that unsettled transactions must be captured for regulatory capital from trade date regardless of booking or accounting treatment, and separates DvP from non-DvP (free delivery) treatment. Binds banks; the framework excludes instruments already subject to the counterparty credit risk and CVA requirements.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

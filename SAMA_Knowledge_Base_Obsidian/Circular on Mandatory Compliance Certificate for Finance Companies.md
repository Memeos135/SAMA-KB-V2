# Circular on Mandatory Compliance Certificate for Finance Companies

A SAMA circular (Arabic original) making the "Compliance for the Finance Companies Sector" certificate mandatory for compliance managers at finance companies, relying on SAMA's mandate under the Implementing Regulations of the Finance Companies Control Law to develop the sector and raise staff competence. Incumbent compliance managers must obtain the certificate within one year of the circular, and it is required when a company seeks a no-objection letter to appoint someone to the role; the company must supply SAMA with the certificate once obtained. It binds finance companies operating in the Kingdom and their compliance function heads.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, BNPL/finance

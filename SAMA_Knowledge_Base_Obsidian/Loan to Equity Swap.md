# Loan to Equity Swap

Covers conversion of debt into equity (including conversion of owner loans into equity or another subordinated form) as a restructuring measure and as a supporting condition in workouts, alongside personal guarantees, capital increases, additional collateral and financial-indicator covenants. Debt-for-equity swaps are treated as measures requiring a supporting business plan, monitoring by asset/equity type, and compliance with applicable limits on such holdings. Applies to banks taking equity positions in restructured borrowers.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential

# IRB Credit Risk Templates

## Why this community

Quantitative Pillar 3 disclosure of IRB credit risk: exposures distributed by PD grade with associated risk parameters, and backtesting of estimated versus realised default rates.

## How members connect

- The backtesting template tests the same PD grades and obligor populations disclosed in the exposure template, so the two must use a consistent rating scale and segmentation.
- Shared IRB defined terms (PD band, obligor grade, defaulted exposure, average PD) and the same supervisory approval precondition.
- Explicit cross-reference between members makes the backtesting disclosure an accountability layer over the reported parameter estimates.

# Compliance Officer

Concept node for the "Compliance Officer" as defined in the Compliance Principles for Finance Companies and Real Estate Refinance Companies: the head or manager of the Compliance Unit, distinct from Unit Staff. The node covers the officer's appointment, independence, reporting line and responsibilities for the compliance programme and for escalating compliance-risk matters. It is relevant to finance companies and real estate refinance companies licensed by SAMA, and interacts with leadership-appointment (fit and proper) requirements.

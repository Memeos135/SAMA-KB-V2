# Multi Factor Authentication (MFA)

Refers to the general control in the Minimum Verification Controls requiring multi-factor authentication for each login to the member organization's application, complementing the OTP requirements and single-device/concurrent-login restrictions. It applies to member organizations under SAMA supervision offering e-wallet, lending, crowdfunding or other fintech business models. Relevant when assessing customer authentication adequacy and liability allocation in unauthorised-transaction and fraud disputes.

**Regimes:** payments, governance/risk, BNPL/finance

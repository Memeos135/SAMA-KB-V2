# Credit Risk Classification Stages

Part of SAMA's Rules Governing Credit Risk Exposure Classification and Provisioning, issued under the Finance Companies Control Law and its Implementing Regulation (notably the provisioning duties in Article 13 and Article 62). It requires finance companies to classify exposures, individually or collectively, into three stages mapped to regulatory categories (Regular, Special Monitoring/Substandard, Doubtful, Loss), with Stage 1 covering exposures showing no significant increase in credit risk since origination and Stage 2 those with SICR. It supersedes the category definitions in the earlier Provisions Guidelines circular and binds licensed finance companies.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, governance/risk, banking prudential

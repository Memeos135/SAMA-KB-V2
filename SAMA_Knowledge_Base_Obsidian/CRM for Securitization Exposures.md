# CRM for Securitization Exposures

Node on credit risk mitigation as applied to securitisation exposures — recognition of collateral, guarantees, credit derivatives and tranched cover when determining risk weights for securitisation and resecuritisation positions. It interacts with the general CRM rules and with disclosure of secured versus unsecured exposures in the Pillar 3 credit risk templates. Binds SAMA-supervised banks recognising credit protection on securitisation exposures for capital purposes.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

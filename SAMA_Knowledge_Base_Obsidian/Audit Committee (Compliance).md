# Audit Committee (Compliance)

Concept node for the Audit Committee's role in the compliance regime for finance and real estate refinance companies: it is the body to which the Compliance Unit reports directly and which oversees the effectiveness and independence of the compliance function. Its duties sit alongside separate Board and Executive Management duties toward compliance set out in the same principles. It binds the boards and audit committees of SAMA-licensed finance and real estate refinance companies.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, BNPL/finance

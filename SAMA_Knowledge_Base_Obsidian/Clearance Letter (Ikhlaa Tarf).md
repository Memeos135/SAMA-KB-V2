# Clearance Letter (Ikhlaa Tarf)

Concept node for the Clearance Letter (khitab ikhlaa tarf) — the confirmation issued by a selling financier that a customer's debt has been settled — as it operates in SAMA's Instructions on the Purchase of Individual Consumer Finance Debts between Finance Companies (issued under the Finance Companies Control Law, RD M/51). The instructions require the purchasing company to hold a consumer finance licence, to disclose new financing terms and costs and obtain documented customer consent before buyout, to settle existing debt through the SARIE system with prescribed payment data, and to withhold release of surplus financing to the customer until the clearance letter is received from the selling company; the selling company must issue liability statements or debt-confirmation letters within one business day, facilitate transfer within one business day of complete requirements, justify any refusal in writing, and notify the customer of settlement. It binds finance companies operating in the Kingdom.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, consumer protection

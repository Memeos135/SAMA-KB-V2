# KYC & CDD Compliance

## Why this community

Operational AML/CTF onboarding and identification duties — KYC, CDD and beneficial ownership verification — as applied across banks, exchange houses, deposit-taking finance companies, POS/merchant acquiring and cross-border cash movement.

## How members connect

- KYC and CDD function as the shared defined-term hub; each sectoral instrument (DTFC Regulations, Exchange Houses Operations Manual, non-resident property owner account rules, POS sale rules) attaches its own scope to those same measures.
- Beneficial owner verification links entity-level onboarding to external registry sources (GAA data services), creating a verification-source question rather than a new obligation.
- Exchange-sector chain: the Exchange Houses Operations Manual is the base manual, updated by the Exchange Companies Procedures circular, and carries CDD, correspondent banking and FATF-aligned requirements.
- Cross-border cash reporting (including King Fahd Causeway movements and cash transport) sits alongside CDD as a separate declaration/reporting duty on carriers and transporters, not on account onboarding.
- Merchant-as-customer point: KYC before POS sale/operation extends customer identification obligations to acquiring relationships (KYB), distinct from the retail customer relationship.

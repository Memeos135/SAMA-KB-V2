# Sensitivity Analysis

Sensitivity Analysis is one of the methodologies described in Section 5 of the Rules: it measures the change in portfolio value from shocks of varying degrees to a single risk factor or a small set of closely related factors, without evaluating the relationships between factors, with impact typically estimated on capital. The Rules leave methodology choice to each bank based on business nature, size, complexity and risk profile; SAMA does not prescribe risk factors or a common covariance matrix.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential

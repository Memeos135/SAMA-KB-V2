# Supervisory Framework for Payment Systems and Operators

This node is SAMA's supervisory (oversight) framework for payment systems and their operators, grounded in the Payments and Payment Services Law and its Implementing Regulation. It sets out SAMA as the competent authority for regulating, supervising and overseeing payment systems, defines oversight objectives of safety and efficiency, and applies international standards (PFMIs) through ongoing monitoring, self-assessment, supervisory assessment and change requirements. It binds licensed/designated payment system operators, including systemically important and unclassified systems.

**Regimes:** payments, governance/risk

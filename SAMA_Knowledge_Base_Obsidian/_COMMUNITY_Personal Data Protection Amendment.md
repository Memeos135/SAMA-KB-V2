# Personal Data Protection Amendment

## Why this community

Amendment to the Kingdom's general personal data protection regime, affecting how financial institutions lawfully collect, process, transfer and retain personal data.

## How members connect

- Standalone instrument at law level (Royal Decree), sitting above SAMA-issued data, credit information and consumer protection rules that presuppose lawful processing.
- Interacts with AML/CTF record-keeping and CDD data retention duties, where the data protection regime must be read as constraining purpose and retention, not overriding statutory retention mandates.
- Relevant to cross-border data transfer and outsourcing arrangements where customer data leaves the controller's direct control.

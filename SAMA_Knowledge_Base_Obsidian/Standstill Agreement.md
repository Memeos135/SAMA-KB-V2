# Standstill Agreement

Describes the standstill agreement as typically the first formal step in a multi-creditor workout: creditors agree not to enforce rights for defaults during a limited period, giving the borrower breathing space to prepare a survival strategy while creditors form a joint approach; it may also cover interim financing or deferral of principal/profit payments. For MSMEs a simplified template is contemplated, and in single-creditor relationships an informal understanding may suffice. Relevant to banks and their legal teams documenting NPL workouts.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential

# Finance Company Licensing Requirements

Licensing dimension of the amended Executive Regulation of the Finance Companies Control Law: which financing activities require a SAMA licence and how activity-specific licences (micro consumer finance, deferred payment/BNPL, debt-based crowdfunding, microfinance) are defined and delimited to a single activity. The supplied extract contains only the covering circular and the definitions chapter, so specific licensing conditions, applications and approvals must be read in the full regulation. Binds companies seeking or holding a finance licence in the Kingdom.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, governance/risk

# Template CCR5 - Composition of Collateral for CCR

Pillar 3 template CCR5 requires banks to disclose the composition of collateral posted and received in support of counterparty credit risk exposures arising from derivatives and securities financing transactions, including CCP-cleared trades. Reporting is by collateral type (cash by currency, sovereign and agency debt, corporate bonds, equities, other) and by segregated versus unsegregated status, at fair/carrying value. It is mandatory for all banks, semiannual, with fixed columns but flexible rows and an explanatory narrative.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

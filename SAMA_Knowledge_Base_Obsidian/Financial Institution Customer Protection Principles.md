# Financial Institution Customer Protection Principles

SAMA circular (Arabic) transmitting the first edition of the Financial Institutions Services Tariff Guide and notifying amendments to three fee-related provisions: the administrative-fee article of the updated Consumer Finance Regulations, the relevant special conduct rule in the Principles and Rules for Financial Institution Customer Protection, and the costs/fees clause of the Standard Consumer Finance Contract for Individuals. It governs what fees supervised financial institutions may charge for services and products, defines core terms (Financial Institution, Customer, documented channels, identification certificate, debt/liability-clearance documents, account statement) and caps total administrative fees on financing. It binds all entities under SAMA supervision, effective within the stated grace period from publication, and supersedes conflicting provisions.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, BNPL/finance, banking prudential

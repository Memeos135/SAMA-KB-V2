# Loan-to-Value Ratio

Sets out the definition and prudent calculation of the loan-to-value (LTV) ratio for real estate exposures — loan amount (including undrawn committed amounts, gross of provisions) divided by property value held at origination value, subject to downward revaluation on SAMA instruction or a permanent idiosyncratic value loss, and upward adjustment only for value-enhancing modifications. It also requires mortgage underwriting policies with defined repayment-ability metrics, including occupancy-type metrics where repayment depends on property cash flows. Binds banks applying the standardised approach to real estate exposures.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

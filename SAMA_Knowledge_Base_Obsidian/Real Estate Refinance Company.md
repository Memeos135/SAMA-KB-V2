# Real Estate Refinance Company

Defines the Real Estate Refinance Company as a joint stock company licensed by SAMA to carry on real estate refinance activity, together with the related defined terms (Real Estate Financier, Real Estate Finance, Borrower, Primary/Secondary Market, Real Estate Finance Contractual Rights). Scope limit: the Company must direct its services and products to real estate financiers, and may only deal with non-real estate financiers after obtaining SAMA's no-objection. Subject to SAMA supervision under both the Real Estate Finance Law and the Finance Companies Control Law regimes.

# Compliance Principles for Banks

Issued under the SAMA Law and the Banking Control Law, these Compliance Principles replace the earlier compliance manual for banks and set the framework for managing compliance risk. They define the compliance function, compliance policy, compliance unit, head of compliance, compliance officers and compliance risk, and allocate responsibilities to the board and senior management, including policy approval, reporting, cross-border/foreign operations issues and outsourcing of compliance tasks. They bind local commercial banks and licensed branches of foreign banks (where the branch head assumes board-level duties), guidance-only until end 2015 and mandatory thereafter.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential, AML/CTF

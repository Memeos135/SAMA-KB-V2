# Finance Lease & Registration Laws

## Why this community

Asset-based finance regimes in KSA — finance leasing and real estate finance/refinance — including contract registration, enforceability of executive bonds, standardised contracts and responsible lending, with an attached AML/CTF customer due diligence layer applying to the licensed entities involved.

## How members connect

- Classic hierarchy: the Finance Lease Law and Real Estate Finance Law (royal decrees) are elaborated by their Implementing Regulations, then operationalised by SAMA rules and circulars (contracts register procedures, executive bond extracts, standard real estate financing contracts).
- Registration chain: the Contracts Register is the pivot — registration rules create the record, and the executive bond extract rules convert a registered lease contract into an enforceable instrument, so both cite the Finance Lease Law.
- Refinance/secondary-market branch: Rules Governing Real Estate Refinance Companies and the Saudi Real Estate Refinance Company sit downstream of the Real Estate Finance Law, with governance and risk management obligations and Capital Market Law cross-references where securitisation touches the secondary market.
- AML/CTF overlay: the AML Law and Terrorist Crimes implementing regulations, CDD/KYC measures, FATF standards and the FIU attach to the same licensed financiers and to remittance/SARIE channels — a distinct regime whose 'Customer' concept must be reconciled with the lessee/borrower under the finance laws.
- Consumer-protection overlay: Responsible Lending Principles and the deductible-ratio limits constrain individual real estate and lease financing regardless of the contractual form.

# Third-Party Service Provider

Defines the Third-Party Service Provider as an entity undertaking an outsourced activity on behalf of a bank, expressly excluding head offices and related entities of foreign bank branches operating in KSA, and situates it against the definitions of Outsourcing and Overseas entities. It sets materiality factors (substitutability, ability to meet regulatory requirements, customer loss exposure, affiliation, sharing of personal/financial/credit or unpublished data, subcontracting complexity), SAMA and auditor access rights to records and data at provider premises, and register content on the provider. Outsourcing to a provider located overseas requires prior written SAMA no objection with specified information, given confidentiality and data-access risks under the Banking Control Law.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential, data/credit info

# Bank Licensing Criteria

## Why this community

Entry-level authorisation of banks in KSA: the substantive minimum criteria SAMA applies to licence a bank, plus the incremental conditions imposed on digital-only banking models.

## How members connect

- The general licensing guidelines set the baseline minimum criteria (ownership, capital, governance, fit-and-proper management, business plan) and anchor them to the Basel Core Principles as the supervisory benchmark.
- The digital-only guidance is an overlay, not a substitute: it incorporates the general criteria by reference and adds conditions tied to the 'Digital-Only Bank' defined term.
- Cross-regime linkage into technology risk: the digital-only overlay pulls the SAMA Cybersecurity Framework into the licensing assessment, so IT/cyber maturity becomes an authorisation gate, not only an ongoing supervisory expectation.
- Hierarchy to keep straight: Basel principles (international benchmark) → SAMA licensing guidelines → digital-only supplement.

# National Address Requirement for Postal Services

A SAMA circular (Arabic) reiterating that supervised financial institutions must supply the National Address when submitting requests to postal/courier providers for delivery of documents linked to customers' financial products and services. It follows a Transport General Authority decision obliging postal service providers to refuse shipments not addressed with the National Address from 1 January 2026, and reaffirms earlier SAMA instructions that the National Address registered with Saudi Post (SPL) is the primary reference for customer addresses. It binds all financial institutions supervised by SAMA.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, governance/risk, other

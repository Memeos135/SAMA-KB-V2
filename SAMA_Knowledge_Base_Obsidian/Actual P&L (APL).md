# Actual P&L (APL)

A defined-term node for Actual P&L (APL) in SAMA's market risk framework: the profit and loss produced by the bank's daily P&L process, including intraday trading, time effects and new or modified deals, but excluding fees, commissions and valuation adjustments capitalised elsewhere (e.g. CVA) or deducted from CET1. APL is compared against hypothetical P&L and model outputs in backtesting and profit-and-loss attribution, and governs which valuation adjustments enter the comparison and at what level (desk versus bank-wide). It binds banks seeking or maintaining internal models approach permission.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

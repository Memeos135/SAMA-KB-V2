# Total Eligible Provisions

Defines total eligible provisions for IRB purposes as the aggregate of specific provisions, partial write-offs, portfolio-specific and general provisions attributed to IRB-treated exposures, plus discounts on defaulted assets, while excluding provisions against securitisation exposures. It also sets the attribution rules where a bank uses the standardised approach for part of its book, generally pro rata to credit RWAs, subject to SAMA discretion over internal allocation methods. Binds banks using IRB, including partial-use banks.

**Regimes:** banking prudential

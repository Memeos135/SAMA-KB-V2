# Own-LGD Estimation

Sets out the requirements for banks' own estimates of loss given default under the advanced IRB approach: LGD must reflect economic downturn conditions where necessary and cannot fall below the long-run default-weighted average loss rate for that facility type, with explicit treatment of dependence between borrower and collateral/collateral provider risk and of currency mismatches. Also addresses partial recognition of collateral where the effect cannot be modelled, and LGD in repo-style transactions, guarantees and credit derivatives. Binds SAMA-licensed banks approved for A-IRB.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

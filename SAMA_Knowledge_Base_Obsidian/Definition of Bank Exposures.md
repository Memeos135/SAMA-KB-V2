# Definition of Bank Exposures

Defines the bank asset class as exposures to banks, certain securities firms and other financial institutions treated as banks, covered bonds, domestic PSEs not treated as sovereigns, and MDBs not eligible for a 0% risk weight, plus subordinated debt and regulatory capital instruments of those entities subject to stated conditions (not equity exposures, not deducted from capital or risk-weighted at 250% under SAMA's Basel III guidance). The related standardized-approach text sets the regulatory retail criteria and risk weights, including transactor treatment. Binds SAMA-supervised banks classifying and risk-weighting counterparty exposures.

**Regimes:** banking prudential

# Purchased Receivables Quantification

Sets out the eligibility and quantification treatment of purchased receivables under the IRB framework, splitting them into retail and corporate receivables and specifying when the top-down approach may replace obligor-by-obligor default risk assessment. It confirms that corporate purchased receivables may use A-IRB only where the individual obligors qualify, that retail receivables are A-IRB only, and that certain on-balance sheet uses require SAMA approval. Applies to banks adopting IRB, including the asset-class roll-out obligation.

**Regimes:** banking prudential

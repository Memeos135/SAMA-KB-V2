# Template CCR7 (RWA Flow IMM)

Pillar 3 template requiring a flow statement of RWA movements for counterparty credit risk exposures measured under the internal models method (IMM), broken down by driver (including model updates and methodology/policy changes specific to IMM). Interpretive guidance in the context clarifies that CCR7 splits by EAD methodology rather than risk-weighting methodology, and that IRB model changes affecting IMM-valued exposures should be captured in added rows. Binds banks disclosing under SAMA's Pillar 3 framework that have approval to use IMM.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

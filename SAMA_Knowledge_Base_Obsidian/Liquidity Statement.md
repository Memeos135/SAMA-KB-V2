# Liquidity Statement

Chapter 5 liquidity requirements for DTFCs plus completion instructions for the Appendix B liquidity statement. It mandates a board-approved liquidity risk management plan (management structures and MIS, net funding measurement, contingency funding, internal controls), a 20% minimum holding of liquid assets against deposit and short-term liabilities, statutory deposits with SAMA of at least 4% of deposit liabilities, a cap of 15 times total capital on deposit liabilities with a one-month remedy, and monthly reporting by the 15th business day. Binds every deposit-taking finance company; SAMA may vary the statutory deposit percentage.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, BNPL/finance, governance/risk

# GCC-NET POS Circular

## Why this community

Requirements applicable to POS acquiring and payment services routed through the GCC-NET regional switch, covering participation, service handling and cardholder-facing conditions.

## How members connect

- Standalone payments-regime instrument; obligations attach to banks/PSPs operating POS acceptance rather than to consumers directly.
- Links outward to the wider payment services and merchant acceptance framework, including merchant onboarding as a customer relationship for AML/KYB purposes.

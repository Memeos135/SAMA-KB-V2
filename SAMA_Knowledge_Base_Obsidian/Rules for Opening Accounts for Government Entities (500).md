# Rules for Opening Accounts for Government Entities (500)

Section 500 of the SAMA bank account rules, governing accounts for Saudi ministries and government entities (including donation accounts, non-budget-funded activity accounts and Wilayah trust-fund investment accounts) as well as for foreign states and GCC/non-GCC government and quasi-government bodies; section 600 covers clearance accounts. It requires Ministry of Finance approval routed through SAMA, naming the account in the entity's name with a stated purpose, and specified authorized-signatory documentation. It binds banks and, procedurally, the government entities seeking accounts.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk, AML/CTF

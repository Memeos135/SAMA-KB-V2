# Standardized Approach for Credit Risk

This node addresses the standardised approach (SA) for credit risk as applied in SAMA's Basel framework, including use of the simple or comprehensive approach with standard supervisory haircuts for credit risk mitigation and treatment of failed trades and non-DvP transactions. It also covers the split between SA-computed RWA and modelled (IRB) RWA in disclosure, and the five-year linear phase-in of the prohibition on IRB for equity exposures. It binds banks calculating credit risk RWA under SAMA rules.

**Regimes:** banking prudential

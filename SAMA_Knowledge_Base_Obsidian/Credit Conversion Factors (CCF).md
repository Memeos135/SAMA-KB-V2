# Credit Conversion Factors (CCF)

Concept node on credit conversion factors used to convert off-balance sheet commitments and undertakings into credit exposure equivalents for risk-weighted asset purposes, including the 10% CCF for unconditionally cancellable commitments and the rule to apply the lower of two applicable CCFs where an undertaking to provide a commitment exists. SAMA may impose higher CCFs where a bank's practical ability to cancel is constrained. Relevant to banks applying the standardised approach to credit risk and to related counterparty credit risk and credit derivative treatments.

**Regimes:** banking prudential

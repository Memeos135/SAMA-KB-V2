# Template KM1 - Key Metrics

Component of SAMA's Pillar 3 disclosure requirements covering the overview of risk management and key prudential metrics: Template KM1 (key metrics on capital, buffers, RWA, leverage ratio, LCR and NSFR), Template KM2 (TLAC, G-SIBs only), Table OVA (risk management approach) and Template OV1 (RWA overview). It sets out completion instructions, treatment of ECL transitional arrangements and pre-floor RWA/ratios, and cross-template linkages, binding banks subject to SAMA's Pillar 3 regime; TLAC disclosures are not required unless SAMA specifies otherwise.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

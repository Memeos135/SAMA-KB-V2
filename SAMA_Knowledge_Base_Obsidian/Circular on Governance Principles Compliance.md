# Circular on Governance Principles Compliance

A SAMA circular extending mandatory application of the Key Principles of Governance for financial institutions supervised by SAMA to all finance companies and payment service providers. It prescribes a compliance pathway: a current-state governance gap assessment, a board/managers-approved roadmap, submission of both to SAMA's compliance mailbox within the stated deadline, and full compliance within one year of the circular or by the end of the sitting board's term, whichever is earlier, with SAMA retaining discretion to extend on justified grounds.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, payments, BNPL/finance

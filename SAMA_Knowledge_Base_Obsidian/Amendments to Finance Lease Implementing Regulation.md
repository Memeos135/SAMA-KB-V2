# Amendments to Finance Lease Implementing Regulation

A circular notifying a Governor's decision amending several articles of the Implementing Regulation of the Finance Lease Law issued under the Law's Article 27 rule-making power, agreed with the Minister of Justice. The amendments cover Article 10(5) (lessor must obtain a SAMA no-objection letter for finance lease products offered to individuals or microfinance beneficiaries before launch, to ensure fairness of terms), Article 17 (contract signing to take place at the lessor's premises with the lessor bearing the related costs), and Article 25(2) (aligned with Article 9(8) of the Enforcement Law). It binds banks, finance companies and finance lease contract registration companies in the Kingdom.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, consumer protection

# Rules on Outsourcing

SAMA's Rules on Outsourcing (Version 2.0, December 2019) set the governance, policy, risk-assessment, contractual, monitoring, audit and documentation requirements for outsourcing arrangements. The Board retains ultimate responsibility for the outsourcing policy and all arrangements, must approve the policy covering Material business activities, and banks must notify SAMA of legal/regulatory breaches and file an annual outsourcing return within 30 business days of year-end. The rules bind banks licensed under the Banking Control Law, including local and foreign bank branches and banking subsidiaries in Saudi Arabia, and cover domestic, foreign and related-party service providers; insourcing under bank supervision and certain arrangements (market data vendors, clearing/settlement, correspondent banking, utilities) fall outside scope.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential

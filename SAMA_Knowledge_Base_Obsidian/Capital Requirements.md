# Capital Requirements

This node gathers the financial resources and capital adequacy obligations in the Implementing Regulation of the Payments and Payment Services Law (with a second SAMA source), covering the duty of payment system operators to maintain adequate financial resources and assess capital adequacy relative to the systems they operate. For unclassified payment systems it sets a floor expressed as at least six months of the highest operating cost of the system plus paid-up capital of ten million riyals. It binds payment system operators and, through the licensing chapter, payment service providers subject to SAMA licensing.

**Regimes:** payments, banking prudential, governance/risk

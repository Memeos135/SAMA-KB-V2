# Customer Care Department Establishment Controls

SAMA's second-edition Controls for Establishing a Customer Care Department in Banks (issued 1444H/2023, replacing the earlier complaint-unit controls), setting the minimum requirements banks must meet for customer care, complaint handling and financial education. It requires the department to be created by board decision, report directly to the most senior executive, be independent of other departments, be adequately resourced, and comprise at least three units (complaint handling, quality and performance analysis, awareness and financial education); the department head's appointment needs SAMA's prior written no-objection. Binds all banks licensed by SAMA to operate in the Kingdom.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, governance/risk

# Systemically Important Financial Institutions

A definitional node in the LEX Rules appendices explaining Systemically Important Financial Institutions per the FSB (institutions whose distress would materially disrupt the financial system) and the Basel Committee methodologies for identifying G-SIBs and D-SIBs, as adopted through named SAMA circulars. It also touches the definition of regulated and unregulated financial entities used when applying exposure treatments. It is relevant to banks determining whether tighter interbank exposure limits apply.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

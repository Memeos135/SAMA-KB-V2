# Aggregation of Capital Requirement

Node on aggregating components into the total regulatory capital requirement, covering how Tier 2 items are summed and how deductions (own instruments, reciprocal cross-holdings, investments in capital instruments and other TLAC liabilities of unconsolidated financial entities, 5%/10% thresholds) reduce the capital base. It links to the composition-of-capital disclosure rows and, for market risk, the aggregation of modelled and standardised charges into the total requirement. Binds banks calculating and disclosing regulatory capital.

**Regimes:** banking prudential

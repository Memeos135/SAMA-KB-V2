# Securitization Due Diligence Requirements

Establishes the due diligence banks must perform to use the securitization framework's risk weight approaches, failing which a 1250% risk weight applies to the exposure, alongside the general credit risk due diligence duty to understand counterparty risk profiles at origination and at least annually. It defines securitization and resecuritization exposures and the range of underlying instruments, and requires due diligence proportionate to the bank's size and complexity, performed at solo entity level where possible and demonstrable to SAMA. It binds all SAMA-supervised banks holding securitization or credit exposures.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

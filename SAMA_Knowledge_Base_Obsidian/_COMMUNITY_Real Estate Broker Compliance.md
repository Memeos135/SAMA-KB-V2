# Real Estate Broker Compliance

## Why this community

Compliance expectations placed on, or in relation to, licensed real estate brokers as a regulated counterparty within the KSA financial and payments perimeter.

## How members connect

- Standalone item; its legal weight comes from the interface between real estate brokerage licensing and SAMA-supervised entities dealing with brokers.
- Relevant to AML/CTF designated non-financial business and profession concerns, and to KYB/merchant onboarding where a broker is treated as a customer.
- Also engages escrow/account-handling and payment acceptance duties for property transactions.

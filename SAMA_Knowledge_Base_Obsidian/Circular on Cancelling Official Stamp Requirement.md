# Circular on Cancelling Official Stamp Requirement

A SAMA circular prohibiting licensed finance companies from requiring establishments and companies to affix an official stamp on documents and papers submitted in financing transactions, following Ministry of Commerce and Investment decisions and a Royal Order on improving investment-environment competitiveness. The prohibition applies without prejudice to Know Your Customer requirements, and is effective from the circular's date. It binds licensed finance companies.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, AML/CTF, consumer protection

# Bank Account Data Disclosure to Authorities

A SAMA circular (with attached instructions) setting the mechanism banks and licensed money-exchange businesses must follow when governmental or non-governmental bodies request customer documents, information, data or disclosure of banking relationships and accounts. It splits cases into those where direct disclosure is prohibited without SAMA's no-objection — criminal matters routed via the Public Prosecution, civil/enforcement matters via the competent judicial authorities, and other agencies' statutory powers, all channelled through SAMA — and a defined set of exceptions where direct disclosure is permitted. It binds banks and licensed exchangers in the Kingdom, which must decline non-conforming direct requests in writing citing this circular.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, data/credit info, banking prudential, other

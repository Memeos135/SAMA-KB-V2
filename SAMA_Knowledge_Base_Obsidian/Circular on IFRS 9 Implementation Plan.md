# Circular on IFRS 9 Implementation Plan

A SAMA circular requiring all licensed finance companies to begin immediate preparation for implementing IFRS 9 (Financial Instruments) — covering classification and measurement, impairment on a forward-looking expected-loss basis, and hedge accounting — replacing IAS 39, effective for periods on or after 1 January 2018 with early adoption permitted. Companies must submit quarterly progress reports to SAMA starting from end of Q1 2017 and acknowledge receipt within one week. It is grounded in Article 71 of the Implementing Regulation of the Finance Companies Control Law and binds finance companies operating in the Kingdom.

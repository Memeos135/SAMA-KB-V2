# SAMA Basel Framework

Concept node for the SAMA Basel framework (issued by circular in December 2022 and subsequent updates), the baseline prudential regime setting minimum capital requirements for credit risk, market risk and equity exposures. It is the reference standard against which Shari'ah-compliant contract stages are risk-weighted, and it continues to govern areas carved out of the Islamic add-on (e.g. ijarah-structured real estate finance exposures). Relevant to any question about which capital rulebook applies to a given exposure.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

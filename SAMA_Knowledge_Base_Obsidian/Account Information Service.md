# Account Information Service

This node covers the account information service (payment account information service) under the Implementing Regulation of the Payments and Payment Services Law, addressed in the part on requirements for providing payment account information and payment initiation services. It is relevant to licensing scope, customer consent and information/contract requirements for accessing payment account data. It binds SAMA-licensed providers of account information services and interacts with data protection and customer protection obligations.

**Regimes:** payments, data/credit info, consumer protection

# SEC-ERBA

Node covering the external ratings-based approach for securitisation exposures (SEC-ERBA) within SAMA's Basel-aligned capital framework, together with the Pillar 3 securitisation disclosure section that reports such exposures (Table SECA and templates SEC1–SEC4). It sets out how risk weights are derived from external or inferred credit assessments, the operational requirements for using those assessments, and alternative treatment for term and short-term STC securitisations; the disclosure part distinguishes all securitisation exposures (SEC1/SEC2) from those actually treated under the securitisation framework with recognised risk transfer (SEC3/SEC4). Binds banks licensed and supervised by SAMA that hold, originate, sponsor or invest in securitisation exposures.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

# Template MR1: Market Risk Standardised Approach

Template MR1 is the quantitative Pillar 3 disclosure of market risk capital requirements calculated under the standardised approach, broken down by risk class (e.g. interest rate, equity, FX, commodity, credit spread) and by sensitivities-based, default risk and residual risk components. It complements the internal models templates and the RWA overview table. Applies to banks subject to SAMA's Pillar 3 disclosure requirements.

**Regimes:** banking prudential

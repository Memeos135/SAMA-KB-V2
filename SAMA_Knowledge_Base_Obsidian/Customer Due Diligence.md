# Customer Due Diligence

Article 15 of SAMA's Agent Banking Regulation requires each Bank engaging in Agent Banking to maintain a CDD programme tailored to its circumstances, agent types and risk level, covering KYC, information security and data privacy/confidentiality. Agents must identify customers by means the Bank deems appropriate and verify the purpose and nature of the banking relationship; where an Agent doubts the credibility of customer information it must cease dealing with the customer and report to the Bank's MLRO. The obligation binds the Bank, which remains responsible for its Agents' compliance — a delegated-performance, non-delegable-liability model relevant to AML reliance analysis.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, banking prudential, governance/risk, data/credit info

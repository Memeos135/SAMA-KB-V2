# Non-centrally Cleared Derivatives

Concept node defining the entity scope of the SAMA margin framework: covered entities — all financial firms plus systemically important non-financial entities, the latter identified by group-wide aggregate month-end average notional of non-centrally cleared derivatives for March–May exceeding SAR 30 billion (inter-affiliate trades excluded). Central banks, sovereigns, multilateral development banks, the BIS and non-systemic non-financial firms are outside scope, and the requirements bite only where both counterparties are covered entities. It also sets the phased application from 1 September 2016 and the later thresholds (€50 billion, then €8 billion) for two-way initial margin subject to a €50 million threshold.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

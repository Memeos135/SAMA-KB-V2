# Approval Requirements and Controls

This section of the Banks Investment Rules covers portfolio monitoring, diversification, annual portfolio-level stress testing and resourcing, together with approval controls: banks must obtain SAMA's non-objection for investments requiring regulatory approval under the Banking Control Law and Ministerial Decree No. 3/2149, approved at portfolio level, with prior non-objection for changes to structure or limits. It also requires investment channels and product-level approval requirements to be set in bank policy, including that Money Market Fund investments be made through CMA-licensed entities and that Saudi riyal funds be invested onshore. It binds banks supervised by SAMA.

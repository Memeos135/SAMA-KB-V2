# Rating Overrides

Sets the rules for using external ratings in the standardised approach to credit risk: consistent use of SAMA-recognised ECAIs (no cherry-picking), use of global rating scales, and the multiple-ratings hierarchy (higher risk weight where two ratings differ; second-lowest logic where three or more). It also governs when an issue-specific or issuer rating may be extended to an unrated exposure, the requirement that the rating cover the full amount owed, and the bar on double-counting credit enhancement already reflected in an issue rating. Binds banks determining risk weights for rated and unrated exposures.

**Regimes:** banking prudential

# Circular on Equal Treatment in Financing and Insurance Coverage

A SAMA circular to banks and finance companies requiring equal, non-discriminatory access for all segments of society to financing products and to insurance coverage under finance lease contracts, based on the fair-treatment principles in the banking and finance customer protection principles. It links to SAMA's earlier circular to insurers prohibiting refusal of compulsory or comprehensive motor cover based on the age of the actual user of a leased vehicle, and bars financiers from refusing customers on that ground.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, BNPL/finance, other

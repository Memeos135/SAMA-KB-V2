# Rules Regulating Money Changing Business

The Rules Regulating Money Changing Business set SAMA's licensing, regulatory and supervisory framework for money changers, defining Money Changing Business, Money Changer, Customer, Invested Capital and Third Party, and stating objectives including financial inclusion, innovation and customer protection. They prohibit unlicensed practice or use of money-changer style titles, with a narrow carve-out allowing licensed hotels, hotel apartments and travel agencies to exchange currency for their own customers subject to AML Law compliance and sale of currency to a licensed bank or money changer. Management of each licensed Money Changer is responsible for compliance and for approving the required policies and procedures.

**Regimes:** payments, AML/CTF, consumer protection, governance/risk

# SME Financial Awareness

## Why this community

SAMA's promotion of financial awareness and education programmes aimed at SMEs, sitting at the softer end of the consumer/customer protection and market-conduct spectrum.

## How members connect

- Single-member cluster: an encouragement-style communication rather than a hard obligation, so compliance consequence is expectation-setting and supervisory dialogue.
- Links conceptually to financial literacy and fair-treatment duties owed to business customers in the banking and finance company conduct rules.
- Relevant when assessing whether SME-facing disclosure and onboarding practices meet SAMA conduct expectations.

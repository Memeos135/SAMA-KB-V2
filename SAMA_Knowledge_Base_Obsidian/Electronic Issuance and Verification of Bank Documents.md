# Electronic Issuance and Verification of Bank Documents

Rules (first issue) attached to a SAMA circular governing the electronic issuance of bank documents and the electronic verification of their authenticity. They require banks to process all customer document requests through technical systems, issue documents electronically in Arabic (and English on request), include issuance dates and confidentiality/authenticity markings, provide a verification means on every document, and comply with the Personal Data Protection Law, the Electronic Transactions Law and SAMA's information security and business continuity frameworks; a minimum list of documents (bank certificate, debt confirmation, account statement, clearance letter, IBAN certificate) must be available. Binding on banks licensed under the Banking Control Law, effective from the date stated in the circular.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, consumer protection, data/credit info

# Risk-Theoretical P&L (RTPL)

Concept node for the risk-theoretical P&L used in the P&L attribution (PLA) test under the internal models approach, produced by a trading desk's risk management model from the risk factors it captures. It underpins whether a desk remains IMA-eligible, and connects to the framework's defined terms (risk factor, risk position, risk bucket, risk class, sensitivities) and to rules on aligning and documenting RTPL input data with SAMA notification and justification.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

# Wire Transfer

Section 14 of SAMA's AML/CTF requirements governing cross-border and domestic wire transfers in any currency (including serial and cover payments) sent, received or processed in Saudi Arabia, including card- and mobile-initiated transfers. It sets the minimum originator and beneficiary data that must accompany and be verified with each transfer, a customer declaration on knowledge of the beneficiary and legitimate purpose, batch/combined transfer treatment, a domestic exemption with a three-working-day information response deadline, obligations of receiving and intermediary institutions to detect and act on missing information, confidentiality of exchanged data, and currency restrictions on inbound transfers routed to beneficiaries abroad. Binds financial institutions and intermediary institutions operating in Saudi Arabia.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, payments, sanctions/TFS

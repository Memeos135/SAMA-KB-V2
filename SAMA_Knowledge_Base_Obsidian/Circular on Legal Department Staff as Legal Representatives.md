# Circular on Legal Department Staff as Legal Representatives

A SAMA circular informing finance companies that, following a Minister of Justice decision adding a provision to the Implementing Regulation of the Code of Law Practice, legal department employees of a private legal person are treated as its legal representative once licensed by the General Department of Advocacy and holding a power of attorney from the authorised signatory. Such employees may litigate for the finance company before competent judicial bodies in up to three cases at a time, subject to listed application documents and conditions.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, other

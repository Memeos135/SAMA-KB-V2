# Debt/EBITDA Ratio

Concept node on the Debt/EBITDA leverage measure used in SAMA's problem-loan guidelines as a proxy indicator in the initial viability assessment of a distressed borrower, signalling excessive leverage and repayment risk beyond a bank-set threshold. It sits within the broader illustrative (non-prescriptive) set of liquidity, solvency/leverage and profitability ratios that Workout Units — independent of the original loan approval — apply, with cash-flow analysis substituted where MSME financials are unreliable. Guidance for banks assessing borrower viability and restructuring options.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential

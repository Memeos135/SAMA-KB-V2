# Counterparty Credit Risk Framework

Bridging provisions in SAMA's credit risk capital rules that route exposures giving rise to counterparty credit risk — OTC and exchange-traded derivatives, long settlement transactions and securities financing transactions — to the exposure-amount calculation in SAMA's Counterparty Credit Risk (CCR) Framework for RWA purposes. Adjacent material covers credit conversion factors for off-balance sheet commitments, including unconditionally cancellable commitments and SAMA's discretion to require higher CCFs. Applies to banks licensed and supervised by SAMA.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

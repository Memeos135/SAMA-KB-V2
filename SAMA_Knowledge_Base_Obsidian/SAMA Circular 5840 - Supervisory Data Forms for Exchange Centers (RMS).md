# SAMA Circular 5840 - Supervisory Data Forms for Exchange Centers (RMS)

A SAMA circular addressed to exchange centres setting out the supervisory data returns to be filed to the Central Bank through its regulatory reporting portal, including monthly returns, total sales and purchases, currency export and import, and bank sales/purchase forms. It fixes the use of SAMA-provided templates, submission and validation steps, return due dates, and the resubmission route for corrections. Relevant to regulatory reporting obligations of licensed money exchange businesses.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, other

# Professional Risk

A concept node within SAMA's operational risk guidance for banks, defining professional risk as the exposure arising directly from providing financial products and services, and treating it as a leading and fast-growing component of operational (pure) risk. It covers professional errors and omissions by staff (mis-transfers, unauthorised trading, booking errors) and the resulting liability toward customers and third parties, noting that losses on the bank's own account are uninsurable and must be controlled internally. It is addressed to directors and senior management of banks operating in the Kingdom, as guidance rather than a prescriptive rule set.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential

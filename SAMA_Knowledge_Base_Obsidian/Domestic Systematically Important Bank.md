# Domestic Systematically Important Bank

Defines Domestic Systemically Important Banks — banks whose failure would seriously harm the domestic financial system or real economy — by reference to the BCBS D-SIB framework adopted through identified SAMA circulars. In the LEX Rules this status reduces the permissible interbank exposure limit to 15% of the lending bank's eligible capital where either the lending or counterparty bank is a D-SIB (or G-SIB). Applies to banks subject to SAMA's large exposure regime.

**Regimes:** banking prudential

# Dispute Resolution

Addresses dispute resolution in SAMA's risk mitigation requirements for non-centrally cleared OTC derivatives. Banks must agree and document with counterparties the mechanism for determining when discrepancies in material terms or valuations amount to disputes and how they are resolved as soon as practicable, with material disputes escalated to senior management and the Board under clear criteria. It binds SAMA-supervised banks in their bilateral derivative relationships.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential, other

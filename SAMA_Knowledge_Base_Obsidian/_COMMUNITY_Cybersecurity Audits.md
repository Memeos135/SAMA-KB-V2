# Cybersecurity Audits

## Why this community

Audit of cyber security controls — the assurance obligation requiring internal (and where applicable external) audit coverage of the cyber security domain.

## How members connect

- Single-node cluster: audit coverage is an enforceable, standalone control requirement within the cyber security framework's governance layer.
- Obligation chain: audit findings must be tracked to remediation, linking this node to the institution's compliance and risk-reporting duties toward SAMA.
- Distinct from periodic review — audit is the three-lines-of-defence assurance function, so scope, independence and reporting lines are the key legal questions.

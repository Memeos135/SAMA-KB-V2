# Electronic Crime

A sub-element of criminal risk in SAMA's Operational Risk Management Guide dealing with computer and telecommunications-related crime against banks, spanning ATM, credit card, POS, corporate/cash management, EDI and retail electronic channels. The accompanying insurance discussion explains Electronic and Computer Crime (ECC) cover as a companion to the Bankers Blanket Bond / Financial Institution Bond, responding to third-party fraud and unexplained loss involving computer systems, electronic data, communications, transmissions, forged fax and voice-initiated transfers. Directed at banks assessing how to control versus transfer electronic crime exposure.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential, payments

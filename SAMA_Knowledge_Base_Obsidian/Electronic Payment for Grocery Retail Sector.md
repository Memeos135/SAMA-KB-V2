# Electronic Payment for Grocery Retail Sector

A SAMA circular implementing the mandatory provision of electronic payment acceptance by all groceries and food supply stores in the Kingdom from the stated date, as part of the anti-commercial-concealment programme and cash-reduction strategy. It obliges banks and payment service providers to be ready to open merchant bank accounts and e-wallets, to receive and process requests for SAMA-authorised acceptance channels (POS terminals and QR) across all service channels in line with the applicable regulatory and operational rules, to apply Merchant Category Code 5411 for this activity, and to brief staff internally.

**Regimes:** payments, governance/risk

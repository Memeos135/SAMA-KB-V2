# Responsible Finance Government Support

## Why this community

Rules on how government subsidy/support is calculated and applied within responsible-finance products, affecting cost-of-borrowing disclosure and the amounts a consumer is actually charged.

## How members connect

- Single-member cluster; its legal weight comes from cross-regime effects rather than internal edges.
- Feeds the finance-company and real estate finance regimes' pricing, APR/total-cost disclosure and early-settlement calculations.
- Consumer protection consequence: support amounts must be reflected transparently in the contract and in consumer-facing statements.

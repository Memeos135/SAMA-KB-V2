# Potential Future Exposure (PFE)

Concept node for potential future exposure, the forward-looking add-on component of counterparty credit risk exposure at default under the standardised approach (SA-CCR), combined with replacement cost and scaled by a multiplier recognising excess collateral or negative mark-to-market. It is the driver of EAD used in credit risk RWA and in the counterparty credit risk Pillar 3 templates. Applies to banks with derivative and securities financing exposures in both banking and trading books.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

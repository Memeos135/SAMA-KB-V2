# Prompt Execution of Judicial Rulings Circular

A Governor-level SAMA circular (Arabic) directing supervised entities to promptly receive and execute judicial rulings and decisions issued against them without delay or procrastination, and to raise any objection through the competent channels within the prescribed statutory periods rather than deferring compliance. It binds banks, insurance and reinsurance companies and insurance-related free professions, finance companies, exchange businesses, and credit information companies.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, consumer protection, other

# Core Cyber Threat Intelligence Principles

Domain 1 of SAMA's Financial Sector Cyber Threat Intelligence Principles, setting the baseline expectations for planning, collecting, processing, analysing and disseminating threat intelligence around the intelligence lifecycle. It covers foundational principles such as defined CTI roles and responsibilities, a dedicated and resourced threat intelligence team, internal and external communication channels, intelligence sharing, delivery of actionable intelligence and continuous improvement. It binds Member Organizations supervised by SAMA, including those that outsource their CTI capability.

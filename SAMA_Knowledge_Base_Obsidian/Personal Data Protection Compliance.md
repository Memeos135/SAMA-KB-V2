# Personal Data Protection Compliance

A SAMA circular reinforcing compliance with personal data protection requirements for customer data, referencing the Personal Data Protection Law and SAMA's data governance policies, rules and controls. It responds to observed practices where individual customers were required to disclose personal data not necessary for the product or service, directly or via a third party, and requires institutions to align with SDAIA and NDMO rules, review disclosure practices, secure data, use it only for the purpose collected, and report remedial actions to SAMA by a set deadline. Binds all financial institutions supervised by SAMA.

**Regimes:** data/credit info, consumer protection, governance/risk

# Capital to Risk-Weighted Assets

## Why this community

Prudential capital adequacy reporting — the periodic return through which a supervised institution reports regulatory capital against risk-weighted exposures to SAMA.

## How members connect

- A single reporting instrument; the obligation is one of accurate, timely submission in SAMA's prescribed form, distinct from the underlying capital-adequacy calculation rules.
- Depends downstream on asset classification and provisioning outcomes, since credit-risk weights and capital deductions reflect those determinations.
- Isolated in the graph — read alongside the applicable capital framework and reporting circulars rather than as a self-contained standard.

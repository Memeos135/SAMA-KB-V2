# Financial Statements Comparison Form

A SAMA template (Version 2.0) from the Exchange Centres Supervision Division for comparative quarterly and annual financial statements of exchange centres, covering balance-sheet lines (foreign currency inventory, amounts due from/to related parties for cash drawings, credit sales and cash support, fixed assets, zakat provision, end-of-service benefits, equity and retained earnings) and income-statement lines. Income-statement data is reported for the three-month period in quarterly filings and for the full year in annual filings.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential

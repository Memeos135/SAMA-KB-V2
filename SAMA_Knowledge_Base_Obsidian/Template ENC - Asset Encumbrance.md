# Template ENC - Asset Encumbrance

Pillar 3 template requiring disclosure of encumbered versus unencumbered on-balance sheet assets at period-end carrying amounts, using a template-specific definition of "encumbered assets" (restricted from liquidation, sale, transfer or assignment by legal, regulatory, contractual or other limitations) that differs from the LCR definition by excluding the monetisation aspect. Reported semiannually on the regulatory scope of consolidation including securitisation exposures, with narrative on material changes. Mandatory for all banks under SAMA's Pillar 3 framework.

**Regimes:** banking prudential

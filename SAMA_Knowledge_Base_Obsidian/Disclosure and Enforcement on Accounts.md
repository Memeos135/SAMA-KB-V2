# Disclosure and Enforcement on Accounts

Governs how banks respond to requests to disclose, and to enforce against, customer balances, accounts and banking relationships. Defines enforcement as blocking, compulsory deduction, cheque issuance and transfer from customer accounts; requires strict confidentiality; channels requests through SAMA except where SAMA's instructions provide otherwise; and mandates a full relationship search (active, closed, suspended and inoperative accounts, deposits, safe deposit boxes, credit cards, remittance memberships and other products), including sole proprietorships and company participations of the person searched, in the form and within the timeframe SAMA specifies. Binds banks; relevant also to court-related accounts and enforcement court arrangements described in the same instrument.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk, other

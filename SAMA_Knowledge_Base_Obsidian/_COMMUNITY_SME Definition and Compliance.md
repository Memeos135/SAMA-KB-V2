# SME Definition and Compliance

## Why this community

Scope-setting for how SME status is defined in KSA and when SAMA-supervised entities may apply a lighter or optional compliance treatment to dealings with SMEs.

## How members connect

- All three instruments turn on a single defined term - the SME classification adopted by the General Authority for SMEs (Monshaat) resolution - which the SAMA circulars import rather than restate.
- Hierarchy runs from the underlying SME definition resolution to SAMA's defining circular, then to the operative circular that attaches optional compliance consequences to entities meeting that definition.
- The linkage is cross-referential: the optional-compliance circular is unusable without the definitional circular, since eligibility (headcount/revenue thresholds) determines who benefits.
- Practical consequence: misclassifying a counterparty as an SME misapplies the relief; SME status is a scope filter, not a waiver of AML/CTF or consumer-protection duties that apply on other grounds.

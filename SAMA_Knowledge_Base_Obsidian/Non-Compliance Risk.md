# Non-Compliance Risk

Defined-term node for non-compliance risk (compliance risk) in SAMA's Compliance Principles: exposure to legal or administrative sanctions, financial loss, or reputational damage arising from failure to observe laws, regulations and instructions, financial-crime/AML requirements, or codes of conduct and sound professional practice. It frames the mandate of the compliance function and the scope of the compliance policy, and applies to all banks and foreign bank branches licensed in KSA.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, AML/CTF, banking prudential

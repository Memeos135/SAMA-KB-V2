# Code of Conduct and Work Ethics in Financial Institutions

SAMA principles on conduct and work ethics in financial institutions, setting the minimum standards of professional behaviour, integrity, transparency, objectivity and loyalty expected of institution personnel. Topics include stakeholder dealings, AML/CTF and suspicious transactions, anti-bribery and corruption, gifts and hospitality, conflicts of interest, confidentiality and disclosure, misuse of inside information and market manipulation, whistleblowing on actual or potential violations, remuneration and incentives, and consequences of non-compliance. They bind all personnel — board and committee members, executives, employees, contractors, consultants and third-party staff — of SAMA-supervised banks, finance and leasing registration companies, insurers/reinsurers and credit information companies, with the board required to adopt a conforming policy.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, AML/CTF, consumer protection

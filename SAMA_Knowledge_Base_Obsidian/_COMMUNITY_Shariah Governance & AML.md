# Shariah Governance & AML

## Why this community

A mixed prudential/Shariah/financial-crime cluster: Basel-derived capital, large exposure, margin and liquidity requirements for banks, alongside Shariah governance for finance companies and Islamic banks, and the AML/CTF compliance and suspicious-transaction reporting chain.

## How members connect

- Prudential spine: Large Exposure Rules, margin requirements for non-centrally cleared derivatives and the LCR/NSFR guidance all cite the Basel/BCBS-IOSCO source frameworks and share definitional building blocks (exposure value, connected counterparties, eligible collateral, HQLA tiers).
- Shariah limb: Shariah Governance Instructions, the Shari'ah Committee and Profit Sharing Investment Account rules (Mudarabah, PER/IRR reserves) connect to prudential text through Shari'ah-compliant bank treatments in the liquidity framework.
- AML/CTF limb: transaction monitoring feeds suspicious transaction reporting to the Financial Intelligence Unit, with the AML/CTF Compliance Function as the accountable owner.
- Senior Management and board-level committees (ALCO, Shari'ah Committee, compliance function) are the shared governance nodes tying the three limbs to one accountable body.

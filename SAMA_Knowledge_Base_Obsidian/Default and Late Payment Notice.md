# Default and Late Payment Notice

The late payment and default provisions (Article 8, with the related contract-termination procedure in Article 9) of SAMA's mandatory standard consumer finance contract for individuals; the consequences of late instalment payment must also be flagged in the signed contract summary sheet. As standard-form terms they are non-negotiable and govern the financier's remedies and the beneficiary's exposure on missed instalments. Binds banks and finance companies providing consumer finance to individuals.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, BNPL/finance

# KYC and AML/CFT Requirements

Node within SAMA's bank account opening and operation rules covering customer identification, due diligence and AML/CFT-linked controls that banks must apply when establishing and maintaining relationships, including correspondent banking. It requires senior management approval for new correspondent relationships, AML/CFT self-certification and control assessment of the correspondent (including no shell-bank access), adverse-information screening, restrictions on how correspondent accounts may be used, and prohibits accounts for certain non-resident financial institutions, funds and investment companies. Binds SAMA-licensed banks, and is expressly to be read together with the AML Law, its Implementing Regulation and the counter-terrorism financing law and guidelines.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, banking prudential, governance/risk

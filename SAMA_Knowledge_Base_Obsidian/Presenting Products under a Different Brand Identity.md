# Presenting Products under a Different Brand Identity

SAMA circular setting minimum standards where a financial institution offers or promotes products under a brand different from its original corporate identity and trade name, issued under the SAMA Law and framed as consumer protection, disclosure and transparency. Requirements include addressing brand/identity impersonation (cyber and fraud risk) in the risk management strategy and policy, engaging brand-protection service providers, updating usage and data-privacy policies, registering the new or updated trademark and notifying SAMA, running an awareness campaign, and displaying the trade name together with the brand in customer calls, first SMS contact, on websites and alongside any statement of SAMA supervision, including the licence number. Binds all SAMA-supervised financial institutions from its date.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, governance/risk

# Template SEC2 - Securitisation in Trading Book

Pillar 3 disclosure template for securitisation exposures held in the trading book, reported by the bank's role (originator, sponsor, investor). Like SEC1 it captures all such exposures regardless of whether they qualify for the securitisation capital framework; related trading book capital charges are disclosed under the market risk section rather than in SEC3/SEC4. Applies to banks subject to SAMA's Pillar 3 disclosure requirements.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

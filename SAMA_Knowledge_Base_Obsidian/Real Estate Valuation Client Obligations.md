# Real Estate Valuation Client Obligations

A SAMA circular addressed to banks, finance companies (including real estate finance) and insurance companies in their capacity as clients of real estate valuation services. It requires them to deal only with valuers holding full membership of the Saudi Authority for Accredited Valuers, to govern the valuation process by separating financing/underwriting staff and decisions from valuer selection and report receipt, and prohibits any conduct that influences or coerces the valuer or valuation firm (fee withholding, blacklisting threats, inducements, supplying target or expected values, dictating valuation methods, imposing inadequate timeframes, linking fees to work volume, or excessive bank guarantee demands). It also requires supplying sufficient information on the asset, setting acceptance criteria for financeable properties, and bearing related costs.

# Quality Assurance & Improvement Program

This node covers the quality assurance and improvement programme required of the Internal Audit Unit under SAMA's Principles of Internal Audit for local banks, alongside related requirements on audit information technology, periodic reporting to the Audit Committee, and retention of records, documents and reports. It governs how banks evaluate and evidence the quality and effectiveness of their internal audit activity. It binds locally licensed banks, their internal audit units and Audit Committees.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential

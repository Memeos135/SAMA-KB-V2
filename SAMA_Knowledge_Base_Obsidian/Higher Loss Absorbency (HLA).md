# Higher Loss Absorbency (HLA)

Sets out the scoring, bucketing and Higher Loss Absorbency (HLA) surcharge for designated D-SIBs: banks scoring above a 10% cut-off are automatically classified as D-SIBs (SAMA may add others by supervisory judgment), and are allocated to one of five buckets driving the HLA charge. HLA must be met entirely with Common Equity Tier 1 and sits on top of the bank-specific target capital adequacy ratio set by SAMA; SAMA may also impose recovery and resolution plans and other measures. Binds banks designated as D-SIBs.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

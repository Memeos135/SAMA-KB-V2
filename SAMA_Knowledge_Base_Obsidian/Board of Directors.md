# Board of Directors

Cross-instrument concept node for the Board of Directors of a SAMA-supervised institution, aggregating the board's duties as they appear across governance, internal audit, compliance and related principles. It captures the board's ultimate responsibility for approving strategy, policies, charters and control frameworks, for appointing and empowering control functions, and for overseeing risk management and the internal control environment. It binds boards of finance companies, real estate refinance companies and other institutions supervised by SAMA under the referenced instruments.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, BNPL/finance, banking prudential

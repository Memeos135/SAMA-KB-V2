# Technical Sorting Instructions for 6th Issue Saudi Banknotes

SAMA's technical sorting instructions for the sixth issue of Saudi banknotes, setting uniform fitness criteria, counting/sorting controls, counterfeit and material-defect handling, quality levels, density measurement, sensor and quality-control requirements, and follow-up obligations. It applies to "financial institutions" as defined for these purposes — banks, cash centres and exchange centres supervised by SAMA — with staged effective dates by denomination and a requirement to coordinate with SAMA to obtain specimen notes for machine programming.

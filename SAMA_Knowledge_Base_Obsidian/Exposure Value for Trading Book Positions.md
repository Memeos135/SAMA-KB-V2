# Exposure Value for Trading Book Positions

Appendix VII of SAMA's Large Exposure (LEX) Rules for Banks, setting out how banks must measure exposure values for trading book positions and aggregate them with banking book exposures to the same counterparty. It confirms that single-counterparty default risk in instruments such as bonds, equities, derivatives and options is captured by the large exposure limit, while commodity and currency concentrations are not, and specifies valuation conventions for swaps, futures, forwards, credit derivatives, credit-linked notes and options. It binds all banks licensed and supervised by SAMA.

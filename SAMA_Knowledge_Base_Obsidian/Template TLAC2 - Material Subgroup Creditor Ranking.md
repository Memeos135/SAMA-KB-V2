# Template TLAC2 - Material Subgroup Creditor Ranking

Pillar 3 disclosure template TLAC2, requiring a material subgroup entity that has issued internal TLAC to a G-SIB resolution entity to disclose its creditor hierarchy at legal entity level, showing nominal capital and liabilities, excluded liabilities, TLAC-eligible amounts and residual maturity buckets. It binds only banks designated as G-SIBs, completed per material subgroup entity within each resolution group and grouped by resolution group, semiannually, in fixed format with narrative on jurisdiction-specific credit hierarchies. Relevant only from the TLAC conformance date; not applicable to foreign bank branches in KSA.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

# Benchmark Rate Reporting

## Why this community

Supervisory reporting of interest/return rates on banks' assets and liabilities, and the reference standards used to classify and price those exposures.

## How members connect

- The reporting guidelines are the parent obligation; both other members are incorporated by reference as mandatory classification and pricing inputs.
- ISIC4 fixes the economic-activity taxonomy used to segment reported balances, so counterparty classification errors propagate directly into reporting non-compliance.
- SAIBOR is the referenced benchmark for rate reporting, linking this cluster to benchmark-integrity and pricing-transparency expectations.
- Hierarchy is guideline-level instruction relying on external standards, not standalone rules — read the guidelines first to see the scope of incorporation.

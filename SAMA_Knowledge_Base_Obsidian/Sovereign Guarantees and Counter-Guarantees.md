# Sovereign Guarantees and Counter-Guarantees

Provision within the standardised credit risk mitigation rules allowing the 0% risk weight for Saudi sovereign/SAMA exposures to be extended to the guaranteed portion of an exposure where the guarantee is denominated in and the exposure funded in Saudi Riyal. It also permits indirect sovereign counter-guarantees to be treated as sovereign guarantees where the counter-guarantee covers all credit risk elements, operational requirements for guarantees are met, and SAMA is satisfied the cover is robust. Binds banks applying the standardised approach to credit risk.

**Regimes:** banking prudential

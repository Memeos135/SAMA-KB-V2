# Compliance Principles for Finance and Real Estate Refinance Companies

SAMA's Compliance Principles establishing the compliance unit/function, the compliance officer's role and the unit's duties, including reporting lines to the audit committee, staff training and awareness, reporting irregularities to SAMA and the audit committee, quantitative and qualitative measurement of non-compliance risk, and maintaining a classified database of applicable instructions. They also require periodic review of the customer care department (semi-annual) and of collection activities or outsourced collection agents (annual), with real estate refinance companies excluded from those two reviews, and map the wider regulatory perimeter (AML/CTF, BNPL rules, consumer protection, anti-fraud, debt collection, governance and conduct instructions). They bind finance and real estate refinance companies, and guide finance support and finance lease contract registration companies, which SAMA may require to apply them.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, BNPL/finance, AML/CTF, consumer protection

# Shariah Committee

The provisions governing the bank's Shariah committee: meetings at least quarterly, majority quorum and majority voting with a casting vote for the chair, a 75% individual attendance requirement, and documented minutes and voting records. It also sets the committee's substantive mandate — supervising Shariah compliance of Islamic banking transactions, issuing binding Shariah decisions the board must rely on, and reviewing compliance and internal Shariah audit findings. Disclosure of member CVs and performance-assessment procedures, plus integrity screening of nominees, applies.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential

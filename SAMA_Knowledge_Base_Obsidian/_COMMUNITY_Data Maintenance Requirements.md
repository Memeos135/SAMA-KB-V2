# Data Maintenance Requirements

## Why this community

Record-keeping and data retention obligations attached to risk measurement and rating systems — what data must be collected, maintained and made available to supervisors and validators over time.

## How members connect

- Single-node cluster: the data maintenance duty stands as a standalone compliance obligation rather than part of a cross-referenced chain.
- Functionally dependent on upstream regimes (rating/model governance, capital calculation) because retained data is the evidentiary basis for demonstrating compliance.
- Interacts with AML/CTF and credit information retention rules where the same records serve multiple regulatory purposes; retention periods should be reconciled to the longest applicable requirement.

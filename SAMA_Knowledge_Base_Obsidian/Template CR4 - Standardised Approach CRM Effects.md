# Template CR4 - Standardised Approach CRM Effects

Template CR4 is a Pillar 3 quantitative disclosure showing, for portfolios under the standardised approach for credit risk, exposures before and after credit conversion factors and credit risk mitigation, together with resulting RWA and RWA density. It sits alongside definitions of the standardised approach (including simple vs comprehensive CRM treatment with supervisory haircuts) used across the disclosure framework. It binds SAMA-supervised banks using the credit risk standardised approach.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

# On-Balance Sheet Netting

Concept node distinguishing accounting balance sheet netting from regulatory (Basel) netting, as reflected in the LI1/LI2 reconciliation templates and in margin-agreement netting mechanics. It clarifies that regulatory netting rules may allow more or less offsetting than the accounting framework, producing incremental netting adjustments in the reconciliation between financial statements and regulatory exposure amounts. Relevant to SAMA-supervised banks preparing Pillar 3 disclosures and calculating counterparty exposure.

**Regimes:** banking prudential

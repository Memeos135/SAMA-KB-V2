# Template CCR4 - IRB CCR Exposures by PD Scale

Pillar 3 disclosure template CCR4 within SAMA's Pillar 3 Disclosure Requirements Framework, setting out the semiannual, fixed-format disclosure of counterparty credit risk exposures broken down by regulatory portfolio and PD band, including EAD post-CRM, average PD, obligor numbers, average LGD/maturity and RWA. It binds banks that use foundation or advanced IRB approaches to compute RWA for CCR exposures, requiring separate templates where both F-IRB and A-IRB are used, and excludes CVA charges and CCP-cleared exposures. Rows for portfolio breakdown are set by SAMA, and a narrative on model scope, RWA coverage and period-on-period changes is expected.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

# D-SIBs Framework Saudi Arabia

SAMA's August 2014 circular and enclosed framework establishing how domestic systemically important banks (D-SIBs) are identified and designated in Saudi Arabia, using an indicator-based measurement approach aligned with the BCBS D-SIB methodology. Designated banks must meet additional Higher Loss Absorbency capital requirements, effective 1 January 2016. Addressed to all banks operating in the Kingdom, at senior management level.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

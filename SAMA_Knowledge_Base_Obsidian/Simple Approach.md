# Simple Approach

A node grouping material on approach selection and use within the credit risk framework — the securitisation hierarchy (SEC-ERBA, SEC-IAA, SEC-IRBA), equity investments in funds (look-through, mandate-based and fall-back approaches), unsettled transactions, and the conditions for phased rollout and partial use of the IRB approach. It requires banks adopting IRB for an asset class in a business unit to apply it to all exposures in that class and to agree a realistic implementation plan with SAMA, with no capital relief for intra-group transactions during rollout. Binds banks moving between standardised and internal approaches.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

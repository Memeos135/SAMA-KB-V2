# Credit Record (Credit Information Report)

The Credit Record concept as defined in the Executive Regulation of the Credit Information Law: a report issued by a licensed credit information company containing credit information about a Consumer (natural or legal person having credit dealings). Related defined terms include credit information, public records (specialised funds, banks granting government loans, judicial bodies, government committees, bankruptcy and insolvency files), negative information and adverse decision — a decision by a Member against the consumer's interest based on the credit record. It is relevant to bureaus compiling records and to Members relying on them for credit decisions, consumer disclosure and dispute handling.

**Regimes:** data/credit info, consumer protection

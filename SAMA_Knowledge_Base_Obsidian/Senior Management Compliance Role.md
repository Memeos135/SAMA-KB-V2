# Senior Management Compliance Role

Node covering senior management's compliance responsibilities under SAMA's Compliance Principles. "Senior Management" is defined as the executive leadership of a local bank (CEO, managing director, general manager) and the senior executives who run the bank's business and propose and implement strategic decisions, and the branch head for foreign bank branches. It is the layer that must implement the board-approved compliance policy and act on the compliance function's advice and reporting.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential

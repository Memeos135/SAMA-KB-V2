# SFT Collateral Composition

## Why this community

Pillar 3 counterparty credit risk disclosure of collateral used in securities financing transactions and derivative exposures.

## How members connect

- Standalone template within the CCR disclosure series; its scope is set by the counterparty credit risk and collateral treatment rules, which supply the defined transaction types.
- Links outward to the CRM and CCR exposure templates so that posted and received collateral is presented consistently across the disclosure set.

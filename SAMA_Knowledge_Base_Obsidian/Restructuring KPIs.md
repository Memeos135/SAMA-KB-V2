# Restructuring KPIs

The performance-measurement strand of SAMA's restructuring guidance, listing metrics banks should use to assess the efficiency of workout activity (volume and value of evaluations, agreed modified solutions and resolved positions over a period; length of approval steps) and its effectiveness (success rate of each restructuring solution by portfolio segment). It links to the broader NPL strategy requirement for monitoring, annual review and defined roles and reporting lines. Directed at banks' management and workout functions.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential

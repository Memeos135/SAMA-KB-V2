# Financing Value Management Account

A SAMA circular (Arabic) addressed to debt-based crowdfunding institutions operating in the Kingdom, issued under the Central Bank Law and the Finance Companies Control Law, on pooled accounts used to manage financing value. It requires that such accounts be used solely to manage participants' financing amounts and be segregated from the institution's own funds, be named "Financing Value Management Account – [name of the debt-based crowdfunding institution]", and that SAMA be notified on opening or closing them at banks in the Kingdom. It also mandates a written policy and controls on account operation (authority matrix, multiple-control principle by risk, periodic reporting to senior management, no linkage to the institution's own financial obligations or rights), documented reconciliation of executed financing volumes against the pooled account balance, and that the bank account agreement reflect the regulatory requirements and allocate responsibilities between the bank and the institution.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, governance/risk, consumer protection

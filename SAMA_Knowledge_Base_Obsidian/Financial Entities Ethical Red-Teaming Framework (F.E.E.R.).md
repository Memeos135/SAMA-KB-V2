# Financial Entities Ethical Red-Teaming Framework (F.E.E.R.)

SAMA's Financial Entities Ethical Red Teaming (F.E.E.R.) Framework sets out an intelligence-led, threat-based cyber-attack simulation regime for SAMA-supervised member organizations, structured in four phases (preparation, threat intelligence/scenario, execution, lessons learned) with defined Green, White, Blue and Red Team roles. It governs appointment of a Test Manager by SAMA, scoping of critical information assets, procurement and qualification of a Red Teaming Provider (contractual terms, NDA, Letter of Authorization, staff certification), execution controls, reporting and remediation tracking. It binds member organizations undertaking red teaming exercises and, indirectly, their external testing providers.

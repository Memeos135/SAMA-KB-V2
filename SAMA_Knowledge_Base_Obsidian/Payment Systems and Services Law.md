# Payment Systems and Services Law

Concept node for the Payment Systems and Services Law and its Implementing Regulation, which make SAMA the competent authority for the regulation, supervision and oversight of payment systems and their operators in the Kingdom. The Law underpins SAMA's oversight mandate to monitor and assess existing and planned systems against safety and efficiency objectives, to set controls and standards aligned with the CPMI-IOSCO PFMIs, and to require changes where needed. It binds payment system operators and, through the Regulation, licensed payment service providers.

# Basel III: Finalizing Post-Crisis Reforms

This node covers SAMA's adoption of the Basel III post-crisis final reforms and the associated glossary of SAMA-issued standards (credit risk, counterparty credit risk and CVA, market risk, operational risk, leverage ratio, LCR and NSFR), which are the cross-referenced sources for Pillar 3 templates such as key metrics (KM1). It defines the standard-by-standard architecture of the Saudi prudential framework and the metrics banks must disclose quarterly. It applies to SAMA-licensed banks.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

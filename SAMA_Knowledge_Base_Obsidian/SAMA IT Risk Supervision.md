# SAMA IT Risk Supervision

Node covering the SAMA Cyber Security Framework's risk management and compliance section together with its risk-related glossary (risk register, risk tolerance, risk treatment, risk-aware culture, root-cause analysis). It requires Member Organizations to run a defined, approved and implemented cyber security risk management process — identification, analysis, response, monitoring and review — aligned with enterprise risk management and focused on confidentiality, integrity and availability of information assets, with periodic review and audit of control compliance. Relevant to how SAMA supervises IT and cyber risk at supervised institutions.

# SAMA Rules on Large Exposures of Banks

Cross-reference to SAMA's Rules on Large Exposures of Banks, which supply the measurement methodology for exposures and the group-of-connected-counterparties limits applied when calculating related-party exposures. Under the Related Parties Rules, related-party exposures are measured per the Large Exposures Rules, and where a related party sits within a connected group, both the related-party cap and the overall group exposure limit apply. Binds banks; open the Large Exposures Rules themselves for the measurement and aggregation detail.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

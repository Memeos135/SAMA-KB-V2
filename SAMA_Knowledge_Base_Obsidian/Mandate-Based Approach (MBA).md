# Mandate-Based Approach (MBA)

The mandate-based approach is the second-tier method for equity investments in funds, used where the bank cannot meet the conditions for look-through but can rely on the fund's mandate or applicable regulations to infer the underlying exposures. The source situates it within the hierarchy alongside the look-through and fall-back approaches, with rules on partial use, leverage adjustment, funds investing in other funds and application by IRB banks. Binds banks holding fund equity exposures and feeds the RWA figures disclosed in Pillar 3 overview templates.

**Regimes:** banking prudential

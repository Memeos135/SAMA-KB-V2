# Remittance Membership

Defined term in the Remittance Centers Guide: a register held with a bank's remittance center created under a signed Remittance Membership Opening Agreement between the center and the customer, containing the customer's information and creating mutual rights and obligations. Related provisions govern membership opening conditions, mandatory bilingual name and national/resident ID fields, linkage of membership numbers to identity, manager approval, approvals for high-risk customers, and termination where identification or source of transactions cannot be verified or the membership is misused. Relevant to defining the customer relationship for AML/CFT purposes in remittance services.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, payments, consumer protection

# Real Estate Finance Circulars

## Why this community

Prudential and conduct limits on real estate finance - maximum financing ratios, eligibility of land financing, mortgage registration accuracy, and non-discrimination in financing terms and insurance cover.

## How members connect

- The Real Estate Finance Law and its Implementing Regulation are the parent instruments; each circular either amends a numeric limit under them or clarifies application of a specific article.
- The financing-limit and land-financing circulars form an obligation chain: the maximum LTV/financing cap is set by circular, then a further circular fixes whether land-only financing falls within the Article 12 regime.
- Mortgage registration and equal-treatment circulars address conduct duties on the same contracts - registering security for the actual contracted amount and not differentiating financing or insurance terms between comparable customers.
- Cross-regime note: the equal-treatment and registration duties overlap with consumer-protection principles and with insurance conduct rules, so a single real estate finance product can attract obligations from more than one SAMA regime.

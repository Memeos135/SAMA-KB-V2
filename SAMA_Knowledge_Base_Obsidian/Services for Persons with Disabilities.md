# Services for Persons with Disabilities

SAMA instructions on services provided to persons with disabilities by financial institutions, aimed at fairness, financial inclusion and removal of physical and behavioural barriers so that customers with disabilities can manage their financial affairs independently and privately. It defines "person with disability", "disability", "financial institution" and "accessibility", and imposes requirements including establishing a dedicated administrative unit, accessible premises and branches, accessible self-service and electronic channels, transparency and fairness in dealings, and financial education and awareness. It binds all SAMA-supervised financial institutions — banks and foreign bank branches, insurance and reinsurance firms, finance companies, finance lease registration companies, credit information companies, payment companies and exchange businesses — with a three-month implementation period and a compliance plan due within one month.

# Table MRA - General Qualitative Market Risk

Qualitative Pillar 3 disclosure table requiring banks to describe their market risk management objectives and policies, including trading strategies, trading vs banking book designation policies, stale positions, internal risk transfers, and the market risk governance structure. Mandatory and annual, in flexible format, for all banks subject to the market risk framework.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

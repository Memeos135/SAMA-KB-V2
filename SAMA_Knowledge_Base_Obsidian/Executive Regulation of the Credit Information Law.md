# Executive Regulation of the Credit Information Law

The Executive Regulation of the Credit Information Law (issued under Royal Decree M/37 dated 5/7/1429H), the implementing framework administered by SAMA. It covers licensing of credit information (credit bureau) companies — including the joint-stock form, minimum paid-up capital of SAR 50 million, application contents, fees, five-year licence term and renewal, and prior written SAMA approval for branches, mergers and constitutional changes — plus scope of credit information services, governance, the credit record, supervisory requirements, confidentiality, member obligations, objections and complaints, and the committee for violations and credit information disputes. It binds licensed credit information companies and Members (government or private entities that exchange credit information with at least one bureau).

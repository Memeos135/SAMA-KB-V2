# Securitization External Ratings-Based Approach (SEC-ERBA)

Sets out the External Ratings-Based Approach for securitisation exposures, including the operational requirements for using external credit assessments and inferred ratings, and the alternative treatment for STC-compliant term and short-term securitisations. It sits within the hierarchy of approaches (SEC-IRBA, SEC-ERBA, SEC-IAA, SEC-SA) and the mixed-pool rules requiring SAMA approval in specified cases. Applies to banks in Saudi Arabia calculating capital for securitisation positions.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

# Customer Due Diligence & Distribution

## Why this community

Circular-level due diligence expectations where onboarding/KYC intersects with the distribution of investment products through electronic money institution channels.

## How members connect

- Both instruments are SAMA circulars imposing customer-facing verification duties rather than framework-level rules.
- Shared concept: the depth of information required about a customer (notably income/source of funds) before a product or channel may be offered.
- Cross-regime interaction: AML/CTF customer due diligence expectations are applied within a payments/EMI distribution setting, so the EMI's 'customer' and the fund's investor must be reconciled.

# Specialized Lending Sub-classes

Rules identifying five specialised lending sub-classes within the IRB corporate asset class, defined by characteristics present in legal form or economic substance (typically exposure to an entity created to finance or operate physical assets). Part of the broader exposure categorisation requirement under which banks must demonstrate to SAMA that their asset-class assignment methodology is appropriate and consistent over time. Binds banks using the IRB approach; note the IRB approach is not permitted for equity exposures.

**Regimes:** banking prudential

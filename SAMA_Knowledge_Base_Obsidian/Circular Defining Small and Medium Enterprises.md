# Circular Defining Small and Medium Enterprises

A SAMA Banking Policies Department circular adopting the SME definition approved by the board of the General Authority for Small and Medium Enterprises (Monsha'at), setting micro, small, medium and large enterprise thresholds by annual revenue with full-time employee headcount as the fallback criterion where revenue data is unavailable (e.g. new entities). It binds banks, finance companies and the credit information companies (SIMAH and Bayan).

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, banking prudential, data/credit info

# Net Stable Funding Ratio (NSFR)

This node defines the Net Stable Funding Ratio itself — available stable funding over required stable funding, to be maintained at 100% or more on an ongoing basis — and explains its calibration by funding tenor, funding type and counterparty, and asset tenor, credit-creation and bank roll-over behaviour assumptions. It also records that SAMA may impose more stringent standards on an individual bank based on its funding risk profile and Sound Principles compliance. It applies to banks under SAMA supervision.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

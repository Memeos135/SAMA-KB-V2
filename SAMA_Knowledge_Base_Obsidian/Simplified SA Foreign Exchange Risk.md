# Simplified SA: Foreign Exchange Risk

The foreign exchange risk class within SAMA's simplified standardised approach to market risk capital (CR_FX), including additional charges for non-delta option risks arising from FX instruments. Its output is added to the interest rate, equity and commodity risk charges, with the aggregate scaled by 12.5 to produce market risk risk-weighted assets. Applies to banks under SAMA supervision that are eligible for and apply the simplified standardised approach.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

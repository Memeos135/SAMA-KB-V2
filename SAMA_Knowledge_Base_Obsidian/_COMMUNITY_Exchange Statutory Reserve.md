# Exchange Statutory Reserve

## Why this community

Statutory reserve requirements applied to money exchange centres — a prudential capital/liquidity buffer condition attached to the exchange licence.

## How members connect

- Standalone prudential obligation directed at licensed exchange businesses rather than banks; scope is limited to the exchange regime.
- Sits alongside, and is distinct from, the AML/CTF obligations that dominate the exchange sector — reserve compliance is a solvency/licence matter, not a financial-crime one.
- Non-compliance bears on continued licensing and supervisory action by SAMA.

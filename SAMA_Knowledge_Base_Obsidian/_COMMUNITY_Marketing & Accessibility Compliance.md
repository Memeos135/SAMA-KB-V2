# Marketing & Accessibility Compliance

## Why this community

Consumer-protection conduct obligations governing how financial institutions communicate with and serve the public — fair, non-misleading advertising and marketing, and equitable access to products and channels for persons with disabilities.

## How members connect

- Both sit in the consumer protection / market conduct regime rather than prudential or AML rules, and bind the institution's dealings with the 'Consumer' as a defined term.
- Shared obligation logic: disclosure that is clear, accurate and comprehensible — advertising rules set what must not mislead, accessibility rules set for whom information and channels must be usable.
- Both operate as ongoing conduct duties enforceable through SAMA supervisory action and complaint handling, not one-off approvals.
- Typical hierarchy: consumer protection principles issued by SAMA, elaborated through instructions/circulars addressed to banks, finance companies and PSPs.

# Appendix C Asset Quality Classification

Appendix C asset quality return and its completion instructions, read with Chapter 6, prescribing how DTFCs report loans by classification category (Normal, Watch, Substandard, Doubtful, Loss) with number of accounts, outstanding amounts, minimum provisioning percentages, provisions required, and security held. It also carries return definitions for on- and off-balance sheet items (loans reported net of provisions, treatment of reserves appropriated from retained earnings, credit risk equivalents at 100% weight). Binds every DTFC reporting to SAMA.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, BNPL/finance, governance/risk

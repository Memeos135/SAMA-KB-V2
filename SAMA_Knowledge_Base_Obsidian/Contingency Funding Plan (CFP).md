# Contingency Funding Plan (CFP)

Sets the requirement for finance companies to maintain a Contingency Funding Plan addressing strategy and procedures for handling liquidity crises and covering cash-flow shortfalls under stress. Prescribes minimum content: definition of a liquidity crisis event, quantitative and qualitative early warning indicators with monitoring ownership and frequency, response options by time horizon, roles and responsibilities, balance-sheet modification plans, prioritised alternative funding sources, activation authority, escalation, and periodic testing and updating. The Board must approve the CFP at least annually.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, BNPL/finance

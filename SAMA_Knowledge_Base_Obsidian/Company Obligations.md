# Company Obligations

An obligations article within SAMA's draft Rules Governing Bancassurance Activities, setting out what the licensed insurance Company must do when a Bank markets and distributes its insurance products. Duties include obtaining SAMA's prior approval for material amendments to the Agreement, submitting an annual training plan for Authorised Employees (covering marketing/distribution techniques, product workshops and AML/CTF training), preserving confidentiality of Bank and Client data, periodically reviewing the Bank's conduct of Bancassurance Activities across electronic and non-electronic channels, and paying commission within SAMA-set limits. It also carries a tying prohibition barring the Bank from conditioning banking products on insurance purchase or vice versa, unless coverage is required by a competent authority.

**Regimes:** other, consumer protection, governance/risk, AML/CTF

# Protection and Safekeeping of Protected Funds

A dedicated part of the Implementing Regulation of the Payments and Payment Services Law dealing with the protection and safekeeping of protected funds, i.e. customer/e-money holder funds received by licensees. It governs how such funds must be segregated, held and safeguarded against the licensee's own money and creditors, and is a core safeguarding obligation for licensed payment service providers and electronic money issuers operating in the Kingdom.

**Regimes:** payments, consumer protection, governance/risk

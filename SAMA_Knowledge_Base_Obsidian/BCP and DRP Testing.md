# BCP and DRP Testing

This node covers the testing obligations for business continuity and disaster recovery under the SAMA BCM Framework. Member Organizations must define, approve, execute and monitor BCP simulation exercises at least annually with well-planned scenarios (including cyber security scenarios) and consider an integrated BCM test across all critical services and functions, and must run a DR test combined with BCP at least annually, evaluating readiness of DR infrastructure supporting critical systems and producing improvement recommendations. Test scenarios must cover activation of the crisis management team, and testing serves both to validate the plans and to train employees and third parties.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential

# SARIE Instant Payments System

A Governor-level SAMA circular announcing the launch of the SARIE instant payments system (effective 21 February 2021) and setting binding conditions on participating banks. It requires signing the system joining agreement, adherence to the latest technical/operational documents and SLAs, compliance with transaction caps (Single Transaction Limit and Quick Transfer Limit), a customer fee ceiling of SAR 0.50 for transfers up to SAR 500 and SAR 1 above that, adherence to the SARIE brand and customer journey guidelines, staff training, and rebranding of channels and public materials. It binds all banks operating in the Kingdom as system participants.

**Regimes:** payments, consumer protection, governance/risk

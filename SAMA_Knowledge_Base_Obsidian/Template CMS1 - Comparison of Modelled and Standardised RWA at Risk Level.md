# Template CMS1 - Comparison of Modelled and Standardised RWA at Risk Level

A Pillar 3 disclosure template within SAMA's adopted Basel disclosure framework requiring banks to compare risk-weighted assets produced by SAMA-approved internal models against RWA that would result from the corresponding standardised approaches, broken down at risk-category level. It specifies which exposures belong in which row (credit risk excluding counterparty credit risk, CVA and banking-book securitisations; equity phase-in treatment; settlement/failed trades) and how minimum capital requirements are derived. It binds banks subject to SAMA's capital adequacy and disclosure requirements that use modelled approaches.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

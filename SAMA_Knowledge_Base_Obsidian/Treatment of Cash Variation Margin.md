# Treatment of Cash Variation Margin

Sets the cumulative conditions under which cash variation margin may be treated as a form of pre-settlement payment for leverage ratio purposes — non-segregation and free use of cash received, at least daily mark-to-market exchange, currency specified in the contract/MNA/CSA, full extinguishing amount, and coverage by a single legally enforceable master netting agreement. Where met, cash variation margin received reduces the replacement cost component (not PFE) and margin provided may be deducted from the exposure measure; it also introduces the clearing member/CCP client clearing treatment. It binds banks calculating the leverage ratio for derivative exposures.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

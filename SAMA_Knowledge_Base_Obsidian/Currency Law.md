# Currency Law

The Currency Law, issued by Royal Decree No. 6 dated 1/7/1379H, establishing the Saudi Riyal as the monetary unit, its subdivisions and par value in gold, and vesting the exclusive privilege of minting, printing and issuing Saudi currency in SAMA. It allocates decision-making between the Council of Ministers, the Minister of Finance and SAMA over denominations, design, withdrawal and replacement of worn notes and coins, seizure of counterfeit currency, full gold and foreign-currency backing of issued currency, legal tender status and limits on compulsory acceptance of subsidiary coin, signature of banknotes, withdrawal of the gold sovereign, and SAMA's record-keeping and periodic publication duties. It binds SAMA, government bodies and, as to legal tender and counterfeiting, all persons in the Kingdom.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, other

# Minimum Paid-up Capital Requirements

Capital-adequacy dimension of the amended Executive Regulation of the Finance Companies Control Law, covering minimum paid-up capital by financing activity and related prudential limits such as large exposure measured against paid-up capital and reserves. The provided extract shows only the circular and definitions, so the numeric capital thresholds must be verified in the full regulation text. Applies to all finance companies licensed by SAMA, including those adjusting status after the repeal of the microfinance and micro consumer finance rules.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, banking prudential, governance/risk

# Commodity Risk

A defined risk class within SAMA's Minimum Capital Requirements for Market Risk framework, sitting alongside general interest rate risk, credit spread risk, FX risk and equity risk. It governs how banks identify commodity risk factors and compute capital requirements (including under the sensitivities-based and simplified standardised approaches), and it is one of the risks that may arise in the banking book and be moved via internal risk transfers with capital recognition. Binds SAMA-licensed banks calculating regulatory market risk capital.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

# Simple, Transparent, Comparable (STC) Framework

Node defining the simple, transparent and comparable (STC) securitisation framework and the conditions for alternative (preferential) capital treatment, limited to traditional securitisations including ABCP conduit exposures and transactions financed by such conduits. It allocates responsibility: the originator/sponsor must disclose sufficient transaction-level information, the investor must make its own STC compliance assessment before applying the alternative treatment, and STC criteria must be satisfied on an ongoing basis; retained positions with achieved significant risk transfer are assessed by the retaining originator. Binds SAMA-supervised banks acting as originator, sponsor or investor, and drives the STC columns in Pillar 3 template SEC1.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

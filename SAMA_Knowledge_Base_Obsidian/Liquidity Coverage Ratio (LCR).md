# Liquidity Coverage Ratio (LCR)

Explains the Liquidity Coverage Ratio as SAMA's short-term liquidity standard requiring banks to hold sufficient high-quality liquid assets (HQLA) to survive a 30-day stress scenario, and positions it as complementary to the NSFR plus the associated liquidity monitoring tools. It cross-references earlier SAMA circulars implementing the amended LCR (2013) and the monitoring tools (2014). Applies to banks supervised by SAMA; LCR definitions are also used as the baseline for NSFR asset categorisation.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

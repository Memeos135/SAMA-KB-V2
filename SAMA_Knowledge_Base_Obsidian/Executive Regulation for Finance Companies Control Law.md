# Executive Regulation for Finance Companies Control Law

Amended Executive Regulation of the Finance Companies Control Law issued by SAMA, together with the covering circular that repeals the Microfinance Activity Rules and the Micro Consumer Finance Companies Rules and gives finance companies a rectification period. The extract covers Part One definitions (financing laws, finance contract, instalment, term cost, total cost of financing, finance amount, APR, board and senior management, exposure, large exposure, controlling interest) and SAMA's supervisory mandate over the finance sector. It binds licensed finance companies, SAMA-licensed digital intermediation companies and debt collection entities acting for financiers.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, governance/risk, consumer protection

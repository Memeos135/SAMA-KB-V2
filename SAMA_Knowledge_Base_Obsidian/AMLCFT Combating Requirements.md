# AML/CFT Combating Requirements

The anti-financial-crime component of SAMA's Principles of Conduct and Work Ethics: a conduct-level duty on financial institution personnel to combat financial and administrative corruption, covering money laundering, terrorist financing and suspicious transactions alongside bribery and corruption. It expresses ethical/behavioural obligations for staff rather than the operational AML/CTF control framework, which sits in the AML Law, its Implementing Regulation and SAMA's AML/CTF Rules. Binds all personnel of SAMA-supervised financial institutions, with managers responsible for awareness and acknowledgement.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, governance/risk

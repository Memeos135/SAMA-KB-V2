# Market Risk & CVA Templates

## Why this community

Pillar 3 quantitative disclosure templates for market risk (standardised and internal models approaches) and credit valuation adjustment risk, plus the RWA overview and modelled-versus-standardised comparison tables that sit above them. The regulatory problem-space is comparable, consistent public disclosure of capital requirements by risk type and calculation approach.

## How members connect

- All templates feed the same aggregate: OV1 is the RWA overview into which the market risk, CVA and counterparty credit risk lines are reported, so each detailed template references it as its reconciliation point.
- The CVA templates are tiered by permitted calculation approach (reduced basic, full basic, standardised) with a separate flow statement for SA-CVA movements, mirroring the approach hierarchy in the underlying capital rules.
- MR1 (standardised) and MR2 (internal models) are the paired disclosures for market risk, and both feed the CMS1 comparison of modelled versus standardised outcomes; CMS2 extends the same comparison logic to credit risk asset classes.
- Disclosure obligations here are derivative: the templates do not create capital requirements but require publication of results computed under the capital adequacy framework, so scope depends on which approaches the bank is approved to use.

# Correspondence Relationship

Section 13 of SAMA's AML/CTF guidance/rules for financial institutions, setting the due diligence expected before and during a correspondent banking relationship: gathering sufficient information on the respondent's business, reputation, supervision and AML/CTF standards, clarifying each party's AML/CTF responsibilities, applying ongoing transaction monitoring to correspondent accounts, and obtaining senior management approval (with input from the AML/CTF compliance officer) before onboarding. It anchors on Article 9 of the AML Law and Article 68 of the Law on Combating Terrorism Crimes and Financing, and imposes an absolute bar on relationships with shell banks or with foreign institutions that permit shell bank use of their accounts. Binds SAMA-supervised financial institutions.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, banking prudential, governance/risk

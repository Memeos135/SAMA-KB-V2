# Agent Registry (Art 29)

Article 29 concerns the registry of banking Agents maintained in connection with the Agent Banking Regulation, sitting in the section on Agent Registration alongside annual reporting obligations. It complements the transparency duties requiring Banks to publish and update Agent details (including terminated Agents, relocations and transfers) and the SAMA non-objection process for contracting Agents. Binds Banks operating Agent Banking arrangements.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, banking prudential, consumer protection

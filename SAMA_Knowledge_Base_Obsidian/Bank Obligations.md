# Bank Obligations

Part of the Bancassurance Rules setting out the mandatory minimum content of the bank–insurer Agreement (term, termination, distribution channels, training, KYC, compliance, covered insurance products, marketing, premium collection, commission and its calculation, client care and complaints, claims handling) together with the bank's obligations and professional conduct duties. It makes the bank fully responsible for Authorised Employees' conduct of bancassurance activities and imposes honesty, competence, disclosure, accuracy and confidentiality standards toward Clients and the insurer. Binds banks conducting bancassurance in the Kingdom.

**Regimes:** consumer protection, governance/risk, AML/CTF

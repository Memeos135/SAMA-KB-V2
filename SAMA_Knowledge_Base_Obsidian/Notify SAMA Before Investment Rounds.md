# Notify SAMA Before Investment Rounds

SAMA circular requiring non-bank financial institutions to notify SAMA at least five business days before conducting an investment round, submitting the timetable, purpose, size, effect on ownership structure and financial position, the type and structure of the instrument, and any other supporting or requested documents. Notification does not displace other obligations, including obtaining SAMA's prior no-objection where required. It binds finance companies, payment service companies, exchange houses and SAMA-licensed finance support entities.

**Regimes:** governance/risk, payments, BNPL/finance

# Rules for Regulating Buy Now Pay Later (BNPL) Companies

SAMA rules issued under the Finance Companies Control Law regulating Buy Now Pay Later (BNPL) companies: licensing procedure and conditions, internal organisation, conduct-of-activity rules, supervision and compliance. The defined BNPL activity is financing a Customer's purchase of goods or services from Stores at no term cost to the Customer; the activity may not be carried on without a SAMA licence. The covering circular exempts BNPL companies and other finance companies conducting the activity from the DBR/affordability ratios in the Responsible Lending Principles for aggregate financing up to SAR 5,000/threshold stated, without affecting other obligations, including Article 19 of these Rules.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, consumer protection, governance/risk

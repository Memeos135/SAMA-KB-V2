# Cash Transport Companies and Secure Bags Circular

A SAMA Currency Department circular (Arabic) implementing Ministry of Interior/Crime Research Center recommendations requiring large companies and commercial establishments to move cash only through licensed cash-transport (cash-in-transit) firms and to use secure bags/containers approved by SAMA, including containers permitting automatic tracking and self-destruction of notes if the planned route is altered. It binds banks and exchange companies operating in the Kingdom, licensed cash-transport companies, and licensed operators of unified cash centres, with SAMA field inspections and penalties for non-compliance.

# Synthetic Securitization

Defines synthetic securitisation for capital purposes as a structure with at least two stratified tranches of differing credit risk where credit risk of the underlying pool is transferred using credit derivatives or guarantees rather than by sale of the assets. Read together with the scope rule that banks must apply the securitisation framework to both traditional and synthetic structures (and hybrids) on an economic-substance basis. Binds banks calculating regulatory capital and assessing significant risk transfer.

**Regimes:** banking prudential

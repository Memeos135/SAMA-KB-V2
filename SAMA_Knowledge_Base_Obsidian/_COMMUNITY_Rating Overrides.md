# Rating Overrides

## Why this community

Controls over human override of model-generated ratings, including the circumstances permitting an override and the documentation and monitoring expected.

## How members connect

- Isolated node but legally dependent on the rating system design and use-of-statistical-models requirements, which permit judgemental adjustment only under defined controls.
- Override frequency and outcomes are an input to validation of internal estimates and to supervisory assessment of model reliance.

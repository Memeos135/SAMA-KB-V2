# PV01 Sensitivity

PV01 is the interest-rate delta sensitivity measure used in the sensitivities-based method: the change in an instrument's market value from a one basis point shift in the relevant rate risk factor. The standard requires sensitivities to be derived from the pricing models the bank's independent risk control unit uses for market risk or actual P&L reporting, with alternative formulations permitted only if demonstrated to SAMA as producing very close results. Binds banks using the standardised approach for market risk.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

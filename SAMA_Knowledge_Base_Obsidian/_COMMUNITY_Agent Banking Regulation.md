# Agent Banking Regulation

## Why this community

SAMA's Agent Banking Regulation: the conditions under which a bank may deliver banking services through third-party agents, covering agent eligibility, fit-and-proper vetting, agent-level due diligence, registration, and the permitted/prohibited activity perimeter.

## How members connect

- The Regulation is the parent instrument; the article-level nodes (Arts 9, 13, 14, 19, 20, 29) are its operative obligations and should be read as a single onboarding-to-oversight chain: eligibility → fit and proper → agent due diligence → registry notification → activity limits.
- Two distinct diligence concepts intersect: Agent Due Diligence (bank assessing the agent, akin to KYB of a contracted party) versus Customer Due Diligence (AML/CTF obligations on the end customer performed at the agent channel) — the bank retains legal responsibility for the latter even when the agent executes it.
- The Bank Board of Directors node anchors non-delegable governance accountability: outsourcing the activity does not outsource the obligation or supervisory liability.
- Permissible and Prohibited Activities define the scope limit that constrains everything else, and cross-regime interaction with the AML/CTF preventive-measures regime arises wherever agents perform identification or transaction handling.

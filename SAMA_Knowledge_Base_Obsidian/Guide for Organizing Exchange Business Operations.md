# Guide for Organizing Exchange Business Operations

A SAMA circular issuing a guide organising the operational procedures of money exchange businesses, grounded in the Rules Regulating Money Changing Business and the licensing requirement to conduct exchange activity. It distinguishes licence categories — Category A (permitted to carry out domestic and cross-border money transfer in addition to exchange) and Category B (buying and selling foreign currency, travellers' cheques and bank cheques) — and directs compliance with related instructions, ministerial decisions and AML/CTF rules. It binds all licensed exchange houses of Category A and B operating in the Kingdom.

**Regimes:** AML/CTF, payments, governance/risk

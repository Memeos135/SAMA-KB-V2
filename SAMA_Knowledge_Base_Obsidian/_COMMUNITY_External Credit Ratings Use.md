# External Credit Ratings Use

## Why this community

Pillar 3 disclosure of how a bank uses external credit assessment institutions (ECAI) ratings when applying the standardised approach to credit risk. It covers the qualitative narrative on which ratings are recognised, for which exposure classes, and how they are mapped to risk weights.

## How members connect

- Single-member cluster: a qualitative disclosure table that sets the interpretive basis for the quantitative standardised-approach templates elsewhere in the framework.
- Depends on the defined concepts of eligible ECAI, exposure class and risk-weight mapping used in SAMA's capital adequacy/credit risk rules; the table discloses application, it does not itself set the mapping.
- Sits at the circular/framework-annex level beneath the credit risk capital regulation it implements.

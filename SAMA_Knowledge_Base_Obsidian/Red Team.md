# Red Team

Defined role within the F.E.E.R. Framework: the Red Team comprises the external Red Teaming Provider's staff who execute the agreed attack scenarios against the member organization's defined critical information assets. It operates under contract with the member organization (NDA, liability terms, Letter of Authorization), is introduced to the White and Green Teams, must meet the framework's experience, methodology and staff-certification requirements, and adjusts or halts scenarios at the White Team Leader's direction. It reports observations and findings chronologically and joins the replay and feedback sessions.

**Regimes:** governance/risk

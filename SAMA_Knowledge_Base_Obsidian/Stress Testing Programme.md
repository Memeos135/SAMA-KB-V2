# Stress Testing Programme

Concept node for the stress testing programme required of banks using the internal models approach for market risk capital. It requires rigorous, comprehensive stress testing at both trading desk and bank-wide level, covering low-probability market, credit and operational risk events, linear and non-linear positions, and both quantitative and qualitative elements, with results reported to senior management and periodically to the board. Banks must combine SAMA-specified scenarios with their own bank-specific scenarios.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

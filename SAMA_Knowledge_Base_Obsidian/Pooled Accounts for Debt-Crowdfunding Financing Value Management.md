# Pooled Accounts for Debt-Crowdfunding Financing Value Management

A SAMA circular (Arabic original) on pooled/collective accounts used to manage financing value at debt-crowdfunding entities, issued under the SAMA Law and the Finance Companies Control Law and reinforcing the Rules for Practising Debt-Based Crowdfunding. It requires that pooled accounts be used solely to manage financing value and be segregated from the entity's own funds, be named "Financing Value Management Account – [entity name]", that SAMA be notified on opening or closing such accounts at banks in the Kingdom, that written policies and authority matrices with multi-level control and periodic reporting govern operation, that the account carry no financial obligations or rights of the entity, that reconciliation between executed financings and account balances be performed and documented, and that the bank account agreement reflect the regulatory requirements and allocate responsibilities. It binds debt-based crowdfunding entities operating in the Kingdom.

**Regimes:** BNPL/finance, governance/risk, consumer protection

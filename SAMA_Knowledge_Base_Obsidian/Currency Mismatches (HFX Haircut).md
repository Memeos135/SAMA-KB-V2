# Currency Mismatches (HFX Haircut)

Part of SAMA's Minimum Capital Requirements for Credit Risk dealing with currency mismatch between an exposure and its credit protection or collateral. It requires the protected amount to be reduced by an 8% foreign-exchange haircut (HFX) based on a 10-business-day holding period with daily marking to market, scaled up by the square-root-of-time rule where revaluation is less frequent; it also covers related collateral haircut rules (basket haircuts, non-eligible instruments in SFTs). Binds SAMA-supervised banks calculating credit risk-weighted assets under the standardized approach and applying credit risk mitigation.

**Regimes:** banking prudential, governance/risk

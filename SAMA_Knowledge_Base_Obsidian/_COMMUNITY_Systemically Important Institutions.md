# Systemically Important Institutions

## Why this community

Identification and differentiated prudential treatment of institutions whose failure would threaten financial stability, split between the global designation regime (G-SIBs) and SAMA's domestic designation (D-SIBs).

## How members connect

- All three labels share a single underlying concept — systemic importance — with SIFI as the umbrella term and G-SIB/D-SIB as the two designation tracks.
- The linkage is definitional and hierarchical: Basel-derived global criteria feed the domestic assessment methodology SAMA applies to locally licensed banks.
- Designation is the trigger condition for add-on obligations (higher loss absorbency, enhanced supervision, recovery/resolution planning), so the terms operate as scope gates rather than free-standing duties.
- Cross-regime relevance: designation status also conditions governance, risk and capital expectations applied to the same entity.

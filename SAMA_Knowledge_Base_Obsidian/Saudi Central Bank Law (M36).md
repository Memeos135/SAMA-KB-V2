# Saudi Central Bank Law (M/36)

Node anchored on the Saudi Central Bank Law (Royal Decree M/36) as the source of SAMA's supervisory mandate; the attached source is SAMA's circular issuing the updated Oversight Framework for Payment Systems and their Operators, which replaces the earlier framework for payment and financial settlement systems. It is issued under the Law of Payments and Payment Services (M/16) and its Implementing Regulation, and sets the framework's scope, classification of systemically important versus non-classified payment systems, supervisory methodology (self-assessment and supervisory assessment), supervisory activities, operator obligations and the applicable PFMIs. It binds payment systems and licensed payment system operators (PSOs) in the Kingdom.

**Regimes:** payments, governance/risk

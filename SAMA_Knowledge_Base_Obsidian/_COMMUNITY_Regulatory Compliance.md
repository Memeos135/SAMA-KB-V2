# Regulatory Compliance

## Why this community

The general obligation to comply with applicable SAMA and other regulatory requirements, and to demonstrate that compliance on supervision or examination.

## How members connect

- Single-node cluster acting as a catch-all compliance duty that other regime-specific obligations ultimately roll up into.
- Functions as a cross-reference anchor: breaches of specific rules (AML/CTF, payments, consumer protection) are also breaches of this general duty.
- Sits at the top of the law → regulation → circular hierarchy, so it inherits scope from whichever instrument invokes it.

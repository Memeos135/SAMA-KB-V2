# Minimum Requirements for Exchange Center Premises

SAMA circular (Money Exchange Centers Supervision Division) issuing the minimum requirements that must be met in the premises of exchange centers, adopted under paragraph (c) of Article 15 of the Rules Governing Money Exchange Business, with a one-year window to bring existing premises into compliance. It sets conditions on permitted location (main commercial street, mall, airport/border crossing) and SAMA prior no-objection before leasing, minimum floor areas and entrance setbacks (with a special threshold for the Central Haram Area), 3D engineering design by an approved office consistent with the financial sector security and safety guide, fit-out and customer-facing features (dedicated women's service window, accessibility, complaint and satisfaction mechanism, signage matching the commercial register and licence, insurance against fire and theft, national address), and technical requirements including an automated accounting/control system capable of applying AML requirements and screening against international prohibition lists in Arabic and English, off-site secure data backup with no external party access, and counterfeit-detection equipment. It binds licensed money exchangers and exchange institutions of categories (A) and (B).

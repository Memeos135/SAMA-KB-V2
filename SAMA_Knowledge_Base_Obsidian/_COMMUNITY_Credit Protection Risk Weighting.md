# Credit Protection Risk Weighting

## Why this community

Credit risk mitigation under the capital framework: who may act as a protection provider, how guarantees and credit derivatives change the risk weight of an exposure, and how residual mismatches are penalised.

## How members connect

- Eligibility of the guarantor is a precondition to the substitution treatment — the risk-weight rule cross-references the permitted range of protection providers.
- The haircut rule is a corrective overlay: recognition of protection is reduced where the protection is denominated in a different currency from the underlying exposure.
- All three share the same defined terms (eligible protection provider, protected/unprotected portion) and sit within the same standardised-approach chapter, forming a single obligation chain: eligibility -> recognition -> adjustment.
